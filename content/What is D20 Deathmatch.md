**D20 Deathmatch is a live Dungeons & Dragons (D&D) actual play show where player characters fight to the death in PvP arena battles.**

Every episode, champions created and played by guests from the D&D and streaming community are ripped out of their own worlds and dropped into [[The Void]]. There, in the arena of [[The Dread Fortress]], they have to fight for survival while chat watches live. The show is hosted by **Mudcat** as [[The Arbiter]], the DM who runs the match, and **Caustic Phoenix** as [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer]], who rules the Void. It's produced by Bad Guys Studios.

## How a D&D deathmatch works
- **The champions:** each guest brings a character built for the fight. Most matches are one on one, with team fights, gauntlets and battle royales along the way.
- **The arena:** a new battlefield every time, full of hazards, traps and whatever chaos the Arbiter has planned.
- **To the death:** whoever doesn't die wins. If nobody dies, it's a shared victory. Death in the arena isn't always the end, and plenty of champions have come back.
- **Dried Blood:** after the match, the cast hangs around for the after-show to talk about what just happened.

## Is it real Dungeons & Dragons?
Yes. It's played live with D&D 5e rules (now the 2024 rules), plus a lot of homebrew chaos. Underneath the fights runs a full story about the Grandmaster, the Arbiter and the Void that has built up over seven seasons. That story is what this wiki, **the Black Tome**, records.

## Who is it for?
Anyone who likes D&D and TTRPG actual play shows, PvP fights, or watching a good plan fall apart. Fair warning: it's an adult show, with crude humor, gore and a lot of swearing.
