The **chinchillas** of the Void ("chinkillas") are the Dread Fortress's workforce and a staple of chaos events.

- **Unionized.** They take regulated lunch breaks (a whistle blows: "That's lunch, boys"), refuse overtime, expect triple pay when Thea promises it, and are no longer allowed to clean up biohazard. "It's canon. It's been established."
- Immune to magic, and throwable.
- They range from tiny to moose-sized. In Chinchilla Forest they roll in pools of talcum powder.
- Notables: **Frank**, the cigarette-smoking lead chinchilla with sciatica (S7E5); a hard-hat foreman; a hibachi chef; **Wicked**, Jinx's chinchilla; and **Chinchilla the White**, kicked into the sun by Akumu, who came back platinum and fifteen feet tall (S7E3).
