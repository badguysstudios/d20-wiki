**Skypiercer** is the three-part prequel mini-series (it aired inside Season 3) that tells how Elusia became Grandmaster. It's the only story that leaves [[The Void]].

- **Before:** on the Night of the Long Shadow, Agdrel killed his brother Faucius Golden Shield and became [[The Shadow King]]. The Knights of Chaos gave their lives in a blood rite to send young Elusia to **Cor'thun**, a realm outside the Void. There she was raised and trained by Empress Juntikar.
- **The story:** after decades of study, Vythorn ([[The Arbiter]]) crossed into Cor'thun to bring Elusia home. With **Atalantos** and **Kaja** she returned and defeated the Shadow King after his 300-year reign. Elusia became Grandmaster and the first Deathmatches began, about 400 years before Season 1.
- **Epilogue:** Vythorn sealed the Shadow King's soul in [[The Box of Chaos]] as "Gerald." The effort turned his wings white.
- **Themes:** death, a long life, and why the people around us matter.
