Round two of the Battle for the Crown sent the Grandmaster's team of [[Cosmonius]], a vain centaur monk, and [[Locket]], a vengeful tabaxi archer, against the Arbiter's pair: the lubricated bugbear [[Silky Slim]] and the robot-bodied [[Baron Voltágè]]. The fight took place in the silent, rotting [[The land of the corrupted|land of the corrupted]]. Along the way it brought a zoo of transformed champions, a mob of heckling children, an exploding puddle of lube and a Kraken. It ended with a death scene nobody saw coming, and a plea for the bloodshed to stop.

## The Story

### Before the Fight

With the Arbiter one point behind in the tournament standings, [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] ran through [[The Dread Fortress|the Dread Fortress]] in a panic, heels in one hand and her dress in the other, shouting for him. She found [[The Arbiter|the Arbiter]] lounging sideways on her throne, feet on the table and a cigarette in hand. A card on the back of the throne read "Belongs to the Grandmaster." She ordered him off, "Feet off, butt off. That is mine until you actually try to earn it," and complained that she never got his calendar invite. He was having none of it: "I don't work for you anymore, and I don't have to send you calendar invites. Manage your own Google shit."

The Arbiter accused her of letting [[The Box of Chaos|Gerald]] help her champions cheat the week before. She said it was a random draw and that his champions died because he picked weak ones. He called it "a fluke… a little performance anxiety," clapped open a portal, and brought out his team. First came Baron Voltágè, whose soul the Arbiter had placed "in a nice new robot body." Then came "our original champion, Silky Slim."

The Grandmaster shoved them aside for a grander entrance. [[Goblins]] wheeled in a creaking red-draped curtain, and she pulled it open on "the bad asses of the arena." The goblins applauded "because they have to."

### The Champions

**Cosmonius** trotted out like a dressage horse. He was a topless centaur monk from [[The Feywild|the Feywild]], with flowing Fabio hair, a chestnut horse body and a much-admired mustache. He finger-gunned the walls and ceiling, because he always assumes someone is scrying on him. He introduced himself as "the monk who can heal himself by punching himself in the face," and admitted he hadn't expected to see the light of day again after his first bout.

**Locket** kept it short. She was there to stop the Arbiter: "He is a phony. He is a fake, and he deserves to go down. You have no idea what I've been through." The Arbiter agreed they had history, and twisted the knife: "I'm sure a lot of people thought they weren't going to die. You know, your friend [[Lyrus]], for one, maybe."

**Baron Voltágè** was delighted to be back in a body, though it was nothing personal: "the Arbiter just offered dental." He announced he was rebranding, and instead of cackling he would now make "servitor noises." He vented steam from the back of his neck. **Silky Slim** turned out to be a large bugbear in a beret, a vest and briefs, lounging on the biggest pillow in sight with a squirt bottle of hibiscus oil. "Oh, hey, Daddy. I heard you needed some hips and nips." He oiled Cosmonius's dry coat without asking. When the Grandmaster offered the Baron some oil, he declined: "I'm a professional, Grandmaster. I lubricate it before entering."

### Into the Land of the Corrupted

The Arbiter's portal opened onto a cold, damp and utterly silent place. There were no birds and no sound. Barren trees reached almost to the moon, the ground was close to freezing and smelled of rot and death, and boulders ten to fifteen feet high stood among them. "Welcome, dear champions, to the land of the corrupted," said the Grandmaster. "Nothing grows well here… Perhaps spilling a little blood might bring back my garden."

Slim opened by pouring a protective circle of oil around himself. He then challenged "Robo Daddy" to a fight, and the Arbiter had to remind him the Baron was on his team. Slim flew into a rage and stomped around squirting oil into the air. The Baron went back to back with him: "Mr. Slim, you must protect me while I commence my unnecessary startup sequence." He then shifted from robot to truck and back again with Transformer noises, sped himself up with magic and raised a shield. Cosmonius cantered across the field trailing Feywild pixie dust and blessed himself and Locket. The Grandmaster held up [[Doug the Pug]] so he could get sparkles too, and Doug licked at them. Locket scrambled up a boulder, picked Slim as the weaker target, and drew **first blood** with two arrows. Slim was unbothered: "Silky Slim ain't no pincushion, lady. He's luxurious."

### The Zoo and the Children

The first chaos event turned everyone into animals. Locket became a giant bat, Slim a giant badger, and the Baron a robotic walrus. Cosmonius became a giant goat, and his mustache hung off both sides of its face as a goatee. Badger Slim tunneled under the goat and burst up biting. Cosmonius checked, for science, whether he was a fainting goat. He wasn't. The walrus slapped its own stomach, hauled itself up a ten-foot rock tooth by tooth and gored the bat. Goat Cosmonius rammed the badger onto its back, crying "Cosgotnius!"

Then the Grandmaster ended the zoo, and a crowd of bully children, eight and under, began heckling from the stands. Their insults stung everyone but Cosmonius. The Arbiter approved: "Children can be cruel. That's why I like them." The Baron fired back at them, "Well, you're adopted and your parents were killed in the purges of 1528," and two of the children passed out. Locket shed a small tear: "That hurt my soul."

### Slippery Slim

Slim's next swing went badly, and he stabbed himself in the right butt cheek with his own glaive. The Baron helped by harpooning a hose into Slim's buttocks and pumping him full of green growth fluid: "Oh, hello, my name is Silky Slim now. I'm a big boy." The Arbiter added that he now had an enlarged heart. The Baron then punched Locket with electric gauntlets for sharing his rock. She disagreed: "This is my rock, okay? I do not share."

Cosmonius tried twice to wrestle Slim and slid off both times, once ending up face-first in Slim's perm. He then talked the bugbear into a rebrand: "Your name should be Slippery Slim." Slim loved it. The Baron lightning-bolted the centaur, and Cosmonius got a twitching "charley horse" in one back leg. Then Locket asked a question. The Grandmaster looked it up live: lube is flammable. Locket touched her quiver, lit her arrows in her signature move, and fired a perfect shot. **The lube went up like lighter fluid.** "How would you do this to me with my lube? You're hateful. A hateful woman… Curse you, Cat Lady." The Grandmaster called it "Classic Locket." Cosmonius told the Arbiter he was backing the wrong horse.

A singed Slim pole-vaulted onto Locket's rock, chopped her and sprayed oil in her face. Cosmonius ate the one chocolate bar he had saved from his last fight's piñata, galloped over in a sugar rush, beat Slim with his hooves, and punched healing into Locket's arm: "Come on, get back on the horse!" Locket brought out a Helm of the Gods she had barely used and prayed for "this one chance to get something right" for herself and her friends [[Toddleheim]] and [[Flynry]]. Then she filled Slim's head with dissonant whispers.

### The Ground Gives Way

"I think you're going to lose," the Grandmaster told the Arbiter. In answer, he snapped his fingers. The rotten ground withered and holes opened around the rocks, and he blew a gust that knocked Locket off her perch. The shaking scrambled the order of battle, and the Grandmaster's team now went first. Locket put four arrows into Slim, the last one because "It's personal." He dropped, then swapped his hibiscus oil for a lavender bottle and fought off death, just barely.

Cosmonius grabbed the Baron and galloped straight into a hole with him, then teleported back out in a puff of fey sparkles. The Baron fell thirty feet onto an altar in a ruined cavern below. Its pillars had crumbled, trees grew toward the light, and to one side lay a lake with "not a single ripple." Up top, Slim retreated into the brush and survived on handfuls of nutrient-rich grave dirt, sniffing his lavender lube to get it down. Below, the Baron touched the still water. A tentacle flashed up and vanished. He jetted out of the hole on thrusters, declared "I was way more happy with my old brand," returned to screaming, and called lightning down between his enemies.

### The Fall

Locket walled off the hole with an updraft that the halfling-sized Baron could not fly through. Underneath all that armor, it turned out, he was a halfling. Cosmonius leapt the pit, rode the wind to the rock behind the Baron and shoved him back in. Then he followed him down on purpose: "You know what hurts more than falling 50 feet? Having a centaur fall 50 feet on top of you." **He landed hooves-first on the Baron's head** with a perfect roll, and finished him off with punches. The Baron lay dying but conscious and spotted four tentacles rising from the lake. He taunted Cosmonius into keeping up the beating so the centaur wouldn't notice.

The Kraken rose, with row upon row of teeth and black eyes glinting in the moonlight. The Baron played dead and was spared. Cosmonius hopped from rock to rock: "Thank you for the opportunity to train, Grandmaster. Glorious as ever."

### Deaths

Up top, Locket tracked Slim by the new lavender scent. He tried his seductive "hips and nips" dance on her. She was taken (the Grandmaster reminded everyone of [[Baptiste]], a beautiful elven boy with long silver hair) and shot him anyway, apologizing: "It's nothing personal… Blame the Arbiter for this." She shouted up at the Arbiter, "Are you happy, Arbiter? I have to do this to this poor creature." He stared back with no emotion. Her second arrow killed Silky Slim. His last body roll fell flat, a shot of lavender oil rose and trickled down "like doves," and his lavender ghost floated up to complain: "Hey, Cat Lady, you suck… It's very personal. You killed me." Only then did the Arbiter wince: "God damn it, you slippery bastard."

Below, Cosmonius threw himself at the Kraken with all six limbs: "Grandmaster, you seem to have a pest problem. I'm dealing with it for you." He numbed one tentacle. Locket jumped down to help and spent her first moments eating goodberries. Slim's ghost drifted onto Cosmonius's back to criticize his split ends and his roots ("No, no, please don't tell anyone"). The Kraken summoned a lightning storm, which Cosmonius soaked up and turned back on it. Then it grabbed him and flung him into Locket, and both went down hard.

Then the Baron died. Steam burst from his helmet seam, and a barely formed, slimy baby with a single cybernetic arm crawled out. It dragged itself between the fallen victors and looked up at the sky. "Look what they make us give. As these two bicker over something as trivial as a throne while gambling with our lives… It doesn't have to be this way." Cosmonius crawled to it and took its hand: "It's okay, little one. Your suffering is almost over." The Baron's last words were "Why the long face?"

> "Good one. Enough. Enough bloodshed." (Cosmonius)

The Grandmaster drifted down and sent the Kraken back into the deep with a wave. The ground lifted everyone. The fallen stopped at the Acolytes' level, and the victors rose to the throne beside Doug. "There are two ways to win every battle," she told [[The Acolytes]]. "The first is to survive… The other way to win is to win the hearts of your fans." She called a vote on who had put on the best show and promised one final round the next week.
