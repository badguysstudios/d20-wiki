Two of the smallest champions ever to enter the arena faced off under a full moon: **[[Sila]]**, a scarf-wearing rat on a lifelong hunt for "the cheese," and **[[Ol' Pumpernicklejack]]**, a three-foot redcap who arrived straight off the toilet. What began in a graveyard became a lava surf, a rocket ride into the Grandmaster's lap and a brawl through a truly disgusting tavern. It ended with one champion dripping with toilet water and the other entombed in fondue, exactly as she asked.

## The Story

### Chess Night at the Dread Fortress

The evening began with [[The Arbiter|the Arbiter]] wandering the halls of [[The Dread Fortress|the Dread Fortress]], following the sound of snorts and laughter to the throne room. He poked his head in to ask whether [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] was actually having fun, and she told him to "either open the door or close the door. You're letting a draft in." The throne room had been rearranged. A table now stood in the middle of the purple rug, and across from the Grandmaster sat [[Claude]], the Fortress's small, bearded janitor, his tall blue hat slightly askew and his tiny feet dangling well short of the chair's edge. The two of them were playing chess.

When the Arbiter asked why, the Grandmaster explained that Claude was "better than you," noting that the Arbiter hadn't beaten her in "50 years, 52?" He objected that Claude didn't even speak English. She countered that "everybody knows the French play chess exceptionally well," then admitted she was only guessing that Claude was French. She sent him back to clean the toilet.

Then she realized it was Sunday. With a snap of her fingers, her everyday robe became her full black and gold armor, and she began to panic. Had there been champions? An arena? Had [[Thea]] been involved? The Arbiter assured her that "this time I let Thea touch none of it because she has proven herself unreliable time and time again." Nobody knew where Thea was. The Grandmaster found that worrying: "There's nothing more terrifying than Thea idle." The Arbiter guessed she was "probably like doing worm stuff." He promised the cream of the crop, which he said was skimmed for one purpose only: "It's for making dead champions."

### Two Champions, Both Very Short

The Arbiter clapped, the ripples of sound became visible and tore open a portal, and he announced "Sila versus Old Pumpernickel Jack." First came a large iron boot, then another. The boots were misleading. Their owner was about three feet tall, hunched and emaciated, with leathery skin, bushy white whiskers, bloodshot red eyes and an enormous red gnomish cap that cascaded down most of his body. His first words were "I was on the toilet, what's going on?"

The Grandmaster asked whether the Arbiter had grabbed the right one, since this one looked "mostly dead already." The Arbiter insisted Jack had been on his watchlist "for a number of seasons." Jack asked for parchment paper, the Grandmaster declared that "the Dread Fortress spares no expense on its parchment," and a row of [[Goblins|goblins]] filed in carrying every kind imaginable. Jack chose the roughest sheet, shoved it down the back of his trousers to finish the job, and politely asked whose acquaintance he was making.

"I'm down right here," came the answer. About an inch shorter than Jack stood a rat in britches and a jacket, her blue scarf billowing behind her and slapping him in the chin. Asked whether she'd brought note cards, Sila said she had her nose. The Grandmaster had never seen a talking rat and was not convinced rats were clever. Sila promised to change her mind: "Just you wait. I will get the cheese." The Arbiter announced he was already pulling for Jack.

### Into the Midnight Mausoleum

Another clap opened onto [[The Midnight Mausoleum]], a graveyard stretching to the horizon. Fog seeped between the stones, the grass was "well fertilized, but hungry for more," and a bright moon shone on every tombstone and on the ring of [[The Acolytes|Acolytes]] cheering from above. The Grandmaster begged the champions to "bring forth true blood under this moonlight," then spread her wings and took to the night sky with the Arbiter.

Sila moved first. She scurried around a gravestone on all fours, nose to the ground, and threw a dull dagger that sliced into Jack "like cutting through cheese," trimming his beard. ("It'll hurt more that way," the Grandmaster noted.) Then she did what she had really come to do. She picked the lock on a grave and robbed it with such speed that the Grandmaster dubbed her "Sila Fast Hands." Sila came from a guild of rats who loved disguises, and she was searching for the cheese, "and she will find the cheese, whether it kills her or not."

The first grave was shallow and held the decomposed body of a minotaur, still wearing a few scraps of trousers. After some debate over the name (Brormir? Boromir?), the hosts settled on [[Tormiir]], a minotaur champion of years past. "They all look the same when they die," the Arbiter shrugged. Sila covered him back up immediately, muttering "Not the cheese, not the cheese, not the cheese."

Jack, meanwhile, saw his own blood dripping. He took off his red cap, held it under the wound so each drop darkened the red, and said "Oh, Missy." When the cap went back on, he pulled from beneath it a giant iron sickle larger than himself. He flew into a rage, his wild magic filling the graveyard with ghostly shapes. A huge minotaur spirit rose beside Sila. Jack's first swing landed, though she twisted away from the worst of it. His second was a perfect strike, and the minotaur spirit exploded next to her. Sila dodged the blast, said "I thought he was coming to help me," and moved on to the next tombstone.

"Grandmaster, do you have any idea what the fuck she's talking about?" the Arbiter asked. "Yes, obviously. It's cheese," she replied. "Well, it's not Gouda." She did compliment Jack's fashion sense, since his boots matched his weapon. The Arbiter agreed that "if you die, then you're already dressed for your funeral."

### The Minitaurs

The Acolytes called for chaos, and the Arbiter made a point of saying this one wasn't his. Tiny white spikes pushed up out of the dug grave, followed by claws and hooves: hundreds of **[[The minitaurs|minitaurs]]**, each about three inches tall, swarming to drag the champions into the earth. Both shook them off easily. The minitaurs' stabbing at their toes felt like a great foot massage, although one sword-wielding minitaur kept stabbing away between Jack's toes. Jack wondered aloud whether anyone would rather fight one giant minotaur or a thousand minitaurs. The Grandmaster decided her minitaurs had been insulted enough and swept them all into the crowd with one gust: "Congratulations, chat. You are now inundated with thousands of minitaurs." She declared herself "0 for 2" on cool statements and chaos events alike.

### Chuck the Cheese

The Acolytes roared for Sila, and she spent their favor on more grave robbing rather than on Jack. This time she struck gold. In an unmarked grave lay one of her own kind, possibly bearing her guild's sigil. It was her best friend, **Chuck the Cheese**, who had been playing hide and go seek with her. "I've been searching for years."

The Arbiter asked whether they were going to ignore the obvious reference. The Grandmaster claimed she didn't know what he was talking about. Because Sila spoke to the body as if it were alive, Chuck's corpse stirred and began mimicking her every move. Jack suggested, "Let's cut the cheese, shall we?" Sila did exactly that. She stabbed her best friend's body to coat her blade in his necrotic ooze and threw it into Jack.

Jack's wild magic then summoned Chuck's actual spirit. "Hey, buddy, why are you stabbing me? What's going on?" Chuck's body grabbed a dagger and copied Sila's throw, hitting Jack, which the spirit found impressive: "Oh, whoa, that was a pretty good throw on my part." Then Chuck's spirit exploded beside Sila with a cry of "Stop it! Goodbye." Jack mopped his bleeding vest with his cap, slammed it back on, and chanted "Blood, blood, blood." He charged through a crowd of rising spirits and landed two sickle blows that left Sila badly hurt.

### The Floor Is Lava

The crowd went wild. Blue slushies flew, Acolytes tore off their clothes and stomped minitaurs in the stands, and both champions received a surge of heroic power. The Grandmaster warned that anyone who died without using it would be buried so deep "that no one will ever find you or be able to raise you from the dead."

A second chaos event followed, and the graveyard turned to lava. Both champions hopped neatly from tombstone to tombstone, Sila with perfect grace, and the Grandmaster complained that they were laughing in her face. Out of pity, Jack volunteered to fail. His iron boots began to glow white-hot and melt, and he was slower from then on. "You're a very grand master," he offered. She refused to take his pity.

### The Coffin Raft

Sila wiggled her tombstone loose like a seesaw ("We don't put a lot of maintenance into this place," the Arbiter admitted) and turned to the Acolytes for advice. They split into tidy huddles and held up billboards, and the winner read: "Ride a floating coffin through the lava." An Acolyte named August tossed down another pizza to heal her. Sila hopped into Chuck's coffin, which turned out to be cheap plastic. Inside the lid was carved "You can't keep a good cheese down." The Grandmaster reasoned that he "died a winner. Well, until Sila found him."

Then Sila invited Jack aboard. "Come on! Do you wanna die?!" Jack protested that she had been throwing knives at him "not five seconds" ago, but he jumped on, and she rowed them both through the lava. "I never promised I was trying to kill him," she explained.

Both of them smelled it at once. Up in the Grandmaster's booth sat a platter of "the official sanctioned cheese of [[Doug the Pug]], the beloved dog of the arena." The Grandmaster offered it to whoever killed the other. Doug approved, since his desire for blood outweighed his desire for cheese, and he had twelve refrigerators of the stuff in the Fortress basement anyway. Sila asked Jack how much he liked cheese. "I like blood, that's kind of my whole thing."

Jack let fate decide between Sila's plan and his own instincts, and fate chose instinct. His eyes went red: "Oh, Missy, Missy Mouse." He barreled into her and knocked them both off the raft. When his iron boots hit the lava, the pain launched him like a rocket with Sila still in his arms. They arced high into the sky and crashed straight into the cheese platter on the Grandmaster's balcony, right beside Doug, sending cheese everywhere. They wrestled on the floor like siblings. Jack swung his sickle, and Sila twisted free with a "Not today!"

The Arbiter rose, flared his white wings, and picked them both up by the scruffs of their necks. "We don't have a lot of rules, but there are some rules."

### Emmery's Tavern

The arena was ruined ("kind of a shithole now… covered in lava and corpses"), which left plenty of work for the goblins and [[Chinchillas|chinchillas]]. The Grandmaster wasn't having champions fight near her or Doug. So the Arbiter wound up and punched both of them across [[The Void|the Void]] like meteors, past the Dread Fortress and past the moon Vendolyn, and down through the roof of a small tavern. The patrons were knocked out cold. In the corner, a large bartender kept rubbing down the bar with a crusty rag, utterly unfazed: "Welcome to [[Emmery's Tavern]]." The bar had a hearth, upturned tables and barrels labeled "number two." The prize cheese was now locked in a case "until you win. If you win."

Sila sniffed for real cheese and found only processed slices, squeeze cheese and plastic-wrapped singles, smelling "like feet and burritos." It drove her feral. She rolled on the sticky floor until her fur was caked in grit, pulled out the rocks to make jagged daggers, and stabbed Jack with them. Jack's wild magic answered by teleporting them both, grappled together, into the tavern's chimney. After a long clatter, the rat hit the fire at the bottom with Jack landing on top of her. She put out the flames with her butt and rolled out smoking.

The Acolytes chanted "Jack, Jack, Jack." He dragged his sickle across the floor toward the prone rat: "Burned blood is still blood." His magic teleported him again, this time into the tavern's overflowing toilet. There was a splash, brown liquid seeped under the door, and then he burst out, leaving a Jack-shaped hole in the door. Sila called it "the crappiest attack you've ever done to me." It was also his most devastating. The bartender watched his toilet erupt and sighed, "I just started that brew."

### Cover Me in Cheese

From Sila's eyes, the final moment came as a reeking silhouette with only the shine of an iron blade. The blade cut through fur and flesh, and her blood fountained over Jack, "a blood that he has been begging for from the very beginning." Jack slipped on the toilet water and fell flat on his back ("Ah, my sciatica"), his head coming to rest facing hers.

> **Sila:** "Cover me in cheese."

Then came heavy footsteps. The half-dressed bartender emerged with a pot of fondue and poured the golden liquid over her, encasing her as she had wished. He told Jack the tavern was closing. Jack's only question was "Do you have any parchment paper at all?" The door opened, and the Grandmaster stood there holding a roll of it. "Oh, Pumpernickel Jack. Congratulations. For victory is yours!" She led him out to a standing ovation. When asked for words of wisdom, he burped, and the crowd erupted for the greatest burp ever heard in a finale speech. She raised his hand and soared him into the sky. His pants, fresh from the bathroom, could not stay up, "leaving a full moon for our acolytes."

## Dried Blood

Chuck the Cheese returned to speak over his friend, buried in filth and golden cheese. He called it "kind of living the dream," since "you found the cheese in the end." He also revealed that he had starved to death stuck in his hide-and-seek spot, missing his fifteen children, inside a cheap, non-biodegradable coffin. The Grandmaster promised Chuck a permanent place in the graveyard, with a new plastic coffin. Sila's final words were that "a Twinkie is what they called me in high school," adding, "I'm not a rat of many words these days, you know?" Jack left the Acolytes with this advice: before you sit down in the cubicle, double-check that there's still paper there.
