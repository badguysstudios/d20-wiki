## What happens
### Opening — the Arbiter looks for the Grandmaster
1. **0:00:35** The show is back in the Dread Fortress "after a extended break." **The Arbiter walks the halls in his Italian leather boots** ("leathery kisses"). His route:
   - He passes **the chinchilla room, where the chinchillas are locked in a training simulator, "X-Men style," fighting each other.** "Don't worry, they get a break. They are unionized."
   - **Thea is nowhere to be found.**
   - He peeks into **the koi pond room** and **the brewery**. Nobody is there.
2. **0:01:37** He hits **the intercom button on the wall**: "Grandmaster. Grandmaster." Her voice crackles back: "What do you want?… In the throne room, where else would I be?" Her voice echoes down the stonework. He magics himself into the throne room.
3. **0:02:21 — Spanish lessons.** The Grandmaster is sprawled across her chair with a notepad and pen. She practises: "I kill. Yo mató. He kills. El matá. She kills. Ella matá… Nosotros matamos."
   - She explains: "We should be going international."
   - Her reason for learning languages: "That way they know all the insults I'm hurling at them… It's not an accidental death."
   - The Arbiter agrees it makes sense. When he kills somebody and wants it to sting, "I do do a lot of monologuing."
   - She wants **"to stab, blood, murder"** translated next and scolds him for not writing them down. He finds a pen.
4. **0:03:45 — Thea's new job.** The Grandmaster asks whether he brought someone to murder. He reminds her: "**That's Thea's job now. I've delegated as management.**"
   - The GM had assumed they were only pretending, because "**Thea is a potato of a brain.**"
   - The Arbiter points out that Thea "made that video last time."
5. **0:04:10 — Claude.** The GM says Thea's video "talks poorly about Claude."
   - The Arbiter asks what Thea's deal with Claude is. **The GM didn't know Claude worked there until the video.**
   - "Does she think Claude is a withered [wizard?]" The GM: "He's just the janitor. He's not a wizard. He has no magical powers as I'm aware of. **Have we had him take the magic test?**"
   - The Arbiter: "Why would we? He's a janitor." The GM adds it to the follow-up list "after the deathmatch."
   - She admits: "They told them champions, right? They're less likely to say yes if we just call them murderers."
6. **0:05:09 — The champions arrive.** "Let me see if Thea actually did what she said she was going to do." The Arbiter claps, and the sound waves break through the portal into the throne room.
7. **0:05:44 — Big Business enters.**
   - **Plumes of steam billow from his base.** He stands somewhere between 6'7" and 7'9"; nobody can tell through the steam, "but he big and you know he mean business."
   - He gets up from **his chair in the lobby** and walks in: "I've been summoned."
   - "**Big is my business, and business is big.** Who do you want me to fight?"
8. **0:06:38 — Bog Boy enters.** He rises out of reeds in the corner.
   - **He is a gnarled old turtle with a spiked shell** that is spray-painted and filled with refuse, fishing lines and fishing net.
   - He wears **an apron made from an old band shirt**. His clothes drip with moss and bugs fly around him.
   - He carries **a tin bucket**: "It's me, **Bog Boy, Freak of the Creek, and no one touches Clementine.**" A big catfish splashes out of the bucket.
   - "**I'm here for the swamp and the swamp is here for me.**" He points a curled digit at Big Business: "You know what you did."
9. **0:07:54** Big Business: "I've been meaning to take him out. You know what they say, **one bog down, one more shopping mall up.**"
   - The GM: "That's not what they say, actually." She polls the room on whether anyone would wear it on a shirt.
   - She warns: "Just because it's the tallest doesn't mean that you're winning this." The Arbiter: "Sometimes the tallest does winning."
10. **0:08:40** The GM wants the fight, not "this bog business… this mall business." She adds: "Your words mean nothing here. Though maybe Clementine's would if she talked."
    - Bog Boy: "**Clementine, don't speak.** Don't speak, but you do only speak to me." He holds her up: "She beautiful, ain't she?"
    - The GM: "Disgusting… I'm going to have to vote with big business on that."
    - She asks the Arbiter to leave "before Freak of the Creek stinks up the rest of my throne room."
11. **0:09:33 — The arena.** "Let's go somewhere cold… Maybe we can mitigate some of this stench."
    - The Arbiter rips open a portal onto **a snowy mountaintop**.
    - "Many dead bodies have died here. I guess they were live bodies when they died." **Their bones, armor, swords and other objects lie strewn through the snow.**
12. **0:10:17 — The GM's charge.** She pushes the Arbiter aside to stand front and center:
    - "This will be one of yours graveyard… no more than one of you is making it out alive and that's a hope at best."
    - "The two of you have a grudge and it is going to be a **death grudge**."
13. **0:10:59 — The stands.** She flies up into the clouds. They part to reveal **a ring of Acolytes holding blue slushies**, chanting "Big business!" and "Bog boy!" (the second chant "has a very different rhythm").
    - "Feed them blood and bone tonight, and we shall feast well."
    - She takes **a chair fit for royalty** among them.

### Round 1
14. **0:11:51** Initiative is a flat d20. Big Business: "I have a D20 with a bunch of sides on it." **Bog Boy 19, Big Business 7** ("Working the crowd too much").
15. **0:12:29 — Bog Boy's claws.**
    - He picks up a skeleton bone and **sharpens his beak on it** (he's "kind of a snapping turtle"): "That's what I'm going to do to you, big business."
    - He **sets Clementine down gently in the snow and lights a little fire.**
    - "Big Business, I'm going to poke some holes in you, sink you to the ground."
    - The claws hit. He aims for **the backhand of one of Big Business's shovel ("scoop") hands, the one that smacked him "way back when, when he was just a little turtle in the woods."** "This one's a little bit personal."
    - Signs in the stands read **"Save the turtles!"** Bog Boy adds: "And the frogs. Save the frogs, too."
16. **0:14:26** Big Business tries to fling the turtle off: "**You haven't grown up a day.**"
    - Bog Boy rights himself in the air and comes down with **Flurry of Blows**.
    - The GM: "gathering all that turtle power… None of us are allowed to take a breath of oxygen" while he finds the button.
    - **The flurry misses: a clear-coat shield drops over Big Business and the blows slam into it.** The GM: "Embarrassing."
17. **0:16:29 — 720p.** The GM tells the champions to drop their cameras to 720p: "you all are just too much for 1080… The children, they may gouge their eyes out. It's just so violent."
    - Bog Boy: "It's just too beautiful to be seen."
    - "**Stuart, refresh.**" The Arbiter: "Sorry, Stuart had to do that." The GM: "Thanks, Stuart. Freaking Stuart."
    - She mixes up the names ("Big bog… I can merge the two of you").
    - **Bog Boy lands in the snow and questions his decisions.** The Arbiter: "What did I do?"
18. **0:17:50 — Big Business's bulldozer.**
    - The GM: "Oh, Great Warforged, you've taken a hit."
    - **His core charges bright green. His "odor cannons" launch up and shower the audience with gold coins.**
    - He puts his hands together and **turns himself into a giant bulldozer**. The GM: "Is Big Business a Transformer?" Big Business: "Transformers, the most capitalism-based robots ever."
    - The GM: "Big Business has a lot of buttons."
    - **The maul charge hits (26).**
19. **0:19:30 — The redirect.**
    - **Bog Boy uses Deflect Attacks to reduce the damage to nothing, then redirects it.** The GM praises his politeness.
    - Bog Boy: "This one though, I bite onto one of your shovels and I turn one of your arms around so it kind of turns back into you."
    - **Big Business rolls a natural 1 on the save. He was distracted by Clementine jumping up in her bucket**, "those beautiful scales, those whiskers coming off the cold sunlight."
    - **Bog Boy drives the shovel into Big Business's own gut**, "like if you're shoveling snow and you get stuck on the driveway." It deals 18.
    - Bog Boy: "Big business, baby. I mean, I'm Bog Boy."
    - The GM: "Save the turtles!" The Arbiter: "You attacked him, and you ended up taking 18 points of damage?… **I have some bets I need to go change real quick.**"
20. **0:22:24** Big Business: "Oh, you got me right in the business." The GM urges him on: "Yell a loud insult so an avalanche falls on his head. I don't care."
21. **0:23:33 — The crowd.** The Acolytes chant "Big business" despite the fumble and **gift him a heroic surge** ("You can go plus ultra one moment").
    - **Slushies fly everywhere.** The Acolytes pour them over themselves.
22. **0:24:32** Big Business: "That's what you get for paying them off." He turns slightly to the right and goes full steam ahead: "**We're going to pave you into a parking lot.**"
    - **The charge misses (13).** The GM: "I've never been so hyped up and then so disappointed."
    - The Arbiter: "At least you didn't damage yourself this time."
    - The GM: "Big business, you are building no mauls this time."
23. **0:26:01 — Warforged blood.** The GM asks whether a Warforged has blood. "Oh, you just bleed money?" The Arbiter: "It's all money in here." Big Business: "**I'm like a piñata!**"
24. **0:26:32 — The dispute.**
    - The GM: "Then you can build a whole fence around your swamp. Wait, are you Shrek?"
    - Bog Boy: "He's no longer in the swamp. You know, he moved far, far away."
    - Big Business: "**I'm going to get my fish wife back.**" Bog Boy: "Big business, you know she left you." **Clementine's bucket slowly starts to freeze over.**
    - Big Business: "**That's my land. I'm going to build a mall on it, and I'm going to get my fish wife back.**"
    - Bog Boy: "**I have squatter's rights.** Your fish wife left you. This is my swamp now."
    - The Arbiter asks whether the fish wife is a mermaid. **No. Clementine is "just a catfish. Bottom feeder."** The Arbiter was going to ask "which half."

### Chaos event — the mechanical bull (0:28:28)

    - **The air "smells of PBR and cigarettes."** Bog Boy: "Hell yeah."
    - **A giant mechanical bull bursts up under both champions.** "He who falls first shall take damage… who can yee-haw for the longest."

    - "**He used to hang out with this man named Mandroid. Mandroid knew how to connect the machines, various vending machines and the like.**"
    - The Arbiter: "I think I'm just not going to think too hard about it." The GM: "Theater of the mind, the curtains close."
28. **0:31:39** **Bog Boy gets a heroic surge** as the crowd chants his name after he's thrown off. He takes 10 from the fall, doubled for the nat 1.

### Round 2

30. **0:32:40** Bog Boy: "**Chico del Panto, which is Bog Boy in Spanish**" (he admits he Googled it).
31. **0:32:56 — Clementine goes to the Grandmaster.** Bog Boy asks her to look after Clementine: "She is too distracting to me." **The GM throws down a reel, hooks the pail and lifts Clementine up to sit beside her.**
32. **0:33:29 — The oil.** Bog Boy: "**Remember that time when you took all that oil and you poured it all through the swamp. The only thing that it did is it made me slicker.**"
    - **He slides across the snow in a zigzag, withdraws into his shell, and the shell glows a radioactive color.**
    - He leaps and casts **Elemental Burst**.
    - The GM: "I can't believe you sludged the swamp, big business."
    - Big Business: "**I was trying to grow an oil tree.**… You plant the oil, then you come back and you got an oil tree."
    - The Arbiter: "You're not a botanist." He agrees that it "makes perfect sense." The GM: "Not make sense at all."
    - **Big Business fails the save and takes 17 lightning damage.** Bog Boy: "I am a frog. I mean a toydle [Tortle]."
33. **0:36:19 — Bog Boy hides.**
    - He uses Patient Defense to disengage. **Lightning-blasted snow goes up, and when it settles, a turtle leg is seen far away, just straight running.** "Oh boy."
    - Stealth 20 against Big Business's insight. **Little scanners come on Big Business's head**, but he only gets a 9.
    - **Bog Boy tucks into a snow-covered rock outcrop "like Frodo and Sam" by the gates**, and his shell is covered in snow.
    - Big Business: "Bog boy loves Lord of the Rings." Bog Boy: "Big fan. Love reading them books."
34. **0:38:57 — A slushy for Big Business.** An Acolyte throws a slushy across the arena. **His chest receptacle opens and catches it.** It pours over his coins and wires and "lubricates" him, setting up a critical hit.
35. **0:40:01 — The earthquake.**
    - Big Business: "You got a little slippery compared to last time, bog boy, but you ain't still ready for the big league."
    - **He uses Lay on Hands. Steam, cash and coins pop out of his exhaust pipes, and his leg rolls "all sevens"** ("Are we in Las Vegas?… Ding ding ding").
    - "If I know one thing about the bog boy is that he ain't never go too far."
    - **He "piston power punches" the earth and sends an earthquake toward the hidden turtle.** The GM: "Triple P."
    - He rolls with disadvantage but still hits (26) for a critical 25, plus a **smite** (his first time playing a paladin): "Standard mortgage. Smile."
    - The description: **Big Business jumps on cash-fueled legs and smashes down with both scoops.** "**That's what you get for not having a decent portfolio, son.**"
    - **A rock falls from the mountain onto Bog Boy's shell.** He rolls out: "Oh, Lord."
    - The international gag continues: "dose D8," "dos, the Spanish," "for the Italian."
36. **0:44:59** The Acolytes wince for Bog Boy and **toss him a blue slushy too**, setting up his own crit.

### Round 3
37. **0:45:27 — The real Freak of the Creek.**

    - Anime-style, he wipes the blood away: "**Now you just unlocked the real freak of the creek.**"
38. **0:46:31** The GM announces "round number trois." A language muddle follows: tres, "trace," "dos. No, wait." The GM: "I think that's German for three." Bog Boy: "**Ramstein. That was my band shirt.**"
39. **0:47:04 — The grapple.**
    - **Bog Boy's grapple strike auto-crits. Big Business fails the save and stays grappled** (his escape roll is a 9). The GM: "Everyone point and laugh."
    - Big Business: "He's just so damn slippery. Get off me."
    - **Bog Boy clings to his chest "like a little toddler backpack" and whispers: "You know when I win all this… you're gonna lose and everyone's gonna get what I want."**
40. **0:49:13 — The shield of cash.** Big Business spends his heroic surge to equip his shield. The GM: "I want a fucking cool way in which you reveal this awesome shield."
    - "**You forget one thing, bog boy, a big business always ready for a tussle.**"
    - **His turbines spin up and hordes of cash spray out, hitting Bog Boy in the face.**
    - "Business is booming." **A stock ticker inside him constantly prints money.**
41. **0:50:30 — The flurry.** The grapple crit lands for 19. Then a **heightened flurry**:
    - The first blow sails wide: money is flying and he can't see. The Arbiter: "Just punching George Washington in the face."
    - The second lands with a clang (12): "I got you."
    - The third lands too, "like punching through a waterfall."

    - The Arbiter: "Grandmaster, what the fuck does that mean?… **Are we sure that he's not Thea?**"
    - The GM: "He probably has just drank the oil that big business has flooded his swamp with… He's maybe lost his mind."
    - The name chant: "Big, big, big bog boy businessman." The GM: "I still have my money on big bucks. Sorry, big business." The Arbiter: "He's a turtle. He thinks he's a frog, so us forgetting his name is not that big a deal."
43. **0:55:33 — Bless.**
    - Big Business shuts the turbines down and unequips the shield. His core glows higher with "cha-ching, cha-ching": "**Oh baby, we just hit new record high profits.**"
    - He casts **Bless** on himself ("everyone got laid off because of that") and on two Acolytes:
      - **B-Town**, whom he spotted at the start.
      - **Cookie Monster Steve**, who has "a really big cookie": "Are we going to get in the cookie business, baby Steve?"
    - The Acolytes go crazy. **Scribbs screams "I don't want to be a frog."**
    - **The Acolytes remove Bog Boy's fatigue just before it would have made him take critical damage.**
    - The GM welcomes **Raiders**: "Will we become a Maulstrip?… Will we become frogs because a turtle says so?"
44. **0:58:09 — The campaign slogan.**
    - Big Business: "**We've got to stop you from turning people into frogs. And in exchange, we're going to build a shopping mall.**"

    - Big Business: "You've got to go in for nature or completely destroy nature." The Arbiter: "There's no in between."

    - Someone says "Well, now I'm 85" (labelled Bec; speaker uncertain).
    - Bog Boy starts a story: "When I was a little boy playing by the swamp…" A little chair comes up for it.
    - The GM cuts him off. She makes eye contact with "poor Clementine, the fish wife": "How? Why? I can't… **I need to go find Doug the Pug.**"
46. **0:59:23 — Halftime.** Big Business has 50 HP and Bog Boy has 32.
