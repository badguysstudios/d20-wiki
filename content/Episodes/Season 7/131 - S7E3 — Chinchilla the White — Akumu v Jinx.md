## What happens
### Opening: Stuart's closet
1. **0:00:53** The Arbiter's **Italian leather boots** clack through the deep halls of the Dread Fortress. He reaches **a small, humid, hot closet full of screens** and stands over a small figure: **Stuart**. "Stuart, we need to talk. The last couple of deathmatches. Listen, you've dropped the ball." 2. **0:01:48** The Grandmaster's voice bellows down the hallways of **the basement dungeon where the Arbiter has hidden Stuart**. Her wings flare and her heels clack as she searches for him. "Where are you?"
3. **0:02:13** The Arbiter claims he's just "working a couple things out" with Stuart. The GM says the issues are "on your plate," and the Arbiter quickly switches to teamwork and "any resource that he needs." The GM says she'll be there for the extra-long hours.
