## What happens
### Opening — the Grandmaster's private bathroom
1. **0:00:43** Bec narrates the Acolytes' arrival at **the fortress itself**: "not the hut, not the cabin." They come deep underground, through the wide-open skull, into empty hallways. The Grandmaster is nowhere to be found. **The clacking of the Arbiter's "disgusting Italian shoes"** echoes as he opens door after door. "Grandmaster, it's Deathmatch Day. I need you." Bec scolds him for not screaming as cued.
2. **0:01:41** A yelp comes from behind a heavy wooden door. The Grandmaster: "**I'm pooping!**" Arbiter: "Oh, yeah, no, I smell that now." She asks if he brought toilet paper. **She sent Thea for toilet paper 30 minutes ago**: "It's an emergency." The Arbiter says that's on her, given how often she scolds him for sending Thea on errands. **He just saw Thea running around the lava fields**, so she won't be back soon.
3. **0:02:16** The GM explains it is "**meant to be a private bathroom**". Thea doesn't pay attention to that. She begs him to use his "fancy little hands" and magic, or she'll be late to the Deathmatch.
4. **0:02:44 — The toilet paper.** The Arbiter **walks a roll over and sets it on the floor under the stall door, just far enough that she has to lean awkwardly off the toilet to reach it**, "just to make you uncomfortable." "The smell is very strong. This is as close as I'm getting." She tips forward on her toes, the seat cracks, and her fingertips miss. "Come on!" **He kicks it toward her.**
5. **0:03:27 — Champions in the bathroom.** The Arbiter claps, rips a portal open in the bathroom, and introduces **Livid Lady Lin and Gandor Thalion**. "Welcome to the Grandmaster's private bathroom."
6. **0:04:04 — Lin's introduction.** Livid Lady Lin is **a five-foot "wizard machine", very hench under blue and black robes**. Her spellbook looks small on her because she's so buff, and **she uses it as a weight**. **She sparks a little flame on her thumb, like a match, to air out the bathroom.**
7. **0:04:52 — Gandor's introduction.** "My name is **Gandor Thalion Umbrae**, and you will address me as such using the entire thing every time." "You have three names?" "**I have many names. These are the three that are the most expedient for the likes of you.**" Arbiter: "What, are you planning to shoot a president or something?" Gandor: he has not yet "gazed into the orb of time" to see if that is his fate, "but it is not outside the realm of possibility." Arbiter: "I'm not going to remember that."
8. **0:05:32** An eye peeks through the stall gap: "Just one more minute… Shake hands or something."
9. **0:05:49 — Old rivals.** Gandor: "**We know each other too, too well.**" Lin: "I would say nice to see you again, Gandalf, but it ain't." Gandor: "You're looking as pugnacious as ever, Liv." "Why, thank you." "That was not a compliment." Lin: "**I don't use my book learnings for learning words. I use it for learning spells to kick your ass.**"
10. **0:06:19** The toilet flushes and the door pops open. The GM washes her hands, nods to Lin ("Nice to meet you") and to Gandor. "**There's no smoking in the bathroom.**" Gandor: "What kind of establishment is this?" "A private one." **She ate 12 Brussels sprouts today "and everybody's gonna pay for it."** She asks for fresh air.
11. **0:07:06 — The arena.** **The Arbiter's portal opens directly on the Grandmaster's toilet.** They jump through to **a giant snowy mountain region**. Millions of Acolytes fill the grandstands above, throwing **blue slushies** and ripping their shirts off, "nipples hard in the cold weather." **They chant the champions' names, except Gandor's**: it's too long, too many names. Gandor: "**I will remember this, all of you.**"
12. **0:08:02** The Arbiter flares his wings: "Acolytes, are you ready for some blood?… Sorry, Grandmaster, not for you." **The GM comes through last, closing the portal and the toilet seat behind her.**
13. **0:08:11 — The GM's speech.** They are on this peak "not for the view" but for spilled blood, broken bone and torn flesh. "Show us who you really are in every hit, every spell. Give us a story so that when your bones do lay to rest under this snowy peak, it is your name that will be remembered… that will sprawl down this mountainside like an avalanche for all of eternity." She flies up among the Acolytes. "Do not disappoint me."
14. **0:09:31 — Initiative.** Gandor rolls 19 and Lin rolls 8. Gandor: "**I shall be undefeated.**" The GM checks that everyone agrees 19 is higher than 8.
15. **0:10:00** **The Arbiter shoves Doug, asleep in his chair, out of his seat.** "The dog was here first. Fucking Doug."

### Round 1
16. **0:10:23** Gandor levels his staff at Liv: "**Today, we settle this once and for all. Have at thee!**" A crystalline orb congeals between his whirling hands. "**Orb of Infinite Ponder!**" (Confounding Shadows). Lin fails the Wisdom save. Table: "Were they children's books?" "I don't read books for learning… I read them for hurting." **Her gaze is absorbed by the levitating orb, so she can't see him. He steps back into the mountaintop mists.** GM: "All right, John Cena." "Love a game of hide and go seek."
17. **0:12:44 — Biological warfare.** Lin: "You know what I do to things that I can't see?… **I use biological warfare.**" Arbiter: "Breaking the Geneva Convention." **Popeye-style, she squeezes a can of baked beans, swallows them down and casts Gust of Wind ("Baked Beans")**, a horrific wind in every direction. Bec: "Be one with the wind!… This is very eco-friendly." Lin: "It's a good thing we're in the toilet." A stuck template on screen becomes, per the GM, **the expelled wind "solidified into a giant brick"**, a "60 foot long shit brick."
18. **0:15:06** Gandor fails the Strength save and is **blown 15 feet away**. Moving toward her costs double for one minute, "while I concentrate on farting very, very loudly." **The GM adds a d4 of noxious poison damage "because it was the beans"**: it rolls a 4, "but it burns the nostrils." Patrick: in the windy haze, **a little light tries to go up on Gandor's pipe.**
19. **0:16:44 — Thea.** A **small pink impish pig-like creature** off to the side vacuums up the scent: "**I'm going to turn this into a candle!**" GM: "This is the strangest wizard versus wizard duel I have ever seen." Arbiter: "I've seen stranger, but that wasn't [was in?] Tijuana." GM: "Buckle in… this is gonna beat Tijuana." **The Arbiter literally pulls a seatbelt out of his chair.** "This is not a rollercoaster ride."

### Round 2
20. **0:17:40** "Dos en Spanish!" The GM says "Gandor Thalion, hombre!" and it echoes across the mountain. Gandor: "Thank you for getting it right. Liv, what is wrong with you?" "Everything." "**Foul creature, give me your blood!**"
21. **0:18:15 — "Give Me Your Blood"** (Blood Bolt, with advantage from being unseen). It hits. **The orb shatters into "a trillion pieces", raking her skin and cutting her veins. Gandor grabs her blood out of midair**, then realizes he has this woman's blood all over his hand: "Why is it thick?… Do you need a doctor?" He gains temporary hit points.
22. **0:19:44** Lin: "Listen, I don't know if you know much about women, but once a month…" She thanks him for helping her "pass through things very quickly": "Can I sign up next?" Table: a women's health clinic. Arbiter: "**He moonlights as a gynecologist.**" Gandor goes pale and sallow. "**Liv, you are undeserving of the tower. You are a foul creature and you will lose.**" "That's not a nice thing to say to a lady." "A lady, of course."
23. **0:20:41 — "You Wouldn't Hit a Woman, Would You?"** (Mage Armor). After a sheet hunt, Drew adds the spell to her list. Lin asks "Patrick, did you remove my…" and Patrick answers "You can't prove I touched any stuff." **To Gandor, the buff, gruff champion is replaced by a mirage of a frail, petite, cute person**, and her voice comes out tiny: "You wouldn't hit a woman, would you?" Patrick: "Genuinely speechless. Confused. Perhaps aroused."

It's a herd of giant fluffy white chinchillas.** The GM: "in our world, we call them chinchillas" (heard as "chinkillas"). DC 16 Dex. **Both are trampled by hundreds of tiny paws.** "Get him off!"

### The tower

Arbiter: is this "some sort of weird sexual euphemism?" Gandor: "**It's a literal wizard's tower and I will have it.**"

"**If you're not in the tower business as a wizard, then you're doing something wrong.**" GM: "Does it have a pool, though?" Lin: "One of the West Wings has a pool." Gandor: "**It has reflecting pools, it has pondering pools, it has summoning pools.**"
31. **0:27:36** Lin: "Oh, shut up with your pondering pools." Gandor: "I will ponder what I like, woman." Lin: "**Didn't your mom ever tell you if you ponder too much, you'll go blind?**"
32. **0:27:51 — "Shut the Fuck Up"** (Zip It), cast as a reaction to her vocalizing. **Lin's mouth fuses shut, "smooth like a Ken doll."** The DMs rule it lasts at least two turns.
33. **0:29:08** Mouth fused, **Lin casts Mold Earth to write "fuck you" in the snow** (inferred from "So right in the snow, in the ground, fuck you"). Arbiter: "Good recap." GM: most spells need a verbal component. Arbiter: "Listen, I could do it, but that's me."

### Round 3
34. **0:29:49** GM: "**You're the only one talking, so say a lot!**" Gandor: "I plan on it." Patrick: finally she can't speak over him, "you best believe I'm about to pontificate." Arbiter: "He casts this only because he wants the monologue."

You have no right to inherit its tomes. You are not worthy of its orbs and its many pools.** Have at thee!" **He swings his quarterstaff ("just vengeance") and misses.**

Lin: no. **It's like the Mouth of Sauron from the Lord of the Rings extended edition.** Arbiter compares it to "that guy from Hellboy… Clockwork guy." **She spits a glob of blood: "Gandor, we need to talk."** GM: "Your teeth have got to be so dry not having lips."

Gandor: "**I'M NOT THE COMPUTER GUY, MAN!… I AIN'T NO ORBS!**" GM: "That's a different wizard, an IT wizard." Arbiter: "He only knows orbs and orb related subject matter." Lin: "Orbs and orb related activity. Orb iOS." GM: "Hard to get funding in those areas of research."

### Round 4
40. **0:36:41 — "The Big Sad"** (Intrusive Despair). **Gandor grabs the wrist of her stabbing hand and pulls her in, though he's "weak as hell"** and she doesn't move at all. "**You are the most undeserving spellcaster I have ever encountered. And the fact that we are the two finalists for this lease is unacceptable. You will capitulate.**" She must make a Wisdom save (DC 17) before each of her next three actions or attacks. Lin has +0 Wisdom.
41. **0:38:38 — The Acolytes' slushies.** The Arbiter tells her **the Acolytes have given her five blue slushies, a +4 to everything** ("until they don't like you"). **She succeeds with 23.** Asked how she rejects the doubts: "She just doesn't hand them." Patrick: "There's just nothing going on up there." **Gandor gets so angry his orb explodes.** GM: if he can't get the tower, "**there's an empty space up here in Lynn's head… You can move in there.**" Lin: "If anyone wants to pay me rent, there's plenty of room."
42. **0:40:25 — "I think it's time we called your mom"** (Summon Draconic Spirit). Lin mimes a phone call: "Hello, Mrs. [Gandor?]… he's been a right fucking terror… he was swearing… he's smoking, and **I caught him pondering his orbs again**… I'll see you in five." The GM lets her describe the draconic spirit however she likes.
43. **0:41:21 — Gandor's mother.** **An old woman steps out of a portal in a pink fluffy bathrobe, curlers in her hair, a cigarette pinched between her lips.** "**I thought I told you to stop pondering them orbs!**" **She takes off a slipper and throws it** (the breath weapon). Gandor panics: "**This is an illusion! You cannot hit me without my consent!**" and casts Shield ("You Cannot Legally Hit Me Without My Consent"). It's a Dex save, so Shield doesn't help.
44. **0:42:57** **The slipper matches the bathrobe and has little floppy bunny ears.** In a Matrix move, Gandor leans back and it skims across his chest, so he takes half. He snaps back up face to face with his opponent and his mother.
45. **0:43:44 — Adrenaline vs fatigue.** The Arbiter explains that Lin's five adrenaline means maximum damage plus the roll, and Gandor has five fatigue on top. **The half-damage slipper deals 31.** "The Acolytes have chosen who they're cheering for tonight." Bec: "The effort to gift was equal, but in different directions." The Arbiter adds that Gandor also has five adrenaline, so his next hit crits.
46. **0:44:49 — Gandor's heroic surge.** The GM grants Gandor a **heroic surge** ("go plus ultra on one thing"). "The thing you don't want is to die without using it. And in that case, **I will revive you just to pummel you back into the earth again.**"
47. **0:45:45** Nobody knows what round it is. The GM proposes a breather. Arbiter: "I could use a bathroom break now that the smell is dissipated." GM: "Don't mention bathroom around me right now. It's still a sore subject."

## Who and what appears
### Characters
**[[Characters/Livid Lady Lin|Livid Lady Lin]]** (Emily Graymoor). Called Liv by Gandor.
- **A five-foot, very buff wizard** in blue and black robes. She lifts her hefty spellbook as a weight and lights a thumb flame like a match.
- Reads books "for hurting," not for learning words. Wisdom +0; "nothing going on up there."
- A long-time rival of Gandor. **They are the two finalists for the lease of a 100-floor wizard's tower.** She wanted its adventurers and gold.
- **Cut her own fused mouth open with a dagger, leaving her lipless like the Mouth of Sauron.**
- **Summoned Gandor's mother**, whom Gandor's dragon breath then killed.
- Won the duel and the lease. Planned to watch Gilmore Girls. **Killed by the Arbiter's Power Word Kill**; her dust was vacuumed up by Thea with a Gilmore Girls DVD box set.

**[[Characters/Gandor Thalion Umbrae|Gandor Thalion Umbrae]]** (Patrick Perini).
