The worms had been promised a show. Under the deal that ended Thea's revenge, the first Deathmatch of the new era had to be held at the [[The Ether Drifts|Ether Drifts]], in front of the worm delegation, and [[Thea]] had found both champions herself. One was **[[Zhora Bloodfang]]**, a six-and-a-half-foot half-orc barbarian who wanted to retire and run her dad's bakery. The other was **[[Bezil]]**, a tressym the size of a house cat who had come for a kraken. The Arbiter called him a house pet, and the Grandmaster took responsibility for him anyway. Over eight rounds Bezil set Zhora's mohawk on fire, clawed her butt, killed her with a flaming hairball and slashed a scar down her face. She came back held together with rainbow duct tape, gained a cowboy squirrel as an ally, and squeezed him until he stopped moving.

## The Story

### Guests from the Hollow

The Arbiter and [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] returned "for another bout of chaos and death and blood." The Grandmaster had forgotten what they did on Sunday nights because there was blood all over her notes. The Arbiter sympathized: his whole journal was covered in blood too. Thea came in to report that the champions were ready and the guests from the worms were expected. She had prepared her home and made a gift basket of gummy worms and bloody fingers from the last champions, but she could only find about five fingers. Somebody had been eating the extra body parts. When the Arbiter asked whether it was her, she said she wouldn't say no and wouldn't say yes, and dove behind the basket with her tail sticking out.

As part of the agreement, the first Deathmatch of the new era had to be held at the Ether Drifts. The Grandmaster warned that the champions had to be top notch or the worms would come back demanding more. The Arbiter suggested they could just destroy the entire worm civilization. That was Thea's family, the Grandmaster said, so no. And where would he find another assistant like her? "I'd make another one." She reminded him that no one else in the kingdom would ever work for him, and that his employee evaluation was in three months. Thea didn't want the champions to see the Dread Fortress first, because it looked really cool and they might think her home was less cool. So the Arbiter clapped open a portal straight to the Ether Drifts, where the two champions were waiting.

### A Cat and a Barbarian

Bezil was no bigger than a house cat: copper fur, iridescent golden eyes, ear tufts tinged the green of oxidized copper, and huge leathery bat wings on his back. Sitting up at his full height, he was about twelve inches tall. He yawned, and with a little thaumaturgy the yawn came out as a dragon's roar. The Grandmaster asked whether he was the pet of the champion who was coming. Bezil strutted over and waited. "This is when you're supposed to give me scritches." She dropped to her knees and scratched behind his ears. He began listing his ancestry ("My great, great, great, great…") and gave up. He had come, he explained, because they'd told him he could get a kraken to chase and eat. The Grandmaster had a whole lake of them and offered him his pick of color and size. When she called him tiny, he informed her that he was tall for a tressym, and stretched his ears for an extra two inches.

"Thea, sidebar," said the Arbiter. "Did you get a cat for this deathmatch? … You can't just bring random house pets into the arena." Thea pointed out that house pets can't talk. The Arbiter told the Grandmaster he'd had nothing to do with it: if it failed, it was on Thea.

Zhora was in the back: a huge half-orc woman about six and a half feet tall, with thick tattooed arms and locks pulled up into a ponytail. She'd seen so many battles, and she was ready to fucking party. Then she learned her opponent was "that adorable little one-foot monster." "You're saying I gotta hurt the little good boy?" The Grandmaster wanted him killed, not just hurt: spill his blood, crush his skull in her hand, "you know, the classics." The Arbiter suggested stomping on him or setting him on fire. "A lot of ways to skin a cat," Zhora said, sweating. Bezil reassured her: it was her or the kraken, so she had to go. Then he made biscuits on the ground.

The Grandmaster pulled Zhora aside and offered power, gold and connections, and also the Arbiter's entire collection of Italian leather shoes. What Zhora really wanted was to restart her dad's bakery, find a nice wife, have a family and retire. And to make it complete: "Him on my mantle!"

The Grandmaster opened her wings, and the Acolytes rose above the Ether Drifts, ethereal in bliss, over shifting sands, a giant purple planet and a setting sun. She asked the champions for more than blood and gore: "a story that will last longer than any fight." The Arbiter announced that the Deathmatch was brought to them by the worm council and the delegation of worms, "including Old Todd over there. Looking at you, Todd," and screamed at [[Stuart]] about the round cards.

### Two Natural Ones

Zhora went first. She unslung a huge battleaxe and asked whether Bezil counted as an animal, since she could talk to animals and might intimidate him. He was "animal adjacent at the very least," the Arbiter decided. Bezil disagreed with the utmost rage: "I am going to chew on your bones." Zhora planted her axe, dropped into a deep squat, made the meanest face she had, and called him out. She rolled a natural 1. Bezil walked up slowly, sat down a millimeter from her nose, curled his tail around his feet and said, "I would like to scratch you." The Grandmaster ruled that from then on, handling Bezil as an animal would be contested. The Acolytes who had brought signs for Zhora lowered them about two inches.

Zhora stepped back and dodged. Bezil swiped at her and rolled a natural 1 too. Zhora took the chance to make him an offer: they could both walk out right now. She had a warm soft bed for him, and milk (almond, rice, goat, lactose-free), feline greenies and kraken jerky. Bezil wanted a full live kraken. The Arbiter reminded them both that the contract said someone had to die, and the Grandmaster sweetened the pot with a chain of bakeries or two krakens. Kraken symbols spun into place in Bezil's eyes like a slot machine. He flew up to about seven feet, just high enough to look down on her. Thea, watching her two finds open with a pair of ones, told them, "Don't fucking embarrass me and roll another nat one or I'll come out there and kill you myself." When the Arbiter threatened to take away her pope hat, she reminded him she had earned it "with my own efforts and a little bit of a sewing machine."

### The Meow

Zhora took the two-handed axe in one hand, spun it and swung with all her weight behind it. It was going to hit. In the instant before it landed, Bezil looked at her with sheer shock and let out the cutest little meow. It was Silvery Barbs. "This isn't how we play. This is not nice, Mr. Bezil." Her reroll only grazed him, and he stood on the axe head and bounced off it. She punched him and tried to grab him by the scruff of the neck, which she'd heard neutralizes his kind. "Why do you all try to neuter me?" he asked, slipping free. "I just want to love you."

Bezil answered with a cone of fire that singed the side of her mohawk ("Oi, oi, oi, watch it. I look good") and smelled strongly of sardines. Then, because she had tried to grab him and he does not like being grabbed, he dove past her swing with the advantage the meow had given him and rolled a natural 20. He asked where Zhora looked softest. "A boob or a butt, probably." He clawed straight into her left butt cheek. "Oh my days! You're not playing fair. I appreciate that, but also, bad kitty!" [[Baptiste]] stood up in the crowd and yelled "That's right, stab her," the Acolytes ripped their clothes off and spilled their blue slushies, and in the front row the worm delegation writhed in excitement.

The Acolytes demanded a chaotic event, and the Grandmaster released **the dehydration bug**. Every drop of moisture began to leave the champions' bodies. Zhora looked around for water, found none, and took the damage. Bezil turned to the crowd, flapped weakly as if the thin air hurt him, and stared at whoever had the best-looking goblet of water. Commodore Scrib threw him a slushie and Gator threw another, and Bezil's next hit was guaranteed to be critical.

### Hairball

Seeing the crowd go wild for a cat, Zhora raged. Her tusks glinted, her muscles rippled, and her ass clenched so mightily that the bleeding stopped. The Grandmaster said she had never heard a rage described as a butt clench before, and that it was perfect. Zhora's reckless swing went for the top of his wing. Bezil sacrificed a leg to save it, throwing up one little paw, and in slow motion Zhora began sobbing as the blow landed. "Are you all right? Are you okay? I'm not supposed to give a shit. … Does anyone have a band-aid?" There are no band-aids in the arena. Her second swing ("For your Dunkin' franchise. I'm sorry") drew a second Silvery Barbs, this time from nothing more than a pitiful look. "No more butterfly kisses!" It hit anyway. She promised his corpse would go on her mantle, "stuffed with only the finest hay," and asked him to forgive her. He said never.

Bezil limped. Then the clouds parted, which was impressive since they were in outer space, and an old copper dragon, one of his many-times-great ancestors, looked down at him: "Get your shit together. Make them a scratching post." Bezil popped his paw back into place. A bead of fire rose up through his chest, and with the unmistakable hack of a cat bringing up a furball, he spat a flaming hairball of Sorcerous Burst at her. Asked whether cats keep their magic in their bellies, he said, "They keep it in their butts." The eights kept exploding. It came to 85 damage, and Zhora, a nature barbarian, had no resistance to fire.

The Acolytes gave her a heroic surge. Zhora caught the hairball in her white-hot rage and tried to Hadouken it back. It didn't work. The blast carried her backwards into a cart of drinks that was left smoldering around her body. Bezil, gleaming with a dragon's pride, forgot she existed and turned to the hosts: "So do I get my kraken now?" The drink slipped out of the Grandmaster's hand, and the show went to a break to "figure this whole thing out."

### Duct Tape

After the break, Thea came rolling in on dozens of rolls of duct tape, in camouflage, black, blue, orange and rainbow, and began piecing Zhora back together. Did the arms go on the top half or the right half? Which butt cheek was which? Did Zhora want her head facing forwards or backwards? (Forwards, please.) Zhora felt a little creaky but workable, and Thea gave her the rest of the roll in case she needed it later, then left to go get toast. The Grandmaster explained to Bezil that his contract also required the fight to last a certain amount of time, and offered him double or nothing: four krakens if he could kill Zhora again. "Jackpot" flashed over his head, and they shook hand to paw. Zhora returned at half strength and, by the Grandmaster's ruling, still raging, since rage is an emotional state and would carry on even if she were a ghost.

### Jameson

Zhora threw a handaxe that clipped his wing ("That's a warning shot, babe") and brought the greataxe down with a battle cry that shook the ground he wasn't standing on. Bezil raised a fiery Shield that looked, at a glance, like a flaming ball of yarn. She complained that he had an unfair advantage because he was cute. "To everyone," he said. Except the squirrels in his neighborhood, who hated him. He pointed his tail at the railing, where a squirrel sat with a silver toothpick and a little cowboy hat. That was **[[Jameson]]**, "like the liquor." "He looks like a right bastard," said Zhora. The enemy of her enemy was her friend. She invited Jameson onto her shoulder, and he drew two revolvers, spun them on his fingers and fired them into the air. The Grandmaster congratulated her on her new pact. Bezil: "I did this to myself."

Out of pure spite, Bezil's shadow grew into a cat ten times his size, just as adorable, and pounced on her, a Blindness/Deafness spell sharpened with a sorcery point. Zhora opened her arms ("Baby!") and was smashed into the ground. "It's okay, sometimes love hurts, everyone." While she was blind, Bezil took the handaxe she'd thrown and sat on it. "I have claimed this axe as mine. I sat on it, so it's mine."

Blind and deaf, Zhora stomped a crater into the asteroid like an earthbender, ripped out a four-foot hunk of rock and hurled it where he had been. Natural 1. She threw another; it missed. Clawing at the shadow cat, she found it warm, with the comfort of being chosen by a cat, and almost gave in: "Oh no, I just want to live in here. Focus up! We got a bakery to find!" She rolled a natural 20 on the save and tore it off her face. The Arbiter ruled she could never be blinded or deafened again. "You will not keep making a fool of me."

Bezil flung her own handaxe back at her with Catapult, then let it slow as it reached her so she could snatch it from the air like a warrior princess. She did, and then smashed it on the ground. The Grandmaster told Bezil everything was falling apart; did he want the four krakens or not? He spent his own heroic surge trying to cast two spells at once. As he failed, the flame inside him took the shape of a second, ever-larger Bezil, his draconic spirit, rising out of him. Then came revolver fire from the crowd. Jameson, now wearing a big cowboy mustache as bushy as his tail, shot the spirit to pieces. "That's for what you did to my begonias, you son of a bitch." "Fuck your begonias! They were never in season and they were ugly too." Jameson started rolling a cigarette. Zhora: "Brother, I never knew I needed you."

Bezil retreated twenty feet up, landed eye to eye with the Arbiter and told him, "You must be a dog person." The Arbiter, looking at [[Doug the Pug|Doug]], said he very much was not.

### The Scar and the Hug

Zhora climbed the stands after him with the reflexes of a panther and only her handaxe. She had seven adrenaline and he had seven fatigue, so a hit would be devastating. Bezil folded himself in his wings, a gift from his metallic-dragon blood, and both her swings missed. She overreached and nearly fell. "Fuck you, little cat!" "Fuck your bakery!" "Fuck your krakens!" Goaded by the Grandmaster ("Is this how you're going to handle a kraken? By flying away?"), Bezil landed nose to nose with her, yawned, and breathed a Cone of Cold into her face. She survived with frost in her eyelashes, blue lips under her smeared lipstick and goosebumps everywhere. "I'm gonna fucking get you for that!" He answered by raking three claws down her face from forehead to lip.

Zhora wiped the blood across her face and laughed. "I'm even sexier now. … No lady's gonna be able to resist me." When she told them she'd fought a giant ten-foot cat and lived, they'd swoon. The Grandmaster agreed (everyone loves a scar, especially three across the eye) and gave her a permanent bonus to Performance.

Then she grabbed him. "Oh, you smell like strawberries and sunshine, you do. … You're a good boy." She squeezed him for serious damage and mashed her cheek against his. "I wasn't joking about the matching jammies." Bezil clamped his teeth onto her cheek. "Oh, you're giving me little kisses, are you?" She promised him tiny costumes and hats for every holiday. He could match Jameson: Jameson in a silver cowboy hat, Bezil in a brown one as the sheriff. Thea appeared with a platter of tiny hats, and Zhora picked out a rainbow sombrero to start.

"I must commit an atrocity," said Bezil. He tried to liquid his way out of her arms, and she held him together. "This is handling." Then a blue orb rose in his chest and turned red in his throat as he transmuted a Cone of Cold into a cone of fire, point-blank. She pinched his snout nearly shut and took only the edge of it, and was left with one hit point. She asked if they could be friends. "Never!" So she planned the taxidermy instead: copper marble eyes like his granddad's, or, at the Arbiter's suggestion, "the yellow eyes of a coward." She settled on one yellow and one gold, because he fought valiantly.

Bezil had seven hit points left and she had one. Her next swing bounced off his wings. "Are you denying my love?" "Yes!" He told her to go pet Jameson if she liked him so much. Jameson said he didn't much like being manhandled, and that no one could really domesticate him anyway: "Ask my ex-wife."

Zhora apologized and squeezed one last time. In his ear, too quiet for the crowd, she said, "Don't worry, brother, we will be reunited in Valhalla." The crowd went silent at the death rattle. "I love you. I hope you know I fought you because I respected you." Bezil peed as he died. "That's called scent marking," Zhora said, "and that means you like me. I am yours in death, sir, I am."

### For Bezil

"To Bezil!" The Grandmaster told Zhora to keep the body as her trophy. Thea, who had been wheeling out two giant tanks of krakens for the winner she expected, had to roll them away. The Grandmaster named Zhora an unexpected comeback champion, a reminder to never give up, and handed her the contract for twelve new bakeries. Zhora slung Bezil around her shoulders like a stole and gave her speech. She thanked her estranged family, her father ("Dad, you are sweet human bacon. No one ever expected you to be able to pull a lady like Mom, but you did"), the gods above and below, 3M for the fantastic duct tape, and Bezil: "Thank you for the donation of your organs and body. They will be cherished." The Grandmaster raised a mug. "For Bezil!"

## Dried Blood

At the table, the champions agreed the double natural 1 was quite an introduction. The meow was improvised on the spot, and chat called it the best Silvery Barbs they had ever seen. Mudcat noted that hardly anyone brings Silvery Barbs or Shield to a Deathmatch. Candace the Magnificent admitted she had picked the barbarian path of the wild heart before seeing Bezil's art, and was furious with herself. Brutha Queon said Bezil had been built as a support caster, with Haste and Slow, and had to be rebuilt to be destructive. Bezil had peed out of spite, and Zhora now needs cleaning supplies they don't make in this realm. Chat, it turned out, kept loading Bezil with fatigue not out of hate but because they wanted Zhora's hot cocoa, and Zhora confirmed that by the end the invitation was open to all the Acolytes.

Asked about her bakery, Zhora said its best item is the **cat croquembouche**: cream puffs shaped like little cats, glazed in golden syrup and filled with catnip-scented sweet cream. The mascot is Bezil. The Grandmaster questioned how many greats really separate Bezil from his copper dragon ancestor ("at least 12"), suggesting he might turn out to be some other kind of dragon entirely, or part manticore. Bezil: "So that's where I get my temper from." Mudcat, delighted to be handed a cowboy squirrel, noted he now has a whole stable of rodents to play, alongside a New Yorker chinchilla who smokes and a union boss. The Grandmaster floated a sponsor-a-cat commercial ("for two coins a day, you can save this cat") and said she'd love to see the two of them back as a tag team.

Bezil's final words for the Acolytes: "meow." Zhora's: stay hydrated, love on your pets, and come on down to **Bezil's Bakery**, where it's buy two, get one free on all milk bread.
