## What happens
### Cold open — the man in the slate blue tunic
1. **0:00:45** Far from Dämmerung and from the Dread Fortress lies a realm "wreathed in cold light" in **perpetual twilight**. A castle stands above a sprawling, thriving castle town that runs out to raw earth.
2. **0:01:18** Inside the castle, **a man in a slate blue tunic** stares out a window at the city, the twilight sky and **a shadowed sun**. He speaks to an unseen listener.
   - He says that at one point in the Void's history **the Shadow King "was broken, murdered,"** and his unparalleled power was **"trapped within a box… used for spectator sport."**
   - He says "Manipulating the raw stuff of Lux [?] itself was no easy task."
   - "Now that power has been flung across this microcosmos, and I am filled with dread at the possibilities."
3. **0:02:27** He warns that "even a fragment of that power will usher disaster." He says the power "should never have been used in such a way to begin with. Not when there is so much to be done," and hopes his listener will "in time… see the truth of the matter." The scene leaves the realm.

### Arrival at the Rust Blood Alliance war camp
4. **0:02:56** The view crosses the cosmos and passes Void lifeforms back to the party. **Grim**, a small goblin leading a squad of five other goblins, takes them up a ladder-stairway into the **Rust Blood Alliance war camp**.
5. The camp is full of Underdark peoples: drow, kobolds, goblins, hobgoblins, bugbears and **Kuo-Toa** ("walking fishmen"). They carry crates, dig trenches and raise barricades. **When the party appears, "the record scratches" and everyone stares.** Grim hurries them along.
6. **0:04:19** Perception checks:
   - Nehebka rolls a nat 20 (33 total).
   - Grimnar rolls a 6: "I don't see shit. I don't hear shit. I'm just here to have a good time… **I am following the will of chaos. I don't need to be present.**"
7. **0:04:49** Nehebka notices the following:
   - Everyone wears a standard uniform: short or long coats, leather boots, some armour, a few masks and helmets.
   - Weapons include pikes, swords, and **staves bent into an L or J shape with a little crystal tip**, plus J-shaped wands.
   - She overhears: "Who the hell are they?", "**I'm going to need more than three to win this war,**" and, most notably, "**Wasn't there another knight here already?**"
8. **0:06:12** They pass a mess hall with grilled vegetables and a giant pot of slop. The smells are "at best, nauseating," with possibly cooked leather in the mix. They arrive at **a large off-white bell tent guarded by two drow in full regalia with halberds**. Grim: "I want to introduce you guys to the big wigs."
9. **0:07:58** More of what Nehebka sees:
   - Some soldiers wear ornate versions of the uniform with gold shoulder stripes and harder elbow and knee armour. **From her time "as a Knight of the Void Domain"** she reads them as officers or specialists.
   - The various unit insignias seen last episode appear on coats. It is one army with different markings.
   - **Grim's squad** wear spiked boots, heavier plated armour, thick metal-plated gloves, **gas masks and red berets**. No other squad wears red.
10. **0:10:01** Grimnar asks what shape the forces are in. The camp is active and tired but not haggard, with a constant flow of supplies. **The camp is pristine and well defended**, with undamaged battlements and watchtowers.

### The war council tent
11. **0:11:29** Inside the tent:
    - A round table holds a parchment map covered in stone tokens. Some are shaped like the Titans from the last fight; others are triangles, ovals and squares, some stacked to mark fortifications.
    - Officers bicker: press the attack, fortify, or tend the wounded.
    - At the centre stands **a tall drow woman (about 6'2") in deep black plate with intricate silver filigree**. Her arms are folded and her face is stoic.
    - Silence falls when the party enters. Grim: "Hey, everybody, look at what I found out in the Blastlands."
12. **0:13:15** **Grimnar introduces himself as "a Void Knight of the Dread Fortress."** Nehebka says nothing, so he introduces her ("Not chatty today") and Muffy ("Hello").
13. **0:13:44** A stocky, bushy-browed **male duergar** pushes forward. His beard is in a spiral braid balled at the top, and he carries a large hammer. He asks: "Can these people be trusted?"
15. **0:14:51 — Nehebka speaks.** She clocks the duergar: "**It is not about if we can be trusted. It is about what our purpose is. And we see that you have a problem that is soon becoming ours.**"
    - Her persuasion succeeds (18).
    - Muffy's insight on Triss fails; Triss gives nothing away.
16. **0:16:29** The duergar introduces himself as **Gund Ashbrow, "chief artillerist here for the Rust Blood Alliance."** "Your mannerisms are far too chaotic to be programmed. So I guess you're not spies. I'll buy it. For now."
    - Muffy: "have you encountered very, uh, programmed people? Often?"
    - Gund: "you're not made of metal. So that's a start." Part of him wants to "poke and prod and see if you've got a little metal underneath there."
17. **0:17:08 — The sand threat.** Nehebka threatens that sand "will go down your throat and you will never be able to breathe again."
    - Triss smirks. Gund: "You dare? What insolence…"
    - Nehebka: "**I do not threat. Everything I say is a promise.**"
    - Gund: "I'll be watching you… I hate sand anyway… It's coarse." Grimnar: "It gets everywhere." Gund: "It's everywhere."
18. **0:18:32 — Grim vouches for them.** "They were neck deep in Warforged by the time we got to them." He describes each:
    - Nehebka: "total mastery over the earth."
    - Grimnar: "lightning from the sky."
    - Muffy: "One of the most dexterous and nimble things I've ever seen."
    - "This could be our chance, guys."
19. **0:19:27** Grimnar says they were sent by the Dread Fortress to investigate "dark happenings." Gund: "Dark happenings? In case you haven't noticed, we're at war."
20. **0:20:11 — The two who came before.** Nehebka asks whether more of them are coming, since "one has already come."
    - Triss: two others came **a day or two before the party**, claiming to be "some sort of advanced scouting party." They made no mention of the Dread Fortress.
    - The party knows nothing of them. **Stolas** had told them other groups were going elsewhere, and only these three were going to Dämmerung.
    - **Grimnar has served the Dread Fortress for about 20 years.** His history check (12) says a dispatched squad normally keeps its region until it asks for help. A second team isn't standard, but it isn't impossible.
21. **0:23:03** Triss: "Perhaps we do need to tighten up on security." She describes the two:
    - **Dressed in blues and grays, in chain mail and heavy hoods. Each carried a longsword; one carried a shield.**
    - No discernible heraldry. They kept close together with "an air of nervousness."
    - They knew the enemy's force composition, and **"They spoke of chaos."** Triss's people had no name for this energy before.
    - They said **"a large accumulation of chaos… is the root of our problems here,"** and offered to help solve it.
22. **0:24:53** Nehebka looks straight at Grimnar, whose (bear) ears figuratively perk up. Grimnar: "**Sure, chaos can be used for nefarious purposes, but it is in itself not harmful or not evil. It is a fundamental force that drives the fate of all of us.**"
    - Triss: the only fundamental force driving them right now is "the amount of shells that we can put up per day."
    - Triss: "if all of this falls, it's over for all of us."
23. **0:26:29** Which way did the two go? Triss's scouts followed them **into the Blastlands, toward a large mountain range where most of their troubles come from.** The party "should count yourselves very fortunate" to have landed in the Blastlands and lived. Muffy: "Very dextrous."
24. **0:27:41** Grimnar asks how the war began. Gund slaps the table: "where's that damn squid? They'll tell him." Triss gives him a dagger-sharp look.
25. **0:27:56 — The war's history (Triss).**
    - "This is year 10."
    - It began as Underdark against Overworld. Overworlders invaded their homes and temples for treasure and adventure.
    - Losing badly, every Underdark people formed one coalition "under one banner" and **made something to help win.**
    - "**It stopped regarding us as allies and started to regard us as resources and obstacles.** As it stands, **the Warmind** means to bring us to extinction."
    - Nehebka: "So you created your own problem." She glances at Gund, the chief artillerist.
    - Triss: "we made our own problem meant to save us. And now it is our damnation."
26. **0:29:54** Grimnar asks why no one has heard of a ten-year war. Nehebka: "Are we to know about every ground conflict…?" Muffy: "once that involves mentions of chaos, maybe."
    - Triss: they called for aid once the Warmind turned on them. By then **"The Warmind seemingly casts an umbrella over Dämmerung."** It intercepts and distorts messages. "**Magic wanes and waxes… it is unnatural.**"
27. **0:31:39 — The plan.**
    - The chief mountain lies **about two days' travel south**. A long-prepared, "desperate operation" will be staged there.
    - "We've managed to harness another power. You might call it divine intervention."
    - The aim is to destroy the Warmind's lair, either by bringing the mountain down on it or by going into its caverns.
    - This is the same direction the two strangers went. Nehebka: "It would seem we are aligned." Triss: "Good."
28. **0:33:12** Triss asks them to "engage with the camp and see what needs to be done," to lift spirits.
29. **0:33:56 — The squid.** Grimnar asks how they know they aren't repeating their mistake.
    - Gund: "because the squid's not involved. It's my specialty."
    - "They got the tentacles on their face. They eat your brain." **Illithids, "Mind Flayers."** This is the party's first time hearing of them.
    - Muffy's history check (14): as Void Domain knights patrolling the Void, they have seen scattered reports of **"tentacled shell-shaped… voidcraft" and void knights attacked by "tentacled vampires."**
30. **0:36:27** The Warmind was built **with** the illithids. Nehebka needles Gund: "you believe that you alone… now have the means to your salvation."
    - Gund: "Everybody here contributes… even the squids."
    - The illithids "told us they were making a brain. Something that could think," to out-strategize the overworlders. "Their culture is all about brains and eating brains… We were desperate."
31. **0:37:58** Muffy: "I'm not nervous that it's your brain." Everyone looks down. **Triss clears the tent except herself, Gund and Grim.**
    - Triss: "We made sacrifices that we felt were necessary to survive."
    - "**When I say that we all collectively contributed to this Warmind, it is both literal and physical.**"
32. **0:39:17** Grimnar senses omission. Kyle confirms they are getting **"an abridged version."** There has been no mention of the overworlders since the first comment.
33. **0:40:31** Nehebka: "So you gave a part of yourself… now the very part that you gave seeks to destroy you."
    - Triss: "Cruel irony."
    - Nehebka: "Is it ironic? Or is it just a natural consequence to action? Too many, how do you say, cooks in the kitchen."
34. **0:41:08** Gund cracks his knuckles: "You weren't there… It was us or them… what was the other alternative, huh? Just let them wipe us out?"
    - Nehebka: "**Forgive me if you misinterpreted. Forgive me if you thought I cared…** Your feelings are not the important part."
    - Gund: "Tris, I'll let you do this." He walks out.
35. **0:43:08 — The angel.** Triss puts her hands on the table.
    - In the early war they fought summoned powers.
    - With the Warmind's help they **struck down "the corpse of an angel, a gargantuan being of light and justice."**
    - **Gund plans to "harness that literal divinity… and weaponize it,"** just as they made the camp's munitions.
    - The weapon ("armament") is Gund's project. Its construction site is under heavy guard.
36. **0:45:53** Triss and Gund have worked together "since the beginning."
    - When the Warmind turned, there was **"an exodus" from the Underdark to the surface.**
    - "dwarven bastion cities were crossing with drow conclaves, Goblin warrens, kobold dens… There wasn't enough space for all of us and the walls were closing in."
37. **0:47:09** Triss offers the barracks ("less populated than they have [been]"). She invites them to **a meeting tomorrow morning about the armament.**

### Grim's tour
38. **0:49:16** Grim: "don't let Gund get you down. Dude's grumpy. He's always been grumpy. And Triss, love her to death. But she's got a lot of stuff on her shoulders."
39. **0:50:01** Grim describes his squad and the stakes.
    - **"me and my red caps"** rescue folks from the Blastlands and run commando operations. "this is our home."
    - **There is a city with a gate: "if the Warmind gets through, then it has access to the gate… it could spread everywhere."**
40. **0:50:47** Nehebka asks whether the Warmind, made of pieces of everyone, already knows what they know.
    - Grim thinks it doesn't know about the armament, which was only begun "maybe a couple of months ago. It's genuinely last ditch, and don't tell anybody I said that."
    - "I don't think that the warmind can put skin on a warforged and have it act like a person yet… But I also don't want to be proven wrong."
41. **0:52:39** Grimnar scans the crowd for anyone watching them "in more than a novelty kind of way." He keeps his doubts private because there are "too many ears."

### The barracks — the party confers

    - Tables with liquor bottles, cards and dice.
    - A pinup on the wall and books lying around.
43. **0:54:57** Grimnar checks the tent flaps for eavesdroppers; nobody is there. The players had separately wanted Muffy to look under the beds.
44. **0:55:42 — Their doubts.**
    - Grimnar: "there was a specific lack of information on these overworlders." Muffy: "Also a lot of information given to us."
    - Grimnar: "**out of chaos comes order. Perhaps this is the wheel of chaos and they should reap what they have sown.**"
    - Nehebka: "too many strings lead to the same location… our job will inevitably benefit them, but their plight is not ours."
    - Grimnar: "If they're willing to kill an angel and use its power for themselves, what else are they capable of?"
45. **0:57:28 — On Gund.**

    - Nehebka: "**He is the single piece of sand that will break the hourglass…** his actions shall be the ultimate downfall of Dämmerung."
    - Muffy: "there's irony in him showing up right as this was beginning."
46. **0:58:23** Nehebka: the drow, duergar and illithids are "all of the same land underneath… What strikes me is how far they are willing to go." She asks what new feeling drives this "divine bomb."
    - Grimnar: a giant brain made of brains "is probably pretty intelligent… **perhaps this war is already over and they don't know it yet.**"
    - Nehebka: the two strangers are the problem, "Especially if the power that the Warmind is now using is of the Shadow King."
47. **1:00:55** Nehebka: "I will consult the Sands… Something tells me that this is going to be a test. For all of us."
48. **1:01:22** The barracks smell musty, of socks, mud and sweat ("not you Mudcat, like mud mud"). Outside, Muffy sniffs out the cooked meat.
49. **1:03:09** Nehebka stays behind; Grimnar ("He's really just interested in the food") and Muffy head to the mess. **Nehebka attaches sand particles to their clothing so she can find them**: "**She is the sand, the sand is she.**"

### Nehebka's divination ritual
50. **1:04:08** Nehebka's ritual:
    - She sits in the middle of the barracks and chants in an archaic language.
    - From an empty hand she draws piles of sand into a circle around herself, and **draws an ankh** in it.
    - A dome of floating particles surrounds her as she meditates and casts **divination as a ritual**.
    - The sand forms hieroglyph-like signs.
51. **1:05:46** She prays to **"My god-king"**: "These two wandering travelers. Where do their feet take them? What is their purpose in this ruined land?"
    - (Table: Kyle jokes "What if these two are Cabals? No!")
    - She hears **a sudden inhale, "as if air has just filled lungs that… haven't tasted air in like millennia."**
    - The voice: "**Their steps, their purpose. Shadows link them. Chaos beckons.**"
    - Kyle re-reads it as "shadows lengthen, chaos beckons," then adds "**a new king rises**" and "**every king eats [seeks?] its crown.**"
52. **1:09:23** She recasts (the 25% failure roll passes) and asks: "**How is this king seeking to find its crown?**"
    - She hears another exhale and feels **"the heat of a distant desert sun against your back," "wind snaking over dunes long since lost."**
    - A radiance moves over her head to directly in front of her, "as if this presence is now regarding you in earnest."
    - The voice: "**When something is broken, its pieces lie scattered. And every piece has a reflection. When [one is] bathed in light, oh what shadows it will cast. Look where the darkness gathers.**"
    - The sand rushes back into her skin and hair.
53. **1:12:31 — The mail courier.** Coming out of the trance (perception 18), she senses someone at the door. Her tremorsense picks up **a heart rate "going through the roof."**
    - A young, bulky **hobgoblin mail courier**: "Are you okay?" Nehebka: "**The sands have no feeling, child.**" He: "I'm just a mail courier."
    - She "dusts herself off"; the sand only runs to her feet and is sucked back in. "**Do your job, as I have done mine.**"
54. **1:15:46** The courier asks whether she's a magic user.
    - Nehebka: "**If that is your primitive way of asking, can I affect the world around me? Yes.**"
    - He asks for help **reinforcing the wards that keep the camp camouflaged.** "I will see what can be done."
    - She tags him with sand too. He is too shaken to notice: "there's this crazy sand woman in the barracks. What's going on here?"
55. About 20 in-game minutes pass for the two rituals.

### Pooling the omens
73. **1:37:02** Nehebka: "**The two that have come before us, I believe that they are in alignment with what was the Shadow King.**"
    - She recites the omen as "shadows, length[en], and chaos, and new king rises and **every king needs a crown**."
    - She doesn't know whether "the pieces" act alone or seek each other. "What happens if one finds a crown within the Warmind? Do they all vie to be the king?"
    - Grimnar: "if this king is rising, it would be a great threat to the Grandmaster." Nehebka: "which means we must deal with this war mind too."
74. **1:40:53 — Grimnar listens to chaos.** Nehebka asks what Chaos has told him.
    - He closes his eyes. A balloon-like static raises the hair on their arms as he charges his body with chaos energy.
    - **Nehebka casts guidance by stroking his face; her fingerprint is left in glass, hot to the touch.**
    - Kyle describes the vision:
      - The hall's noise fades. He hears his own heartbeat, **then "another heartbeat. Distant… beating with intensity."**
      - Multicoloured chaos energy flows around him, dips into the earth and **"rockets off south."**
      - On a large mountain peak, **"a pulsing orb in the distance of raw chaos, condensed."**
    - Grimnar: "**chaos has shown me the mountain… I believe that is where my path lies.**" Nehebka: "the sands lead me there as well."
75. **1:44:51 — Muffy paints the map.**
    - From her bag she takes **the one plain sheet of paper she always carries ("never changing it. It'll just change paintings")**.
    - She paints with a **dry brush**. The image reveals itself like invisible ink or corrosion: watercolour edges, oilier toward the centre, then becoming 3D and solid.
    - Muffy is "a little bit of both" on chaos: she taps into it as a primal force and is not fully religious about it.
76. **1:48:33** The painting (performance 7) shows the following:
    - **The mountain, with a tiny black crevice about 25% of the way up: the entrance.**
    - The mountain glows in **the same magenta as the attacking warforged**.
    - **The whole skyline has the oil-slick sheen of chaotic energy.** Nehebka: "Then we have our destination."
77. **1:50:16** Grimnar: "**Chaos cannot be controlled. It can be wielded. It could be directed. But trying to hold on to too much will lead to ruin.**"
    - Nehebka: "You are the acolyte of chaos. What would you have us do…? To understand, to deal with, to contain."
    - Grimnar: keep it out of the two figures' hands, "or for this king… the first step is to deny it to them." Nehebka: "Then we are of one accord."
78. **1:52:16 — The chip.** Muffy offers Nehebka a chip with hummus.

## Who and what appears
### Characters
**Grimnar** (Drew)
- A bear-like Void Knight of the Dread Fortress, about 20 years in its service, with burned fingers.
- A devotee of chaos ("the wheel of chaos"; Nehebka calls him "the acolyte of chaos"). He holds that chaos is not evil: "It can be wielded… trying to hold on to too much will lead to ruin."
- **Can mimic others' abilities**: he copied Nehebka's tremorsense and the Physician's magic resistance. He senses chaos visions and crackles with chaos lightning.
- Whittles figurines; carved David. Eats whole fish. Took a sandy finger and sandy rum without hesitation.

**Nehebka** (CriticalBard)
- A sand being: "She is the sand, the sand is she." Has tremorsense (30 ft) that detects heartbeats.
- Rituals with an ankh in sand; prays to and serves **the God-King, "the dark Lord she originally served"** (pyramids, sarcophagi, desert). "In her mind, she still serves one person only."
- "I was not created to eat. I was created to protect." A guardian; meditates rather than sleeps.
- Turns sand into glass and sculpts; casts wall of stone from sand; leaves a glass fingerprint when casting guidance.
- Commander Kaim interrupts her meditations (DM's improvised image).

**Muffy** (JustSeum)
- A small fennec-fox creature with large ears; can stretch about three inches taller on her hind legs.
- A painter ("I paint there") who reveals images with a dry brush on the one sheet she always carries.
- Carries a "call to home" on her back. Ran Cabals and Krakens badly. Her mother "just called me Muffy."

**Trissavin (Triss) Baenerret**: drow war mistress of the drow conclave on Dämmerung and leader of the council. About 6'2", in black plate with silver filigree. Stoic, desperate (per the Physician). Has worked with Gund since the war began.

**Gund Ashbrow**: duergar chief artillerist of the Rust Blood Alliance. Spiral-braided beard, hammer. Leads the angel-weapon project. Calls illithids "the squid"; hates sand.

**Grim**: goblin leader of the Redcaps (red berets, gas masks). Rescue and commando work. He found the party and vouched for them.

**The Physician**: an illithid, "one of the last of my kind," who leads the Nightingales. He wears a mechanical plague-doctor-like gas mask and a bloody surgical apron with blades and a wand. Mind-read Nehebka and was rebuffed by the God-King.

**The Nightingales**: four white-masked, veiled medics. Each night they destroy the Alliance's fallen, brains and bodies, so the Warmind can't take them ("counting corpses").

**Calvin**: hobgoblin of the 23rd Madcaps, with a high-and-tight haircut. Subject of Nehebka's glass sculpture and Muffy's planned "Calvin the Hobbs" comic.

**The 34th Deep Divers**: Kuo-Toa tunnelling squad, seven at the start and fewer now. They worship the "Changebringer"/"flux bringer" (chaos, "many names"). Salute is two chest slaps and a gargle. They adopted the party as the "35th."

**Unnamed NPCs**
- The bugbear chef: never heard of the Grandmaster; a Deathmatch recruitment target.
- The young hobgoblin mail courier: asked for help with the camouflage wards.
- The drow guards.

**The two strangers**: in blues and grays, chain mail, heavy hoods, longswords and one shield. They claimed to be an advanced scouting party, knew about chaos, and headed through the Blastlands to the mountain. Nehebka believes they are aligned with the Shadow King.

**The man in the slate blue tunic**: an unnamed figure in a twilight castle realm who knows the Box of Chaos history and dreads the scattered power.

**The God-King**: Nehebka's deity. Answers via breath and desert sun.

**The Warmind**: an elder brain built by the illithids with the coalition, literally from the peoples' own contributions. It turned on its makers, grew from the black star, blocks communication and travel, and builds Warforged and Titans.

**Mentioned**: Stolas (gave the mission); Elusia Skypiercer ("ruler of all of us"); the Shadow King.
