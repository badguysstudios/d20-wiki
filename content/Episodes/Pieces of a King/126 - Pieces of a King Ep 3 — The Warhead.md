## What happens
### Cold open — Trissavin Baenerret (0:00:44)
1. **0:00:44** Kyle narrates the past of **Trissavin Baenerret**, a drow.
   - When she was young, before **the Damaranians above crusaded into the Underdark** and put her people to the blade, she dreamed of being **a priestess**, a leader of her people and a beacon of hope.
   - When she was older, **the Illithids and Duergar** told her of a plan for **a weapon to protect the inhabitants of the Underdark** and repel the Damaranians once and for all. She didn't believe in it, "but what choice did they have?"
2. **0:01:30** **A meeting was held and papers were signed approving the project.** She told herself this was what leadership meant: hard decisions.
3. Later she tasted the air above after fleeing the tunnels below, with many races around her and few of her own.
   - **She was one of the first to organize a breakaway from "the Warmind's betrayal."**
   - Seeing the destruction it had wrought on both sides, she collapsed in tears.
4. **0:02:14** "Not but a day ago, **five strangers** appeared before" her, each offering salvation from the nightmare loosed on Dämmerung.
   - She and the alliance are all that stand between **the Warmind, the Last City, and the Planar Gate**. She protects "the last of the Damaranians above and those below."
   - She is "terrified of the possibility of hope." Going to bed with a tear on her cheek, she thinks: "maybe, just maybe."

### Morning in the barracks
5. **0:03:04** The party had a tense encounter last night with **an Illithid known only as "the Physician" and his Nightingales**, then slept in the barracks.
   - Morning sounds: cots rustling, springs squeaking, boots being laced.
   - **Calvin the Hob**, fully geared and well rested despite last night's drinks, taps Grimnar awake. "Hey, you awake?" "Well, I am now."
   - Calvin points them to breakfast at the mess tent and says there's a lot of activity at the command tent. He wanted to say hello before everyone ships out.
6. **0:05:20 — Muffy paints.** Muffy is up early, bright-eyed and bushy-tailed, painting as a morning meditation. Nobody can tell whether her paintings are magical or "just coloring pictures."
   - She is painting **a portrait of the three of them as "the 35th"** with a logo she keeps blotting out.
   - She works at the desk where she set up her game, **Cabals and Krakens**.
7. **0:06:34 — The insignia shop.** Soldiers crowd her for unit insignia: crossed bones and a sword, "and also like a laser," a skull, a flaming sword.
   - Muffy: "What kind of laser?" The table likens it to a tattoo shop in a small redneck town.
   - Grimnar (as a soldier, played for laughs) wants "no regrets" across his chest, and a demon "with a bone sword through its head" whose face is his face.
8. **0:08:13 — Nehebka doesn't sleep.** She kneels in deep meditation, hands on knees, a slow, soft cloud of sand drifting around her. "Considering her job, sleep is not an option."
   - The soldiers keep a respectful distance after her display the previous night (the sand wall, two heads, the bottle).
9. **0:10:24** Calvin offers her coffee or water. Nehebka: "**I am born of the desert sands. What exactly do you think water would do?**" Calvin wonders whether she'd get soggy; he's never met a person made of desert sand.
   - Grimnar tells her to cut him some slack. Nehebka: "**I could cut many things. Slack is not one of them.**"
   - Calvin decides he doesn't want to be bisected.
10. **0:11:44** A whistle sounds and gruff voices call "Gather on!" The soldiers file out, grumbling.
    - One: "more trenches to dig."
    - Another hopes breakfast isn't slop and gruel again.
    - A third wants "two laser swords and a skull with a flamethrower" on their breastplate.
11. **0:12:45 — The mustache portrait.** Muffy calls her painting "the worst painting I've ever made" and digs out an older one she took by accident: **a perfectly scrolled portrait of the Grandmaster with a mustache.** "See? It's art."
    - Grimnar: "technically, yes, it is art, but I also think it might be treason… I'm not going to tell her."
    - Muffy: "I think she would love it." Grimnar: "**It always seems so stuck up to me.**"
    - Muffy rolls it up: "You just don't know art. It is in the eye of the beauty of the holder."
12. **0:15:25 — Nehebka's divination (ritual).** She asks about **the two who went toward the mountain**. A dome of sand forms around her, showing images inside "like the sphere from Las Vegas."
    - She sees darkness, lightless tunnels, metal beams, muck, sloshing footsteps and **two people breathing, one more haggard than the other**.
    - She feels a spreading warmth in her side and a grip against her ribs.
    - The impression is of violence and fear, but also responsibility and duty. **They have met resistance and one is wounded, but they are pressing on.** They are underground, apparently resting, and have not yet arrived.
    - Others can only see the visions from inside the dome. Kyle restates as canon that **Nehebka can let allies pass through her sand barriers**.
13. **0:21:38** Muffy pokes her snout into the dome at neck level ("Oh, wow"). The sand dissolves elegantly back into Nehebka, "like a fairy dusting."
14. **0:23:02** Grimnar is "old-ish" and stiff. He has spent the whole time stretching loudly in the corner, joints popping.
15. **0:24:22 — A Nightingale approaches.** From the tent flap Grimnar sees the camp waking and smells breakfast from the southern mess.
    - **A white-masked Nightingale** walks unhurried from the command tent toward their tent. Everyone gives it a wide berth. Grimnar shudders.
16. **0:25:41** Muffy offers Nehebka a hand up, a strong, statue-steady pull (a nat 20). **Nehebka's hand is rough and scaled, "60 grit" sandpaper.** She accepts the help with a look of appreciation. Muffy gives a gummy, happy-pup smile.
17. **0:27:45** Through **tremorsense**, Nehebka feels the Nightingale's unnaturally still heartbeat, unlike the soldiers' living, anxious ones.
    - It enters with its arms folded neatly behind its back: "**My master has wished to see you in the command tent, if you would be so kind to join us.**"
18. **0:29:00 — Grimnar reads the Nightingale.** As he did with the Physician, he **adopts one of its resistances**: psionic resistance.
    - Unlike the Physician's, which felt otherworldly and alien, **this one feels trained and applied**.
    - Nature: it resembles Nehebka's intensity, built through intense practice and meditation.
    - Arcana: **something on the Nightingale's person also generates part of the resistance magically**; he can't tell what.
    - Compared with the Physician, the Physician's psionics are "a lake" and the Nightingale's "a puddle." Kyle notes Grimnar first rolled acrobatics by mistake: "Doing a backflip to determine…"

### The salutes (0:33:36)
19. **0:33:36** Muffy salutes the Nightingale. Kyle recalls **the 34th's salute: two fist bumps on the chest and a gurgle**. He notes the **Dread Fortress / "Domain of the Void" salute** has never been defined and lets Sam define it: "This will be canon."
    - Sam's first thought was the Parent Trap handshake, rejected "for the sake of Becca and Drew."
    - **Muffy's salute: a Trekkie-esque hand with two fingers up and the others held down, one arm tucked behind the back, upright.** The phrase is "**up, up and away**"; the meaning is "the Fortress, reporting for duty."
    - Muffy's finger doesn't close all the way (Sam's real surgery), so she holds it back.
20. **0:36:28 — The Nightingale salute.** It returns **two bumps on the chest, then crosses its fingers and bows its head**. No gurgle. Drew expected a birdman call.
21. **0:37:20** Muffy leaves the hodgepodge insignia painting for the soldiers and **keeps the 35th portrait as a gift she hasn't shown her friends yet**.
22. **0:37:40 — "After you."** Grimnar keeps trying to put the Nightingale in the middle. The Nightingale keeps stopping: "no, after you, please." Grimnar: "No, I insist, after you."
    - They trade places all the way to the tent "like a never-ending series of doors."
    - Soldiers tense as they pass. One carrying a crate of potatoes goes "Oh, okay"; others half-salute or look away.

### The war council (0:39:39)
23. **0:39:39 — Outside the command tent:**
    - the two drow guards;
    - **Gund Ashbrow's personal guard** of duergar, in charcoal-gray armor with black-and-red checkered trim and a smatter of silver painted across the helmet brows to look like ash;
    - **Grim's redcaps**;
    - the other four Nightingales.
    A redcap: "All right, finally, this meeting started." A duergar: "well, you all are the honored guests." A drow: "The war mistress awaits inside" (Nehebka checks that it's true).
24. **0:42:09** Inside are **Trissavin, Gund, Grim and the Physician**, the Physician not wearing his full mask. The battle map is gone; there's a spread of breads, cheeses, jams and wine.
    - Trissavin: "**You all are from the Dread Fortress. You all are perhaps our last hope.**"
    - She promises to take them to the site and show them "our implement that will hopefully rid us of the Warmind."
25. **0:45:03 — Nehebka on the weapon.** "I will not say the morality because I do not understand the term," but she hopes its use is truly the last resort and that they understand the consequences.
    - Gund: "**Of course it's a last resort**… If I had the option to, I would just make more artillery… we know the cost."
26. **0:46:23** Nehebka asks what comes next if this fails. **The Physician:** the Warmind **marches on the Last City, on Dämmerung Prime, reaches the Planar Gate, and spreads across the Void**. He's "so curious to see what the response would be" from the Dread Fortress if it spilled into other realms.
    - Muffy: "I don't like the sound of that." Grimnar: "you almost seem curious to let it happen." The Physician: "a curiosity that I do not wish to see fulfilled. More of a nightmare."
27. **0:48:53 — Insight on the Physician.** He's honest, but at the very back of his mind he has wondered many times how the Warmind getting out would play out:
    - whether it would spare them if they hid;
    - whether opening the Planar Gate would bring help from the Dread Fortress;
    - whether it would become "somebody else's problem."
    These are "the things that keep him up at night"; the scale of what he helped build is immeasurable.
28. **0:50:09 — "Fall, fight, or join?"** Nehebka asks what they'll do if the Warmind marches on Dämmerung Prime. **Trissavin cuts in: "We will fight. We will fight to our last breath. No matter the cost."**
29. **0:51:27 — Nehebka's confession.** To the Physician: "I understand power. I understand survival. That is what I was made to assist with. Someone else's. And for all intents and purposes, **I was the war mind for my domain.**"
    - Gund panics, Trissavin's eyes go wide, Grim lets out a low whistle, and the Physician's tentacles spread: "Really? You are a war mind?"
    - Nehebka: "Not in the same terminology or application… **I was created to do one thing, and that was to protect.**" She asks whether he, the creator, stands with his creation or with them. **The Physician: "I stand with them."** (His line is merged into speaker 03's segment.)
30. **0:53:01 — The Physician's vow.** The Warmind holds the minds of "**20 millions**," a collection of thoughts, fears and strategies, and would add anyone it caught.
    - "**If this city were to fall… it would be my duty**," looking at everyone in the room, "**to make sure that the Warmind could not gain access to their minds. And then I would make sure it couldn't gain access to mine.**"
    - Nehebka: "**Time, as the sands say, will tell.**" Muffy stands mouth agape, tongue out like a cartoon.
31. **0:54:44** The Physician asks whether they will truly help or abandon them. Grimnar, distrusting the Physician and his Nightingales: "**We will do what chaos allows.**"
    - The Physician: "Unknown variable. Some would say random chance."
    - Muffy: "Do you not use chaos here? It could be the answer to all your questions."
    - Kyle's sidebar: Grim and the Physician approve; Trissavin and Gund "will remember that."
    - Trissavin: "There's been enough chaos as it is, but if that is what helps us survive and win this war… I will allow it."
32. **0:57:22 — Trissavin and Muffy.** "Do you have family, Muffy?" "**I have a lot of siblings. There was a whole litter of us.**" She would do "anything" to protect them.
    - Trissavin asks how committed she is: if Muffy could protect her family from chaos by enforcing order, would she? Muffy: "**I'm committed to the Grandmaster. If she asked me to do anything, I would do it.**" "I see. Well, I can accept that."
    - Muffy goes very still.

    - "**Do not mistake our intentions**… our goals are aligned… And if we are correct, **what we are here for also resides within this Warmind. And we will take it and destroy it if that is what we must do.**" Spreading to other places "would be antithetical to what we must do."
    - She steps right up to Trissavin: "**I would hope that your intentions do not turn your malevolent gaze towards us.**" Trissavin: "I will not dream of it." Nehebka: "**As one who does not dream, good.**" She floats back.
    - The Physician will remember that, and so will Muffy. Sam: "don't be getting knocked if you buck with little Muffiana."
34. **1:01:20** Grimnar: "Posturing aside, what is it that we want to do now?"
    - Trissavin: they'll go to **the site of the Solar**, see **the warhead**, and hear the plan on the way.

35. **1:02:05 — Breakfast first.** Grimnar (inferred; the line merges with Kyle's at 1:01:58): "Well then it seems like we have a plan… **can we have breakfast first? I'm starved.**"

    - Grim: "Let him eat… full bellies, before we decide to go touch weapons of mass destruction." Muffy: "Food for all!" Grimnar: "**You could all be dead this afternoon, so might as well eat.**"

### To the Solar's Grave (1:03:44)
36. **1:03:44 — The transport.** They board **an organic, insectile, tracked creature-vehicle** with Trissavin, Gund, the Physician, Grim and all their retinues.
    - It crosses a beautiful Dämmerung landscape far from the trenches: greenery and mountains, then **tall factory pipes and a large city framed against mountains**.
    - It then descends into **an underground tunnel** and accelerates for **an hour of "horrific silence."**
37. **1:05:31 — The Solar's Grave.** An above-ground research facility, more industrial than the camp: metal buildings, duergar in lab coats or heavy armor, drow and hobgoblins focused on research and development.
    - Cannons and crates of shells are everywhere.
    - Organic war machines include **troll-like constructs carrying swivel turrets of colored crystals** and crossbows.
    - Gund: "We're here. Everybody out." Gund "is always mad."
38. **1:08:06 — The Solar.** From an overlook they see **the colossal, winged corpse of a fallen solar**: iridescent armor, lustrous gold and white wings, smirched with mud and flecks of gore.
    - Crews harvest its bones and gold, and **giant drill-like syringes extract marrow**. Every part not yet used in the weapon is being tested and refined.
    - **Gund looks proud. Trissavin is tight and still**, the opposite of Gund. Grim waits, uninterested. The Physician watches the party.
39. **1:10:29 — Trissavin explains.** "**This is the solar… the Damaranians above, they called it down. And this is why we needed to make the Warmind.** If we didn't, this would have wiped us out. **We just didn't expect it to work that well.**"
    - Is it the only one? "We don't know anymore. **Our connection to the above is not what it once was. The Warmind has seen to that.**" In the war with the Damaranians, several temples and practices were lost, "which… in time, I hope to rectify."
40. **1:12:00** Nehebka feels familiarity with "something summoned and discarded like this."
41. **1:13:17 — Grimnar's tale.** He has never seen anything this large, but **soldiers who traverse the Void told him of a colossal corpse in the Void, "bigger than a world," whose power was being harvested.** "But soldiers say a lot of things."
    - Kyle contrasts it with the "Voidal fauna" seen on the portal relay: this one is humanoid, clearly conscious and powerful.
42. **1:15:07** Grimnar, half under his breath: "**it kind of reminds me of the Grandmaster a little bit**" (the wings and armor). Muffy: "Missing a mustache." Kyle: "the Grandmaster and the Arbiter have wings… Makes you wonder."
43. **1:16:04 — The plan (Gund).** One warhead is ready and **five more are in development**, for a **multi-pronged attack**:
    - one warhead deployed **through the air**;
    - tunnels dug by subterranean teams lead under **the mountain**, where **an operation led by Grim** would deliver a warhead to cause a cave-in that crushes the Warmind, or with enough "chaos," blow it away in one go.
    - They can't fix the current warhead to any long-range device ("I can't shoot it out of a cannon as much as I would want to"), so **someone must deliver it**, and he leaves that to the Dread Fortress experts.
    - Muffy: "Is this our area of expertise?" Gund: "**you fell from the sky.**" Muffy: "Came from the Void, but okay."
44. **1:18:14 — Suspicion.** Nehebka and Muffy wonder whether they're being used as a sacrificial play, since nobody seems to have planned how the carrier gets out. Nehebka casts guidance on herself in plain sight ("I wonder").
    - Insight (17 and 15): the Physician, Trissavin and Grim don't mean to use them as lambs, and information is missing.
    - **Gund hadn't planned it, but is willing to let them be sacrificed if it gets done.** He half-expects the outsiders to work some superhero miracle.
45. **1:22:47 — Muffy vs Gund.** Muffy invites Gund along. He says only "the squid" or Grim would go with them; **his task is to direct ground and artillery forces in the Blastlands to distract the Warmind.**
    - Muffy: "send them a letter… Are you going to be in a tent outside of the Blastlands?" Both step forward. "Are you insinuating that I'm not going to be fighting?" "I am! How did you pick up on that? Very wise you are."
    - **She intimidates him, with Grimnar looming behind her "like a giant bear" (17).** Gund's face twitches; he wants to swing but won't ruin the alliance in front of Trissavin. "Look, you don't have to worry about anything. I'm going to be out there so that we can be your path."
    - Muffy: "**Have somebody paint me a picture so I can make sure of it.**" The Physician snickers.
46. **1:26:13** Grimnar: "I'll carry your [bomb]. Just give it to me. We're going that way anyways."
    - Gund will fetch and seal the warhead, and **Grim will give them a map of the tunnel network**. Learning they are **the 35th Deep Divers**, he says **the 34th "and all of their previous iterations" dug those tunnels.**
47. **1:27:12 — Found.** An echo, then another. Smoke plumes rise south of the Solar.
    - Gund: "No, that's not possible." Trissavin steps back and **directs her guard in Sylvan**.
    - The Physician puts on his mask: "**Well, my friends, looks like we've been found.**"
    - Break at 1:28:13 (cut). Kyle's recap misspeaks "the corpse of a fallen soldier" for the solar.

### Round 1
54. **1:45:34** Grim's squad advances. A bolter loads a shell with a loud crank. The drow sprint 60 ft. Nehebka has initiative 18.
55. **1:47:57 — Grimnar's fireball.** "**Hold the line!**" Lightning surges around him and his eyes turn black.
    - His already torn and burned hands char further, and **the lightning burns away some of the fur and skin around his eyes**.
    - The lightning heats into **a giant fireball** that he hurls over Gund's shields about 300 ft (Spell Sniper, 4th level, heightened against the lieutenant).
    - **It hits nine enemies and reduces several smaller units to slag.** The cannons still stand with fewer crew. Only one soldier escapes, with a nat 20.
56. **1:54:01 — Nehebka.** She dashes and her lower half becomes **rotating tornadic sand (sand form)**. To Grimnar: "**Let your storm strike true. If it includes me, so be it.**"
57. **1:55:57 — The flare.** The lieutenant advances and fires **a red signal flare**. Silence, then the distant droning of mighty **war horns from the west and the south**. Drew: "A fireball too early." "The Titans are coming."
58. **1:57:58 — Tree stride.** Nehebka touches a solar-morphed tree. It is encased in sand, which rips it open to reveal a swirling mass.
    - Through **tree stride** (spending a second slot so Muffy can use it too), she and Muffy shoot deep into the enemy line. Grimnar stays put: "I just launched a fireball 300 feet. I'm staying."
59. **2:00:41 — Muffy vs the lieutenant.** She rages and attacks recklessly, battle cry, paintbrush out, and **wails on the flare-raising lieutenant**.
    - A critical hit plus Divine Fury necrotic damage. **She breaks its knees** (Slasher: slowed and grievously wounded, disadvantage on attacks).
    - "**They can't go for those toes, but we can go for theirs.**" Nehebka: "Remember that, everybody."

    - Grim casts **his own fireball**, steered by the players to miss Nehebka and Muffy. Muffy plants her blade in the lieutenant's foot and leans on the hilt in the heat.

    - Grim: "how'd you like that one?" Grimnar: "**It was fine. Six out of ten.**" Grim: "I'll try to make that a ten out of ten."
    - (Kyle calls someone "Mr. Iron Tooth" while casting, at 2:09:58.)

### Characters
**[[Characters/Grimnar|Grimnar]]** (Drew).
- Old-ish and stiff, and stretches loudly.
- Called "the bear" by Trissavin; looms "like a giant bear." That he is a bear is inferred from these lines.
- **Adopts resistances from creatures he reads** and **copies spells cast on him** (death ward), along with chaos-energy tells: eyes going black void, multicolored lightning.
- Turns lightning into fireballs and fires chaos-energy eye lasers. Using his power chars his hands and burns fur and skin around his eyes.
- Finds the Grandmaster "stuck up" but won't report the mustache portrait.
- Has heard soldiers' tales of a world-sized harvested corpse in the Void.
- His answer to the alliance: "We will do what chaos allows."

**[[Characters/Nehebka|Nehebka]]** (CriticalBard).
- "Born of the desert sands," scaled with rough hands.
- **Doesn't sleep**; meditates in drifting sand. **Doesn't dream.**
- Divines through a sand dome; has tremorsense.
- Sand form, tree stride, death ward (sand around the heart), longstrider, shillelagh on her scimitar.
- **"I was the war mind for my domain"; created to protect someone else's power and survival.**
- Says what the party seeks "resides within this Warmind."

**[[Characters/Muffy|Muffy]]** (JustSeum).

- "I'm committed to the Grandmaster. If she asked me to do anything, I would do it."

- Duergar commander with an "Iron Within" retinue. Proud of the Solar's harvest.

**[[Characters/Grim|Grim]]** (Kyle).
- Hobgoblin ("Devastator") leading the redcaps.

- Copied Grimnar's fireball. Approves of chaos.

**[[Characters/The Physician|The Physician]]** (Kyle).
- Illithid with tentacles and a mask, master of the Nightingales.
- **Helped build the Warmind.**

**[[Characters/The two at the mountain|The two at the mountain.]]** Unnamed in this episode. Traveling underground; one wounded.

**[[Characters/The Solar|The Solar.]]** A winged, armored, gold-and-white divine corpse "called down" by the Damaranians. Being harvested for the warheads.
