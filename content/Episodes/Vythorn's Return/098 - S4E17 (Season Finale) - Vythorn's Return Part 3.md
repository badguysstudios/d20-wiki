The fourth season ended without a Deathmatch. It ended with a coup. Fil returned **Vythorn**, [[The Arbiter|the Arbiter]], to [[The Void|the Void]] carrying a forbidden new power and a thousand years of grudges, and he walked into the old Coliseum to settle things with [[The Shadow King|the Shadow King]]. By sundown he had shredded a tyrant, made a deal with a god, and taken the Void for himself.

## The Story

### Waking at Home
Vythorn woke on soft ground under a sunset. He was no longer with his companions and no longer in Giant's Breath. Around him he heard rustling branches, birdsong, the hum of bugs, and far off the familiar roar of a crowd. "There's a warmth to it," the narration offered. "You've found home, Vythorn." He dug his hands into the dirt and grass. Then [[Fil]] shook him awake: "Get up, Vythorn… Wake up."

He lay a short way from an old arena he had shared with [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] time and time again. This time it was not empty. [[The Acolytes]] were cheering the end of yet another Deathmatch, Elusia was there as always, and in the sky above hung a figure who did not belong: Agdrel, the Shadow King.

Fil whispered that she had kept her promise. "I brought you here as promised. A gift, you could say. We've placed our bets on you, Vythorn." She pointed him toward the arena and his fate. Vythorn needed no persuading: "I am more than ready to kill Agdrel." Fil sensed how hard his mind was burning and warned him that once he started down this path, he was "not to deter."

### The Book and the Storm
As the sun went down, vicious chaos storms rolled in. The book that had come with him, the testaments of [[Morir's Cataclysm]], floated up in front of him and flipped open in a flurry of pages to one particular passage. Its words could not be read. They lit up, lifted off the page, circled him, and then burned their way into his flesh and into his essence.

The power was intoxicating. "This book matches your anger," the narration said: his frustration, his fury, his "unbridled rage to have what you want and take it away time and time again." It was "not chaos, but something powerful" that completed him. Looking down at the words seared into his hands, Vythorn felt the new power mix with the chaos energy of the storms now that he was home. He raised a hand and pulled the storms closer until they filled the sky.

He stepped out of the brush onto the battlefield just as the Shadow King's throne descended from the heavens. Lightning cracked across the field. "**Agdrel!**" Multicolored bolts blew the arena's ornate structure to pieces and singed the trees around it.

### The Confession
"You're going to die for what you've done to me," Vythorn said. "And for what you did to my family."

The Shadow King only smiled. He didn't deny a thing:

> **The Shadow King:** "Well, it only took you about a thousand years to put it together, but you got there. Good for you."

He admitted all of it, and told Vythorn not to feel special: "You're not the only orphan that I've created." He had killed many parents of children with a gift for chaos, raised the orphans "under my tutelage," and planned one day to "feast upon the fruits of their labor," siphoning off the chaos energy they spent years refining. Vythorn, though, was the prize. "A certain aptitude for chaos. I was saving you for last. Like a dessert. Oh, thank you for serving yourself up on a platter for me."

### Lightning Against Shadow
Vythorn's rage boiled over in a burst of energy from the words on his skin. He moved with a speed nobody had seen from him before, jumping through space and time and hurling lightning bolts. The Shadow King answered with blades of shadow and ethereal energy that grew from his arms. Lightning scorched Agdrel's flesh, and his shadow blades went straight through Vythorn's side.

**Mortally wounded, Vythorn turned back time.** He retraced his steps and learned Agdrel's movements until he was always one step ahead. The two locked together, lightning against shadow, blinking around the field faster than the Acolytes could follow. The words on Vythorn's arms burned brighter as his rage went white hot.

Then he remembered what [[Master Skreca]] had told him, the testaments, and "the teeth that were gnashing in the hellscapes." He ripped open portals shaped like teeth and maws. Through them lay hellscapes of ash, fire and death, and the tears in reality closed in around the Shadow King.

"What? What is this?" Agdrel demanded.

"**This is your fate.**"

The maws tore off the Shadow King's arm, leaving only a shadowy limb. They bit into his neck and face and stripped away his flesh, his sinew and finally his whole physical being. All that remained was a shadowy figure with yellow eyes: the soul of the Shadow King. Vythorn bound it in place, imprisoned "in this coffin of light."

### The Moment of Doubt
Vythorn held the soul with clenched fists and white knuckles. Behind him, Fil's small voice asked, "Vythorn, what will you choose?"

The question cut through his fury, and his clearer thinking came back. He turned a hateful look on her. "I'm trying to decide if these are my own actions or if you're somehow pulling my strings." Fil asked whether feeling powerful wasn't what really mattered. He had made these choices, "as far as you know." Vythorn would not settle for that: "It's not enough to just feel in charge. I need to control my fate."

That flicker of doubt was all the Shadow King needed. Agdrel, now "an empty soul squirming to survive," slipped between Vythorn's fingers in ethereal shadow form, darted past the gnashing maws, and shot up into the chaos storm clouds. "No, no, no, no, no!" Fil chuckled. The Shadow King's dark, corrupted energy was gone.

### The Grandmaster
"Arbiter!" The Grandmaster ran toward him across the debris, still in chains, and called him by his name. It was strange for him to hear it in this place. "Vythorn! You're here. You're alive. Quick, help me." She showed him the choker sunk deep into her throat. "Please, you have to release me now."

Vythorn's eyes stayed fixed on the sky where Agdrel had fled. When he finally looked down at her, it was with pure hatred. With one hand he pulled her close with his energy. With the other he put a dagger to her throat.

[[Goblins]] and [[Chinchillas|chinchillas]] stormed the field to help her, scrambling over the wreckage. "Vythorn, what are you doing?" "I'm doing what's necessary." He raised a hand and, as Elusia had seen before, the goblins' eyes went white and empty. They stopped a few meters away and formed a ring around the two of them, their minds now his.

The argument that followed had been a thousand years in the making. Vythorn claimed he had done what she could not and defeated the Shadow King. The Grandmaster shot back that he had been killed, and that she had defeated the Shadow King first, back in [[Skypiercer]], before somebody brought him back to life. "You only defeated him because I brought you here," Vythorn said. "Every step of the way, I opened every door for you." She asked who had actually walked through those doors, and where he had been when Agdrel took the throne and when she led the charges to change the Void for the better. He called her Void "weak, easily manipulated," too blind to see the threat he posed. She replied that "just because a threat exists doesn't mean you destroy any hope for anything to exist."

He pushed the knife in and began to cut. **For the first time, he saw fear in her eyes.**

### The Contract
Time froze. Raindrops hung in the air and the goblins stood like statues. Fil was done with talking. "You've made your decision. These talks mean nothing. Don't let her dissuade you. I feel it in you, the potential. Who is it that you want to be? Who are you going to become?"

Vythorn closed his eyes and breathed deeply.

> **Vythorn:** "I am the Lord of Fate. And this is my domain."

Fil sealed it. Their contract was written and the bets were placed. The Void would be his to play with and to practice on, with his own bets to make, under his full command, and "we will be watching to see what you make of it." There was a catch: "Don't fall short of the fate that you have promised us or it will all go away. Because we can do that." She laughed.

Asked what world he would build, Vythorn described a unified Void, a strong nation that would push past its own borders to new planes of existence and challenge [[Champions|champions]] from every realm. "We will conquer them. We will conquer them all." Was he ready to make it so? "It already is."

### Where Her Story Ends
Time started again. Vythorn stopped the blade. In Elusia's frightened face he saw a friend, even if they stood on opposite sides now. Sure that he had already won, he signaled the goblins. "I won't kill you today. But this is where your story ends. This is where your fate leads you."

"My fate, my fate!" The Grandmaster fought back, but the goblins locked a second set of chains on top of the first. They were Vythorn's own old chains, the heavy, clanking ones he had worn as punishment for his last attempt on her throne. She had to be dragged from the arena.

Then Vythorn's eye fell on one goblin in particular: a scrawny one that ran on four legs, had a short snout and obviously struggled to breathe. He lifted the imposter off the ground with magic, spun him around and stripped off the goblin outfit to reveal [[Doug the Pug|Doug]], the Grandmaster's missing pug. "Agdrel's too stupid to notice, I suppose, but I'm not. You can join her." Doug was shackled and marched off with his mistress.

### The Lord of Fate Addresses the Void
The Acolytes were furious. Hauling off the Grandmaster while she was still in chains struck them as unfair, "not in the spirit of chaos, not in the spirit of honor." They poured out of their seats, dropped down from the Coliseum, jeered, threw things and charged at him. Vythorn raised his hands and froze them all in place.

Then he spoke to them. The Grandmaster was weak, he said. She could not defeat the Shadow King, and he had. "By the rules of our Deathmatches," by everything the Grandmaster herself proclaimed, he was the rightful ruler. He promised to carry the Void's power beyond its borders, promised splendor and glory, promised to be fair. Under the Lord of Fate, he would decide for all of them.

> **Vythorn:** "You don't have to love me. You don't even have to like me, but you will all serve me. Welcome and good night."

## Notable Moments
- The Testaments of Morir's Cataclysm burned their words into Vythorn's skin and fused with his chaos power.
- Vythorn, mortally wounded mid-duel, simply turned back time and fought again until he was one step ahead.
- Living, toothed portals into hellscapes ate the Shadow King's body down to his yellow-eyed soul, and the soul still escaped in the moment Vythorn spent wondering whether Fil was pulling his strings.
- The Grandmaster was led off in the Arbiter's own old chains.
- Doug's goblin disguise had fooled the Shadow King, but not Vythorn. At the table, the Grandmaster's player admitted she had hoped for "a doggy rescue situation," and the Arbiter's player replied, "I spotted Doug a mile away."
- An entire crowd of outraged Acolytes was frozen mid-charge for the new ruler's first speech.
