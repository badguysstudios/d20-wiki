## What happens
### Opening narration: falling through space and time
1. **0:00:08** The recap. Vythorn has recently left his companions. He opened a portal that separated him from them while they were "hidden still within the mirror". **"While he betrayed them, now separate, secretly leaving them, he holds his commitment still to return to the void, to return back to Elusia's side."**
2. He falls through space and time with blue and green auras all around him, but **he feels nothing**: no breeze, no wind, not even air when he breathes. Each time he reaches out to the aura, it slows. The blues and grays fade to white, then black, until his feet find ground.

### The swamp
3. **0:01:40** His soles sink and water seeps in ("Your shoes were not meant for this. Desert life may have suited them well, but here, it is moist."). He is in a **cool, humid swamp with no life at all**: no fish, no bugs, no gnats. There are gnarled trees and dead branches, barely any light, **a black, murky river**, and **a small dock with a little boat and a lantern at the end**. He is "not in the void."
4. **0:03:23** Vythorn looks around and feels **the sludge squish into the gaps of his Italian leather boots**. He goes to the dock to work out whether he has ever been here. History check: he knows **without a doubt he has never been here**. The place looks incredibly untraveled and hard to reach, and the dock clearly hasn't been used in a long time. **The black, slimy droplets on his fingers have not come off.** They cling as if suctioned to his skin.
5. **0:05:33** Each plank creaks and moans as he walks, "as if you've ripped open a crack." The trees begin to move and part, and **the river widens**. The boat gives only a little when he steps in, because **the liquid is much denser than water**. **The lantern brightens as he steps in.** There is **one oar**.
6. **0:06:50** The lantern hangs on linked chains and can be moved from front to back. It is beautiful and ornate, but **its glass is frosted**, so he can't see what gives off the light.
7. **0:07:54** He crouches by the lantern and studies the substance, trying to tell whether he is on the material plane or still connected to **the Sands of Eternity**. Arcana: **the substance is living** (it still clings to him), with a slight electric magical charge. In the lantern light he thinks he sees something familiar.
8. **0:09:20** In the warmth of his skin and the lantern light, **the dollop grows to the size of his palm**. Inside it swirl **magical black and glitter gold, with faces and eyes looking back at him** before they are swept back inside, "torn between looking at you and the light." He flings it off, and it splatters into the river ("Interesting, but I don't really have time for that").
9. **0:10:39** He decides to follow the current toward civilization, rowing canoe-style. **He puts the lantern as far from himself as possible, at the front of the boat**, because he suspects the light and warmth set off the sludge.
10. **0:11:53** As the oar cuts the water, **droplets splash the lantern** and small dots begin to cover it. Survival check: 4. "As a scholar, being in a boat and knowing how to use one is a rather new experience."
11. **0:12:51** **He throws the paddle down in frustration and casts gust behind the boat.** "You can cheat your way, oh wise scholar, or outthink that of the boat." It works: the boat "suddenly you've got an engine to this sucker". It flies down the river with a disruptive booming sound.

### The three-way fork
12. **0:14:26** The river splits into **right, left and center**. He looks for signs of a traveled path (insight 18). **The right branch is darker, with lower branches and fog sitting on the water.** **The other two are brighter and faster, with glimmers of light ahead**, "little lanterns or fireflies or stars," hidden by fog.
13. **0:15:40** He mutters **"smarter, not harder"** and steers the gust toward the fastest, brightest path, the **left**.
14. **0:16:03** The boat careens down the river. The lights stay vague, "as if you've lost glasses… poor grandpa can't find them," but they give a warm glow. **The river widens until he can't see either bank.**
15. **0:17:25** There are no stars and no branches, only fog and lights. "You could be out in the middle of the ocean." Because he is going so fast, **the black material keeps splashing onto the lantern and sticking, until three quarters of his light is covered.**

### The ink creature
16. **0:18:28** He shifts forward to wipe the lantern (sleight of hand 17) and clears a few droplets. But so much has built up that **it pushes back, almost aggressively, and climbs up to hide the rest of the panels**. Only an inch of light remains. In that dim light he sees **the murk seeping over the boat, "as if claws and tendrils pull it down."**
17. **0:20:06 — Dispel magic.** Bec asks how he feels. Inside, Vythorn is getting apprehensive but stays composed and refuses to act rashly. **His eyes glow "that milky void," multicolored lightning crackles at his fingertips, and he fires a small bolt of purple electricity.**
18. **0:21:45** **The substance laughs at him.** It **forms a mouth and eats the magic**, then grows larger. It leaves him "just a sliver of light" as if mocking him. The side lights are gone, and all that remains is one little light and "a creature laughing at you."
19. **0:22:49** Vythorn: "What are you?" The creature: **"What am I? Oh, you should ask who we are. That's the question."** "I don't have time for your games." "It's not a game." It crawls down the side of the boat toward him, a silhouette against the light behind it.
20. **0:23:31 — Witch bolt.** "Allow me to reveal your true nature." **He reaches into the sky and pulls the clouds backwards and forwards in time, drawing a storm that once hung over this place, and calls a witch bolt down onto the creature.** He feels the familiar magic gather, then **nothing happens**. **"Oh, they didn't give you permission to do that, did they?"** The slime slithers up his ankles and calves.
21. **0:24:42** "I don't need your permission." **He takes off his boots to escape. The creature "will happily gobble your boots up."** *(Vythorn loses his Italian leather boots.)*
22. **0:24:47–0:25:56** The creature: "Why you? Why are you here?" It says **"We are trapped, and now you are trapped,"** and it pins both his legs. The boat sinks to the level of the river. It wraps his torso and offers to "take you somewhere else… You have to be specific." Vythorn: **"I need to get back to the Grandmaster. Let me go."** "Oh, the Grandmaster. Did you hear that? He wants to go back to the Grandmaster. Why?"
23. **0:26:04 — The dagger.** Magic has failed, so he **draws a dagger and stabs**, on a "dirty 20", right where its head should be, judging by its voice. **The murk engulfs his fist and wrist; he seems to go all the way through it, and it keeps laughing.** "Silly boy, silly boy… Come with us and you will stay here forever."
24. **0:27:29** It does him no harm, but the pressure is suffocating, "like a python slowly beginning to crush you."
25. **0:27:55 — Blink.** Panicking now and fearing he'll die here when he needs to reach the Grandmaster, he focuses all his magic and tries to **blink into the ethereal plane**. **The creature appears in his mind: "We told you. They didn't give you permission to do that. That's not how it works here."** Vythorn: "Then educate me." "Then come with us… You have to just let go."
26. **0:29:17 — Letting go.** **He lets go.** The slime becomes "like a warm blanket pulled up to your neck," and gentle fingers caress his cheeks: "Trust us if you can." He is pulled under and floats weightless in pitch black. Then **the light from the lantern comes to him, dancing in a small infinity symbol**, hypnotic: **"It's time. Wake up."**
27. **0:30:57** He breathes in water. He follows the light upward through what feels like an ocean until he breaks the surface, gasping.

### The lake and the tower
28. **0:32:14** The light floats into the sky. **A lone tower stands on its own carved-out piece of land jutting into a lake.** The swamp is gone. The tower looks welcoming, with flickering lights and shadows moving inside.
29. **0:33:35 — The swim.** He rolls a natural 1 to swim. "You spend a lot of time in libraries… maybe even learning and reading about how to swim, but you've never done so a day in your life." He flounders and splashes. After two or three feet **the water is only two feet deep, "as if the ground itself has risen just to make you look like a fool." "Fortunately, as far as you know, no one else can see this."**
30. **0:34:41** He stands up soaking wet and bootless, trudges out very unhappy, drops to his knees by the shore to recompose himself, then sets off for the tower: "we can worry about that later."
31. **0:35:59** **A small black head bobs in the lake with nothing where its eyes should be.** Its voice dances across the ripples: **"If they don't like you, you can always come back to see us."** Vythorn: **"I'll be back to see you. And I'll dance on your grave!"** Bec: "Real mature. You feel good about that?" "Yeah, I feel great about that."
32. **0:36:47** Perception 17: **soft music comes from the top of the tower.** There are lights up high, a door, and a little cart, but **the grass is undisturbed. There are no trails at all**, as if no guests have ever come and gone.
33. **0:37:50 — Testing his magic.** He tries prestidigitation, **snapping his fingers to turn back time on his clothes to before he was soaked.** "Vythorn, the endless scholar, you go back to your roots and you bend time itself." **It works.** His clothes are clean, pressed and wrinkle-free. He is still bootless.
34. **0:39:16 — The socks debate.** He wears no socks with his boots. "Good boots don't need socks." Bec: "Good boots do need socks." "Oh, if they're made right… Oh, if they're Italian." Bec: "I don't know enough about Italian boots, but that doesn't sound right to me."
35. **0:39:27** **A rock whizzes in and hits him in the back of the head**, and a voice shouts, **"they gave you permission to do that."** He doesn't turn around to dignify it. He clenches his jaw and walks on.
36. **0:40:05** The wooden front door has two lit torches beside it. **Seven lanterns hang in the windows, much like the lantern on his boat.** He immediately trusts the place less and casts **protection from evil and good** on himself, against "aberrations, celestials, elementals, fey, fiends and undead". Bec: "You think you do that?" "Yeah, I try to do that at least." *(It's unclear whether the spell took effect.)*
37. **0:41:15 — The knock.** He knocks loudly (strength 17; Bec: "Oh, Paige Turner…"; Drew: "I know how to knock on doors"). It's a slow, steady pounding: "less of a polite knock and more of a just like, open the fucking door." His knuckles end up sensitive. Upstairs there is still movement and chatter, but nobody comes to the door.
38. **0:42:48** **The door has no handle.** It is plain wood from top to bottom. He pushes it and it swings open easily.
39. **0:43:09 — The tree.** Inside is a bare circular stone room with a dirt floor. Behind the door, **roots spread from the base of a massive tree growing inside the tower**: young, vibrant, strong and sturdy. There is no ladder and no stairs. The tree climbs to the top floor, where there are a few broken boards and the sound of chatter. It also branches out of the tower ("the backyard is by far more exciting"). He can't tell which came first, the tower or the tree. It has brightly colored leaves.
40. **0:45:53** He **climbs the tree** (athletics 16). **Being barefoot helps; his toes grip the trunk.**

### The gathering in the in-between
41. **0:46:46** At the top is **a beautiful, enchanting room**: the tree blossoming inside it, **books everywhere**, a warm hearth, and an ornate ceiling full of lanterns. It is crowded with **15 to 20 individuals**: aarakocra, halflings, elves and more, reading, chatting by the fire and sipping tea.
42. **0:48:10** **No one pays him any attention.** They bump him with shoulders and elbows but never meet his eyes, "oblivious to your existence or uncaring."
43. **0:48:28** "Excuse me, can anyone tell me where am I?" **All conversation stops at once.** Everyone freezes mid-action and every eye turns to him. Unsettled, he says he needs to get home and promises payment when he arrives.
44. **0:49:30** The crowd answers one by one:
    - A small halfling: "Oh, aren't you important? Excuse us."
    - An elf: **"We were expecting you. We wanted you to be here, but sure, we can bump you up on the agenda. It just wasn't your time yet."**
    - A large, boisterous man: "Come, fine. Be front and center. The gentleman says he has places to be and that he'll even pay us."
    - **The whole group laughs like a choir, each on a different note, in sync.**
45. **0:50:27** Vythorn: "The Grandmaster is in danger and I need to get back."
    - An aarakocra: "Oh, we've never heard of trouble before. Trouble never exists." Vythorn: "We have been in very different places then."
    - A small old tortle: "Why is yours any more important than anyone else's?" Vythorn: "It's not my trouble… The Grandmaster is in danger. The Void is in danger."
    - A mouse-like creature with large white ears: "Why do you care about them?" "Why do you care why I care?" **"Well, we're your way back."**
46. **0:51:50** More of them speak:
    - A snake-like creature slithers between his feet and nearly trips him: "This is our home that we've invited you into."
    - **A gigantic nine-foot woman, her head brushing purple branches and flowers among the lanterns:** **"Here is my home. It is in between all… We exist between every plane, every time, every space, in between."**
47. **0:53:09** Vythorn: **"Am I still dead?"** A little boy crawls out: "No, no, you're not dead… it's not really a dead place, but it is an end if you want it to be or if you can't move forward."
48. **0:53:43 — The winged woman.** At the back sits a woman whose eyes have never been on him; **she stares at the floor**. She has **long, soft chestnut-brown hair, brown cheeks, and long white wings folded behind her.** "Vythorn, breathe. We've asked you to be here. And you are already eager to leave."
49. **0:54:35** "Do I know you?" She asks the others whether they know him:
    - **A leonin: "I know you."**
    - **A human male with bright blonde hair: "I've known you too."**
    - **A bear with white fur: "I've known you."**
    - The woman stands: "See, we've all known you. We have been waiting… For you, Vythorn."
50. **0:55:34** Exasperated, she goes to **her little brewing cauldron** and holds out her empty glass, and an elf quickly refills it. The elf: "We've all been sitting here waiting for you for your right time. And now that you're here, you just demand everything."
51. **0:56:10 — The blank books.** He secretly scans the nearest books (stealth 15) and gets away with it, looking only "lost in thought." **Every wall holds books, scrolls and old leather tomes, but not one has anything written on the spine. Even the open books the patrons are discussing have blank pages.**
52. **0:58:02** "Fine. You've been waiting for me. What can I do for you?" "Take a seat. Have a conversation." **A small cat pushes at the backs of his heels.** Vythorn: **"I'm allergic to cats."** He sits anyway.
53. **0:58:38** The woman: "Finally. Vythorn [transcribed "Skythorn"], you seem exhausted. Can I get you a drink?" "If I say no, are you going to get me one anyway?" **A drink is already in his hand**, placed there by a small owl-like woman with kind blue eyes and snowy features. It's warm.
54. **0:59:50** "What is it that we want?" She and the room laugh. **"We have no needs. We are here to help you, actually."** "In the way that we want." Vythorn: "If you're only helpful in the way that you want, is that truly very helpful?" **"Ah, I see why you've called yourself the Endless Scholar. You ask so many questions and you think you're smarter for it."**
55. **1:01:05 — How he got here.** A small tabaxi pops up over the armchair at his right shoulder: **"We are in the in-between, and as you were going from one space to another space, we decided to just reach out and pluck you. It was so easy. Fishing's harder, actually… we see ourselves in you."** The woman: "You have to learn to ask better questions."
56. **1:02:09** Why stop him from getting home? **"You're not ready to go home… We see potential in you… It's murky. It's unclear. You need focus. That's what we provide."** Vythorn: **"What I need is to get back to the Grandmaster before the Shadow King murders her."**
57. **1:02:49** A small lizard-like creature with an eyeglass pats his left hand: "That is not what you need. You think you need that… because you think that you're smart, but you're not that smart… You sit and you sit and you sit and you think."
58. **1:03:32** He bristles. The woman says they meant no insult: "You are intelligent… Read so many books, probably more than even in this room… there is always more to learn." Vythorn: "Which are the right questions?" "That is very smart."

### "Who are you?"
59. **1:04:25** She asks who he is. **"My name is Vythorn. I am the Arbiter of the Dread Fortress, right hand of the Grandmaster."** "Oh, these are titles… **Half those you have actually given yourself… All of those you've given yourself.** Don't hide behind those titles. Just like these books here… Let's get into your pages a little bit."
60. **1:06:04** "I am a scholar. A mage… What else is there? **I'm not much of a bowler.**" The leonin lets out a huge chuckle. "What is the growth you've made?" Vythorn: "I've worked my way from nothing to the right hand of the Grandmaster." "That's a lot of fast forwarding… who were you before all of those fancy titles that you wear like clothes?… Where did you begin?"
61. **1:08:03 — The drink.** **For the first time he drinks.** It is **sweet with cinnamon, warm, "like a comfort of Christmas on a cold day."** He stares at the cauldron, then locks eyes with the winged woman: **"I don't have a home. Is that what you want to hear?"** She replies: **"To know where you're going, you need to know exactly where you've been."**

### The first memory: the burning house
62. **1:09:01** The drink makes him drowsy and dreamlike. **He is somewhere familiar and sees a young boy who looks a lot like him.** She asks what he sees. **"I see death."** "For a young boy already… What young boy sees death so early?"
63. **1:10:46** The dream is made of cloud and takes the form of whatever he pictures, as soon as he thinks it. Vythorn shapes the fog: **a young boy in great distress, standing lost in the rain as if just waking from a dream. His hands are on fire, but his flesh doesn't melt; the fire is magical and under his own control. Behind him a large two-story house is burning, with screams coming from inside.**
64. **1:12:48** "You've always had a lot of potential from the beginning, haven't you?" Vythorn: **"I suppose my family would say too much potential."** "Dangerous, weren't you?" **"Still am."** "That danger. That left you alone, didn't it?" "Never really alone. There's always people around."
65. **1:13:31** The scene breaks. **A polar bear** is in his face, with deep brown eyes and a wet nose, snarling a little: "Who stays with you long enough to call home, to call friends, to call family?" Vythorn: **"I've had many friends. I've had teachers and for a time even the Shadow King himself I would have called friend."**
66. **1:14:34** The lizard taps his bare foot with a claw: **"Friends don't put friends in chess [chests?]."** Vythorn: "A lot happened between those two times… You obviously don't understand the intricacies of friendship."
67. **1:14:59** The cat, now on the back of his chair, asks him to explain. He says: "Sometimes you have to make the hard choice for the greater good." She taps his head with her tail: "You didn't put friendship first, did you?" **"So I was supposed to, what, help Agdrel destroy the Void?"**
68. **1:16:07** The winged woman: **"No, you weren't supposed to do anything. There's no right or wrong. You made a choice… We simply look at why… We do what we do. We are who we are. And we watch. And we bet. And we keep an eye on all these stories."** His story is "fascinating" for its choices.
69. **1:17:02** He side-eyes the cat, and the cat side-eyes him back. The old tortle woman explains: "It's very boring in the in-between. So we follow interesting lines… **Every now and then we're allowed to collectively agree and intervene. And we want to know if we want to intervene on you.**" The loud man at the back explains why: **"For our enjoyment, of course… We sit here all day. We wait all night."**
70. **1:18:25** Vythorn: "My entire life? The lives of others? Just a game to you? Something to bet on?" The winged woman: "Not a game, but it's how we get invested… **when you live as long as we do, well, living isn't the right word**… Most people like it when we help." Vythorn: "I guess most people have a better sense of humor than…" "That we do also agree on… You are very dry-humored." **"I've never seen much use for it."**
71. **1:19:31** **His drink refills itself to the brim.** The giant woman: "Why do you learn, oh endless scholar?" "Why does anyone learn? To know." "You've sacrificed lives, integrity, so much for it… And you do it just because?" Vythorn: **"I don't really know."**
72. **1:20:53** The winged woman leans in with her chin on her crossed hands: "You've put the entire kingdom… in danger. For the sake of knowledge… For knowledge, you've done that before. Haven't you?" "It wasn't a choice I was making. I chose to help those people."

### The second memory: the Shadow King's "death"
73. **1:21:54** She touches his forehead. **He is back at the moment the Shadow King was first slain, "or so we thought."** Vythorn stands over him. **The Shadow King is breathing heavily, his essence hanging by a thread, and Vythorn hasn't pushed him over the edge.** A cautious tabaxi in a scholarly vest, carrying handbooks, asks him to walk her through his process.
74. **1:23:08** Vythorn: **"I… couldn't just let his power go to waste. His knowledge."** "You absolutely knew the risk… He was so dangerous the first time." **"That's why I took precautions."** "If you had chosen to make sure the Shadow King was for sure dead… Even risen from the dead by a necromancer… would limit it. But you didn't do that."
75. **1:24:28** Vythorn: **"Who do you think kept tabs on the Void all of these years? Who do you think kept peace and order while the Grandmaster held her games?… The power allowed me to find ways to make sure things ran smoothly."** The snake pulls at his feet: "You're picking truths." The woman: "There are a lot of truths and we know them. It's interesting to us to hear the ones that you choose to tell."
76. **1:25:44** "Did you really keep all that knowledge for running the void? **The Void's run many times by itself without that. In fact, centuries.**" Vythorn: **"Without me, the Shadow King would still be in power or worse. I'm the one who brought Elusia back… brought her across the planes, brought her home, made sure she could get into the Dread Fortress."** "You are very important, aren't you?"
77. **1:26:49** Vythorn: **"Had I not taken the Shadow King's power, how long would her reign have lasted? She's a great figurehead, very inspirational. Inspiration only goes so far."**
78. **1:27:10 — The seed.** **"The Shadow King himself, he works off of the threads of minds. The overthrowing of Elusia wasn't just his idea, not when you did it. That was a seed that was already planted by you."** Vythorn: "I had no intention to overthrow the Grandmaster." **"Never? You were never dissatisfied by her rule. Just like you were never dissatisfied by her father's rule."** Vythorn: **"I don't know, maybe? Sometimes, who's not?"**
79. **1:28:12** "Keep making the choices that you make. We love that. But know why you make them." "Why don't you tell me?" **"No. Mortals aren't privy for that."**

### "What is it that you dream of?"
80. **1:28:34** What happens if they send him back? He doesn't know: "I need to find a way to defeat the Shadow King." "Then what?… What is it that you dream of, Vythorn?" **"I just… I don't know."**
81. **1:29:46** **An angry owlbear flips the table with the brew, snuffing out some candles.** "Ugh, I hate it when they do this. Just wing it… The inconsistencies." The winged woman calms them.
82. **1:30:39** Her wings open as she grows more agitated: **"You kept the Shadow King in a box, you go from plane to plane, you've sacrificed people and their own lives and their own dreams. For what?… Losing your parents to your own abilities… You barely have a friend or family member to stand by your side… It can't be by accident."** "You fought a creature you knew nothing about and you had no power to do so, yet you kept fighting… It's not for friends. It's not for a loyalty of your country. Don't tell me that it's for knowledge for knowledge sake."
83. **1:32:20** Under his breath: **"I want to control fate."**
84. **1:32:42** Silence. Then a soft chuckle, and **the whole room erupts in laughter at "the funniest thing that you possibly could have ever said."** The lizard slaps his foot, heaving. "Fate!"
85. **1:33:23** Vythorn *(partly garbled)*: **"I will be the God of Fate. And I will control what happens to me in my life. I will decide. I will decide for everyone."**
86. **1:33:48** The winged woman looks him in the eye for real: "You want to be the god of fate. That's a tall order… We don't care about your titles. It's the choices you've made. **As the God of Fate, what choice will you be making?**" She lights up, energized. Vythorn: **"The only choice that matters. I'll choose to move the pawns instead of being one."** *(Then, to the laughter: "It's not funny.")*

### The reveal: the God of Fate
87. **1:34:58** "You asked earlier… who we are." The cat: "Some call us **desire, some ambition or providence**." The bear: "**Risk, destiny, luck, hope even, fortune, demise, even fate.** You see, we are many things. **And we are one.**" **Every being in the room loses its shape and is pulled into the winged woman.** Only the two of them are left in an empty room. **"You want to be us."**
88. **1:36:17** Vythorn: **"I suppose I want to kill you."** "What was never alive cannot be killed." **"Then you won't mind."**
89. **1:36:35** She looks serious: **"The only power you have here in the in-between is that which I give you."** **She offers him control of "the fates of all of those in the void."** Not all fates of all times and spaces, "but… much of what you desire." Vythorn: **"It's a start."**
90. **1:37:21** "If you impress us with your choices, then perhaps you'll get elevated… **You could be one of us.**" They too make choices, intervening when they want. **The task: a special tome that "notes power of all sorts. Chaos and time and luck. All things that you need."** He is to find it as "an adventure." **"Keep your focus, for if you falter in your vision, we will make sure your dream does not come true. Or anything else you want. That is the bet you make with us."** She holds out her hand.
91. **1:39:34** He stares into her eyes, unblinking: **"My vision has never been clearer,"** and takes her hand.
92. **1:39:49** **"Good. Now go find the Testaments of Morir's Cataclysm."** **She blows on him. A wave of gust pushes him back, and he blacks out.**

## Post-show recap
Bec calls this "this post-show recap." It has no "Welcome to Dried Blood" marker. Drew and Bec talk about the episode out of character. The only in-world material is Bec naming the God of Fate and giving some backstory, which is marked as creator lore below.
- **The boots:** Bec: "As a DM, I consider this entirely successful based on that loss alone."
- **Design:** Bec held back information on purpose, so Drew would have to be active and make choices. That was "the first part of the test" from the God of Fate: how Vythorn makes choices and reacts. She notes the swamp mud as a metaphor for getting stuck in decisions.
- **Drew on Vythorn:** he is naturally curious but presses on. "Even though he's been killed and just come back from the land of the dead," he feels invincible at times and full of self-confidence. Bec credits Drew's real-life Eagle Scout background for the careful checks at the fork.
- **Illusion of choice:** the left and center branches would have made no difference. **The right branch loops back, and he would have been offered the choice again.** "It didn't matter which choice you made at all." It never occurred to Vythorn that his choices might not matter.
- **The ink creature:** Bec had no idea how he was supposed to escape until it became clear. She wanted Vythorn to have to give in to someone else's will. **The people in the black liquid stand for being worn out by decision-making and getting stuck supporting other people's goals**, and Bec ties it to her own experience of depression ("am I just going to… get myself McDonald's or Wendy's today?"). Drew: for Vythorn it wasn't defeat but logic. He had "solved for all of the potential variables," it was a trick question, and letting go was "the only thing that makes sense."
- **The lantern** stands for Vythorn's vision. It shrank because he was moving too fast.
- **Vythorn and Elusia (creator interpretation):**
  - Drew says that in Vythorn's mind he is her equal in contribution. He brought her back, opened the door to the Dread Fortress, and "runs the logistics of the Dread Fortress, does the taxes and things."
  - Bec adds that he pulled together the armies, and Elusia "only had to train them with her three individuals."
  - Drew concedes that Elusia led the army, inspired the people and killed the Shadow King, and that Vythorn overinflates his part: "I put all the pieces on the board… but I'm outside of it."
  - Bec: Vythorn is fine with Elusia's decisions because he feels he gave her the chance to make them, "a very much so God of Fate power."
- **Image:** Bec says Vythorn cares how the powerful see him. Drew: he is "famous to famous people." He works in the shadows, isn't used to "existing in the light," and has probably never said "I want to be the God of Fate" out loud before.
- **Shadow King's escape:** Drew thinks Vythorn sees it as a testament to the Shadow King's ability rather than his own failure. He feels a little infallible.
- **Age:** Drew calls Vythorn "really, really old… thousands of years." Bec: "a baby for the God of Fates."
- **Open threads (Drew):** can Vythorn keep working under the Grandmaster now? "Really hard to be the god of fate if you are… doing the taxes at the Dread Fortress." Will his intentions stay out in the open?
  - Bec teases that he worries about consequences from the God of Fate, "and not with the Acolytes, Doug, [E]lusia." Drew: "nobody cares about the Acolytes."
- **The God of Fate's name (creator lore):** **"It's Philomena. Phil for short."** Bec first planned **"Temora," going by "Tim,"** but dropped it as too close to Monty Python and the Holy Grail.
- **Phil's backstory (creator lore; Bec: "This won't impact the story at all"):**
  - Phil was once a single god of fate in the early eons, watching life begin and offering luck and intervention to make dreams come true.
  - That grew boring, so the interventions became arbitrary.
  - Out of boredom, **Phil split themself and pulled in multiple individuals**, who bid on stories and intervene by luck or interest.
  - They have always existed. "Living and dying… mean nothing to them." Ultimately they are one.
- **The masks:** the beings are meant to recall favorite characters "who have made very interesting choices," worn "like masks, costumes."
  - Drew: "The bear resembles **Orin** a good deal… and then shout out to **Gitsy**."
  - Bec: Gitsy "has a very interesting story that got her there." Gitsy "was known to not take her own fate into her own hands and she notably followed the dreams of others while she was alive in that story. I have other plans for her."
  - Phil herself is the chestnut-haired woman with big white wings. A line in Bec's segment, probably Drew interjecting: "I didn't know she's supposed to be reminiscent of the Grandmaster."
- Drew: "Vythorn is Alice in Wonderland." Bec: "Are these people all on drugs here?" Drew got to "play purposefully dense."
- **Next time "you will have some friends joining you."**
- Drew's sign-off: "Suck it, nerds, and good night."

## Who and what appears
### Characters
**[[Characters/The Arbiter|Vythorn, the Arbiter]]** (Drew)
- **Status:** he left his companions inside the mirror through a portal, which the narration calls a betrayal. He is sworn to return to the Void and to Elusia. He asks "Am I still dead?"; the in-between is "not really a dead place."
- **Losses and habits:**
  - **Lost his Italian leather boots** to the ink creature and is now barefoot.
  - Wears no socks with good boots.
  - Can't paddle a boat and **can't swim** ("never done so a day in your life").
  - Allergic to cats (or claims to be).
  - "Not much of a bowler."
  - Has a dry sense of humor and has "never seen much use for it."
- **Magic:**
  - Time-bending. His prestidigitation turns back time on his clothes; his witch bolt drags a past storm into the present.
  - His eyes glow milky void and multicolored lightning crackles at his fingertips when he casts.
  - **In the in-between his magic works only with permission**: dispel magic, witch bolt and blink failed; gust and prestidigitation worked.
- **Childhood:** his earliest shown memory is a young boy in the rain, hands burning with magical fire he controls, outside a burning two-story house full of screams. Phil says he **lost his parents to his own abilities**. "My family would say too much potential."
- **Relationships and history:**
  - Had teachers and many friends.
  - **Once would have called the Shadow King (Agdrel) a friend.**
  - **Spared the dying Shadow King to keep his power and knowledge**, kept him "in a box," and used that power to keep order in the Void while the Grandmaster held her games.
  - Claims he brought Elusia back across the planes and into the Dread Fortress.
  - Phil says he was at times dissatisfied with Elusia's rule and with her father's rule, and that **the idea of overthrowing Elusia was a seed he planted**. He answers: "maybe? Sometimes, who's not?"
- **Ambition:** **"I want to control fate… I will be the God of Fate."** He accepted Phil's bargain: control of the fates of everyone in the Void, possible elevation, and the quest for the Testaments of Morir's Cataclysm.

**[[Characters/The God of Fate (Philomena|The God of Fate (Philomena, "Phil")]]** (Bec). Named in the post-show.
- **Nature:**
  - Exists "between every plane, every time, every space." "What was never alive cannot be killed."
  - Many and one: desire, ambition, providence, risk, destiny, luck, hope, fortune, demise, fate.
  - Watches, bets on and sometimes collectively intervenes in mortal stories for enjoyment, because the in-between is boring.
  - "Plucked" Vythorn out of his plane travel.
- **Forms shown in the tower:** halfling, elf, a large boisterous man, aarakocra, an old tortle woman, a mouse-like creature with big white ears, a snake-like creature, a nine-foot giant woman, a little boy, a leonin, a blonde human man, a white bear / polar bear, a cat, an owl-like woman with blue eyes, tabaxi (one scholarly, with a vest and handbooks), a lizard-like creature with an eyeglass, and an owlbear.
- **Final form:** a chestnut-haired, brown-cheeked woman with long white wings, who brews at a cauldron.

**[[Characters/The inkslime creature|The ink/slime creature(s)]]** (Bec). The living black-and-gold substance of the swamp river, with faces and eyes inside it. It eats magic, mocks him with "they didn't give you permission," and ate his boots. "We are trapped, and now you are trapped." **It speaks as "we" and tells him to "ask who we are,"** but it's not stated whether it is part of the God of Fate. The light that led him out says "It's time. Wake up." A head in the lake invites him back "if they don't like you." The post-show says these are beings stuck in the in-between.

**[[Characters/The Shadow King|The Shadow King / Agdrel.]]** Seen only in memory: dying and spared by Vythorn. "He works off of the threads of minds." Vythorn fears the Shadow King will murder the Grandmaster.

**[[Characters/Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|Grandmaster Elusia.]]** Not present. Vythorn says she is in danger, and calls her "a great figurehead, very inspirational."

### Places
- **[[Places/The lifeless swamp|The lifeless swamp]]**: a black, dense, living river, a disused dock, a one-seat boat with a chained, frosted lantern. **A three-way fork**: the right branch is dark and foggy and loops back (per the post-show); the left and center are bright and fast. The river widens into a bankless expanse of fog and lights.
- **[[Places/The lake|The lake]]**, deep at first and then only two feet deep. A slime head bobs in it.
- **[[Places/The tower in the in-between|The tower in the in-between]]** ("Here is my home. It is in between all"):
  - On a carved-out spit of land, with undisturbed grass, a cart, two torches, and **seven lanterns in the windows like the boat's**.
  - A handleless wooden door.
  - **A massive young tree grows up through it**, with purple branches, flowers and brightly colored leaves.
  - The top room has a hearth, a cauldron, lantern-filled ornate ceiling, and **books and scrolls with blank spines and pages**.
- Mentioned: the Sands of Eternity, the mirror (where his companions remain), the Void, the Dread Fortress.

### Items
- **[[Items/The Testaments of Morir's Cataclysm|The Testaments of Morir's Cataclysm]]**: a tome that "notes power of all sorts. Chaos and time and luck." Vythorn's quest.
- The boat lantern (light drawn to the slime) and the guiding light that dances in an infinity symbol.
- Vythorn's Italian leather boots (eaten) and his dagger.
- **[[Items/The God of Fate's drink|The God of Fate's drink]]**: sweet, cinnamon, warm "like Christmas." It refills itself and opens memory-visions that he can shape like clouds.

### Lore statements
- **The in-between exists between all planes, times and spaces.** There, the God of Fate grants or withholds power.
- **The God of Fate bets on mortal stories and sometimes intervenes collectively.**
- Phil: "The Void's run many times by itself… In fact, centuries" without Vythorn's stolen knowledge.
- **Phil calls Vythorn mortal** ("Mortals aren't privy").
- Phil: the Shadow King "works off of the threads of minds."
- Creator note: Vythorn is "thousands of years" old.
