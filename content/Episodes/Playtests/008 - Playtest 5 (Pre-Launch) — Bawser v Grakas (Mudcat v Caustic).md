**[[Bawser]]**, a towering yellow brute with a green shell, and **[[Grakas]]**, a wheezing, blue-bearded swordsman who never wanted to be there, met in a drizzly forest arena. Bawser expected to be the scary one. Instead he got out-muscled, out-hidden and out-scared, and spent part of the fight sobbing behind a rock. It ended with a flood, a forest fire, a hug, a burial and a rebirth, and it may be the most tender way any fight to the death has ever finished.

## The Story

### A Legally Distinct Arrival

The match was billed simply as "Bawser versus Grakas." Everyone was very clear that Bawser was spelled "with an A. Very clearly, it's an A… legally distinct from any other intellectual property." The arena was announced as a "completely new, never before seen" forest, which happened to be the same rainy forest as before. It came with a cliff, giant mushroom ledges, tall trees, and a nice drizzle "that makes your skin shimmer and glimmer."

Bawser landed "like a comet," all big yellow body and green shell, and let out a roar that shook the leaves and scattered the birds.

> **Bawser:** "I am claiming this map! It is mine! I'll find you!"

He tried to listen for his opponent in the falling leaves and couldn't hear a thing. So he decided he hated leaves ("Leaves are the worst. They're the literal worst") and tried to set the tree on fire with his tinderbox. The tree was far too wet to burn. He threw the tinderbox on the ground.

### The Man Behind the Tree

Grakas, meanwhile, was hiding behind a tree and having a bad time. "Oh, I don't like this… What am I doing here?" He shook his inhaler and took a puff. "If we just stay behind the tree, nobody can see us." When he tried to climb the trunk, he slipped in mud that smelled faintly of hibiscus.

Bawser bellowed a generous offer: "Grakas, show yourself and I may not tear you to a million little pieces. Heck, I'll even let you keep one of your arms." Grakas was rattled that the monster knew his name, and he shook so hard the tree dropped its leaves. Bawser threw a mud ball in completely the wrong direction. Then he started pelting the spectators with mud pies, demanding, "Show me where he is!"

Grakas answered with an eerie whisper: "You should just go away. You're never gonna catch me and it's not worth your time and you're stupid for trying." Then he scrambled twenty feet up the tree. Bawser followed his muddy footprints to the trunk but couldn't spot him in the branches. In his frustration he punched the cliff and broke off giant mushroom ledges, while Grakas clung to his branch "like a monkey."

### Catching Hands

Grakas dropped out of the tree with his two short swords, his "short sword choppers," and put a curse on Bawser. Bawser went for a grapple. The two locked hands in a dead standstill until the little swordsman shoved him off and slapped his hands away. Bawser was deeply offended. "No, that's my thing. I'm the big bully. I do the big strength thing… What are you doing wrong here?" He flew into a rage.

Grakas then struck with a blow so frightening that the brute recoiled. "How did you become the scary one on the field?" Bawser hurled his spear and javelin, and Grakas cowered behind his crossed swords. So Bawser gave himself a pep talk: "I may not be very strong right now, but I can at least look strong." His sheer presence made the forest tremble. Grakas fumbled completely, lost his advantage, and was terrified in turn, which the table called "a Nudo reverse card." Bawser loomed over him, belly to forehead: "If I were a comforting person, you would actually really like this hug."

### Blurs, Ice and Lightning

Grakas blinked back up the tree in a burst of magic and turned himself into a drunken blur of blue hair and blue beard. "I have a master's degree in computer science. This is not what I signed up for when I took fencing lessons." Bawser promised to "eat you up, spit you out, then eat you again, and then poop you out." His javelin struck home, and his spear knocked Grakas clean out of the tree.

Bawser's frenzied greataxe swing missed the blur and stuck fast in the mud. Grakas started shivering so hard that he coated himself in a layer of ice. Then lightning ripped across the arena, "as if the gods themselves… are disappointed in the fight." It struck both champions. Grakas compared it to Thor "smacking Mjolnir onto the field."

### The Strong One Breaks

Bawser's next swing went so badly wrong that his greataxe flew ten feet behind Grakas. He squished his opponent's blurry face between his claws: "You should have been afraid of me. Why are you stronger than me?" He was close to tears. Being the strong one was his whole identity, and this small wheezing man was out-strengthening him and out-hiding him at once. Grakas's second sword clinked harmlessly off Bawser's shell, and he needed another puff of his inhaler.

Bawser gathered himself, meditated, and breathed fire. Out came a tiny matchstick puff that singed Grakas's eyebrows.

> **Bawser:** "No! I'm supposed to be the strong one! My fire's not even working!"

He tore free of the crackling magical tether Grakas had hooked into him and ran away crying. "You started this," Grakas called after him. "You're a big tough guy with a big tough shell. You're gonna run away?" Bawser wept behind a rock.

### The Dam Breaks

Then a dam burst and the arena flooded. Grakas backflipped to safety on the rocks ("All those tumbling classes are really paying off"). Bawser slipped on the ground his own tears had made slick and was swept to the edge of the map, because "not all turtles can swim." Soaked and miserable, he laid out his life's great grievance: "It has always been… some other small little dude with bright colors always coming to wreck my day. I can't just be my cool badass self by myself now."

The crowd wept with him, "like a bad scene from The Notebook," and their tears lifted his spirit. "You cheered me at the beginning of this fight and I will not let you down." He found his greataxe, raged again, and swore: "For you, my spectators, my fans, I will not fail you until I give my last and final breath."

### A Free Hit

Grakas had a surprising change of heart. "This seems like it means a lot more to you than it does to me… Just go ahead and hit me." He had a master's degree, but he was drowning in debt and the job market was bad, so he might as well let Bawser take him out. Bawser was touched: "No one's ever let me hit them before." He hugged Grakas, lifted him up, and threw him twenty feet into the air. "You are the nicest little thing I've ever met. I will never forget this."

Grakas landed and reconsidered. "That hurt, and I didn't really care for it, so I'm gonna go ahead and fight back now." Bawser offered an upside: "Remember, if I kill you, you don't have to pay your college debts."

### Fire and Ice

Bawser seized him for good ("Thank you, little one. My mother would be proud of me"). Just then more lightning struck and set the whole forest ablaze. Bawser held Grakas in the burning tree and declared, **"We will burn in this fiery hell together."** Grakas's blue hair turned orange as it caught. "Of all the ways that you could die, I always thought that fire would be the worst." His last desperate strike nearly turned the tide, but he collapsed, "like Anakin at the end of Revenge of the Sith."

When Bawser demanded to be named the winner, Grakas gasped, **"You win, you win, you win."** Bawser carried him out of the fire by the neck and gave him the highest honor he knew:

> **Bawser:** "Little one, though you may be wheezy and small and blue, you are mighty… Bao Bao respects you. At any time you may come to my castle and we will feast. As long as you tell everyone that I beat you. That's the one rule."

He doused Grakas with his water skin.

### Born Anew

Bawser then dug a hole and buried Grakas up to his head to heal him: "either it heals you or it holds you." He also decided that "Grakas is a terrible name," and proposed "Baby Bow-Bow 2." The dirt turned out to be medicinal, and Grakas clung to life. Bawser carved "Baby Bow Bow" into the ground in several places and made a promise: "When you awaken, you will be born anew… now you have orange and blue, for you are fire and ice. You shall come be my pet in my castle."

That night, a hand shot up out of the hole. **"I am Baby Bow-Bow!"** The buried man's hair and beard burst into flaming orange, and he walked off, glowing like a torch, toward Bawser's castle.

## Who and What Appears
**[[Characters/Bawser|Bawser]]** (Caustic Phoenix). "With an A… legally distinct." Huge, yellow body, green shell, claws, scales; Caustic Phoenix calls him "a full dragonborn. Ish," a dragonborn with a shell (and jokes about turtles). Barbarian (rage, frenzy, intimidating presence), greataxe, spear, javelin, breath weapon. Calls himself **"Bao Bao" / "Bow-Bow."** Hates leaves. Has a **castle** and a mother ("Mama Bowser"). Insists on being the strong one; cries when he isn't; plays to the crowd. **Winner.** Took Grakas as his "pet."

**[[Characters/Grakas|Grakas]]** (Mudcat). Half-elf hexblade warlock / College of Swords bard; two short swords; **blue hair and blue beard**; asthmatic (inhaler); took fencing and tumbling lessons; **master's degree in computer science** and student debt; cowardly but deadly. Spells: vicious mockery, hexblade's curse, wrathful smite, shield, misty step, blur, Armor of Agathys, lightning lure, booming blade, eldritch smite. Lost, survived, buried and **renamed "Baby Bow-Bow"**; rose with flaming orange hair and beard and headed to Bawser's castle.
