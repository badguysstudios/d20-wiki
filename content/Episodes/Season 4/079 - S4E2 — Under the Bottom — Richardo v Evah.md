## What happens

The fighters stand on **rickety, worn boards with a treasure map in the centre: "perhaps a pirate's cove once used many, many years ago."** Elusia: "Break all their bones, but more important than that is to **break their spirit**. Crush them!" She flies up into the sky.
11. **0:13:36** Acolytes "like angels in the sky" cheer, with **popcorn and blue raspberry slushies**. **The Shadow King sits on a gold throne with garland, on "cushion and cushion and cushion, for he has a very sensitive ass. Or he is a sensitive ass. We have yet to determine."**

### The arm-wrestling misunderstanding

Who's got the strap?" Evah: "This is a joke, right?… I came here to fight, not play little kid games." Elusia: "Ricardo, just think of this as **unregulated on the streets arm wrestling**."
13. **0:16:44** Richardo: "How much gold you got?" Street rules mean they play for gold. Evah: "**This whole place is a joke… You dishonor the art of the fight.**"
14. **0:17:37** The Shadow King stands, and **gold shoots up like a fountain out of the shadows of the torchlight and piles on the table**: "Happy now?" Richardo: "There's no table still, but all right." Elusia tells Evah: "you don't need to wait for permission. Begin."

### Round 1
15. **0:18:52 — Evah rages.** She stomps her feet side to side, squares off, tenses her muscles, and her face goes red: "**I'm going to crush you. Yeah!**"
16. **0:19:33 — The helmet flip.** Richardo goes from dumb to dead serious ("I know what this is"). He **flips his helmet backwards** to "make it special" and rages. He's about 6'2", "normal human-sized," though he's an Aasimar. With the helmet backwards he sees only pure darkness, so he tilts it up to see feet. He walks in a circle and ends up facing the wrong way. Drew: "reminds me of the antelopes on the savannah."
17. **0:22:22** He **jumps off the table, "comin' top rope,"** with no weapon drawn. A **hammer fist to Evah's throat** ("a slap across the throat for tradition") is followed by a **gut punch** to her "rock solid abs." Bec: "Classic one-two punch."
18. **0:24:49** His war cry is "**brush your teeth**" (a meme). Evah sees through it (persuasion vs insight). Richardo: "You see how hard I just hit you? I bet you're feeling that." Evah spits. **Richardo wins the intimidation-off (14 vs 12)**, so Evah takes a bane on her first attack.
19. **0:28:24** Richardo: "Okay, maybe no brushing of the teeth. Smells fine." Bec suggests he lick it off the floor. Richardo: "**He likes the spit. It fuels him!**… It's still warm." Evah: "**I brush them every day. And every night.**" Bec: "Strong teeth leaves her strong bites."
20. **0:30:17 — The X.** Evah attacks recklessly with her **glaive**. She does flashy swirls, twists and turns, then **slashes across his chest ("Not the pecs")**. A second slash crosses the first and forms an **X: "Marks the spot because we're in a pirate cove."** Her **polearm master** bonus attack **stabs right in the centre of the X**. She slams the glaive into the ground: "**This is how you play the game.**"
21. **0:32:34** Richardo admires the bleeding scars: "**I think this will get me some ladies.**" He flexes his arm-wrestling motions while bleeding.

## Dried Blood (after-show)
- Drew hosts. Dan: "There was so much blood, is it actually dried by now?"
- **The blood fountain:** Kat gave Richardo a One Piece-style X scar, so Dan went "straight to anime spray." He moved his chest to hit as many Acolytes as possible.
- Kat's favourite moment was finally breaking the fear; she had planned for charm, not fear. Dan used the fear to "mess around a little bit." Bec: at least a third of Evah's damage came from the arena. **The table skirt / "wooden tutu"** was a favourite; Kat felt "required" to make the table pun.
- Drew: all three play in Runaway Robot (Joel)'s Spelljammer campaign. Kat usually plays a "chaos goblin" and wanted Evah to be "an absolute powerhouse." Dan's usual character there is Helene [?]. Dan normally plays honourable paladins or rangers. Richardo was inspired by a dumb fighter he once played and by arm-wrestling YouTubers (Juji Mufu [?], Devon Larratt [?]). Arm wrestling and strongman are sports where everyone is competitive but "the bestest of friends."
- Kat modelled Evah's attitude on **the Imperium** (wrestling), disrespecting the shot callers. She expected to die, but not to be "insta-KO'd by the Shadow King himself." Bec: "the Shadow King is very similar to an ancient dragon. So very doable."
- **Creator note (Drew):** "The Grandmaster runs the arena in this very honorable way… **people get to live, even the people that die get to come back and live again, and like the Shadow King just has zero of that.**" Chat didn't expect it even though "I killed someone at the end of the first match." Drew enjoys coming up with "the most brutal way I can murder people."
- **The shadow-blade kill** was inspired by the liquid Terminator in Terminator 2 stabbing through a man's mouth.
- **Richardo is based on *Over the Top* (1987)**, a Sylvester Stallone arm-wrestling trucker film; flipping the hat is "like flipping a switch" into berserk mode. Hence a barbarian who flips his helmet but "is stupid enough to where he cannot see." They riff on Stallone vs Danny Trejo (on the "wish list"), Trejo's donut shop and Iron Chef.
- **Final words:**
  - Kat: "I let my actions do the talking for me."

## Who and what appears
### Characters
**Richardo "Over the Top" Johnson** (Danotage)
- **Who he is:**
  - A **fallen Aasimar** barbarian, about 6'2".
  - An **arm wrestler** who arrived thinking the deathmatch was an arm-wrestling match.
  - The Shadow King calls him "Dick Johnson."
- **Fighting style and gear:**
  - **Flips his helmet backwards to rage, and then can't see.** The helmet has a spike.
  - Fights with fists and a one-handed axe that "should be two."
  - Received a **Holy Avenger** (+3, radiant) from the Box of Chaos.
  - His "golden arm" is his right arm.
  - His "final form" is the **necrotic shroud**: shadowy body, sprouted wings, frightening aura. Also uses healing hands.
- **Family:**
  - Has a **wife "back home."**
  - His **mother Juanita** is in the stands. She throws planks "with her strong wrestling arm" and wears a mask. Inferred: she taught him arm wrestling, from "You wanna know who taught me the arm wrestling? Good luck" (0:57:19), which he says in reply to the jokes about his mother.
- **Personality and lore:**
  - Has four nards, kept in his back pocket "only on Fridays."
  - The truck-stop Slim Jim story: 249 of 250 copper, so he threw the shopkeep.
  - Wears off-brand "Lug" sandals.
  - Wants the Shadow King's crown.
  - Carved with an X scar and one "eye."
- **Fate:** Killed by Evah, impaled on her glaive.

**Evah (Gro'Murgosh [?])** (imkattkatt)
- **Who she is:** A barbarian who rages and uses battle-master maneuvers (disarm, parry, grappling strike).
- **Gear:**
  - A glaive (polearm master), which **Elusia gifted the ability to split in two**.
  - Smashed a table into a tutu around Richardo.
- **Personality:**
  - Taciturn: "my actions do the talking." Rarely talks.
  - Despises the Shadow King and his tricks: "the art of the fight."
  - Brushes her teeth morning and night, and has a large spit capacity.
  - Had a dermaplaning appointment.
