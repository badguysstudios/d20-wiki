## What happens
### Opening — the throne room of the Dread Fortress
1. **0:06:16** Bec's narration welcomes the Acolytes to the Dread Fortress. They make their way down "through the spurts of volcano and lava, the heat melting your skins off." Down the hallways, **"chains clanging, a snarky look on her face,"** the Grandmaster charges through and bursts into the throne room.
2. **0:06:50 — The painting session.** She finds **the Shadow King leading a painting session for a horde of small goblins.** An easel is set up with watercolors in greens and blues, but **the only color anyone has used is red.**
3. **0:07:14** The Shadow King: "Elusia, come here. Come tell me what you think of this painting." He says he has "been known to dabble" and thought he would instruct the goblins in the craft.
4. **0:07:28** The Grandmaster looks around. **Kevin has only put red on his canvas** (transcript: "Kevin has just read on there"). **Theodore has painted a beautiful, detailed watercolor skeleton.** She admits the Shadow King has "actually done a pretty good job" teaching them.
5. **0:07:42 — The blood paint.** Shadow King: "Fun fact, the paint is made of blood." "Whose blood?" "That's not important." She looks over and sees **a line of chinchillas that have been donating blood.** "None of them were killed. No chinchillas were harmed in this. I hope." The Shadow King adds: "Yet."
6. **0:08:18 — He forgot the Deathmatch.** The Grandmaster had put it on his calendar: "It happens the same time every single Sunday." He says he is "still learning."
7. **0:08:32 — Don't kill the winner.** She asks him to promise this time that he won't kill the winner (she says "Last time…"). He refuses: "I would like to tell you I could promise that, but I can't." She complains that it's hard to find good champions "when they're all going to die at the end." He answers: "Obviously, if they're dead, they're not very good, are they?"
8. **0:08:51 — The socks.** She asks whether he is even wearing socks. **The Shadow King is barefoot.** She snaps her fingers, and a goblin brings him **fuzzy pink spa socks with green polka dots**. "Oh, those look comfy. Thank you."
9. **0:09:15 — The portal.** A goblin brings up **the sharp amulet. She clenches it in her fist, and blood pours down her arm** as it opens a portal. Two champions walk through: "I give you The Scourge versus Bone Daddy."

### Introductions
10. **0:09:44 — The Scourge.** "Oh, you call my name, beckoning me forth from parts unknown. I have come to do justice, to put right what the Bone Daddy has put wrong. **The Scourge awakens.**"
11. **0:10:17** He objects that "everybody knows **the no-sleeves thing is the Scourge's thing**," and Bone Daddy is stealing his gimmick. He is **a Scourge aasimar** ("literally born"). He condemns Bone Daddy as a "no good necromancer **digging up people's pets and their grandmas**, bringing them here to serve and combat. It's not right."
12. **0:11:03** The Shadow King: "this is maybe the best champion you've brought so far." Scourge: "What do you mean, maybe?"
13. **0:11:10 — Bone Daddy.** He is **six foot seven, with dark hair** and "the amount of charisma to fill a pool of your blood." He tells the Scourge: "I've got a bone to pick with you." He says the town is "only big enough for one no-sleeves wearer, minus the Grandmaster and the King." When he wins, the Scourge will have to tell everyone that Bone Daddy is better: "**And when you do that, you're a loser, and if you don't, you're a liar.**"
14. **0:12:31** Scourge: "Not even gonna dignify that with a response beyond the response I just dignified it with." Bone Daddy: "**You're a dingus** and you don't belong in the same ring as me." The Scourge erupts ("A dingus! Start the match!").
15. **0:12:55** The Grandmaster steps between them and pushes them apart with "her very buff arms." "This is my home. This is the fortress. We're going to take you to someplace where you can truly spill blood."
16. **0:13:15 — The chinchillas.** The Scourge raises his hand because the Grandmaster requires it, then asks: "What's the deal with the chinchillas?"
    - The Grandmaster explains they are an employee perk. **Every goblin who works there gets health insurance, pet insurance, and a pet chinchilla for their mental health.**
    - The Shadow King: "**the chinchillas have their own union, so we're very pro-union here.**"
    - The Scourge asks whether he could come on the payroll afterward, because he "would also enjoy a mental health chinchilla." The Grandmaster: "We'll see if you're worthy first."
17. **0:14:13** Bone Daddy's final words: when he gets his "winning tin shell" (probably "chinchilla"), "I'm gonna add him to my army, my army of undead." The Grandmaster: "Bonus, they do come with armor. It's absolutely adorable and **tiny rapiers. They go through fencing classes.**" The armor and rapiers inferably belong to the chinchillas, from this exchange.
18. **0:14:34** She clenches the amulet in her left hand, and both her forearms are now covered in blood. A second portal opens. Scourge: "**Age before beauty, Bone Daddy.**" He then walks through first anyway: "I suppose I walked into that like I'm gonna walk into this portal."

### The arena
19. **0:15:12** The audience screams.
    - Thousands of Acolytes float ethereally high above, calling for blood and glory.
    - Small children wave little banners, **mothers drink their Slurpees**, and fathers watch the kids ("they're great babysitters").
20. **0:15:50 — Arm fights.** "A very dark dungeon where we have arm fights. You would think it'd be arm wrestling, but **we actually just find random arms from previous deathmatches and then we slap each other till someone dies.**"
    - Blood, arms and bodies are tossed aside around the cavern.
    - **A waterfall** drips "against the rough textures of this concrete."
    - High above sits **a small drink station full of ale.**
21. **0:16:40** The Grandmaster's one task: "kill the other and put on the greatest show we've ever seen here. **Should you fail, you may not leave.**" She flies up to her throne among the Acolytes, which has **"a tiny little cushion where a dog once slept."**
22. **0:17:14** The Shadow King: "now that the fanfare's done, I suppose we should get to the killing each other part." **The Scourge wins initiative.**
23. **0:17:49** The Scourge addresses the crowd: "**a real dad's not a babysitter.** You're not watching them, you're raising your kids." He says the ones "carrying the Scourge's banner, the Black Neathers [?]" are about to be uplifted. The children of fathers cheering "this absentee father" are about to be disappointed.

### Round 1
24. **0:18:39 — Hammer and Anvil.** The Scourge casts **Conjure Animals** and summons **two Quetzalcoatlus**. They "don't look like normal Quetzalcoatlus." **They have jet black skin with white accents, "just like me."**
25. **0:19:23** He **wild shapes into a giant constrictor snake.** The Grandmaster: "We're truly wilding it up in here." Every being in the arena must be named, so he names the birds **"The Hammer and the Anvil, so we can break this guy down between us."**
26. **0:19:45** He moves toward Bone Daddy without attacking: "I want the Bone Daddy! Gaze upon what lay before him." The birds get their own initiative and will act at the end of the round.
27. **0:21:31 — Why he's called Bone Daddy.** Bone Daddy says the fathers in the audience are rooting for him, not "someone named Scourge, if that is your real name."
    - He is called Bone Daddy "not just for **my proclivities in the bedroom and in the dungeon**."
    - He calls the arena "**a nursery for me. This is where I would raise my children.** Would you like to see my children?"
28. **0:22:56** Scourge: "I must apologize, Bone Daddy. I fell asleep in the middle of that long, boring monologue."
29. **0:23:08 — The children.** Bone Daddy casts **Danse Macabre**, choosing skeletons ("Of course, I'm the Bone Daddy, obviously"; "Oops, all skeletons").
    - The Grandmaster loves that "your name aligns with being the raising of the bones on many levels." Bone Daddy: "Every imaginable level."
    - For the rousing speech she grants a d4 for extra skeletons, and he raises **one more**: "he doesn't even need a blue potion to raise bones."
    - The Scourge: "The Bone Daddy is a very attractive man that fills the Scourge with some uncomfortable emotions. I can admit it, I'm man enough to say it." The Grandmaster: many fathers in the stands "feel differently right now."
30. **0:24:56 — The children's names.** "Dabby, Dibby, Dabby, Doobie, and Darcy," then the sixth, **Dino**. Later in the turn he also calls them **Gabi/Gibby** and **Dobby** (see Spelling). The Grandmaster: "Beautiful names that I will not remember… I got Darcy."
31. **0:26:17 — Darcy.** The Grandmaster asks chat for love for Darcy, the final skeleton. She has **a few strands of blonde hair and one green eye** still in her skull, "lucky to have anything honestly organic on her." "Even though she doesn't have lips, you do get a sense of a small smile."
32. **0:27:01** The skeletons carry **short swords** ("Nothing but short bones").
    - **The first skeleton rolls a nat 1.** Scourge: "See, they don't want any part of the Scourge!" Bone Daddy: "**Gabi, you're fired.**"
    - Several skeletons then hit the snake, including a glancing blow and a hit by Dino, for 14 damage.
33. **0:29:19** Bone Daddy raises his arms to the crowd: "This is what you want to see from the bone daddy connecting with his children." The Acolytes throw devil horns as the skeletons "slice and dice towards this anaconda."
34. **0:30:27 — The massage.** Bone Daddy gets behind **Dobby**, the one who missed, and rubs where his shoulders would be: "You got this, Dobby. The first time, it's okay… You get a little nervous in front of the crowd." The Grandmaster: "Are you massaging Bone?" The Scourge holds concentration on his summons.
35. **0:30:57** Scourge: "Is that it for your pitiful first round?" Bone Daddy: "Nice little hot start here."
36. **0:31:35 — The birds' flyby.** Hammer and Anvil make flyby attacks and **both miss**.
    - Bone Daddy lashes out with **his whip**, but "they scurry off like the little cowards they are."
    - For a performance roll, the Scourge describes the scene: the roof is low, and the spectral audience throws off the birds' perspective. They beat their wings, shriek and caw, and "**actually might have knocked the head off of one or two skeletons.**"
    - Bone Daddy: "I don't take too kindly to that." Scourge: "I don't take too kindly to what they just did to my glorious snake hide." Bone Daddy finds it "a little bit weird seeing this voice come out of a snake."
37. **0:33:29 — Snakeskin shoes.** The Shadow King: "**[E]lusia, you know, if that one dies, I could use a new pair of shoes.**" Bone Daddy offers to make boots from the snake. A cobbler riff follows: "I've been known to cobble once or twice in my past… oh we got a cobbler here, put him on the payroll… cobbler the goblin" (speakers uncertain). The Grandmaster: "You can't play favorites like that."

### Chaos event — the metal chairs (0:34:09)
38. Bone Daddy drew first blood, so he flips the coin: heads is the Shadow King, tails the Grandmaster. **He calls heads "in memory of the skeletons who had their heads knocked off."** The Shadow King gets Bone Daddy, and the Grandmaster gets the Scourge.
39. **0:35:11 — The Shadow King's dive.**
    - He stands, cracks his back, and **giant spectral shadow wings flare out from his back**, blotting out the light behind him. Bone Daddy: "Can we talk this out?" Scourge: "does that mean this just became a tag team match?"
    - **He folds up his chair, dives, and whacks Bone Daddy across the face.**
    - He then soars around the audience smacking heads. **He takes the head off Commander Scribd "right off the shoulders."** The Grandmaster: "You've picked on Scrib a lot. Why don't you just take out Cup of Tiefling?" He rips another Acolyte's head off: "**Edo's head pops off!**"
    - Bone Daddy holds concentration.
40. **0:37:04 — The Grandmaster's dive.**
    - She balances on the railing. The Scourge "did not consent to this, but fine."
    - She dives "like a falcon," and **the chair comes down on the Scourge's head and bends around it** ("you do have a rather strong head"). The Scourge rolls to disbelieve.
    - **Tiny Tweety birds circle his head.** Scourge: "**Hey, those aren't the birds I intended to summon.**"
41. **0:38:27** Shadow King: "**I think I'm getting the hang of this, Elusia. That was fun. I see why you like this.**" Grandmaster: "Right? It's actually very exciting." Both champions took 9.

### Round 2 — the Box of Chaos
42. **0:39:41 — Hero points.** The Acolytes loved how both took the chairs and are still standing, so each champion gets a hero point ("plus ultra, if you will, go super Saiyan"). "Use it before you die or we will be angry at you."
43. **0:40:20 — The box lands.** It falls like a comet: a chest wreathed in pink and yellow flames that leaves a small crater.
44. **0:40:34 — The Shadow King explains the Box of Chaos.** "It demands a sacrifice at your leisure. You can give it anything… It's not picky. But in return, it will give you a magic item."
45. **0:41:00 — Fuelling the box.** "It's going to need a soul to fuel it."
    - He locks eyes with the Acolyte **great to be a Gator**, picks him up with a shadowy ethereal hand, and **wrings him like a wet wash rag** with a second hand.
    - "All of his black soul… and blood and other stuff" oozes into the box. The lid flips closed and fire shoots out the sides: "There! It's ready."
46. **0:41:40 — The Scourge's payment.** "As that great thespian bard **Brad Pitt** once said, what's in the box?"
    - The DMs riff on payment methods: "Cryptocurrency?", "It takes Venmo," "It has PayPal," "It's got Zelle."
    - **The Scourge pays with PayPal, plus Bone Daddy's head when this is all over**: "Cash up front with the tip on the end." Shadow King: "Just the tip though." Scourge: "Just the tip of his head."
47. **0:42:57 — The chronometer.**
    - Pink and yellow fire shoots up past the Acolytes, and a stopwatch floats out.
    - The Scourge describes it as **a tiny hourglass filled with golden sand**: "which reminds me of **my own sainted mother watching over the Scourge.**" He has been "blessed since he was a little aasimar with the presence of the divine."
    - It has one charge: roll a d6 to either slow time (an extra action and double speed) or see a warning from his future self (a reroll).
48. **0:44:32** "The Scourge sees no drawbacks and activates the watch immediately." **He slows time** and gets two actions. "I don't need to save it if he's already dead."
    - Bone Daddy: "He doesn't count that high very often." Scourge: "**I don't have fingers to count on currently, all right?**" Bone Daddy: "You just got one tail."
49. **0:45:23** The Scourge lists his grievances: "Bone Daddy has attacked me with **his own absentee children**. I see where they were. They were skeletons." Bone Daddy: "Don't you say absentee. **I am no absentee daddy.**" The Scourge adds: "accosted by a steel chair."
50. **0:45:58 — The glow.** "Now, the true power of the Scourge will be revealed." He activates **Radiant Consumption** and glows with searing light that burns everything within 10 ft each turn. The Grandmaster: "**So let me just get this straight. Your bones were cracked and now you're a glow stick.**"
51. **0:47:07** He strikes at Bone Daddy and **misses**: "I just wanted you to know what's coming for you when you see these fangs." He **heals himself** (15), and **his radiance burns the skeletons**. Hammer and Anvil are out of range.
52. **0:48:45 — The mom joke.** Bone Daddy: "you brought up your mother and I just want to say, rest her soul, but **she did get to learn both reasons why I'm called the Bone Daddy**." Scourge: "You said you weren't gonna repeat that story."
53. **0:49:18 — The giant skeleton.**
    - The skeletons **climb up each other like a ladder onto each other's shoulders**. **Darcy, on top, elbow-drops down through them**, and all six **amalgamate into one giant skeleton** ("a giant godly skeleton").
    - The Grandmaster allows it: "It is not biologically correct in any way whatsoever." Bone Daddy: "Bone Daddy has no regard for anatomical correctness." Scourge: "That is not where tibia or fibia goes, but fine."
    - The amalgam is "human-esque," and the Grandmaster asks that they not put flesh over it.
54. **0:51:05** The giant skeleton (the merged form is called "Darcy" from here on) hits the Scourge and slashes down the snake around its neck, "gutting it."
55. **0:52:02 — Blight.** Bone Daddy **raises skeletal hands from the ground that wrap around the snake**, and a necrotic surge saps its life. The Scourge saves, and "**the divine nature of the Scourge gives me resistance to necrotic damage**," so only a quarter of the damage lands.
    - Bone Daddy (sarcastic): "It is the Bone Daddy's one and only weakness, his necrotic resistance."
    - Drew: "Come to the wrong place, Bone Daddy" (speaker uncertain).
56. **0:54:17** Bone Daddy compliments the Shadow King's artistic ability with the painting.
57. **0:54:46 — Bone Daddy's offering.**
    - When he opens the chest, **a dark shadowy figure with yellow eyes and long pointy fingers crawls out of the lid.**
    - He offers **his complete collection of state quarters, "all 50."** He says Alaska was the hardest to get, being the newest.
    - Scourge: "Hopefully he's got some antibiotics." Bone Daddy: "**Throw it a Wyoming and you got a deal. I got two Wyomings that got your name on it.**" Grandmaster: "Those are collector's items."
58. **0:56:05 — The amulet of health.** Pink and yellow flames rise, and from the shadowy creature's hand floats **an amulet of health** (Constitution 19). Bone Daddy laments that he pushed his Constitution and "now it doesn't seem to matter."
    - He describes it: "**a skull pendant, bleached white in the desert sun, with red bleeding out, bleeding onto Bone Daddy's chest, going down to his navel**," and he gives a little gyration.
59. **0:57:24 — The gyration.** "He's trying to distract me with his gyrations." The Shadow King calls a performance vs insight contest, which Bone Daddy wins. **The Scourge is hypnotized by "the Bone Daddy's bone"** and has disadvantage on his next attack. The Scourge calls him "chaining tibia" (transcript: "He's chaining tibia").
60. **0:58:24 — The air force.** "Sending in the air force."
    - **Hammer** clangs a glancing hit "square, right on."
    - **Anvil** "recalibrates, pulls her wings in, and hurdles from high atop the rafters, **blinding you by the spotlight as she plunges down**." It's a critical hit (likely the nat 20) and lands heavy damage.
    - Scourge: "Probably thought it was gonna be **a work in the park** to eliminate the Scourge."
61. **1:00:37** Bone Daddy holds concentration: "**My girl Darcy's still with me. I love you, Darcy.**" The Grandmaster: "Darcy winks back at you. The only child."
62. **1:01:12** The Scourge to his birds: "**Daddy loves you both.** Thank you for making sure Team Scourge didn't just get slapped around." He apologizes for being hypnotized "by the undulating gyrations, but have you seen this man?" The Grandmaster: "This is why it matters who you surround yourself with."
63. **1:01:59** Break: "think about what you've done tonight. Acolytes, we'll see you after these commercials."

### Round "4"

    - "**The burning flames of the Scourge actually ignite even his own angelic flesh.** Although I have resistance to radiant damage, I do burn."

72. **1:08:58 — The Darcy mech.** Bone Daddy spends his hero point: "**All right, Darcy, it's time to open up.**" **The rib bones open up on Darcy's back and he hops in, wearing her like a mech, "protecting me with her lovely bone."**

73. **1:10:44 — Wither and Bloom.** Bone Daddy quickens **Wither and Bloom** onto the Scourge, Hammer and Anvil: "a con saving throw from all three of you dinguses." **All three fail.** The Grandmaster: "That's what happens when you summon dinosaurs to a tiny arena." His necrotic resistance softens it for the Scourge.
74. **1:12:02** **Darcy runs Bone Daddy into the middle of them** so he can heal from the bloom. A coin flip for an opportunity attack goes to the Scourge (he called tails), but **he misses**. The Grandmaster: "You're moving to the butt of the snake."

    - Bone Daddy has Darcy plunge her sword down the Scourge's back. **It's a nat 1.**

76. **1:15:47** Scourge: "**See, she's starting to understand all those birthday parties and ballet receptions that you missed before raising her from the sleep of the ages.**" Bone Daddy: "**I'm sorry I failed you, Darcy. I failed you.**"

### Chaos event — the shrinkage (1:16:25)
77. The arena turns cold, and a breeze chills them to the bone. Goosebumps rise, "and some shrinkage begins to occur."
    - Scourge: "**Look, it's not always like this. I'm just tired, okay?**"
    - **Everyone and everything shrinks by one size.** Darcy goes from huge to large ("Darcy doesn't look so giant anymore"). Hits do less damage, and **everyone's voices go up in pitch for one round.**
78. **1:17:47** Scourge: "I expressly had **a new voice modulation clause in my contract**." The Grandmaster turns to **Thomas the Goblin**: "Can you just go knock on [the heater] a few times?" He grabs his hammer and runs off.
    - Bone Daddy: "you're really hurting my reputation as Bone Daddy right now."
    - Scourge: "**The snake is usually much larger. Much, much larger.**"
79. **1:18:24** Bone Daddy is now flanked between the birds. **Both Hammer and Anvil hit** (less hard, since they can't fly as far). Scourge: "Please do hurt him, Hammer." Bone Daddy holds concentration. Grandmaster: "The hair's still good. All in place still."
80. **1:20:27** The Grandmaster says the bad things happen "only because of our amazing acolytes… When they tip, when they sub." Scourge: "**You notice the Bone Daddy shows up and suddenly it's all tips and subs.**"

### Chaos event — the Hexadome (1:21:13)
81. The Shadow King rolls a 3 ("Easy come, easy go"), and the Grandmaster shows him "how it's done" with a 14. She lets him run his own event.
82. As everyone grows back to normal size, the Shadow King stands: "Let's make this a little more, uh, comfortable, shall we?"
    - **The floor separates, and below are planks of wood floating on water.**
    - "**Let me introduce you to the Hexadome**": an ethereal, almost electric dome forms over them and stings anyone who touches it.
    - "Close quarters combat here as we begin round number five."

### Round 5 — the kill
83. **1:22:11** Scourge: "the flame of judgment burning, echoing off the Scourge, will soon be your undoing. And yet **I will do you the honor that you would not have done for me. I will bless you with a warrior's death.**"
84. **1:22:55** "Can I get one hit on this man sometime tonight?" With advantage from the flank, **it hits.** Bone Daddy, "for the first time tonight," remembers shield, but too late to matter: "**Some people are more concerned about their performance than the mechanics of the game.**" Grandmaster: "putting on a good show is a big part of it. Make sure they're all satisfied."
85. **1:23:56 — The smite.**
    - "As I constrict my coils around you in a grapple and pull you in close, you will witness the full fury of the Scourge as I unleash **a level four smite**." The Scourge "was not able to unleash his divine fury until now."
    - Grandmaster: "**If only the Bone Daddy used protection.**"
    - He whispers in Bone Daddy's ear: "**Why wouldn't you let me love you, Bone Daddy? Why didn't you love me back?**"
    - His radiance adds to the damage.
86. **1:25:31 — The death of Bone Daddy.**
    - Bone Daddy collapses to his knees and falls out of Darcy's chest. "**Darcy, my girl. Take your daddy for one last ride.**"
    - Darcy drops to one knee and **picks him up in her arms, "and carries him the way that he never carried her. He wasn't there for her."**
    - He puts his hand to her face: "**Darcy girl, I'm sorry. I done you wrong. I never got to do you right. Will you forgive your Bone Daddy?**" **A single tear drops from Darcy's void eye.**
    - "Let's go out the way we always wanted, eh?" Darcy gives a slow nod, **walks to the edge of the wooden platform and takes one final step off it into the void below.** "As Bone Daddy is no more."
87. **1:26:57** The Grandmaster: "A beautiful moment." The crowd cries tears of sadness and heartbreak as the top of Darcy's skull slowly sinks out of sight.
88. **1:27:16** Scourge: "Give it up for the Bone Daddy kids. **You should all fly your Bone Daddy flags with pride!**"

### Aftermath — a piece of Bone Daddy
