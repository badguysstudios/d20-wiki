A whiskey-soaked wood elf fighting to save his Meemaw met a sleep-deprived monk haunted by a giant ghostly cymbal monkey, deep beneath [[The Dread Fortress]]. What followed included an alien abduction, a raccoon named Dolores, a case of rabies, two trips through the [[The Box of Chaos|Box of Chaos]] that rearranged one champion's anatomy twice, and a rude knockoff genie. By the end, **[[Sam Cletus]]** had become Samantha Cletus, the Jolly Chimp had a broken hand, and **[[Ben Zappa]]** was locked in a box with his monkey.

## The Story

### Locked in the Pantry

The night opened with no Grandmaster in sight. [[The Shadow King]] called for Elusia and found her knocking from behind a wooden door. She had gone into the pantry to put away supplies for the monthly employee appreciation party, a big [[Goblins|goblin]] and [[Chinchillas|chinchilla]] affair, and had ended up locked in. The Shadow King doesn't carry keys ("Who's going to rob me? I'm the ruler of this entire place"), so at her urging ("Come on, magic boy") he stretched his fingers into shadowy tendrils, formed a key, and popped the door open. He explained that he was only there for snacks: "Get out of the pantry. I'm trying to get Doritos." She threw a bag of Cool Ranch at his chest.

Reminded that she had a Deathmatch to run, [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] dashed to the throne room, looking far less pressed and ironed than usual. She listed her troubles: the changing of the months, "we've got to let the werewolves out," the party, and "the chinchillas decided to poop everywhere." The Shadow King suggested therapy, adding helpfully, "I killed a therapist once, and it really, really helped." Then her amulet sliced into her fingers, as it does every week, and a portal crackling with bright blue sparks delivered the night's [[Champions|champions]].

### Grandma's Boy and the Monkey Man

Sam Cletus announced himself with a drink in hand: "I'm gonna beat your ass for grandma." Ben had done nothing to his grandmother. Meemaw had "done caught the sickness" and could no longer spit tobacco and smoke at the same time, and Sam wanted the prize money to get her back on her feet, because "ain't nothing gonna kill my Meemaw, except me." When the Shadow King asked whether she'd tried killing a doctor, Sam replied, "We don't listen to no damn doctors, boy."

Ben Zappa wanted something simpler: to be rid of the spirit ruining his life. It was like the antique-store monkey toy with cymbals, only a ghost "with a fist the size of a car," and it kept him from work, sleep and dates. The Shadow King summed up the matchup: "You've brought me some sort of grandma's boy and a champion who spanks his monkey too much." Sam demanded respect for "Mrs. Meemaw," then deliberately spilled whiskey from his magical Starbucks tumbler on Ben's only pair of shoes. Ben insisted the spirit wasn't imaginary, and Sam took a closer look. He saw something enormous looming behind Ben: red-ringed eyes and a wide grin full of sharp teeth.

The Grandmaster refused to let the fight happen inside the Fortress ("I just cleaned up a ton of chinchilla shit, and I ain't picking up blood now next"). She jinxed Sam for the whiskey on her floor. A tangent followed about housekeeping and the Shadow King's botched taxes, which he blamed on [[The Arbiter|Vythorn]]. Sam called her "Shakira," and she bounced a Hydro Flask that [[Henrietta the Goblin]] had brought in off his skull. It did nothing, "probably because your head's pretty empty anyway." Fed up, she shoved them both through a red portal without so much as a welcome.

### Abducted

They landed in a cold, dark, abandoned throne room buried deep beneath the Dread Fortress. It was a ruin of tiered steps, statues and cobwebs, lit by a pale green glow that sucked the heat out of anyone who got close. Where the ceiling should have been, an ethereal opening revealed [[The Acolytes]] in the stands. In their impatience they had already summoned chaos.

A humming metal disc descended, ringed with lights "as if it's a streamer's LED lights," and The aliens beamed both champions up. Ben clung to the stones ("I'm not trying to get probed here"), but they gave way. Sam embraced it: "This ain't my first fucking rodeo, boys. I'm going to try to seduce the alien." He tried to clap cheeks and failed, since the aliens had no cheeks: they were flat as sheets of paper. They still took pity on him and worked on his "empty brain," sending him back smarter. Ben, who had resisted, came back without his left eye. In its place was a glow-in-the-dark band-aid with an alien smiley face.

### Dolores

Pushed over the edge, Ben heard cymbals crashing in the distance and summoned the spirit's arms: matted black fur, pale and far-too-wrinkly knuckles, yellowed nails crusted with antique dust, and tiny cymbals on every finger. The blast of their arrival drew first blood, and the arms followed up with a solid punch. "I told you, this giant spirit monkey toy just shows up and ruins things," Ben explained.

When Sam called the Grandmaster "baby," she snapped, "No one calls the Grandmaster, baby!" and invited the Acolytes to empty their pockets on him. Raccoons, eyeball hot dogs, popcorn and waterfalls of beer rained down. Sam came out of it with ramen, beer, potatoes from an Acolyte named Dazzle, plenty of water, and one raccoon. He named her **Dolores** and launched her at Ben's chest. The monkey arms tried to swat her back like a ping-pong ball, but Dolores, used to big branches, weaved through the hands and latched onto Ben's face. Ben shrugged off her rabies at first, until Sam cried "Aha, pocket sand!" and twisted his luck with a flick of magic. Ben now had rabies. There was no help to be had, as the Grandmaster put it: "We do have insurance. It's just not for the champions."

### Into the Box

Word reached the arena that there was a rabies vaccine in the Box of Chaos. A pink-and-yellow comet crashed onto the throne itself and became a gold-and-pink chest with fire inside that gave off no heat. "It demands a sacrifice," the Shadow King intoned. "Drop of blood, a toe, cryptocurrency." He seized the Acolyte Aether Flux with a giant spectral hand, ran a shadowy javelin through Aether Flux's chest, and pulled out a black, viscous soul. The body dropped behind Sam. Two clawed shadow fingers and a pair of yellow eyes peered out of the box.

Ben dashed up the steps with Dolores still on his face, tore her off and held her over the box. "You fucking wouldn't," said Sam. He would. The box pulled her in and responded about as well as it ever has: cheerful raccoon chatter, a welcome party, because "they've always wanted a pet." Out came the shadowy figure, now in a raccoon-skin hat, with Ben's prize: the **Raccoon of Annihilation**, a cute squeaky charm that is secretly a weapon of mass destruction if squeezed too hard. Ben then hurled a bone-dusted rock with the monkey arms ("You throw raccoons, I throw rocks"). It pinged off Sam's bottle by pure accident, and Sam kicked it back like a hacky sack. The arms, enjoying the game, clapped instead of blocking, and the rock left a raccoon-shaped mark on Ben's forehead.

### Deletus

Sam wanted his soulmate back. "You spooky bitch, you give me back my soulmate," he demanded, reaching into the box, and the box dragged him in headfirst. In a black tornado with yellow eyes, he and Dolores were pulled together and fused. He emerged with a raccoon mullet, black eye-rings, a toothy snout and talons, his Hydro Flask melded into his body ("I'm naturally drunk now"). The only part of him still wood elf was his penis. "I am now Deletus. And I will not be questioned by any mortal man or woman on this godforsaken earth!"

The were-coon charged up the stairs and missed spectacularly. Ben stepped aside, and Deletus tumbled back into the box. The fusion flipped: raccoon became human and human became raccoon, leaving him with a raccoon face, nice hair, human teeth, and a raccoon penis. "I don't consent to any of this!" The box spat him out. A coin flip declared him more attractive overall, and a second flip declared his dick "so much worse." The Grandmaster called a break to wash her eyes with bleach.

### The Jolly Chimp

After the break, the Shadow King's throne collapsed under him (he had eaten all the cake) and goblins stacked themselves into a pyramid chair. He named a goblin "Fernando" to roll for him, only to learn that "out of the 235 employees we have, we have zero Fernandos." The Grandmaster listed the staff's generous benefits, including a pet chinchilla on hiring and a union. The Shadow King immediately cancelled all of them, and she immediately said that was not true. Henrietta, a tiny goblin with bright blonde hair, did the rolling instead.

Then the monkey woke up properly. **The arms spread wide, a grin and eyes appeared behind Ben's head, and the cymbals crashed together on Sam** with perfect force. Ben kept aiming for the groin, "because it's the only part of you that has actually hurt me in this fight," and the cymbals smashed the raccoon penis. The rest of the spirit took shape around him as **the Jolly Chimp**: a full chimp head in a bloody red fez, hypnotic red-black-white eyes, and a grin of flat, perfectly white teeth. "I didn't ask for this. I didn't want this thing," Ben protested, but he began to clap, the chimp clapped in sync, and the Acolytes chanted along.

Sam answered with meth: "I was made for meth." He chewed a handful of blue crystals and explained that Meemaw had laced her cookies to compete with the Aasimar bakers who moved into the deep woods. The rush revived him, and he tackled Ben using "gravity, physics, leverage" and his alien-boosted brain. Even the narrator called it "the dumbest fight I've ever seen." Both of them fell into the Box of Chaos.

### The Knockoff Genie

Inside the smoky box ("Are we in a hot box?") they found the Acolytes' second chaos event: a battered golden lamp. The Grandmaster warned that it could manage an exorcism but not a vaccination. Sam clubbed Ben with it ("And on the first swing, the Lord said, let there be light"), then wrapped a sweaty Nike band around his fists to double his rabies output. Ben grabbed for the lamp and got a handful of raccoon dick instead, which turned the Jolly Chimp's grin into a horrified frown. Once it was ruled that raccoon testicles do not retract, the chimp squeezed, and Ben flurried into Sam's nuts like a speed bag. Then the chimp took over Ben entirely. A man-sized cymbal ape punched from above and below: "we're making biscuits out of these balls here."

Sam survived with ten of his twelve inches left and rubbed the lamp with his damaged penis. A sleepy red genie appeared ("You've summoned me. This is terrible timing") and granted one grudging wish to fix it. The dick healed at Dolores's normal size, and Dolores had no dick: Sam now had a raccoon vagina. "I'm Samantha Cletus and I'm here to whoop ass and save my fucking Meemaw. I don't care how many dicks I got to crush." Down to almost nothing, Samantha fired a sawed-off shotgun and missed, then sulked in a corner the box didn't have.

### Samantha Cletus for Life

Ben softened. He was "only kind of sorry that I just smashed your penis into a vagina," and he offered a way out. Samantha told him about Meemaw, the wrong pond, and the alligator mites that didn't go away on their own. She suggested he just give up. He declined. When Ben threw a punch, Samantha used the Acolytes' gift to flex so hard her pectorals burst like balloons, breaking the Jolly Chimp's hand and leaving it with only one cymbal. She followed with coke, meth, and a stunning hip kick that Ben shook off.

Ben tried the lamp again. The genie told him to "fuck off," since it only grants one wish a day, so he threw the lamp at Samantha. In slow motion she heard Meemaw's voice telling her to mind her manners and dodge, and she kicked it back. Ben pointed out that the genie could have cured Meemaw's cancer. The moment had passed. **The Jolly Chimp brought down its one good hand.**

> **Samantha Cletus:** "You got this monkey off your back, but you got a Samantha Cletus for life."

"In that squishing sound like a bug, Samantha is no more." The Shadow King appeared beside the box and peered in. Ben admitted he and the chimp had kind of bonded, though not with the rabies. "I'll see what I can do about the rabies," said the Shadow King, "but in the meantime, I'll just let the two of you have a little privacy." He shut the lid and locked it with a key made from his own finger.

## Dried Blood

Back at the table, StakeTCC said that if Sam Cletus ever came back, he'd live in the box again: "I like the box… I thought it would be safer." The Grandmaster noted the season's huge fascination with the Box of Chaos. As for Meemaw, she is "actually very proper," disapproves of everything Sam gets into, and guides him from a distance, because "sometimes when you love things, you have to let them go." Her cancer comes from the dip. KidCalrissian also revealed that his champion for a live show he had already played, which hadn't aired yet, is [[Chef GPT]].
