## What happens
### Opening — the jingle-bell boy and the chinchilla army
1. **0:00:07** The Acolytes zoom into the Dread Fortress. The **Shadow King walks the hallways in fancy leather boots with silver straps and tiny bells hooked on the back.** He didn't put the bells there. "The rest of us did, so we knew when he'd be coming close." Bec calls him **"this little jingle bell boy."**
3. **0:01:19** The Shadow King asks what all this is. Elusia says it is training: "We want to make sure that our security is fully aware and ready to protect the Void against anything or anyone who might try to invade or disrupt the games."
   - He calls it "admirable… At least you're on board with keeping the regime safe."
   - She hears snark in that. He replies: "it's not like many are going to be attacking the Dread Fortress when I sit on the throne."
4. **0:01:57** Elusia says the chinchillas may be there to protect "**all the goblins and the civilians that we have here in the Dread Fortress. You don't seem particularly inclined to protect them.**" Shadow King: "**That's a fair point. They don't mean a lot to me.**"
5. **0:02:17** The Shadow King admits he was "**just looking for a bathroom**, but yes, let's go to the Deathmatch."
6. **0:02:24** Elusia claps. **The chinchillas part "like an ocean"** and open a path to his throne ("Ugly, as it may be, but I may be biased in my description").
7. **0:02:48 — The summoning.** The chinchillas circle and beat their sticks on the tables, walls and floors, building a rhythm. **Elusia clenches the amulet around her neck until it slices her fingers and blood drips.** She announces "Storm versus Grug the Wizard!" The champions step out of a blue portal.

### The champions introduce themselves
8. **0:03:52 — Grug.** He calls himself "**the most powerful wizard that one has ever seen.**"
   - He was once "a unintelligent barbarian." He climbed the tallest mountain, found the most powerful wizard and told him to make him the most powerful magical being on the planet.
   - "**Puny Wizard, make me powerful!**" The wizard laid his hands on him. "**My muscles are pumping with magical aura!**"
   - **Henrietta the Goblin** runs up and offers her a cup of black coffee. Storm shoots it in one.
10. **0:05:26** The Shadow King asks whether the knives are strapped on or organic. **Storm rolls onto her back, puts all four legs in the air, and the knives come out: "shink."** She has **ten organic knives**.
    - "Do you not know how cat work? You've never met cat before?"
11. **0:05:55 — The Fortress's cats.** The Shadow King asks whether there are any cats in the Dread Fortress. Elusia: "**Several, actually.** They are the ones that help keep the mice and rats at bay. It's a constant invasion. **Besides the chinchilla army, the cat army also does a very notable job.**" Shadow King: "I barely knew we had chinchillas."
12. **0:06:12 — Chinchilla lore.** Storm asks: "Are chinchillas snacks?" Elusia says no: "Chinchillas are pets and armies."
    - "**Every employee here at the Dread Fortress gets an employee emotional support chinchilla** to help them out. And there's also their own bodyguard."
    - Shadow King: "**Trust me, you don't want to mess with the chinchillas. Their union is very strong.**"
    - Elusia says they're "perfect for cuddle puddles." If Storm wins, "you might be able to get one of those. They do a nice little pile on if you survive."
13. **0:06:37 — Poses for the Shadow King.**
    - **Storm** rolls onto her back and shows her belly with all her knives out.
    - **Grug** shows off his "awesome wizards books." They are not spell books at all, just huge nonsense tomes such as tax records of a medieval county and dark 15th- and 16th-century codices.
    - Grug flexes, and **his wizard robes tear at the biceps and neck**, giving "a nice little peek of flesh."
    - Shadow King: "I suppose we'll have to see which is stronger, those books or those kni[ves]."

### The arena — the meditation valley
14. **0:08:25** Elusia summons a **purple portal**. Beyond it is a cool, almost brisk place on a bright day: a breeze ruffling fur and beard, and "a beautiful little calm babbling stream." It is "perfection and beauty, a site of meditation."
    - "**Destroy it,** the Grandmaster will say. I give you this landscape for you to demolish."
15. **0:09:21** She points up. **Rows and rows of Acolytes fill the stands overhead "as if you have been placed in the Coliseum itself."**
    - "They are the ones that demand that your story be told today. **So make sure that it's a fucking good one.**"
    - She flies up to sit among the Acolytes.
16. **0:09:58** Shadow King: "**I'd like to see this river filled with blood by the end of the Deathmatch.**"
17. **0:10:42** Initiative is tied at 19. A straight d20 roll-off gives Grug 7 and Storm 17, so **Storm goes first**.

### Round 1
18. **0:11:41 — The boomerang.** Storm stays back and throws her **boomerang, "that slightly bent stick,"** at Grug's face. It knocks him square on the forehead and leaves a red line that may bruise.
19. **0:12:32** Grug: "Is very nice looking with mark. Oh, how dare you! Such a pretty kitty indeed. **Now face the wrath as I summon my mythical magical energy.**" He goes "full stream magic" (a rage).
20. **0:13:06** While he powers up, Storm hits him with the boomerang again. Bec: when they're "powering up" like Super Saiyans, "that's the prime time to hit them!"
    - Storm: "Another mark very nice on you. You are doing a wonderful job."
    - Bec: "**You now have an equal sign on your forehead.**"
21. **0:14:21** The boomerang zooms across the field. **The crowd accidentally does "a duck" instead of a wave** to avoid being hit.
22. **0:14:48 — Grug wants a familiar.** "Oh, bad kitty. **Now you will see that you must bow down to me and I will graciously accept you as my familiar.**"
    - He bolsters his magic and **Wild Surges**. He says he "teleports" 20 ft, but the surge table roll is a 6: **multicolored protective lights surround him** (+1 AC).
    - He yells "**BRRRRR! … Blur.**" Elusia: "I've never heard these spell words before. I quite like them."
23. **0:17:01 — "Death Touch!"** Grug punches Storm and yells the spell name. Bec: "Are you just naming your things? This finger's dead touch, this one's pickles." Grug: "No, this is, I'm a wizard. These are magical spells." Storm: "This one makes me a sandwich."
    - The punch hits for 9 bludgeoning. **Storm yowls like a cat whose tail has been stepped on.**
24. **0:17:36 — Grug's curse (player, out of character).** Grug was **cursed by the wizard to think he is the most powerful magic user**. He uses no real magic, only accidental wild magic.
    - Bec relays chat (Cranston): "**Grug is really Conan the Librarian.**"
25. **0:20:11** Storm: "**I love Grug. I don't want to fight Grug. I want to be friends with Grug.** … He seems like he needs help." Elusia: "Don't we all?" Grug's feet are now wet from crossing the streams.
26. **0:21:02** The Shadow King's end-of-round roll is a 9. Elusia: "Disappointing as ever… even though you are a sad disappointment to all of us…"
    - The Acolytes are enthralled, so **both champions get a hero point**. Bec explains: "This is your plus ultra… over 9,000 going super Saiyan."

### The Box of Chaos (0:22:41)
27. The Shadow King snaps his fingers. **A yellow and pink box slams onto the battlefield like a meteor.**
    - "I'm going to need a volunteer." **He plucks Iron Trout out of the stands with his magical spectral hands and wrings them out over the box "like a wet towel."** Their soul drips like liquid into the box.
    - The box explodes in a column of pink and yellow fire. **A shadowy figure made of that soul claws its way out, eyes bursting yellow.** "This is the Box of Chaos."
28. **0:23:34** Shadow King: "Should you make a sacrifice to it for a free action, you will get a magic item in return. So, good luck making a deal." Elusia adds that anything works: "The lint in your pocket, the knife in your paw."

### Round 2
29. **0:24:13** Storm: "You stepped on my tail. Sad about that." She casts **Thunder Wave** at close range. Grug makes his save and takes half.
    - Storm: "I did 12. **My dad would be very proud of me.** … I'm so proud of you."
30. **0:25:36 — "Entangled!"** Being hit triggers Grug's wild magic (7). He yells "**entangled!**" and wild plants climb up and consume a 10-ft radius around Storm, making it difficult terrain. Storm: "I'm a very agile person… You got like built-in cleats."
31. **0:26:49** Grug: "**Magic shall protect me, for I am Grug, the most powerful wizard.**" His aura grows brighter, but **it's really the Acolytes shining flashlights on him "so you look like you glow. They support this visual that you have of yourself."**
32. **0:27:23** Storm swipes with her cat claws ("Is this like a boop?"). She misses. "No goal, he was in the crease."
33. **0:29:16 — Puss-in-Boots eyes.** Storm stays put and makes herself small. Her eyes get big, her ears go down, and she does "the big blinky" to appeal to Grug's "sweet nurturing caring about tiny creature side."
    - Her persuasion 18 beats his insight 7. "For a moment, you are taken in by the pure adorableness… a slight moral dilemma." **Grug has disadvantage on his next spell.**
34. **0:31:17 — The hug.** Grug: "**So cute, too cute. Maybe hug**," and he goes in for a grapple. He misses. **He tenderly hugs a rock next to Storm instead** ("it's got a top part and a bottom part").
    - Grug: "**Wait a second! We're in the middle of a battle! How dare you!**"

35. **0:33:27 — "BURNING HANDS!"** Grug brings a heavy tome down on Storm's head while yelling a random spell name, and it lands ("boop, right on top of your head").

    - With his "magic," Grug once per round **shoves her 5 ft back**: "Cuz my spells are so powerful."
36. **0:36:02 — W-I-Z-A-R-D.** The Shadow King: "I'm not sure they know what a wizard actually is." Grug spells it out: "**W for Wonderful, I for Intelligence, Z for Zap, U for Ultra, R for Radiant, D for Damage.**" Elusia: "You should have chosen a shorter word."
37. **0:37:01 — The rock.** Between the two champions now stands "the fake Storm, the rock version… not Dwayne the Rock Johnson." Bec adds that his face is carved into it, "like a small little monument."

### Chaos event — the chicken wing siren (0:37:18)

39. **0:38:54** **Storm resists** and stands there confused, hearing how annoying the song really is. **Grug is entranced** ("the delights and sounds of chicken wings fill your mind"). He walks past Storm to the edge of the map and reaches up to the siren.
40. **0:39:23** Storm takes an opportunity slap as he passes and misses. As a cat, she is attuned to pitch, and the song pierces her ears so badly she has to cover them.
41. **0:39:50** The siren pelts the open-mouthed Grug with chicken wings for **5 chicken wing damage**.
    - Rulings: "**Chicken wing damage cannot be halved as no resistance exists to it.**" The arena has other unique damage types: "**Pillow damage, chicken wing damage, rock damage.**"

43. **0:41:47** The Shadow King rolls 16: "seven better than nine." Elusia: "Look at you doing math."

### Round 3 — the hairball chariot

## Dried Blood (after-show)
**In-character / player-stated canon (marked Dried Blood)**
- **Storm's death in space (player-narrated):**
  - Storm "has been wandering trying to be reunited with her family." When she was very young **she was separated from her entire tribe**. She has been searching, making herself stronger, becoming a warrior, and **seeking internal enlightenment, "that's why she went the monk route."**
  - As the cold set in, **it awakened her last childhood memory of her family, which involved snow** (she's a snow leopard). Growing colder made her feel more at home and happy.
  - "**When she closed her eyes for the final time, she imagined that she was right next to her tribe again. So she just went home.**"
  - The opener "Storm has been cleaning herself off" is also in this voice.
- **Grug's curse (player-stated):** Grug was always told he was **"too dumb to be a wizard."**
  - The curse makes him hear spell names and believe in them ("he got hit but he's just like… mage armor").
  - **Whenever he dies, the curse means he'll always have a happy ending in his head and die smiling, "saying, I was the greatest wizard."**
- **Grug's jump, as the player explained it:** "he muscled his way up into the… jumping on particles… So muscular, it looks like magic… He falls! Gravity, it works."
- **Last words to the Acolytes:**
  - Grug: "**Always wash your hands.**" (Bec: "You don't want to dirty those spell books.")
  - Storm: "**Is helpful to have knives for feet.**"
  - Bec: "Remember, wash your hands and attach knives to your feet and you will live a half decent amount of time."

**Creator notes (out of character)**

## Who and what appears
### Characters
**[[Characters/Storm on the Horizon|Storm on the Horizon]]** (PixiesandPins). **A snow leopard monk**, cat-speech ("Do you not know how cat work?").
- **Ten organic knives on her four feet ("knives for feet").** She fights with a boomerang, an iklwa (10-ft reach), cat claws, Thunder Wave ("Fist of Four Thunders") and Shatter, plus Flurry of Blows, Stunning Strike and Deflect Missiles.
- She takes catnaps and shoots black coffee.
- She sacrificed a hairball to the Box of Chaos and got **the flying hairball chariot**.
- She tricked Grug twice by promising to be his familiar.
- **She won, then was sent into space by the Shadow King's "victory lap" and froze.** Dried Blood: she was separated from her tribe as a child and died imagining herself home with them.
- She likes treats and wants to be Cheetara.

**[[Characters/Grug the Wizard|Grug the Wizard]]** (BanzaiBabee).
- A **former barbarian who forced the most powerful wizard on the tallest mountain to "make me powerful"** and was **cursed to believe he is the most powerful wizard**. In truth he rages and punches, with wild magic surges, and yells spell names at everything ("Death Touch," "Burning Hands," "Magic Missile," "Fireball," "Stone skin," "Mage armor," "Color spray," "Dimension Door," "Black tentacles").
- His "spell books" are nonsense tomes (medieval tax records, old codices). He throws them like boomerangs and writes new "spells" in them with a crayon he also eats.
- His muscles burst his robes. His chest beam and "buns of holiness" blind or knock out Acolytes.
- He had never noticed clouds or looked up at the sky.
- He wanted Storm as his familiar and dreamed of a magic tower.
- He died falling, smiling, believing he was the greatest wizard.

**[[Characters/The Shadow King|The Shadow King]]** (Drew).
- He wears fancy leather boots with silver straps; **others secretly hung bells on them**.
- He sits on the Dread Fortress throne and speaks of "the regime." The goblins and civilians "don't mean a lot to me."
- He barely knew about the chinchillas or the cat army.
- He ran the Box of Chaos with Iron Trout's soul and called the metal-chair event a "family activity" with Elusia. He saved Grug from drowning ("Try not to die too early").
- He sent the victor into space with a spectral falcon.

**[[Characters/Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer]]** (Bec).
- She **trains the chinchilla army of the Void** for Deathmatch and Fortress security, and to protect the goblins and civilians.
- She is snarky toward the Shadow King.
- She summons portals by cutting her fingers on **the amulet around her neck**.
- She watched from among the Acolytes. She metal-chaired Storm, then gave her aspirin ("two tiny little blue pills").
- She crowned Storm champion.

**[[Characters/Theodore the Goblin|Theodore the Goblin]]**: leads the chinchilla drill from the front, stick in hand.

**[[Characters/Henrietta the Goblin|Henrietta the Goblin]]**: serves Storm black coffee.

**[[Characters/The chinchilla army of the Void|The chinchilla army of the Void]]**: martial artists with sticks; "murder in their eyes." Each Fortress employee gets one as an emotional support chinchilla and bodyguard. They have a strong union and form cuddle puddles.

**[[Characters/The cat army|The cat army]]**: several cats in the Dread Fortress that keep the "constant invasion" of mice and rats at bay.

**[[Characters/The chicken wing siren|The chicken wing siren]]**: sings "Chicken wing, chicken wing, hot dog and bologna," pelts victims with wings, then turns into a chicken and flees. Linked to the halftime chicken wing stand.


**[[Characters/The Box of Chaos soul-figure|The Box of Chaos soul-figure]]**: a shadowy figure with yellow eyes. It reacts with disgust to the hairball.
