## What happens
### Opening — the Shadow King's throne room
1. **0:05:57** The Acolytes zoom into the isolated volcanic Dread Fortress. Heels clack down the long hallway, and a loose dog barks in the background. "It's not Doug, though. Don't worry. **Doug has not been replaced.** We just have tons of dogs in the fortress." As the Grandmaster nears the Shadow King's throne room she hears a buzzing sound.
2. **0:06:46 — The moon landing.** She finds **the Shadow King slouched in his throne with the lights out, lit only by a black-and-white TV with its antenna up.** She throws the door open and almost terrifies him. He says he's "catching up on the history." He claims the moon landing was faked: "You can see the strings. And why is the flag waving?… This is definitely done on a soundstage." He adds, "I know a thing about illusions." She retorts, "You know nothing about the moon, though."
3. **0:07:37 — The flat Void.** The GM asks, "What are you going to tell me next time? The Void is flat?" He answers, "It's not. I mean, it's one contiguous plane." She asks whether he ever traveled the land he ruled. He says: "**I've been to the Shadowlands and Prexmarin and not too far beyond that.** Why would I?… There's nothing out there."
4. **0:08:11** GM: "**For one who seeks knowledge and is a scholar, you're pretty fucking dumb, aren't you?**" She turns the TV off with its little dials and offers him "a little piece of history that you do love. Blood and violence." He agrees that this exists.
5. **0:08:52 — The champions arrive.** She clasps her amulet, splices into her own fingertips and opens a portal. Two champions walk through, "grand, not necessarily in size": "**Nigel, Nice Louie [Night Louis], and Sprinkles!**"
6. **0:09:26 — Sprinkles** says he is "really not a fighter." He just likes to be a nice friend. "**I made a bet with a friend of mine that I could win this fight with the power of friendship.**" The GM defends the idea: "it works in every freaking anime… If it worked for Naruto." The Shadow King complains that Naruto has too many filler episodes, and she agrees.
7. **0:10:27 — Nigel** has been "a fan of your work for a long, long time." He introduces himself in a husky voice as "**BAFTA award-winning actor, Nigel Night Louis**." He is drinking **bubbly** and says he's sponsored by it, specifically cherry. The GM claps in **two goblins, Sarah and Nigel** ("there's two Nigels now"), who bring a can of bubbly for the Shadow King too. Nigel lists the other flavors: lime, lemon, and **teardrop**, "a sadness-induced bubbly that I drank before performances to make me really understand the depth of the human experience." Sprinkles asks for a cherry, and Nigel tosses him one.
8. **0:11:50 — Appearances.**
   - **Nigel** has long white-gray hair slicked back and a salt-and-pepper beard. He wears a big coat like Tommy Wiseau's in *The Room* over a Renaissance dress shirt, "half Shakespeare and half Tommy Wiseau," with big sunglasses. He looks perpetually hung over.
   - **Sprinkles is a halfling.** He wears a neatly fold-rolled dress shirt, a vest, slacks and dress shoes, "very fit to be like a waiter." He goes around admiring the pillars.
9. **0:13:00 — The tour.** Nigel the Goblin gives a tour: "Over here, we have another pillar, and over here is the other pillar." Sprinkles says he's a fan of both. Nigel: "**I remember my first time on PCP.**" The GM covers Sprinkles' ears: "He's clearly innocent… At least spill some blood before you expose him to different substances."
10. **0:13:39 — The Rock.** Nigel asks whether Sprinkles is the butler: "I thought you guys told me you had **Wayne the Dwarf Bronson** for this match." The GM says Wayne was double-booked. Sprinkles explains that he was **a PA on "Ack Blattom"** (the Rock's newest superhero film) and also worked with him on *Tooth Fairy*. GM: "It's not a throwback if you're old." Nigel: "I fucking am."
11. **0:14:41 — The rider.** Nigel asks whether his **Teen Choice Award** was waxed for the match: "It was in my contract." The Shadow King backs him: "Did you wax the Teen Choice Award or not?" The goblin Nigel stumbles off to the prep room. Nigel also demands **raw asparagus** ("I like to eat it crunchy"). Goblin Nigel returns at once with a silver platter of lightly chilled, freshly cut asparagus.
12. **0:15:58 — The fan.** Sprinkles recognizes Nigel from **"There Won't Be Blood"**. Nigel delivers his famous line: "**You drink my smoothie!**" Sprinkles says the movie was long, but Nigel was "electrifying." Nigel pulls a pen to sign his cheek, and Sprinkles offers **a grappling hook** to sign instead ("pretty badass, actually").
13. **0:16:52 — The surfboard.** The goblins bring Nigel's **surfboard prop from "Sponge," the CW's gritty live-action SpongeBob SquarePants adaptation**, which won him the Teen Choice Award. Shadow King: "Did you get slimed or what?" Nigel: "All the time… **It's like a ritual of humility** and humbleness when you win this very prestigious award."
14. **0:17:38 — The blood cannon.** GM: "We do not have slime here, but we do have blood." **The Shadow King pulls a CO2 cannon full of blood from under his throne and blasts both champions with it.** "Now that you're both dressed for the arena…" There are chopped guts in the blood too. Sprinkles: "Free food… steak tartare." Shadow King: "**Sure, if you like to eat goblin.**" Both goblins freeze and turn slowly. "Don't worry, it's no one you know." Nigel: "**Eating goblin is illegal in most places here.**"
15. **0:18:52 — The baby Bjorn.** **Goblin Nigel climbs onto Nigel's back.** Nigel: "Small compatriots… It looks good on camera… **You can be my Mandalorian.**" He asks for an adorable sound, gets one he doesn't like ("maybe stay silent"), and lectures it: "It's about being raw… The steak and the blood that you drink." Tiny goblin hands poke out from under his cloak. **He makes a little baby Bjorn out of his award-outfit kit, and Goblin Nigel cradles himself in it.** "I'll protect you for my life. My life is meaningless. My life is about the art."
16. **0:20:47 — The friendship bracelet.** Sprinkles asks Nigel his two favorite colors. Nigel is offended to be asked as "a **Cable Ace Award** winning performer," but answers: "**red, like blood, and blue, like the sky. I openly weep while staring at the sky once a day.**" Sprinkles produces **a red-and-blue friendship bracelet** "so we can always remember each other as the bestest of friends." Nigel suspects he keeps them pre-made in every color combo. Sprinkles: "**I typically win employee of the month** just because I'm so good natured." He hopes they both survive and "take home the prize together."
17. **0:22:15 — The arena.** "Come, dear award winners, one of a Teen Choice Award and one of the Employee of the Month Award." The portal opens onto **a deep, cold, damp forest. The ground glows, green fireflies dance among bare branches, and nothing grows high: bare roots and trees, with moldy, wilted grass.** GM: "This land is, well, not really meant for living. So I figured it's the perfect place for dying… make sure that it's a damn good story." The Acolytes scream above in a lifted, ghastly fog, **in the same husky voice Nigel uses**. "**Give us a performance we won't forget.**"
18. **0:24:07 — The trowel.** Sprinkles asks for a trowel. **The Shadow King snaps one out of the ether**, and Sprinkles starts working on the dead grass. Shadow King: "While you're down there, maybe you want to dig your own grave." "I'm small, but I'm not that small." "Well, I guess they can't all be winners."
19. **0:25:20 — Sprinkles' angle.** Nigel asks what he's after. Sprinkles: "My friend… **she said that I would not be able to win this without exceeding amounts of violence. And, you know, I myself am a pacifist.**" Nigel answers that to win awards he eats dirt: "**I'm one with the earth. You will be one with the earth.**" Sprinkles: "I'm a gardener." Shadow King: "Everyone does have a heart on the inside, and I would like to see his." He vanishes, *pfft*.

### Round 1
20. **0:27:11 — The acting lesson.** Nigel: "Normally people pay lots and lots of money for this, but… you're gonna get an acting lesson for free… **You're gonna hate me by the end of this**… Let's start with pleasure and fun." **He flings the friendship bracelet at Sprinkles**, says "Knock, knock," never finishes the joke, and **casts Hideous Laughter: mage hands tickle Sprinkles everywhere** ("Goochie goochie goo, buddy"). **Sprinkles rolls a nat 20 and resists.** "What were you saying about the knock knock joke?"
21. **0:28:31 — The necrotic dirt.** Nigel backs off 5 ft ("come on, hit me, make me bleed") and makes a Con save for **the necrotic ground he ate earlier. It's another nat 20, "back to back."** The deadly dirt energizes him instead ("It must be like made of cocaine"), and he gains advantage on his next turn. Drew: "Apparently he did a movie where he only ate dirt the entire time." GM: "You were not supposed to have that option." **Nigel licks more dirt** to end his turn: "Yum, yum, yum."
22. **0:30:13 — The hug.** Sprinkles walks slowly in for a hug ("Let's just bring it in, okay, fella?") and takes the Dodge action. Nigel refuses to be hugged and tries to **slap him like a little glove** ("How dare you?"). In the roll-off, Nigel's Dex (14) beats Sprinkles' Strength (12). **Nigel grabs Sprinkles by the face and stops the hug**: "If I'm gonna get my EGOT, I'm gonna get my Oscar today, I'm gonna have to induce some trauma into you apparently, cuz that's what acting is. **It's projecting trauma.**"
23. **0:32:52 — Sprinkles opens up.** He was raised with many siblings who called him "**stinky head**," and he cried for a long time. He's proud of his hygiene, "all kinds of soaps," and fought his acne. People mocked his appearance and **the hair on his feet**. Now he's confident: "the kindness that we can pick up along the way is easier when it's spread." Nigel: "You were getting there, but then you lost the plot and started being nice again." He whispers: "This is gonna hurt me more than it's gonna hurt you. **I'm gonna fucking corrupt you.**"
24. **0:34:53 — The family secret.** The end-of-round roll for the GM is a 6: "You are an absolute disappointment to me." When someone says a six is enough, the Shadow King replies, "My mom said that." GM: "How would you know what my mother said?" **Shadow King: "Because she was my sister-in-law. It got really confusing there at the end."** The Grandmaster sits in shock at "this very unwanted piece of family history" laid at her feet before the whole arena. "This is absolutely a great moment and I am handling very well and will not have a breakdown over [it]." She kicks off round 2 "in a very erratic format."

### Round 2
25. **0:35:57 — "You want a hug?"** Sprinkles offers the Grandmaster a hug. She explodes. She'd rather shove the Shadow King's face down a trash can, let a lawnmower run it over, put it through a weed eater, grind it into sausage, **feed it to Doug**, squish what Doug poops out, "throw him over the freaking waterfall and hope that I never see him after that." Nigel: "That's raw… **Celestial beings and they have all this weird humanistic trauma**… use it." Shadow King: "**Elusia, I'm really proud of you.**" She scoots her chair away from him inch by inch, loudly. "I just want to see blood spilled this day."
26. **0:37:26 — Suggestion.** Nigel: "You wanna see blood? You'll see blood… you're gonna see my blood." He casts **Suggestion: "come on, fucking hit me… draw blood."** Sprinkles fails (Wisdom 5). Nigel **lies down prone** to make it easier. Sprinkles' advantage rolls are **a 1 and a 2**. "Oh, this is embarrassing on multiple levels as a pacifist." **The Acolytes boo: "You stink."**
27. **0:39:32 — The voice.** **Sprinkles hits himself in the head: "No, not yet, not yet."** Nigel: "You got a little voice that talks to you too, huh?" Sprinkles: "**Yeah, sure, she's my friend… She made a bet with me.**" Nigel: "**And I'm a fucking Colonel of Khorne.**" Sprinkles points out he can't be, "because you're currently a guy." Nigel: "**Gosh, you're harder to coach than Jonah Hill.**" Sprinkles: "Is Jonah Hill a gnoll, perhaps? Gnoll Hill."
28. **0:40:58** He swings again (18) and **punches Nigel for 1 point of bludgeoning damage**, without the trowel. Sprinkles panics: "No, no, no… I haven't lost yet."
29. **0:41:47 — The can.** The GM asks if he needs anything, and he asks for another cherry bubbly. **She throws one down, rolling an 18 to hit against his AC 10, for 1 point of "can damage."** It leaves a small dent in both him and the can. Nigel: "Can damage better than can't damage." GM: "Points deducted." Sprinkles: "I like doing can't damage… because we're all friends." Shadow King: "I'm sorry, I fell asleep while you weren't attacking."
30. **0:43:14 — The song and the truth.** Nigel brings out his **Tony Award for best actor in a play**. "We need to be truthful and vulnerable." He sings a song from "**the musical rendition of the life of Britney Spears**," trained by "the untrained Russell Crowe" from *Les Misérables*: "…should have a warning, it's dangerous, I'm falling." **He casts a truth spell** (transcribed "Black Hat's known the truth"; likely Zone of Truth) and **chooses to fail it himself**. Sprinkles also fails. Nigel is a **changeling**: **he reshapes part of his face to look like Sprinkles'** ("You can be anybody you wanna be"), then changes it back. "**Why do you need to please people so much to make friends? Why can't you be mean and still have friends?**"
31. **0:45:25 — The dark passenger.** Under the truth spell, Sprinkles says: "**It's because the dark passenger that resides inside me scares me a bit, so I have to counteract it with kindness.**" Nigel: "I just got shivers… that's our angle." Sprinkles: "Oh no, she's gonna be angry… **I don't get angry. That's her job.** I just get sad." Nigel turns to the crowd like an acting class: "This is what happens when you finally start telling the truth to yourself."
32. **0:46:42 — Nigel's truth.** "**Listen, I fucking hate my stuff… I constantly watch movies so I can look at myself and cry and yell at myself on the screen.**" He also loves himself: "Look at this chin… **I literally made it myself. I can change the way my face looks.** But also… **Sometimes I don't know who I am.**"
33. **0:47:36** Sprinkles: "**She doesn't want me to know who I am because I made a mistake… a long time ago. And now I'm living with it.**" Nigel: "This is good exposition." "I tear the line between reality and storytelling… **I may just be a face in a box.**" Sprinkles: "It's her. It's not me… **I don't want to lose this bet because that means I've lost who I am.**"

### Chaos event — the grandma zombies (0:48:38)
34. The cheering Acolytes trigger a chaos event. The dead ground shifts and mounds, skeletal hands burst out, and skeletons rise across the forest. They are well armed but wear **sweet old-lady dresses and carry purses full of Werther's: "grandma zombies."** "Should you get too close, they'll try to give you a deadly embrace… **Meemaw meemaws for days!**"
35. **0:50:17** Sprinkles: "**Are you sure about this? Do you really want to meet the Dark Passenger?**" Nigel: "Absolutely… Older women get a lot of hate and vitriol inside the acting industry… Let's beat them up the same way we beat each other up."

### Round 3 — the Dark Passenger
36. **0:50:51 — The transformation.** **Sprinkles closes his eyes. When he opens them they hold a demonic flame. He rages: his bones crack, his arms and body extend, and his jaw drops and cracks.** "**Finally, now it's time for bloodshed.**" The dark passenger points at Nigel: "**So, would you like me to rip out your spine? Or should we take care of these things together? Send them back where they once came?**" Nigel: "I want to see impulse. You do you, hombre."
37. **0:52:36 — Claws.** His phalanges sharpen and push out of his fingertips as knife-like claws. **His first attack is a nat 20 with Brutal Critical.** Nigel: "now we're playing." He casts **Silvery Barbs**, and the reroll is a 12. "Not the face, not the face." **His face shifts and he slips out of the way.** Dark passenger: "**Coward, I will take your face and shove it up your ass.**" The second attack is a **nat 1**. "Come here. Why don't you give me a nice big hug?"
38. **0:54:39 — Grandma's kiss.** Nigel flips his hair, now "**late 90s Nicolas Cage hair**," and rages. Both have advantage on the grapple, and Nigel wins. "**Give grandma a little kiss.**" **He shoves Sprinkles into a zombie, Gertrude.** She deals 9 necrotic damage: jaw hanging, she licks his face and presses the inside of her mouth onto him. GM: "As we all know, grandmothers have very corrosive breath." Nigel: "Is the rage coming out? **Unleash the cage, baby.**" Dark passenger: "**You made a mistake bringing me close to you.**"
39. **0:57:28 — The surfboard swing.** Nigel lets go, steps back, **grabs his Teen Choice Award** and swings it up (19) for 12, halved by rage. You hear a sound like the SpongeBob surfboard.
40. **0:58:38 — Three attacks.** Sprinkles: "**Do you want to try method acting as a corpse?**" Reckless, he attacks three times. "**Sprinkles isn't here anymore. Sprinkles is a pacifist, but the dark passenger certainly isn't.**"
   - The first claw hits for 12.
   - **The second is another nat 20.** Nigel uses **Cutting Words**: "**What are you, Paddington?** Paddington Bear? What is this, a weird CW remake? It was a really cool CW remake that I was in." It cuts the damage by just 1.
   - **Infectious Fury**: Nigel fails the save and takes **40 psychic damage** on the crit. "Not the bees! Not the bees!" (speaker uncertain). Dark passenger: "**Finally, someone's come to take out that fragile ego and cut you to death with my pieces.**"
   - The third hit does 5.
41. **0:63:03 [1:03:03] — The grandmothers' gift.** The two end "butt to butt… cheek to cheek, surrounded by meemaws": **Gertrude, Hannah, "Arbitrary," and other random old ladies**. The grandmothers chant the champions' names like "old deadly sirens," but it feels comforting "in that wonderful grandma way." **Both champions get hero points, "thanks to the gift of the grandmothers that came from the gift of the Acolytes. You know, we do a re-gifting process."** GM: "go Super Saiyan or Rose Gold… Plus ultra."
42. **1:04:32** 10-minute break announced. **1:04:47** Shadow King: "Welcome back, Acolytes. Let's get this over with." (Cut.)

### The Box of Chaos (1:04:51)
43. **A beautiful pink-and-yellow meteor** falls into the arena and crashes between the champions and **Meemaw Anna**. The box opens to hot flames and an oddly cool space. Shadow King: "**Champions, this is my former home. It's more spacious on the inside than you might think.**" You sacrifice to it and receive a magic item. "If you don't use it, you lose it."
44. **1:05:53 — Majestic Wolf.** "We're going to need some juice." **The Shadow King's spectral hand whips over the crowd and grabs an Acolyte sitting quietly: "You, Majestic Wolf!"** It forms **a giant spectral bow and fires Majestic Wolf like an arrow across the arena into the box.** **Majestic Wolf hits their head on the side of the box, breaks their neck and falls in.** The lid slams shut, the box rumbles, and a column of pink and yellow fire shoots into the sky. **Majestic Wolf's transformed soul crawls out with yellow eyes: "Let's make a deal."**

### Round 4
45. **1:07:51 — Face off.** Nigel thinks the flames will hurt, so he **shoves his head in, "going full Nicolas Cage, trying to go Ghost Rider," and offers the skin of his face.** The face doesn't burn. Two yellow demon eyes stare back, and **Freddy Krueger-like hands saw the skin off his face**, cutting through nerve endings and sinew. Then, like Dwight from *The Office*, the box spirit slaps the face on itself. GM: "**Face off.**"
46. **1:09:36** The box gives him **an Ioun Stone of Fortitude (+2 Constitution; his HP rises to 137).** GM: "Turns out you can manage to eat more of this dirty, dirty dirt." Nigel: "**I'm gonna win this Oscar without a face.** You try acting without a face."
47. **1:10:44 — Breaking the award.** He goes into **a berserker frenzy**, **snaps his Teen Choice award over his knee and dual-wields the pieces.** "**You see the soul of SpongeBob leave**, as I had conjured it for that performance." "**You wanna know what it costs to win all these awards?**" Goblins (00): "We wanna know the cost."
48. **1:11:41 — The SAG award.** His reckless attacks with his **SAG award** hit, and one is a **nat 20**. He slams Sprinkles in the head with both pieces "like that little wind-up monkey," for 39.
49. **1:13:31 — The gyrations.** Nigel does weird body gyrations, with blood flying from his fleshless face. **His performance check is a 30: "basically Abed from Community doing the Cage."** **The GM awards an extra d6 of "Nick Cage damage," its own category.** Drew: "No one's resistant to Nick Cage damage… **We all had to watch Gone in 60 Seconds. No one's immune.**" Nigel: "Nick Cage damage is ascended psychic damage." **The Acolytes are stunned, essentially paralyzed, for the next few minutes.** Sprinkles: "**Sprinkles has been hurt physically, but I have been hurt emotionally.**"
50. **1:16:19 — The bracelets.** **Sprinkles sacrifices all of his friendship bracelets to the box**, "as this voice screams in the head of the body that is Sprinkles": "**No, the friendship bracelet! It's worth so much!**" The figure in the box, still wearing Nigel's face, catches them and lines its arms with them. **The first result tosses them back: "We don't want this."**
51. **1:17:47 — The cat.** Nigel's face flings out of the box too. GM: "**There's a small pet cat actually right now that has wandered into the Box of Chaos and it is wearing your face.**" Sprinkles suggests an Evil Dead-style Necronomicon. Bec suggests the *What We Do in the Shadows* cow with a human face. "Everyone believe what you want to believe for this cat in the box."
52. **1:18:32 — The cloak of Nigel.** The box rumbles again. The figure, no longer wearing Nigel's face, holds **a cloak made from Nigel's face, sewn together with Sprinkles' own friendship bracelets.** It's a **reskinned hellhound cloak that lets him polymorph into Nigel and use Nigel's whole kit and hit points. "It's a cursed item, but we'll worry about that some other day."** Sprinkles: "**I'm already cursed. I have a demon living inside my brain.**" Dark passenger: "**I'm gonna save this for later. And then in your dying moments, you can watch as you kill your own career.**"
53. **1:20:19** Nigel's own face whips around on the cloak. "Do it. **Me versus me. I'm killing myself. This is art.**" He is shirtless from "bulking up for a Marvel movie or a DC movie." Three reckless claws hit (20, 21, 18), and Infectious Fury triggers again (Nigel's Wisdom save is a 4). Drew: "**You never go full Nick Cage.**" 24 damage. Dark passenger: "**Do you want to be an actor? Why don't you try playing dead?**" GM: "You'd be perfect for the role." Drew: "You're hired."
54. **1:23:17 — The grandmas leave.** The Shadow King rolls a **nat 1** at the end of the round, "the worst," and says he rolled one last time too. **The Grandmaster snaps her fingers and six goblins run out, each grabbing a grandmother and dragging her off through a dark portal.** Holes remain where they were. The smell of death returns moments later, because **the grandmothers have gone up to the Shadow King's seat to beat him with their purses: 42 points of "purse damage." "Shame on you, Sonny!"** Werther's-laden purses and brooches strike him.
55. **1:25:20** The Shadow King takes the hits unmoving in his throne. **Then his eyes flash yellow and the grandmothers fly into the sky.** "That was interesting. Can we move on?" **"Every now and then we'll see a grandmother floating in the sky… still unable to land."**
56. **1:26:12** The GM explains hero points again: "dream big… an ultra move… **Be reckless with your dreams.**" She warns that a forgotten hero point brings "the wrath of the Acolytes."

### Round 5 — Nigel's last performance
57. **1:27:42** Nigel to "Evil Sprinkle": "You're my biggest nemesis… **I need to make you the hero and I've gotta be the villain.**" **He kicks the box open and jumps in whole: "All the best performances happen after you're dead."** He gives his life so this is his last performance ever: "regardless of what happens today, I'm dead." GM: "This is unhinged."
58. **1:28:20 — The skin suit.** Drew: "Fuck it. We're doing it live." **The shadowy soul fillets Nigel's skin off piece by piece. Nigel dies in the box, and the shadow spirit comes out wearing him like a skin suit, with only the shadow face inside where the face should be.** Everything it does now deals an extra d10 necrotic. The new voice: "**Aren't we all just meat sacks being worn by any persona that we want to have?**"
59. **1:29:28 — Danny DeVito's toe knife.** It shapes itself into **a shadow Robert De Niro**: "**I'm going to fucking slice you up.**" It pulls out "**my little toe knife… given to me by my best friend, Danny DeVito**." Sprinkles: "Maybe you're a bad fella." It hits for 40 bludgeoning ("it's a dull knife") plus 15 necrotic.
   - **The knife is a prop from *It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia*.** DeVito, "a devil… an infernal," gave it to the shadow in the box.
   - It carries whatever disease lives in a toe "that has existed for multiple millennia" and never had its shots.
   - Sprinkles takes **an infected wound**.
60. **1:32:15 — The name.** GM: "Should we still call you Sprinkles?" "**My name is Melchizedek, the Dark One, and I have come to find a more suitable host.**" "**That is the name of my flesh puppet. That pathetic little halfling, the quivering whelp.**" GM: "So we have flesh puppet versus flesh puppet."
61. **1:32:54 — The hostile takeover (hero point).** Melchizedek: "**This… slightly occupied vessel of this famous actor… is a much more worthy vessel for a Lord of Darkness than a tiny little halfling named Sprinkles.**" **He tries to kick the shadow out of Nigel's body. He takes the face off the cloak and uses it to "lid myself into the body."** "**I want a new home!**"
62. **1:33:28 — The arena moves inside Nigel.** "**Our arena changes as it is now taking place inside Nigel, as spirit versus spirit. You two will fight for ownership of Nigel Night Louis's body.**" Both fighters use Nigel's capabilities. GM: "There are two wolves inside of you." **Sprinkles' body is left unoccupied outside: "Oh, gee, what the heck is going on, guys?"** Inside, the two are incorporeal and can trigger Nigel's bodily actions.
63. **1:35:25 — The asparagus.** Melchizedek: "**I'm gonna make Nigel piss. It's gonna smell like asparagus!**" Nigel's player: "Sometimes you plant a seed… and the opportunity comes up out of nowhere." GM: asparagus piss is very toxic, Con save.
64. **1:36:03 — Their true forms.** **Nigel's essence takes the form of a bright-eyed little Newsie: a high schooler who just did his first musical and was told for the first time that he was worth something.** Melchizedek: "**I'm going to tear you apart limb from limb, boy.**" **Melchizedek's true form is a nine-headed dragon-snake thing.** GM: "Your childhood version of yourself face off this nine-headed demon." The kid does a little box step. GM: "We have no sympathy for the British children."
65. **1:38:08 — Hold Person.** Melchizedek drops his rage: "**Hold still, boy.**" **He casts Hold Person, and the Newsie freezes mid box step.**
66. **1:39:24 — The stomach acid.** The Shadow King's roll of 9 crosses the chaos threshold, "despite the two nat ones in a row and taking 46 points of damage." "I'm not sure there's much I can add to this. It's already so chaotic." He and the GM both planned the same thing.
