A gentle halfling waiter who wanted to win with the power of friendship faced a changeling method actor who wanted to win an Oscar. **[[Sprinkles]]** and **[[Nigel Night Louis]]** met in a dead, glowing forest. What followed involved zombie grandmothers, a face fed to [[The Box of Chaos]] and a nine-headed demon of hatred charmed by the Footloose dance. In the end the battle moved inside Nigel's own body, and nobody walked out.

## The Story

### Moon Landings and Family Secrets

The [[The Acolytes|Acolytes]] swooped into [[The Dread Fortress]] as heels clacked down its long hallway and a loose dog barked somewhere in the dark. It wasn't [[Doug the Pug|Doug]], the audience was assured: "Doug has not been replaced. We just have tons of dogs in the fortress." [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|The Grandmaster]] followed a strange buzzing to the throne room. There she found [[The Shadow King]] slouched with the lights off, lit only by a black-and-white television with its antenna up.

He was "catching up on the history," and he had concluded that the moon landing was faked. "You can see the strings. And why is the flag waving?" He added, "I know a thing about illusions." When she asked whether he'd tell her next that [[The Void]] was flat, he said it was "one contiguous plane." He then admitted he had never travelled much of the land he ruled: "I've been to [[The Shadowlands]] and [[Prexmarin]] and not too far beyond that. Why would I?" The Grandmaster switched off the set and offered him a history he did love: blood and violence.

### Two Award Winners

She cut her fingertips on her amulet and opened a portal for the champions. Sprinkles, a halfling dressed neatly as a waiter, confessed he was "really not a fighter." He had made a bet with a friend that he could win with the power of friendship. The Grandmaster backed the plan: "If it worked for Naruto." Nigel had long, slicked-back white-gray hair, a Tommy Wiseau coat over a Renaissance shirt and big sunglasses. He introduced himself in a husky drawl as a "BAFTA award-winning actor" sponsored by cherry bubbly. Two [[Goblins|goblins]], Sarah and another Nigel, fetched drinks. Nigel the actor listed his flavors, including "teardrop," a sadness-induced bubbly he drank before performances.

The pre-show became one long celebrity rider. Nigel demanded that his Teen Choice Award be waxed, and the Shadow King backed him. Goblin Nigel brought out a silver platter of raw asparagus, then the surfboard prop from "Sponge," the CW's gritty live-action SpongeBob, which had won Nigel the Teen Choice Award. Winners were slimed, Nigel explained, as "a ritual of humility." The Fortress had no slime, but the Shadow King had a CO2 cannon full of blood under his throne, and he blasted both champions with it. Sprinkles spotted chopped guts in it and called it steak tartare. "Sure, if you like to eat goblin," said the Shadow King, as both goblins turned slowly to stare.

Goblin Nigel climbed onto the actor's back, and Nigel made him a little baby carrier out of his award-outfit kit: "You can be my Mandalorian." Sprinkles recognized Nigel from "There Won't Be Blood" and got his grappling hook signed. Then he asked Nigel's favorite colors. The answer was "red, like blood, and blue, like the sky. I openly weep while staring at the sky once a day." Sprinkles handed over a matching red-and-blue friendship bracelet.

### Into the Dead Forest

The portal opened onto a cold, damp forest of bare roots and moldy grass, with glowing ground and green fireflies drifting through the branches. "This land is, well, not really meant for living," said the Grandmaster. "So I figured it's the perfect place for dying." The Acolytes shrieked from the fog above, somehow in Nigel's husky voice. Sprinkles asked for a trowel and started gardening. He told Nigel that his friend had said he couldn't win "without exceeding amounts of violence. And, you know, I myself am a pacifist."

Nigel offered a free acting lesson: "You're gonna hate me by the end of this." He flung the friendship bracelet back, started a knock-knock joke he never finished, and set spectral hands tickling Sprinkles everywhere. Sprinkles rolled a perfect natural 20 and shrugged it off. Nigel then rolled his own natural 20 against the deadly dirt he'd been eating, which energized him instead. Apparently he had once made a movie where he only ate dirt.

Sprinkles walked in slowly for a hug. Nigel grabbed him by the face to stop it, explaining that acting "is projecting trauma." Sprinkles opened up anyway. His siblings had called him "stinky head," and people had mocked his acne and the hair on his feet. Nigel was disappointed when he drifted back into kindness. Then the end of the round brought the night's biggest bombshell. When the Grandmaster asked how the Shadow King could possibly know what her mother had said, he replied: "Because she was my sister-in-law. It got really confusing there at the end." He had loved her mother, and he had also killed her mother's husband, which explains the confusion. The Grandmaster announced, in front of the whole arena, that she was handling this very well.

### Truth and the Dark Passenger

She was not handling it well. When Sprinkles offered her a hug, she unloaded. She would rather shove the Shadow King's face down a trash can, run it over with a lawnmower, grind it into sausage, feed it to Doug, squish what Doug pooped out and throw him over a waterfall. "Elusia, I'm really proud of you," said the Shadow King. She scooted her chair away from him, loudly, inch by inch.

Nigel magically urged Sprinkles to hit him and even lay down on the ground to help. Sprinkles rolled a 1 and a 2, and the Acolytes booed. He smacked his own head, muttering "No, not yet," and admitted that the voice in his head was the friend who'd made the bet. He finally landed a single feeble punch and panicked. The Grandmaster threw him a can of bubbly, which dented both the can and the halfling.

Then Nigel brought out his Tony Award, sang from the musical about Britney Spears, and cast a spell of truth that he deliberately let catch himself. A changeling, he reshaped part of his face into Sprinkles' and asked why Sprinkles needed to please everyone. The truth came out: "It's because the dark passenger that resides inside me scares me a bit, so I have to counteract it with kindness." Nigel confessed in turn that he hated his own work, watched his films to cry and yell at himself, had sculpted his own chin, and sometimes didn't know who he was. "I may just be a face in a box," he mused. That line turned out to be prophetic.

### Meemaws for Days

The cheering Acolytes set off a chaos event. Skeletons clawed out of the dead earth, well armed but wearing sweet old-lady dresses and carrying purses full of Werther's. "Meemaw meemaws for days!" cried the Grandmaster. Sprinkles gave one last warning: "Do you really want to meet the Dark Passenger?" Nigel did.

Sprinkles closed his eyes and opened them on demonic flame. His bones cracked, his body stretched, his jaw dropped, and knife-like claws pushed out of his fingertips. "Finally, now it's time for bloodshed." The first slash was a natural 20, and Nigel only escaped by shifting his face out of the way. Nigel grappled the creature and shoved it face-first into a grandma zombie named Gertrude, who licked its face and pressed the inside of her mouth onto it with her corrosive breath. Then he clubbed it with his Teen Choice Award, which made a sound like the SpongeBob surfboard. The Dark Passenger answered with a frenzy of claws and another natural 20, and the rage rippled into Nigel's mind. Wedged cheek to cheek among the meemaws, both champions received a surge of power from the grandmothers, a gift from the Acolytes passed on through "a re-gifting process."

### Face Off

A pink-and-yellow meteor crashed into the arena: the Box of Chaos. "Champions, this is my former home," said the Shadow King. To give it some juice, his spectral hand snatched a quiet Acolyte named Majestic Wolf, nocked them on a giant shadow bow and fired them across the arena. Majestic Wolf broke their neck on the side of the box and fell in. Their soul crawled back out with yellow eyes: "Let's make a deal."

Nigel shoved his head in, "going full Nicolas Cage," and offered the skin of his face. Clawed hands sawed it off, and the box spirit slapped it on itself. The Grandmaster's verdict: "Face off." In exchange Nigel got a stone of fortitude and vowed, "I'm gonna win this Oscar without a face." He snapped his Teen Choice Award over his knee, watched the soul of SpongeBob leave it, and beat the Dark Passenger with the pieces and a SAG award. Then he performed a fleshless-faced dance so unhinged that the Grandmaster invented a new category: Nick Cage damage. Nobody is immune to it. The Acolytes were left stunned. "Sprinkles has been hurt physically," said the Dark Passenger, "but I have been hurt emotionally."

Sprinkles' body fed all its friendship bracelets to the box, while a voice inside screamed that they were worth so much. The box threw them back. A small pet cat wandered in and was now wearing Nigel's face. Finally the box produced a cursed cloak made of Nigel's face, stitched together with Sprinkles' bracelets, which let its wearer become Nigel. "I'm already cursed," said the Dark Passenger. "I have a demon living inside my brain." Nigel loved it: "Me versus me. I'm killing myself. This is art."

Meanwhile the Grandmaster had six goblins drag the grandmothers off through a dark portal. They reappeared at the Shadow King's seat and beat him with their purses. "Shame on you, Sonny!" He took it without moving. Then his eyes flashed yellow and the grandmothers flew into the sky, where some of them are said to float still, unable to land.

### Nigel's Last Performance

Nigel decided he had to be the villain so his nemesis could be the hero. "All the best performances happen after you're dead." He kicked open the Box of Chaos and jumped in whole. The shadow spirit inside skinned him and came out wearing his body like a suit. It took the form of a shadow Robert De Niro and drew a dull, diseased toe knife, a gift from its best friend Danny DeVito. The blow it landed left Sprinkles' body with an infected wound.

Then the Dark Passenger gave its true name: **[[Melchizedek]], the Dark One**, come "to find a more suitable host." Melchizedek used the face-cloak to seal itself inside Nigel's occupied corpse. "I want a new home!" The arena changed. The fight now took place inside Nigel's body, spirit against spirit, for ownership of it. Sprinkles' abandoned body was left wandering outside: "Oh, gee, what the heck is going on, guys?"

Melchizedek's first move was to threaten the body's bladder: "I'm gonna make Nigel piss. It's gonna smell like asparagus!" Inside, Nigel's essence appeared as a bright-eyed little Newsie, a schoolboy who had just done his first musical and been told for the first time that he was worth something. Melchizedek's true form was a nine-headed dragon-snake. It froze the boy in mid box step. Then a chaos surge arrived: the Chinese food Nigel had eaten earlier "isn't meant to sustain both of you," and an avalanche of chunky vomit and acid swept both spirits to opposite shoulders. As the narration put it, the only true Nigel left was Nigel the Goblin, still riding in the baby carrier on the corpse's back.

### Footloose

The boy broke free by tap dancing, singing "Now is the time to seize the day." Melchizedek loathed dancing, until Nigel started the Footloose dance and Melchizedek, with a natural 1, was utterly charmed. "Do you have a bad demon daddy?" the child asked. Melchizedek explained that it was "an angel who was scorned from heaven for my transgressions against my god," and added, "But this dancing is pretty fucking tight." The boy suggested channeling those God issues into art. A roll on a hastily built table of art forms landed on yodeling, and Melchizedek yodeled. It was really good.

Charmed or not, Melchizedek beat the boy with an Emmy and a SAG award, "awards you have not yet won." The Shadow King declared it boring and said he had a dentist appointment. The Grandmaster talked him into one more round. He swelled Nigel's body to gargantuan size and sliced the chest open so the Acolytes could see. Outside, Sprinkles' body and Nigel the Goblin gardened together, and a beautiful flower field bloomed at the foot of the split-open corpse. Sprinkles was thriving.

### The Final Blow

"You see, kid, don't lose your passion. We're all gonna kill our demons, but unfortunately, my demon is my own," the child told Sprinkles. Then he picked up a broken bone from his own corpse and imagined it was an Oscar. He stabbed Melchizedek in the heart three times, "almost like killing myself… ego death style." Then he lay down and watched an imagined Oscar acceptance speech as he faded. Melchizedek's last thought was that it had been an unfulfilling existence: "hurt people hurt people."

The Grandmaster raised her goblet to the champions for taking ownership of an identity and claiming it as their own. "Shadow King, are you not entertained?" He admitted it was "a very satisfying ending, in fact," and said it deserved an award. Then he raised a hand, and green slime rained from the heavens until it filled the arena. Nigel and Sprinkles, both still lying there, drowned in it. There was no winner.

## Dried Blood

Sprinkles' parting words were about the darkness inside: it's okay to have it, and sometimes the only answer is "to live through spite. Not to spite anyone else, but to drive all of your anger to the part of yourself that says you can't do it." Nigel's were about craft: "Since the dawn of time, storytelling has been one of the most important things for people to share with each other." He urged everyone to tell stories because they care about the stories, not about how they look telling them.
