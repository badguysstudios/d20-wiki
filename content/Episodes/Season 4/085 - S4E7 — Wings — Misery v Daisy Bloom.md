### Round 1

Or perhaps the sting of steel.**" He attacks with advantage against the prone Daisy and **crits on a 19** with the greatsword. Daisy: "This might not last the whole night, you guys."

Bec asks for perception, since two walls block her view. Daisy only sees **a dusty butterfly fly over: "a small little bit of hope," a sign there is life here somewhere.**
24. **0:19:44 — The tears.** Misery stands stoic. Up close, Daisy notices that **Misery has been crying the entire time, a slow steady stream of tears.** Bec adds that **sand sticks to the tears and builds small mounds at his feet**, which are later called **sandcastles.** Misery: "That's not cute."

Misery: "maybe I can grant you the sweet release of, well…" **He misses, and his greatsword dives into the dirt.** As Daisy crawls away, **Misery leans down and stares into the blade, "as if somewhere else. Memories, memories, memories."**

She lies dramatically among the cacti in "a very vogue moment" and tries not to touch them. **Acolytes, some of whom have clearly been hurt, yell down: "Don't touch the cacti!"** It's "just an echo in the wind."

Daisy: "It's not a steed, it's a friend. Summon friend."

Misery: "Maybe you should've asked for their name before this." The moth answers: "**My name's Janet.**" Daisy: "Thank you, Janet… Something amazing is gonna come from this." Janet nods: "**I'm here for you. Let me lift you.**" **Janet rises to seven inches.** Daisy: "**That's what the power of friendship can do for you. Believe it.**"

Misery: "You're not here to learn, you're here to kill each other." ### Round 2 — the death of Janet
32. **0:33:00** Misery to the crowd: "**Is this a mockery to me? Is this some kind of sick joke? This is what you put me against? This cannot be over fast enough.**" He saunters past the wall, dragging the greatsword through the sand, nose to nose with Daisy. "**This will hurt me more than it hurts Janet.**" He attacks the steed.
33. **0:34:30** **Janet is slain.** "There's like a warp, like the frame rate changed a little bit." At the end of the strike, **Janet slides apart. No blood comes out; black and white butterflies escape the body, fly up, turn to dust and blow away.**
34. **0:34:59** **The dust drifts up into the stands, and the Acolytes choke on "the dead mini versions of Janet."** Bec: "Baby Janets are ashy… **We are all Janet. We have all now consumed some Janet in our life.**" Daisy argues this empowers her, since everyone now carries a piece of her friend.
35. **0:35:55 — Daisy's backstory.** Elusia asks for a story where Daisy ate a dead friend and was empowered. Daisy tells it:
    - She grew up "a scion of village town." She was an orphan: both parents were killed in "a really bizarre planned thing by a **supernatural force of evil**," something "going on my whole life."
    - She was **chosen "by this, like, alien" to defeat them.**
    - She was there, aged "three or eight," lying between her eviscerated parents in their blood.
    - She "learned to be this, like, lethal fighter… good at everything that I tried to do because that's, like, kind of how this goes."
    - She **lolled her tongue out and slurped up all the blood**, "a couple liters maybe," with "a lot of dirt in it… vitamins and minerals." Then she "popped up like, you know, **pushing daisies**."
    - Afterward "**I took out like 15 villages**… I don't even think that they were all like a part of it." Seeing a mob is the only thing that calms her, "because they were there too and they drank some of the blood with me."
37. **0:39:32 — 7-Up.** Elusia: "**I can't even drink one liter of 7-Up**… 7-Up's gross, but my family's blood mixed with dirt is perfectly fine… clearly, you've lost your mind." Shadow King: "Get out of here with that 7-Up!" **The crowd/DMs award Daisy a d6 inspiration for the story.**
38. **0:40:09 — Misery's first flashback.** As he listens, Misery has flashbacks in still frames: **children laughing, the flash of a blade, Misery on his knees at a grave.** He cries even more, and the sandcastles grow. The dust of Janet lies at his feet.

Bec says no: "I'm gonna let you drink blood and heal from that… but I'm not gonna let you take a spike into your body and be like, what if the damage is healing?" She contests, citing her plate, but Misery wins (18). **Needles get into the joints of her plate, the armpits and the backs of the knees.** Daisy: "**This is like when you fall in snow and it goes in your butt**… Ruins your whole life." Her life lesson: "You see a new cactus… you get a new boyfriend, you're like, I can change him… **But the cactus always gets you in the end, baby.**"
40. **0:43:40** **Misery grinds one of his sandcastles back into the earth with his heel, looks at Daisy and gestures: bring it, bring more.**

### Round 5 — Daisy becomes the moth

## Who and what appears
### Characters
**Misery** (DeeEmSteve). A fighter, "a good, even six foot," who calls himself "the embodiment of misery and pain and stuff."
- **Cries constantly.** His tears gather sand into little sandcastles at his feet.
- Butterflies follow him and are part of his imagery: he crushes a black butterfly, Janet dissolves into butterflies, and butterflies meld into his sword.
- "No longer experience[s] any joy from food or drink"; "Misery doesn't like anything." He calls poetry a waste of his skills.
- **Two fighting styles, one of them Blind Fighting.** Also has Indomitable, Action Surge, Second Wind and Extra Attack.
- **Weapons:** a greatsword carried on his back, and a **Vorpal Greatsword** from the Box of Chaos, reshaped into a void "blade of nothingness."
- **Flashbacks (stated):**
  - children laughing, a blade, Misery at a grave
  - a beloved friend hugs him, then a knife goes through someone and Misery coughs blood
  - Misery's eyes glow and wings sprout from his back, and the friend's silhouetted head slides off
  - Misery, bandaged in the gut, pulls a greatsword marker from a fresh grave and walks into a sunset
- **Inferred** from 0:59:59, 1:14:46, 1:32:26 and 1:41:54: a friend he loved betrayed him, stabbing him in the back. Misery, sprouting wings, beheaded the friend, buried him, and took the sword from the grave. The episode title "Wings" likely refers to this. This is not stated outright.
- **He had a prior "promise"/"deal" with the Shadow King** (0:14:10, 1:41:54).
- **After winning, he asked to swap places with "him":** "Put me in the ground and give him a life." The Shadow King sealed Misery in a stone coffin underground. Misery smiled for the first time in years.

**Daisy Bloom, Beacon of Village Town** (TAYvanbiljon). A paladin ("Captain Paladin," per Bec) in plate.
- **Gear and abilities:** two warhammers (Two-Weapon Fighting), a javelin, three lanterns and a glow stick. She casts Find Steed and Sanctuary, has "stupid protection spells" but no healing, speaks Primordial, and animal handling is her dump stat. She has a "mysterious patron."
- **Lore and personality:**
  - Her makeup "is not makeup"; she was "born this way."
  - Her favorite sauce is ketchup.
  - She brings "the power of friendship and love and light" and came to make Misery her friend.
  - Her orphan story: parents killed by a supernatural force of evil; chosen by an alien; drank liters of her parents' blood mixed with dirt; "took out like 15 villages"; mobs calm her.
  - Pockets her rib bones.
  - Says she pulls different steeds from the Void each time.
  - Devoted to Criss Angel (has a poster, prays to him).
  - Over 30.
- **Transformation:** cast Find Steed inside herself and became 12% moth (moth on top, human legs).
- Thigh-choked Misery. Decapitated by Misery's vorpal crit.

**Janet** (voiced by Bec). A glowing, mid-sized moth summoned by Find Steed, with a harness. She told Daisy her name, carried her 6–7 inches off the ground and winked at her ("You be you"). **Cut apart by Misery. She dissolved into black and white butterflies and dust, which the Acolytes breathed in: "We are all Janet."** Once called "Jaina."

**The Shadow King** (Drew).
- Rules from the Dread Fortress throne room ("my throne room"). Smokes ribs low and slow with Kansas City sweet sauce. Goblins serve him.
- "I might have killed your friend and taken over… your throne and your empire, but I'm not a monster."
- Killed Acolyte Forty and fed his soul to the Box of Chaos. Raised the sand tornado.
- Honored Misery's deal with the stone-coffin burial.
- Claims nobody has seen him and Criss Angel in the same place.

**Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer** (Bec).
- **Brings champions by cutting her hand/forearm with her amulet to open a blood portal** ("pour down quite easily at this point").
- Hates vinegar and mustard barbecue sauces. Says she and the Shadow King are "still somewhat related." Can't drink one liter of 7-Up.
- Prefers wine to "human beans."
- Gave both champions Heelys; she has never owned Heelys herself. She was upset when no one thanked her.
- Ran the dodgeball event. Rules the arena "strictly David Copperfield only."
- "Shadow King just put that one in my pocket" (unclear).

**Forty / "40 man"**. An Acolyte (chat member) who brought a box with a hole cut in it, which held his own genitals. The Shadow King killed him with a shadow blade and put his black soul in the Box of Chaos. His body was left on the field.

**Souls in the Box of Chaos:** Gator, Blissful, Vegeta and Scribd (named by Bec; "they're all adults… just souls"). **A creature with two yellow eyes and a clawed hand** is inside the Box.

**"Him"**: the friend Misery asked to be given life in his place ("He was always better than me. He was everything to me."). It isn't shown whether he was brought back.

### Places
- **Dread Fortress**, amid rumbling volcanoes. **The throne room** (now the Shadow King's) has a grill. **A full floor of the fortress is a kitchen.** There is a hallway leading to the throne room.
- **The excavation arena**: abandoned, dusty sand with low (5 ft) walls. People dug until "they found something that they really shouldn't have dug up." It has a tiny cactus garden with pink and yellow blooms. It's one of Elusia's "favorite escapes." Acolytes float above it.
- **Village Town**, Daisy's home.

### Items
- Elusia's amulet (opens portals with her blood).
- Ribs (the goblins' cooler), with Kansas City sweet sauce.
- Light-up name-brand Heelys, one pair each.
- Deep-fried moth nachos sold to the Acolytes.
- **Box of Chaos**: falls from the sky when the Shadow King snaps. It's fed with souls and erupts in heatless pink/yellow fire. Any sacrifice, used as a free action, may yield a magic item.
- **Misery's Vorpal Greatsword (void blade).**
- Forty's box with a hole.

- The Box of Chaos holds Acolyte souls and gives items for sacrifices.
