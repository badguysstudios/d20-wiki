The Season 4 finale set **[[Ricky Red Dragon]]**, a "red dragon" cursed into human flesh after one rave too many, against **[[Traxton]]**, a 45-year-old emo elf who would very much prefer you call him Spider. They fought in a forest clearing [[The Shadow King]] had been quietly turning into a festival venue. Along the way there were crossing fireballs, a magic beard, a Christmas nutcracker, and a coming-of-age story that finished in the worst possible way for everyone involved.

## The Story

### The Secret Pre-Party

[[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|The Grandmaster]] walked the echoing halls of [[The Dread Fortress]], her heels clacking on stone, until she heard techno thumping up from somewhere deep in its dungeon-like depths. She threw open the doors of the Shadow King's throne room and found strobe lights, lasers, and the German techno song from *Super Troopers* playing on loop. "Well, this is embarrassing," said the King. "You should have knocked first." He shoved a keg behind his throne.

The lights drew a crowd of [[Goblins|goblins]] who treated the music "as if this is their soul music." The Shadow King tried to blame the song on "the one with the hair," which did not work, because every goblin was bald. A lice problem a couple of weeks earlier had been solved with a full shave. Once the King admitted to liking "that girl music," the Grandmaster ordered him to share the keg. The goblins mobbed it, ripped it open, and drank from wherever it poured: the floor, their hands, each other.

The bickering continued. "Well, if you were more fun, I would have invited you to the party," the King told her. He then asked what the party was even *for*, since Sunday nights for him meant reruns of *Heroes*. She reminded him that he was the king and was supposed to represent them. His reply was "I am so tired of you." She clenched her amulet, its bladed edge slicing her fingers, and her blood opened a blue portal for the champions.

### Enter the Dragon (Allegedly) and the Spider

Ricky arrived in sunglasses and flip-flops. "My name's Ricky Red Dragon. I'm a red dragon," he explained. A curse at a techno concert had trapped him in a squishy human body, and he was fighting for the prize: his scales back. The Grandmaster called that "absolutely doable." Spider introduced himself as "pretty much the coolest elf from my part of the woods" and told Ricky he was sorry about his stupid skin. Ricky shot back, "They don't call me Chromatic Red for nothing."

The Shadow King had a soft moment with Ricky, as someone who "was once a shadow monster" and knew what it was like to lack flesh: "I had skin and then I didn't have skin and now I have skin again." Ricky's sunglasses meant he couldn't tell if the King had a tail. Maybe there were five. Spider got a cooler reception. The King complained that they'd had "like four teenagers die this season." Spider explained, "My mom called me Traxton," and that Spider was one word, "like Seal." The King decided he could die. When Spider called his rival "Idiot Dragon Man" and doubted he looked like a dragon, the King agreed, betraying their bonding moment without a flicker: "You're here for my entertainment, don't take it personally."

### The Coachella Ground

The arena was a forest clearing full of loud birds, with a ring of shiny purple portal circles in the middle and lightning-like energy dancing between them. The Shadow King admitted, "I was building a venue for the next Coachella." The Grandmaster had no interest in festival crowds leaving trash in her space. "I gift you this Shadow King's potential Coachella ground," she told the fighters, "and I beg of you, destroy it." She spread her white wings before a sky filled with the screaming faces of [[The Acolytes|the Acolytes]] and made her speech. The fight was a gift to them, and the champions' tale would be written upon their hearts, "perhaps in a freaking EDM song."

### Darkness and Butthurt

Ricky asked about the rules ("Murder, maim, pummel into the ground…"), then teleported thirty feet up a tree to take the high ground. With no wings, he asserted dominance by T-posing. It worked so well that Spider was rattled. Spider answered by casting magical darkness on him. "I'm not afraid of the dark, bitch. I'm an adult," said Spider. "I am terrified of the dark," said Ricky, who promptly fell out of the tree backside first. "If only you had a tail to land on," the Grandmaster sighed. Spider didn't notice the fall and kept shouting insults at the empty treetop.

The Shadow King couldn't see the action either, so he made his eyes glow yellow to pierce the darkness. When the Grandmaster insisted the Acolytes deserved the same, he snapped his fingers. A wave of shadow rolled through the stands like a stadium wave, and every Acolyte's eyes turned yellow.

The crowd's roar spurred both champions on. Ricky stumbled out of the dark, spotted the glowing eyes, and poured "all of my butthurt anger" into a banishment spell. Spider snuffed the spell out. Ricky tried to snuff the snuffing and failed. Taking pity, the Grandmaster "banished" Spider anyway, into the Dark Circle, which was just his own darkness. "I said I wasn't afraid of the dark," Spider admitted. "I may have been lying." Ricky gave away his position by yelling back that Spider was the one who sucked. The Shadow King asked where she had found the two stupidest people on the planet. "It was a Longhorn Steakhouse," she replied. Spider had ordered the salad. "It's healthy, and I'm gonna nutritionally kick your ass."

### Angst, Beards and Dabbing

Spider dropped his own darkness, spotted Ricky, and hurled a psychic javelin made of "all of my teenage angst." "You ever heard of My Chemical Romance? It's going 24-7 over here." Ricky, nursing a headache, adjusted his fake beard. The Shadow King was appalled to learn it was fake. Ricky explained, "It shows everybody that I'm 6,012 years old."

Then Spider dabbed. Ricky was charmed ("I kinda like this guy… Do I have to kill him?"). The Grandmaster was not. She threw her whole carton of buttered popcorn at Spider's head, which wasn't even vegan. "Never dab in my arena ever again." A goblin wrote the new rule in a notepad: no more dabbing in the arenas.

### Chin Dragon

After all that talk about scales beating fur, the Grandmaster clapped and a gigantic [[Chinchillas|chinchilla]] charged through the forest. She named it Chin Dragon, and the Shadow King asked why she couldn't just call it Chinzilla. The champions briefly agreed to team up against it. It simply trampled both of them and ran out of the arena. "Our peace treaty was short-lived," said Spider. "All it took was a pair of cute fuzzy paws," the Grandmaster gloated. "Suck it."

### The Circles and the Box

Ricky flicked a firebolt at Spider that zigzagged "like clip art," then stepped onto a portal circle. It coated him in purple armor, but he now had to open every turn with a lyric from an annoying song, starting with "Friday." Spider stepped onto another circle and was turned into a fuzzy fruit bat with a blonde tuft over one eye. "Damn it, I shouldn't have named myself bat." The Grandmaster dubbed him Bat Boy, "because you haven't hit puberty." His bite missed. Ricky complained about his breath ("It's called halitosis, and it's a medical condition") and handed him a mint, then the whole bin.

Baffled by this kindness, the Grandmaster summoned [[The Box of Chaos]]. A pink and purple chest fell from the sky like a comet. The Shadow King lifted an Acolyte named Toolkit 71 from the crowd ("I'm going to need your soul. You can keep your body!"), ripped them in half, and slammed their soul into the box. Shadowy claws and yellow eyes peeked out. "Actually, it's a funny story," the King explained. "I used to be the box of chaos." He added, "I killed the guy who made the box." The box's maker was [[The Arbiter]].

Ricky sacrificed a spare unwashed beard, and the box's tormented occupant came out wearing it. After some buffering ("Do you think we'd lay fiber out here?"), it offered him "the beards of many things." Ricky put on the white one. It felt like a warm summer's day and turned him tougher and wiser on the spot. "Ricky," the Arbiter-voice of the table declared, "you pulled the Sun card." Spider offered the box everything he had for one thing: "If you have any extra puberty in there to give, I need it desperately." Puberty hit, and it hit hard. The box also handed him a wooden, unmistakably phallic rod holding one fireball. "This is what manhood feels like."

### Crossing Fireballs

Spider fired the rod. Ricky fired back. The two fireballs met tip to tip in the air, sealed by Ricky's perfect natural 20, and the explosion engulfed them both. The Grandmaster tried to describe it from above and gave up partway: "I'm not finishing this." Ricky took the full blast, was left barely standing, and collapsed. He played dead convincingly, tongue out. He was, after all, "a dragon that goes to raves… People die at parties all the time."

The heat woke a second chaos event: healing rods of various sizes sprouted from the ground like fungus, smelling of pineapple and, on one side, asparagus. Still "dead," Ricky whispered "Somebody once told me…", rolled over in a fake death spasm, and licked one back to health. He then conjured an illusion of the Acolyte Ristin8012 beside Spider, which kept saying "Look, he's dead. Don't attack him." Spider believed it completely. "I've never killed a man before. Does that make me a man if I've killed another man?" He tried to high-five the illusion, then went and stood straddling Ricky's body, flexing with his rod.

### The Wall and the Nutcracker

"That is his biggest folly of the night," said Ricky. "Oops, I did it again. I cast Wall of Force. Goodbye, bitch." A wall of force shot up between Spider's legs and launched him twenty feet into the air. His newly dropped testicles survived the landing. Ricky crawled to the box and dove in face-first. It turned out to be shallow. From the trash and bones at the bottom he pulled a classic Christmas nutcracker, a fine magical maul painted in what he called many colors. He was colorblind and wearing sunglasses. "All I know is I'm a red dragon!"

### The Final Blow

Spider was furious. "Why would you blow my testicles away like that?" He unleashed a destructive wave from his mouth, all his unresolved teenage angst at once. Ricky had one last piece of wisdom: "All that angst you have built up, it never goes away." "No," Spider gasped. "It can't be true." Ricky crumbled to dust. His final words went to the Shadow King: **"I'm not really a dragon."** "You son of a bitch," the King replied. The Grandmaster threw the popcorn bowl full of rocks she had been saving at Ricky's dusty face, for the emotion of it all. "Dragons don't have to tell you they're dragons," the King grumbled. "You just know."

The Grandmaster saluted the winner: "Spider, today you have grown in more than one way. We are proud of you." Spider put on Ricky's sunglasses and told the crowd, "Stay cool, cool babies," and everyone wept. Then the Shadow King dissolved into shadow and reappeared behind him. "You've kind of grown into the name… let me help you truly look the part." **Spider shrank into a tarantula, and the King booted him out of the arena.** Rising into the air, the King closed the season with a promise: "While I'm around, there will be death in the arena."

## Dried Blood

In the after-show, Spider's player said he built the character while chatting with his tall, 19-year-old nephew, and that the two champions would have had "cool uncle-nephew vibes" in a longer campaign. This was Ricky's player's first Deathmatch. He had kept mouthing off and waited for a thunderbolt that never came. The Grandmaster explained the line: "You can insult me. It's when you insult my arena that I get real pissed." He called the crossing fireballs "the most passionate and potentially satisfying encounter that this show has ever had." Pressed on whether Ricky was a dragon, he first said no, then insisted he was.
