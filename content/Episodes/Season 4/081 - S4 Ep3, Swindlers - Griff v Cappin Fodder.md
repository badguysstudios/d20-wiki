## What happens
### Opening: the throne room of the Dread Fortress
1. **0:01:26** Bec narrates. The Dread Fortress halls are dark and eerie, lit only by candlelight. **The Grandmaster huffs and growls her way to the throne room.** When she pushes open the doors she hears "twist it, flick it, bop it." **The Shadow King sits on his throne surrounded by Bop-Its, playing one. Whenever it calls "bop it," he bops the four goblins standing around him on the head instead.** The goblins are bruised and "clearly… the subject of abuse once again." Broken Bop-Its are stacked in a tower that keeps growing along the side of the throne.
2. **0:02:50** The Shadow King: "**These are all defective. Obviously they're not working correctly or I would have won.**" The Grandmaster tells him to stop hitting the staff, who are breaking along with the toys: "you pop it with your hand, you have two of them, use them." The Shadow King demands another Bop-It. **A goblin named Hagrid ("Hagrad"), according to his name tag, stumbles out in fear and tosses him a new one.**
3. **0:03:34** The Shadow King fumbles through it and scores **an 11**. The Grandmaster: "That's not even impressive… **I got more when I was 12. You gave me a Bop-It as my freaking birthday present one year. You saw me get to like 52.**" She refuses to play ("That is a child's game") and informs him that **a Bop-It 2** now exists. "Bop-It 2?"
4. **0:04:13** The Shadow King: "Shouldn't you have champions for me?" She agrees, "if it means you'll stop beating on our poor little goblins." **He smacks one more goblin.** Two goblins arrive with a small stretcher, clearly used to this, and carry Hagrid out. The Grandmaster apologizes for mispronouncing his name, promises to talk to **HR** about a pronunciation guide, and Hagrid gives a small wave of thanks. **She tells Thomas, "one of her favorite goblins," to get Hagrid a gift basket or fruit basket** for his bedside (heard as "possible debt").
5. **0:05:04 The summoning.** "I brought for you two champions… we'll demonstrate to you the worthiness of our realm." **She grips the amulet, which cuts blood into her hand as it drips. The ground rises into a circle, and two opponents step through:** "I give you Griff and Cappin Fodder."
6. **0:05:39 Cappin Fodder's introduction.** "I can't believe I'm finally here. I am Cappin Fodder." He'd been having a snack to get pumped up. He is "a halfling pirate, the bestest pirate in the world," and anyone who stumbles on him gets their kneecaps [done in]. He calls Griff "a scrawny looking satyr… You are more than a midnight snack."
7. **0:06:26 The insult exchange.**
   - Griff: "There's a lot of people who call me a snack." He adds that **his kneecaps go the other direction**, so the kneecapping will be harder than Fodder imagined.
   - Fodder: "Your kneecaps go backwards?… That's unnatural." Griff: "we don't need your prejudices in here." Fodder "does not know the meaning of the word." Griff: "Yeah, that's apparent."
   - Fodder: "You look like you must have been born on the highway, because that's where most accidents happen."
   - Griff: "**I get that the first 40 years of childhood are the hardest**, but it doesn't mean that you have to just say the first thing that comes to your mind."
8. **0:07:40 Griff's introduction.** "My name is Griff and I am… a games player, a bar fly." Some would call him a pool shark, "but **my game is more like Stratego or Go or Pente**. And I love to hustle… and convince people that they've got a chance of winning when indeed they do not."
9. **0:08:21 The Monkmaker.** Fodder: "You a fibber. I don't deal with liars… I deal with pirates, rogues, and thieves." **On his ship is "the Monkmaker. It's a pair of scissors. Snip, snip… we filled a whole monastery full of them."** The Shadow King: "Well, I like the sound of that. **My money's on this one.**" Fodder: "I better be getting a cut of that."
10. **0:09:11** Griff starts a height joke ("He does have to aim high with his, never mind"). Fodder (inferred speaker): "I don't wanna hurt you, old man… It's the last thing on my list, but it's still on my list." Griff (inferred speaker): "Do you have a shortlist?"

12. **0:09:58 The music goblin.** The Grandmaster says the music "sounds like a rocking wedding intro." The Shadow King: "One of the music goblins must have taken it upon himself. Don't worry, I'll kill him." **A shadow blade comes out of his arm and chops the top off the music goblin's head.**
13. **0:10:18** The Grandmaster: "we bring you two together in holy matrimony for a fight of the ages." **She rips the amulet into two pieces, splitting a space in time into a portal.** Fodder: "**Those who die first shall go first.**" Griff: "After you then." Fodder walks through.

### The arena: the temple
14. **0:11:01** A temple, old and broken, with uneven stones. It's "foreign and cold" to the champions, but the Acolytes find it "quite familiar." The fire gives off a light glow, yet it's cold all around. **A large statue of "a great acolyte" stands with palms open**, surrounded by candles and small artifacts, in "an unearthly and unholy place." It's "a temple of doom of our own… There's no rolling balls that we know of." Fodder: "It won't be if it's the one that I know." Rats scurry by.
15. **0:12:06** **There is no ceiling. Above, in an ethereal, ghostly way, thousands of Acolytes circle, cheering**, with slushies, flags and lit sigils. Named in the crowd: **Pethel, Master of Squids**; Oddthority; Crystid; Ombad; Scribd; Gator; Harvey; Night Garriant.
16. **0:12:57 The jumper.** Fodder: "**Hey, you up there. Top row. Jump.**" Someone does. **The goblins hurry out to clean up the mess**: "It's supposed to be one of your two blood first, not another one." Fodder: "I'm just that influential."
17. **0:13:24** Griff: "**If they really wanted to kill themselves, they'd climb up your ego and jump off your intelligence.**" Fodder: "Are you reading from the same list of insults that I am?" Griff: "I don't need a list. I just need to look at you." "Bring it on, old goat man."
18. **0:13:44** **An intimidation contest decides initiative. Fodder wins, 22 to 19.**
19. **0:14:18 The statue is Vythorn.** The Shadow King: "**I'm enjoying the atmosphere here with the large statue of Vythorn there. It's very gauche.**" He has them start face to face. The Grandmaster: "It's actually like stomach to face." "Oh yes, he's very short."

### Round 1
20. **0:15:02 Griff described.** A medium-sized satyr with goat legs and backwards knees, and breeches ending just above the knee. Hooves clamp to the cobblestones. Brown leathers over a green tunic. **His horns are decorated with beads and baubles of different sizes along their length**, and the hair between them is pulled back in a tight braid. He carries **a simple staff** and has "a gleam in his eye."
21. **0:15:51 Fodder described.** A typical halfling pirate in brown leathers. **He wears a bandana wrapped over his eyes with little slits cut in it so he can see**, a "weird intimidation factor" that doesn't really work. **His whip is about his own length but reaches 10 feet.**
22. **0:16:25 Disarm.** **Fodder whips the staff out of Griff's hand** and takes it. "Is this yours?" "You gonna give that back?" "**Nope!**" He runs 25 ft, shouts to the audience "**This is for you!**" and hurls the staff, pointy end first. Griff declines the opportunity attack: "I don't need to be timey-wimey to beat him."
23. **0:19:40 Harvey's new hat.** **The staff hits Acolyte Harvey Hesko in the temple.** Fodder: "That's what he gets for not keeping his eye on the fight." The Grandmaster: "**Pay attention!**… It's a new hat. It's all the rage these days." The staff stays in Harvey's head.
24. **0:20:12 The satyr dance.** **Fodder mimics a satyr's dance** (performance 16). Griff rolls history to judge it. The Grandmaster narrates that **Griff is "almost a little ashamed" that Fodder does a satyr dance better than he does.** Griff: "is that a truffle shuffle?… **It's the dance we do when we hunt for truffles in the Fae realm.**" He declines to demonstrate.
25. **0:22:02** The staff is about 45 ft up in the first level of the stands. Griff walks up to Fodder and looks him in the eye: "that kind of pissed me off… **My eyes are down here. I have not yet begun to piss you off.**"
26. **0:22:22 Griffy is born.** **Griff snatches up one of the passing rats, and it transmutes in flames into his Wildfire Spirit, a burning phoenix** hovering beside him. Fodder makes the save. Griff: "I assume rats aren't cute… You should have seen this one." Bec: "It looked like your mother." Fodder: "**Don't you just love nature despite what it did to your face?**"
27. **0:23:41** **Griff and the phoenix teleport 15 ft back in a burst of flame.** Fodder saves again. Griff flips away, winks, and says, "bring it on." Fodder: "**Cappin Fodder never runs from a fight.**… He really should. There was that one time where she was very big." The Shadow King: "Talk about your mama."

### Chaos event: Dolly (0:26:02)
28. The Shadow King rolls 18 ("Bow down before me"). The Grandmaster: "We're not gonna bow down before you because you figured out how to roll a dice." She has Griff roll instead. **The Acolytes summon a chaotic event, and a nat 20 lets the Grandmaster "pick my favorite one."**
29. **0:26:58** A rumble under their feet. Tiles shift as if a gopher were rummaging beneath them, and small laughter escapes. One tile slowly and magically rises to reveal a hole. **Two glassy, unblinking eyes with long lashes look out.** "Will you be my…" **"It's Dolly, our favorite haunted doll,"** holding out her hands to both of them.
30. **0:28:38** **Griff points at Fodder: "He will. He'll do it."** Fodder turns to Griff and "very, very seriously" apologizes for his earlier words. Then he rummages and **pulls out a blunderbuss**: "I was not there before, but thank you." "I don't know how to shoot the doll."

### Round 2
31. **0:29:21** Fodder: "This thing needs to die." **His blunderbuss shot hits (16), and three of Dolly's fingers shatter on the ground. Without moving her shoulders, her head turns toward him and her eyes glow.** The Grandmaster: "your blunderbuss knows better than you… A haunted porcelain doll. It's smarter than Fodder is."
32. **0:30:58** **Fodder tries to punt Dolly toward Griff** and misses (12). She rises from the hole, floating on her own. **As a reaction she casts unholy fire on Fodder. He fails, and she burns his toes and fingers "as she had lost hers."** Griff: "it's all fun and games when you're rolling against me, but get the little doll and you just freak out."
33. **0:32:16** Dolly turns to Griff with both hands out: "**Will you be my friend?**" Griff: "I didn't do nothing to you. Yeah, let's be friends."
34. **0:32:53** **Fodder "boot scoot boogie"s 30 ft away in a crab scuttle**: "Nope, nope, nope." The Grandmaster: "as you skedaddle-daddle away."
35. **0:33:26** Dolly: "**Please be my friend.**" Griff: "**You like party tricks?**" Dolly: "Yes, but I don't like it alone." She keeps coming closer with her hands out.
36. **0:33:54 The party trick.** Griff: "just imagine this is a birthday party." **He starts singing happy birthday, "the non-copyright version,"** and **casts Scorching Ray. All three rays hit, the last a glancing blow, boosted by the phoenix.** Dolly's hair singes off, one ray takes off the whole right side of her face, and another hits her core. Griff, to Fodder: "could have been you."
37. **0:35:46 The explosion.** **Dolly soaks in the fire, her cracks rumble, she glows an unholy green, and she explodes in a poison bomb.** Both must save, Griff at a higher DC because he's closer. The stench is foul, and **Griff takes poison damage that eats at his throat and lungs.** The Grandmaster: "**How's that ceramic Dutch oven?**" Reply (speaker uncertain, probably Griff): "It's like Thanksgiving with grandpappy. Chainsmoker."
38. **0:37:27** "Dolly is no more." The Grandmaster notes for D20 history: **"this is the first time Dolly has been attacked by both players."** The Shadow King: "**It really shows a couple of psychopaths here, attacking a doll.**" Fodder: "You're the one who resurrected that thing from the ground." The Shadow King: "Don't look at me, I didn't create the doll, this is all hers thing." The Grandmaster: "**Dolly has a mind of her own**." Her shattered pieces are gone.
39. **0:38:13** Griff takes "an existential crisis" as a free action. **He names the phoenix Griffy ("It's counterintuitive, but I like it").** Griffy attacks Fodder "who made me waste a spell slot on creepy doll" with fire (a 27 to hit). **Fodder's shirt burns away a little more.** Griff: "That's for getting that doll summoned… you're throwing my stick away." Fodder: "**It's a deathmatch. Read the room.**"
40. **0:40:34 Harvey returns the staff.** Griff asks the crowd who else wants to get banged in the head by a thing Fodder stole. **Harvey pulls the staff out of his skull and throws it at Fodder.** It misses and lands at Fodder's feet.
41. **0:41:09 Tug of war.** **Griffy dives for the stick, and Fodder uses his reaction to fight for it.** Griffy talks in a Southern accent ("y'all"; Griff: "he's an extension of me"). **Fodder wins easily (23 vs 14)**: "no chicken nugget." He wrenches it free and launches Griffy away. The following exchange is speaker uncertain (06/02):
    - "Hey, don't you hurt my bird."
    - "It's the value pack. It comes with fire sauce."
    - "You belong in a big bag."
    
    Griff: "I was gonna play nice, but mm-mm… **I burned that mangy rat to a crisp on purpose to summon him and you will not disrespect my flaming bird.**" Fodder (inferred): "Oh, spicy chicken nugget, I'm sorry."

### The Box of Chaos (0:43:06)
42. The Acolytes beg for something new, but **the Shadow King's box is empty.** He has slumped over: "I'm awake. Box, box, oh no… Who looks good? One, two, three. **You, Prince Vegeta!**"

45. **0:45:04** Fodder offers "this very valuable and much emotionally attached staff of me." **The Grandmaster mishears "statue of myself"** and is delighted by the idea of him carrying around a commissioned statue of himself: "I'm deaf, but it creates the best stories."
46. **0:45:49** The Shadow King rolls a 3 to match the coming round 3. **The Acolytes gift each champion a hero point**: "if you die with it in your hand, we will be very angry and we'll remember that forever. **Thank you, Harvey. Your aim sucks, though.**"

### Round 3
47. **0:46:39** Fodder throws Griff's staff into the box, hoping for a Vorpal Sword or "**a cannon that can fit me inside it.**"
48. **0:47:06 The steal.** **Griff spends his hero point. Griffy grabs the staff mid-air, Griff casts Shillelagh on it ("thanks for giving that back to me"), and then he throws it into the box himself: "Here you go, box, my staff."** The Grandmaster: "You swiped the gift back and gifted yourself." Fodder: "the goat man's got some moves." Griff: "**I literally have hops. It's on my sheet.**"
49. **0:49:02 The ghost step tattoo.** The box rumbles and flips open, and fire shoots skyward. **The shadow's empty hand stretches across the floor, crawls up Griff's body and brands a tattoo on his left forearm: a picture of "Drippy."** It's **the ghost step tattoo**, with one charge that makes him incorporeal (resistant to bludgeoning, piercing, slashing) until his next turn. Griff to Fodder: "Hey, suck it, you."
50. **0:50:13 Boo.** Fodder boos. Griff: "feed your own ego. I'm busy." The Grandmaster: "you're a ghost, so boo's the appropriate response." **Griff tries to get the whole arena booing for him**, and they all boo ("Boo, you suck!"). Fodder: "it's not that you suck… I get it."
51. **0:51:10 Whip it.** **Fodder closes in and whips Griff's hindquarters**: "Booty hit. Booty hit. Whip it good." The whip lands twice. **As a slasher, he cuts deeper and slows Griff ("You walking with a limp").** Then he runs. Griff: "Coward." Fodder: "**Words can't hurt me.**"
52. **0:53:02** Griff (satyr speed 35) **casts Cure Wounds at 2nd level, boosted by the spirit, for big healing**, "undoing all his hard work." He turns the limp into "a nice walk like a gangster I saw in a movie once," gets close, and **Griffy teleports them both away in flame.** **Fodder burns his Indomitable reroll on the save, still takes the fire**, and admits, "that was not worth my indomitable at all." Griff: "I'm so glad you could accommodate me." Fodder: "You stop using those big boys, Goat Grandpa." Griff: "**I'd agree with you, but then we'd both be wrong.**"
53. **0:55:30 Ghost mode.** **Griffy lands on Griff's shoulder, Griff says "boo" and hits the tattoo, and both go incorporeal** (the Grandmaster allows it). **He bites his thumb at Fodder**: "So you have fun playing with yourself." Fodder: "**Never give Cappin Fodder a chance to get ready.**"
54. **0:56:19** Break: "take your popcorn break." It's cut. On return the Shadow King jokes about the commercial budget (table talk: the "Dice Daddy" ad wins again).

### Round 4: Fodder enters the box

Line on 05, speaker uncertain: "I love myself." Griff: "Doesn't look it." **Vegeta makes room for him: "It's very cozy."** Fodder: "Spin pal, spin pal."

Fodder asks if Vegeta gave it to him. Drew: "**It was once Vegeta… it's Vegeta's soul. It's been repurposed.**" (speaker uncertain, 06): "Thank you. Ouch. No problem." Is it permanent? The Shadow King: "**Unless you have another arm laying around.**" "Okay, I'm good, thank you."
59. **1:02:11** Fodder to Griff: "Mr. Goatman, I'll make you do me a favor. Run into this multiple times." Griff: "Come on over."
60. **1:02:36 Cannon Fodder.** Fodder retcons with **his hero point: the box tips over and launches him like a cannon, spearheading at Griff with the new blade.** But Griff is still incorporeal. **Fodder shoots straight through Griff's chest and hits the ground head first**, taking the only damage. "Totally worth it." On the sound of passing through him (06, speaker uncertain): "It's kind of gross, but I like it." **Griff flips a Go piece and watches him pass**: "**I am so sorry for your illness. You appear to have an acute case of hey look at me disease, and I will cure you of it.**" Griff: "Best hero point I've ever seen."
61. **1:04:48 Flame Strike.** Griff backs out of the splash zone, moving his hands as he lectures: "**If you've ever studied religions… So often, gods of fire and lies and deception. And that's because people will bring fire into their houses. They'll put it in the center of the house and then it betrays them.**" **Flame Strike, fire and radiant, boosted by the spirit. Fodder saves for half.** Fodder: "**You look like a before picture.**" Griff: "Oh, what? The Fire Marshal General?" **Griffy hits him with flame as well.** Fodder: "Watch it, chicken nugget."
62. **1:06:47** The once icy room is now hot, and so are the tiles under Fodder. "There's no fire extinguishers anywhere. No sprinklers… It's not safe." Griff: "**I came in early, made sure there's no fire extinguishers**… disable them so they'll explode instead."

The shadowy creature claws its way out: "**I thought maybe you would like a game of chance.**" It flips **a soul coin.** Griff calls tails and it lands heads, so **instead of temporary HP for Griff, the coin heals Cappin Fodder** ("those burning scars heal, but your shirt does not"). Fodder: "**Do that again, Grandpa Goat.** A couple more of those."

### Round 6: Fodder's end
75. **1:20:02** Fodder is still held and can only think. "**I think very nasty thoughts at you**." The Grandmaster asks for the action he wishes he could take: "**It involves Cappin Fodder's teeth and the legs of Griff roasting over a spit with some sriracha sauce and some garlic powder.**" He fails the save (5).

The Grandmaster: "**Asses to asses, butts to butts.**" "Amen."
82. **1:27:18** **The Grandmaster flies down: "We will never forget you. Oh, that grand booty." She slams the chest shut.** Griff: "Booty's going to chest… he would have wanted it this way." "He was a pirate. Who doesn't love booty?"

## Who and what appears
### Characters
**[[Characters/Griff|Griff]]** (Critical_Dice). A medium-sized satyr with backwards knees, beaded and baubled horns, a braid, brown leathers and a green tunic.
- **Background:** a games player and bar fly, a hustler at Stratego, Go and Pente who lets people think they can win.
- **Powers and gear:**
  - A simple staff, and later a Go set; both were sacrificed to the box.
  - Wildfire Spirit **Griffy**, a phoenix made from a rat.
  - Spells: Scorching Ray, Produce Flame, Cure Wounds, Shillelagh, Flame Strike, Hold Person.
- **What he says about himself:** satyrs do a truffle-hunting dance in the Fae realm. "I literally have hops." He claims to have disabled the temple's fire extinguishers.
- **[[Items/Ghost step tattoo|Ghost step tattoo]]** (a picture of "Drippy") on his left forearm.
- **Winner. The Shadow King shoved him off the ledge instead of rewarding him, and he teleported to safety.** Per Dried Blood, he is now in hiding.

**[[Characters/Cappin Fodder|Cappin Fodder]]** (QuincysTavern). A halfling pirate, "the bestest pirate in the world," with Intelligence 9.
- **Gear:**
  - A bandana over his eyes with slits.
  - A 10-ft whip his own length, a blunderbuss, a dagger and a hand axe.
- **Lore:** his ship has "the Monkmaker" scissors ("filled a whole monastery"). He hates liars. He is a slasher fighter with Indomitable and Action Surge.
- **[[Lore/The Box of Chaos|Box of Chaos:]]** jumped into the box himself. **His right arm was melted off and replaced with a short-sword arm of life stealing, made from Vegeta's repurposed soul.**
- **Death:** crushed into the floor by the Vythorn statue, then burned to ash by Flame Strike. **His ashes were swept into the Box of Chaos.**

**[[Characters/Griffy|Griffy]]**. Griff's phoenix Wildfire Spirit, transmuted from a temple rat. He has a Southern accent ("he's an extension of me"), is called "chicken nugget," and lost a tug of war over the staff to Fodder.

**[[Characters/The Shadow King|The Shadow King]]** (Drew). Rules from the Dread Fortress throne.
- Obsessed with Bop-It: he bops goblins instead of the toy and claims the toys are defective. **He once gave Elusia a Bop-It for her birthday.**
- **Killed a music goblin with a shadow blade.**
- **"I was it once" (the Box of Chaos).**
- **Says he killed Vythorn: "almost sorry… He was a great student."** He animated Vythorn's statue.
- Cheated Griff out of his reward and dismissed the Acolytes.

**[[Characters/Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer]]** (Bec). She is summoning champions for the Shadow King.
- **Summons champions with an amulet that cuts blood into her hand; ripping it in two opens the arena portal.**
- **Wears a choker that tightens and silences her when she defies the Shadow King.**
- Protective of the goblins (Thomas, Hagrid). Got to 52 on Bop-It "when I was 12."
