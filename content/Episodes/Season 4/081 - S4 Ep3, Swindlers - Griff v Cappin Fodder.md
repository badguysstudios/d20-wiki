Two silver-tongued **swindlers** met in a ruined temple under the gaze of a stone Vythorn: **[[Griff]]**, a satyr hustler who wins by letting you think you can, and **[[Cappin Fodder]]**, a halfling pirate with a bandana over his eyes and an insult for every occasion. The trash talk never let up, even as the fight served up a haunted doll, a phoenix made from a rat, and a pirate who threw himself bodily into [[The Box of Chaos]]. Then the Shadow King cheated the winner out of his prize, and nobody could stop him.

## The Story

### Bop-It in the Throne Room
The halls of [[The Dread Fortress]] were dark, lit only by candlelight, as [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] huffed and growled her way to the throne room. Behind the doors she heard a chant: "twist it, flick it, bop it." **[[The Shadow King]]** sat on his throne surrounded by Bop-Its. Every time the toy called "bop it," he bopped one of the four [[Goblins|goblins]] standing around him on the head instead. A tower of broken Bop-Its grew steadily beside the throne. "These are all defective," he declared. "Obviously they're not working correctly or I would have won."

The Grandmaster told him to stop breaking the staff along with the toys: "you pop it with your hand, you have two of them, use them." A terrified goblin named Hagrid, going by his name tag, stumbled out with a fresh Bop-It. The Shadow King fumbled his way to a score of 11. The Grandmaster was not impressed. She had done better as a child, on a Bop-It he had given her for her birthday: "You saw me get to like 52." She refused to play such a child's game and told him, to his astonishment, that a Bop-It 2 now existed.

When he asked where his champions were, she agreed to fetch them, "if it means you'll stop beating on our poor little goblins." He smacked one more goblin anyway. Two goblins arrived with a small stretcher, clearly well practiced, and carried Hagrid away. The Grandmaster apologized for mispronouncing his name, promised to ask HR for a pronunciation guide, and sent [[Thomas the Goblin|Thomas]], one of her favorite goblins, to fetch Hagrid a gift basket.

She gripped her amulet, which cut into her hand until it bled. The ground rose into a circle, and two [[Champions|champions]] stepped through. Cappin Fodder introduced himself as "a halfling pirate, the bestest pirate in the world," promised to do in some kneecaps, and sized up Griff as "more than a midnight snack." Griff pointed out that satyr knees bend the other way, so the kneecapping would be harder than expected. The insults flew from there. Fodder said Griff looked like he'd been "born on the highway, because that's where most accidents happen," and Griff replied, "I get that the first 40 years of childhood are the hardest."

Griff called himself a games player and a bar fly. He was less a pool shark than a man of Stratego, Go and Pente, someone who loves to "convince people that they've got a chance of winning when indeed they do not." Fodder had no patience for liars. He dealt with pirates, rogues and thieves, and his ship carried a pair of scissors called the Monkmaker: "we filled a whole monastery full of them." The Shadow King was charmed. "My money's on this one."

The prize was announced: the survivor would join the hosts for game night. When the entrance music turned out to sound like a rocking wedding intro, the Shadow King blamed a music goblin, grew a shadow blade from his arm and took the top of the goblin's head off. Undaunted, the Grandmaster joined the two champions "in holy matrimony for a fight of the ages" and tore the amulet in two, splitting time into a portal. "Those who die first shall go first," said Fodder. "After you then," said Griff.

### A Temple of Doom
The arena was an old, broken temple of uneven stones, cold despite its firelight. The champions found it foreign, but [[The Acolytes]] found it oddly familiar. At its heart stood a great statue with palms open, ringed by candles and little artifacts. There was no ceiling. Above it, thousands of Acolytes circled like ghosts, waving flags and slushies, among them Pethel, Master of Squids. Fodder shouted up: "Hey, you up there. Top row. Jump." Someone did, and the goblins hurried to clean up the mess. Griff had a reply ready: "If they really wanted to kill themselves, they'd climb up your ego and jump off your intelligence."

Fodder won the staredown that decided who would strike first. The Shadow King admired the décor, especially "the large statue of [[The Arbiter|Vythorn]] there. It's very gauche," and set the champions face to face. The Grandmaster corrected him: stomach to face.

Fodder opened with his whip, a weapon as long as he was, and snatched Griff's staff right out of his hand. "You gonna give that back?" "Nope!" He ran off, yelled "This is for you!" to the crowd, and hurled it pointy end first into the stands. It struck an Acolyte named Harvey Hesko in the temple and stayed there. "Pay attention!" the Grandmaster scolded. "It's a new hat."

Fodder then performed a satyr's dance, and he did it so well that Griff was almost ashamed. It was, Griff explained, "the dance we do when we hunt for truffles in the Fae realm," and no, he would not demonstrate. He marched up to the pirate instead: "My eyes are down here. I have not yet begun to piss you off." He snatched up a passing temple rat, and it burst into flame and became a burning phoenix, a wildfire spirit that hovered at his side. The two vanished in a burst of fire and reappeared out of reach. Fodder insisted that "Cappin Fodder never runs from a fight," then admitted that maybe he should.

### Will You Be My Friend?
The Acolytes called for chaos, and the Grandmaster got to choose her favorite. The tiles rumbled as if a gopher were digging underneath, and small laughter leaked out. One tile rose to reveal a hole, and from it peered two glassy, unblinking eyes with long lashes. It was **[[Dolly]]**, the haunted doll, holding out her hands. "Will you be my…"

"He will," said Griff, pointing at Fodder. Fodder turned and, very seriously, apologized to Griff for everything he'd said. Then he pulled out a blunderbuss and shot the doll. Three of Dolly's porcelain fingers shattered. Without moving her shoulders, she turned her head toward him, and her eyes began to glow. He tried to punt her toward Griff and missed. She floated up out of the hole and burned his fingers and toes with unholy fire, "as she had lost hers." Fodder scuttled thirty feet away like a crab: "Nope, nope, nope."

Dolly turned to Griff with her hands out. "Please be my friend." Griff asked if she liked party tricks. She did, but not alone. So he sang her happy birthday, "the non-copyright version," and blasted her with three rays of fire boosted by his phoenix. Her hair burned away and half her face went with it. Dolly drank in the fire, glowed an unholy green and exploded in a poison cloud that ate at Griff's throat and lungs. "How's that ceramic Dutch oven?" asked the Grandmaster. She noted for the history books that this was the first time both champions had attacked Dolly. The Shadow King thought it showed "a couple of psychopaths."

After a brief existential crisis, Griff named his phoenix **Griffy** ("It's counterintuitive, but I like it") and sent it to scorch the pirate's shirt for summoning the doll and throwing his stick away. "It's a deathmatch," Fodder said. "Read the room." Up in the stands, Harvey pulled the staff out of his skull and threw it back. He missed, and it landed at Fodder's feet. Griffy dove for it and lost a tug of war to Fodder ("no chicken nugget"), who flung the bird aside. Griff had stopped playing nice: "I burned that mangy rat to a crisp on purpose to summon him and you will not disrespect my flaming bird."

### Stolen Sticks and Ghost Tattoos
The Acolytes wanted something new, but the box was empty. The Shadow King roused himself, scanned the crowd and chose: "You, Prince Vegeta!" That Acolyte's soul became the box's new fuel. "The Box of Chaos is a nifty little trinket," the Shadow King explained. "I should know, I was it once." Offer it something valuable or sentimental, and it gives back a magic item.

Fodder offered Griff's staff. The Grandmaster misheard this as "a statue of myself" and was delighted by the idea of him lugging a commissioned statue of himself around. The Acolytes granted each champion a token of their favor. "Thank you, Harvey. Your aim sucks, though."

Fodder threw the staff at the box. Griff used the crowd's gift at once. Griffy caught the staff mid-air, Griff enchanted it, thanked Fodder for giving it back, and tossed it into the box himself. "I literally have hops," he said. "It's on my sheet." The box flipped open in a jet of fire. A shadowy hand crawled up Griff's body and branded his left forearm with a ghost-step tattoo, a picture of "Drippy," that could turn him incorporeal for a moment.

Fodder booed. Griff got the whole arena booing along with him. Fodder answered by whipping Griff across the hindquarters ("Booty hit. Booty hit. Whip it good"), cutting deep enough to leave him limping, and then ran. "Coward." "Words can't hurt me." Griff healed himself, turned the limp into a gangster's strut, and teleported again with Griffy in a blaze that caught Fodder in its wake. "I'd agree with you, but then we'd both be wrong," he said. Then he touched the tattoo, went ghostly with Griffy on his shoulder, and bit his thumb at the pirate.

### Cannon Fodder
Fodder then had his finest idea. He tightened the bandana so he could see even less, crouched like a man about to be fired from a cannon, ran with his arms flung back, and dove head first into the Box of Chaos. Prince Vegeta made room: "It's very cozy." The Acolytes' jaws dropped. "We've seen toes and teeth and daggers and honey thrown in, but a whole person?"

Inside, the box's fire melted away the skin and bone of Fodder's right arm. A shadow spirit stepped up ("Here, use this") and jammed a blade into the bone, giving him a short-sword arm that steals life with every hit. The sword, it turned out, had once been Vegeta's soul, repurposed. Was it permanent? "Unless you have another arm laying around."

The box tipped over and fired Fodder at Griff like a cannonball, blade first. He passed straight through the ghostly satyr's chest and hit the floor head first. "Totally worth it." Griff flipped a Go piece as he watched him go by. "You appear to have an acute case of hey look at me disease, and I will cure you of it." He backed away, lecturing on gods of fire and deception and how people invite fire into their homes only for it to betray them, and called down a pillar of holy flame. Fodder dove clear of the worst of it. "You look like a before picture," he said. The icy temple was now sweltering, and Griff admitted he'd come in early to disable the fire extinguishers.

Griff fed his Go set to the box. The shadow creature offered him a game of chance and flipped a soul coin. Griff called tails. It landed heads, and the coin healed Fodder instead. "Do that again, Grandpa Goat."

### Vythorn's Lesson
Healthier, the self-proclaimed "Cap'n" (the Grandmaster wondered if he was the off-brand kind) announced his plan: "I'm going to run at the goat and kill it." His sword arm bit once and drank deep. "The ladies love scars," the Grandmaster said, but his next swings were "just dancing moves." Griff looked him dead in the eye: "You better run." "Or what?" "Mess around and find out."

Fodder grabbed Griff's leg, reasoning that if he was going to burn, Griff would burn too. Griff stepped one hoof on Fodder's knee and one on his shoulder and sprang free. The Grandmaster scored the prancing a perfect ten. "You might even call me a prancing pony." Griff flicked Fodder on the forehead, said "stay there," and froze him in place with a spell. Fodder's will failed him completely. The ground half-swallowed him, and Griff and Griffy teleported away in a burst of fire that seared the helpless pirate.

The hosts called up a chaos event of their own. Gazing at the statue, the Shadow King grew nostalgic:

> "I'm almost sorry I killed him. Not really sorry, but like a little bit. He was a great student. But maybe now he can teach a lesson for me." — The Shadow King

The statue of Vythorn, the Endless Scholar, lurched to life and brought both fists down. Against Griff it missed so badly that its whole fist crumbled away around him. Against the frozen Fodder it could not miss, and it hammered him like a nail halfway into the floor before going still again. "Captain Crunched," Fodder offered. "This is what you get for getting stoned," said Griff.

### Asses to Asses
Still frozen, Fodder could only think nasty thoughts, and he described them: "It involves Cappin Fodder's teeth and the legs of Griff roasting over a spit with some sriracha sauce and some garlic powder." Griff walked over slowly and set a copper piece on his head. "Why don't you go down to the corner and buy yourself a clue?" He stepped back and called the holy fire down one last time. The Grandmaster cut in for one final staring contest, but the flames fell all the same.

Fodder looked up at the goat who was about to fry him and gave his verdict: "Whatever kind of look you were going for, you missed." As he burned, he stared up at the Acolytes, and mostly without blinking they stared back and saluted as he turned to ash and blew away on the wind. Griff swept the remains into a tricorn hat with Griffy's burning tail feathers and dumped them into the box. "From whence you came." The Shadow King objected: "What does this look like, a trash can?" The Grandmaster flew down to deliver the eulogy ("Asses to asses, butts to butts… We will never forget you. Oh, that grand booty") and slammed the chest shut. He was a pirate, after all. Who doesn't love booty?

### The Reward
The Shadow King melted into the shadow of his throne and rose out of the floor in front of Griff, pleased with the finish. Griff had heard he granted winners their heart's desire, and he asked for "the go set. The original. The platonic ideal." The Shadow King promised to make something happen, put an arm around him and walked him to the ledge before the statue. "Hey, acolytes! Gaze down upon your champion and witness his rewards." Then he shoved Griff off ("You'll find your set down there!") and turned away without looking back. The moment he was out of sight, Griff teleported to safety with Griffy.

"Acolytes, everyone can go home. We're done here," the Shadow King announced. The Grandmaster began to protest. "I'm sorry, what can't I do?" The choker around her neck tightened until her throat collapsed and she could not speak. "Speak up if you have something to say. No? Well then, welcome and good night to D20 Deathmatch!"

## Dried Blood
Cappin Fodder was not quite finished. His ashes pulled themselves together into a pair of eyeballs and a rough mouth to share one last philosophy: "Alright, just as long as you're chaotic and you're having fun, screw the rules." The Grandmaster agreed: "Who needs rules? Overthrow the system!" Griff, meanwhile, was said to be lying low, watching how the Void's chaos works and hoping to get out alive. The emptied temple might just suit a squatting satyr.
