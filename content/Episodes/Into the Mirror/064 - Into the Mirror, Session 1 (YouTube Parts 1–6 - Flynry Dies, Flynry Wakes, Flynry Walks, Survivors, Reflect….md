## What happens
### Intro narration
1. **0:00:02** Show intro: "Welcome, Acolytes, to D20 Deathmatch Into the Mirror." The campaign continues the story that began with Fate of the Arbiter in Season 2.

### Part 1: Flynry dies
2. **0:00:20** **A literal meteor falls from the heavens onto the battlefield.** Flynry sees visions of his friends **Toddleheim and Locket** standing before him, smiling. He accepts his fate, **returns from his beast form to his human form**, and reaches out a hand to them.
3. **0:00:57** He feels **two hands on his shoulders: his fallen comrade Orin**, comforting him. Then **Orin's giant werebear hands** brace him for impact as his knees begin to buckle.
4. **0:02:13** Flynry thinks back to **the Hall of Mirrors, where Locket and Toddleheim "gave something of themselves" to pull him back from the brink once**. He realizes the mission is over and feels **peace, even joy: "we did it… his role was played."**
5. **0:03:46** The visions fade and the hands vanish. He stands alone. **The meteor strikes; a giant flash fills the arena while the Acolytes watch his body obliterated** "by this celestial entity."

### Part 2: Flynry wakes (the dream village)
6. **0:04:03** He seems to close his eyes, then open them. The burning comet is now **warm, pleasant sunshine**. He wakes **beneath an oak hundreds of feet tall, possibly hundreds of years old**, beside a small river in a picturesque countryside. There is a cool spring breeze and the smell of flowers, grass and **warm bread**.
7. **0:06:24** He rolls a **nat 1** on Wisdom. **He becomes certain this is the true reality** and that the arena, the Grandmaster, the Arbiter, Toddleheim and Locket were "a really complex and crazy dream." He remembers it all, but as a dream.
8. **0:07:49** Flynry stretches after "the longest afternoon… a nice nap by the tree." He follows the smell of bread east down a marked path. **To him this direction feels like home, which has been the theme of his journey**: he has been nomadic and never felt he had one.
9. **0:09:59** He reaches a **small village**. Stoves are lit, children play, and **tables are being carted into the town square**, normally the market, for a **feast or festival**.
10. **0:11:53** Faces "come back" to him, not as remembered but **as if they had always been there**: **Gregor the Baker**, and **old lady Margaret** beating dust out of a rug. Villagers nod and tip their hats to him.
11. **0:12:36 — "Father!"** A man in his mid-twenties, in fine robes that suggest station or learning, rushes in to hug him. Flynry: "My boy!" He can't recall the name. The son: **"It's me, Orin!"** Memories flash back of **naming his son after his fallen comrade**.
12. **0:14:51** Flynry's eyes well: "I'll never forget you, my son. You look as handsome as ever." Orin says he came **"all the way from Prexmarin"** (heard "Prexmarn") and still beat his father there. Flynry admits he dozed at the tree.
13. **0:16:00 — The retirement.** Everyone is there to celebrate Flynry tonight: **"It's not every day that someone retires from the Grandmaster's service."** Flynry isn't a center-of-attention guy. The word "Grandmaster" buzzes him, but he chooses not to break the illusion: "when you're in service to the Grandmaster… that work never really ends, does it?"
14. **0:17:40 — The other history.** New memories appear "as if they were always there." In them **he won the Battle for the Crown instead of dying**, coming out triumphant **with Professor Stabbz**. **The Grandmaster awarded him some of the highest honors in the Void and a place in her court**, and called on him to serve from time to time.
15. **0:19:49** He fills a tall wooden flagon from the nearest tap. **Olaf**, the village blacksmith, arrives in his apron, with a burly braided beard and soot-covered clothes: "Hey, you can't start without me." He is a long-time friend who has **forged many of Flynry's arms and armor**, competitive, with a fighter's nature, but "pretty good dude."
16. **0:21:35 — Olaf vs Flynry.**
    - Flynry teases him: if he napped more he wouldn't always be late.
    - Olaf: "Not all of us can afford to retire… We can't just be handed things by the Grandmaster." He boasts he could have won "that fight" and **"Hands behind my back, I could have beat the Arbiter."**
    - Flynry: "You know very little of the Arbiter, I think." Olaf: "He knows very little of me."
    - Olaf **pours a giant tankard over his face**, drinking whatever lands in his mouth.
17. **0:22:42 — The drinking contest.** **Flynry loses the first round badly.** He belches and says he hasn't "quite woken up from my nap."
    - Olaf: "You don't have the good stuff." He lifts his apron and pulls **a skin from his pants**, filling Flynry's cup with booze so strong it can be smelled from across the table.
    - Flynry: "Olaf, if I wanted to drink oil, I think I could go find a deposit for that." Olaf: "Oh, well, the oil wouldn't be as smooth."
    - Olaf leaves chugging the skin, gurgling as he tries to laugh mid-drink.
18. **0:24:25 — The feast.** It is a huge potluck with every kind of food, desserts and drinks, and a small band plays in a corner.
19. **0:25:20 — Orin's toast.** Orin clinks his glass with a dagger. He is dressed like a politician, and Flynry half-remembers that **Orin is an accomplished politician in Prexmarin** even in his mid-twenties. Orin shows off a little for the friends who never left home, then speaks seriously: "you are one of the best leaders and men and examples that a son could ever have. And I am honored that you're my father." Olaf stands: "I would like to say something. Good job." He sits back down.
20. **0:28:12 — "Did your mother not come?"** Emboldened by drink, Flynry thanks Orin and asks about his mother. Orin says she was **"just sitting right here at the table."** Flynry rolls a **nat 1** on Perception and can't find her. Afraid of being wrong, he covers: "She's probably stepped out for a breath of fresh air." Orin: "Are you sure that you're all right? You're being very strange today." Flynry blames Olaf's mixtures; Orin: "don't let him give you the good stuff. It's pretty much just straight fuel."
21. **0:31:14 — Lillian.** As Orin goes looking, the name **Lillian** rings in Flynry's head: his wife.
    - BdTeej describes her (OOC framing): a "Beauty and the Beast" pairing. Flynry is a hulking, bearded, long-haired, linebacker-like man; **Lillian is petite and Belle-like**, a couple nobody would have expected.
22. **0:33:07** Night falls and **floating magical lanterns, hovered by the town's mages**, light up. **A woman in a green dress on the dance floor** is Lillian, seen "for the first time but also for the thousandth time." Flynry is very drunk ("the good stuff is getting to you"). From the head table he watches her dance: playful, lighthearted, linking arms and laughing with everyone.
23. **0:36:56** He crosses the dance floor "like a battlefield," dodging drunk dancers, invigorated. She makes eye contact, smiles without a word, and holds out a hand. **Flynry can't dance, but here he's "not terrible"**; maybe she leads. More memories flood in: being young, the time after the Battle for the Crown, the Grandmaster's service, moving to the countryside, having Orin. **He feels complete; nothing has ever felt this easy or perfect.**
24. **0:39:19 — The whisper and the crumbling.** **Lillian leans in and whispers in his ear.** No words are heard in the transcript. **She crumbles in his arms with a crooked smile on her face, her body falling apart into sand.** Buildings collapse and people run screaming. The sky goes black, the sun darkens, and **everything becomes a blowing desert of green sand. Flynry is alone.**

### Part 3: Flynry walks (the green desert and the minotaur)
25. **0:41:22 — The desert.**
    - The sand is **"like the color of a mirror, reflected too many times,"** a faint pale green.
    - The sky is black space full of nebulas, black holes, suns and planets.
    - **Columns of sand rise like fingers reaching to touch the heavens; their tips are pulled off into space, forming more nebulas and celestial events.**
    - The sand blast is **surprisingly cold**, and everything wavers like a mirage.
    - **No matter what point on the horizon he walks toward, he never gets closer.**
26. **0:43:14 — The over-shift.** In panic, Flynry shifts to feel safer and **"shifts too far."** The seams of his clothes rip and **he becomes more the animal than a man with a beast form.** He screams in confusion, sorrow, loss and fear, and curls into a rock-like beast ball in the sand.
27. **0:44:46 — Two lives.** He now holds both lives. **He understands that his true reality was his death and that the village was some aberration**, yet he keeps **memories of a wife, a son and a whole life he never lived, though those people don't exist.**
28. **0:46:00** A sandstorm rolls in with small tornado spouts, and he feels he is not alone. He screams into the void: **"WHO'S THERE?!"**
29. **0:47:01 — The sand minotaur.** Only the wind answers. Then the storm's sand pulls down and swirls together into **a giant minotaur of green sand with a giant axe**, backlit by nebulas. It charges. **Battle is the most comfortable Flynry has felt since waking**, and he charges back.
30. **0:49:23 — Fight log.**
    - **Flynry goes first with Inflict Wounds.** His claws are bigger than usual, "less an enhancement and more a transformation," and his vertical leap has improved. He **latches onto the minotaur's broad chest going for the heart, tearing fiber away while necrotic venom oozes from the cuticles of his claws into the wounds.** He follows with an unarmed strike.
    - **The minotaur gores him with a horn, flipping him 10 ft onto his back (prone).** It swings its greataxe recklessly and misses. **The axe blade shatters into sand on the ground and reforms as it's lifted.**
    - BdTeej (OOC): "I'm kind of rooting for the Minotaur now." Drew: "Yeah, me too."
    - Flynry flurries and lands more unarmed strikes. **One punch misses because it goes through a hole he'd already made in the chest; the sand refills the holes almost as fast as he punches them.** He looks for a structural weak point but finds none: "He's made of sand."
    - **The minotaur lands two reckless greataxe hits across his chest, then his back.** The wounds are **"cold and lifeless, even colder than steel… like ice,"** jagged and torn like sand ripping flesh. The minotaur's sand now struggles to hold together in places.
31. **0:59:07 — The sword from the sand.**
    - Flynry, realizing this land doesn't follow conventional physics, asks whether he can manifest power here. Survival 27.
    - **Spending a hero point**, he understands the sand. He plunges his arm up to the elbow into it and imagines a weapon.
    - **He pulls out the hilt of a greatsword wreathed in a blue searing flame, "one that would almost be required for… glass blowing."** Drew rules it is now on his sheet and he is proficient with it.
32. **1:01:55 — Glassing the leg.** Spending a ki point, Flynry buries the blade in a leg to hinder it. **The sand turns molten and cauterizes into mirror on both sides of the cut, and the sand can't reform past the reflection.**
33. **1:03:54 — The kill.**
    - Flynry takes a power stance and holds the sword low behind him. **Its flame touches the sand and travels to the soles of his boots, so he creates glass under his feet as he sprints** and the sand can't hinder him.
    - He leaps with immense bestial power and **brings the greatsword down between the minotaur's eyes, slicing its whole body in half.**
    - **The cut faces become mirrors: in one he sees his human form, in the other his bestial form**, both looking back with a determination and grit he hasn't seen in a long time. "The first of many foes in a new world."
    - The loose sand blows away. **Two chunks of mirror in the minotaur's silhouette** remain on the ground.
34. **1:05:44 — The mirror sword.**
    - **The sword cools into a mirror finish.** It is still a sword and still his.
    - In it he sees both his halves, then **things that aren't there: reflections of Toddleheim and Locket in a life after his death**, and other people and places he knew.
    - Then the sand in the blade shifts, the sand at his feet shifts the same way, and **it points him toward a spot on the horizon.** He is now somehow connected to the sand and the mirror, and the sword gives him "the ability to travel."
35. **1:08:54 — "Truth."** Exhausted, confused and afraid, but comforted, he looks the blade over. **Where the crossguard meets the blade a small gold flicker appears: in a language he doesn't know but can read, the word "truth" inscribes itself.** He slings the sword onto his back with a feeling that it will be okay somehow.
36. **1:10:16** He walks **"for days, weeks, months, maybe years. Indiscernible."** He is lost, but he has a heading.

### Part 4: Survivors (Toddleheim and Locket)
37. **1:10:55 — The Battle for the Crown, from the stands.**
    - Toddleheim and Locket each fought their own deathmatch for the Grandmaster's honor and survived. They had **spent months trying to make the Arbiter's betrayal known** to the people and to the Grandmaster.
    - They watch as Acolyte spectators while Flynry fights for the Grandmaster and dies under the meteor.
    - **Toddleheim** feels "just absolute pain." He raises an arm toward Flynry and says only **"Flynry."** Unknown to him, Flynry was reaching back toward the visions of them and Orin.
    - **Locket** feels "Sadness, loss, confusion. Why? It had to be Flynry." **She was at 6 HP at the end of her own battle**, close to death herself.
38. **1:13:37 — A hollow victory.**
    - The Grandmaster stands victorious and raises the hands of the remaining champions. **The Arbiter's attempt to overthrow her is defeated.**
    - The mission succeeded, but Flynry is dead. So are other friends lost along the way: "**Orin. Gon. Kenny. Dissolved into acid.**" Who dissolved into acid is unclear.
39. **1:14:40 — In the Grandmaster's service.** **The Grandmaster takes them into her service and offers them a place in her court**, with missions, jobs, adventures or time off: "the freedom to do whatever it is you want… as a reward."
    - **Toddleheim** searches his books, scrolls, tomes and every library he can find **for a way to bring Flynry back.**
    - **Locket** keeps busy on jobs for the Grandmaster. **Every night she writes letters to her twin sister (a Tabaxi)**; all of this has taught her the importance of family.
40. **1:16:13 — Baptiste.**
    - Some nights **a rock hits Locket's window.** Below stands **Baptiste**, an elven gentleman in flowing cloaks with daggers across his body, who "often has come to call, whether it's warranted or not."
    - Baptiste: **"Hello, my lady. Would you have any interest in practicing your stabbing?"** Locket: "it would be worth giving it a stab." Baptiste: **"My knives are sharp. And I have many a honing stone."** Locket: "My suave elven."
    - Drew fades the scene out "before we get way too involved there." They have "made some sort of connection."
41. **1:17:38 — The Grandmaster's letters.**
    - **The Grandmaster has known of Toddleheim's search.** Flynry was also her champion, and **it doesn't sit right with her that he died this way after so much sacrifice**, so she has considered a way as a reward.
    - After many months **Toddleheim gets a vague letter: his friend "may not be completely gone… might still be reached."** It summons him to **the Dread Fortress** whenever he is ready, with whomever he wants. Locket gets a similar letter.
42. **1:20:13** Toddleheim: "Well, I can't believe this, finally… I've missed him so, so very dearly." Locket: "Me too."
    - Someone, probably Toddleheim, teases her: "I did hear a man named Baptiste in the area." Locket: **"Oh no, Baptiste and I are just… just friends."** Her player admits no roll is needed to see she's lying.
    - Drew: **"There's a lot of dagger strikes in your headboard."**
    - They meet in the town square and set off at once.
43. **1:21:38 — The road to the Dread Fortress.** They travel through **Prexmarin** and many other cities and regions. **The Dread Fortress stays on the horizon whichever way they turn, always following them.** The **orc skull across the lava field** glows with the same heat they felt leaving it on their first adventure "to save the Arbiter at that time."

### Part 5: Reflections (the Arbiter and the mirrors)
44. **1:22:25 — Inside the Dread Fortress.**
    - They walk **through the orc's mouth** into cooling tunnels and caves that become a more ordinary castle.
    - **Portraits of the Grandmaster hang on the walls, and so do portraits of "a man with a golden shield."**
    - With their status they have free rein. **A goblin heaves open the large iron-and-wood doors of the throne room**, where they were just before the final battle "where things went kind of sideways with the Arbiter."
45. **1:23:21 — The Arbiter on the throne.**
    - **The Grandmaster's throne, with a large stone face in her likeness above it, is occupied by the Arbiter.** He sits sideways with his feet dangling off, **chained hand and foot, in plain sackcloth**, looking like he's been sleeping in a cage ("because he has been"). He is **picking his teeth with a knife.**
    - **A tiefling woman they don't know** stands beside him.
    - Arbiter: **"Welcome back. It's good to see you again."**
46. **1:24:51** Toddleheim: "Where is the Grandmaster?"
    - Arbiter: "I just missed her. **She's on a tour of the Void, taking in the sights, serving her people… she's left me in charge of the affairs of the Dread Fortress.**"
    - Toddleheim calls it a lie, given his attire. The Arbiter admits **"It's true that I'm a prisoner in this place… She beat me fair and square. I'm honoring our deal."** But he has "a certain set of expertise… the Grandmaster has found her uses for me still."
    - **"I know why you're here."** He tells them to ask "the Grandmaster's servant herself. One closer than I."
47. **1:26:36 — Elianna.**
    - **"I am her personal assistant. Thank you very much… My name is Elianna."**
    - She is **about 5'2", with curly black hair, light violet horns, and a light purple tail** swishing under her skirt. She wears **bright red glasses on a reading chain woven with teal and black strands**, and carries a book at her waist.
    - Reading from the book: the Grandmaster is visiting, and **"She does have a spa treatment at three."** "I heard great things about you. Now, maybe we should talk without him here."
48. **1:28:04** Elianna glares at the Arbiter and **holds her nose as she passes**. He is unbothered, smiling. Locket, without saying goodbye: "See you soon." No response.
    - Elianna: "so sorry about the stench. **We haven't washed him in a few days because no one wants to go near him.**"
49. **1:28:56** Locket explains they were sent to retrieve a lost friend. Elianna, scribbling notes, says **they do need the Arbiter, "because he does have the information on such retrieval."** They had just left the room. Locket: "Now we gotta go back."
    - Elianna: **"I hate talking with him. You have no idea what it's been like with him around."** Locket: "We've had to deal with him… many, many times."
    - **Elianna offers brandy** "to take the edge off because he's unpleasant." She **takes a very potent shot from a tiny vial** in her pouch: "I'm ready to go now."
50. **1:30:38 — The Arbiter's offer.** He is smugly aware that the Grandmaster did give him this task and that he stands between them and what they want: "So, good discussion."
    - **"Leaving the void, not an easy task. Finding the dead, even if you can make it to the plane. Not easy. But, fortunately for you, I am an accomplished mage."** Elianna: "Pardon me."
51. **1:32:10 — Down to the Hall of Fates.** He leads them down stone steps they've never taken. **With a flick of his wrist he opens a large door with intricate locks and mechanisms.** Inside is **a line of mirrors floating above an abyss: the Hall of Fates.**
    - Arbiter: **"I thought you might be nostalgic for it."** He jabs at them, "knowing this is the place you lost Orin."
52. **1:33:11 — What happened to Flynry's soul.**
    - The mirrors are **"portals, in some ways, living creature in another way. A connection between planes."**
    - **When Flynry looked into the mirrors and couldn't pull his gaze away, a piece of his soul was taken. "And upon his death, that debt was collected. I know where he's at. I can get you there."**
53. **1:34:34 — The test.**
    - **They must each look into a mirror and resist the urge to enter.** Toddleheim: "This sounds awfully suspect to me." Arbiter: "Believe me, don't believe me. I don't care… I'm doing this because I have to. **It does give me a certain amount of pleasure to mess with you. But the Grandmaster has given her orders, and for now, I am obliged to obey them.**" He raises his shackles.
    - Locket asks whether a lost soul-piece can be recovered. **The Arbiter: "Not many people have taken the journey… Even fewer have returned… I've never been myself… I've heard tale of those exiting and finding their way out, but I've never seen it."**
    - Locket: "This seems risky… But we have done that before." Toddleheim: **"part of my soul died that day anyway. So we'll see how much is left."**
54. **1:37:39 — Toddleheim's mirror.**
    - The Arbiter grabs his shoulder: "Remember, you must not walk into it." **Elianna gently taps Toddleheim and casts Guidance with a thumbs up: "you've got this."**
    - **The first time here, Toddleheim was the only one who refused to look at all**, though he heard squeaking, like something streaking across glass.
    - His desire hasn't changed: **"just a warm bosom to rest my head."** In the mirror is **his ideal woman, pressed against the glass and squeaking back and forth.**
    - He resists, sweating and leaning forward. **Images of his past wife calm him.** He mutters: "Well, it is a good bosom. Bless, I must take my leave." (Speaker uncertain for the rest of that line: "Don't keep rambling like that. You're going to chafe.")
    - **Elianna overhears it, clears her throat to cover, and scribbles furiously.** Locket doesn't hear.
55. **1:42:40 — Locket's mirror.**
    - **Last time she saw "an elven gentleman" (Baptiste).** Toddleheim: "All the best, darling."
    - **This time she sees what she first takes for her reflection: it is her twin sister, where she is now.** "You've been estranged. You've been looking for her."
    - Locket: "I just want to reach out and give her a hug… This is fake. This is fake. I've done this before." **She resists and steps away.**
56. **1:45:47 — Elianna is the chaperone.** The Arbiter: **"the Grandmaster did say that she thought they needed a chaperone."** Elianna shakily puts away her book and glasses and trips on the stairs going up.
57. **1:46:37 — Elianna's mirror.**
    - Drew: the mirror shows "your deepest desires… what would call on your soul to walk in."
    - Her player: "God damn it, making me show my hand right away."
    - **Elianna sees a cozy fire in an isolated cabin, snow outside, and on a small couch, the Grandmaster snuggled up with her under a blanket.**
    - She whispers **"oh, my sweet lady"** and clutches her chest, staring without blinking. Drew calls it "a memory of yourself with Elusia Skypiercer."
    - She barely succeeds. **She hovers her hand over where the Grandmaster's face would be and pulls it back: "No. She wanted me to come back safely and I will do that. I'm not going to let her down."**
58. **1:48:58 — Why Elianna came.** She came willingly; she'd do anything for the Grandmaster, though she is sad to leave her side.
    - Narration: **the Grandmaster cared deeply for her champions, Flynry included, and trusts Elianna to help them.** She sees something special in her. **"Even though she is very old, she sees some of herself in you. Back when she had just arrived at Cor'thun and was lost in the world after the death of her father."**
59. **1:49:38 — Messages for the Grandmaster.** Arbiter: "I guess the hard part's over… is there anything that I should relay to the Grandmaster on your behalf before you go?"
    - Locket: they plan to be back and are very grateful.
    - Toddleheim: **"Tell her I said, thank you for trying."**
    - Elianna: **"Tell her I will keep our promise."** She glares at the Arbiter. Arbiter: "Very well. I will relay the message."

### Part 6: Decay
60. **1:51:31 — The betrayal.** The Arbiter is in "such a good fucking mood" today: helpful, uncomplaining, twiddling his thumbs. "It's your friend you seek… **I only know of one way to get there.**" **He raises a hand and casts Disintegrate on all three.**
61. **1:52:40** **"Like Orin," their bodies petrify: they gray, chip off and fly into oblivion** while the Arbiter cackles: **"That's one way to solve my problems."** They panic, not knowing whether this was his plan, the Grandmaster's plan, or whether any of it was true. Their eyes go black.
62. **1:53:46 — The green desert.** They open their eyes together in the **green-sand desert**: cold, stinging wind, a black sky of nebulas and planets, sand-fingers rising into the sky to make new stars, nothing for hundreds of miles, everything tinted mirror green.
63. **1:55:22** Locket grabs for Toddleheim and Elianna to make sure they're real. **Elianna huddles them close: "What just happened?"** Toddleheim: **"It would appear that we are in the in-between."** Elianna: "Only heard of this place."
    - Toddleheim kneels a short distance off, runs his hands through the sand, looks up: **"Flynry, we are coming."**
64. **1:56:15** Elianna has heard of the mirror and this plane through the Grandmaster and the Arbiter. **The plane has a name, but it doesn't come to her.** She confirms **people rarely come back from here**, only whispers, and she has never known anyone who has.
65. **1:57:09** **Locket, with her Tabaxi eyes, can find no direction**: no structures, just wasteland.
66. **1:58:13 — Toddleheim reads the sand.**
    - "Be calm." He meditates, reciting tomes he's learned, and **sand swirls up around him.** Arcana 25.
    - **The sand itself is magical; everything here is imbued with magic.** He may have come across this place in some random book "sometime in your long life."
    - Insight 25: **he has felt this kind of magic before. It is "evil in nature. Not necessarily malicious in itself."**
    - In the sand's movement he hears **screams of people suffering. The sand is made of broken souls: "Souls of the dead who have been destroyed. Completely. Nothing left but this sand. And whatever imprint the magic leaves."**
67. **2:01:17** Toddleheim lowers the sand: **"It's the sand. We have to use the sand to get out of here… See how it creates new things."**
    - Elianna: "Is your friend here?" Toddleheim: "It's too hard to tell." Locket: **"This is still part of him somewhere at least. I don't think he's completely gone."**
68. **2:02:18 — The scream.**
    - Burning with frustration, Toddleheim falls to his hands and knees, **plunges his fists into the sand and weeps.** The sand vibrates wildly and he screams **"Flynry!"** as a wave rolls across the desert.
    - Attuned to the souls, **the sand parts in a line "like the bottom has dropped out," a crack pointing in a direction.**
    - Locket: "You did it, Toddleheim. This is, this might be the way." Toddleheim, on his back, breathing hard and wiping his tears: "Flynry, we're coming."
