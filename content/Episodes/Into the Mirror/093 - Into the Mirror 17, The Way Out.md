## What happens
### Cold open — the party falls to Sky's raiders (mid-combat)
1. **0:00:00** The file opens mid-fight against Sky's band in the sands outside the library. An enemy stab at Toddleheim misses. The players admit they're "not really in a position to be taunting."
2. **0:00:54 — Gravity Sinkhole.** The enemy **wizard** uses Careful Spell to spare his allies and casts **Gravity Sinkhole** in the middle of the party. A **void orb** forms; everyone feels heavy and is sucked toward the point. **The Arbiter fails the save, and so do Flynry and Toddleheim.** Cerys and Elianna resist the pull and take half damage. **Flynry and Toddleheim go down.** Flynry, Toddleheim and the Arbiter are dragged together into the centre.
3. **0:03:49 — Hellish Rebuke.** Elianna, left on 3 HP, Hellish Rebukes the wizard. He fails the save and takes 12 fire. He answers with an ominous "Hmm."
4. **0:04:58** Flynry's player complains that even with the Arbiter on their side, every enemy still has the Arbiter's snark. Drew: "**The day you realize that the Arbiter's just me.**" (table talk)
5. **0:05:13 — The Orc Raider.** An **Orc Raider** with a greatsword cuts down **Cerys** and then **Elianna**. The table shouts "TPK!"
6. **0:06:46 — The Arbiter surrenders.** **Sky, the leader**, points his **glaive** at Vythorn: "Doesn't have to be this way. You could just surrender and you can live. And we'll take your stuff." Stuck in the gravity void, **the Arbiter looks around at the bodies and shrugs**, since he "doesn't really have a dog in this fight." He has no real weapons, so he **ceremonially takes out a dagger and throws it down: "You win."** All the final blows were non-lethal.
7. **0:08:18 — Vythorn tries to get the party killed.** Vythorn tells Sky: "If you want my stuff, you can take it. But… **You sure you don't want to kill them? They're very troublesome. I've dealt with them before.**" Sky looks at him: "**There's something wrong with you.**" The party is unconscious and never learns of this. Flynry's player later confirms at 0:20:09 that Sky never told them.
8. **0:08:55 — The army.** Sky whistles, and **an army comes over the sand dune**. Elianna's player: "I knew it." **Sky's mages heal the party** back to consciousness with a few HP.
9. **0:09:01 — "Give us half your stuff."** Sky: "I didn't really want to make this a big deal. We just wanted some stuff… You all have been through a lot today… Give us half your stuff."
   - **Elianna throws some gems.**
   - **Cerys** offers a **torch, rations and a shield**: "What do you want from us?"
   - **Toddleheim throws one of his old books** in the sand.
   - **Flynry unslings his shortbow** and tosses it down.
10. **0:10:32 — Something magical.** Sky looks disappointed. **One of his dragonborn** whispers in his ear that **there's something magical here**, and his mages nod.
11. **0:10:55 — Flynry's deception.** Flynry says the magic they sensed is their own spellcasting, then blames **the Arbiter's aura**: "we just ran into that guy in that building, and he's done some things that I can't explain… there's something off about the presence he brings." The deception roll has advantage because of Sky's existing doubts about Vythorn, and **it works**. Sky: "**Forget it. Forget the whole thing.** … I guess you can keep your stuff."

### The outlanders — "free thinkers"
12. **0:12:26 — The invitation.** Sky invites them home, since they're "notable fighters" and he has "quite a group, a collection going on."
13. **0:13:08** Cerys: "Are you all outlanders?" Sky: "A dirty term, I suppose, but **we like to consider ourselves free thinkers**." "Free from what?" "**Free from the rule of the queen? The king and that place?**"
14. **0:13:41 — On the city.** The party says they're new. Sky: "**Things there aren't what they seem.** Seems to be a well-oiled machine, people living in harmony, but between you and me, **I don't trust the queen very much.**" Flynry: "Especially not after what happened to the king." Sky: "**A fair bit of fuckery around that.**"
15. **0:14:47 — Sand sickness is a lie.** Cerys mentions the king's **sand sickness**. Flynry explains it as long exposure to the magical sands that mess with the mind. **Sky spits**: "We've been living out here for years. **Just another lie of the queen.** … Keeps her people locked inside of those walls. **Or did you think the walls were for protection?**" He adds: "**We can get in those walls if we want. We know the way in.**"
16. **0:15:53 — Black market.** Sky says they are "in the business of rare artifacts… **something of black market dealers**. We find things out here and we sell them to folks within the walls. Or whoever's buying." Elianna says all they did in the library was read, so he can go scrounge.
17. **0:17:10** He won't name his home unless they come: "**Information carries with it a price.**"
18. **0:17:34 — The party debates.** Elianna leans on Flynry, whom she has taken to be the leader of the original group. Flynry's player: "If you'd known Flynry before this place, you would not have assumed that he was the leader of anything besides maybe lunch." Flynry speaks in character: "**All that is certain is this group, what we have, our goals, our ambitions.** How is this group any different than the group in the city? Besides the fact that they tried to murder us." Elianna asks Sky if they can come and learn about the culture. Sky: "**We're very hospitable people… first impressions notwithstanding.**"
19. **0:19:43 — The library dampens magic.** As a gesture of good faith, Sky warns that **the library has a certain effect on magical properties**, so they must walk a distance first. After a few miles, **the dampening that Elianna and Toddleheim felt fades. Vythorn notices too but says nothing.**

### "Take me to the heart" — the hollow soul gem
20. **0:21:26** In the green blowing sand under the celestial sky, Sky halts the army and whispers. Flynry hears it with a perception roll of 23: "**Take me to the heart.**" This is not a portal like Motorios's. Instead **the sand cracks, parts and falls into stairs** leading down into a chasm, then through an archway into **a huge hollowed-out cavern of green and blue gems and crystals, like being inside a giant hollow crystal.**
21. **0:23:10 — It's a soul gem.** History rolls reveal that its colour and texture are **almost exactly those of the soul gems in the city**, only far larger. Flynry asks whether it matches the gem **Locket** is made of: "That one's similar, but not the same." Drew confirms "**the soul gems are dead people**," and this one looks exactly the same.
22. **0:24:19 — The town.** **Hundreds of thousands of people** live there. There are hanging and stilt structures, **pulley elevators**, landing zones and crystal being mined and hauled: "an underground mine town… a working town." Sky leads them over hanging bridges to **his residence carved into the crystal wall**: a long hall with cushions, tables and food, where a few dozen people follow them in.
23. **0:26:16 — Long rest.** The party rests and eats. **Sky lounges on a beanbag-like cushion**, picking at a plate. **Vythorn stands at the side and won't sit.** Sky: "Do you drink food? I don't know what you like, but I'm sure we have it."
24. **0:27:53** Flynry: "**Admittedly, this is a decidedly warmer reception than we've received in the city.**" Sky: "**It's a nice place, I guess, if you don't know any better. But it's just another prison.**" He adds: "Here, you're free to come and go," but it takes a lot of currency to run.
25. **0:28:33 — The remnants of a vast soul.** Flynry: "This entire structure is made of currency." Sky: "**It's no secret to anybody here that this is the remnants of a vast soul.** Can only imagine the kind of creature that would possess something like this." They can only mine so far "before the whole thing collapses on us." He looks up through the translucent roof: "**a lot of unspent time here**."
26. **0:29:38 — History.** Sky has been there "a few decades." Others have been there "hundreds, thousands of years, perhaps." "**I don't know a time when the queen wasn't in charge here.** … Several centuries at least."
27. **0:30:34** Cerys says she's been here longer than the others and brought them to the city: "There's nowhere else to go, other than the wilderness." Sky: "Well, I suppose that's good news for us, then, if our secrets are safe."
28. **0:31:01 — Other strongholds.** "**This is not the only stronghold that exists outside the walls. There are many cities.**" Each has a different way in: "sometimes speaking the name is enough, other times you have to be shown, or **even through the reflective surfaces**." Flynry perks up at "reflective surfaces." Sky: "You do know about the different planes?"
29. **0:32:07 — Information for information.** Sky asks what they were doing in the city and library. (Table gag: nobody has noticed "**Elianna's third boob**", the orrery hidden in her shirt.) Cerys: "We've been looking for a way to get back to our homeland." Sky says his people have scoured the library many times for similar reasons. "Some of us wish to go home. Others of us are content here."
30. **0:33:17 — Echoes of planes.** Sky: "**reflective surfaces, mirrors, sometimes in the crystal themselves, they can lead to different locations…** some sort of reflections of the living world or different planes. **Not the plane itself, but echoes of planes. Potentially, they were once connected and have somehow broken apart.**" Every surface leads somewhere different. He won't share others' secrets until he trusts them.
31. **0:35:20** He says there are a few other cities as big as Mortorios, all hidden. He recommends one: "**sort of cold, very cold, mountainside, if you like the snow.**" Flynry: "**I like the living. That's the plane I'd like to return to.**"
32. **0:36:06 — The Oasis.** A mage whispers to Sky. "There is one place that was said to be connected to the living. At one point… not anymore, certainly not unless you possess some sort of power to open a portal. **It's called the Oasis.** This was once **an adjoining of worlds**." Older folks claim it leads to the land of the living. Cerys: "I feel like I've heard that before."
33. **0:37:47** Cerys says they'll go alone: "You did just… nearly kill us all." Sky: "**You don't trust us, that's smart.**" He'll show them the door in the morning: "If you don't come back, I'll know it, maybe it worked. And, you know, or you're dead."
34. **0:39:13 — The dampening zones.** Elianna asks about the library's power. Sky doesn't know whether it's "an ancient power or a curse or the building itself." Within a radius, "**my spellcasters are weakened… Teleportation becomes very difficult. Opening pathways… even to our own home becomes seemingly impossible.**" There are other zones like it, which he takes to be "**the natural state of the sands. As the sand shifts, so does the structure and power of this place.**" He has seen only a handful, mostly barren patches: "Maybe there's something under the sand… I'm not really the book learning type."

### The kobold's shop
35. **0:41:46** Sky walks them half a block through the haphazard town to a small structure: "**Cyrus**, some folks, some customers." **A small kobold**, "this small dragon figure," climbs up to his shop window: "**What do you want? What can I do for you?**" Inside are piles of junk, weapons on the right and magic items in the back.
36. **0:44:04 — Weapons.** "You can find plus two weapons here easily." **Cerys takes a +2 longsword that deals "righteous holy damage."** **Toddleheim** at first holds back because he has nothing to trade. The kobold says, "**That stuff's all fine. You can just take what you want. I already worked out a good deal with your friend here**," so Toddleheim takes a **+2 warhammer**.
37. **0:44:43 — The Golden Lady.** Flynry describes a small statue "gold to bronze in coloring," with inscriptions on the sides, which he knows from the large book. Kobold: "**The Golden Lady.** I've heard of it. Traders here and there say they've seen it or had it. **Everyone's owned it at one point or another. I've never seen it.** Does it exist? Maybe." An insight roll of 27 shows he's telling the truth. He offers to put out feelers: "maybe you'll come back in a week."
38. **0:45:50 — The red pendant.** Flynry asks about "**a small eye-shaped pendant with a red gem in the middle**." The kobold **dives into a pile of jewellery and disappears**, then pops out with **a silvery ruby necklace**. It looks similar to the one in the book but **lacks the inscriptions**. His pitch: "**You wear this thing, you are immune to disease. Nothing can harm you. Well, except like swords and stuff.**" He glances toward Flynry's belt line: it also suppresses "ailments… **you look like a man who likes to have a good time.**" Flynry: "**Party animal I am**." His player notes he has never been to a party in his life.
39. **0:48:35 — The Atlas trade.** Flynry offers the **Atlas of Endless Horizons**. The kobold's eyes widen: "**This is not what you want. This is fine, but I have better.**" He swaps the necklace for **an oval, vertical red pendant**: "**If you ever find yourself close to death, you'll want to be wearing this baby.**" It is the **Periapt of Wound Closure**. Cerys: "That would have come in handy about 20 minutes ago." Flynry: "**I'm quite familiar with being close to death.**" **The kobold chucks the Atlas over his shoulder into a pile of magic lights.**
40. **0:53:09 — Elianna's amulet.** Elianna wants the disease necklace. The kobold suggests a nice shirt ("**Shirt on your back looks great**"), which the table runs as a gag about the orrery in her top. He then notices her **amulet: a rose-gold chain with an emerald**. **He bites it**: "Oh, this is different. [Nana?] Soul Gem. Unique." He trades it for **the Periapt of Health**. She doesn't need the amulet to spellcast.
41. **0:56:21** The kobold says to come back if they find stuff.

### Morning — Sky's nature, and the door
42. **0:56:57** They rest and fit their new items overnight. Flynry wakes disheveled and comfortable after not sleeping on the ground. Sky: "**Time to go, or have you decided to stay?**" Cerys: "We will still be on our way." Sky again: "You sure you want to leave? It's a nice place." "**Just looking to give folks a home, I suppose.**"
43. **0:58:17 — Sky's problems.** "**The problem with being in charge is that people always want something from you.**" His people want to live on the surface behind walls, want food and clothing, and "**some of them are begging me to invade Mortorios and overthrow it. I don't really have any interest in that.**"
44. **0:59:20 — Why exiles.** Elianna asks why his people aren't allowed in the city. "**Thrown out for speaking a little too loudly against the monarchy. Trying to keep a few extra soul gems for ourself. Making waves generally… many of us just don't have a place within the walls. We don't serve a function.**"
45. **0:59:52** Elianna asks whether he's thought of leading them within the walls. "**I don't really like leading these people.**" She asks: "**Are you happy?**" Drew notes Elianna asks every NPC this, and her player says she's very empathetic. Sky: "Does it really matter here? … **at least here we get to be in this giant soul gem.**"
46. **1:00:42 — The sword's reflection.** As they did before with a guard, **Flynry turns his back and angles his sword (the mirror) so Elianna sees Sky's reflection.** In it, Sky looks no younger; he's already young-looking. But as he walks past Vythorn, **the reflection shows Sky reaching into Vythorn's pocket and pulling something out.** In reality, that isn't happening. It is a glimpse of his urges: **he's always thinking about finding things. "Nothing sinister… he's kind of a pickpocket."** **Elianna pulls her shirt over the orrery** and checks her pockets: "Well, if we find any places that you can scrounge through, we'll just drop by and let you know."
47. **1:02:54 — The polished gem.** Sky: "The door's open, or the theater's open, or I don't know." He leads them to **a small cave where part of the soul gem has been highly polished** into a reflective surface, whether by people or by nature he isn't sure. "If you're looking for the Oasis, this is the only way I know how to get there… **The stories of the living world, I think, are just tales.**"
48. **1:04:03** Flynry checks for resonance between the orrery, his sword and the mirror. There is none, but the surface clouds and swirls behind their reflections. Sky backs away: "**I hope we meet again and hopefully the next time you're not passing through here as soul gems.**" Cerys: "Ha! Funny. I hope not." (Table: "He's so pushy… He's totally Hondo.")

### The Oasis
49. **1:05:35** In the mirror they see themselves somewhere else, as with the snowy mountainside before. In a blink they stand **in a field of lava: broiling heat, the smell of sulfur everywhere**. In the distance is **a large structure with large rings that arc over in different directions, like the orrery.** Flynry: "**Oasis, my behind.**" Toddleheim: "This is not what I was expecting either." Cerys: "That thing up ahead kind of looks like our thing."
50. **1:06:53 — The orrery reacts.** Elianna clutches the orrery in her shirt. **It vibrates and, as they get closer, glows blue.** (JRPG-physics gag.) She pulls it out so it doesn't shine through her shirt. Cerys: "This might be the key."
51. **1:08:00** Vythorn: "**Well, let's… I'm still here. Let's get on with it.** … I've been in worse." He leads them over lava pits.
52. **1:08:47 — What lives there.** Perception rolls reveal:
    - creatures swimming in the lava, and **humanoid figures clawing their way out**;
    - **large humanoid giants walking on the horizon**;
    - fiery **snake-like, cat-like and dog-like creatures** moving across the shelves.
53. **1:09:34 — The structure.** It hovers over a large opening in the lava on **four bridges in a cross shape**, with **a large round central platform** under the rings. The writing matches the orrery's, in many languages, some readable and some not. **Runic sigils cover the platform.** Vythorn: "Looks like might go well with that device you've got."
54. **1:10:51 — No keyhole.** An investigation finds no stand, keyhole or sword slot. Cerys suggests speaking a place-name. Flynry: "**Every key needs a lock.**" **The orrery's phrases are repeated in the platform writing**, though not in the same places. Near the centre **the orrery begins to float, whirl and glow**, as if pulling them in, but nothing more happens.
55. **1:12:52 — Vythorn takes over.** "**It's definitely some sort of key, though, as the book said… I think maybe I can decipher it.**" To Elianna: "**May I?**" He **interrupts the orrery's spinning and adjusts its rings, and the giant rings overhead move in response.** "I think I'm on to how this works."
56. **1:14:35 — The guardians wake.** The machinery grinds and shakes. **Humanoid fire figures (fire elementals) appear from doorways at the ends of the bridges.** Vythorn: "**I need more time… I suppose that you're just gonna have to defend me.**" The bridges are 15–20 ft wide.
57. **1:16:31 — Mold Earth.** Toddleheim blocks off the right bridge with an embellished **Mold Earth** wall ("the doom music just kicked in"). Behind the elementals appear **two large cat-like figures carrying large swords: Rakshasa.** "Whatever was set in motion to guard this place is now activated." Flynry: "**Are you sure you know what you're doing?**" Vythorn: "**No!** … just give me five minutes." "Don't have five minutes."
58. **1:17:53 — Haste.** **Cerys casts Haste on Vythorn** "to make him work faster." Initiative is rolled.

### Fight — the Oasis guardians
59. **1:19:17 — Rain.** Elianna goes first. She casts **Create Water as a 30-ft cube of rain over the party**; Drew rules the fire elementals have disadvantage in it. She then summons her **spiritual weapon**.
60. **1:21:29** The walled-off elemental pounds the Mold Earth wall, but **the earth is volcanic, so its fire barely cracks it.**
61. **1:22:19 — Comprehend Languages.** **Toddleheim casts Comprehend Languages**, touches the rings and ground, and **shouts out words and phrases to Vythorn**, giving him advantage. He then tries **Sleep** on the far Rakshasa. It "**didn't even break stride**" (it has more than 20 HP).
62. **1:25:27** An elemental's two touch attacks miss Cerys. Toddleheim: "**Too slow, baby!**"
63. **1:25:56 — Flynry.** He stays in the rain and swings his sword, but it barely bites the elemental ("metal against rock-type enemies"). **He sheathes it and falls back on his monk roots with Flurry of Blows**, taking a little fire damage from touching it.
64. **1:28:21 — The rings break the bridges.** Vythorn's Arcana and Insight checks, with Toddleheim's help, make **the rings around the bridges spin so fast they smash pieces of the bridges**. **The elementals and Rakshasa on the far ends fall into the lava below** and are knocked out of the fight, not killed. The central platform is cut off.
65. **1:29:58** An elemental already on the platform attacks Vythorn and Toddleheim. **The heavy rain sears it for 28.** It hits Vythorn again.
66. **1:31:24 — Abjure Enemy.** Cerys uses **Channel Divinity: Abjure Enemy**. **The elemental is frightened, its speed drops to 0, and it is stuck in the rain.**
67. **1:32:21 — "Halt."** Elianna casts **Command ("Halt")** on the other two. **It fails because fire elementals don't speak Common, Infernal, Abyssal or Celestial.** "They stop for a second and then they start moving again." Her **spiritual weapon** then deals force damage and finishes the frightened middle elemental. Flynry reacts with **Opportunist** and an unarmed strike; Drew rules his fists count as magical.
68. **1:36:52 — Cause Fear and Acid Splash.** Toddleheim casts **Cause Fear** on the two elementals by Cerys and Flynry. Both fail, so they can't move closer to him. **Acid Splash** deals 8 ("It is effective").
69. **1:40:23** A frightened elemental hits Flynry and Cerys. **They don't catch fire because they're in the rain cloud.**
70. **1:41:41** Flynry's **Inflict Wounds** rolls a **natural 1**; he follows with Flurry of Blows.
71. **1:43:10 — The elementals flee.** Vythorn, hasted, works on: **rings align and some stop spinning, while the outer rings "really take off"** in a hot wind. **The elementals that can still move jump off the platform and leave.** Cerys won't bother with opportunity attacks; someone offers to "attack them with insults."
72. **1:44:58 — The giant's rock.** **A flaming giant rock comes hurtling at them, thrown by a giant.** Toddleheim has the lowest Dex save and is targeted. **Flynry rolls a natural 20, tosses Toddleheim out of the way and takes the rock square on the chin.**
73. **1:46:47** The giant lumbers through the lava toward them. Cerys: "**You have got to get us out of here, man. By Thorn.**" Vythorn: "**I'm working as fast as I can. Just give me one moment. Just hold him off for a few seconds.**" Flynry plants his sword in the shattered, burning rock and looks back at Vythorn: "**This is it.**"
74. **1:47:11 — Boss fight.** The rain douses the rock's flames. **The giant reaches over the side of the platform, its top half towering like a raid boss, and brings its greatsword down on Cerys.**
75. **1:48:07 — Radiant consumption.** Cerys has everyone step back, **"goes full supernova" and blazes radiant light while screaming.** The 10-ft aura turns out to do only 5 damage, and it costs an action. Elianna's player: "It's like David and Goliath, if Goliath won." Flynry's player: "one of those balloons you let go of." **Drew rules the initial burst does 5d6 "because that was way too cool to be this lame"**, for 20 total. Her +2 longsword then misses.
76. **1:51:11** Elianna: "**Hey man, Vythorn, how's it looking?**" Vythorn: "**I am almost there. I'm almost there.**"
77. **1:51:32 — Banishment and Reduce fail.**
    - **Elianna casts Banishment at 4th level.** The giant has +5 Charisma and saves. Flynry's player: "Was he just picking up ladies at the giant bar?" Drew: "**Just because he's big doesn't mean he's not cool.**" She hits it with her spiritual weapon and steps behind it: "Yeah, okay, well, hurry up."
    - **Toddleheim tries Enlarge/Reduce.** The giant's Con is +10 and it saves ("He'd have been under the platform"). Toddleheim adds a **Magic Missile** for 17.
78. **1:55:06** Flynry's **Stunning Strike** through his greatsword attack doesn't stun. His follow-up blows land.
79. **1:56:52** The giant swings at Cerys and misses, then **cleaves Flynry for 23**: "I need some loving here." The table notes the new periapt is about to be useful.
80. **1:57:57** Vythorn's checks are high: "**That thing is looking pretty damn complete**… a matter of a few more moves." There are "like five brinks." Cerys **provokes the giant with her radiant light** to keep its focus on her.
81. **1:59:26** Vythorn turns to Elianna while fiddling with the orrery: "**Your friends are going to die.**" An uncertain voice adds: "[Due?] thanks to you. Can't you heal them?" Drew has Elianna roll a secret d20; the players hate it.
82. **2:00:46 — Cure Wounds and Sanctuary.** Elianna, "squishy," reluctantly **runs up to the mangled Flynry**. She heals him with **Cure Wounds at 3rd level** for 21 ("Oh, those hands are feeling nice"), then casts **Sanctuary** on him as a bonus action. Flynry's player: "Flynry sits down… starts writing a thank you note. **Dear Anna, thank you so much for the sanctuary.**"

### The betrayal
83. **2:03:32** As Elianna rushes forward, they all hear Vythorn behind them: "**I wish it could be different, but this only works for one.**" **He rips the orrery apart and pulls it over himself like a portal he's pulling himself through.**
84. At the same moment, **Toddleheim, still comprehending the languages, realizes that a line he had fed to Vythorn without reading it properly said the key is for one person: one use.**
85. **2:04:30** "**Nothing personal, the Grandmaster has to be saved.**" Vythorn dives into the portal. **An explosion from the orrery goes over their heads and rips the top half of the giant completely off**, leaving "a stump of blood… in the sky." **The Arbiter is gone, and the party is left standing on the platform.** The file ends here.

## Who and what appears
### Characters
**Flynry** (BdTeej)
- Former monk who reverts to monk technique: Flurry of Blows, ki points, Stunning Strike, magical fists, Opportunist. He also casts Inflict Wounds.
- **Carries a sword whose reflection shows a person's true nature or urges** (the "Sword of Truth" in his player's words). He used it on a guard before and on Sky this episode. Uses a greatsword in the fight.
- Knows from "the large book" about **the Golden Lady statue** and **an eye-shaped pendant with a red gem**.
- **Traded the Atlas of Endless Horizons for the Periapt of Wound Closure.** Lost his shortbow to Sky's toll.
- Wants to return to "the living."
- Never been to a party; "not the leader of anything besides maybe lunch."
- Took the giant's rock for Toddleheim.

**Elianna** (ParallaxStella)
- Tiefling cleric: Hellish Rebuke (once per long rest), spiritual weapon, Create Water, Command, Banishment, Cure Wounds, Sanctuary. Speaks Common, Infernal, Abyssal, Celestial.
- **Carries the orrery hidden in her shirt** (the "third boob" running gag). It vibrates and glows blue near the Oasis.

### Lore statements
- **Soul gems are dead people** and serve as currency. The outlander cavern is one gigantic soul gem.
- **Reflective surfaces (mirrors, polished crystal) lead to "echoes of planes"** that may once have been connected and broke apart. Every surface leads somewhere different.
- Strongholds open by speaking a name, being shown, or a reflective surface.
- **"Sand sickness" is called a lie of the queen** by the outlanders, who have lived in the sands for years.
- Outlanders were exiled for dissent against the monarchy or for keeping soul gems, or have "no function" within the walls.
- **The Oasis key works for only one person.** Vythorn's stated reason for taking it is saving the Grandmaster.
