In a cold desert of blue sand, **[[Locket]]**, **[[Toddleheim]]** and the Grandmaster's own personal assistant **[[Elianna]]** finally found their lost friend **[[Flynry]]**, mid-battle and barely recognizable. What began as a scrappy scorpion fight turned into a tearful reunion and a grim lesson about where they really were. It ended with a sudden, shocking death.

## The Story

### Claws in the Sand

The fight was already raging when this chapter began. Flynry slammed his sword down and a streak of flame raced across the sand, while a second sand scorpion burrowed up to snap and sting at him. It missed him entirely. His time in this place had changed him. He swept his **"now rich black cloak"** over one scorpion's head and spun it aside "like he's bullfighting," then sidestepped a tail that crashed into the ground where he had been standing.

Locket came over the hill and froze for a moment at the sight of Flynry's new look, reflected in fire and glass. Then the tabaxi ranger dove in with her short swords. These scorpions were nasty work. Every claw she hacked off reached back to snap at her, and every severed piece sucked sand up from below to re-form. She kept hacking until the creature thinned out, then finished it with a final slash across the torso. "They're pretty hefty, so be careful," she warned the others.

Toddleheim had noticed that Flynry's flames turned the sand to glass, and he tested whether his own fire would do the same. His first bolt missed. A volley of magic missiles knocked a chunk off a scorpion, and the chunk quickly grew back. Flynry smiled at the old wizard and showed him how it was done. With a blade that "seems far too wieldy for its size," he spun into a backhand slash. Wherever the flaming sword cut, the scorpion turned to glass, and those limbs never grew back.

### The Assistant Goes to War

Elianna had never been in a battle. She was among people she barely knew, somewhere she had never been, facing monsters she had only read about. Her response was a steady stream of "fuck, fuck, fuck." She still outlined the scorpions around Flynry in **blinding neon purple** fairy light, which glowed vividly against the blue sand, and she wrapped Flynry in a sparkly purple shield of protection. "Flynn's been alone a long time," his player admitted. "We're ready to have whatever buffs or bonuses." Elianna muttered under her breath, **"I didn't sign up for this."**

The scorpions mobbed Flynry, and wave after wave of claws and stingers missed him or glanced off the shield. He flipped and spun with an acrobatic grace nobody had seen from this large man before, "very like Legolas with a big sword," or "like LeBron James is doing a one-on-one against some elementary schoolers." Toddleheim's next fire bolt glassed a glowing scorpion and left "a shiny reflective surface as an imprint." It proved that fire hit these sand creatures twice as hard.

Flynry tried a sweeping greatsword spin. It caught the glowing scorpion and carried on into a badly wounded one that had been creeping toward his back, thinking it wasn't the target. Aqua flame blinded the creeper and it was "slashed in half, dead ski." His follow-up at "Captain Glowy" found nothing, because the scorpion simply sank into the sand and popped up elsewhere. "Ever slippery."

Then came Elianna's moment. She summoned a spectral **scythe**, which spun through the air, sheared off both of a scorpion's pincers and took its head. The head landed right at Flynry's feet, "almost like it's a gift," gazing up into his eyes. He gave it an inquisitive look, then nodded at the stranger: yes, this is the way. **Elianna tried to curtsy and fell over.** Inspired by the scythe, Locket landed a perfect strike on the scorpion in front of her and made "scorpion sushi" of it with her short blades.

### Something Bigger

The last three scorpions began to retreat. For the first time Flynry showed real urgency, "like the thought of these escaping is deeply bothersome." He drove his sword down at one and missed. The scorpions didn't so much burrow as *become* the sand. For a moment all was calm, and the cold wind kept blowing.

Elianna felt it first. "Does anyone else hear or feel that?" ("This is Dune," her player noted.) A blast of sand erupted into the sky behind Flynry. The little scorpions had poured back together into one **giant scorpion**, "double on everything," which burst up from under their feet. Toddleheim very nearly announced a fireball, which alarmed everyone ("We're all about to get singed"). He settled for a doubled fire bolt and a heavy volley of magic missiles. The giant snapped its claws into Elianna and stung Flynry, poisoning him, though his natural resistance blunted the venom.

Elianna screamed in pain. She was, after all, a personal assistant. Then she remembered the Grandmaster's words that she must come back safely. In a sudden calm she pointed at the scorpion and stared, and **a burst of purple flame engulfed it.** The giant scorpion exploded.

### The Reunion

At last they could look at their friend properly. Flynry's hair color had changed, and so had his stature and his once distinctive voice. "It almost doesn't seem like him, but you can tell it's him." **"My friends, you've made it,"** he said. Toddleheim rushed up and hugged him: "My boy, how you've grown." They owed this to [[The Arbiter|the Arbiter]], Toddleheim explained, who "sent us here in the most painful fashion." Locket added that [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] had written to say there might be a way to bring him back, "and we immediately came."

Flynry's eyes glistened. He had been "surrounded by so much evil," he said, but that was a story for later. He thanked Elianna for "a rather eloquent dispatching of a troublesome scorpion." She introduced herself as the Grandmaster's personal assistant, and Flynry decided he wanted one of those. "I have a few I could recommend," she offered. Toddleheim doubted the Arbiter had built this place: "There's too much power here, simply."

### We Are All Dead

Elianna knew the name of this place from books and whispers: **[[The Sands of Eternity]]**. The name stirred something in Toddleheim, a book of legends he had fought over in some dark library decades ago. It was a realm for the dead, apart from every other underworld. Only souls somehow attached to it, or consumed by it, ended up here. **"I do regret to inform you that we are all dead,"** he announced. If there was no fix, "I look forward to an eternity fighting scorpions alongside you, brother." Locket realized this was why arriving had hurt so much: the Arbiter had disintegrated them.

The news helped Flynry fit some pieces together. He knelt and pulled a **wooden spoon** out of the sand. Before leaving home base he had bought one for [[Frank]], the disgruntled chef who cooked the heroes' meals with bones and sticks. **"Your experience here is what your mind makes it,"** he said. His sword, his armor, even himself, were "of the sand." He had battled a kind of psychosis here, and his origins here were "based on lies," so part of him held back. Was this reunion even real? For him it had been years. For them it had been months. "There is no rising of the sun. There is no shifting of the stars. There is simply the void."

Elianna healed his scars, and Flynry said he had forgotten how refreshing healing magic could be. She explained she had learned it "because I'm constantly tripping and hurting myself." In her notebook she had been sketching him for the Grandmaster and quietly fretting over the promise she had made.

### Truth

Flynry's sword bore one word: **Truth**. "It is a mirror shaped like a sword." In it he still saw his old self, with the velvet cloak, the slightly beastish face and the long brunette hair and beard. Elianna looked into it seeking hope. She saw **[[The ziggurat|a ziggurat]]** crowned with a mirror-like gem, beaming light into the void sky. The vision then rewound along a path until she was looking over her own shoulder. "This sword has shown you the truth." She sketched the map, and Flynry gently turned it the right way up for her. In all his years here, he said, he had never seen a single structure.

When she asked again, the blade showed only her reflection. "Smells like single use," Toddleheim remarked, and when he asked, it showed him only his own reflection too. Frustrated, Elianna kicked sand at it, and the blade drank the sand in "as if it was a puddle."

### The Arrow

Locket stepped up and took the sword. **"Show us the way,"** she said, thinking of the disintegration and of finding Flynry again. Past her reflection she saw a shadow-cloaked figure atop a dune, then several figures, one perhaps chasing another. She realized it was no vision. She turned with the sword in hand, and **an arrow went straight through her throat.** Her blood poured out almost instantly and she sank to her knees, staring in shock at Flynry and Toddleheim. Flynry slid through the sand to catch her, and as his arms closed around her, **her body turned to sand and poured through his fingers.** In the Sands of Eternity, a soul that dies is gone forever. Locket was dead for good.
