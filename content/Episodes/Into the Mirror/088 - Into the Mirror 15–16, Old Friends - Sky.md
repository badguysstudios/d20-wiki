## What happens
### Part 15, "Old Friends": the Arbiter in the library

#### Meeting the Arbiter
1. **0:00:00 The Arbiter wakes from his illusion.** The party stares into the face of the Arbiter.
   - He looks different from the last time they saw him.
   - For **Flynry** it has been some time since they met. For the rest, he **killed them not too long ago**.
   - He wears a wry smile that wrenches up his cheeks: "**Yes, I can make good use of you.**"
   - He takes in the library and starts scheming.
2. **0:00:45 Recognition.** The Arbiter asks, "Sorry, have we met?"
   - Flynry suspects a trick: "**This is a trick. This is an illusion of this place.**"
   - The Arbiter recognizes Toddleheim ("him and, well, just him") and points at Elianna as well.
3. **0:01:55 Where are they?** The Arbiter: "What matters is you're here, and now we can get to work. What is this place?"
   - Flynry: "Looks like a library to me." The Arbiter: "No library I've ever seen."
   - Drew notes the Arbiter is "oblivious to the fact that he already murdered you guys once."
   - Toddleheim: "We're off somewhere." The Arbiter: "**Everybody's somewhere. Be more specific! Aren't you a scholar?**"
4. **0:03:13 What he remembers.** Elianna asks for the last thing he remembers.
   - The Arbiter: "**being in chains and... Serving the Grandmaster. I remember killing you and then I went to lunch and then moved on.**"
   - His eyes widen: "You have to tell me where we are right now. I have to get back to the Grandmaster."
5. **0:04:07 Cerys is lost.** She asks what is going on.
   - Elianna: "**Well, he's the one that killed us.**"
   - Cerys: "Killed you essentially to bring you here... And it looks like you're dead now too, I guess. Welcome."
6. **0:04:43 Flynry's accusation.** "**He's also a murderer, a thief, a liar, and the source of evil in my life.**" The Arbiter: "Sorry, do I know you?"
   - Cerys suggests they work together and asks Flynry why he's being so dramatic.
   - Flynry: "Because this man is the reason that I'm in this mess in the first place. This has killed my friends, has betrayed everything that I've known, has been the source of my pain, my anguish. My loss!" He stands defeated, thinking of the time he arrived here.
7. **0:06:01 Kill him?** Cerys: "So do we kill him then?" Flynry: "I certainly would like to."
8. **0:06:07 "The meteor."** The Arbiter cocks his head: "**Flynry. Ah, I see. You've done something with your hair. Are you still upset about the meteor, or...?**"
   - Someone (likely Flynry) says "I'm long past the meteor."
   - The Arbiter points out that he killed the others, not Flynry, and doesn't see why Flynry is so sour with him. The speaker split is unclear.
   - Flynry: "I'm sure she's happy to be rid of you." The Arbiter: "You don't understand. **I'm dead.**" Flynry: "**The best news I've heard in years.**"

#### The Arbiter's story
9. **0:07:08 Why he cares.** The Arbiter: "**I was murdered in the Dread Fortress by somebody now who is presumably holding the Grandmaster hostage, or worse.**" Flynry asks why he cares.
   - The Arbiter: "**I've known Elusia longer than any of you. A thousand years.**"
   - Flynry: "And you tried to overthrow her, have her killed."
   - Flynry: "In a tournament that came down to the last fight, one death determined whether or not you stand here before us supposedly remorseful, or whether you would remain reigning over a stolen kingdom, spitting on her corpse."
10. **0:08:08 "Let me tell you a story, children."** Demeaning, he gathers them round.
    - "**Many years ago, I rescued the Grandmaster. She had been exiled after the death of her father. I brought her home to challenge her uncle, a man known as the Shadow King.**"
    - The Shadow King was "a very powerful mage. Bent on bolstering his own power, becoming a god. He was siphoning the life force from the void." Together they overthrew him, and **Elusia killed him**.
11. **0:09:30 The "small miscalculation": the Box of Chaos.** "**Knowledge is the most significant source of power in all of the universes. And a knowledge contained within the soul of the Shadow King would be a waste.**"
    - "**I created the Box of Chaos to contain the soul of the Shadow King.**"
    - "Snake was playing me the entire time, slowly regaining his form." He doesn't know how.
    - He siphoned the King's power "for a time, use it for the good," but "his intentions clouded my mind."
    - Flynry: "Sounds like a dose of your own medicine to me."
    - The Arbiter: "**if you want my honest opinion, it was him that killed you, not me. I wasn't even in control of my faculties.**"
    - Toddleheim: "**A likely story.**"
12. **0:11:11 Orin.** Flynry: "**I assume it was your lack of faculties that... obliterated Orin?**"
    - The Arbiter: "Let me level with you, I don't really know."
    - "The power I was siphoning from the Box of Chaos was... intoxicating in some ways, and other ways... it whispered to me."
    - He believed he was serving the Void, "whether that's the truth or not, I don't know. Doesn't matter at this point, we're all dead... What I do care about is saving the Grandmaster."
13. **0:12:17 Why not leave it to them?** Flynry: "Why not entrust us to save the Grandmaster? Tell us what you know... and return you to the sand here and now."
    - The Arbiter: "if these folks here couldn't even defeat me, then how do you expect to defeat the Shadow King?"
    - "**His power has grown. Exponentially, he siphoned off my own life force, along with the power I stole from him and amplified the knowledge I increased over the years, now his, at his command. He is a god.** Not even I am most likely going to be able to stop him. Not in my current state, anyway."

#### Elianna tests the story
14. **0:13:24 What Elianna knows.** Flynry asks Elianna, who spent the most time with him recently, if any of it is true. She rolls insight (24) and history (13).
    - From being around the Dread Fortress and close to the Grandmaster, she knows the story is factually true: he helped the Grandmaster overthrow her uncle and save the Void.
    - **That was when the Grandmaster named him the Arbiter, "700 years" ago.** Despite the recent conflict, **he has served her for 700 years** (Drew's narration).
    - She also knows the Grandmaster "**would welcome the challenge**" and values "deciding things in honorable combat." That is why **she kept him around (held captive) rather than executing him.**
    - Elianna relays that the first part is true, but "she does have her reasons. I still don't trust him. **And he's always been a pain in the ass.**"
15. **0:16:58 An apology, sort of.** The Arbiter: "What if I said I was sorry, would that help any here?" "No." "Good, because I wasn't probably going to."
16. **0:17:30 The throne room.** Asked what happened to the Grandmaster, he is "a little fuzzy." The Shadow King's influence "seeped into my mind."
    - "I remember now... **I died in the throne room.**"
    - "He somehow has been able to... **Rebuild a body, I think using the sacrifices to the Box of Chaos. An arm here, a leg there. Cryptocurrency.**" He is "very powerful now."
17. **0:18:18 Size.** Drew: the Arbiter is **about 8 feet tall**. "Him and the Grandmaster are both pretty large in stature."

#### The tackle
18. **0:18:35 Elianna tackles the Arbiter.** Flynry sees her line up and **sidesteps to open the lane**.
    - She wins strength against his athletics (12 vs 9), sprints and tackles him. She sits on his chest **bashing his head with her book**.
    - Flynry stands with arms crossed, laughing. Drew later notes "**He's also not as strong here as he used to be.**"
19. **0:20:24 Time rewinds.** As the book comes down, **the Arbiter's eyes flare with milky, void-white swirls**. Everyone seems to blink.
    - **Elianna is back where she stood before the tackle**, and he is upright again: he has shifted time.
    - Elianna: "**God, not here too!**" She stomps her foot.
    - The Arbiter brushes himself off: "Maybe we could just talk first. If you want to fight, then we could do it after. **Be more than happy to kill you again.**"

#### "My name is Vythorn"
20. **0:21:17 Truce talks.** Cerys looks for common ground: they're all looking for this Grandmaster.
    - The Arbiter: "Maybe we've gotten off on the wrong foot. I killed you. You have said some terrible things about me... I let it go."
    - "**My name, let's begin here, is Vythorn.**" Elianna's player asks for the spelling: V-y-t-h-o-r-n.
21. **0:22:18 His aims.** "We have a common interest. **I want to see the Grandmaster brought to safety, and I would like to see the Shadow King dead. Um, and I would like my power back.**"
    - **Elianna flips him off.** Vythorn: "**Yes, you are Elusia's type.**"

#### Flynry's test: the sword
22. **0:23:38 The condition.** Flynry **unstraps his sword, walks slowly to the Arbiter and sets it point-down in front of him**: "**Stare into this and tell me what you see. Lie and I'll know.**" Cerys: "Oh, good. Good idea."
23. **0:24:15 What Vythorn sees.** "I don't understand. **I see myself.**"
    - "Not bad, honestly. Little gray around the beard. Probably use a haircut."
    - He sees himself as the party remembers him, **with his full power**.
    - Flynry slings the sword back and tells Toddleheim: "**Nothing's changed. It's the same as it's always been.**"
24. **0:25:29 Toddleheim agrees to work with him.** "Well, we're in a bit of a pickle here. We still need to get home... Plus, with the skill he's just shown, he could probably kill us all again anyway. **Let's watch each other's backs.**" Vythorn: "Agreed."
    - Elianna agrees too: "I want all of what he wants, except for the last part, him getting power."

#### The blood oath
25. **0:26:12 Flynry asks Cerys to swear.** Cerys gives her name. Flynry: swear "that you are with us, and that he is ultimately never to be trusted, and should always be considered our enemy, although we may have a common goal."
    - Cerys: "**Oh, swear. You know I'm a paladin, right?**" She wants something to seal it.
26. **0:27:05 The contract.** **Elianna slams down her little notebook with two pens: "Contract?"** Flynry: "Contract. **A blood oath.**" Cerys: "A blood oath?" Flynry: "Unless you think that's too much for you."
    - Toddleheim (heard as "Donahoe") stands in the back stroking his beard and smirking.
    - Elianna is excited; contracts are part of her normal daily work.
28. **0:28:30 The signing.** Cerys cuts her hand and seals it on Elianna's paper. **Flynry slices his own and lays his mark over hers, leaving a bloody X on the page.**
    - Cerys: "**So I swear fealty to you all. And if this one tries to get me to turn on you, I will not.**"
    - Flynry: "**I swear that you now will be under this crew's protection in dangerous and perilous times from now until the end of this quest.**"
29. **0:29:49 The witness.** Elianna takes the book to **Toddleheim to sign as witness**.
    - Flynry: "**Did we just get blood married?**" Elianna: "I will go down with this ship."
    - Toddleheim: "With blood, I assume." He signs his name with a pen, chuckling. Elianna snaps the book closed: "All done."
    - Vythorn: "Well, that was exciting. Great spectacle here."

#### The Sands of Eternity
30. **0:30:38 He knows the place.** Vythorn: "**I know this place. We're in the Sands of Eternity.**" He seems almost excited.
    - The players debate how much to tell him. Toddleheim: "We have to show him the orrery. And the queen."
    - Cerys: "Just know that I'm a traveler of these realms, and I helped get them here. ... We've been trying to find a way back." Vythorn: "To the living plane?"
31. **0:32:12 His illusion.** Toddleheim asks about it.
    - "It wasn't much different than you're seeing before you. **Except these damn books kept turning to sand.**" He pulls one, and **this time it doesn't turn to sand**. "I'm something of a reader."
32. **0:32:52 Scholar against scholar.** Vythorn: "You should understand, you're a fellow scholar." Toddleheim: "Indeed, I'm not quite as obsessed with gaining power."
    - Vythorn: "**Power, knowledge, call it whatever you want. Is it any different?**"
    - Toddleheim: "**Oh, yes. One seeks to be knowledgeable... the machinations of the universe, even. And one just seeks to rule. There's a very big difference, scholar.**"
    - Vythorn: "Perhaps the Shadow King's aim is to rule, but I have no aims to rule."
    - Toddleheim: "And that knowledge ended me up in this wonderful place."
33. **0:34:05 Lore: the Sands' history.**
    - "**the Sands of Eternity was once adjacent to the Living Plane.**" The door was regularly opened by those who lived there, people came and went, and there was trade.
    - "**That all ended some 2000 years ago, maybe? The doorways closed and the realm itself seemed to have vanished. Other than the connections through the mirrors.**"
    - Since then, the living have been unable to come here. Some scholars theorize it was destroyed.
34. **0:35:44 An experiment.** On an insight check, Elianna (15) gets the sense that **he wasn't 100% sure where he was sending them through the mirror. They were "a little bit of an experiment."** Cerys: "Thanks. Wow. I get it."
    - Vythorn: "presumably there's a way to open the door. How long have you been here?"
    - Flynry quietly hangs his head; it is a painful thing to think about.
    - Cerys: "**I can tell you I've been here longer than the rest of them. And as far as I know, There's no way out.**"

#### The library's books
35. **0:37:39 Reading.** Everyone searches the books.
    - Most are mundane: histories, science and legal documents, much like a normal library.
    - Others describe animals and planes foreign to them.
36. **0:39:10 Toddleheim's find.** He finds books on **the Sands of Eternity itself**, with pictures of cities, **Moratorios** among them.
    - **There are other cities, peoples and cultures**, so the place is bigger than they imagined. Maybe "you just have to know how to navigate the sands."
    - A second look (a poor roll) only confirms the sands were once connected to other planes.
    - Vythorn finds nothing beyond what he already knew.
37. **0:41:48 Vythorn asks Cerys for a scholar.** She looks to Flynry.
    - Flynry has a realization: the goal is to get back, and what happens to the people here is of no consequence. He gives her a nod.
    - Cerys: "**There is a king and queen of these lands.** ... the king is affected by an ailment, but the queen at least can maybe help... She's the one that kind of sent us on our way to try to look here."
    - Vythorn: "we don't have a lot of time. **If you knew the Shadow King like I know him, then you would know that the Grandmaster is not safe, and neither is the Void.**" "I will follow your lead then."

#### The Orrery of Dreams
38. **0:44:39 The device.** Flynry: "Royalty mentioned a device... **capable of creating opportunities for transversal.**" Vythorn turns his head very slowly: "What sort of device?"
39. **0:45:17 The tome of special items.** With an idea of what to look for, he vanishes behind a shelf amid shuffling and flipping.
    - He returns with a **huge tome** and slams it on a table. It describes "a number of items with special properties in this place": mundane-looking scientific artifacts, statues and the like.
    - He flips past a page showing **the Orrery of Dreams**, and Cerys asks him to hold it.
40. **0:47:17 Cerys comes clean.** "Look, it's true, we haven't been the most forthcoming with you... cryptic." Vythorn: "**Shocking.**" Cerys: "**This item is what we're looking for.**"
41. **0:47:53 What the book says.**
    - The Orrery is a very powerful item **crafted within the Sands of Eternity**, with "**an immense power capable of shaping reality**."
    - It says nothing specific about doorways. Vythorn: "Is there something you know that I don't?"
42. **0:49:11 What the queen told them (recap).**
    - The queen said the Orrery could open a doorway between the planes. Her mages would decipher it so she, and hopefully everyone else, could go through.
    - It was unknown how many could pass, and their knowledge of it was "fractured."
    - When it collided with Flynry's sword, it transported them here, "conveniently" to the Arbiter.
43. **0:50:27 Who holds it.** Elianna has it; she moves her bag behind her.
    - Cerys: "We have it right now. It's what we use to get us here."
    - Flynry: "**Well, I don't think we used it at all. I think it used us.**"
    - Vythorn: "**It brought you here. Yes. To me.**" Elianna: "No, to a library."
    - Cerys: "it's kind of interesting that the thing brought us to this library to meet this arbiter of yours. **Maybe there's some sort of fate play here.**"
44. **0:52:08 Showing it to Vythorn.** Vythorn asks to examine it.
    - Toddleheim fears this is where they die again; at the queen's court, others could step in if he betrays them.
    - The players also find the queen "kind of shady." Flynry's player jokes she and the Arbiter would form a secret handshake.
    - **Elianna sets the Orrery on the table, shooting daggers at the Arbiter** and ready to tackle again.
    - **He doesn't touch it.** He kneels way down beside it and studies the rings.
45. **0:54:27 The inside ring.** "There's some mumbo-jumbo about the truth and some cryptic flowery words, but written here on the inside ring..."
    - "It does speak of this is **some sort of key. It's a piece of a larger machine**... it's a part of a whole."
    - "**I don't believe it will be its full potential until we bring it to the right place or find the right mechanism that unlocks it.**"
    - Elianna: "We literally just got here and had to deal with you."
46. **0:55:54 The neighbouring pages.** Flynry studies the pages before and after the Orrery.
    - **Some items in the book were created together and can interact with each other.**
    - **Before:** a small statue or figurine, perhaps cast bronze.
    - **After:** a very ornate amulet with a gem of red hue.
    - **All of the objects bear similar script**, like the Orrery's, which might be readable with the actual item.
47. **0:58:07 The sword of truth.** Toddleheim looks for Flynry's sword and finds it.
    - It is listed as "**the sword of truth**" (Drew: "which, you know, you aptly named it"). It is one of these sand-made objects.
    - It "**has the ability to look beyond the physical... to gaze beyond what is apparent or what can be seen with the naked eye.**"
    - Drew's "race cars" and Flynry's "transform into a truck" are table jokes.
48. **1:00:09 Vythorn keeps the book.** Flynry: "I think we need to take this book with us."
    - He fumbles the name ("Viking... Thornbush"): "You're big enough to carry this book."
    - Vythorn: "**Don't worry, it's Vythorn. It's a large name for a small brain.**"
    - He closes the tome with one hand and **it disappears**. "I'll keep on to it."

### Part 16, "Sky"

#### Trying to leave
50. **1:04:22 Orrery and sword.** Trusting Cerys's talk of fate, **Flynry touches the Orrery and his sword together again. Nothing happens.**
    - In the dragon's lair they had spun and whirled, and the sword had glowed blue-greenish. Now there is no interaction.
    - He clicks them together faster ("This was different the first time"), gives up and hands the Orrery to Elianna.
51. **1:05:28 The city's name.** Toddleheim recalls that saying the city's name might take them back.
    - **The only entrance is the door they came in by.**
52. **1:05:55 Through the crack.** Flynry peeks and sees **three figures** on the sand a short way off. The man shouts: "**Come on out! We know you're in there.**"
    - Flynry: "Turns out that they're looking for us. Or rather him, more likely." Vythorn: "Hey, I just got here."
    - Flynry starts pulling books off the shelf, mimicking Vythorn.

    - Leader: "I'm sorry, I didn't realize this was your home. Is it yours?" Flynry: "**It's no one's.**"

56. **1:11:15 "You can find us in Mortorios."** Flynry: "Look, we don't know you, and we've had our fair share of unfortunate run-ins for today. **If you want to speak with us, you can find us in Mortorios.**"

    - **Cerys walks to the Orrery and says "Mortorius." Nothing happens.**
    - Flynry tries once more: "You can find us in... **Wartorios!**"
57. **1:12:17 The inscription in the mirror.** Elianna re-reads the Orrery; there's nothing new.
    - She holds it up to Flynry's sword to see if the inscriptions change in the reflection: "They look backwards to you." The result is unclear in the audio.
58. **1:13:16 The steward.** The leader: "**There's only one way out of there. Trust me, I know.**"
    - Flynry: "That's a very curious thing to say."
    - Elianna: "Maybe he's the librarian?" Flynry relays the question.
    - Leader: "**Well, I have no interest in books, but I am a steward of this place, in a way.**"
59. **1:13:43 Opening the door.** Flynry: "I'm opening the door now, but mind you, **we are great in number and very powerful.**"
    - **Elianna shoves the Arbiter out in front.** He rolls his eyes: "**I don't know why you all are such cowards**."
    - He opens a door that is large even for him and walks out onto the sand. The others follow.

#### Sky

#### Fight: Sky's raiders, round 1

    - Vythorn also saves. **Toddleheim is baned.**

66. **1:25:12 Elianna's turn.**
    - She casts **Silence**, a 20-ft sphere around the raiders.
    - She casts **Sanctuary on Toddleheim**. Flynry: "You're living large right now, bro."
67. **1:27:43 Flynry shouts into the silence.**
    - He takes position at the front. He doesn't realize what Elianna cast; he thinks the semi-transparent bubble is protection.
    - Feeling strong after the Bane save, he yells to intimidate them, but it comes out as nothing. **The raiders look at each other and shrug.**
    - Elianna: "Flynry, I cast silence, just letting you know."
68. **1:28:52 Flynry attacks the orc.** He runs at the orc with the big sword.
    - One greatsword strike lands and one misses.
    - He follows with a **Flurry of Blows**, two unarmed strikes, both landing. "He's kind of beefy."

70. **1:33:13 Toddleheim the Great.**
    - He considers Acid Splash but there are too many friends nearby ("Oh, it hurts so much! My skin!").

## Who and what appears
### Characters
**Vythorn / the Arbiter** (Drew)
- **Situation:** Dead and in the Sands of Eternity. He was murdered in the Dread Fortress ("I died in the throne room") by the Shadow King, whom he believes is now holding the Grandmaster hostage "or worse." He had been stuck in an illusion in the library where books kept turning to sand.
- **Last memories:** being in chains serving the Grandmaster, killing the party, then "lunch."
- **His account of the past:**
  - He rescued the exiled Elusia after her father's death and brought her home to challenge her uncle, the Shadow King.
  - He then created the **Box of Chaos** to hold the Shadow King's soul, siphoned its power, and was clouded and whispered to by it.
  - He claims the King, not he, killed the party, and says he doesn't know whether he obliterated Orin.
- **His relationship with Elusia:** says he has known her "a thousand years." Per Elianna's knowledge (Drew's narration), she named him Arbiter 700 years ago and he has served her for 700 years.
- **Stated aims:** Grandmaster safe, Shadow King dead, "my power back."
- **Size and power:** about 8 ft tall. Weaker here than before. Can still rewind a moment in time (undoing Elianna's tackle) and grant Fortune's Favor and Chronal Shift rerolls. Cannot do large time reversal in the dampened area outside the library. Casts thunder step.
- **Other actions:** made the tome disappear "into" his keeping. Says he did not know for sure where the mirror would send the party.

**Flynry** (BdTeej)
- **History:** Has known the Arbiter longer than the others; the Arbiter says he "didn't kill" him. He blames the Arbiter for killing his friends, betraying all he knew, and "my loss." The Arbiter mentions "the meteor" and a new hairstyle.
- **Time in the Sands:** has been in the Sands a long and painful time.
- **Gear:** carries the **sword of truth**, which shows Vythorn his true (full-power) self and can ignite with cerulean, sand-colored flame.
- **Character:** strong, showy and prickly. Fights hand-to-hand with Flurry of Blows. Swore the party's protection to Cerys in a blood oath. Downed by the orc, healed back up.

**Elianna** (ParallaxStella)
- **Background:** Close to the Grandmaster and familiar with the Dread Fortress and its history. Contracts are part of her daily routine.
- **Tools:** carries a notebook and two pens, and a book she used to bash the Arbiter.
- **Actions:** tackled the Arbiter; carries the Orrery, which she hid in her cleavage. Casts Silence, Sanctuary, thaumaturgy, Counterspell, Mass Cure Wounds and Toll the Dead. Flipped off the Arbiter.

**Toddleheim** ("Toddleheim the Great", Ahnubyss)
- **Character:** A scholar. Distinguishes knowledge from the desire to rule. Witnessed the blood oath.
- **Discoveries:** found the books on the Sands, including Moratorios and other cities, and found the sword of truth's entry.
- **In the fight:** baned, warded, and counterspelled while answering Sky's offer.

**Cerys** (Miss_Magitek; the YouTube cast says Locket)
- **Background:** A "traveler of these realms" who helped bring the others here. Has been in the Sands longer than the rest and believed there was no way out. Knows of the king (who has an ailment) and the queen.
- **The oath:** a paladin who admits, perhaps only to the table, that she is an **oath breaker** paladin. Swore a blood oath of fealty to the party against Vythorn.
- **In the fight:** knocked unconscious by Steel Wind Strike, healed, then scored a smiting critical on Sky.

**Sky**
- **Appearance:** a horned tiefling leader of outlander raiders, with a spinning, glowing glaive.
- **Magic:** casts Bane, Hellish Rebuke and Counterspell. Resistant to fire.
- **Claims:** calls himself "a steward of this place, in a way" and says "There's only one way out of there."
- **Motive:** robs travelers to feed "mouths" and mentions going "back home." Offered Toddleheim surrender.
- **Status:** barely standing at the end.

**The orc raider:** has a greatsword and rages. Downed Flynry, hit Vythorn, and shrugged off Toll the Dead.

**The wizard raider:** slender, possibly a dragonborn or "lizard man." Cast Steel Wind Strike on the whole party.

**Elusia, the Grandmaster:** Named to Cerys for the first time. Per Elianna, she welcomes challenge and honorable combat, which is why she kept the Arbiter alive. Vythorn: "you are Elusia's type."

**The Shadow King:** the Grandmaster's uncle and a mage who siphoned the Void's life force. Killed by Elusia, his soul held in the Box of Chaos. He influenced the Arbiter and rebuilt a body from sacrifices to the Box. Now "a god."

**The queen and the king of the Sands:** The king has an ailment. The queen sent the party to the library and wants the Orrery so her mages can open a doorway. The players find her "shady" (table talk).

**Orin:** someone the Arbiter is said to have "obliterated." Not otherwise explained.

### Places
- **The library (Sands of Eternity):** a large building with one large door. Holds mundane books and books of other planes, the Sands themselves, and a tome of special items.
- **The Sands of Eternity:**
  - Once adjacent to the Living Plane, with doors and trade.
  - Cut off about 2000 years ago; reachable only through mirrors since.
  - Has multiple cities and cultures, with outlanders roaming.
  - The area outside the library dampens some magic.
- **Moratorios / Mortorios:** a city of the Sands and the party's base. Likely the "walled city" Sky mentions (inferred from 1:15:15 and 1:11:15).
- **The Dread Fortress and its throne room:** where the Arbiter was murdered.
- **The Living Plane / land of the living**.
- **The dragon's lair:** earlier, where the Orrery and sword first reacted.

### Items
- **The Orrery of Dreams:**
  - Crafted in the Sands; "immense power capable of shaping reality." Believed by the queen to open a doorway between planes.
  - Its inside-ring script names it a **key, part of a larger machine**.
  - It did not react to the sword this time, and saying the city's name near it did nothing.
- **The sword of truth:** Flynry's sword, sand-made, "the ability to look beyond the physical." Ignites in cerulean flame.
- **The tome of special items:** shows the Orrery, a bronze-like figurine and a red-gemmed ornate amulet, all sharing script and able to interact. Vythorn took it and made it vanish.
- **Elianna's notebook:** holds the blood oath, a bloody X, and Toddleheim's witness signature.
- **The Box of Chaos:** as described by Vythorn.

### Lore statements
- The Arbiter created the Box of Chaos to keep the Shadow King's knowledge, and was influenced by it.
- The Shadow King rebuilt his body from sacrifices to the Box of Chaos.
- The Sands of Eternity's history and the 2000-year closure.
- In the Sands, "If they were dead, they'd be sand" (Drew, 1:48:18): the dead here crumble to sand.
- Books in the Arbiter's illusion turned to sand until he was freed.
