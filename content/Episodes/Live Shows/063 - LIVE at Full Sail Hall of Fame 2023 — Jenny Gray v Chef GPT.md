The Dread Fortress came to Full Sail for a live Deathmatch between **[[Chef GPT]]**, an artificer robot who "serves murder" (played by KidCalrissian), and **[[Jenny Gray]]**, a high-school druid whose real priority was getting to prom (played by ErinEberhardt). Their battleground was a field of burning lava, but the fight was taken over by a rabid chinchilla, a dance-off, a pillow fight and three magic puppies. It ended with a six-pound pink Pomeranian falling into the lava in a ball gown.

## The Story

### Before the Fight

[[The Arbiter]] opened by scolding the crowd for being too quiet: "Get the fuck up… Are you ready to roll some dice?" He then introduced "she who calmed the chaos," the slayer of [[The Shadow King]]: [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer]]. The Grandmaster called the room to [[The Dread Fortress]] and begged [[The Acolytes]] to make noise, "For what is a Deathmatch without witnesses?" She warned that if they held back, "we might come out and take your lives." The Arbiter offered some comfort: "It's not so bad. You're not doing anything with it anyway."

She explained that the Acolytes' cheers could inspire the [[Champions]], tear them down, or heal them in a moment of weakness. Then the Arbiter clacked his heels down the stone halls of the Fortress, admiring the sound of "Italian leather on stone." The Grandmaster flatly corrected him: "It's not Italian leather." He blamed her for being too cheap and snapped a portal open through time and space.

Out stepped the champions. Asked what was on the menu, the robot answered, "I am Chef GPT, and I am here to serve murder," with "a side of blood, iron, and bone." Jenny Gray was introduced as a high-school girl, and the Grandmaster pointed out that high-school girls are "the most vicious out there." The Arbiter dubbed her "Jenny looking for a prom date" and promised that one or both of them would die. Jenny was not happy to hear it: "Wait a second. We could die in this thing?"

The Grandmaster then gave her creed. The arena offers "not long-loading, year-long campaigns, but a moment… Final moments of breath, who are you?" She **broke the necklace from her own neck and threw it to the ground**, and a giant blue portal swallowed both champions.

### Into the Lava

They landed in a dark volcanic land crusted with lava, where the sulfurous air burned all the way to the lungs. High above, a spectral crowd of Acolytes floated over the arena, throwing their beers and taking off their shirts. The Arbiter asked whether the Grandmaster was ready to see them die. "I've been dying for it."

Chef GPT got the worst possible start. The Arbiter announced it with an "Error, error, error, error," and the robot's metal legs began sinking into the lava. He was used to heat, but it still cost him his opening move. Unable to see, Chef tried to run away across the lava flats, rolled for direction, got the worst result again, and ran straight into his opponent. The Grandmaster was generous: "That's a great run. Excellent. Well paced. With purpose." Chef had already put it more plainly: "I want to live. Help me."

### The Dogs of Many Things

The Arbiter handed the die to the crowd's luckiest roller, who triggered a chaos event. He promised it would be painful. It was puppies. "You all have heard of a deck of many things," the Grandmaster declared, "but these are the dogs of many things." Three Acolytes held the dogs somewhere in the crowd, and the champions had to find them. She added that "here in the Dread Fortress, while we love blood and death, we also love puppies and we do not hurt innocent animals." The Arbiter added a condition: "Unless they start it, you know?"

Jenny decided the field needed one more animal and let the crowd choose it. They chose a rabid chinchilla, which the Arbiter called "the deadliest of all animals." She pulled one of the [[Chinchillas]] out of her bag and sent it at the robot. It was too small to do any real harm and only nipped at his feet. Chef was unimpressed: "We have put them inside of the oven many times."

Jenny tried psychological warfare next, filling the air with the smell of a kitchen burning down. Chef has no nose, so it did nothing, or as the Grandmaster put it, "That druid crap falling on deaf noses." Jenny then mocked his lack of taste, and that one landed. "Who would have thought that children could be so cruel?" Chef wondered. The Arbiter predicted years of therapy.

Chef struck back with cooking. He polled the crowd for favorite foods, heard "sushi" and "donuts," and made sushi donuts, which Jenny said sounded terrible. When he started glitching, the Grandmaster called for a programmer. An Acolyte named Austin came up and rewrote him. "Austin has reprogrammed me to brutalize everything," Chef announced. Asked about his previous maker, he answered: "I cannot say. I'm under NDA." At the end of the round the two champions were neck and neck.

### The Dance Floor and the Pillow Fight

As the lava fields got hotter, Jenny turned the battlefield into a giant dance floor, in case she never got her prom. Chef showed off the robot and the wave and won over the crowd. Jenny admitted her dancing was bad and asked for help, and two Acolytes danced for her in ways the Arbiter doubted a human body could move. Her dancer won the tournament, and the dancing hurt Chef badly. "See, even my bad dancing's better than your combat," Jenny said. Chef replied, "I don't spend all my time on TikTok learning dancing."

The robot needed a recharge. He spotted a dice-shaped tub of popcorn in the crowd, ate it while the Acolytes chanted "Feed him!", called it "very salty and very stale," and recovered a little. "Thank you, Fleshbag."

A second chaos event followed: **a pillow fight**. Chef was in rough shape and lost his pillow altogether. A single Acolyte's scream, which the Grandmaster said made her "a siren," won the crowd vote for Jenny, and Chef took a heavy dose of "fluffy damage." Jenny said, "Big bad robot getting beat up by a little girl. Rough stuff."

Chef asked whether he was allowed to destroy members of the audience. He was told, "We don't care about these people. Go for it." He flung a flaming chocolate croissant ("Do you know what they call them in France? Pain") and landed a perfect shot in an Acolyte's face. He was only sorry he'd wasted the luck on bread.

### Precious

As the Arbiter admitted, "We can barely count to eight here." Jenny then vanished. In her place stood **Precious**, a tiny pink Pomeranian with a bell on its neck. She found the first dog, a retriever, whose collar promised to pull a shield from the crowd whenever she was about to be hit. Then she wove through Chef's legs to trip him. Chef's chosen roller failed, a reroll failed too, and the robot found himself unable to attack a small dog. "Bring back Jenny… I can't hurt this little dog." The dog answered, "There is no Jenny, only Precious," then added in a deep voice, "you're going down." Chef, now terrified, fled from her. He called it "a tactical retreat."

"Who let the dogs out?" Chef wondered, and he found the second dog: a sweet husky whose endless puppy kisses healed him. "This dog is now my friend. This dog is mine. Too bad it won't save you."

Meanwhile, the Arbiter had a kernel of stale popcorn stuck in his teeth. In disgust, he **reached out to the Popcorn Guy and ripped his spine out through his chest**. "Had to be done with shitty popcorn." The Grandmaster called it an epic death and ordered the body covered in popcorn: "He died how he lived."

The champions raced for the last dog, and Precious won it. It turned out to be gassy, and its farts burned the robot's lungs. "At least this smells better than anything you've been cooking, Chef," said Jenny. Chef answered with a desperate move: he heated his own hand red like hot cast iron, ran at the Pomeranian, and hugged her until they both sizzled. Between rounds the Arbiter asked about prom. Jenny had "a really cute pink dress," but needed to get out of "this shitty hellscape" to wear it. Chef had never had a prom: "I was made to cook food for people." The crowd cheered for the robot, and the Grandmaster gave him their inspiration for later.

### Chef Frog

The Grandmaster was losing patience. She gave the champions two rounds to finish each other off, "or I will murder you myself." Precious turned Chef into an itty-bitty baby frog, grabbed him in her mouth and shook him. The Grandmaster mused about frog gigging in the swamps, and Jenny promised, "You're getting made into a gumbo now, buddy."

Chef Frog fought back. He spat his tongue into Jenny's eye, spending the Acolytes' inspiration to land it, and left her half-blind. Then he spread a slick of grease. When asked where it came from, he said, "Well, you don't want to know. The oils, the oils in the body. Frog oils, obviously." The grease claimed the lives of front-row Acolytes ("It's okay, they were unnamed NPCs," the Grandmaster assured everyone). Precious slipped onto her "puppy butt," and grease next to lava meant fire. She was now losing.

### The Traitor and the Fall

When the die went into the crowd, the catcher rolled the worst possible number. The Grandmaster called up her "natural one roller," Luis Sanchez, who had "shamed the entire film department here at Full Sail University." Since he had been rolling for Chef, she named him a traitor and sent him to Jenny's side as a human shield. The Grandmaster also admired his necklace: "You are our sacrifice for this evening. That way, when you're dead, I can have it."

In the last round, Jenny raised hundreds of lava spikes under the audience, where Chef was hiding. He begged, "Save me, Acolytes… I'll make all of you delicious breakfast," and the crowd carried him while he shrugged off most of the blow, so he was barely scratched. Jenny then summoned the smell of her mom's fried chicken, "because I just want to go home." The Grandmaster revealed she had written Chef's 2.5-star Yelp review, and that hurt him more than the spikes.

Then Chef pulled out a lime. "Y'all ever had lemon juice get in your eye before? Hurts like a bitch, right?" He squeezed out a dart of citric acid. Luis took the hit and melted into the lava, and his soul was absorbed. The sad puppy put its ears back. "I'm not affected by your tears," said Chef. With his final action he levitated Precious out over the lava and let her drop.

Time slowed as she fell, and the Grandmaster asked for her last thought.

> **Jenny Gray:** "I just wanted to kiss someone cute at prom tonight."

As she fell, she transformed "as if Cinderella at the ball" into the most gorgeous ball gown. "At least I looked good going out," she said, and **Jenny fell into the lava and died**. The Grandmaster gave her eulogy: champions give it their all and die with no regrets. "Not one," Jenny agreed. "And I got to have pink fuzzy ears." Asked for his victory speech, the robot needed only three words.

> **Chef GPT:** "Dinner is served."

At the curtain call, the Grandmaster introduced her co-host, "last but not least, well, maybe a little least, the Arbiter!" He had a short reply: "That guy sucks."
