## What happens
### Opening — the illusion and the Dread Fortress's workplace
1. **0:00:04** A hype man (04) works up the crowd: "Louder! … Stand up! Scream!" He calls it "Welcome to D20 Dungeon!", which is probably a mishearing.
2. **0:00:24** The Arbiter welcomes the Acolytes to the Dread Fortress. Before that, he has to introduce someone "very special. **At least she would like me to say that contractually**… all the way from the Void. She who defeated the Shadow King. Who pierced the sky?" The answer: "The Grandmaster, Elusia Skypiercer!"
3. **0:01:01** The Grandmaster: "We come to you far, far away to bring you our show live here at GCX… **this change of venue changes nothing!** We are here for blood, for violence, and for… Chaos!" She promises the crowd that its cheers and jeers can inspire or hurt the champions: "in this arena, every voice matters."
4. **0:01:58 — The grand illusion.** She opens her arms. **Smoke unfurls behind her** ("I promise it's not a fart") in blues, greens, yellows and pinks. It turns the room into a hellish landscape: **deep red lava gurgling up from the ground and a large skull looming in the background, the entrance to the Dread Fortress itself.**
5. **0:02:56 — Rent.** The GM calls it "our home." The Arbiter objects: "It's like your home, though, right? **I just work here.**" The GM: "Yeah, **I do charge you rent.**"
6. **0:03:08 — Recruiting.** The GM: "we are always looking for more employees." The Arbiter lists the benefits:
   - Lots of unions: **the chinchilla union and the goblin union.** "Are you guys pro-union?"
   - Health benefits, including **a mental health chinchilla**.
   - PTO.
   - **"All the ping pong balls you can eat. They're delicious."**
   - **Unlimited snow cones.**
7. **0:03:44 — The Sunday rule.** The Arbiter: "It's not Sunday, though, we can't do it… **It's the Arbiters' union rules. We signed it in the contract. I don't work on any day but Sunday.**" The GM makes an exception for GCX: "Just because it's not Sunday doesn't mean we can't have a Deathmatch… **Chaos happens every day.**"
9. **0:04:59** Asked whether he brought champions: "I brought some friends over. I guess they can fight to the death." "Are they cool?" "**No, not really.**"

    - He rolls 8 against Navik's 19. **Navik deflects it with just his raised hand**, so the nunchuck goes "straight to the hand." The GM: "It just turns out hands are very intimidating to you."
26. **0:19:12** A second swing hits (21 vs 7): **"swishing that towel around, sweat flying everywhere," and the sweat seeps in as necrotic damage.** A line under Ragu's speaker ID: "Your sweat tastes like liquid death." It reads more like Navik's line; speaker uncertain.
27. **0:20:22** For his third action, Ragu has a crowd kid roll ("Hey, kid, you have a whole life ahead of you"). Navik's minion rolls an 8 on defense and fails him ("You failed me this time minion"), so Ragu lands another hit.
    - Navik over-acts the pain. Ragu: "you're dead, you don't feel anything… you only bones, you need meat."
28. **0:21:42** The Arbiter realizes they forgot to announce "**Round number one!**" "That's on us." The GM: "That's you."
29. **0:22:03** Ragu, distracted: "Oh, she's pretty… **When I'm done killing you, I'm gonna take her out to Orbeez.**" It's unclear whether "she" is the GM or someone in the crowd.
30. **0:22:30 — Navik's turn: lightning.** He attacks twice. The first hits (19 vs 15): "**I think we'll start with some lightning as I crackle in thunder.**" Ragu: "What a wake up call." Navik: "Are you awake yet, Minions?"
31. **0:24:08 — The first minion, "Redemption."** A crowd member lies on the ground and is raised: "**Rise, my thrall! Now attack him.**" They roll a dirty 20.
    - Ragu's defender is an Acolyte celebrating a birthday ("Happy birthday… Congratulations on not dying yet!"). They fail.
32. **0:25:28 — The deep fry.** The GM punishes the failed Acolyte: "**a claw comes out, grabbing this acolyte, picking them up, dropping them down into a deep [fryer]**… you sizzle till you die… Like a mozzarella stick." Navik: "**I told you they were my minions… They taste like chicken.**"

### Round 3 — the pep talk and the Box of Chaos

    - Chemicals intermingle, and "**something larger, boxier begins to emerge from your rectum. And you poop out our Box of Chaos.**"
    - Ragu: "**This is a really shitty box.**" Using it costs an action, and either champion can pick a minion to roll for it.
    - Ragu: "I'm just gonna be out here with this open butt?… That's not the breeze I wanted." The crowd chants "Clench!"
51. **0:52:17 — Crying heals.** "**I'm gonna cry and heal.**" The GM: his tears mix with the shit below, "releasing a strangely wonderful aroma… **there's nothing better than tears and shit.**" He regains one Fighting Spirit.
52. **0:52:47 — Opening the box.**
    - Ragu: "It's my box! **This is my boo-boo box.**"
    - The Arbiter: "**The box lid creaks open and black shadowy fingers and yellow eyes peek their way out. What do you want?**" It's Drew voicing the box.

    - He picks "friends," not minions, to roll. His chosen one has red shoes ("which means blood").
53. **0:54:33 — The blue pill.**
    - The friend rolls a **natural 1**, so Ragu rolls again himself and gets an 8.
    - "**From the hand of the blackened soul floats a small blue pill. This should keep you going all night long.**" Ragu: "I'm sorry, who has those problems?"

    - The GM loses track of actions. The Arbiter: "Don't worry, that's why I'm here." The GM: "**I'm just a pretty face.**" The Arbiter: "She doesn't do anything… This guy knows all about your boss taking credit for your shit."
55. **0:57:04 — Off the ropes.** Navik: "Finally, a fight!" Ragu calls on his friends and hits (19 against Navik's 18 after the GM fixes the math). He comes "**off the ropes**" with a punch, restaged front and center "into the light." Navik: "**I liked it better when you missed every hit.**"
