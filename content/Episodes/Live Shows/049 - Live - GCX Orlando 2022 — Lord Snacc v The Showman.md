When the [[The Arbiter|Arbiter]] arrived at GCX Orlando without any [[Champions|champions]], the Grandmaster pulled two volunteers out of the crowd. The barbarian **[[Lord Snacc]]** faced the bowler-hatted bard **[[The Showman]]** in a quiet autumn forest that did not stay quiet for long. Before it was over, the whole room had been drafted into the fight, the Box of Chaos had shared its dreams, and the Showman had died twice.

## The Story

### The Forgotten Champions

The night opened with the Arbiter announcing "the Grandmaster herself, Elusia Skypiercer!" [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|The Grandmaster]] welcomed the [[The Acolytes|Acolytes]] with a joke that she had "taken our Dread Fortress from further North Orlando and brought it here," and promised them blood, glory and death. Then she asked the Arbiter whether he had brought the two champions.

"Shit. Uh, I got the toothbrush." He hadn't packed deodorant either ("That explains a lot"). Checked-bag fees were "really fucking ridiculous," he explained, so only one bag had made the trip. When the Grandmaster asked whether he'd planned to check the champions in his bag, he asked back, "Where else do you put champions?" She sent him to sit down, drink a glass of water and think about what he'd done.

With no other option, she asked for Acolytes willing to sacrifice their lives. Her first pick was "you with the broken horn," who happened to be holding a microphone (the Arbiter: "What a random coincidence"). He introduced himself as **Lord Snacc**, "a large man, a barbarian from a faraway land. Some would even say in space," a worshipper of "a spherical god." Told to wrap it up, he began again from the beginning: "Well, I was born a small child." For the second champion the Grandmaster called "Pikachu, get up here," and up came a masked stranger. He was the Showman ("Yes, legally"), and he worshipped no gods, liked long walks on the beach, and held a Disney annual pass. He only learned the fight was to the death after he'd agreed to it. Lord Snacc did not mind: "I do not fear death!"

### Into the Autumn Woods

The Arbiter raised his hands and slammed them down, and a portal tore open in time and space. A cool breeze blew through it, carrying autumn leaves. The Grandmaster described the arena: a deep forest where the cold air iced the lungs and crimson leaves crunched underfoot. There was an owl, a fox and a squirrel (the Arbiter told the squirrel to go fuck itself), and a path led to a small clearing "just large enough for a good old battle," lit by the "setting sun of someone's life tonight." She told the champions to be adaptive and vicious, and "whether you live or die, tell your epic story so that you may be remembered." Then her wings opened and she flew up to the Acolytes.

The Showman worked the crowd at once ("Let's kill this viking") and found, to his surprise, that "there are knives everywhere."

### Bees, Leaves and Cheese

Lord Snacc moved first. He trusted the Acolytes more than himself, so he let one take his opening shot, and it missed. A louder Acolyte fared better. Snacc's "trusty blaster," the Fourth Horseman, turned out to be "a large multi-barreled blaster" rather than four horses. The Showman sniffed that "four horses would hurt more."

Snacc sprinted into close range. The Grandmaster narrated the collision as "two bodies intertwining in the forest… Tip to tip," and Snacc insisted it was not intimate, then gave his opponent a wink. The Showman introduced his knives, all named after the cast of *Friends*: Chandler, Rachel, Joey, Phoebe ("she's the weird one"), Ross ("I don't like Ross that much") and Monica, his favorite, who "gets around."

Then he cast a spell of fear. Snacc protested that nothing frightened him, and that he had "400 brawn," but the spell took hold anyway. What terrified him was the Showman's bowler hat, "so round and weird-shaped and out of fashion." When the Grandmaster asked the crowd for Snacc's other fears, they shouted "Bees? Leaves? Cheese!" and soon the whole room was chanting "Cheese, cheese, cheese" while Snacc begged them to stop. The Showman crept closer making buzzing noises and briefly wandered off the stage to hang out with Turtleman, an Acolyte he had befriended. [[Doug the Pug]], the Fortress mascot, had stayed home, so an Acolyte rolled for chaos in his place. The Arbiter hoped they weren't "a dick like Doug."

Snacc brought out his glaive and danced to the music for momentum ("Every fighting game ever!"), but the fear spoiled his aim. His next swing only "gently tickle[d] The Showman." Then the barbarian's anger rose, and he roared, "We are going to fight forever!" The Arbiter approved: "That's the heart of a champion!"

The Showman answered by charming Turtleman into the fight, sealing the deal with a pin ("that's all I've got"). Turtleman threw Rachel at Snacc and hit, and then kept her. "No one likes Rachel," said the Showman. "She has nice hair, though," said the Grandmaster. Snacc's tough hide soaked up half the damage, which worried the Showman: "That's a problem, I only came with knives."

### Tag, You're Blistered

"Chaos!" The Arbiter ordered both champions down to the front of the room for cardio: a game of tag, in which the fifth person touched would get hurt. Snacc was "it." He tagged the Showman, the Showman tagged Turtleman, and the touch went around the room until the fifth one landed back on Lord Snacc. The spot on his arm went red, blistered and poured pus. The Showman credited "the power of friendship." Asked who his friends were, he answered: "Turtlemen."

### Fro-Yo and the Shimmy-Off

The Arbiter turned to flirting: "Looking like a snack. What are you doing later?… If you're not dead?" Snacc knew "a great ramen place," the Arbiter suggested fro-yo, and the Grandmaster cut them off with a promise: "If you win, I will buy you Froyo myself… Only one person gets Froyo tonight."

Snacc was still afraid, so he fired from a distance. The Grandmaster asked if that was a shotgun, and the Arbiter explained: "He's got a glaive and a shotgun… It's a classic loadout." His next swing missed ("Every time! Every time!"), and when the crowd started chanting "Cheese?" again, the sound echoing through the forest scared him ten steps backward. The Showman threw a knife at him as he retreated. It barely scratched him ("Death by a thousand cuts"), and the Showman, running low on knives, auctioned Ross off to the crowd from $10 ("He's a paleontologist").

The Grandmaster scolded Snacc for thinking about dessert, and the Arbiter explained to the room that "she's mad because I was putting in a new wing in the Dread Fortress and I didn't get the permits done." She had the Acolytes stand for a blessing, "Happiness and fro-yo!", which steadied Snacc's next move. The Showman tried to drive an Acolyte mad with a spell ("We never said I had to be the one to make them"), but the man shrugged it off. The Grandmaster's verdict: "Crown of Madness was a failure, because I'm the only one that can wear a crown around here."

Distancing himself from all the fro-yo talk, the Showman performed a shimmy and asked whether the Arbiter was satisfied. The Arbiter counter-shimmied, matched him exactly and called for a close-up. The Grandmaster suggested a touring duo, then ruled: "No dancing."

### Gerald Opens Up

For a little something extra, the Grandmaster summoned [[The Box of Chaos]]. It fell from the sky at Lord Snacc's feet and flipped open in "a column of pink and yellow fire, no heat is produced, because it's magic." A shadowy figure crawled out with his fingers hooked over the side. His name was Gerald. "It was my father's." Snacc said his father must have been a lovely man. "And it was his mother's." Gerald then asked what the fuck they wanted. The rule was simple: sacrifice something, and Gerald might grant what you desire. There was no fro-yo in the box.

Snacc turned around, rummaged mischievously near his pants and announced, "My other horn has emerged!" He'd "never leave home without it." Gerald was unsure what to do with it and refused to clean it ("then all the flavor will be off of it"), but he sent back a small potion. The crowd chanted "Chug, chug, chug!" as Snacc's wounds closed. With some help from the Arbiter, who was tired of watching him fail, Snacc finally broke the fear. "You are no longer fearful, cheese is now your friend." "I love cheese!"

His luck didn't last. He tossed his die into the crowd and it came back a natural 1. "The acolytes giveth and the acolytes taketh away." He still landed a sprinting slash, and the Showman, caught bluffing about how hard he was to hit ("No, you're full of shit," said the Arbiter), was stabbed repeatedly. He was "down to two livers now," thanks to "a hobby outside of this."

Then the Showman did what no one ever had: he asked Gerald what *he* wanted. "No one's ever asked me that before. I don't know. I guess I'd like to paint and start a family… Maybe someday have a couple of shadowy kids, make some shady deals." He'd also take a knife. He was "kind of a Phoebe guy," so the Showman sacrificed Phoebe and got a little shield to wear around his neck. He struck a patriotic pose and turned around. "That's America's ass."

The remaining cast of *Friends* came to life. Snacc ducked Monica and Chandler, but Joey struck true. "How you doing?" said Snacc. "And that's how you get a spinoff!" said the Showman. The hosts' arithmetic collapsed ("25 divided by 2 is 25"), and the Arbiter noted they were "slowly descending into madness."

### The Crowd Turns

Snacc rallied ten Acolytes to "clap your asses off," and the Grandmaster turned the applause into thunder that slammed into the Showman: "That clapping is not for you. It hurts." The Showman replied that "it's not wise to put an audience against a bard, because two can play this game." He turned the whole crowd against Snacc, everyone booed, and five Acolytes attacked him with whatever weapons they could imagine: Turtleman, two hat people and the man who'd bought Ross. The first struck perfectly. "I trusted you," said Snacc. The Arbiter described people "taking their tops off, spilling beer everywhere… Don't do that, just pretend."

"I don't want to die," Snacc admitted. "I just want somebody to die," said the Arbiter. "It doesn't have to be you." The Grandmaster pointed out that a dead winner would save her about five bucks on fro-yo.

### The Storm

With Acolytes once more standing in for Doug, chaos struck again. The breeze rose to a gale, the orange sun vanished behind "dark and angry, blue and grumbling" clouds, and lightning fell, played at the table by ping-pong balls thrown at the champions. Neither dodged; the Showman fumbled completely. "This is Florida!" cried Snacc. The bolt left the Showman barely standing, and the crowd chanted "Finish him!"

### The Hammer and the Fake Death

After a pause to send a sign-breaker to the corner (and then promise him fro-yo, because "we can't stay mad at you"), Lord Snacc made his speech: "Here we are. Gathered around friends, family, grandmasters, arbiters, strange men in bowler hats. I happen to have in my pocket here a hero point." The crowd echoed "My true might!" and he brought "the flaming hammer of inferno and doom" down on his rival, burying him in the forest floor. "Oh, it's so moist! Oh, there's critters in here!" The Grandmaster told him to just die already, declared Snacc the winner, and the Arbiter started dragging the body away.

"But wait! He's getting back up!… Shh! He's just sleeping!" The Showman had faked his own death. He rose from his earthly grave holding a sword he'd found in the dirt. "Did you just steal my sword?" asked the Grandmaster. "I am very difficult to kill. I've had practice." He stabbed Snacc "like a shish kebab," and the two faced off in the dark under the rumbling clouds.

### The End of the Showman

There was no sign for round seven ("Honestly, I thought they'd be dead by now"). The Grandmaster wanted "bones broken." Snacc swung recklessly with all his might, and two glaive strikes did it. The first took "both livers" ("No one give that man a drink later"). The second left the Showman in "a fountain of red," and the Grandmaster took her sword back. On his knees, he crawled toward Snacc for his last words: "I'll be back. I will always be back. And you'll find me behind the counter at the fro-yo stand. And that fro-yo's not gonna taste right. And just know it's me."

"Goodnight, sweet prince," said Snacc. "Not a prince." The Arbiter dragged the body off, checked he was really dead this time, and took the hat, pants and jacket with him. "One death has finally happened in our fortress," the Grandmaster declared, and named Lord Snacc the winner. His victory speech: "Victory is all I ask." ("Beautiful, succinct, and 100% original.") She told the Acolytes to burn this battle in their hearts and chase the perfect roll at every moment. Then she told them to go away, or buy the hosts a drink.
