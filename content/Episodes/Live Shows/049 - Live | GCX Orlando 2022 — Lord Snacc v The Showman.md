## What happens
### Opening — the Arbiter forgot the champions
1. **0:00:00** A hype opener (speaker uncertain): "Welcome to D20 Deathmatch!… Are you ready to roll some fucking dice?"
2. **0:00:30** The Arbiter introduces "all the way from the Dread Fortress, the Grandmaster herself, Elusia Skypiercer!"
3. **0:00:43** The Grandmaster welcomes the Acolytes to GCX: "**We have taken our Dread Fortress from further North Orlando and brought it here** for you all to witness blood and glory and death!"
4. **0:01:07 — No champions.** She asks whether the Arbiter brought two champions. "**Shit. Uh, I got the toothbrush.**" He didn't bring deodorant either ("That explains a lot"). His excuse is that they could only bring the checked bag, since checked-bag fees are "really fucking ridiculous." GM: "What, were you gonna check the champions in your bag?" Arbiter: "**Where else do you put champions?**" She sends him to sit down, drink a glass of water and think about what he's done.
5. **0:01:31 — Volunteers.** The GM asks for Acolytes willing to sacrifice their lives. She picks "**you with the broken horn**." The Arbiter: "What a random coincidence that this stranger has a microphone."
6. **0:02:05 — Lord Snacc.** "**My name is Lord Snacc.**" He describes himself as "a large man, a barbarian from a faraway land. **Some would even say in space.** I worship **a spherical god** who will grant me the strength to overcome any challenge." The Arbiter tells him to wrap it up, so Snacc starts over: "Well, I was born a small child." Someone, probably the Arbiter (speaker uncertain), answers: "Get the fuck off the stage."
7. **0:02:47 — The Showman.** The GM needs one more and refuses people volunteering others. "You. I choose you. **Pikachu, get up here.**" The Arbiter greets the "masked stranger." He introduces himself as "**the Showman**. Yes, **legally**." He worships no gods, likes long walks on the beach, and has **a Disney annual pass**.
8. **0:03:42** The Arbiter: "One of you will die today! Hopefully." The Showman: "Wait, this is to the death?" Arbiter and GM: "We'll talk about that later… whoever happens to still be alive." **Lord Snacc: "I do not fear death!"**

### The arena — the autumn forest
9. **0:04:14** **The Arbiter raises his hands above his head and slams them down. A portal rips open in time and space, and a cool breeze blows through carrying autumn leaves.**
10. **0:04:29** The GM describes the arena. It is a deep forest dense with trees, where the cool autumn air "ice[s] over your lungs" and crimson leaves crunch underfoot. There are little animals: an owl (Snacc: "Hoo-hoo!"), a fox, and a squirrel (Arbiter: "Go fuck yourself"). The path leads to **a small clearing "just large enough for a good old battle,"** with a couple of rocks, trees and dead leaves about to fall, "perfect for this **setting sun of someone's life tonight**." She charges them: "The arena should change the way you fight. Be adaptive, be vicious, and most of all, whether you live or die, **tell your epic story so that you may be remembered.**" **Her wings open wide and she flies up to the Acolytes.**
11. **0:06:04** She tells the Acolytes that their cheers, taunts and applause will aid or hurt the champions. Lord Snacc: "Will you be my chosen champions?" The Showman: "You all seem like a very nice crowd… **Let's kill this viking.**" He adds: "Oh, no. **There are knives everywhere.** I don't know how that happened." GM: "Accidents happen."
12. **0:07:06** The Arbiter flies up into the sky and calls initiative. **Lord Snacc rolls 18 and the Showman 11.** They start in the clearing 30 ft apart.

### Round 1
14. **0:09:06** Snacc trusts the Acolytes more than himself and asks one to make his attack roll. The Arbiter picks a man for his nice shirt ("Good job, Tesla Man!" [?]). He rolls a 4, and the attack misses. Snacc: "**You shall burn!**"
15. **0:10:06** Snacc asks for "a more reliable acolyte," and the Arbiter picks the loudest one ("Look at the lungs on that guy"). The roll hits (22). The Showman tells Snacc: "**Turtleman is so much cooler than you.**" Turtleman is an Acolyte in the crowd.
16. **0:10:41** The Showman asks what he was hit with. "The Fourth Horseman, of course." "Like four horses." "No, no, **it's a large multi-barreled blaster**." The Showman: "Oh, you name yours too." The blast does 12. Snacc: "It hurts, doesn't it?" The Showman: "**Oh, four horses would hurt more.**"
17. **0:11:35 — Closing the gap.** Snacc runs at the Showman "at full sprint of course, is there any other way?" The GM narrates them colliding: "**Two bodies intertwining in the forest. When two become one**… This is an intimate, intimate show. **Tip to tip.**" Snacc: "It is not intimate!" The Showman: "Oh no, it's going to get pretty intimate, I promise." **Snacc gives him a wink as a bonus action.**
18. **0:12:28 — The knives.** The Showman introduces his weapons: "**I have Chandler and Rachel. And Joey and Phoebe, she's the weird one. And then Ross, I don't like Ross that much**… Monica! Oh, **Monica's my favorite. She gets around.** We're not going to use Monica."
19. **0:12:48 — Fear.** The Showman casts **fear**. Snacc: "But nothing frightens me!" Showman: "Right, but you don't look like you have much wisdom." The Arbiter: "The first step is finding your brain, the second step is using it." Snacc: "**But I have 400 brawn.**" He rolls a 9 against DC 16 and fails. He argues that nine is more than sixteen "depend[ing] where you're from… Or if we're playing golf."
20. **0:14:24 — The bowler hat.** "You said you don't feel fear, Lord Snacc." **What terrifies Snacc is the Showman's bowler hat**: "It's a bowler hat, I've not, I've not seen-" "**Look at it!** It's so round and weird-shaped and out of fashion." His head doesn't fit it very well.
21. **0:15:08 — Bees, leaves and cheese.** The GM asks what Lord Snacc's worst fears are. The Acolytes shout "**Bees? Leaves? Cheese!**" The Arbiter: "You're lactose intolerant." Snacc begs, "Stop saying them, please!" **The whole room chants "Cheese, cheese, cheese."**
22. **0:16:00** The Showman moves closer **making buzzing noises** ("It's so scary"). The GM wonders whether they should just share a mic, since "they didn't give us any restrictions." **The Showman briefly runs off to "hang out with Turtleman."** The Arbiter: "If you fight better off the stage, you fight off the stage."
23. **0:17:01 — Doug the Pug.** The Arbiter's chaos roll is "a solid six." The GM: "I didn't happen to bring **Doug the Pug, our wonderful D20 Dread Fortress mascot**." An Acolyte ("Arthur" [?]) rolls for Doug and gets a 9. Arbiter: "Hopefully you're not a dick like Doug." HP: **Showman 73, Lord Snacc 105.**

### Round 2
24. **0:18:28 — The glaive and the dance.** Snacc: "I've also brought with me **a glaive**. It's very long and sharp." The Arbiter asks the "**music goblin**" to turn the music up. Snacc does a fighting-game dance ("Every fighting game ever!"), and the Arbiter shouts "Hadouken." The GM asks for a performance check. He rolls 19 and gains momentum: an extra d4 if he hits. Because he is still frightened, he rolls with disadvantage and misses.
25. **0:19:46** The Showman: "I was ready for this moment, Lord Snacc. It's terrifying." Snacc: "**Stop it! Don't do weird things with your hand.**"
26. **0:20:06** The Arbiter quietly bets on the Showman: "I like **the bandage thing** you got going on." Showman: "Oh, thank you, it's very uncomfortable… **I can't see out of my left eye.**"
27. **0:20:15** Snacc's second glaive swing hits, but only for 5. The GM denies him the d4 ("I didn't give it him for both, because I don't care"). "**You gently tickle The Showman. Do you have any sensitive spots?**" Showman: "I really wish I took Vicious Mockery right now."
28. **0:21:26 — Rage.** Snacc: "I feel like I'm getting angry." The GM praises his "I language… that's what my therapist told me." Snacc: "**I feel… BREEDY!**" [greedy?] The Arbiter: "It's not an emotion, but that's fine." The Showman realizes, "Oh wait, he's a barbarian." Snacc: "**We are going to fight forever!**" The Arbiter: "That's the heart of a champion!"
29. **0:22:08 — Turtleman tagged in.** **The Showman casts suggestion on an Acolyte, Turtleman** (the Arbiter also calls him "Tensile Man, Cody" [?]). The DC is 16 and Turtleman rolls exactly 16. The GM: "The tournament is redeemed." The Showman then **bribes him with a pin** ("At least one, because that's all I've got"). GM: "Bribery is allowed." **Turtleman agrees to fight.**
30. **0:23:34** Turtleman **throws a dagger** (the knife Rachel) at Snacc. The Showman gives him **Bardic Inspiration (d10)** and beatboxes while he rolls ("I can't floss"). It hits (24), and Snacc: "I'm afraid I'm feeling quite hurt at the moment." **Turtleman keeps Rachel.** Showman: "**No one likes Rachel.**" GM: "She has nice hair, though."
31. **0:24:54** The dagger does 11, but **Lord Snacc is resistant to piercing**, so it is halved and rounded up to 6 ("we love a little extra pain"). Snacc: "And all damage, except apparently that of the brain." Showman: "That's a problem, I only came with knives."
32. **0:25:28** "Do I have to say *the* every time?" "No, we're friends now… **just Showman**. We're on a second name basis." Snacc fails his fear save again (8). The Arbiter: "Yeah, it's definitely wet down here."

### Chaos event — Tag (0:26:18)
33. The Arbiter rolls 17. "**Chaos!**" Both champions have to walk down the stairs to the front of the room ("Surprise, we're doing cardio today"). They roll d6s, and **Snacc gets 6 to the Showman's 4. Snacc is "it."** The rule: **after five touches, the fifth person touched takes damage.**
34. **0:27:32** Snacc tags the Showman, who tags **Turtleman** ("He's my homie"). The tag passes around the room, and **the fifth touch lands on Lord Snacc.**
35. **0:28:17** "**As that touch hits you, that spot on your arm begins to grow red… it begins to blister and get red and pus pours from it, burning your arm!**" The base damage is 6. The Arbiter wants it tripled, so the GM adds dice until it reaches **19**. Arbiter: "See, he's more upset about it now, so I think it worked correctly." Showman: "**I had the power of friendship on my side.**" Snacc: "Who are your friends?" "Turtlemen."
36. **0:29:10** HP: **Showman 68, Lord Snacc 80.**

### Round 3 — Fro-yo
37. **0:29:35** The Arbiter flirts with Lord Snacc: "**Looking like a snack.** What are you doing later?… If you're not dead?" Snacc: "**I know a great ramen place.**" The Arbiter suggests fro-yo instead, and the Showman says he likes fro-yo too. The GM cuts in: "**If you win, I will buy you Froyo myself**… **Only one person gets Froyo tonight.**"
38. **0:30:27** Still afraid, Snacc decides to attack from a distance. He fails his fear save (13), and his first attack misses: "This glass is not working!" The second hits for 11 with rage. The GM asks, "Is that a shotgun?" The Arbiter: "**He's got a glaive and a shotgun. What's so confusing about this?… It's a classic loadout.**" The players bicker over reading the die ("Two circles is the eight. Two lines is the 11"; "I have higher wisdom").
39. **0:32:45 — Frenzy.** Snacc's frenzy attack misses. "**Every time! Every time!**" The Acolytes shake their heads, then start chanting "Cheese?" again. The Showman: "I was going to cast enemies abound, but I guess I don't have to now."
40. **0:33:35** The GM rules that **the cheese chant echoing through the forest frightens Snacc so much he takes ten steps back.** This provokes an opportunity attack. **The Showman throws a knife** and hits, but it does only 3 after resistance. Arbiter: "**Death by a thousand cuts.** It'll take at least a thousand." GM: "Did you go to **Sam's Club** before this?" Showman: "I'm running out of knives. I gave one to Turtle Man."
41. **0:34:54 — Ross auctioned.** Snacc suggests he keep giving his weapons away. **The Showman gives away Ross**, starting the bidding at $10. An Acolyte bids 12. "**He's a paleontologist.**"
42. **0:35:40** Snacc: "I still want the fro-yo, but I'm willing to wait!" GM: "Forget the fro-yo!… you should be focusing on the fight, not on the dessert!" The Arbiter explains to the crowd: "**She's mad because I was putting in a new wing in the Dread Fortress and I didn't get the permits done.**"
43. **0:36:02 — Blessing.** The GM has the Acolytes stand and say a blessing: "**Happiness and fro-yo!**" Arbiter: "That's what life's about." **Snacc gets a d6 inspiration on his next roll.** Snacc: "I love you, Galleon!" [?]
44. **0:36:39 — Crown of Madness.** The Showman asks the music goblin to pump up the music and **casts Crown of Madness on an Acolyte** ("That guy, get up here!"). The GM: "Run faster. Just don't fall. I don't know if we have insurance." Showman: "**Death by a thousand cuts. We never said I had to be the one to make them.**" He also jokes that the man looks like "the actor from Terminator 2." The man stumbles on "what's nine minus four," and the Arbiter calls a −1 Wisdom. **He rolls 16 and saves.** Showman: "I'm going to cast Lord [Crown] of Madness on your friends next."
45. **0:38:16** The Arbiter turns on his own fro-yo bit: "now you're kind of getting a little pushy about it… **it wasn't endearing, now it's just creepy.**"
46. **0:38:31** GM: "**Crown of Madness was a failure, because I'm the only one that can wear a crown around here.**" Showman: "It looks quite good on you." "Correct."
47. **0:38:51 — The shimmy-off.** The Showman takes five steps back "because… he is talking about Froyo way too much, and I don't want to be associated with that." He **shimmies** and rolls a 16 for performance. "**Does that satisfy you, Arbiter, my shimmy?**" GM: "I think you could win like a county fair." **The Arbiter counter-shimmies** and calls for a camera close-up. He also rolls 16: **a tie.** GM: "You guys could probably do, like, a duo sometime. Go around, travel the country." Showman: "Maybe we should just change this to a dance-off." GM: "No dancing."

### The Box of Chaos and Gerald (0:41:19)
48. The chaos roll is 16, and an Acolyte re-roll gives 10. The GM adds "a little something extra": "**I'd like to summon the Box of Chaos.**" The crowd shouts "Chaos!" A physical box is brought out ("It's an actual box"). **It lands at Lord Snacc's feet.** The GM: "the Box of Chaos is a free action to interact with, **owned by Gerald**."
49. **0:41:57** The Arbiter narrates: "As that box falls down from the sky, the lid flips open, **a column of pink and yellow fire, no heat is produced, because it's magic.** Out of the lid, **a shadowy figure crawls out, his fingers hanging over the side.**" Gerald: "What's up?"
50. **0:42:19** Snacc: "Hello, small, small thing." Gerald: "I have a name. **It's Gerald.**" Snacc: "Gerald's a lovely name." Gerald: "**It was my father's.**" "Your father was a lovely man." Gerald: "**And it was his mother's.**" Snacc: "Well, that's just odd." Gerald (inferred speaker): "**What the fuck do you want?**"
51. **0:42:37** The GM explains the rule: **to get anything from the box you must sacrifice something of your choice, and Gerald might grant you something you desire.** The Arbiter confirms there is **no fro-yo in the box**. Snacc: "Okay, that's a bit disappointing."

### Round 4
52. **0:43:35 — The other horn.** Snacc: "Tiny box, I have a sacrifice for you." He turns around and works "mischievously near his pants." "**My other horn has emerged!**" "From where?" "**I'd never leave home without it!**" He drops it in the box. Gerald: "**What the fuck am I supposed to do with this?**" Snacc: "It's very precious to me because of reasons." Gerald: "What do I, like, sit on it or something?" GM: "Clean it first." Gerald: "**But then all the flavor will be off of it.**" Showman: "Oh, Gerald, no."
53. **0:44:55** The box roll is a 1. "Fire shoots to the sky and **a small bottle of potion floats its way over to you.**" The crowd chants "**Chug, chug, chug!**" "You start to feel tingly inside… Your wounds close up a little bit." **Lord Snacc regains 7 HP.** Showman: "Seems excessive, but okay."
54. **0:46:24 — Cheese is his friend.** The Arbiter gives Snacc advantage on his fear save "because I'm just tired of watching you roll this shit." Adding the d6 from the fro-yo blessing, **he finally breaks the fear.** GM: "**You are no longer fearful, cheese is now your friend.**" Snacc: "**I love cheese!**" The Arbiter: "This guy's metal as fuck over here."
55. **0:47:17 — The Acolytes take away.** "My rolls have been absolute shite all day." Snacc tosses his d20 into the crowd and wants "four good hits before it hits the ground." It comes up a **nat 1**. Showman: "**I love this crowd!**" Snacc: "I'm number one, right? That's what that means." GM: "**The acolytes giveth and the acolytes taketh away.**" He gets disadvantage on his next attack.
56. **0:48:11** He forgets the disadvantage ("Very little wisdom") but still hits on 19. "**I really don't like being stabbed this much.**" He **breaks into a full sprint** and does 10 slashing with rage. The Showman tries to claim slashing immunity, then gives up: "No… I'm wearing **bandages**." Arbiter: "You think you would be, you got a lot of knives."
57. **0:49:30** Snacc's frenzy attack ties the Showman's AC, and the GM calls a glancing blow. **The Showman then admits his AC is actually 15.** The GM calls for a deception roll; his 17 loses to the Arbiter's 21 ("No, you're full of shit"). He takes the full 11, "**getting stabbed multiple times, blood oozing from your body.**"
58. **0:50:35** HP: **Showman 36, Snacc 84.** Showman: "I needed those organs." GM: "That's fine, organs are optional." Showman: "**I'm down to two livers now.**" The GM asks how many livers he started with. "**I just have a hobby outside of this.**" "That's not probably legal." Snacc: "Allegedly."
59. **0:51:36 — What Gerald wants.** The Showman goes to the box: "Gerald, you're not asking the right question. **I want to know what you want.**" Gerald: "**No one's ever asked me that before. I don't know. I guess I'd like to paint and start a family.** Maybe someday have **a couple of shadowy kids, make some shady deals**… I'd take a knife, though." The Showman would give him "the one that had the pregnancy" (Rachel), but she's gone. Gerald: "**I'm kind of a Phoebe guy**." "She's kind of crazy, and I like that." **The Showman sacrifices Phoebe.**
60. **0:52:42** He rolls a 3. **The box grants a shield that he wears around his neck: +1 AC.** "This would have been really helpful, Ellie [?]… when I said 16 earlier." GM: "You were just psychic, apparently." Showman: "**I feel very patriotic right now.**" He strikes a pose and turns around. GM: "**That's America's ass.**" Snacc: "You beat me to it by one second."
61. **0:53:45 — Animate objects.** He moved 15 ft to reach the box, so he is now in front of Snacc. "**I take out the entire cast of friends that I have remaining. Monica, Chandler, Joey, who is my favorite. And I cast animate objects. We're getting Disney up in here, Lord Snacc.**" The GM has the crowd do a countdown and gives everyone **a d4 blessing** ("Remember that I am so generous"). The Arbiter and GM joke about writing permission slips and forging doctor's notes for the Acolytes' bosses, "for legal purposes, we will not do that, but we would do it."
62. **0:54:40** "I really am down to three. I did not think this through." He throws all three animated knives: **9 and 15, then a natural 20. "Joey's coming for you, Lord Snacc!"** Snacc: "**How you doing?**" He narrates trying to get a groove going and **dodging the first two, "Monica! Chandler!", "and then he just can't get there"**: Joey hits him. Showman: "**And that's how you get a spinoff!**"
63. **0:55:51 — The math.** The crit damage turns into a muddle (10, 15, 18, 23). The GM declares "**I say you do 25 more damage!**" Arbiter: "We're just slowly descending into madness here." Snacc: "I don't think you have the right to make fun of my intelligence anymore." The GM forgot to halve it for resistance, and they rule "**25 divided by 2 is 25**" ("It's a very strong knife"). The Arbiter's chaos roll is a "kind of cocked" 19. HP: **Showman 36, Snacc 59.**

### Round 5 — Applause and enemies abound
64. **0:58:13** "We're in the latter half of the Deathmatch here. The Acolytes have watched the blood flow." The Arbiter calls for a camera shot of both champions and a round of applause.
65. **0:59:08 — Thunderous applause.** The GM tells Snacc to **get 10 Acolytes up to clap with him for advantage.** He counts down and rallies the back of the room: "**I'm counting on you to clap your asses off!**" The Showman tries to sneak in a reaction spell during the countdown. Arbiter: "That sounds like thunder damage to me!" GM: "**You will get advantage and your damage will be thunder damage.**" Arbiter: "That's what she said."
66. **1:00:08** Snacc is losing his furs. GM: "Please stay clothed. That's a look. It's not a good one, but it's a look." Showman: "It's really **2005 rawr** of you." The hit lands (22) for 7 thunder ("**four's a good number… It's her favorite number**"). "The waves of sound coming towards you, hitting one after another. **That clapping is not for you. It hurts.**"
67. **1:01:46 — Enemies Abound.** Showman: "**Lord Snacc, it's not wise to put an audience against a bard, because two can play this game.**" He casts **Enemies Abound**: "Everyone cheer this man! **Boo him!**" The crowd boos Snacc. "It literally makes him believe everyone is his enemy… A lot of these spells don't have numbers to them, unfortunately."
68. **1:02:32** The GM rules that **five Acolytes attack Snacc**, each with any weapon they imagine: +5 to hit, 2d4 damage rolled by the bard. The five are **Turtleman, two "hat people," and the man who has Ross**. Showman: "He has Rachel and he has Ross, so this is perfect."
69. **1:03:31** The first roll is a **nat 20 (critical)**. Snacc: "I trusted you." Two rolls of 13 miss. The GM forces a re-roll on one ("That is not good enough!"), which hits on 19. The last misses on 12. The GM slips: "**Shaq, I mean Snacc.**" Showman: "Strike him out, Captain." The Arbiter narrates: "**The crowd goes wild, people taking their tops off, spilling beer everywhere, all over the arena.** Don't do that, just pretend." The total is 19 damage, "a quiz… You've all passed."
70. **1:05:40** HP: **Showman 29, Snacc 40.** Snacc: "I don't want to die." Arbiter: "**I just want somebody to die. It doesn't have to be you.**" GM: "If you die, then I don't have to buy you Froyo." Arbiter: "**We are running a little tight at the Dread Fortress. We've got some remodels going on.**" GM: "I'm going to save like five bucks."
71. **1:06:20** The Arbiter's forgotten chaos roll is a 2. The GM calls for an Acolyte "in lieu of Doug the Pug." A man with multiple backpacks rolls a 4, then a 5 with his d4 blessing. Another man ("Don't talk about it, be about it") gets a 5. A third, "He was all after it earlier," rolls a **12**. Together that is enough.

### Chaos event — The storm (1:08:27)
72. "Champions, once again, stand. **As you stand in this forest, that cool breeze begins to pick up. The wind blows hard… that beautiful orange yellow begins to disappear behind a large swath of clouds, dark and angry, blue and grumbling.** Lightning strikes from the sky." DC 16 Dex save.
73. **1:09:05** **The Showman rolls a natural 1.** Lord Snacc: "**This is Florida!**" He rolls 15 and fails too. GM: "You two are awful!" Lightning is represented by **ping-pong balls thrown at them** (Showman: "I don't want to hit it with a plastic ball." Arbiter: "Ping pong balls where you draw the line?"). **Snacc takes 9. The Showman takes a critical 21 and drops to 8 HP.** Showman: "That's all I need." Crowd: "Finish him!"

### Round 6 — The sign, the hammer and the fake death
74. **1:10:23** Round six starts with the ring girls ("Come on, green girls!" [?]). Someone breaks a round sign ("Break that shit! Do it!"). GM: "**I love that sign. You go sit in the corner and think about what you've done**… You just go around destroying people's property?" She sends the culprit to the corner.
75. **1:11:05** Showman: "Can't get good help around here." GM: "**We're hiring.**" Arbiter: "Maybe if you offer dental…" GM: "**Listen, we have vision**… they lose teeth so often in these fights. It's not worth it." "We're not talking about my employee plans." Showman: "**I have some spare teeth if you want them**… Pounds." Showman: "In the arms of the angel." The GM gives in to the culprit's puppy eyes: "Okay, fine. **I'll take you out for Froyo later**." Arbiter: "We can't stay mad at you."
76. **1:12:35 — The monologue.** Snacc: "**Well, well, well… Here we are. Gathered around friends, family, grandmasters, arbiters, strange men in bowler hats. I happen to have in my pocket here a hero point.**" "I feel a surge of light coming on. **And now I show you my true might.**" The crowd repeats "My true might!" three times.
77. **1:13:38 — The Inferno Hammer.** "**I bring the flaming hammer of inferno and doom down upon you!**" It is an auto-hit for 15 plus dice, and the room chants "Damage!" It does **35 damage.** GM: "**You begin to crumble and fall as that hammer smashes you into the ground, burying you deep within the dirt of this forest.**" Showman: "**Oh, it's so moist! Oh, there's critters in here!**" GM: "Shut up. You're dead. Just die already." **She declares Lord Snacc the winner, and the Arbiter starts dragging the body off stage.**
78. **1:15:03 — Not dead.** Showman: "**But wait! He's getting back up! I use my hero points! Oh! Shh! He's just sleeping!**" He uses his hero point and **a custom innate feature that lets him feign his own death**, regaining 2d8+10 HP.
79. **1:15:27 — The Grandmaster's sword.** "**I found this in the earth of this forest! And I am rolling with it!**" GM: "**Did you just steal my sword?**" Showman: "Oh, this is yours? You dropped it." GM: "I saw you die." Showman: "**I am very difficult to kill. I've had practice.**" The stolen sword gives **+1 to hit**. "You rise from your earthly grave with your stolen weapon."
