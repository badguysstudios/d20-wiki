When [[Thea]] and a crowd of party animals drank every last drop of booze in [[The Dread Fortress|the Dread Fortress]], she recruited three champions to restock it: **[[Frigidoom]]**, a radioactive refrigerator; **[[Bob the Barbarian]]**, a jorts-clad Florida man; and **[[The Duke]]**, a rat magician with a bottomless hat. A simple beer run to [[Emmery's Tavern]] became a drinking contest, a whodunit and a brawl with the werewolf **[[Billy]]**. It ended with the newly bonded team being told they would have to fight each other in next week's Deathmatch.

## The Story

### The Booze Is Gone

Deep in the Dread Fortress, [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] followed the sound of screaming and cheering, burst through the kitchen doors and demanded, "What is going on in here?" Inside was absolute debauchery. [[Goblins]], [[Chinchillas]] and Thea were wasted, and nothing drinkable was left anywhere in the kitchen. Thea's first concern was secrecy: "Oh, don't tell the Arbiter. He'll get really mad because I forgot to invite him."

[[The Arbiter]] promptly appeared in the doorway, his Italian leather boots clacking. Thea explained that they had planned to invite him, "but we know that we didn't want to." The Grandmaster agreed he was "a bit of, well, a drink hog." The Arbiter replied that his stress demanded "a subsequent amount of booze that is equal to my station." Then he broke the bad news that the Grandmaster's own secret stash had been in the kitchen too. It was not much of a secret, he pointed out, "when everyone in the Dread Fortress knows where it's at." Not one barrel had survived.

The Arbiter clicked his pen. "This is going in your performance review." Thea bargained that if she replaced all the alcohol she would get five stars, and he gave her two hours. She clapped her tiny bony fingers, a pink spiral portal opened, and she announced she had "the Team C."

### Assembling Team C

First through the portal came Frigidoom, "a fridge exposed to radioactive waste," with slimy green tentacles of gelatinous waste. He opened his body to a rancid stench of rotting food and offered a long-expired beer. The Arbiter cracked it open with the bottle opener on his boot heel, sipped ("Exquisitely fermented"), and poured the rest over Thea's head. "So this counts as my bath for the week, right?" Thea asked. "You should be so lucky."

Next came Bob, a bare-chested barbarian from the Florida swamps, wearing jorts so tight that the Arbiter asked whether he was seeing a testicle. "Yes, I have four," said Bob, and he refused to loosen them. "You can never have tight enough jorts." Unimpressed, the Arbiter told Thea, "So far, you've brought me like half of a person," so she fetched a third: the Duke, a rat man who arrived in a panic and gasped at "a big, big, big fridge."

The Grandmaster made her pitch. She offered full employment as "the sword of the Dread Fortress," with immortality, a home, a sauna and a chinchilla, "as a pet, not to eat." The Duke asked for a bathroom made entirely of cheese, which meant the Arbiter would have to talk to "our cheese guy." Frigidoom wanted a new touchscreen shell. The Duke rummaged in his hat, pulled out what might have been underwear, then what might have been ten rats, and finally a tablet. The Duke vowed to solve the mystery of Bob's four testicles, and Bob was grateful, "for it has puzzled me for many years." Their mission was to visit the Fortress's favorite distributor and bring the booze home.

### Emmery's Tavern

The Arbiter's portal let in the smell of stale peanuts, beer and musky, sweaty man. Beyond it was a hearthlit tavern with a monster skull over the fire and [[Emmery]] wiping the same spot on the bar. The tavern ceiling had somehow turned transparent, and [[The Acolytes]] stared down through it. "Finally, an audience to watch my greatness," said Bob. The Duke privately confessed he had never performed for more than three people. Bob grabbed Frigidoom's tentacle and called him "brother." It felt like jello, cold and then stinging, and left his hand smelling like dog shit.

Emmery was baffled. He had just shipped the Fortress about 50 barrels of his "patented number two ale." He used sheep dung to dry the grains, though "there's hardly any shit in it." It was, he explained, "more about mouthfeel than anything." Still, he had bigger problems. His bar had been torn apart, and the town had seen missing customers, burglaries and "people straight up having their throats ripped out." If the team found the culprit, he would hand over his reserve.

Bob declared that this was "against the Florida man code" and asked his brothers to bleed with him. "The Duke of Dookie accepts your request," said the Duke. "In the name of fermentation and spoiled things everywhere, I, Frigidum, will accept this quest," said the fridge. Emmery observed that a simple yes would have done.

### The One-Eyed Fox

At the bar sat a small, mangy fox with one eye scratched out. Frigidoom oozed over and gave it a grape from the living mass of Thanksgiving leftovers inside him, to use as a new eye. The grape was too small and rolled around every time the fox moved its head. The fox scoffed that none of them could keep up with it at drinking, and it offered a deal: outdrink it, and it would tell them what it saw.

The toasts did not go smoothly ("To touch screens and 401ks"), but the drinking began. Frigidoom turned his ale into a boozy slushie in his ice maker. Bob downed his with a perfect roll and roared, "I am a king drinker!" The Duke had never drunk alcohol in his life, and he was soon seeing four fridges: "There's as many fridges as there are testicles." He steadied himself on Bob's shoulder and left a little slab of shit on it. Frigidoom fumbled the final round and got hammered too. The Duke tried to steady himself by pulling a second Ratman out of his hat. That terrified the fox, which bowed, kissed the Duke's toes ("Yes, please bow before the saint of shit") and begged not to be turned into a frog. It said its attackers had seemed bald, but it had grabbed a handful of coarse gray and black hair from one of them. It handed the hair over, then fled with its lucky mug.

### Dragonborn and Every Two Weeks

Frigidoom's warranty was expired and he needed a light, so Emmery handed over a Zippo, plus a pack of Black and Milds someone had left behind. When a card player shouted "put that out," the drunken Duke heard "brighter" and produced a second Zippo. The card table turned out to be dragonborn, who have no hair, and one of them hurled a chair: "You think we hang out at taverns because we like sunlight?" Bob apologized, which stunned the dragonborn, who had never been apologized to in his life. He then tried to bite a chair leg off, failed to leave so much as a tooth mark, and committed to it anyway.

A bigger dragonborn threatened to put his cigar out in Bob's eyeball. Bob calmly offered him the Black and Milds as a token of friendship, and it worked. When a dragonborn tried to lift Frigidoom into a chair and dropped him, Bob comforted his friend: "You are seen, brother." A single wet tear fell from Frigidoom's ice dispenser.

The dragonborn, including one named [[Tabitha]], had noticed a pattern. The attacks came every two weeks, the same rhythm as the visits of a regular called Billy. He was a bald old bloke with "one of those Jack Sparrow things" and long guitarist's fingernails, "but he can't play the guitar." He came whenever one of [[The Void]]'s two moons was full, and he always sat in the back party room. He was in there right now. "Oh, shit," said the Duke.

The dragonborn suggested the Duke fix his mustache before meeting Billy. The Duke's rat entourage restyled it with the coarse hair, sealed with Frigidoom's hardening paste. Thea, sipping number two ale from a sippy cup, told them to deal with Billy however they liked, or they would find poop in their shoes, and she admitted she would probably be the one who pooped in them. "You will always have five stars in my eyes," Frigidoom told her, and he stuck his gold star on her forehead. Thea licked it and screamed, "Killing Billy!" The Duke led a conga line of rats into the back.

### Billy

In the dark, candlelit back room, a large figure in furs sat beside a second shadow. The team smelled iron and heard a dripping. Then the second shadow slumped to the floor. "Shit, are you telling me they stole my kill?" said the Duke. Bob's Zippos sent the figure cowering into a corner. "Yeah, I'm Billy!" he squeaked, and he insisted, "These are fake animal furs!" Then the windows flew open under the full moon, black and gray hair sprouted from his bald scalp, and he became a werewolf. "You found me!"

The Duke's corner men splashed water in his face like a boxing trainer's crew. Bob punched Billy in the face ("My spleen!"), then aimed at the werewolf's testicles and missed: "Damn it, he's been spayed, boys." Bob spun into Billy like a jort-wrapped top. Billy caught him, spanked him, and threw him head-first into the wall. Frigidoom strode over on tentacle stilts and blinded Billy with a Tabasco slap ("My precious eyeballs!"). Then he rammed a fist of toxic bile down Billy's throat.

The Acolytes chanted for chaos, and the see-through roof proved permeable. A flock of flying wombats swooped in and hailed down frozen, perfectly cubed poop. "I'm in my element. I embrace the shit," said the Duke. Only Billy dodged it.

The Duke tried a card trick and ruined it. Next came "the one where you fucking die," a shotgun from his cloak that missed in a mutual fumble. A random katana missed too ("Better take that back to the katana store"). Pocket sand finally blinded Billy. Bob summoned his 20-foot gator Denise and threw a small poisonous caiman into Billy's eye. Billy raked his claws across Bob's chest with "tickle fingers." Bob told him to eat shit, and Billy replied, "I already did. I drank the ale here." Frigidoom's chicken-wing morning star, ice gatling and borrowed shotgun all missed, the last because "I just realized I don't have fingers."

### Behold, the Light

The Duke's anime-style shit bomb missed, and so did an RPG he pulled out of his head, even after Thea handed him the gold star ("I have my first fan"). "I'm a golden god!" crowed Billy. Team C huddled. Frigidoom suggested asking Thea to destroy the moon, but Bob protested that "Florida is a peninsula. Many will die." The Arbiter assured them this wasn't Earth. Then the Acolytes shouted down a reminder of how Billy had hidden from the light.

Bob took Frigidoom by the handle, cried "Behold, the light!", and opened the fridge door. A radioactive blaze hit Billy square on. Battered, Bob summoned a healing Gator Pool full of tiny gator fairies. Then he wrapped himself head to toe in denim, grabbed two gators by the tail, and spun into Billy like a Beyblade. "Taste my denim gator!" **Billy went down.**

### Team C No More

Emmery agreed, "Yeah, sure," to handing over the ale. Thea had proof of death "because he pooped his pants," and Emmery added, "The walls are very thin. I heard him die." The team claimed to be a true democracy with no leader. Thea thought that was stupid and declared they would fight in next week's Deathmatch to settle it. "Oh, fuck your friendship. I'm killing both of you," said the Duke. The damage to the tavern went on the tab, since the Arbiter gives Thea "unlimited budget, I think." They left before Emmery could bill them.

## Dried Blood

The Duke complained that the fight was rigged and promised his 76 lawyers would be in touch. They were, it emerged, all rats. Frigidoom told his teammates to "clear your guts because I'm going to give y'all a stuffing." The Duke, who wears a chastity belt, promised flowers for their graves before he dug them up and sold their bones on the black market. Bob warned his former brothers that they "haven't even begun to see the true depravity of Florida."
