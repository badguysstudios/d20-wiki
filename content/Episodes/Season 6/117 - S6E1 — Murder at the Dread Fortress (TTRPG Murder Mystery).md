## What happens
### Season opening narration
1. **0:05:16** The file's text opens with "Welcome to D20 Top 5!" (likely misheard). After some table talk ("Light-headed, whoa"), Bec says: "We're into season six, baby."
2. **0:05:35 — The state of the Void.** The Void is "freshly returned to order, in the weeks following **the Arbiter's return from death and the liberation of the Grandmaster**." The Shadow King "is, like, gone, but **his soul was torn asunder and the danger he poses still remains as pieces [of] him have scattered about the Void**." The Acolytes rejoice "as **Elusia Skypiercer once again sits atop the throne of the Dread Fortress**."
3. **0:06:19** In honor of her return and "the coming trials ahead," **the greatest feast is held before the evening's scheduled Deathmatch**. Inside the Dread Fortress ("deep within that sulking mouth into our lava terrain"), **busy goblins hang tinsel and light the last candles of the chandelier**. Guests begin to arrive on the **"bloody carpet."**
4. **0:07:03 — Fortress climate.** It's "sweaty hot" with volcanic eruptions around, but **the stone floor is cold**, which is nice if you're barefoot. The Arbiter: "It's the humidity that's going to get you."

### The murder
37. **0:39:00** There's **a weird coughing noise, then every candle goes out at once.** Silence, then **a shriek**.
38. **0:39:41** **The Arbiter relights the candles with a snap.** Dozens of guests are gathered around the large tables in the banquet hall, wondering if it's a show. **Skelman rolls a natural 20 on instinct and is first to notice: Thea is face down in a bowl of gravy.** Blood covers the table and the dishes are scattered. **Thea's blood changes color with her mood, like a mood ring. Fear is green**, so her green blood pools into the gravy and mashed potatoes. She lies at the end of the table **near the Grandmaster's and Arbiter's seats**.
39. **0:41:26 — The doctor.** A weak old figure in a white coat pushes through: "**I'm a doctor.**" They plug **a stethoscope into their own arm**, lift Thea's foot ("It is warm. I think that is good"), shake her limp body, and **smash their head against hers: "I hear no thoughts."** The Arbiter: "To be fair, she didn't have any anyway." Bunga: "Well, she had thoughts of hot dogs." "**I pronounce them dead.**"
40. **0:43:08 — The thread.** The doctor **pulls a small thread from the scene**: "Oh, this is tasty. I will save this for later for me." They pocket it.
41. **0:43:35 — "Medical school."** The Arbiter asks where they studied. "**I attended at medical school.**" Skelman claims Medical School is "the name of a rather prestigious medical school." Bunga: "You shut it, Boney." Skelman: "My name is Skelmin, thank you very much. I was going to offer you a job." **Skelman dubs the doctor "Dr. Octor of medical school"**, and the doctor accepts it: "I am Dr. Octor." Skelman: "**She was a worm 30 minutes ago.**" Diagnosis: "**Something killed this thing.**… This appears to have hurt."
42. **0:45:12 — The reward.** The Arbiter: "this is typical Thea, every time, trying to ruin the fun." He offers **a hefty reward for information on the murderer**: "surely one amongst you is the murderer, I'm not judging, we've all done it." He suggests doing it after dinner. The Grandmaster: "**Death will be discovered.**" She promises **"a prize of your choosing to be negotiated later"** to whoever finds the killer. She tells **the one invited journalist** at the back not to write "deadly night" in the papers.
43. **0:46:26 — Seating by importance.** Name cards are "**in order of importance, so you can clearly tell your place in this realm.**" A muffled voice comes from the mashed potatoes: "You're actually over there." **The Arbiter smacks Thea's head**: "You're dead… It's gas escaping the body." Skelman hears through "vibrations in my teeth."
44. **0:47:30** The three are seated together about three quarters of the way down the table. A flat d20 decides their order. **Bunga is placed first ("Everyone loves bread. This checks out"), then Iris, then "Dick Skelman."** Across from them sit **Stuart, Dr. Octo and Lady Elizabeth**. Skelman: "we're the same rank as those assholes?" Lady Elizabeth: "I am from an esteemed family." Skelman: "I don't know where I came from." Bunga: "Ground, I believe."
45. **0:49:10 — The Poopshoe family.** Asked about her rank, Lady Elizabeth says: "**we are above everyone, but also at the same time below.** Our family is technically been part of the Void for ages… **known for our sewage and [waste] disposable capabilities. The Poop Chute is a family proud tradition. That's why it's our name. It's French.**" Skelman: "**We install a poop chute in every dungeon!**… I've had some leakages before." She slams her trunk on the table: "**Our chutes are slick.**" She slurps wine with her trunk. Skelman: "these mashed potatoes taste like death."
46. **0:50:35 — Stuart's job.** Iris says she's here "to eventually compete." Stuart: "**I'm currently IT, but I will become a mage… the greatest mage**." He stands on his chair, catches the Arbiter's eye, and shrinks back down. Skelman: "You have the confidence of a much taller man."

Turns out he doesn't have really a magic bone in his body.**" Skelman: "Oh, I could give him a magic bone in his body." Bunga objects that Stuart's a child. "It's not a literal child." "His age is actually very hard to determine."

Bunga smooths the butter down his front like a tie.

Bunga: "**almost as misplaced as your elephant foot in your mouth.**" She slams her wine glass down: "**This hoof is pedicured with gems and diamonds. Tim had come out on one of the platters. He hides, then **rolls a natural 1 on mettle**: "**I forgot it was a homing missile!**" **He falls apart "like a cartoon xylophone of bones"**, left on 1 HP as **just a skull on the table, and his magic bone, the big toe**. Arbiter: "**You're the kind of mind that we could really use around this place.**" (Inferred: the offer is to Bunga, from these lines.)

Pieces of a dead body come through portals and re-form, golem-like, then stitched up like Iris.** The husband opens his eyes and coughs out dust "like Hocus Pocus." Arbiter: "**I murdered the guy. I didn't make him a douchebag.**"

"That's not where that belongs!" Arbiter: "**I don't think that guy's a real doctor, Grandmaster.**" Skelman: "I'm starting to get the feeling he didn't go to medical school."

She toasts "Seabunga, Skelbin, and of course, Iris." A long chorus of "Thank you" follows. Skelman: "Good luck next time. I'm a weapon."

I have a few more holes in me than before.**" Iris: "Sorry about that one." Thea: "**It's okay. Now we both have holes.**… Let's do a hole tour." **Thea lands on Iris's shoulder.**

## Who and what appears
### Characters
**[[Characters/Iris|Iris]]** (speaker 00). A dead bride. She wears her own torn wedding gown, has reddish hair and stitches all over, carries a weathered bouquet, and her limbs and eyes come loose.
- Recently dead. Wine leaks from **stab holes in her stomach** ("I was sad").
- **Both eyes are now wine-stained red.**
- She stitches herself with flesh and odd strands, including black thread.
- **She and her husband were stabbed and killed by the Arbiter on their wedding night, at a feast.** The venue had been double-booked for a Deathmatch.
- **She murdered Thea with a knife at the banquet to get vengeance**, then **blew up her resurrected husband Tony with a rocket launcher**. "I've never felt more alive." The Grandmaster praised her energy.
- She says she's there "to eventually compete."

**[[Characters/Richard S. Skelman|Richard S. Skelman]]** (speaker 05). "Richard Skeleton Skelman," "Dungeon Designer and Keeper of the Year," from a long line of Skelmans.
- A skeleton, "one quarter of a guy." He was resurrected without his clothes. He doesn't remember his own murder but knows he was murdered.
- **His voice and thought come from his magical bone, his big toe.**
- His dungeon application was rejected for an overblown bone budget, with an insulting imp messenger.
- He uses Tim and the Poopshoe chutes ("leaky") in his dungeons, and imports monsters from China. He wants to hire Bunga for a butter trap.
- He noticed Thea's body first.
- **Fired a rocket at the Arbiter, which was thrown back as a homing missile. He is reduced to a skull and his toe**, which Dr. Octo stuck to a femur.

**[[Characters/Cornelius Antonio C.A. Bunga|Cornelius Antonio "C.A." Bunga]]** (speaker 03). A hip-high slice of buttered sourdough toast.
- Syrup eyes, syrup mustache and syrup-elemental limbs that leave sticky residue. A leaf top hat and a breadstick cane.
- **Married to Lady Tortiliana**, Lady Elizabeth's rival. They can't have children.
- His ex was Pop-Tart ("that jelly was toxic").
- Runs a dairy establishment. On a diet (unsalted butter).
- Caught the chinchillas with his sticky arm. **Offered a job by the Arbiter** (inferred, 1:37:19–1:37:45).

- Small brain; gold-star chart; wants a hot dog. Banned from hot dogs except "backwards."
- Friends with Tim. **Her blood changes color with her mood (fear = green).**
- Murdered face down in gravy, yet kept talking as a "corpse." **Revived at the end** and perched on Iris's shoulder.
- Claims to have **trademarked "time eyes"** and to earn money whenever the Arbiter uses the spell.

**[[Characters/The Arbiter|The Arbiter]]** (Drew). Opens the "new era" (build champions up, then watch them kill each other).
- Created Thea; rejects Stuart twice. Wants a new "goo guy." Negotiates with the Chinchilla Union.
- **Uses temporal transmogrification ("time eyes") to replay the night in reverse**, with multicolored chaos lightning in his eyes.
- **Killed Iris and her husband at their wedding feast over a double-booked Deathmatch venue.** He apologized, saying he's "working on being a little bit better."
- Stopped a rocket in time and resurrected Tony from pieces.

**[[Characters/Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer]]** (Bec). Back on the Dread Fortress throne; hosts the feast.
- Wanted to bite Bunga. Invites all employees for morale.
- Promised a negotiable prize for finding the murderer. Served weapons for dessert.
- Sided with the chinchillas on pizza. Celebrates rebirth through the Deathmatch.

**[[Characters/Stuart|Stuart the Gray]]** (Bec). The Dread Fortress IT guy, in khaki shorts and a blue polo, with over-gelled spiky hair.
- A fan of the Arbiter. Keeps applying to the magic department ("doesn't have a magic bone in his body").
- Brought a fake truth amulet ("made in China," remote button).

**[[Characters/Lady Elizabeth Barry Ann Poopshoe|Lady Elizabeth Barry Ann Poopshoe]]** (Bec). A wealthy Loxodon in a red velvet cloak and jewels, with her drink taped to her foot.
- **Her family runs sewage and waste disposal across the Void ("the Poop Chute… It's French")**.
- Rival of Lady Tortiliana. Calls everyone trash.

**[[Characters/Jercasso|Jercasso]]** (Bec). The trench coat. **It was three chinchillas, Jervia, Kevin and Sora**, of **the Chinchilla Union, on strike for a pizza party**. They are "Boston chinchillas" who smoke cigarettes. Sora carried the other two.

**[[Characters/Dr. Octo|Dr. Octo / "Dr. Octor"]]** (Bec). A weak old "doctor" in a white coat from "medical school."
- Uses a stethoscope plugged into their arm. Pronounced Thea dead.
- Ate evidence thread. Licks toes; has sticky saliva. Probably not a real doctor.

**[[Characters/Tim|Tim (the Wicked)]]**. A gelatinous cube living downstairs/in the kitchen.
- Acidic goo that leaves bones alone, about 1 sp an ounce. "Needs no rest."
- Has caught three adventurers at once and has devoured cows. Used as Skelman's decoy.

**[[Characters/Tony|Tony]]**. Iris's husband, killed by the Arbiter. He married into "a lot of families for the money" and stole from Iris. Resurrected by the Arbiter, then rocketed to pieces by Iris.

**[[Characters/Lady Tortiliana|Lady Tortiliana]]**. Bunga's wife, mentioned only.

**[[Characters/Pop-Tart|Pop-Tart]]**. Bunga's ex, mentioned only.

**[[Characters/Unnamed|Unnamed:]]** the insulting imp who rejected Skelman; the invited journalist; goblin staff.
