## What happens
### Opening — last-minute prep in the Dread Fortress
1. **0:08:15** Bec opens: "Welcome, Acolytes, to the arena!" The focus is on **Team A**, "soon to be determined what their actual team name is". Tonight the team members find out who will lead.
2. **0:08:40** In a busy room of the Dread Fortress, **the Grandmaster, Thea and the Arbiter rush to finish the last details**: Acolyte invites, and restocking the concession stands with **blue slushies, hot dogs and plizzies**. The Arbiter: "We're already late."
3. **0:09:14** They call for Thea. **She has been lugging around a giant bowl of goldfish, her "pre-show dinner."** The Arbiter tells her the Grandmaster "has instructed me to instruct you to make sure that all this shit gets done."
4. **0:09:46** Thea says she cleaned the arena ("really primed for some blood") and sent the champions their invitations. She reminded them that everyone will be revived, so they can "pull out all the stops" ("Pull them out or stop them?"). **The Arbiter holds up the invitations: they're still in his hand.** Thea's jaw drops. ### The champions arrive
6. **0:11:06 — Copycat, still a worm.** Copycat is still stuck as a worm in the form of **Timothy, the mayor**: "I can't get my leg out from behind my neck… I think my pinky toe is poking my liver." She asks for a chiropractor or **Dr. Octor**. The Grandmaster says Dr. Octor takes too long and asks the Arbiter to do "little magically thingies with your fingers." Copycat: "It's like I'm stuck in a Chinese finger trap, except it's my whole body."
7. **0:12:07 — The Arbiter's fix.** He raises her with one hand. Then he **rips every molecule of her body apart into individual atoms**, "super painful," **and puts her back together.** "That's about as good as I can do."
8. **0:12:38 — Therapy.** Copycat asks whether they cover therapy. The Arbiter: "We only care about the hard sciences here… therapy doesn't work." Copycat wants to see the therapists anyway "so I could steal their identity." The Grandmaster says they have "a plethora of resources."
9. **0:13:12 — The slap.** The Grandmaster calls the Arbiter "deeply disturbed" and **slaps him across the face**: "This is a Deathmatch. You need to be pumping them up." The Arbiter asks what this is really about; "you seem on edge tonight." She answers: "**Deathmatch is returning. I want it to be good.** … I have torn champions limb from limb hundreds of times. That's not your job… **We are a supportive environment.**" Copycat demands compensation: "Please give me your credit card and your social security number." The request is declined.
10. **0:14:25 — Tiny Atlas.** Steam, hissing and whirring come from Atlas's portal, along with the sound of heavy metal boots. **Atlas comes through with only his faceplate, his enormous electrified fists, two boots and "a pair of tiny arms": no torso, no legs, no waist. He is "not completely built" and about knee height.** Thea: "Oh my god! You're adorable!" She knocks on him. Atlas: "**Atlas feels breakable.**"
11. **0:15:48 — The Thea backpack.** **Thea climbs onto Atlas's back like a small backpack to reinforce him.** Atlas: "It's almost like another pair of characters that were on the deathmatch a while ago. I feel very familiar now."
12. **0:16:27 — Robbie Clark and the Hunger.** The Arbiter can never remember the name: "He's got two names… two first names." Robbie: "I remember everybody's name from my class." He says they're ready after watching cartoons and eating cereal, and adds "puppies," which the Hunger echoes. He announces the team as "**the super redacted Unbreakable friends!**" The Grandmaster says it would fit on a cape. Atlas: "I don't wear a cape. I wear a backpack."
13. **0:17:38 — Puppies.** Copycat: "Did you just say you ate puppies?" Atlas: "Atlas cannot eat. He still does not have a mouth." Robbie: puppies "have a great amount of nutrients for their biomass." The Grandmaster freezes, calls it "an internal team thing," and goes to **make sure Doug doesn't come near the arena**.
14. **0:18:23 — The stakes.** The winner gets to **choose the team's name and become team leader**. The Grandmaster says the leader might even ban dog-eating on the team, and Thea promises **stickers** to everyone. **Atlas runs two tiny pipes into the Thea backpack**; there's a soft sound and the pipes retract. Asked what that was, Atlas says: "You will find out once we begin the mayhem and death."
15. **0:19:18 — The arena.** The Arbiter's sound wave opens a portal to **the Ether Drifts**. The champions stand **on an asteroid beside a large gaseous planet, where gravity is lower than at the Dread Fortress** ("We never really talked about what the gravity is at the Dread Fortress, but it's different here"). The Acolytes watch from a floating grandstand.
16. **0:20:03 — The Grandmaster's charge.** "Prove yourself worthy to lead this trio, for it is not just this team that you will champion, but **perhaps my greatest armies as we continue to defend the Void**… destroy your teammates." Robbie: "It's going to be hard to punch all these people that I think are so cool… **Friendly fire protocols disabled.**"
17. **0:21:10** **The Arbiter spreads his white glowing wings** and asks if they're ready for blood. Acolytes throw their grandmothers into space, rip off their shirts and sling beers.
18. **0:21:50 — Pants.** Atlas left his dice "in my pants, which are no longer attached to my body," so the Arbiter rolls for him. Copycat: "You have no mouth, but you wear pants." The table recalls that Atlas has no butthole. Atlas: "**It is a port. It is just not a butthole.** … That is where I keep all of my junk. All of my junk inside my trunk." Atlas has the highest initiative and goes first.

### Round 1
19. **0:22:43 — Sticker barrage.** Atlas patters toward the Hunger's many fangs and jaws. **He fires a machine-gun barrage of stickers into the mouth of each tendril.** He misses.
20. **0:24:27 — The sticker book.** Robbie: "You have fallen for our greatest trap." **He opens his backpack to reveal the Hunger's "nest of the abyss and the shadow," plus a big sticker book that Robbie and the Hunger have been filling "for eons and eons."** The Hunger drags the stickers gently into the book. Hunger: "**Our power grows as does our collection.**"
21. **0:25:12 — The Michelangelo sticker.** Atlas: "As predicted… you have opened your backpack and made it vulnerable." He loads **a huge orange 90s pizza-turtle sticker with a bandana ("the biggest Michelangelo sticker")** onto his fist. He spends a power-up and his Unstoppable Surge and launches it at the nest. **Robbie clips the backpack shut ("Clip"), and the sticker sticks harmlessly to the outside.** "Thwarted by a child's backpack."
22. **0:28:04 — Separation anxiety.** With the sticker on his fist, **Atlas grabs the backpack and wrenches it away. It turns out to be organic: veiny, sticky, dripping fleshy tendrils that lash out as if severed.** This hits. **Robbie and the Hunger now have "separation anxiety."**
23. **0:30:17 — The lost child.** Robbie freezes and looks confused. "**Uh, mister? Where am I? … This is not after school.**" He walks up to Atlas, points at the crowds and fires, and says, "I just want to go home." This is an emotional attack to "crack that iron heart." Atlas's programming: "**Small child, subject, detection, sympathy required, lost child, 404 error, fuck's not found.**" The attack fails.
24. **0:32:39 — The backpack regrows.** Robbie tilts his cap down: "Man, I really thought that was going to work on you, but **you should have seen season one**." **A new backpack erupts from the flesh of his back, revealing he is not a human child.** Hunger: "**Robbie and the Hunger are the one. We cannot be separated… We were unified before the first waves crashed against the first beaches, and we shall still be together when the last star dies in the sky.**" The maw turns back into a backpack and keeps some of the stickers.
25. **0:33:28 — "Fetch."** Robbie tells the stolen backpack Atlas is holding to "Fetch." **It turns into a giant backpack-insect with baby hands, a little baseball cap like Robbie's and a fly mouth. It bites Atlas's hand, turns into "a weird handshake face," and skitters back to sit on Robbie's shoulder, holding Atlas's detached arm.** It hits, with a status effect. Robbie: "That's what you get for not falling for my sympathy trick… **I am the unifying force.**"
26. **0:35:24** Copycat has the Hunger repeat its "first waves" line and takes notes. The Hunger adds that "friendship is the most powerful power in the universe." Its hunger "is for friendship and biomass, **which kind of is friendship but on the most intimate of scales**." The Grandmaster: "I'm learning so much about friendship today."
27. **0:36:09 — Candyland gummy dots.** Copycat twirls into **a crisp chef's outfit with a tray** and serves the Hunger **Candyland gummy dots**, which were **banned and pulled from production for being too dangerously chewy to breathe around**. It hits, and Robbie is stuck chewing.
28. **0:38:36 — The stolen sticker book.** **Copycat steals the sticker album out of the backpack**, which hits: "It's my sticker book now." Robbie: "That's not part of the game!" She tries to scrape the stickers off with her thumbnail as a "crushing loss" status. **It fails because they're stuck fast.** Robbie: they used "the special glue… hidden in the drawer… because it smells funny." It's **Gorilla** glue. "If you put it in your nose, you have to go to the hospital." Copycat keeps the book under her arm.
29. **0:40:58** **Thea, still on Atlas's back, coughs up a d20 "like a cat coughing up a hairball"** and rolls a 10.
30. **0:41:41 — The bet.** The Arbiter "told my sponsor I wouldn't take any more bets." **The Grandmaster backs Robbie Clark** for his invincible backpack and his ready-made team name. **The Arbiter backs Copycat**, who "might have what it takes to annoy the rest of them to death." Atlas, he says, is too tiny to have "the stature of a leader anymore." Grandmaster: "Heights? That's how you're basing leadership? … **Every day I like you less and less.**"

### Round 2
31. **0:42:52 — Fatlas.** **Atlas separates into his pieces, spins each one at high speed to shake off the status, and reassembles around the Thea backpack** ("I feel like Iron Man"). Then, to the sound of an electric guitar, **he pulls apart his shell and encases Thea in it, making her an Iron-Man-style core and makeshift body.** Thea (probably): "**I'm ready for this! I was born ready!**" He heals, using Thea's "vitality." The Arbiter calls that "all that made up stuff"; the Grandmaster adds "Goldfish… lots of goldfish." **The result is a tiny iron Thea with electric wings and rings of energy around the arms, like a fisticuff monk with interchangeable wrist weapons.** Thea (probably): "I'm voting for you, Atlas." Atlas: "**We are no longer Atlas or Thea. We are... Fatlas.**"
32. **0:45:45 — Three Acolytes blasted.** Copycat claims she heard "fatass." Atlas asks to blast whoever said it in the audience. The Grandmaster has him **pick three Acolytes from chat** ("with that nat 20"; which roll she meant is unclear). He spares "down with the puppy killer" and **blows up Commodore, Cassander and "Build up"** (names unclear), who cheered for the puppy killing. **They explode into piles of ash: "We'll put them back together later."** Copycat says she'll spell it **"Phatlas," P-H**. Atlas: "The only word that he knows is pain."
33. **0:47:25 — Robbie's grievance.** "Looking at these two once friends, now traitors… You don't care about my stickers." Copycat's line is the puppies: piglets are "a gray area." Robbie: "**I don't put different biomass above the rest… All biomass is delicious and it is all equal in power.**" Atlas opens his faceplate to show Thea inside. Robbie: "**Just because you can sit in a driver's seat doesn't mean you know how to build a car.**"
34. **0:49:36 — The power of friendship.** "I'm also mostly going to kill you, and then eat your bodies, and then you shall become one with me, because that seems to be the best way to make friends." The Arbiter: "That's just standard leadership." **Robbie activates his superpower: the tendrils stab into Robbie Clark's eyes and form an insular tube, and his body floats up "very Akira-esque."** "We are truly together." **He spins and fires beams of "friendship eldritch energy"**, two at Atlas and two at Copycat. A **natural 20** lands on Atlas's "medley outer shell" and restores some of Robbie's fighting spirit. Against Copycat, **grabbing his book back hard gives her a paper cut "in between my fingers. That's the worst kind"**; the push misses. **Copycat slaps him**: "Get your hands off… I need a band-aid." Hunger: "All shall become one." Atlas tells Copycat she'll be destroyed long before infection sets in.
35. **0:53:04** Robbie: "I just wanted to be friends, and then you guys started being mean." Copycat calls Fatlas "a casserole." Robbie: "I love casserole." Robbie is now in his unified form: "He's got mouths for eyes."

### Round 2 — Copycat becomes the Hunger's mother

Hunger: "**Mom, my brother's an asshole.**" Mom: "**Your brother's a dimension conqueror.**" Hunger: "I want to just be friends with dimensions."
38. **0:56:33 — Parental disappointment.** It hits as a status effect. "**You're grounded… No supper for six whole centuries.**" Robbie deflates and lands back on the ground.
39. **0:57:42 — "Overgrown can opener."** Mom to Atlas: "What are you supposed to be? An overgrown can opener?" Her tentacle attack hits; Foundry lags and the Grandmaster blames **Stuart**. **Her tentacle grabs Atlas by the neck to pull Thea out, but the chassis snaps shut on it like a mousetrap.** "You're a bad little can opener, aren't ya?" Atlas: "**The only thing this can opens is cans of whoop-ass.**" The Grandmaster scores the comeback 10/10 and the Arbiter 6/10. The Grandmaster says he's never delivered a great comeback: "**Maybe if you weren't wearing those Italian leather shoes, you'd be a little faster.**" Arbiter: "You leave my shoes out of this." "They're handcrafted."

## Dried Blood (after-show)
- The players were sent home because the episode ran long; Drew and Bec talk alone ("with words and interpretive dance").
- This is "episode three of the season, but only our first Deathmatch."
- **Bec:** this is the "new updated style" they wanted: a setup episode to get to know characters and build camaraderie, then the Deathmatch. It brought "personal attacks." She loved how the players asked each other's permission to "take liberties" describing each other's characters: "a lot of trust established."
- **The official Team A name is "The Unbreakable Redacted Friends"** (Drew: "It's a mouthful"). T-shirts are planned. Bec won't tell Teams B and C the name, to see what they come up with.
- **Bec lists "the D20 family" now in place:** the Grandmaster, "the Arbiter back in his place where he's supposed to be," Thea, Doug, Stuart, Dr. Octor, the champions, chinchillas and goblins.
- **Drew:** Stuart is "our go-to shit on for anything IT related." He enjoys being mean to Stuart as the Arbiter. Season 1 was meant to be episodic with little lore; the lore has developed naturally, "the best of both worlds." (Bec: "All right, Hannah Montana.")
- **In character (Dried Blood), Drew as the Arbiter:** "Stuart, it's never going to fucking happen." Bec replies: "Stuart, we'll look at getting you some promotion. No, we won't." (Bec's speaker role is uncertain.)
- Bec, explicitly as Caustic: the community's participation matters ("how many of them we crushed in the actual episode"). She wants viewers to see themselves as part of the world, not just viewers. She's off to get Taco Bell.

**[[Characters/Robbie Clark and the Hunger|Robbie Clark and the Hunger]]** (guest, "Cinder" inferred). Appears as a small boy in a baseball cap with a backpack. **The backpack is the Hunger: a fleshy nest of fangs, jaws and tendrils "of the abyss and the shadow."** Robbie "is not a human child."
