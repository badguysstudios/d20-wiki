## What happens
### Opening: the Dread Fortress and the goblin band
1. **0:00:01** The Arbiter "clack[s] his way" into the halls of the Grandmaster in **Italian leather boots "awarded to him for good behavior in his prison cell."** Voices at the table insist they're **"not real Italian"** ("We're truthers"). He begs the Grandmaster: "Just give me one fucking night."
2. **0:00:57** The Arbiter is coming back from **grocery shopping, which is now his job** ("going to Walmart is terrifying and no one else wants to do it"). He offers the excuse that "it's a pandemic."
3. **0:01:16** He follows the sound of **amazing cowbell** backed by rock and metal guitars down **the cracked hallways of the Dread Fortress that still need renovating** ("The kitchen, though, finally is done."). The trail leads to a small black room with a lifted stage and a spotlight on **a group of five goblins: three on cowbell and one guitarist in the back**, under a sign reading **"band tryouts."**
4. **0:02:04** The Grandmaster sits against the wall on a **metal chair**, arms crossed. She explains that these are **band tryouts, an extracurricular activity for the goblins** ("you can't invite everyone"). The **cowbells were free, found in the basement**, and **"Kyle over there already had his guitar."** She predicts that "no one has ever heard of a three goblin cowbell band," says it will help tourism, and wants them as **the opening act before the next match**. She listens for applause from the Acolytes above, hoping they'll back her wish for more cowbell.
5. **0:03:37** The Arbiter repeats his self-improvement mantra: "I'm letting my anger go. I'm letting the hatred go. I'm going to be a better person." He points out that it is Sunday.
6. **0:03:56** The goblins freeze mid-cowbell, crestfallen. The Arbiter has interrupted **Sarah's solo**. The Grandmaster: "**You're a star, Sarah.**" The Arbiter: "**Don't quit your day job, Sarah.**" The GM: "Her day job is here." The band is put off until next week.
7. **0:04:53** The Arbiter claps his **still-shackled hands, chains clanking**. Thunder ripples through the Dread Fortress and a portal tears open in time and space.

### The champions
8. **0:05:35 K-Boom** greets "**Master Chief** and Arbiter." He goes by **K-Boom, "aka Kyle Boomishnow, aka Kaboom,"** names known "across the different realms." He is a **gnome artificer**, 3'2" on a good day ("the doctors say I'm like three foot one and a half, but I round up… for the ladies"). He wears a long robe covered in trinkets, vials and gears over **plate armor**. **He loves three things: explosives, cigarettes and cryptocurrency.** He is here to prove that **K-Boom Coin** "is gonna be the best cryptocurrency that the realm has ever seen" by winning.
9. **0:06:28 Ignacio Marchade**, speaking in a deep voice, has "come back from the realms to whip K-Boom's ass… and when I say ass, I mean die ass." He adds that crypto "went down a lot in the market." K-Boom asks whether he needs to cough or see a physician (an aside about a Lego in someone's cereal is spoken here; the speaker is unclear). Ignacio: "I might need to see one kind of like how you might need to after this match." K-Boom: "Wow, you are very, very dark."
10. **0:07:21 Mort.** The Grandmaster kneels and asks whether Ignacio brought a friend. He has: **Mort, a monkey to whom Ignacio "gave… wings."** K-Boom: "Did you get him from Oz?… That looks like copyright infringement just waiting to happen." Ignacio insists Mort is his own creature.
11. **0:08:11 Doug arrives.** K-Boom says "she came down in a bubble, Doug," and because someone said "Doug," **a tiny, very round pug appears**. He sniffs Mort, and **Mort becomes his best friend**. Doug shares one of his toys. The GM allows Mort in the arena "as long as Mort is potty trained." The Arbiter: "We've had worse in the arena." The GM: "Well, we've had you in the arena." The Arbiter: "I am centered. I am focused. I am the best me."

### The arena: the frozen lake
12. **0:09:36** The Arbiter claps again. There is no portal this time: the room fades and a cold breeze whisks everyone to **a quiet lakeside by a dilapidated old cabin, surrounded by snow-covered mountains**. The lake is shallow except for **a large deeper area in the middle**. "Some would say a good place to die." "I would say it was a good place to watch people die."
13. **0:10:27** The Grandmaster stands at the edge of the dock with Doug at her feet and welcomes them to "our frozen little getaway cabin." High above, the ethereal **Acolytes in a coliseum-style arena** do "the stomp and clap… the song that we can't mention because we don't own the rights to it." They wear **earmuffs and mittens, hold hot cocoa, and huddle together against the cold**. "May every step in the snow, every splash in the water, be a remembrance of the actions you have taken tonight."
14. **0:11:13 Arena features:**
    - **The cabin** is about 20 ft tall, with many boards missing.
    - **A tiny rowboat** with a hole in it sits on the dirt.
    - **The lake is not frozen over despite the cold** ("If any of you know science, most of you know that water freezes, so take that for what you will").
    - The Grandmaster walks through crunching leaves, opens her wings to scatter them, and flies to her throne seat holding Doug.
15. **0:13:05 K-Boom's speech.** He pulls a microphone from his satchel and casts prestidigitation to make a small spotlight. Reading from scrap paper, he delivers an **Independence Day-style speech** adapted to **"the 14th of August"**: "We can't be consumed by our petty differences anymore." He winks at Ignacio. "The 14th of August will no longer be known as **a void holiday**, but the day we… declare in one voice, we will not go quietly into the night… Today, we celebrate our independence day. **K-Boom coin on sale now.**"
16. **0:14:22** With tears streaming down her face, **the Grandmaster gives both champions a d4 inspiration die.** K-Boom: "Totally original. Didn't watch a movie last night." The Arbiter: "I think you have what it takes to be president." K-Boom: "**My dad used to say the same thing before he died tragically from a house fire that I caused accidentally.**" The Arbiter advises him to "use that fire as more fire to fuel you… otherwise his dad died for nothing." K-Boom adds a fourth love: **arson**.
17. **0:15:39 Placement.** K-Boom rolls a 3 and starts in the upper quadrant. Ignacio starts facing him next to the dark water, which looks like a significant drop-off. They are **knee-to-chin deep in water** (waist deep for the gnome, who is "hydrodynamic") and about 65 ft apart. The Arbiter asks K-Boom for the coin's market cap ("I got a little spiel planned out later"). **Initiative: K-Boom 17, Ignacio 14.**

### Round 1
18. **0:18:07** Cold in the waist-high water, K-Boom decides to "heat the place up." "Hey, Mr. Deep Voice with the monkey… You like fire?" "I do not." **His fire bolt misses.** Ignacio: "I see it whittle and hit maybe my pinky toe. I am not fazed… I will end you at no cost." K-Boom: "God, he's from the DC universe." Ignacio: "I lost the rights." K-Boom retreats 15 ft. The GM notes that in water this deep "you could probably pee in the water and no one would know."
19. **0:19:42 The Box of Chaos.** The Acolytes trigger it. The Arbiter: "This is already getting boring." **A giant pink and purple box slams down between them**, splashing water. Pink, purple and yellow flames shoot up in a column, the lid flies open, and **shadowy fingers and yellow eyes, Gerald, creep out**. Sacrificing anything, as a free action, earns a chance at a magic item.
20. **0:20:49 Hero points.** Each champion gets one from the Acolytes. The Grandmaster cuts in to announce it, ruining the Arbiter's flow. One half of the crowd chants "**K-Boom! Crypto, crypto, crypto!**" and the other "**Ignacio Mort!**" Their souls are on fire, but their bodies aren't ("no one will take fire damage on this"). The Arbiter: "We'd say go plus ultra, but you'd probably get sued."
21. **0:22:21 Ignacio sacrifices Mort.** He dashes to the box. Gerald: "Who are you supposed to be and **what the fuck is that?**" (pointing at the monkey). Ignacio: "I'm the man, the myth, the legend, Ignacio. And I offer you tribute… my own best friend." Gerald: "I kinda wanted that thing, whatever it is. I'll definitely take it." Ignacio: "**He knows the deal. He signed on for the trip.**" K-Boom: "I'm calling animal services!" Gerald grabs Mort ("It's okay, come with me. You're gonna really like it in here"), and the lid closes.
22. **0:24:10** The Grandmaster promises **Mort will be well cared for: "All animals in our kingdom are very well fed… Mort will even get his own bedroom."** Ignacio: "This won't be the last I see of Mort." Mort's backstory: **Ignacio found him "strolling through… the Appalachian Mountains"** ("Because he can fly"), cleaned him and fed him. The GM: "Mort needs to settle… Play with Doug."
23. **0:25:11 The Wheel of Stars.** The box rumbles and magical fire with no heat shoots up. Gerald floats Ignacio a small metal item, **the Wheel of Stars**. It gives +1 to Wisdom saves and lets him cast dancing lights, guidance and message. Once, as a reaction, **Portent of Stars** subtracts a d10 from any roll within 60 ft. Once, he can **alter gravity to cast Fly or Levitate**. The GM thinks dancing lights is "the one that distracts kitty cats." (He never uses the item on camera.)
24. **0:27:10** Ignacio throws a **javelin at K-Boom's left pinky toe**. It misses and **lands right beside his foot "almost like it's more of a threat."** K-Boom coaches him: "You're like three inches off… a little more arc." **The second javelin hits the first one and splits it in half**, again missing. K-Boom fakes "oh my God, you almost got me." Ignacio: "**You are lucky I forgot my left contact.**" K-Boom: "I couldn't tell… how dark your face is… can you even see out of that thing?… I just don't think you have eyes."
25. **0:29:22 The red eyes.** Ignacio wears a **deep-set hood with a cloth over his face; only glimpses of glowing red eyes show**. A few Acolytes catch the shimmer and **faint in the stands, then wake fanning themselves**, like the three women fawning over Gaston. Ignacio: "Ladies, don't worry. This Deathmatch will be over in a sitch. And **I know one of you guys have my contact lens**." The GM: one of them stole it before the battle.
26. **0:31:00 Rage.** Ignacio keeps the hood and mask on but **tears off his long sleeves and pulls his shorts even shorter, and his muscles burst through the seams**. His intimidation roll is a 2. Instead of being scared, **K-Boom admires the thighs** ("do you do quads?… I tell myself I'm gonna work out starting Monday"). Ignacio: "**I do Pilates.**"
27. **0:33:09 End of round.** The Arbiter rolls a 4. **Mort, now visiting the Grandmaster's pedestal with Doug** (Gerald presumably allowed it: "You're the boss… Thanks, Gerald"), gets the plush D20 and rolls a 14. The Arbiter: "**My self-worth is not tied to dice rolls.**… I can't control Mort. I can only control who I am."

### Round 2
28. **0:34:28** K-Boom twangs the javelins sticking out of the water, eyes the box ("fuck it, worth it"), and wades about 20 ft closer. "**You know what never misses? Magic missile!**" He taps his chest and **a Predator-style shoulder rocket appears and lays laser dots on Ignacio**. Four darts hit for 10 force. Ignacio: "Oh, you cryptocurrency son of a bitch." K-Boom: "**This was all natural, built in my basement after my father died… before I delved into crypto.**" He asks Ignacio to step 10 ft back from the box so he can use it. Ignacio backs off without needing a persuasion roll.
29. **0:37:14** Ignacio drops to one knee: "This attack is nothing compared to when I go pick up my javelins… But for now, when you go to this box, **say hi to Mort for me.**" He aims his crossbow at K-Boom's **right thigh** and rolls an 11, a miss. The Grandmaster begs and grants advantage. A 10, then a **21 that finally hits** (9 piercing). K-Boom offers to "T-pose for you… You're having a rough day."
30. **0:39:50** Ignacio climbs the snowy rocks for the high ground and rolls an 11 on athletics. **His "thunderous thighs" melt the snow and make it slick**, so he only reaches a ledge about 10 ft up, scraping his knee. He "**do[es] the Peter Griffin for four minutes**." The GM: it may become "a battle scar that many will swoon over."
31. **0:41:30** The Arbiter rolls a 17: "**Suck it Doug, suck it Mort.**" The pets hide behind the Grandmaster's chair. "Doug started this… **One day, Doug. As soon as I get free.**" The GM: "That's gonna just extend your sentence… I can imprison you for threatening my cute little animals." "That's not how the law works." "**I am the law!**"

### Round 3: chaos event (dodgeball)
32. **0:42:34** The Acolytes trigger a chaotic event. To make Ignacio "feel better about his boo-boo," the GM has him roll a d20 and triples it. The crowd grows mob-like, chants "**Give us blood!**", and starts **pelting the arena**. The things thrown:
    - swords, a bag of unpopped microwave popcorn, a fork, sticky spaghetti;
    - snowmen with weird noses, beer, raccoons, **an anchor** ("Who keeps an anchor?");
    - hot dogs, pre-chewed gum, Doritos, "whatever that blue cube is";
    - a whole bag of raw chicken, a frozen quarter-pound brick of ground beef, hot water, a football, and holiday presents ("Christmas has come early").
33. **0:44:57** **Ignacio rolls a nat 20** on the Dex save ("First Nat 20 of Ignacio's life"). He hops around dodging everything and **catches 48 cents in pennies, all 48 of them**. Someone throws down **a fish, which he eats for 3 HP**.
34. **0:45:52** K-Boom flounders in the water. **A fiery cake with a hundred candles lands on him** ("you do like arson, so it is all yours"), and **a seagull dives in and pecks him**. "Dodge everything but a ball… absolutely no balls thrown."
35. **0:46:43 K-Boom v the Box.** He pats out the flames and patches the beak bite ("This place sucks"). He offers the box **one whole K-Boom coin, "one of the first in existence… currently… worth 50,000 gold."** Gerald: "**I've only got 200,000 in my savings account. I'll take four.**" Gerald offers 200,000 gold for four coins, but K-Boom says he wants a cool item, not gold. Gerald: "I didn't realize how the deal was going… I'll be right back." **K-Boom rolls a nat 1 on persuasion** ("It's actually a zero"). **Someone inside locks the box and slides a deadbolt.** K-Boom kicks it. (Whether any coin changed hands isn't stated.)
36. **0:48:41** K-Boom mixes two vials, cigarette in his mouth. Ignacio gloats: "I put the monkey in, and it gave me a wheel of goodness, and you got shit. Eat it." Then: "**Whatever you're doing, please don't throw it.**" "Oh, you said throw it?" **He chucks the vial and flicks the cigarette after it, and they meet in midair as a Fireball.** He is only 15 ft away, so **he catches himself in the blast**. Ignacio saves (21) and takes 16. **K-Boom fails and takes 32 fire, losing his eyebrows** ("that's why I keep just the mustache because it grows back faster"). "K-Boom is not a smart man." GM: "**Fire bad.**" Arbiter: "Fire very bad, as it turns out."
37. **0:52:23 The healing water.** K-Boom dunks himself. **The lake water is magical and heals his burns (7 HP).** He fakes it: "**Oh God, the water, it burns.**" Ignacio's insight beats his deception, and he can see the wounds closing. K-Boom insists "it's infection… The skin is closing so fast… Don't get in the water."
38. **0:54:37** Ignacio wades right up: "**I think I'm going to five finger death punch you in the throat.**" K-Boom: "That band sucks, but okay." He has to **squat to reach the gnome's throat** (8 bludgeoning with rage). His **Intimidating Presence** fails against K-Boom's Wisdom save. K-Boom: "**This ain't the first time a devil's beat me down.**" Ignacio: "Even though you stand tall…" The GM: "Small correction, he stands short." Ignacio: "I wanted to be his dad for 2.5 seconds." The GM: "Now that you've lost Mort, you're now looking for a replacement child."
39. **0:57:46** Ignacio **cartwheels** away (athletics 20), so K-Boom's opportunity attack has disadvantage. K-Boom uses **War Caster** instead: he flicks a switch on his chest, **pauldrons open, and a swarm of daggers flies out as Cloud of Daggers**, centered on himself, while Ignacio is still in range. Ignacio takes 6 (resisted while raging): "**Thank you so much for the daggers. If they stay in me, I shall use them.**" K-Boom: "They're magical… they just kind of like, poof away."
40. **1:00:36** Ignacio **ducks under the water to wash the blood off his face** and heals 2. He **emerges "almost in a beautiful mermaid form," face clear and red eyes burning**, still inside the daggers.
41. **1:01:39** The Arbiter rolls a 10 ("It's average"). **Mort adds a 4** to make it 14. "**First a pug and now a fucking monkey.**" Break.

### Round 4 (after the break)
42. **1:02:30** "Welcome back, Acolyte, to the chaos." The Grandmaster has **a bad case of hiccups**. The Arbiter calls it round three, and Ignacio corrects him: "Number four!"
43. **1:02:49 K-Boom's combo.** His eyes are red, "not red from anger, red from tears, because he is crying on the inside." "**No more Mr. Nice Kaboom.**" He **chugs an energy drink and smashes the can on his forehead**. He **misty-steps 15 ft back, then sprints back in** so the dagger cloud around him slices Ignacio again (10). With a "maniacal crying laugh" ("eat shit"), he **grabs Ignacio's thighs underwater and casts Shocking Grasp in the water**. Both wear metal. Nature checks: Ignacio rolls a nat 20 and K-Boom a 19, so "Y'all know this is gonna be really painful." K-Boom: "**My middle name is Pain.**" Ignacio: "That's what my dad called me." (His real middle name: "**K-Michael-Boom**.") **Both take lightning damage, K-Boom half.** Blood flies.
44. **1:07:39** Even so, Ignacio stays "incredibly photogenic." **Fangirls in the stands post story after story on Instagram.** Ignacio: "Thank you so much, fans… **I'm about to end this crypto, bitch. Love you guys.**" He starts his turn in the daggers again ("I was getting the most out of this spell").
45. **1:08:39** Ignacio moves on top of K-Boom and makes a **frenzied attack**, three lance jabs at "**right arm, left leg, and left earlobe.**" **All three miss: 2, 2, 2.** **A slot-machine sound rings from the sky and gold coins rain into the water around him.** He spends his **hero point** to try again and hits with a 25. "**K-Boom, you made me do this.**" "Fuck you, man. I didn't make you do shit." "That's what you get for using a electric attack." K-Boom: "**You have a very internalized anger you have not resolved yet.**… Would you like some K-Boom coin?"
46. **1:11:20 Master Chief and Doug coin.** K-Boom calls the GM "Master Chief" again. GM: "**Master Chief does not rule in this domain. I am the Grandmaster. Get my name right. And it's Dogecoin with Doug's face on it that rules the day here.**" K-Boom: "**That is Doug coin. Doge coin is completely different.** You don't even know your own crypto… Mario, do you believe this?" **The crowd boos him.** Ignacio: "We made our mess and now we have to sit in it."
47. **1:12:11** A javelin does 8 piercing. Ignacio then fires his **crossbow point-blank at disadvantage (17 and 20). It's a glancing blow** for 4. "How did you enjoy that crossbow, buddy?" "I didn't at all, actually." K-Boom is now full of holes, like **a porcupine "on the wrong side."**
48. **1:14:07** The Arbiter rolls a 16: "**Fuck you, monkey… Both of them. I got two fingers.**" **Doug rolls a 17**: "He's not supposed to roll." "Doug rolls when he wants to roll."

### Round 5: return to monkey
49. **1:14:33** The Arbiter: "**This place is fucking stupid. If I wasn't imprisoned, I would quit.**"
50. **1:14:51** K-Boom: "Big old intimidating tough guy that gave you your monkey… **I'm going to give you a brand new monkey.** There's something my father once told me… **when in doubt… return to monkey.**" He drinks **a brown vial and casts Polymorph on himself, becoming a giant ape**. The dagger cloud dissipates into the water. He spends his **hero point for a second action** and goes "complete Kong style, **Donkey Kong down B**." The first fist misses against Ignacio's AC. **The second smashes down on Ignacio's right pinky toe** for 22 ("ooh, ooh, ooh"). The ground cracks and water seeps through.
51. **1:18:49** Ignacio pulls two javelins out of the water and **plunges them into the ape's chest**. Both hit. **K-Boom rolls a nat 1 on concentration** and reverts, shedding the ape "almost like skin," as "**your typical, now eyebrowless, K-Boom**." "Oh, hey there. I saw you met my friend." Ignacio: "I told you I was gonna get you." The ape's club name was "**K-Konga, the Crypto Monkey.**"
52. **1:21:27 The sack.** Ignacio: "**I'm going to take my lance and I'm going to put it right towards your sack.**" The GM: "You sounded like a magician… **Make this disappear.**" The Arbiter: "That's a low blow given his height." It misses with a 16, but the GM reminds him of **her inspiration die** ("How dare you forget"). He adds a 1, which **makes it a glancing blow to the nuts**.
53. **1:23:07** K-Boom: "**You sacrifice your monkey from Oz and you see me as a giant ape and you stab me.**… I turned into an ape because you got rid of your ape… I was trying to make you reminisce on your monkey." Ignacio: "You turn into an ape and beat the shit out of me." GM: "**If you can't respect yourself, how do you expect your opponent to respect you?**"
54. **1:23:44 The Arbiter's special chaotic event: the water weird.** The Arbiter's roll crosses the threshold. "I think it's time we encounter some of the local wildlife." **A water weird shoots up out of the deep dark pit behind Ignacio** and whips its tail to grapple him. **He resists (Strength 19).** The weird joins the initiative order.

### Round 6: the truce
55. **1:25:28** K-Boom: "I'm going to fuck off, and you can fuck off with me." Ignacio: "**Let's both fuck off. Let's run together, man.**" K-Boom: "**Enemy of my enemy is my friend.**" Ignacio: "**Until we kill each other, but that'll be later.**"
57. **1:27:29** Ignacio takes the **Disengage** action and dashes 40 ft toward the rowboat. **The water weird swims 60 ft and catches right up to him** anyway. Its tail grab misses.
58. **1:30:10** End of the round. "We're out of [round] cards now… **We gotta get a new round card guy, because he can only count to seven.**" K-Boom: "How did he manage becoming a god if he can't ca— This place is so messed up!"

### Round 7: the double death
59. **1:30:33** K-Boom gives Ignacio "a half-ass thumbs up. **You got this, man!**" Then he runs away, lights a cigarette ("oh God, oh God"), and **tosses two vials behind his back that connect in midair: a 4th-level Fireball centered on Ignacio**, catching the water weird too. Ignacio fails his save; the weird saves. The GM: "Have we not played basic Pokémon? Fire is weak to water." **45 fire takes Ignacio to 0 HP.**
60. **1:32:29 Ignacio's dying testament.** "**Even though I fall short and Water Weird kind of entered my game, it has been a great battle. I wish you all the cryptocurrency non-luck. I hope you lose all your stock… But I do wish you a long and healthy life, brother… at least I'll be with Mort now. I love you, and I always have, even though you killed me.** I thought our relationship would grow into something so beautiful that neither of us would be able to stop that love or passion."
61. **1:33:21** K-Boom: "**Wait! Don't go into the light yet!**" The GM: "It's the guy who cast the light." K-Boom **slides down the rocks and holds the burning Ignacio in his arms** ("this kind of hurts a little bit"). "**Hey, we could have been friends. We really could have… even in your death, you will be remembered. Because I have a new line of cryptocurrency coming out. They're called NF trinkets.**" Ignacio: "Oh god, fucking kill me now, please." K-Boom shows him a card he drew during the water weird fight: **a stick figure of Ignacio as an NFT, with stats: "Internalized rage, really depressed, probably has daddy issues."** "They hit the market tomorrow."
62. **1:34:29** Ignacio's last words: "**From now on, K-Boom, your name is K-Boom Marchand.**" K-Boom drops him back in the water and starts pitching to the crowd: "**K-Boom coin on sale now… kboomgetsthatbread at hotmailfirefox.com.**"
63. **1:34:50** Mid-spiel, **the water weird's tail whips up out of the water** for 19 bludgeoning. As K-Boom tries to shield his cryptocurrency, **the tail wraps around both of them and drags both bodies down into the pit**. They sink into the blackness. **Both champions die. There is no winner.**
64. **1:35:25** High above, **Mort sheds a small tear** and his lower lip trembles. The Grandmaster holds him: "**It's okay, Mort. You'll be reunited one day.**" Then **she kicks over the new NFT trinkets and stamps all over them.** "**Arbiter. Clean up this mess.**"
65. **1:35:58** The Arbiter launches into the sky, his **wings glowing in the reflection off the snow**, and **dives headfirst into the deep water to recover the bodies**.
66. **1:36:13 The closing.** Mort on her shoulder, the Grandmaster rises on the water "as if she's wearing one of those cool hydro jet packs." Among the Acolytes she says: "Two champions… not only fought their hardest and not only fought to their death, but both sacrificed their lives in a series of some very smart and some very dumb moves. **You got the variety pack tonight.**" She holds **Mort up Simba-style**: "Tell this to your little ones so they may be **the memory keepers** of tonight… so that way we never be forgotten… **tell them the story of K-Boom and Ignacio and Mort**." Mort's wings open and he spirals into the sky throwing confetti. "**Welcome and good night to D20 Deathmatch!**"

## Who and what appears
### Characters
**K-Boom** (BrandontheDM). **Gnome artificer, aka Kyle Boomishnow, aka Kaboom; his real middle name is "Michael" ("K-Michael-Boom").**
- About 3'2" ("doctors say 3'1.5"). Long robe of trinkets, vials and gears over plate armor. Smokes constantly. **Loses his eyebrows to his own fireball** and keeps his mustache.
- **Loves explosives, cigarettes, cryptocurrency and arson.** He founded **K-Boom Coin**, claimed to be worth 50,000 gold each, and is launching **"NF trinkets"**, with an Ignacio NFT. A self-described sleazy salesman and shill.
- His father died in a house fire K-Boom accidentally caused, and he lost his family young to an arson accident. His father's sayings: "when in doubt… return to monkey."
- His gadgetry was "built in my basement after my father died":
  - a chest switch that opens pauldrons of daggers (Cloud of Daggers);
  - a Predator-style shoulder rocket (magic missile);
  - vial-plus-cigarette fireballs;
  - a brown vial for Polymorph (giant ape, "K-Konga, the Crypto Monkey").
- Has War Caster. Keeps calling the Grandmaster "Master Chief."
- The Box of Chaos locked itself against him.

**Ignacio Marchade** (Karim Cheese). Raging barbarian.
- Deep voice, deep-set hood, cloth mask, **glowing red eyes that make Acolytes swoon**. Wears contact lenses; one was stolen by a fangirl.
- Fights with javelins, a lance and a crossbow. Does Pilates. His dad called him "Pain."
- Mort's owner; sacrificed Mort to the Box and got the **Wheel of Stars**.
- At death he declared his love for K-Boom and renamed him "K-Boom Marchand." Died "but my soul lives on" (Dried Blood).

**Mort**. **A monkey with wings that Ignacio gave him** (taken by K-Boom to be a Wizard of Oz monkey; Ignacio denies it). Found in the Appalachian Mountains.
- Sacrificed to the Box of Chaos. He then hangs out with Doug on the Grandmaster's pedestal and **rolls the plush D20 for the Arbiter's end-of-round rolls**.
- Promised his own bedroom. Wept at the double death, was held up Simba-style, and flew off throwing confetti.

**Doug**. The Grandmaster's tiny round pug, who appears when his name is said.
- Befriends Mort and shares a toy with him. Rolls a 17 on his own. "Doug rolls when he wants to roll."
- The Arbiter threatens him ("One day, Doug").

**Gerald / the Box of Chaos** (Drew). Pink and purple, with shadowy fingers and yellow eyes.
- Took Mort ("I kinda wanted that thing").
- Has "200,000 in my savings account." Offered to buy four K-Boom coins, then **deadbolted the box from inside** after K-Boom's nat 1.

**The water weird**. Rises from the lake's deep pit as the Arbiter's special chaos event. Speed 60 in water. **Dragged both champions' bodies into the pit.**

**The Arbiter** (Drew).
- **Currently imprisoned. His hands are shackled, and he wears Italian leather boots awarded for good behavior in his prison cell.**
- Now does the grocery shopping. Running anger-management mantras ("I am centered… I am the best me"; "My self-worth is not tied to dice rolls").
- Has memorized Independence Day ("he also has it memorized," Dried Blood).
- Recovered the bodies from the lake.

**Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer** (Bec).
- Runs goblin band tryouts. Gave both champions a d4 inspiration. "I am the law!"
- Claims "Dogecoin with Doug's face on it" rules here (Doug coin). Stomped the NFT trinkets.
- Had hiccups after the break.

**Goblin band**: five goblins, three on cowbell and a guitarist. **Kyle** (guitarist, his own guitar) and **Sarah** (cowbell solo, "a star"; her day job is at the Dread Fortress).

**The round card guy**: can only count to seven (an unseen arena helper, as stated).
