## What happens
### Opening: the Dread Fortress and the five thrones
1. **0:00:10** The show opens with the Arbiter out of breath ("Not a young man anymore"). The GM asks how old he is. **Arbiter: "four, four and a half thousand years old."** She thought he'd say four and a half *years*, and muses about celebrating his birthday ("but who cares?"). Drew: "**Despite the fact that he does have an age, he's also timeless and has no birthday.**" Asked where he came from: "Don't ask questions."
2. **0:00:49** In the halls of Elusia Skypiercer in **the Dread Fortress**, **the Arbiter walks the hallway with chains clanking behind him, "a prisoner in this domain now of the Grandmaster's."** He follows the commotion to the giant wooden doors of the **throne room** and peeks in.
3. **0:01:40** The GM says she sent a raven hours ago because she needs his opinion. He asks her to remove the chains so he can move faster. She refuses: "just get stronger… You got like weak little chicken legs." The chains are "a gift… like ankle weights," and he should do sprints up the staircase. "**It's like you skipped leg day for those 400,000 whatever years you've been alive.**"
4. **0:02:29 The thrones.** **Five different thrones stand at the front of the throne room**, because she couldn't decide which to get:
   - **A throne made of swords.** She finds it "a little too overdone… I feel like I've seen that before somewhere." The Arbiter calls it garish.
   - **A purple velvet high throne reaching to the sky with a diamond sun at the top.** Regal, but it makes her look short. Arbiter: "that diamond says I'm richer than you are and I know it."
   - Three more of varying size, colour and gaudiness.

   **The poor goblins** keep shifting the thrones around so she can compare them. She wants to **put it to a vote of the Acolytes**: "They have good taste in everything." The Arbiter mocks trusting "the common man… they're not uncommon." She insists: "**Only the most amazing individuals worship me.**"
5. **0:04:26** She decides it's time for a Deathmatch, after which the Acolytes can vote on the chair. The Arbiter claps; **sound waves visibly ripple** as he rips open a portal and introduces "Verryn Ruth versus Captain Fable."
6. **0:05:18 Captain Fable's entrance.** "Seems to be that I have wandered upon this strange land. However, **if the gods want me to play their games once more, then I must abide if I wish to slay them all.** I raise my sword in this individual's name and blood will be shed."
   - He is **an eight-foot minotaur in pirate attire** with a **necromantic, deathly aura**.
   - He was a paladin **"until the gods screwed them over"**, and carries a chip on his shoulder.
   - He is **a fortune teller and storyteller who loves to tell fables**: "we will see whether this fable will be that of someone's death, or even theirs."
7. **0:06:40 Verryn Ruth's entrance.** **A tiefling man struts forward, his hooves clacking on the cobblestone "like a couple of coconuts."** To the GM: "**It is a blessing to look upon you, Grandmaster. Your visage is fabled.**"
   - He calls himself "**but a humble, forlorn soul**", "**thirsty for glory**."
   - He fights with "**Only that which my patron bestows upon me… blessed by the shadow.**"
   - Matt reveals he is a **hexblade warlock**. The table mocks this ("Tieflings are never warlocks").
   - **He stretches out bat-like wings, then folds them back into his back.** The GM: "Beautiful, majestic, love it."
8. **0:08:57 The throne question.** The GM asks each champion which throne he would sit on.
   - **Fable** wants a throne of riches, skulls and "lots and lots of alcohol," **on a ship "worthy of sailing all of the seas"** rather than in a castle. The Arbiter asks where she'd get a ship this time of year. She'd have **the goblins build one, "like a cute little treehouse."** The Arbiter: "**You saw how the kitchen renovation came out.**" She loves the idea of **a built-in bar in the throne**. Fable says a major image or hallucinatory terrain would also do: "I'm not hard to please."
   - **Verryn casts Minor Illusion and holds up a bust of Doja Cat**: "Do you have any thrones that look like this?" The GM: "**It's magnificent! Why have I been missing this? This is an immediate choice.**" **Several goblins start drooling.** Fable: "Is this some strange divine power?"
   - The GM orders the goblins to cover it, take a picture, and **sculpt one like it**.
9. **0:11:16** The GM: "**Arbiter, we all know the only throne you'll have is a toilet.**" Arbiter: "**I'll have a throne and I'll make it out of your bones.**" Annoyed that Verryn was showing off his wings, the Arbiter **flares one of his giant glowing white wings** to beckon them into the portal.

### The arena: the swamp cathedral
10. **0:11:36** They step into dank, musky air: **the ruins of an old cathedral in a swamp, long overgrown.** The GM: "Know that this place is long forgotten, but you'll never forget the bodies that decay here."
    - It "**smells like farts and death everywhere.**"
    - Feet sink a couple of inches, and **rib cages and skulls pop up**. Wet socks for anyone without waterproof shoes.
    - **Old, fragile tombstones make good rocks** for hitting people.
    - **Ten-foot ruined walls** give cover but aren't stable enough to walk on.
11. **0:13:13** The GM's wings clear the stench for a moment as she flies up above **the green fog that devours the land**. High in the sky, **colourful, brightly decorated Acolytes scream the champions' names and wave flags.** "Make sure that it's worth the memory." **Doug jumps up to join her on her temporary throne, the Doja Cat bust**, and sits at her feet.
12. **0:14:28 Placement.** Fable starts near graves whose headstones have been pushed out by years of overgrowth ("assuredly there are bodies beneath your feet"). Verryn starts behind a wall with half cover.
13. **0:15:40 Initiative:** Fable 14, Verryn 8. The Arbiter flies to the stands, where Acolytes spill beer and shovel in popcorn and cotton candy, **pointing at the field and telling their children "someday this could be you."** It's "a family-friendly event"; "these are very mature kids." Matt: "They'll need therapy later in life, like the rest of us."
14. **0:16:49 The fog.** **The Arbiter flies around the arena at lightning pace and stirs the low fog up to waist height.** From the waist down, nobody can see where they're stepping.

### Round 1
15. **0:17:38 Fable's question.** "I ask this all to who face the end of my blade. **Where exactly does your strength come from?**"
    - Verryn: "**I pull from the abyss, from chaos itself. Its whims carry to and fro, as do mine.**"
    - Fable asks whether he draws on the foul entities that seek to control him. Verryn: "They seek to control, but they're quite poor at it. They give me their power and I do as I will."
    - Fable: "**Then you are a fool for accepting such power. It is not strength, but it's strings attached to a puppet.** If I need to show you true strength, I will beat it out of you."
16. **0:19:13 Divine Sense.** Electricity coagulates in Fable's eyes as he scans for undead ("In a graveyard?"). **He senses something moving under the ground, worming its way under the moss**, hidden in the fog.
17. **0:19:56 Spirit Shroud and the presidents.** Fable moves 25 ft toward it. A small thunderstorm forms above him as he **drags his massive buster sword through the dirt**, and a necrotic shroud forms over him: **Spirit Shroud**. **Skeleton arms writhe out of the ground and grasp at his feet.**
    - The GM: the dead crawling up are well dressed, in clothes from many time periods. **The closest has wooden teeth, and its tombstone reads George Washington.** Andrew Jackson lies further down, and "**Abraham Lincoln, the vampire hunter**," is a couple of rows over ("Zombie Abraham").
    - Fable: "**I do not know your name, nor your presidential history. Regardless, you will aid me in this fight whether you like it or not.**"
    - The GM suggests **Taft, the one who got stuck in the bathtub**, "if you're looking for one with some girth." The fat "did not dissipate around his hips"; he has "**bathtub-shaped bones**" and **giant hip bones**.
    - Fable ends his turn glaring at "the patron-worshipping individual."
18. **0:23:00 Verryn takes to the air.** He raises an eyebrow at the "hoisting of the presidents," **unfurls his wings and flies straight up 30 ft** above the fog. To the Acolytes: "**Is this what you wanted? Two ruthless individuals tearing each other limb from limb for your enjoyment, for your capitalist endeavors? Then you shall have it.**"
19. **0:23:53 "Bitch, I'm a cow."** **Minor Illusion makes the voice "Bitch, I'm a cow. Bitch, I'm a cow" come from Fable's direction.** Verryn: "Seems my opponent has different ideas."
    - Fable: "**What the hell is this? Damned tricks… I'm not a cow. I don't even know what I am. The gods took that from me as well.** Stop saying. I have feelings, you know."
    - The GM awards a d4 of **emotional damage** ("Emotional damage!"). **It's 1.** The Arbiter: "the cold grasp of emotional damage spiking through your brain."
20. **0:25:44 End of round 1.** The Arbiter's roll is a 10 ("good enough"). Half the Acolytes cheer for each champion, so **both get a hero point**.

### The Box of Chaos arrives
21. **0:26:19** The Arbiter swirls the fog into a 10–20 ft clearing, and **the Box of Chaos slams into the sludge with a squish.** The lid flips open, **pink and yellow flames shoot a column to the sky**, two dark shadowy hands creep over the side, and **yellow-eyed Gerald peeks out.** The GM: "this is Gerald… he will grant you something magical if you give him something."

    - Fable: "**I am not a cow. I am a bull. Fear me.**" He fails.

Isn't that right, you piece of shit?**" He says it while looking at Doug. The GM: "Don't speak to Doug that way… I thought you'd be more welcoming compared to how many mean words have always been spoken to you."

They're short.**" **He heals Verryn 10 HP**: "That's the most kind words he could come up with." He adds that **the children's moms "are probably very promiscuous."**
29. **0:33:26 The mom.** **One mom in the crowd winks back, "just waiting for her opportunity."** The Arbiter: "**It's that one mom that's really into my chains.**" **Verryn rips his top a little to show a tattoo reading "MOMS ONLY."** **She blows him a kiss, granting +2 HP.**

Gerald: "**Ah, well, it's not a gun, but it'll fuck someone up.**" **He floats over the Far Realm Shard, a crystal that can tear a slimy tentacle through reality to strike and frighten a creature.** ("Avert the children's eyes!" [Verryn], fight me."

### Round 3
40. **0:56:25** The Arbiter recaps: "trading quips, acquiring codpieces, and other stuff."

    - He charges javelins with electricity. "You skeleton, fire!" A Commander's Strike sends Taft's arrow first ("**He is only as good as his crew**"), but it misses.
    - **The javelin whiffs completely.** Fable: "I'm not suited for this aerial battle… **Come to the ground and face me if this is your true strength.**"
42. **0:58:45 The mushroom.** Fable wanders into a mushroom's area. **The mushroom seems to grow, but he is shrinking: one size smaller for a turn.**
    - Still raging: "**Fight me! I'll take you all on! Screw the gods!**" He flips everything off.
    - The table: "It's finally a minotaur!" The GM: "**minitaurs are also a chaotic event in and of themselves.**"
    - **Taft picks him up and holds him aloft in one hand while Fable bellows "CHALLENGE!"**
43. **1:01:16 Summon Shadowspawn.** Verryn cocks his head and mutters about the "very small cow" on the ground. "**It's a fight face to face, is it? Tell me, Fable. How do you deal with [shadow] daemons?**"
    - He points at Fable's shadow, and **a shadowspawn rises from it in roughly Fable's shape**, smoke-like, flowing in and out of form. It is also mini.
    - Then **Verryn dive-bombs to the ground beside Fable** ("like Thor").
    - Tiny Fable: "**I'll cut your wings off and I'll shove it down your throat.**" "It's so cute when he's angry." "Don't make fun of me."
44. **1:04:25 Despair, and Fable's past.** Verryn chooses **despair** for the spawn: it reaches into Fable's innermost sadness, regrets and baggage, and slows anyone near it. The GM: "What just scared you, Fable?"

    - **He called out to the goddess of the sea and the god of the storm, and to every pantheon. They all failed him.**
    - **That hatred and despair at losing his crew fuels his path of revenge**: "**I will kill every god that will do it.**"
    - **The Acolytes bawl, and their salty tears rain down on the arena.** The skeleton does a rain dance.
45. **1:06:45 Chilling Rend.** The spawn's first strike misses; **the second hits for 16**, and Fable's fear responses explode inside his brain.
    - Fable sees the spawn hurting too: "**You strike me, yes, you don't even know… your power and how it works. The things that you seek, the things that you control, they still damage you from within. You'll pay for this, insolence boy.**"
    - Verryn: "**Good, you remind me I'm alive.**" One of them: "That's why we hurt. **We bleed to know we're alive.**" The GM: "Feels like we could start an emo band any minute now."
46. **1:09:44 End of round 3: the plush d20s.** The Arbiter rolls 11 after two 20s.
    - **Doug hops up with his plushy d20, rolls it and ties**, then **gives the Arbiter a matching plush d20 squeaky toy.**
    - **The Arbiter rips it in half, rewinds time so it's whole again, and rips it again, over and over in front of Doug.**

    - The Arbiter: "**Looks like I'm taking a trip to the basement.**" He winks at the mom ("That's the Arbiter's pickup line"). **On his persuasion roll she swoons, and her girlfriends catch her.**

### Round 4

49. **1:14:19 Thunderous Smite.** "He loves his thunder, he loves his lightning." He raises the sword skyward: "**Fear me!**" **The second swing misses and reverberates off the codpiece "like a bell."** Fable realises casting the smite dropped his Spirit Shroud, which still adds necrotic damage to the earlier hit.

    - **Taft throws down his bow and swings his short sword/scimitar, slashing at Verryn's leg to bring him to a knee.** The aura adds damage because Taft is undead and next to Fable.
52. **1:18:56** The GM reminds both champions to use their hero points: "**The only thing that we hate is a hero point gone unused at the battle's end.**"

    - **With his hero point he draws energy "from the crowd and the chaos gods that have given me power"** for two greatsword attacks.
    - The Acolytes scream his name and beg for the darkness. They watch through **special glasses, like 3D glasses**. The Arbiter: "**The entire Dread Fortress in the arena is like 4D, so they've got those little air jets and like water spritzers behind their seats**." "Every time blood splatters, they get like water in their face."
    - Fable: "**The darkness in my heart is enough.**" He can't see.
    - **Verryn's eyes flick a different colour, as if swapping lenses**, with **a night-vision-goggle sound effect** ("All of my years of Call of Duty"; the Arbiter: "Enemy spotted in your area"). He can see in the magical darkness, so he gets advantage.
54. **1:24:05 The exchange.** Pacing, Verryn: "**Do you feel it, Fable? Your insecurities, your doubts, the weights of all of your past.**"
    - Fable: "**I don't think you understand. They took everything from me. My name, my crew, my life. I have no past, but my future will be forged in blood. Yours.**"

    - **The second hits** for 14 "**sadness damage**," plus the hexblade curse bonus, which Fable reminds him of honestly ("I might be chaotic evil, but I have a little bit of, you know, integrity." The GM: "**A bit. Just a few inches worth.**").
55. **1:28:21 The Arbiter's chaos: the bats.** The Arbiter rolls 18, and the GM lets him bring his own chaos.
    - **He raises his hands, and bats pour from the trees and fog, flapping and pinging. They swarm the champions and dive-bomb in and out of the darkness.**
    - Verryn can see them; Fable can't. "**Damned things, get away from me. What is even attacking me? I can't see in this domain.**"
    - "**Beware of the batnado!**" Fable takes 6 and Verryn 3.

56. **1:31:16 Taft's eulogy.** Fable looks at **the fallen crewmate's little pirate hat on the ground**: "**You fought well and bravely, soldier. Even though I think you only hit once, but that's perfectly fine. You will be remembered.**" A bat smacks him in the face: "All right, now I'm angry."

### Round 5: the kill
58. **1:34:02 Fable's hero point.** Surrounded by darkness and bats, Fable looks at his fallen comrade, and **memories of all his men dying wash over him.**
    - **Lightning surges across the ground. Skeletal and zombified hands burst out, grab Verryn where he stands, and chain him to the ground.**
    - "**Men to arms. This fool worships the gods, worships devils, doesn't matter. His strength calls from a higher power. It is time to show him the true strength, strength in unity. Die by my blade, boy, and kneel before me.**" He aims for the head.
59. **1:36:17 The first swing** hits, but **Armor of Hexes makes it miss**. Verryn: "Varen sees the sword coming… you hear just like a… taunt, making you second guess yourself… **'oh, he's not there, he's over here'**… you end up in mid swing, changing your direction and going through air. And you hear like a small little cackle in your head."
60. **1:37:21 The second swing.** Fable shuts out every thought ("**Death and Oblivion**") and swings again.
    - **It hits, with Aura of Hatred and Thunderous Smite.**
    - **The GM gives Verryn her blessing on the Strength save**: "Be strong. You have the Grandmaster's blessing." It doesn't help. **He is knocked prone.**
    - **A booming thunderclap echoes, and lightning strikes the blade "almost kind of like Thor," embedding it in Verryn's neck and clavicle.**
    - **The divine light dispels the magical darkness.** Verryn falls, conscious but dying.
