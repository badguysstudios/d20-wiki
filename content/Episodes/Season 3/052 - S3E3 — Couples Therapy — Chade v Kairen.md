Most champions come to the Void to kill a stranger. **Chade (Chaedillion) Chadian** and his wife **Kairen** came to work on their marriage. [[Doug the Pug]] pulled the couple out of their home life and dropped them into a lava-filled ruin beneath [[The Dread Fortress|the Dread Fortress]]. What followed was less a fight to the death than a very public counseling session, with flaming swords, a velvet rope from [[The Box of Chaos|Gerald]], and three angry stone copies of the neighbor who bakes the scones.

## The Story

### Kitchen Renovations

The episode opened on chaos inside the Dread Fortress. [[Goblins]] scurried through the halls carrying two-by-fours, drywall and hammers "that goblins probably shouldn't be allowed to have," all headed for the fortress kitchen, "a place we've seen a person die once or twice." [[The Arbiter]] followed, chains dragging behind him, and asked what was going on. [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|The Grandmaster]] reminded him there had been an email memo: "Obviously, we're doing kitchen renovations right now." Then she noticed the goblins were knocking down a load-bearing wall, and the ceiling began to crack.

With the sink overflowing into the pool in the middle of the kitchen, the Grandmaster whistled. Doug popped out of the water soaking wet and shook himself off. She asked him to search the worlds for champions who really needed "to get the fight out of their system." She added that if he found a good enough pairing, "perhaps you could actually replace the arbiter." Doug spun after his tail. The moment he caught it, a small portal opened and two champions tumbled out.

"Doug summoned the champions?" the Arbiter asked. "Yes, yes. Shut up. Just shut up," said the Grandmaster.

### Grievances

The first words out of the portal were Chade's: "Honey, when I said we should go to therapy, this is not exactly what I had in mind." Chade was a huge, square-jawed, mustachioed half-orc in full armor with an enormous greatsword, all of it under a pink apron. He was surprised the armor still fit after years of chasing the kids. Kairen, an elf, had been promised lavender and potpourri, and found the room "a little too red for my whole psyche."

The Grandmaster welcomed them anyway: "It's not therapy, but it is an opportunity to work out your problems. Welcome to the Dread Fortress." The Arbiter doubted Doug's picks were up to a Deathmatch, so she went fishing for grievances, and they came pouring out. Kairen thought Chade was a fantastic stay-at-home husband, "definitely arm candy," but wished he would earn a little gold, since she brought in most of it. Chade pointed out that they had seven children. He had asked her to do one thing, pick up asparagus at the mushroom market, and she forgot after a night out with friends. Kairen countered with the 3,000-gold cooking knives she had paid for so he could slice mushrooms for his tofu. Chade said she had just raided a dragon's hoard, so he thought they could splurge.

Kairen wondered whether this was "one of those smash rooms." Once the Grandmaster understood what that meant, she said they had one. The Arbiter perked up: "Objections rescinded. I think they're going to kill each other."

### The Smash Room

The Arbiter clapped, and the sound tore open a portal to a ruined room full of lava. The heat frizzed Kairen's hair and singed Chade's mustache. A brick path crumbled at its edges into lava fifteen feet below, and ten-foot statues lined the way. "Remember, lava hot, don't touch," the Grandmaster warned. Kairen immediately eyed a statue for the front of their hut. "Even if you break it, it's still mine," said the Grandmaster. "Please don't take my stuff home."

The champions misunderstood what kind of "smashing" was expected. Chade asked whether this was about baby number eight, then turned to [[The Acolytes]]: "Would you like to see us smash one another?" He got awkward silence and one enthusiastic yes from a guy in the back. The Grandmaster went red, wrapped her wings around herself, clarified "with weapons, not with the other stabby things," and retreated to her throne to drink.

That left the Arbiter as host. He confided that he had tried to overthrow her, "with resurrected champions that I had brought back to life," which explained the chains. Kairen decided he must be the manager. They talked decor. The Arbiter had placed the skulls himself. Were they ethically sourced? "They're probably free range."

Before the fight, the couple sorted out the children. If Chade died, Kairen would strap the kids to her chest and take them adventuring. Chade noted that one of them couldn't walk. Then he mentioned the neighbor: "It's Margaret, by the way. She's wonderful… She makes great blueberry scones." Kairen's reply came instantly: "You're eating some other woman's scones?" She made him promise that if she died, the ring stayed on: "No Margaret, no blueberry scones with any other woman." Chade folded his apron neatly and set it in a corner so it wouldn't get bloody. It had been a gift.

Kairen struck first. As a test of their partnership, the Grandmaster asked each of them for the other's middle name. Chade knew hers by heart: Kairen Alexis Rose Chadian. Asked for his, Kairen shrugged: "Maybe he should ask Margaret."

### Rocks, Fire and Apples

Kairen's opening move was to sit down and dig through the rubble for rocks. She hurled one into her husband's handsome jaw. He spat blood: "Throwing stones from your glass house over there? Come on, get over here!" She kept sifting, finding one that looked like the pet bunny Chade had cooked. He protested that he didn't even eat meat, and had cooked it for her.

Chade circled the gap, not foolish enough to cross the lava, and set his greatsword ablaze. Kairen thanked him for keeping her hair from frizzing. When he boasted that he still had it, she reminded him he "could last a little longer than your dash action did." The arguments grew more personal. Chade admitted to buying a toy at a lady's party as an anniversary present, which explained the money missing from their joint account.

Kairen tried to pick his pocket for the gift, and he caught her. She tried to slap him, but she's a little anemic. She slipped on one of her own rocks and fell face first into his chest, felt his gym-built pecs, and bit one. Then Chade landed the first real blow. Pleading about seven children, lice and asparagus, he brought down his flaming greatsword with holy fire stacked on top, and **Kairen caught fire**. "Well, honey, you're looking a little extra hot today." As her hair singed, he shouted, "Yes! Now take me seriously!"

### Doug, Chinchillas and Gerald

At the end of the round the Arbiter fumbled his roll for chaos, but Doug hopped up, threw down his squeaky toy and **rolled a natural 20**. Chade asked if he was a zombie dog. "It's undead," the Arbiter corrected. Chade said Doug looked like the family cat after one of the kids practiced necromancy on it. Doug got huffy and trotted back to the Grandmaster. "He's more of a Margaret person," Kairen sniffed.

The Acolytes demanded chaos. A drilling sound came from the ceiling, and a giant plastic ball holding 203 [[Chinchillas|chinchillas]] dropped between the couple. Each had a tiny knife strapped to its back, and a lead chinchilla rode in a chariot, smoking a cigarette. They were unionized. "We're unionized here at the Dread Fortress," the Grandmaster said. "Oh, that's why the kitchen's not done yet," said Chade. The ball rolled back and forth and missed both of them, and then a whistle blew and half the chinchillas went to lunch. When Chade reminded Kairen she could dodge, the Arbiter asked how it felt to be told how to do things. She realized it was mansplaining.

Then the Arbiter snapped, and the Box of Chaos dropped beside a broken statue, spilling pink and yellow fire. Gerald waited inside to trade for any sacrifice. Kairen forbade Chade from giving up the anniversary toy, and he refused to give up the apron she had given him when their first child, Kaylee, was born. So she persuaded him to give up his **trousers**.

Kairen got so flustered watching him hop out of them that her rapier slipped from her hand and dangled at the edge of the path. He offered to fetch it for her ("She's my wife, of course I am"), and she ignored it to help him finish undressing. Underneath was a split thong with a large smiling goblin face on the front. Chade jogged to the box and did lunges in front of Gerald. Gerald asked why his pants were off, took them, and burst from the box in a column of fire: "Sorry, I have to make an entrance. It's kind of part of my job." Then he gave them **a red velvet rope of climbing**, with a word of advice: "You guys seem kind of kinky, so here."

Somebody asked whether they'd met in Cassandra's spin class, and Kairen exploded: "WHO THE FUCK'S CASSANDRA?!" Chade bellowed that she was his spin instructor: "YOU LIKE THIS ASS? THANK HER!" The Grandmaster called a break to cool off.

### The Arbiter Exposed

After the break, a burst of hot air lifted the Arbiter's potato-sack robe in a Marilyn Monroe moment. Twice that night the whole audience got a look, and he responded by casting enlarge on himself. "A minute's all I'm going to need," he said. The Grandmaster was unimpressed: "Enlarge is not the spell you want if you need to satisfy someone, unless it's just yourself." She added that [[Timothy|Mayor Timmy]], the worm mayor of "that one asteroid we landed on," had been much bigger. A new chaos event scattered five wrinkly, one-eyed sausage worms around the arena that sucked at anyone near them like leeches. The Grandmaster explained they had replaced the usual small grandmas, because there would be too much shame.

Kairen finally put out her own fire. Chade had never mentioned she could, and she pointed out that he hadn't read her spell for her. She grabbed him from behind and wouldn't let go until he flexed free. Then Chade spoke the rope's command, "Hocus pocus. Tie this woman up so I can focus!", and it bound her head to toe. He asked if that meant he won. It didn't.

Tied up, Kairen wriggled and shook until Chade was visibly suffering. He turned the flat of his greatsword into a paddle and smote her backside for a blow the table dubbed "the crit clap." He checked in on their safe word, which turned out to be mango, not pineapple. "Mango, oh no, it's great, keep them coming."

### Three Margarets

The Arbiter had had enough. "I'm fucking sick of this," he declared. He cancelled their chance to redecorate the kitchen and snapped his fingers. The statues broke apart and re-formed as **three stone Margarets**, built from Chade's own memories, and they marched up behind the couple. Chade released his wife ("Own it, moan it, release my wife this moment"). He promised they would destroy every Margaret visage, "Not the real one though. She's a nice fucking lady."

Kairen stabbed the first Margaret in a rage, and it burst into radiant fire. Chade spent his healing touch rubbing her bruised backside ("Aftercare is important, people"), while Gerald cheered from outside the box and tossed over a wooden paddle. Kairen stabbed the second Margaret with the still-buzzing purple massager, which vibrated so hard the statue shattered. The last Margaret attacked with her long, swinging stone breasts and offered buttery scones. Chade explained that Margaret was 86. Kairen accused him of liking older women. He reminded her she was an elf, and that he would die thousands of years before her.

Chade swore a vow against the last statue and cut deep, but it held. Then, with the Acolytes' favor behind her, Kairen leapt to Margaret's head and **crushed it between her thighs**. She finished with a wrestling flip on a natural 20, landed nimbly, and sent the rubble into the lava.

### Off to the Real Smash Room

By now Chade's goblin thong had ripped, and Kairen wrapped her cloak around him: "that's just for me." The Grandmaster finally broke: "Enough! Arbiter! Send them to their bedroom, please!" The Arbiter clapped open a portal to the Dread Fortress's actual Smash Room, with tables, paddles, swings, chains and, yes, lava. Kairen thanked him for fixing their marriage, and the Arbiter took psychological damage. The couple went through, and the Acolytes cheered and threw condoms while parents covered their children's eyes.

Nobody died, so the match ended as a **shared victory**. The Grandmaster sent them off wishing them "merriment and maybe an eighth child," and urged everyone to back their Kickstarter for the dragon-slaying, schooling and asparagus to come.

## Dried Blood

At the table afterward, the players declared Margaret canon: the nice old neighbor who brings scones while the wife is off adventuring. The Arbiter's player suggested this might be the first child ever conceived in the Dread Fortress and floated a commemorative plaque. He also confirmed the Arbiter is now psychologically damaged, and that his roll had made the Arbiter's endowment one inch, canon from then on. The Grandmaster thanked goodness for Doug, who always outrolls the Arbiter, and hinted that chinchillas might become a small theme for the season. The kitchen was promised a backsplash of human skulls ("Don't ask us where we got them from"). The words of wisdom were to communicate, try therapy, establish a safe word, and remember that they "do not condone domestic violence."
