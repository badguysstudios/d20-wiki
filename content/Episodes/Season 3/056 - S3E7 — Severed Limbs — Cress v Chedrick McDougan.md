## What happens
### Opening — the Dread Fortress kitchen
1. **0:00:01** The Arbiter opens the show and says he's "feeling a little lightheaded today… Might pass out in my chair." The Grandmaster: "**Oh, you're always lightheaded. It's easy when you have no brain cells.**"
2. **0:00:28** The camera moves through the halls of **the Dread Fortress, "deep in the subterranean castle."** The stone corridors are cool to the touch. **Paintings of the Grandmaster line the walls** and goblins scurry about. **The Arbiter walks toward the kitchen with "chains still clanking off of his ankles and his wrists,"** and finds a frenzy inside.
3. **0:01:04 — The charcuterie board.** The Grandmaster is making pre-show snacks. Normally there are blue raspberry slushies, popcorn and cotton candy, but this time she wants to "elevate it" with **a charcuterie board**. She doesn't know what goes on one, so **she has had the goblins add random items**:
   - slices of rat;
   - **berries "that Thomas picked from the back bush behind the castle"**;
   - a few jams.

   The Arbiter: "Nothing says Deathmatch like charcuterie." She plans to offer rat slices to the champions to "boost them up."
4. **0:02:07 — Summoning.** The Arbiter is "once again bewildered by the Grandmaster's everything." He claps, visible sound waves ripple out, and a portal tears open. He presents **Cress and Chedrick McDougan**.
5. **0:02:57 — Cress.** "I'm Cress, and I'm here to win the world tournament. **I need that prize money.**" The Arbiter asks whether they're telling them it's a world tournament this time. The Grandmaster: "**Yes, it's a world tournament, shut up.**" Cress is "just a human who likes to fight a lot. But **if I don't win this world tournament, I have to go and get a real job.**" The Grandmaster: "That's true motivation that we can all relate to… Good pick for our first champion, Arbiter."
6. **0:03:58 — Chedrick.** "**Greetings, you peasants.** I am Chedrick McDoodon [sic]. And I plan to bring nothing but pain to whoever comes in my way of winning this tournament." He promises: "**So if you come my way, I'm taking your shoes.**" Cress: "I'm barefoot." Chedrick: "**Well, I guess I'll be taking your feet then.**"
7. **0:04:36** The Arbiter narrates that Chedrick "casually eats his pineapple of death." The Grandmaster wants the pineapple for her board. She runs up with **two goblins carrying a small wooden plate shaped like an axe**, loaded with thin rat slices ("only from the best kind of rats"), cheese cubes and berries. The Arbiter: "They're locally sourced rats."
8. **0:05:15** Chedrick: "The only rats that I know is by Tui [?] and **Master Splinter**. So if they are the ones sliced up, I will let it taste greatness." The Grandmaster: "That's definitely Master Splinter on the plate in front of you." It tastes like chicken.
9. **0:05:57 — "Gamey."** Cress asks whether it's "Skyrat." The Grandmaster says these are **garden rats: "Freshly grown, organic… Vegan rats."** Cress: "Those taste a little gamey though." The Grandmaster is outraged: "**This is rat to table.** You can't buy this anywhere else… some random celebrity named Apple probably ate it." The Arbiter: "**This is the Wagyu of rats.**" Cress admits it was seasoned "a little bit." The Grandmaster: "Okay, a lot."
10. **0:06:50 — The tantrum.** "**You insult my food, I send you to the Deathmatch arena.**" She had planned to give each of them two temporary points and now withholds them. **She stomps away with her arms crossed and kicks over one of the platter-carrying goblins, spilling the charcuterie across the kitchen floor.** Cress: "We just wanted to know if you season until your ancestors told you to stop." Chedrick: "I like her, even though I gotta take her shoes. Or her feet."
11. **0:07:42 — The stakes.** Fight to the death, "or I'll be feeding one or two of you to my newly done vegan rats." The group works out that the opposite of vegan is carnivorous, or maybe omnivore rats. Ruling: "**If you win, I will feed your opponent to the rats and then you can eat the rats.**" Chedrick asks whether they'll be rotisserie. "Yes, **champion-fed rotisserie rat** that's well seasoned." Chedrick: "We're gonna have rotisserie Cress."
12. **0:08:26** The Grandmaster: "Every time on this show, everyone always critiques any food that I bring up… **I am a good cook.**" The Arbiter: "**You asked me to find good fighters, not good people.**"

### The Shadowlands
13. **0:08:36** A second portal opens and dark black fog billows out. The Arbiter: "Grandmaster, I think you'll like this location. **We'll make our way to the Shadowlands.**"
14. **0:09:14** The Grandmaster floats with her feet never touching the ground. Her face shows disgust and she pulls her shoulders in. "**You thought I would like this place?**" She warns: "This is not a land to be trifled with. I'd be careful where you stand, what you touch, and what you breathe here." She takes shallow breaths while her wings fan away the fog.
   - The fog is thick, musty, cold and damp, and it clings to the skin.
   - The rock squishes underfoot. The few living plants have pale, lackluster leaves.
   - "**This land is a monster in and of itself.**"
15. **0:10:04** Her wings part the fog as she rises. **Doug the pug is under her arm and "accidentally leaves a small cloud behind of his own."** They take her **throne in the sky**. The acolytes in the stands scream and stomp, **holding leftover rat slices, cheese and berries from the charcuterie board**. Her charge: "Fight to your hardest, prove your worth, and may only one of you be standing at the end."
16. **0:11:39** The Arbiter: "Acolytes, are you ready?" Fans spill beer, eat cotton candy, and "shovel" down malt cups with wooden spoons.
17. **0:12:06** **Chedrick rolls a nat 1 on initiative**, so Cress gets advantage on her first attack. The Grandmaster: "It's okay, you're a loser. You can rise from this."

### Chaos event — the Thunder Twins (0:13:04)
18. The acolytes have already summoned two chaotic events. Cress rolls a 15. **Storm clouds roll in, it rains, and "the Thunder Twins strike down in anger."** Lightning dodges along with Cress as she tries to move ("your hair feels a little frazzled, your bones a little shattered"). Chedrick also fails his save. **Both take lightning damage.** The Grandmaster: "Wake up, champions." Chedrick: "**I live in the water. I breathe thunder and lightning.**" Cress: "That's a very odd thing to breathe." Chedrick: "I am supercharged."

### Round 1
19. **0:17:06** Cress draws her blade and rushes Chedrick. Chedrick: "**How dare you put your hands on me, you peasant?**" Cress: "I'm not putting my hands on you. I'm putting my blade on you." Her slash hits. Chedrick: "That's what you call a hit? **That's a love tap.**" Her second swing misses.
20. **0:18:49** Chedrick: "I will show you the power of Chedrick Magooda as I slash you with my **double blade**." The first swing misses as Cress bobs and weaves under it. **The second, "with the right," is a critical hit** for a big wound. "You make this too easy for me."
21. **0:20:37 — The pinky toe.** As a bonus action, Chedrick says "there's somebody's ankles I gotta take" and makes **a longsword ankle shot**. It's a glancing blow while Cress leaps, but he clips the side of her bare foot. "Give me your pinky toe." **Cress's left pinky toe is severed** and lands in a puddle. The Arbiter: "Make a necklace out of it."
22. **0:21:42 — The eel.** **A small, slithery dark eel slips out of the river next to them and makes a beeline for the toe.** Initiative pauses so either champion can react. **Cress flips her longsword, stabs the eel through the head, and grabs her toe on the same motion.** She keeps the toe ("I feel like I'm going to be able to reattach this at some point") and **pockets the still-wriggling dead eel**. The Grandmaster: "**Is that an eel in your pocket or are you just really excited to see me?**" The Arbiter: "I'm always excited… It can be both." **The Grandmaster blows a kiss.**

### Chaos event — bound hands (0:24:14)
23. Chedrick rolls an 8 and the Grandmaster triples it to 24 ("Quick math"). A mysterious aura seeps from inside their bodies into their bones and out through their fingertips. **For two rounds, Cress and Chedrick are bound together, holding hands.** Each has only one free hand, so there's no two-weapon fighting. The Grandmaster: "So cute."

### Round 2
24. **0:26:01** Cress keeps her longsword hand free and **swings at Chedrick's held hand**. **It cuts halfway through, to the bone.** Ruling: a clear second hit is needed to chop it off. Chedrick: "**Not my strong hand… You won't get away with this.**"
25. **0:27:28 — Hero points and the Box of Chaos.** The acolytes give **each champion a hero point**. The Arbiter: "push yourself plus ultra… go Kaioken times 20." **The acolytes also summon the Box of Chaos.** The Arbiter snaps, and **the box containing Gerald slams down behind Cress**. The lid flips open and a column of pink and yellow fire shoots into the sky, briefly dispersing the shadow. The Grandmaster: "**Quit dancing on my field!… Quit dancing on the box!**" Two shadowy clawed hands and two yellow eyes rise out: Gerald. Because they're still hand in hand, moving means dragging the other person (a strength contest) unless both agree.
26. **0:29:32** Chedrick wants to see what's in the box. He uses "**my pimp hand**." The Arbiter suggests the half-severed hand "could probably get a little more whip action." Chedrick: "You activated my trap card." **He wins the strength contest and drags Cress to the box** (Cress: "I'm not strong, I'm just fast").
27. **0:30:52 — Fear.** Gerald eyes the penguin. Chedrick: "**His name is Fear, and he fears nobody.**" Gerald: "Oh, that's kind of badass." For his sacrifice, Chedrick offers **"a box of grapes and two apples."** The Grandmaster: "Is that from your own personal charcuterie board?" Gerald takes them with a shadowy hand: "I could eat."
28. **0:32:44 — The Mind Blade.** The column of fire shoots past Chedrick's face ("Holy shit. I'm on fire." The Arbiter: "We're not insured for fire damage"). Cress: "That fire better not jump on me." The Grandmaster: "**Don't give me ideas, Wreck.**" **The shadow hands Chedrick a Mind Blade, a sword made by Mind Flayers that deals extra psychic damage.** Chedrick: "**I can see the future!**"
29. **0:34:54 — Hitting Gerald.** "Let's read your mind." **Nat 1.** He focuses on the wrong mind, **the dead eel's**, picks up nothing, and **strikes Gerald and the Box of Chaos** instead. Gerald: "**I just gave you a cool dagger for apples.**" Chedrick: "It was an accident… It was my left hand. My strong hand still messed up." **Gerald claws Chedrick just above the knee, into the thigh**, leaving three scorched marks ("you're lucky he actually didn't claw any higher up"). Chedrick is now bleeding from his leg and his wrist. Gerald: "**I'm a demon in a box, not a monster.**"
30. **0:36:47 — The sausage joke.** "Can't make charcuterie without sausage." Cress: "You put rat on there, it's turning into a sausage party." The Grandmaster: "**Chedrick, we might put your sausage on the charcuterie board.** I can't believe I said that." Chedrick: "I hate you for that." The Grandmaster: "Reck set me up for it."
31. **0:37:25 — Doug's roll.** The Arbiter's end-of-round roll is a 5. **Doug the pug snorts, hops onto his cushioned platform with his squishy green D20 plushie in his mouth, and throws his own D20: an 11.** The Arbiter: "It's only six higher." The Grandmaster: "When there are only 20 numbers, six higher is notably higher. **You just suck.**"

### Round 3 — the hand comes off
32. **0:38:29** Cress: "I'm going to finish the job on this hand." Chedrick: "Leave my hand alone. This is all I have over here." Her first swing misses because he weaves, oddly well for a man holding hands.
33. **0:39:11 — Silver blood.** The Grandmaster asks Chedrick's blood colour. Chedrick: "**Vibranium color. T-1000… Terminator blood.**" **Bright silver blood pours out, mirror-like, reflecting both of them in the ground.** The Arbiter holds up a Polaroid: "Have you seen this boy?" The Grandmaster: "You're an android?" Chedrick: "Fifty shades of gray blood."
34. **0:39:54** **Cress's second swing chops Chedrick's hand clean off.** "My strong hand." Cress is free to move, **but the severed hand is still clasping hers until the end of the round**.
35. **0:40:32 — "Quit hitting yourself!"** The Arbiter asks, "You gonna slap him with his own hand?" With Flurry of Blows, **Cress slaps Chedrick with his own severed hand**. The blows miss, but **one splatters blood across his face** ("Tasting my own vibranium blood"). The dead hand's grip makes it awkward.
36. **0:41:44** **Cress backflips 10 ft up onto a rock** (Acrobatics 22 after a reroll Bec allowed because the die was ambiguous).
37. **0:42:29** Chedrick skips the opportunity attack. Cress: "You took my toe." Chedrick: "We're not even. **I only took one piece, you took five, literally.**" Cress: "You're the one that was after my feet, bro." Chedrick wonders whether he can regenerate. The Arbiter would let Second Wind reattach the fresh hand, but Cress still holds it.
38. **0:43:29 — The Mind Blade stump.** "**I'm going to attach this mind blade to my chopped off hand.** I'm coming after you, Cress. I'm going to get my hand, and I'm going to take the rest of your feet off." Cress taunts that he can't reach her. Chedrick: "**I am the bungee cord.** My legs will take me anywhere I need to go." The Arbiter: "Who gave it to you? Why do they hate you?" The Grandmaster: "If you have a nickname, that probably means you have friends." Chedrick: "I'm disoriented, okay? I'm just losing blood."
39. **0:44:46** The Arbiter calls it "that **evil dead arm**." **Chedrick climbs the rock** (Athletics 12) and the Mind Blade hits.
40. **0:46:00 — Cress's foot comes off.** **Chedrick crits again, and the psychic dice double too: he chops off Cress's foot.** "**You take my hand. I take your foot. My nubs.**" The Grandmaster: "We're gonna have to like have a puzzle by the end of this as we piece the two of you back together." The Arbiter: "We'll have enough parts to make one complete person… I am **Captain Planet**." Cress: "Captain Planet, he's a hero."
41. **0:47:34** Chedrick uses **Second Wind**. The Grandmaster explains Cress's psychic damage as the pain of watching him heal after hurting her: "a small little tinge of headache." Cress: "**Emotional damage.**" Chedrick: "Refreshing… What do you think about that?" The Arbiter: "That was Fear."
42. **0:48:39 — Fear's roll.** The Arbiter rolls a 7. The Grandmaster: "You just need to get better at this." **Doug flourishes his tail and throws his D20 far across the arena. It bounces at the feet of Fear.** The Grandmaster: "Fear, pick it up." The Arbiter: "Where does somebody learn to speak penguin?… Rosetta Stone or like a Duolingo?" **Fear rolls an 18.** Chedrick makes the penguin "victory sound." **The crowd chants "Fear, Fear, Fear"**, small children try penguin sounds, **a parade for Fear breaks out, and a conga ("Mamba") line weaves through the stands.** The break is called so they can "celebrate Fear."

### After the break
43. **0:51:23** The Arbiter: "Cress versus Chedrick Magudan. Don't forget his last name. He has two names… **The man with one arm. And the monk with one foot.**" Cress says Chedrick sounds like a college football coach. The table riffs on clipboards, whistles, headsets and recruiting ("He's special, he's very special").

### Chaos event — the Boggarts (0:52:34)
44. The Arbiter rolls 11. After a math muddle he settles on event 53 ("What a nerd"). **Two shadows seep from the ground, one in front of each champion, read their deepest fears and transform: Boggarts.**
45. **0:53:43 — Chedrick: heights.** Bec: "Heights!… don't give me heights. I can't make it a height." It becomes one anyway: **his Boggart rises beneath him, grips his feet, and lifts him "like a cheerleader to the top of the pyramid," 30 feet up.**
46. **0:54:13 — Cress: a Karen.** (Cress had joked "a retail job.") Hers becomes **a pale Karen with short blonde hair, spiky at the back and longer in front, and a giant purse.** She **cartwheels her shopping cart** toward Cress. She wants to return everything for cash, though she paid by credit card in someone else's name, and she has no receipt. Cress: "She's definitely the one that tries to **return a hot dog at a Stein Mart** with no receipt." The Grandmaster: "It's had a couple bites in it."
47. **0:56:00** Chedrick: "I have no wisdom." He fails. **He runs 15 ft off the Boggart, falls 30 ft to land right above the chest, and his ankle snaps a little.** Fear was not lifted; he was "just pecking at the ankles of this Boggart."
48. **0:57:18 — The foot as a cushion.** Chedrick reveals he **picked up Cress's severed foot and landed on it**: "**I'd rather crack hers than mine.**" Cress: "That's fine, I got plans, don't worry."
49. **0:57:43 — "I am the manager."** Cress passes and stares the Karen down: "**No, I am the manager. And you gotta go.**" The Karen hesitates, stunned. **Cress Spartan-kicks the cart.** Green beans, corn and Kinder eggs spill everywhere (Chedrick adds ketchup, mustard, licorice, fruit hoops). Cress: "She thought she was finna hit a Supermarket Sweep."

### Round 4 — the limb exchange
50. **0:58:57 — Gerald's revenge.** Gerald creeps up over the box, still angry about being hit with a sword. **He tosses a healing potion to Cress**, and it heals her. The Grandmaster: "That's what you get for hitting the box. Makes friends for others." Chedrick: "**I am a professional box hitter, what you expect?**" The Arbiter leans over: "**Did I find champions or children?**" Someone shouts "Kill each other!" The Grandmaster: "Get your act together!"
51. **1:00:54 — The senzu bean.** Cress asks to "senzu bean" her hero point to regrow her foot and toe. She needs above 17 on a D20 and fails ("You ain't no lizard"). **The ankle grows back, ending in tiny toes and no foot**, and she heals. The crowd cheers "**Goku, no, sorry, Cress, Cress, Cress.**" Chedrick: "Orange Namekian now." The Grandmaster: "**Twinkle Toes.**" Chedrick: "You got a little rascal's ankle."
52. **1:03:01 — Javelins.** Cress throws javelins from her rock. Chedrick: "Your accuracy decreases by 20% because you're on one foot." The Arbiter: "**This guy, he's been playing D&D all of an hour and thinks he's a DM now.**" The first javelin glances off him.
53. **1:05:02 — Feeding the box his hand.** **Cress uses telekinesis to drag the willing Box of Chaos 5 ft closer** ("Gerald's up for a free ride"). Then she **throws Chedrick's severed hand into it**: "That is my gift to you." Gerald: "**Oh yeah, this will go great with my hand collection.**" The Grandmaster: "Sorry, Chedrick. I don't think you'll be getting your hand back." The d100 comes up 64 ("Nintendo 64"). The Arbiter: "**Uh-oh. This is an uh-oh for Chedrick.**" He has to find a list, then has Cress roll a d12: a 3.
54. **1:07:12 — The scroll.** Gerald floats out **a scroll of a ninth-level spell: Mass Healing Word at 9th level** ("It's close to the wish spell though"). The table jokes about which foot she should get: dragon, tiefling, goblin, "the Subway foot." Chedrick wants a dragon foot to jump higher, and they point out he's afraid of heights. **Cress heals a large amount and her foot regrows fully.** The Arbiter's description: "**it's like a skin sweatpants**." Bec: "I'm actually imagining something else that it could look like… I'm so sorry!" She later says, "It's fun to make everyone uncomfortable."
55. **1:11:23** Chedrick now holds "a foot that doesn't matter anymore." The Arbiter: "It's a **souvenir foot**." The Grandmaster: "Put it as a keychain. It'll bring you luck." The Arbiter: "I fought to the death and all I got was this foot."
56. **1:11:44** Cress's **Kensei's Shot** javelin misses. The Grandmaster: "**You're kind of looking like a Krillin out there.**" The Arbiter: "Better a Krillin than a Yamcha." The Grandmaster: "If you miss this next one, you're a Yamcha."
57. **1:12:53 — Foot for hand.** Chedrick: "It seems to me that **my hand gave Cress the most powerful healing spell of all time**. Let's see what this stinky foot could do." He throws Cress's foot into the box: "Unlike you, I was nice to the box." **Gerald peeks at the foot, reaches back into the chest, and holds out Chedrick's hand: a trade.** Chedrick: "Get my damn hand." Cress: "That's how you talk to them when they give you your hand back?" Chedrick: "I'm grateful. I'm very grateful."
58. **1:14:21 — The sewing kit.** Gerald takes the foot and **tosses Chedrick a sewing kit** to sew his own hand back on (Sleight of Hand 13; Bec "was really hoping for a nat 1"). **It's on the right limb and the right way round, but weak: "This is no longer your strong hand for sure."**
59. **1:15:35 — "Stop hitting yourself."** Chedrick says the hand is "already at disadvantage. It's slapping me." The Grandmaster takes the idea, and **his first attack with the hand is against himself. He slaps himself so hard it leaves a mark.** **Gerald's shadow hand is sticking out of the box "like commanding the hand, like he cursed it."** Chedrick: "Hey, hey, leave me alone… **It's got a mind of its own.**"
60. **1:18:17** Chedrick: "Hey, Cress, about that ankle of yours, I'm aiming for it." **He throws a hand axe left-handed at Cress's ankle.** It slices but doesn't sever.
61. **1:19:20 — Blue blood.** Cress: "**My blood's blue.**" It mixes "beautifully with this silver alloy" from Chedrick. Cress: "Got that Quicksilver going."
62. **1:19:53 — The Boggarts return.** **Chedrick's Boggart lifts him 30 ft again. Two Karens approach Cress, mother and grandmother.** Grandma records on her flip phone. Cress: "Let's go straight to TikTok. I don't even work retail and that's giving me freaking PTSD." The Karens: "**The customer is always right**," banging on her rock.
63. **1:21:00** Cress rolls a 20. The Grandmaster: "**Not only are you the manager, you're the owner.** You kicked them to the curb. You banned them from the store." Cress: "**They called the cops, and the cops arrested them.**" They turn out to be drunk ("Those coupons expired 30 years ago"). Her Boggart slinks away, terrified.
64. **1:21:34** **Chedrick rolls a nat 1.** His Boggart, "giggling, laughing," lifts him another 10 ft. **He jumps off willingly and takes fall damage again.** "What is going on?"

### Round 5 — Sir Shadow Ten the Third, Knight of the Night
65. **1:22:53 — The Arbiter's chaos event.** The Arbiter's 18 triggers his event. **He pulls the Boggarts and other shadowy things out of their hiding places and shapes them into a shadow ghast between the champions.** It is partly incorporeal, pours smoke and shadow, and has a long whipping tongue (or tongues). "Wife is a very lucky lady" (speaker uncertain, probably Drew). Chedrick: "Is that plural? Like tongues?"
66. **1:24:00** Both champions try to hold still and hide. Cress rolls 6 and is seen. **Chedrick rolls double nat 20s: "Maybe because you've been bleeding so much, you just look like a silvery background in this space."** He is hidden for two rounds.
67. **1:25:30** Cress checks that her Kensei weapons count as magical and **hits the ghast with a javelin**.
68. **1:26:50 — Blown cover.** Chedrick yells at the ghast: "Hey, but she just hit you. What you going to do about that? **I mean, you can't see me**, so I ain't worried about it." **It turns toward his voice and his stealth is gone.** The Grandmaster: "You talked directly to it. Whoops." Cress: "Fear is mad." **Cress holds her action** behind the rock ("She's playing the long game").
69. **1:28:43 — The name.** The Grandmaster: "Everybody has a name." The Arbiter improvises: "**Sir Shadow Ten the Third… He's a knight… A knight of the night**" (the first "knight" with a K). The Grandmaster: "I would have named Bob."
70. **1:29:17** Cress damaged it, so **the ghast goes after Cress**. **Its claws hit and she narrowly resists paralysis.** **It bites.** She resists the necrotic part ("Every point counts… I'm so squishy"). Her held action hits back. She hunts for a radiant feature, and the Arbiter identifies **Celestial Revelation / Radiant Consumption** (usable only while transformed) and allows the radiant damage.
71. **1:33:08 — The mind-control try.** Chedrick wants to take control of the creature with the Mind Blade. The Arbiter: "This is D&D, all you gotta do is ask." **He hits and gets into its mind. He rolls a 5, then spends his hero point for another try with advantage.** For a moment he thinks he has control, then **it pushes back, throws him out, and deals psychic damage to him**: "Only for now." The Grandmaster: "It's gonna take more than a four." Chedrick: "**A monster with two tongues needs to be taken over… they ain't got no brain.**" Both remaining swings miss, the last because **the ghast pulls its own form apart so the blade passes through like mist.** The Grandmaster: "Saying it louder doesn't make it more effective."
72. **1:36:48** The Arbiter's roll is a 2. The Grandmaster: "**Bless your heart.** As the Karens would say." The Arbiter: "**I'm thinking about building a shrine to you here in the Shadowlands.**" The Grandmaster: "No. No, you know I hate this place… As soon as this battle's over, we're leaving."

### Round 6 — the Grandmaster intervenes
73. **1:37:32** "I am uncomfortable staying in the Shadowlands any second longer." **She gifts both champions advantage. "I banish this shadow creature from my arena."** **She draws her oath bow and spears the ghast's heart to the ground, and it dissipates.** "I demand a fight between these two champions… **show me what you are worth.**"
74. **1:38:57** She rouses the acolytes ("roll harder, live harder, give blood, catch a body… Let me see those devil horns"). Chedrick: "Someone's gonna die." The crowd throws devil horns. The Grandmaster: "**Are we not entertained?**"
75. **1:40:38 — The backhand.** **Cress backhands Chedrick across the face.** It hits, and the Grandmaster adds psychic damage "because that's just like demeaning at this point." **He now has a handprint on each cheek, one from his own slap and one from Cress.** Agile Parry raises her AC while she holds her sword.
76. **1:41:45** She spins into a longsword "anime swish," but **Chedrick limbos under it, Matrix-style,** and pops back up.
77. **1:42:33** The Grandmaster: "You have no more senzu beans… There are no dragon balls to summon you back." Chedrick dances on his toes, "staying warm." Cress uses **Step of the Wind** ("Instant transmission"). She first tries to dash 90 ft, then takes a 45-ft disengage after the Arbiter's correction.
78. **1:44:53 — "She's running."** Chedrick: "**Coming from somebody that really wanted to fight to the death. You're surely running from me now.**" Intimidation against Cress's insight of 19: he fails. The Grandmaster: "**a little trickle goes down your pant leg.**" The Arbiter: "She's not scared of shit." The Grandmaster: "If you survive, **I'll get you a new pair of pants**… We've got prestidigitations."

Made me feel like a little bitch earlier." **The double blade hits twice.** The Grandmaster: "**You done messed up, A-Aron.**" Then "**mind blade jutsu**" misses twice. ### Round 7 — ultimate moves
80. **1:50:18 — The rainbow beans.** **The Grandmaster throws each champion a magical, rainbow, shimmering bean.** Each will describe an ultimate move. **Whoever rolls more damage destroys the opponent's move and the opponent.**
81. **1:51:45 — Cress.** "With my off hand, I throw a hand to the sky and I charge up and summon **a heaven of dawn**." A focused energy beam falls straight onto Chedrick, "engulfing him in the laser fire and lightning… **glassing the spot where he stands**." ### Out-of-character (creator notes)
- **This was BigCheese's first time ever playing D&D.** Reck was shaking off rust and playing a multiclass she'd never played before. She says her current campaign is only her second ever.
- Cheese chose Reck as his opponent because she was "trash talking at **GCX**." Reck doesn't recall it.
- **Cheese's plan was to take the player's shoes.** Reck is "always rocking some Jordans," and he meant to tie them up and throw them over the clotheslines. Hence "I'm taking your shoes," and then the feet. "It all started with a pinky toe" (a Proud Family reference).
- **Reck built Cress on Dragon Ball Z.** The name was chosen like a Saiyan vegetable name. The ultimate move was based on **Gears of War's Hammer of Dawn**. There's a tangent on Gears of War (Cole Train, Dom's storyline) and crying over it.
- **Fear is based on Cheese's friend, who streams on Twitch as a penguin character.** The penguin drill came from their goofy ideas. Reck pictured Naruto's Gatsuuga (Kiba and Akamaru).
- Drew: "I'm always looking for the gut punch… find the emotional gut punch and then just keep punching it."
- Bec: D&D should be playable by anyone; "if a game is fun, it's because the people are fun… None of us know all the rules."
- Closing: Reck and Cheese play up "D&D beef" ("This would be great for our engagement").
