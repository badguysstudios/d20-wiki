## What happens
### Opening — the Arbiter in chains
1. **0:00:02** "Acolytes, we have returned. From where? No one will ever know. It's magic." Drew's narration moves through **the halls of the Dread Fortress**. The walls are damp with moisture and steam from the lava outside, and **pictures of the Grandmaster hang all around the halls**.
2. **0:00:40** In the Grandmaster's throne room there is **only the Arbiter, sitting at a desk**.
   - He is **in chains at the wrists and ankles**.
   - Instead of his normal black robes he wears **plain cloth clothing**, and he has **spectacles** on.
   - He is hunched over a pile of paperwork: "**this makes no fucking sense who needs this many chinchillas**."
3. **0:01:21** **The Grandmaster has been sleeping in.** It's "9 PM on a Sunday night" and she is just starting her weekend. She bursts the doors open with a flourish, and **the gust of wind blows all the papers off the Arbiter's desk**.
4. **0:01:21 — Taxes.**
   - The GM says the chinchilla receipts are **tax deductible** and must be documented. "We wanna make sure that our taxes are filed correctly this time."
   - They have **already filed for an extension three times** and "can't afford to have **the IRS** come visit us again."
   - Arbiter: "We can't afford these chinchillas."
5. **0:02:09 — The 203 chinchillas.** "Everyone needs at least 203 chinchillas."
   - **"If you want to keep goblins happy, you have to have chinchillas."**
   - **Each goblin gets exactly one, to prevent a goblin uprising.** If there were more, somebody would get two, the rest would get jealous, and everyone would need two.
6. **0:02:41** The Arbiter jangles his chains: "It's not like I'm in a position to argue at this point." Paying for the chinchillas will need a deathmatch. The GM assumed he'd arranged one already, since it's "one of the responsibilities I gave you."
   - "Oh, shit." He fumbles through the papers. "You remembered, right?" "Yeah, of course I did."
7. **0:03:06** He claps and rips open a portal in time and space, and two champions walk out: **"Ferndell Ironwind versus Lumos the Spark."**

### The champions and the yogurt feud
8. **0:03:38 — Lumos the Spark.**
   - She is **a high elf**, "pretty cool," and "questionably way too much into **corgis**."
   - This is because she "spent a lot of time with **the faith [fae?] folk**," and **they ride corgis**.
   - "I don't know that there's anything else really to my personality other than that." She's here "to get some revenge."
9. **0:04:17 — The grievance.** "I understand that this dimension's really on fire and stuff, but **there's another dimension** in which I have met this being before."
   - **In that dimension they are both office workers.**
   - **He keeps stealing the yogurt she brings in from the fridge.** "I think it'd be better if he was just dead."
   - Arbiter: "**A true villain after my own heart.**"
10. **0:04:55 — Ferndell.** He is **a dwarf wizard**.
    - It's "really weird" to hear they work in an office: "We do! **We work in an office of bounty hunters.**"
    - "If you have yogurt in the fridge I'm gonna take the yogurt." He offers to buy her more yogurt, then takes it back: "No, we're gonna battle to the death."
    - He wants to be **the highest-earning bounty hunter**, and she's his only competition.
11. **0:05:40** The GM: "yogurt is definitely high on my list." She asks for the best flavor.
    - **Lumos is lactose intolerant**, so her yogurt is **special-order, expensive, strawberry, Greek, high in protein**, "because, you know, bounty hunting, murdering people."
    - The Arbiter: "It's not really about the yogurt. **It's the principle of the yogurt, what the yogurt represents.**"
12. **0:06:28 — The twist.** Ferndell never even ate it. "**I opened it and I took the spoon, just threw everything in the trash can.**"
    - Lumos: several yogurts have gone missing. "I don't just murder people over one yogurt, okay? I'm reasonable."
    - Ferndell: "**I'm not supporting big yogurt here.**"
13. **0:07:01** The GM: "Yes, this is **the pettiness** that I am loving. Stick it to them." "**To yogurt or not to yogurt**…" She picks the arena: "Take them… **To nowhere.**" Arbiter: "Another bad choice for an evening's entertainment."

### The arena of nowhere
14. **0:07:48** The GM calls it "**one of my favorite little getaways**."
    - It is surrounded by **an abyss**. If you fall, "you'll just keep falling for a very long, long, long, long, long, long, long time." The Arbiter interrupts, and she was "making a point."
    - There are safe pathways and **a roaring fire in the middle**, because "**the void out here is rather chilly**."
15. **0:08:17 — Three runes, teleporters.** There's "no guarantee as to where it will take you or if that place will be safe."
    - They are no way to run away: "**if you step in there, You will take us all with you.**"
    - "Our champions are brave. **They face their yogurt problems head on.**"
16. **0:09:15** The Arbiter: "**It's time for a dirty yogurt fight. Not the first one we've had**, but it certainly will be memorable." Call and response: "Acolytes, you've come! For dungeons! And dice. And of course, a deathmatch!"
17. **0:09:15 — Placement.** Both roll a 4 on the d4, so both start in the fourth quadrant. The Arbiter fumbles the math: "It's been a minute since we've done a Deathmatch. Sue me."
    - GM: "You should know how to do math, though. **You're doing our taxes.**" "That is your fault completely." "**We're getting audited.**"
18. **0:11:02 — The crowd above.**
    - High above is a giant crowd raining down **confetti, hot dogs and slushies**. The mess falls into the void "creating **waterfalls of goop and trash**." "It's environmentally disposable. So it's fine."
    - The champions feel empowered by the crowd.
    - GM: "**I give you an arena of nowhere, but an audience of everyone.**" She tells them to fight for their stance on yogurt and also to appease the Acolytes: "Call to them, beg for their pleasure… You might find a few extra bonuses in your pocket."

### Chaos event — the chinchilla stampede (0:12:38)
19. The Acolytes trigger a chaos event before round one. **Lumos rolls a 6.**
    - A rumble shakes the pebbles, and **rats flee from holes in the walls**.
    - **A giant hamster ball drops from nowhere** and starts rolling at them.
    - The GM revises it on the spot: "**let's put a chinchilla in there for story time**." It becomes **all 203 chinchillas rampaging toward them**, "like the Stampede in Lion King of the Wildebeest."
    - Crowd: "Chaos!" "Hamster Chaos!"
20. **0:14:29** Ferndell fails his Dex save.
    - The chinchillas are "small, light, and fluffy," described in chat as "**round rabbits or very fat squirrels**." For him it's "a little bit more of a pride issue." Ferndell: "**My pride is hurt. I have no pride anymore.**"
21. **0:15:02 — The reading lesson.** Lumos struggles to find her Dex save on the sheet.
    - "Oh, was I supposed to read the words?… If you had told me I had to read, I would have just been like, you know what? **He could take the yogurt.**"
    - GM: "I am also illiterate… Attributes. That starts with an A and ends with tributes."
    - Lumos claims it was all intentional ("I was told to play with my food"). Arbiter: "This was all a test. You all passed."
22. **0:17:03** Lumos fails too. **Both are trampled while locked in eye contact, glaring at each other, too focused to notice.** Lumos takes 7 and Ferndell 4. "**First blood goes to the chinchillas.**" GM: "They're very cute, but very dangerous in packs."

### Round 1
23. **0:18:12 — Maximus herds the chinchillas.** Dogs love to chase chinchillas, but **Lumos's dog is "super friendly,"** so he **gently herds them away from her and toward Ferndell**. Animal handling: 16.
    - "What is your corgi's name?" "**My corgi's name is Maximus, obviously.**" Ferndell: "I'm gonna die."
24. **0:19:57 — A TED Talk for chinchillas.** Ferndell counters with a reaction.
    - He gets down to their level to "talk to them. **It's kind of like a TED Talk, but for chinchillas**," to turn them on Lumos. He fails.
    - **The lead chinchilla is smoking a cigarette**: "**Yeah, I'm not convinced.**" "Please?" "Sorry, there's nothing I can do about it. **You know, union rules.**"
    - He takes 6 more as they run off the map, "**Off to join their goblin owners**."
25. **0:21:28** Lumos takes no further action: "Anybody's being bludgeoned to death by chinchillas. You need a break."
26. **0:21:41 — Ferndell's turn.**
    - He casts **shield** on himself (ruled a bonus action). The GM: "a strong offense is a good defense. No, wait."
    - He asks how far away Lumos is. Lumos: "None of your business." It's 30 feet. "You know what else is 30 feet? **Blindness and deafness.**" He casts it at 2nd level. Someone quips "you can't see your yogurt now."
27. **0:23:17 — LASIK.** Lumos passes the Con save, and the Arbiter rules she is **now immune to being blinded or deafened for the rest of the deathmatch**.
    - Lumos: "She's got power eyeballs… **I got LASIK through your action.** I'm going to save so much on contacts."
    - Table: "Free medical."
    - Lumos: "**I said Blizzbear and I would kill each other with our kindness.**" (the episode title). "If you could cure my depression…"
28. **0:24:49** Ferndell moves 30 ft toward the bottom teleporter rune. They are still 35 ft apart.
29. **0:25:18** The GM sums up: "Some blood from chinchillas and some healing as well for eyesight… **Incredibly kind, incredibly unexpected.**"
30. **0:25:34 — The Arbiter's roll.** He rolls a **2**: "It's these chains, Grandmaster… **I just don't have the wrist action I used to.**" The Acolytes laugh.
31. **0:25:47 — Doug the Pug.**
    - As if on cue, **Doug, "our pug," pops up beside the Grandmaster's throne on his own fancy cushion**.
    - He looks down at Maximus and **gives a small nod "as a mutual respect from one dog to another."**
    - Then he picks up **his own plushie D20** and rolls a **13**.
    - Arbiter: "Yeah, but he's not in chains." GM: "**He has a collar on**… it's not chained to anything. **He doesn't even have thumbs.**" "If you're gonna listen to reason, I can't deal with you."

### Round 2 — the Box of Chaos
32. **0:27:00** "Have we summoned the Box of Chaos?"
    - **The Arbiter stands, snaps, and the Box of Chaos drops into the middle of the map.**
    - The lid flips open, and **a column of yellow, purple and pink flame shoots into the sky**, turning the atmosphere pink.
    - **A ring of fire** makes it perilous to reach. Using it is a free action, and a sacrifice earns a magic item, "Could be good, could be bad."
33. **0:28:03** The Acolytes chant their names, and **each champion gets one hero point**: "Think of them as your **Red Bull**."
34. **0:28:53 — The kindness plot.**
    - Lumos feels she has "an unfair advantage in just existing." She had hoped to "decapitate you like really quick and then we could go our own ways."
    - She offers to let Ferndell pet her dog. Ferndell suggests she heal him instead.
    - She wants the credit: "**I really need the credit from other people that like, oh wow, she's like so nice.**" They agree to tell everyone it was her idea.
35. **0:30:13** The GM has the Arbiter roll perception to overhear the "secret mission." He fails. Table: "Our first non-one." **The Arbiter is fidgeting with his chains, counting the rings**, completely unaware.
36. **0:30:46 — The heal.** "I have decided benevolently… to heal my opponent." "Just somebody validate me, honestly."
    - She casts **Healing Word**: 4 HP to Ferndell.
    - GM: "**You're lifting him up so you can tear him down.**" Lumos: "this is the kindness of my heart… this is really petty to do over yogurt… I want the best for him." GM: "**Pretend you're a Josh Groban**, raise this up, and then just drop it."
    - Ferndell: "**Thank you so much. Now it's an even playing field. And I'm ready to murder you.**"
37. **0:33:01 — Word of Radiance.** Ferndell fails the Con save and takes 6 radiant, which burns "**Kind of like a bad sunburn**." Net loss: "I got four back and now I am losing six."
    - Lumos: "**You want some alloy [aloe] for that?**" "Thank you, but I am a-okay."
38. **0:34:43 — Who's the better bounty hunter.** With the yogurt dispute "buried for now," the question becomes who's the better bounty hunter.
    - Lumos: "if our murdering is anything like this, neither of us is very good at it."
    - Arbiter: "The fact that you think Bounty Hunter's murder also indicates your skill level."
    - Ferndell: "**Hey, bounty hunters don't murder. Hard to collect that bounty if they're dead.**"
    - Lumos: "I only go for the bounty that needs murdering… **The dead or alive kind.**" She then moves ("I done did it. All by myself").
39. **0:36:22 — "This is my box."** Ferndell moves toward the box.
    - He casts **Slow** (3rd level) on Lumos, "because I do not want them to get closer to the box whatsoever. **This is my box.**" She fails the Wisdom save. Ferndell reads out the effects.
    - GM: "I did not know Slow was so badass."
    - **He also slows Maximus and four other chinchillas.**
40. **0:38:46** Lumos: "**You can mess with me, but if you're gonna mess with the dog, I am gonna John Wick your ass so bad.**"
    - GM: "**floofs running in slow motion are also super cute**." Lumos: "That booty… some Instagram cloud [clout]."
    - Ferndell: "I'm trying to help you as an influencer." "**Influencer bounty hunters?**" "That's gotta be a thing." "**Corgi butts in slow motion everywhere for the bounty hunters.**"
41. **0:39:30** Ferndell **Misty Steps straight into the box**, skipping the Ring of Fire.

### Gerald's deal (0:39:53)
42. **Dark shadowy hands creep out of the open lid, and a head with glowing yellow eyes pops up.** Gerald: "**Who the fuck are you?**"
    - Ferndell: "**I am Ferndale, the Yogurt Stealer.**"
    - Gerald: "**Is that like an actual title or are you just making that shit up?**" Ferndell: "You made it seem like I had to like present myself with a cool title."
    - Gerald: "Do you want to make a deal or not?"
43. **0:40:26 — The hero point sacrifice.** **Ferndell offers the box his hero point.** The GM warns he doesn't have to give something so precious. "No, why not?"
44. **0:40:50** **The Grandmaster stands from her chair: "Give him two. Two, Gerald… You will give him two items for that hero point. Not one, two."**
    - Gerald: "**Hey, I don't tell you how to do whatever it is you're doing. Don't tell me how to be a box of chaos.**" GM: "Fine."
    - The lid slams shut and the box rumbles. Two d100s: **57 and 21**. It's Ferndell's first ever d100. Lumos: "If I'm lucky, you just murdered yourself."
    - GM: "Don't play to my weaknesses, you fool!" Arbiter: "These are two items I've never even heard of."
45. **0:42:47 — The items.** The lid flips open. **The column of fire gives off no heat; it's some sort of magical fire.** Gerald floats two items over: "**You know, I had these laying around. See if you can make some use out of them.**"
    - **The Fulminating Treatise** ("Treaties"): a thick spellbook that crackles with energy. It holds **Contingency, Fireball, Gust of Wind, Leomund's Tiny Hut, Magic Missile, Thunderwave and Wall of Force**, with **three charges**.
    - **A sentient greatsword** "with a mind of its own." It gives +2 to hit and damage and deals 2d6 necrotic. It has **four charges of detect magic, detect evil and good and detect thoughts**, and the **wounding** property (DC 15 Con). Ferndell: "Is it **Hazuron**?" Arbiter: "It is." This is likely Hazirawn.
46. **0:44:44** Arbiter: "That was a very good use of a hero point, not gonna lie. **Might be one of the better ones I've ever seen.**"
47. **0:45:23** The Arbiter's end-of-round roll is an **8**. "It's better than a two." **Doug hops up, curly tail high, nose up, and throws a 10.** Arbiter: "**Fuck, Doug.** Makes you so cool, rolling his fucking 10s and shit."

### Round 3
48. **0:46:12 — The flying corgi plan.**
    - Lumos reveals that **Maximus can fly** ("I have a flying corgi… I wrote it down in a notebook you cannot read").
    - She planned to sacrifice one of her "**really, really cute boots** … my favorite color," whose **heel is cracked**, by having Maximus fly it over to the box.
    - She worries about him landing over the abyss. Arbiter: "It's a 60 foot throw. **You want to toss your dog over there? I'm not going to stop you.**" "Please stop me."
    - She declines, because her luck can't last. Ruling: typically each player gets one turn at the box.
49. **0:49:31 — The break-room story.** Ferndell: "You brought us here."
    - Lumos tells it. She **found him in the break room holding the spoon**. She "turned bright red" and stomped out, then told a coworker, "**oh my god i'm so mad i could kill him**."
    - "I didn't know that he was literally going to be like okay yeah kill me." Ferndell was "so kosher with being murdered."
    - "Did I want to murder him absolutely did I like mean it literally probably not but you know we're here now."
    - GM: "You weren't sorry about the yogurt."
50. **0:51:02** She dashes, then passes a Wisdom save (18) and **ends Slow**. "**I'm gonna do some down dog with the dog**… Instagram's gonna love this."
    - The Arbiter forgot to advance the round card: "**we need a new card guy**."
51. **0:51:48 — Fireball from the book.** Ferndell casts **Fireball from the Fulminating Treatise**. Lumos saves and takes half (14). GM: "Bring in the heat and she steps to side."
    - Lumos: "**Global warming is still happening in our dimension. I was plenty warm.**"
52. **0:54:25 — The rune.** Ferndell **Misty Steps onto the top teleporter rune.** "Did not think that's what was gonna happen."
    - The whole room warps and stretches (d6), and **both are thrown in random directions into a hellscape**.
53. **0:55:26 — The lava hellscape.**
    - It has **lava rivers** meeting in the middle at a small lava bed, a **raised altar area 15 ft up**, and flames coming out from under the platform.
    - GM: "I don't know if you all know this, **lava is hot**."
    - Initiative is rerolled, and Ferndell gets back-to-back turns.
54. **0:56:28 — Break.** The Arbiter has to go to the bathroom "and **it takes me a while to get out of these chains**." The break is cut from the mp3.
55. **0:56:52** "One of you has gained a t-shirt. **Congratulations, Vegeta.**" (a chat winner)

### Round 4 — the betrayal of Maximus
56. **0:57:24 — Charm Person on a dog.** Ferndell, 45 ft away, wants to cast **Charm Person on a dog**. It needs a humanoid.
    - **The GM's argument:** does the dog sit at the table to eat? Does it **sleep in your bed with its head on the pillow**?
    - Lumos: "For the rules of improv, yes, he does." GM: "**I would argue that's humanoid enough.**" Arbiter: "If he sleeps with the head on the pillow, that's people."
57. **0:59:09** Maximus gets +2 Wisdom ("**he's a smart dog**"; "He's a good boy… I don't just let anybody sleep in bed with me on a pillow"). He fails, and **Maximus is charmed**. Lumos: "**Should have thrown him when I could.**"
58. **1:00:19 — The bribe.**
    - Ferndell first just yells "attack Lumos!" ("that was pretty lame").
    - Then: "**Hey Maximus, I have a bag full of treats in my backpack. He likes cheese.** They're the best cheese that you could ever have in all dimensions of the world. All you have to do is attack Lumos."
    - Animal handling with advantage: 23. **Maximus bites Lumos for 5 psychic.** Arbiter: "**It's really not the teeth that hurt. It's the betrayal.**"
59. **1:01:29 — The stolen yogurt.** As this happens, **Ferndell eats another yogurt he stole from Lumos at the office, hates it, and spits it out** in front of her. On the intimidation roll: "**She's used to this at that point.**" GM: "Slightly ticked off."

### Chaos event — the donkey piñata (1:02:02)
60. Ferndell rolls a **3**. Against the browns, blues and reds of the hellscape, something **red, yellow, bright pink and green** falls from the sky.
    - It is **a brightly colored donkey piñata**, hovering **10 ft above the "four corners" where the lava rivers meet**.
    - History checks (16 and 13). GM: "It's bones, right?" No: they know it holds "**goodies, yummy things that make you feel good inside**."

### Chaos event — tag (1:04:35)
61. A third chaos event triggers immediately ("A third? You want more chaos?"). The Arbiter rolls a **10**.
    - **The Grandmaster stands, opens her wide wings, steps off her platform, floats down between them and holds out her arms.**
    - Lumos: "**Can we just hug?** … it's been like a really long day." Both go in for a hug, with Dex saves (17 and 16).
62. **1:05:44 — "You're it."** **Ferndell reaches her first.** She wraps both arms around him and **whispers "You're it,"** then pushes him away and flies up.
    - His body burns hot where he embraced her, and **the number 4 is burned into his chest**. When it counts down to zero, "there might be something that happens."
63. **1:06:29 — Tag back.**
    - Ferndell moves to Lumos: "**I'm so sorry, Lumos, I just don't wanna be it. Tag, you are it.**"
    - Lumos doesn't dodge. She hugs him, smiling, and whispers in his ear: "**you just lost the game**."
    - **The number 3 is now burned into Lumos's chest**, singeing her skin. Ferndell: "I lost the game. And so many other people have lost the game now."
    - **The tag countdown is never resolved.** The GM later forgets it (see item 90).

### Lumos's wall of fire
64. **1:07:58** "I am just about had it… I want to honestly **burn the whole thing to the ground**… **who touches my dog and casts a spell against me? That's just rude.**" GM: "Scorched earth right here."
    - She weighs Fireball (she'd be in the blast), Daylight ("It's like a big flashlight… like a nightlight"), then settles on **Wall of Fire**, placed diagonally through Ferndell and the piñata.
65. **1:11:19** Ferndell fails his save and takes **25 fire**.
    - Lumos: "**I said John Wick, right? I don't have a gun or multiple guns or any like books to shove into your face or pencils through your eye. This is what you're getting. You're getting fire.**"
    - She pushes the wall out, and **the ceiling and ground turn red from the heat**.
66. **1:12:59 — The candy.** **The piñata catches fire and cracks open.**
    - It's full of **Twinkies, individually wrapped "because… that's what the FDA [says]," and they melt**.
    - She searches for a salvageable Kit Kat. The GM: "**No, no Kit Kats, but two Tootsie Rolls and a Werther's exist.**"
67. **1:13:41 — The Tootsie Roll.** **Lumos takes the Werther's and throws a Tootsie Roll at Ferndell's charred face.**
    - It's a **nat 1**. **The Tootsie Roll was on fire from the piñata; it sticks to her hand and burns her for 1.**
    - "I can't even throw a Twinkie at her." Crowd: "Disappointed!"
    - Lumos: "you're like scorched earth, I'm done with this. And then still things backfire." GM: "**When you're trying to leave angrily and then… your little jean belt gets hooked on the door.**"
68. **1:15:29** The Arbiter's end-of-round roll is 15: "See, we're just warming up."

### Round 5 — Mom
69. **1:15:50 — The Twinkie.** Ferndell searches for unmolten candy (perception 19).
    - **Lumos is kicking around angrily in the ash, and she kicks up a gleaming Twinkie that flies right to him.** He eats it, a free action, for 3 HP. "Every little bit helps."
70. **1:17:03** He **moonwalks back 30 feet** "with style."
71. **1:17:31 — Phantasmal Killer.** He casts it at 5th level. Lumos's deepest fear is **Ferndell's mom**, standing in front of her "evil eyes… horrible and deadly."
    - Who is she? "**A very nice lady**… she bakes cookies… how you imagine a grandmother looking with **cookies fresh out of the oven, a nice white and pink apron, has recipes like everywhere**, but like now looking deadly and murderous."
72. **1:19:15** Lumos fails and takes **33**. GM: "Did she beat you at a bake sale?"
    - Ferndell: **"she throws a cookie at you, but it's a very burnt cookie, so it's honestly a shuriken at this point."** GM: "Mom is terrifying."
73. **1:19:53 — Existential dread.** **Lumos sits down clutching her chest**, "thinking about every choice in my life that's brought me here… **Did I leave a good legacy behind?**"
    - She'll have to save at the end of every turn. "No, I know I'm dying." "I'm bleeding out… I'm coming in and out of consciousness."
74. **1:21:06 — The Grandmaster's inspiration.** **The Grandmaster stands: "Lumos, do not give up hope yet. You can do this… You show mom who's boss," and grants her a d4 inspiration.**
75. **1:21:41 — The dog.**
    - Lumos's biggest worry is Maximus. Ferndell: "**I can take care of your dog after you die.**"
    - GM: "Make sure you have a will that takes care of your dogs, everyone."
    - Lumos: "**You will never see my dog again… Never talk to me or my son.**"
    - She wants to heal Maximus. He isn't hurt, because Ferndell charmed him. "Bribed."
76. **1:22:41 — The hero point.**
    - **Lumos spends her hero point to act twice.** She heals herself for 6: "**Finally, I stopped bleeding when I cough, so that's good news.**"
    - She eats **the Werther's** (d6): "It's exactly what I thought Werther's was gonna get me." GM: "it's just as healthy as the Twinkie."
77. **1:25:57** Ferndell is 35 ft away, "**Possibly a corgi throw if you're strong.**" "**With the last of my strength, I summon a fireball.**"
    - He saves and takes 14 ("we like to round up for pain").
    - GM: "**You're both in the red. Perfect. Well matched**… All for yogurt." Arbiter: "All for yogurt."
78. **1:28:11 — Fast Friends.** As a bonus action she casts **Fast Friends** "**so I can make sure that, like, if we both do die, that Ferndale doesn't hate me**." "No hard feelings, my dude… **I just, I want us to die friends.**" GM: "Good friends push each other to the end in Deathmatches."
    - **Ferndell saves.** Lumos: "**You don't want to be fucking friends, okay?**… I'll take every hint."
    - Ferndell: "**you support big yogurt and I don't support big yogurt.**"

### Characters
**Lumos the Spark** (Lumos's player).
- **A high elf**, obsessed with corgis from time spent with "the faith [fae?] folk," who ride corgis.
- **She owns Maximus, a corgi who can fly.**
- **In another dimension she is an office worker and bounty hunter**, a rival of Ferndell, and very wealthy ("quadrillion" dollars, per her will and Dried Blood).
- **Lactose intolerant**; her yogurt is special-order strawberry Greek, high in protein. She has cute boots in her favorite color (one heel cracked).
- **Spells this episode:** Healing Word, Word of Radiance, Wall of Fire, Fireball, a self-heal via hero point, Fast Friends. She carries a mace and has Daylight, Cure Wounds, Mass Healing Word and Mass Cure Wounds on her sheet.

- **Died to Phantasmal Killer (Ferndell's mom). She left everything to Maximus**, and was revived for Dried Blood.

**Ferndell Ironwind** (Ferndell's player).
- **A dwarf wizard and bounty hunter** in the office dimension. He wants to be the highest-earning bounty hunter there.
- **Steals Lumos's yogurts and throws them out uneaten**; he's "not supporting big yogurt." He self-titled "**the Yogurt Stealer**" for Gerald.
- **Spells this episode:** shield, Blindness/Deafness, Slow, Misty Step, Charm Person (on Maximus), Fireball (from the Treatise), Phantasmal Killer at 5th level.
- **First champion ever to sacrifice a hero point to the Box of Chaos.** He received the Fulminating Treatise and a sentient greatsword (likely Hazirawn).
- **His mother** is a nice cookie-baking, grandmotherly lady in a white and pink apron, as his Phantasmal Killer made her murderous. She sent "kisses," and expects him at **dinner on Friday** (per the GM).
- **Winner**; he slipped local yogurt into the Grandmaster's pockets.

**Maximus**, Lumos's corgi.
- Can fly. Sleeps in her bed with his head on the pillow, so he's ruled "humanoid enough" for Charm Person. Has +2 Wisdom ("a smart dog," "a good boy").
