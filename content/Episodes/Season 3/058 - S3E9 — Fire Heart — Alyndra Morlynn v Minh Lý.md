**Minh Lý**, the bare-chested, sword-dancing "Freshmaker," faced **Alyndra Morlynn**, a starlit elf who came looking for glory, at the [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|Grandmaster]]'s favourite waterfall hideaway. For most of the fight it was a comedy of bulging muscles, a swole Box of Chaos and a dog with too many eyes. Then it turned into one of the most heartfelt endings in the arena's history. When the meteors fell, the two rivals held hands, and the dying Freshmaker spent his last breath saving the woman who had killed him.

## The Story

### Ping Pong at the Dread Fortress

The episode opened in [[The Dread Fortress]], where [[The Arbiter|the Arbiter]] was still in chains as a prisoner. Following a strange sound down the halls, he clanked into a room nobody had seen before: a brand-new game room. It held ping pong, air hockey, arcade machines, a soda fountain, and a pair of neon signs reading "Grandmaster forever" and "the Arbiter sucks." The Grandmaster froze mid-rally with a paddle in her hand. On the other side of the table stood her opponent, [[Doug the Pug|Doug]], the cyborg zombie pug. "He's very good. I can't help it. I have yet to beat him," she admitted.

The Arbiter glared at Doug with "the most hatred anyone could ever have for anything." His eyes blazed with fire, and the ping pong ball melted on the spot. Doug was unbothered. He trotted to his dog-height ball cabinet, took out a fresh ball, hopped back onto the table, looked the Arbiter dead in the eye and farted in his direction.

The Arbiter reminded everyone that they had a Deathmatch to get to. The Grandmaster had completely forgotten and blamed Doug for it. She then counted the two champions the Arbiter had brought: "Math. One, one, two… I'm a good leader. I promise."

### Two Champions and a Painting

The Arbiter's clap ripped open a portal. Through it came Minh Lý, winking at everyone and striking a pose. He carried a longsword tied up with "a really dainty ribbon." Doug sniffed his toe and danced around the ribbon, and Minh fell instantly in love: "I love this dog. And if anything were to happen to this dog, I would kill everyone in the room." The Grandmaster called him "a fighter after my own heart."

Alyndra Morlynn followed. She had long pointed ears, hair "made of starlight," and glittering chain mail so spotless it looked "like a cosplay before you do the weathering." She announced she had heard this was "the place for glory." When the Grandmaster asked whether she was dabbing, Alyndra corrected her: "No, this is my signature pose."

A goblin named [[Theodore]] ran out with an easel to paint the two champions. The Arbiter photobombed the portrait in his most regal chained pose, and the Grandmaster complimented his "nice bubble butt in the picture." The finished painting turned out to be a watercolour of three stick figures. The Grandmaster explained that Theodore was going to art school soon, and that all proceeds from the day's battle would fund his scholarship. The hosts then listed the Dread Fortress's staff benefits: dental, a 401K, generous PTO, and a union-friendly workplace. Minh was so impressed that he vowed to "try to die less now that I've heard this."

### Into the Waterfall

For the arena, the Grandmaster asked for "one of my favorite old spots." This was where she liked to escape with "a couple of books, a bottle of wine." It was a river at the top of a roaring waterfall, under magical trees full of glittering fairies and fireflies. [[The Acolytes]] sparkled in the mist above "like the stars themselves," throwing popcorn that little birds swooped down to catch. The Arbiter set the champions on opposite banks of a knee-deep, fast-flowing river and declared: "Acolytes, watch these two fill the water with blood."

Minh opened by pulling off his top and going bare-chested. "You don't need armor when you have mage armor," he said. His posing drove rows K through L into a swooning wave, and a bachelorette party joined in. One magically gifted Acolyte blew him a kiss that blessed his next blow. Alyndra had her own fan: Corrin, up in the nosebleed seats, cheered so hard they tripped down the stairs and turned it into a somersault. That gave Alyndra the edge she needed, and she landed the first cut.

The crowd soon chanted "Freshmaker, Freshmaker," which drifted out of sync into "French Baker, fresh bacon." Minh began his bladesong, but his first swing only grazed her armour. Alyndra knocked him flat into the river, struck her signature pose over him, and hit him again. She then politely backed off to give him room to get up. The Acolytes answered her "The stars will always guide you" with a laser show from their compact mirrors. At the round's end Doug hopped onto his pedestal and threw his squeaky toy die. It rolled higher than the Arbiter's, and the Arbiter flipped him off.

### Gerald Gets Swole

With a snap, [[The Box of Chaos|Gerald]] slammed down onto a rock in the middle of the river. His pink and yellow flames gave off no heat, and his yellow eyes peered over the rim. Before anyone could reach him, though, the squeaky toy summoned someone from far away. A deep bark shook the trees, and a giant four-eyed dog loomed over the arena. This was Chet, Doug's older brother, home from obedience school. "He went to college and he just totally changed," the Arbiter explained. Both champions fled in terror.

Minh recovered, hasted himself, and leapt through the river to the Box. Gerald took one look at him and said, "Wow, those are big muscles." Minh flexed so hard his shield popped off its straps and into the box as an offering. "That was fucking hot. I'll be right back," Gerald gasped, fanning himself with it. He floated out a suit of poison-resistant armour, which Minh wore as bicep bandanas and a belt "to keep the abs in place." Gerald was so overwhelmed by Minh's physique that he retreated into the box to do dumbbell curls. He came back as "just a huge upper body" who had "skipped every leg day," greeting Alyndra with "What's up, bro."

Alyndra teleported to the box on a beam of starlight and offered Gerald a glowing orb pulled from the sky. "It's body glitter," she said, and Gerald rubbed it onto his new chest. The Box gave her a scroll of **Meteor Swarm**. "This is the best day of my life," the Grandmaster said, jaw on the floor, and threw down a tiny umbrella for whatever protection it might offer.

There was one rescue to make first. Minh fed Chet a piece of protein bar from his backpack, which held nothing but protein bars and nuts, and then realised it was sweetened with something poisonous to dogs. Alyndra revealed "a bit of a vet background" and performed the Heimlich on the giant dog, who spat the bar out. Chet was so delighted by the hug and the snack that he poofed into a tiny eight-eyed Pomeranian, "because the more eyes, the cuter." Alyndra then sat on the Box of Chaos so Minh couldn't use it. "I don't see me sitting on your house," Gerald complained from underneath her. When thunder rolled at the round's end, Doug hid behind the Grandmaster's chair, and Chet curled up beside him: Thunder Buddies under the throne.

### Fire, Steam and Self-Inflicted Wounds

Unable to reach the Box with Alyndra on top of it, Minh dropped a Fireball on both of them. He nearly failed to dodge his own spell before twisting free at the last moment. "You all laughed at me when I said we only needed one champion, but this is how they take themselves out," the Grandmaster crowed. The blast boiled the river into steam. "What the fuck is going on out there?" Gerald yelled from inside the box.

Alyndra waded out of the "hot mess," but she turned her back on Minh, and he punished her with a smite. "You got to keep looking me in the eye for that," he said. She answered with Sacred Flame and complained, "How do you look so hot every time I give you my damage?" Minh tried to soak up the radiant fire, but the Acolytes chanted in unison that he had already used his chance to react, so he took the full hit.

When Minh pinned her against the waterfall, the Grandmaster drenched them both with a bucket's worth of cold water. It only gave Minh goosebumps that puffed him up further. "It was supposed to cool you off!" Alyndra wailed, and dunked her head in the river. The water "wasn't just water," however. It seeped into their nerves and set their arms twitching. When Minh's hasted strike landed, it turned back on him, and a cackling voice echoed across the valley: "Stop hitting yourself! Stop hitting yourself!"

### The Flood

"How about a change of scenery?" the Arbiter asked, snapping his fingers into the chaos energy of [[The Void]]. A wall of water tore down the valley and swept both champions over the falls. The tumble broke Minh's haste, and he was left drained and unable to move. They washed up in grassy shallows by a little rock bridge as the sun began to set.

Alyndra kept swinging at the helpless Minh and kept missing, popping only his mirror images. She then ate a giant poisonous strawberry, spat it out with a swollen tongue, and lied to Minh that it was delicious. He believed her ("Perfect victory dessert") and promptly sank, because he was too muscular to float. The Grandmaster threw him muscle-shaped floaties. When Alyndra finally landed a perfect natural 20, the tingling in her fingers took over and her follow-through sliced across her own chest. The cackling voice rang out one last time and faded.

### Hearts on Fire

Alyndra crumpled. "Maybe we weren't meant for glory. Maybe mother was right." The Grandmaster asked what had brought "these crocodile tears to my fight" and reminded her that nobody could step through the Arbiter's portal unless they were worthy. Alyndra screamed into the stands, "Yes! Did you hear that, mother?" Her mother was somewhere in a far corner of the stands on comped tickets.

Minh, free to move again, gave her a speech of his own while his magic gathered:

> "It doesn't matter what people say. It doesn't matter what they call you… You will survive. You've proven that. Your heart is on fire and that fire is not easily quenched no matter the slings and arrows."

Then he hit her with a vitriolic sphere. Alyndra, choking in acid with almost nothing left, unrolled her scroll:

> "What is glory but leaving your mark upon the world? What is glory but letting them remember your name despite death?"

She stared into Minh's eyes and pulled the stars down on them both. **Minh grasped her arm: "We're in this together."** The stars in her hair rose, grew until they blotted out the sun, and came crashing down. Minh's umbrella and his spells weren't enough, and the meteors were fatal to him by the narrowest of margins. "It's been an honor," Alyndra said. "Alyndra, the honor is mine," Minh replied, reaching out with a charred hand. **With every fibre of his being, he laid his hands on her and healed her, and his will formed a barrier that shielded her from the last of the fire.** His final words told her that as long as she followed her heart, she would always be glorious.

When the light faded, only darkness, ash and a shield remained, "shiny, still in nature." Alyndra picked it up and looked to the Grandmaster, who told her: "Though Minh has left us, his story is now burned into our hearts. And you… Alyndra, you must carry on his story and glory. The drinks are on me tonight."

## Dried Blood

Since Minh had fallen so close to surviving, the Grandmaster granted him a post-credits chance, "like Avengers end scene credits." He clung on through one success and two failures, and then the scene stopped there. All anyone saw was "the barely twitching within the pile of ash." Whether the Freshmaker lived or died has never been revealed. At the table, both players said the silly fight had turned personal. Minh's speech, his player admitted, was "just me talking to me."
