The Season 3 finale pitted **[[Soot Mustam]]**, a twelve-year-old Goliath with the body of a grown man and a hat worn backwards for power, against **[[Huey Leatherface]]**, a feather-shedding bird artificer who collects dog urine for science. They fought in one of [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]]'s glittering treasure rooms, under the eye of her pet dragon Lilac. It was a match of runaway hamsters, militant grandmothers and a pocket black hole. Then, after the champions were gone, the season's darkest secret climbed out of [[The Box of Chaos|the Box of Chaos]] and took the throne.

## The Story

### Face Paint and Rags

[[The Arbiter]] spent the start of the evening searching the halls of [[The Dread Fortress]] for the Grandmaster, who "continues to elude him this evening." He found her by following the sound of giggling and a whirring cotton candy machine. Inside her chambers a line of [[Goblins|goblins]] snaked up to the top of her throne, where she sat at a little table of paints, decorating faces from a board of designs: butterflies, unicorns, Spider-Man, blood dripping from the eyes. The smallest and cutest goblin, Nathaniel, asked for an orc skull and got a bright pink one in the middle of his forehead. Then he held up his chinchilla, which she covered in tiny hearts.

The Arbiter, with a blood vessel about to burst, reminded her it was past nine and the season finale. Faced with the length of the line, she dipped her whole hand in paint and ran down it smacking every goblin in the face. She wiped what was left on the Arbiter's shoulder, since "you're wearing rags anyway." He had been in sackcloth and chains all season as punishment for trying to overthrow her, and he dropped a hint: a fresh pair of clothes "would be great. It's been five months." She told him maybe after the match.

### Leatherface, Leatherfeather

The portal opened in fog and laser light. When the Arbiter checked the name, Huey corrected him gently:

> **Huey Leatherface:** "Well, actually, partner, it's Leatherfeather, but honestly, it's quite a fetching name, so I might just make use of it."

He introduced himself as "the best dang artificer in this side of [[Strixhaven]]," a maker of potions and "little tinkery things" who throws them "real good," with a blunderbuss he called Old Bessie. Soot announced he trained at many gyms, was "very, very fit," and came with his trusty friend Marymoo, a flying squirrel. He tapped his backwards hat: "This gives me my power."

The Grandmaster asked for a space "that I treasure," and the Arbiter opened a portal onto a room blazing with gold. It was one of "the many little treasure rooms that help fund the great adventures we have here at D20 Deathmatch," with gold piles ten feet tall, scattered crowns and jewels, and giant crystals. Lilac, her flying dragon, guarded the hoard. The dragon is named for lilacs, though she does not actually smell of them. [[Doug the Pug|Doug]] hopped out of the Grandmaster's wings, was shooed off by the Arbiter, growled at Huey for eyeing the gold, and peed on his shoe. Huey, unbothered, pulled off his glove to show a scarred, half-plucked hand and scooped the puddle into a vial: "I typically take the glove off because it contaminates the urine." Soot, as a fellow animal trainer, approved. The Grandmaster had Doug whisked back up to her "before any more experiments happen to him," and Lilac's breath laid a wall of fire across the middle of the room.

### A Twelve-Year-Old With Abs

[[The Acolytes]] had already called up three chaos events before anyone moved. The first was the Grandmaster herself, who nocked an arrow and shot Soot. This was the moment everyone learned he was twelve. Huey admitted to being "a little bit troubled that I'm going to kill a 12 year old, but… that's for me and the big cheese upstairs," and the Arbiter assured everyone, "His mom signed the waiver." Soot shrugged off the arrow: "I've been punched harder than that before with an arrow to the face."

Soot summoned a fiery winged steed, Flamewing, out of what he insisted was "a blue and white square," and the table had a long riff on legally distinct collectible bobbles. He vaulted onto Flamewing "like Mario" and soared over the fire wall. His attempt to flick flame in Huey's face mostly burned his own shirt off. That turned out to be a weapon of its own: the sight of a child's enormous abs rattled Huey badly ("Your poor mother").

Huey answered with a vial "distinctly not dog piss," which exploded the flames into fireworks of "blues and greens and purples." He had forgotten he was standing in the blast. It blinded both of them and knocked the goggles off his forehead. "Yeah, Leatherface is right." Soot, it emerged, had trained blindfolded and could sense things up close, but his swing at Huey came up a natural 1. He spent a gift from the Acolytes to try again, confessing his "deep-seated shame" at needing to prove to his mother he wasn't the weakest in his village. He rolled another natural 1. "Your mother starts crying in the stands," the Grandmaster reported.

### Hamsters, Meemaws and a Golden Golem

The second chaos event was a hailstorm of hamster balls. Two bonked Huey on the head and three hit the blinded Flamewing. Then the hamsters calmly found the nearest edge of the room and ran off it. The Grandmaster insisted they were not going to their deaths: one had a map, he was smiling, and "they're going to the promised land." The Arbiter added that they all wore matching tracksuits.

Soot yanked Huey across the room with lightning channeled through Marymoo and missed his follow-up ("Flamin', flamin' poop"). He sent the squirrel to chew on Huey's face for the rest of the fight. Huey declined to worry about rabies, since "it's a lie that the government has told you." He cured his blindness with spit and a salve, assured everyone the dog urine "is for later," and stepped back to where he'd started with his magic boots. Then he sped himself up and shot a twelve-year-old with Old Bessie. Soot explained that his village's training included standing blindfolded while people shot at him. Afterward Huey stood atop a gold pile "like a king," declaring he was too good a man to pocket any of it.

The third chaos event was the Acolytes throwing their own grandmothers into the arena. Four Meemaws with pink purses full of candy and tissues took the field, quickly christened the Golden Girls: Sophia, Dorothy, Blanche and Rose. Anyone who got close had to charm them or be attacked. Soot rode past Sophia confident that "parents love me." She gave him a Werther's and pinched his cheeks. When he tried to look intimidating and rolled yet another natural 1, he puffed his cheeks out so adorably that she hugged him and wouldn't let go. "I'm both upset about this and also comforted because I haven't been hugged enough as a child." While Soot was held in place, Huey flung a loose floor tile at him by magic for a brutal hit, cheering his own name out of nerves.

At the end of the round the Arbiter turned Lilac's eyes white and filled her head with his will. She spewed fire until the gold went molten, and a burning golem of gold and precious metals rose from the hoard. Huey named it Christopher. The Arbiter steered it from the balcony "like he's in a Pacific Rim machine." Unable to see the champions clearly, Christopher went after the grandmothers, cutting down Dorothy and breathing poison on Blanche until she "just burns up."

### The Box Pays Out

The Acolytes summoned the Box of Chaos, and Gerald's shadowy fingers and yellow eyes peeked out of its heatless pink fire. Soot tore off his most precious bauble, the emblem of his hometown, and dropped it in. Gerald took offense: "You think you can just throw trash wherever you want?… You little shit." He returned with a literal trash can, dumped it on Soot's head, and left him holding a Cube of Annihilation, a hole in the multiverse that erases whatever it touches. "Buyer beware," the Arbiter warned.

Soot teleported to Huey's side and landed a smiting blow, then a natural 20. Huey somehow stayed standing. He turned invisible, crept up to the Box, and whispered a sales pitch: "Hey, hey bud, I'm in a real tough spot right now. This steed looks mighty tasty, you wanna buy it?" A long claw dragged all of Flamewing inside. Gerald surfaced picking his teeth and tossed Huey an Amulet of Health in exchange. "Fucking here, take this, whatever."

Soot threw the Cube at Huey, who sidestepped it. Huey then tried to fling it back with magic. He chose not to ask about the consequences, and the uncontrolled Cube drifted toward him instead, plucking his feathers out one by one. He retreated to the edge beside Lilac and offered her an experimental elixir. She hesitantly drank it and beat her wings faster, and the room grew so hot that Christopher melted into slag and Soot took his shirt off again "because it's too hot."

### "I Did It to Myself"

The Grandmaster demanded real murder this time. Soot hit Huey's face with holy fire ("the name was prophetic") and charged him at the edge, needing only to leap the Cube sitting between them. He rolled his fourth natural 1 of the night and stepped straight into the black hole. "I did it to myself." As he came apart particle by particle, he thought about everything he could have done better:

> **Soot Mustam:** "I only became a warrior a year ago when I turned 11. So this is fine. And maybe one day the gods will bless me and I will come back and maybe I won't be a troubled youth."

The Grandmaster came down in tears, closed the Cube ("We wish you well on your next journey"), and lifted Huey's hand in victory. Huey mused that "it is what you do with the gift of life that determines who you are. Well, time to party." She flew him into the sky, a featherless roast chicken of a champion. Then Marymoo, still clamped to his face and now in a grieving rage, clawed him with everything it had, crying "Mary Mew! Mary Mew!" Startled by the squirrel, the Grandmaster let go. Huey fell forty feet ("You're with the hamsters now") and went splat. Marymoo glided down peacefully and was the only champion she took home.

### The Chains Come Off

In the throne room the Grandmaster tossed her sword to [[Thomas the Goblin]] as goblin knights lined the walls. Doug and the chained Arbiter hauled in the Box behind her. The Arbiter pointed out his good behavior, and she agreed and freed him. The chains dissolved in glowing marks and a glitter bomb, and his black spiked leathers and Italian boots returned. She told him she was proud of him. He conceded, "You have proven yourself to be a worthy Grandmaster once again. And yes, I was mistaken."

They planned the next season over wine poured from the sky: more blood, historically accurate feathered dinosaurs, lightning and hail together (the Arbiter demonstrated on her carpet), more mongoose, eating, drinking and vomiting contests, a gauntlet or two, and a Deathmatch somewhere new. They wanted a circus, or even the surface of the sun, instead of yet another haunted forest or [[The Shadowlands]]. "We need to kill them where no one has been killed before." They also remembered poor [[Timothy|Timmy]], the worm mayor who died at an earlier space match, and talked about commemorating him on a t-shirt and sending a fruit basket: "sorry we got your mayor and savior killed?"

### The Return of the Shadow King

Then the Box began to burn. Its flames turned red and black and, for the first time, gave off heat. The Arbiter swore he hadn't done anything to it "recently." As he walked toward it, he slowed. Chaos lightning in the colors of the Void's storms crackled around him, and a shadow peeled out of his body. "Something's… Something's wrong, Grandmaster." He collapsed, and the shadow and lightning were sucked in through the keyhole. The lid blew open, and a darting shadow cut down every goblin in the room.

The Grandmaster stood on her throne with [[The Oathbow]] drawn. A familiar whisper came from just behind her: "Hello, Elusia." It was **[[The Shadow King]]**. "Uncle." He told her that Vythorn had saved a piece of him, believing he could use it, while all along the Shadow King had been using Vythorn and clouding his mind against her. He had rebuilt himself from the Box's offerings, "a toe, a hand, a foot, cryptocurrency… Magic the Gathering cards." The power just drawn out of the Arbiter had made him ten times stronger than he had ever been. Shadow chains wrapped Elusia's limbs and throat and dragged her off the throne, and he sat down in it. "You mean what you stole from my family?" she spat. He replied that her family was his family. Dozens of [[The Dragon Knights|Dragon Knights]] stepped from the shadows, and the banners burned until the room was as dark as it had once been.

When Doug bared his little teeth, the Shadow King thrust a blade of shadow at him. The Arbiter rose and took it through the heart. "Run, run, Doug, run." Doug escaped through a doggy door. The Shadow King called it "a fitting end for a treacherous fool." Elusia begged Vythorn not to give up. Dying, he looked up at her and said, "I am sorry for so many things." She asked why. "For knowledge." He fell dead.

The chains pulled the Grandmaster down until she knelt before the uncle who calls her his daughter.

> **The Shadow King:** "That's so much better. Welcome and good night to D20 Deathmatch!"
