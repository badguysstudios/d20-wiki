## What happens
### Opening — the Dread Fortress and daylight savings
1. **0:07:09** Back in **the Dread Fortress**, the **Arbiter wanders the halls "clanking his chains behind him,"** looking for the Grandmaster. ("What is he always doing on a Sunday night wandering around? Nobody knows.")
2. **0:07:39** The Grandmaster comes running frantically down a stone hallway, opening and slamming doors and shouting for him. **A huddle of little goblins follows her, carrying the charcuterie board** ("because we always have one of those"). She is exhausted from running all over the fortress. She thinks the Deathmatch "was supposed to happen an hour ago" and that they're late.
3. **0:08:32** The Arbiter: "**You do know that it was daylight savings time, right?**" She freezes as it dawns on her. "**We have our own plane.** We could get rid of these stupid rules."
4. **0:08:58 — Bureaucracy.** He points out she could abolish it right now. She refuses. She puts it **on her agenda for Monday morning**, because "**It's not official unless it's done Monday through Friday between nine to five**." There is a procedure: it goes on an agenda, then the agenda is gone through and the act approved. "Is that how you think I rule this country? No. This plane? No. **There is order and process.**" The Arbiter: "I guess I'll just have to defer to your incredible business acumen."
5. **0:09:45** She realizes they're not late and smiles, slightly disappointed that she can't be righteously angry anymore. "Did you bring anyone at least?"
6. **0:10:09** The Arbiter claps. The sound waves visibly ripple through the halls, a portal tears open in time and space, fog rolls out and the champions step through: "**Derick Taladin versus Belet Bladesimmer.** Or something like that."

### The arena — a battle long lost
14. **0:15:12** The Arbiter cracks his back and knuckles ("Don't worry, I got this. This is my thing") and claps the portal open. It is **dark and musty, smelling "of old wood and old death."**

The grounds are ancient, gray and damp, and "**this soil has hungered for blood for hundreds of years.**" They stand inside a ring where some group made its final stand and lost. The **barricade is crushed and beaten, 5–10 feet high in places, made of broken wood**, and hard to get out of. Everything is damp and covered with broken bones and wood, "and maybe a few leftover people here and there."

The Grandmaster promises **great medical insurance, high coverage and benefits; a retirement fund if you make it out alive; and "a gym membership for the gym around the corner."** Belet: "Oh, don't worry about gym. You don't need to know who gym is. You're already pretty big."

## Who and what appears
### Characters
**Belet** (isfridayTV / Friday). Belet "Bladesimmer"/"Blesamar" (surname uncertain) **of Waterdeep**.
- A noble, **seventh in a long line**. Her family gives her nothing, so she adventures for treasure and **mostly kills evil dragons** (but some dragons are nice).
- A fighter: Battle Master maneuvers (pushing, lunging, feinting and trip attacks), Mage Slayer, action surge, second wind, Indomitable. Longsword and two family javelins with a Waterdeep inscription.
- **Squire Shala ("Charlie")**, rescued from the destroyed town of Greenest.
- **Founder (inferred) and member of the Big Nose Club**, which meets Tuesdays at 6 p.m.
