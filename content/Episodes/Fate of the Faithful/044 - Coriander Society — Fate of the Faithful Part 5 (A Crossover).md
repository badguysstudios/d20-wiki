The finale of the Coriander Society crossover sends [[John Carmichael]], [[Asena]] and the werebear warrior [[Lyrus]] into a burning keep in [[The Shadowlands]] for one last showdown with their old nemesis [[Ralzamon]]. There are giant mechs, a candle-headed adjudicator, a flower slime with main-character energy, and a cookie that cures being small. Under the explosions sits a quiet, strange ending: a broken man asking to be allowed to fall, and something ancient in the dark below him, waking up.

## The Story

### Fire and Ice

[[The Void]] is a patchwork of pieces torn from other worlds, and it is ruled by [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]], who stages her Deathmatches to keep the boredom of eternity at bay. In the world of [[Yord]], John and Asena were on the verge of finally beating the psychic mastermind Ralzamon, Master of the Unknown, when [[The Arbiter|the Arbiter]] reached across dimensions and pulled the villain's shade into the Void. The Arbiter meant to use Ralzamon's mind-reading powers to spy on the Faithful, the resistance standing between him and his coup against the Grandmaster. The heroes followed Ralzamon and joined forces with Lyrus of the Faithful. They tracked their quarry to a keep in the Shadowlands, where their ally [[Circe]], Master of Magitek, had blown Lyrus up to enormous size with her ray gun.

The sneaky approach did not survive contact with the enemy. Lyrus felled a Draconic Death Knight, one of the Arbiter's elite servants, but with its dying breath the knight unleashed a Hellball, an explosion of infernal flame. It turned every skeleton nearby into "ash and glass, slurry across the floor" and dropped the giant werebear with an impact that shook the whole fortress.

Circe swooped in on her flying boots and switched her Ray Gun of Amazing Transformation to freeze mode, sealing the dying Lyrus in a block of ice that kept him alive. High above, John hovered in his draconic form with three shadow clones as Aulis the Kindler, a raving fire priest in broken shackles, hurled a fireball at him. John grabbed the Infernal Talisman at his neck. His veins glowed scarlet and he absorbed the fireball, feeding its energy to his patron, [[Tiamat]]. Two psionic knights from the world of Lacunas fled from John's dragon-fear, summoned towering Bogatyr-class mechas, and opened fire with plasma cannons, blasting one of his clones to nothing.

John spotted chains to the north holding some entity in place and declared the rest of the fight "a distraction." Then he swept down with his gun Krontaka and riddled the Kindler with bullets ("gun, gun, gun, gun!"). He called out Ralzamon: "Hey, you pathetic bastard. Stop hiding down there. Come out and face us." As if in answer, Ralzamon floated up from below, glowing blue inside an enormous psychic visage. Beneath him, burning yellow eyes stared up out of the chained depths.

### Cookies, Souls and Thunder

Asena had been shrunk earlier in the adventure. Thanks to a gift from the watching audience, she found a "drink me / eat me" cookie of not being small in her pocket, complete with tag, and ate it. Back to full size, she punched a skeleton to pieces. Her amulet, the Fang of Goltharius, poured out silver mist, and she ripped the soul out of one skeleton and flung it at another. So little of its soul remained that the second skeleton's feelings were merely hurt. "It's very embarrassing. I'm going to punch him now," she said, and did. The Fang then awakened a new power, and Asena vanished in a flash and reappeared beside John high in the air.

Inside the ice, Lyrus refused to die. Thinking of the Arbiter and his father's legacy, he willed himself back: "No, no, there's more to do!" The ice exploded outward and he rose, nearly dead but determined. He healed himself, stepped through the Void and charged across the battlements like a linebacker. Circe flew alongside asking how cryogenic freezing had felt, since she would need "a full report" and possibly tissue samples. When he asked whether she had anything for the bleeding, she offered to freeze him again. He sliced a mecha's plasma bolts in half with his sword, and the pieces blew up two skeletons on the far horizon.

### The Face of a Loved One

John laid Tiamat's Curse on Ralzamon and opened fire, but the figure at the heart of the psychic storm changed. It now wore the face of someone John had not seen since his last mission on Earth, his beloved Carmen. He knew the trick and hesitated only for a moment. He then hurled Asena at Ralzamon in a classic astral yeet and wrapped her in icy armor as she flew. Ralzamon tried to pass his wounds into her mind, and she shut him out with a natural 20. She had no empathy left for the man who had been a thorn in her side from day one.

On the way, Asena's dimension-skipping picked up a passenger. Slakura, a little bouncing flower slime, landed on her shoulder. ("Oh, hello.") Then Asena reached Ralzamon's true self, Josiah Parsons, and cursed him "directly in the face, right in the teeth" before punching him with a fist of astral force.

Below, Lyrus remembered a moment at the Faithful's forge before the mission, when the goblin spy [[Kenny]] had slipped him a potion of healing: "Good luck out there, boss. Sorry I can't go with you." Mid-battle, Lyrus told a mecha, "one second, I'm kind of thirsty," and drank it. Then he slammed his thunder gauntlets together. The mechs were insulated against lightning, because they were built to fight the blue dragons that had overrun their planet, but nobody had planned for thunder. His anime-grade punch blew out the back of one mech. Short on time, he paid 500 gold for an extra burst of action ("Easy, I'm rich. I don't know if you guys know this. I'm loaded.") and kept swinging. Circe phased that mech into the Ethereal Plane. Meanwhile, a planar rift that had cracked open earlier drew in vampiric kobolds that crawled up the walls looking for blood. Their timing was poor, since the keep was mostly skeletons.

### Josiah Parsons

John delivered his verdict: "You've become that which you hate, Parson, and I'm going to destroy you. So just lie back, think of Yord, and just know that your spirit will return there soon." His amulet, the red kiss, whispered of vengeance and hatred, and he resisted it. Then he emptied a technically empty revolver: twelve magic missiles and four bullets. Meanwhile the candle-headed Scorched Adjudicator Vargas finally reached Lyrus, and dark, bloody wax ran down his face as he bit into Lyrus's soul.

John's barrage tore through Ralzamon's visage "like bullets through a windshield." The echo of Ralzamon pulled from Yord was gone. Asena was left holding the glowing Josiah Parsons by the throat over the pit, the version of him she had once faced in a Deathmatch. When she raised her fist again, she saw the man from the photograph, the one from the ill-fated jungle expedition that had changed her life and ended his.

"Asena, enough," he said. She replied: "What do you mean enough? You're not dead, so it's not enough." He warned her that the one who bound him would come looking for her if she killed him. He begged to be allowed to die alone and keep Yord out of that being's sight. Asena saw no lie in him, only a broken man who had fought her because he believed she was an abomination. "So you don't think I'm the downfall of Yord anymore?" she asked. "Things I've seen are far worse than anything you might be," he answered. She asked whether letting go would drop him in the pit. It would. **"I'll do that."** As he fell, his khaki expedition clothes turned back into the hooded robes he wore as a warrior of the Arbiter.

### Something in the Pit

Ralzamon's psychic might had been the only thing holding the chains below. Now the keep shook. When Asena looked down, the eyes looked back, and she felt her life draining toward them. She remembered a moment from her childhood in the tomb of Sennepolker, standing before something ancient that wailed with hunger for life. This was the same kind of hunger, but colder and more calculating, and its bonds were breaking. "You probably shouldn't look down there unless you want to go down there," she advised.

An enormous shadowy hand with joints that bent the wrong way gripped the top of the tower. A horned, wispy shape with glowing eyes hauled itself out. The last mercenary called for "a tactical withdrawal," and his own skeletons turned on him. Asena's stolen laser gun misfired with ruinous power, dumped its whole core, and brought a tower down on a mech. Lyrus shot Vargas point-blank with an ogre-sized revolver, spraying wax and blood everywhere, startled himself, and dropped both Vargas and the gun; Vargas fell forty feet. He landed beside Asena ("Where'd this guy come from?"). Lyrus decided firearms were not for him: "I don't like this." Circe froze Vargas with "the old nothing personal kid," and the battered mechas announced, "Critical damage sustained. Returning to base for repairs." "This is the base. We're leaving."

### Through the Ethereal

John and his images formed a ring, and "John Prime" poured out a silver ewer that raised a shimmering curtain into the Ethereal Plane. Before stepping through, Lyrus ripped open a portal and dropped the frozen Vargas right in front of the shadow monster. Circe admired the work, looked at the monster, said "Eh, screw it," and turned it into a chicken, at least for a while. The vampiric kobolds swarmed the deserting mercenary and drained his lifeblood, and whether Vargas survived depended on which wore off first, the ice or the chicken. Drifting through the muffled Ethereal, which felt "more like a blanket," Asena asked, "Am I in the back rooms?"

### Home of the Faithful

They emerged at [[The Faithful's hideout]], a blacksmith's forge, where [[Baptiste]] was pinning one of the only cushions to the wall with knives. John claimed they had fled immediately, then admitted, "Yeah, no, we killed Ralzamon." Lyrus took the ewer for [[The Seer]] to decide on, explaining that the old man was "like a sharp spoon." He called Ralzamon's survival "the enemy of my enemy," and said Kenny had new intelligence and another band of fighters was coming to help them reach the Grandmaster. The Seer wandered in with his vision clear again. He told Asena that we almost miss the things we chase the longest. "I will miss Ralseypoo," she said.

Lawrence asked whether Asena had taken anything from Ralzamon. A golden clasp she had torn from his cloak had become a scrap of his khaki collar, and it turned up inside Slakura. With it and a tuning fork attuned to Yord, Lawrence prepared a spell to do what few ever manage: leave the Void. While the Seer slept, John quietly swapped the real ewer for a replica and kept the original. He handed out farewell gifts. Kenny got a ninja disguise. [[Frank]] the cook got a vial of acid billed as a spice, which he drank, belched, and pronounced "pretty good." Baptiste got a vibronic knife "for a very personal stabbing." "John Carmichael, all of my stabbings are personal," Baptiste said. "I know," John whispered. Lyrus repaid him with 400 electrum and wished them a happy retirement before they fell in battle, then began shedding his armor on the floor. Circe left too: "As far away from the Arbiter as possible." Lawrence's circle flared purple and black, and Asena felt herself pulled home by her own memories, back to the Crystalline Cavern on Yord. Kenny's farewell: "I didn't really like those guys."

### The Ice Throne

Far away, in a twisted echo of the Grand Throne, the Arbiter sat on a throne of ice, white wings draped over its back and eight colored rings on his fingers. He was an ancient being who remembered the reign of [[The Shadow King|the king who ruled before the Grandmaster]]. He saw a frozen snapshot of the party slipping out of the Void, and among them perhaps one face he had once chosen himself. Before him stood Ralzamon, now merely the man who had faced Asena in the arena. "Once again, you were defeated by her," said the Arbiter. "She was not alone. This was no duel of honor," Ralzamon replied. The Arbiter waved it away. Killing him now would be "like stomping on an ant," and the next phase of his plan had begun. He had one last order: "Gather my champions. For the fight is near."
