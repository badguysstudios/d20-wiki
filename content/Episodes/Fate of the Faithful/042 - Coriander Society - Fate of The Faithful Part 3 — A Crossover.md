## What happens
### Recap (0:00:01 to 0:04:34)
1. **0:00:01** TrmntdByGnomes opens "Coriander Society Adventures" and D20 Deathmatch, episode three of the crossover, with Mudcat, Ninjaman Matt and Pods of War. The out-of-character banter is about too much D&D and depression ("last time on the D20 Deathmatch Depression Power Hour").
2. **0:02:00 — The story so far.** The heroes are in **the Hanging City of Prexmarin** looking for **Kenny the Goblin**, a member of **the Faithful** working undercover. He was trying to learn **Ralzamon's** plan to gain a powerful magical item: **a silver ewer that pours ethereal waters**. It would let **the Children of the Void** enter the Ethereal Plane, walk through walls and assassinate people "without needing the Arbiter's constant attention." The GM: "the guy's busy. He doesn't have time to rip portals for you everywhere." Drew: "**The portals don't just rip themselves.**" GM: "If it's worth ripping, it's worth ripping yourself."
3. **0:02:43** Ralzamon's psychic spying, "now working for the Arbiter," found Kenny. A kill team has come for him: **Circe, the Master of Magitek**, and **Ganyu Sinmal**, "a familiar foe to Jon and Asena." Ninjas stalk the heroes. **Lyrus is dueling Ganyu Sinmal**: "a mini D20 Deathmatch going on right here in the city."
4. **0:03:40** Matt reminds the table that **Asena is tiny**: Circe's shrink ray hit her last time. The GM switches the music to metal ("Death is yet to be determined").
5. **0:03:57** Recap of Lyrus's last turn. He "opened a can of whoop-ass" on Ganyu Sinmal. With every blow, the towering titan grows angrier, and fire flares through the cracks in his armor and "woofs" through **the metal grate that is his mouthpiece**.

### Round A — tiny Asena, the ninja, the power bomb
6. **0:04:34 — Tiny Asena.** She is the size of a house cat. The table talks in "mini mode" voices for her turn. "I don't like being tiny." **She picks up John** ("I just pick up John") and runs away.
7. **0:06:25** She slips in her slime companion **Slakura's** sweet-smelling slime trail (a Streamloots card) and falls flat on her face outside an office window.
8. **0:07:44 — Slakura the key.** Asena asks if Slakura can jump on the door handle and become a key. GM: "That's not how this works… For a point of inspiration, yes." She spends the inspiration. **Slakura takes the form of a key** ("apparently we're the Wonder Twins now"), slides under the door and opens it from the other side. Asena dashes in and closes the door behind her.
9. **0:09:17 — Tides of battle** shifts everyone. Asena slides east through a portcullis-locked door into **a treasury**: "This place is loaded. It's a life-changing amount of treasure." Because she's tiny and has Slakura, she can plunder it. Her rolls turn up **5,000 gold pieces and 200 platinum**, though she's "as small as a cat" and can't carry it.
10. **0:11:12 — The last ninja.** "Would it really be a Coriander Society episode without ninja-related violence?" Last time Asena's **dragon fear** scared most of the ninjas off; one is left. Respecting "the honor of the duel," it leaves Lyrus alone and leaps across the rooftops toward John. It can't see him, so it just skulks in the shadows.
11. **0:12:05 — Ganyu Sinmal's assault.** The armored, fiery creature regenerates. **He grabs "the Werebearer who just tore him apart with his Void Swords"** (opposed Athletics: 19 vs Lyrus's 14). He rages in Lyrus's face; it "sounds like the furnace from Home Alone." Then he **jumps ten times normal distance with Lyrus in his grasp, grows to Huge in midair, and power-bombs him back to the ground.** Lyrus rolls a natural 1 on the save ("I'll see you guys later"). He burns chat's inspiration on a reroll and takes half damage without being knocked prone. The slam almost flattens a bystander, and the shoppers scatter, screaming for the guards.
12. **0:14:16 — The bettors.** Through the roundhouse window John sees **two bear folk, an orc and "a very stylish elven bard"** watching, cheering and placing bets. **John: "Hey, how much you putting on me?"** One bear folk slaps the elf on the back and holds up a heavy coin purse.
13. **0:15:21** Battlefield shift: John moves one square ("could have gone over the edge"). Chat plays the **"Stabtiste" card**: +1d6 on a stabbing weapon or a roll to flirt (a pun on the Faithful's Baptiste).

### John and Circe

### Lyrus goes "even further beyond"

21. **0:26:21** The second swing hits. In fury, **Ganyu Sinmal opens the "spirit eater gate"** (it normally only works on **yokai blossom spirits**) and drains 56 necrotic from Lyrus, healing himself the same amount. **Lyrus's void energy erupts in retaliation (an Artificer "repulsion" ability) and blasts the titan 15 ft into a wall. Another critical, with triple weapon dice. **Ganyu Sinmal cracks open, violet light shining out, on the verge of destruction.** Only the life he drained through the spirit eater gate keeps him alive.

**[[Characters/Asena|Asena]]** (PodsofWar). Shrunk to house-cat size, then restored and enlarged.
- Has the slime companion **Slakura** (key-form, slime trails).
- **Powers:** telepathy; force-glowing punches; a once-per-day astral shift; a primal connection to nature and astral energies; "dragon fear."

**[[Characters/Ganyu Sinmal|Ganyu Sinmal]]** (GM). A towering, armored, fiery titan.
- Metal grate mouthpiece, purple belly fire, the "spirit eater gate"; grows to huge, then gargantuan.
- **A dead creature fused inside the armor, animated by Ralzamon**, who possessed it with ghostly blue eyes ("My master").
- Destroyed by Lyrus and fell into the Beast Below's grasp.

**[[Characters/Ralzamon|Ralzamon, Master of the Unknown]]** (offstage). The Arbiter's general and psychic spy, "middle management."
- **From the Coriander party's world, brought to the Void for the Deathmatches. He died there and is back via "Arbiter's magic or something."**
- Scans for brainwaves ("Kenny thoughts"). Lawrence has a theory about "what happened with" him.

**[[Characters/The Arbiter|The Arbiter]]** (offstage). Circe says deserters are hunted across all realities and torn apart atom by atom; he destroyed the ogre **Chester** and erased his echoes across the multiverse. "Not omniscient." Lyrus suggests they may have to fight him.

**[[Characters/Lawrence Booker Green|Lawrence Booker Green]]** (GM). The Coriander party's researcher at the Faithful HQ.

**[[Characters/Baptiste|Baptiste]]**. A Faithful member present at the HQ; the "Stabtiste" card is named for him; he stabs people.

**[[Characters/The Beast Below|The Beast Below / the lurker]]**. An ancient, chained, tentacled creature beneath Prexmarin that has slept since the place was drawn into the Void. It woke, broke its chains and tore apart the lower city.

**[[Characters/The red-bearded priest|The red-bearded priest]]** (unnamed). Broken-chained shackles, barefoot, serves "the master" and an "empire" that will "rise again." He took the fake ewer.

**[[Characters/The candle-wax creature|The candle-wax creature]]** (unnamed). Waxed face, odd wings, shirtless, twitching, "no longer human." It sends visions of a red moon, blood and murder.

**[[Characters/Mercenaries in Lacunian power armor|Mercenaries in Lacunian power armor]]**, like the Vosky Knights of Lacunas.
