The Coriander Society's second trip into [[The Void]] trades the arena for a shadow war. [[John Carmichael]] and [[Asena]] spend a night with [[Lyrus]] and the Faithful and eat a stew made of boots. Then they steal a glimpse of [[The Arbiter]] forging their old nemesis into something far worse, and go looking for a missing goblin spy in the rain-soaked lower districts of [[Prexmarin]]. It ends mid-fight, with Asena shrunk to the size of a cat, Lyrus locked in a duel with a growing armored monster, and something unknown crawling toward them from between dimensions.

## The Story

### Previously, in the Void

John and Asena had chased their arch-nemesis **[[Ralzamon]], Master of the Unknown**, into the Void. Just as they were about to finish him, a mysterious figure dragged him away by the will of the Arbiter. The heroes landed in the hanging city of Prexmarin, which floats in the Void around [[The Dread Fortress]]. There they met Lyrus, a leader of the Faithful, a secret organization fighting on the Grandmaster's behalf against the Arbiter's coming coup. After cutting through Hothim the Blood Berserker and a pack of knights, they retreated to the Faithful's headquarters, the Forge of the Faithful, to rest.

### A Bad Night and a Worse Breakfast

Nobody slept well. Asena had never been beaten so low and kept turning over the question of how Ralzamon could be alive. Lyrus slept on his enormous work table, which doubled as his drafting desk and his bed and was warping under him, its nails popping loose. The Forge had blacksmiths but no carpenter ("We're in the market for one"). Before bed, John thanked Lyrus for the rescue by spending two hours etching a magical rune of Celtic knotwork into his palm, one that fires bolts of force. Lyrus was surprised it didn't hurt and suspected that "my father would probably say this is cheating."

Morning brought a blessing. The Acolytes called on **[[Doug the Pug]]**, and it was a bones day: the cyborg zombie doggo shone his favor on the party, leaving them fortified for the day ahead. Breakfast was less of a blessing. **[[Frank]]**, the Faithful's cook, was stirring bits of tanned hide into the cauldron "for their nutritional value. It's high in fiber," along with beans, market meat, possibly a rat that "scurried too close," and pineapple. The dish was quickly christened "the Hawaiian boot."

John magicked his bowl into tasting like steak. Asena, who has a cast-iron stomach, declared "I think this tastes great." Lyrus, dead inside, shovelled his down without a word and went back for seconds. Guests weren't allowed out for food unless the Faithful's dagger-man [[Baptiste]] escorted them, so John stuck with the boot and somehow kept it down. Their ally [[Lawrence Booker Green]] looked a little pale afterward. Nobody did the dishes, so John magically scrubbed the whole room spotless while conjuring dancing brooms that accomplished nothing at all.

### The Seer

Lyrus then introduced **[[The Seer]]**, an ancient, blind white dragonborn blacksmith with worn scales and missing teeth. He greeted Lyrus with: "I can hear you, Lyrus. I can smell you. You smell like shoe leather." He mused that "everyone you know and care about eventually dies," and then, without eyes, savaged the craftsmanship of John's revolver, Krontaka. Krontaka is sentient enough to take offense and sent John a pulse of pure indignation. John defended his "dear friend," a bronze gun forged from an ancient spear and a slain devil's blood. The two men eventually settled it with winks.

The Seer sensed in Asena "the nature of a dragon… Though not one from this plane," and a lineage she'd rather leave buried. She told him she was probably older than he was. He admired her smart laser, which she had ripped off an airplane, and foresaw her decimating many a foe with it. For John he saw a diverging path: one road home, another "into the thralls of a duty thrust upon you," and a choice someday between what he wants and his honor.

Lyrus refused a reading, until John reminded him, "We all ate the boots, dude." The Seer's verdict was grim:

> **The Seer:** "Within you I see a tumultuous future, Lyrus. All of strife and pain. You will fight. And you will fight again and again. Until you are amongst the sand."

"What the fuck does that mean?" Lyrus demanded, and stalked off. Asena offered her own interpretation: he should avoid sand. Once Lyrus was gone, the Seer explained that Lyrus had been under great strain since his father **[[Orin]]** died. When Asena said Lyrus was not a great man, the old smith grinned toothlessly: "Not yet." Then he shuffled off, bumping into walls. Lyrus insisted the "old charlatan" could see just fine and just liked the sympathy.

### Guns, Maps and Sparring

John handed the Seer a sword from the tombs on Ashen to be reforged into a knife for his cat, Chester. The Seer was unfazed: "You act like this is the first time I've made a dagger for a cat." He gummed the metal, then pulled from his robes a glowing turquoise crystal warhammer as tall as himself. John also gave his huge five-shot Ogre Revolver to Lyrus, who decided he needed to be "adaptable. Now that I'm in leadership here." On the target range, his first shot went straight through the wall and winged somebody on the other side.

Planning wasn't Lyrus's strength; that was the job of the Faithful's spy **[[Kenny]]**, a small, obnoxious goblin who gambles, though not well. Kenny was overdue. Worse, the Arbiter's servants, the Children of the Void, seemed to know the Faithful's moves moments before they made them, and the trouble had begun right when Ralzamon arrived. The Seer had found no mole. John set out alone into the rainy city, wearing disguise after disguise.

Meanwhile Lyrus showed Asena his hand-drawn maps. He had once dreamed of being a cartographer, but his strict father sent him to war, a man "unable to adapt to new information very well. Probably the reason he never saw the Arbiter's betrayal." He showed her the Dread Fortress, ringed by lava "in all directions," and [[The Eastern Outpost]], where Orin guarded [[The Box of Chaos]] for many years until his death. After Orin died, many soldiers left to work in the shadows. Asena told him what it's like to be a [[Champions|champion]]: you're sent home and remember nothing, except in dreams. Lyrus said that in the Void, dreams are often windows to other planes and times.

### John's Investigation

John's legwork paid off. People had been asking about a magical silver ewer lost in **[[The Tumult]]**, a planar region made from a dead god or titan's body. Poured out, the ewer opens a curtain into the ethereal realm that a whole strike team can walk through. Kenny had been hunting it. Hothim had gone door to door looking for Kenny, and agents dressed as the Grandmaster's soldiers kept hounding him. Kenny was now pinned down in a thieves' safe house somewhere in the city. John also worked out the leak: there was no mole. Ralzamon's psychic powers, amplified in the Void, were how the Arbiter's side knew everything. "It's just Ralzamon being Ralzamon."

Back at the Forge, John knocked "Shave and a Haircut" while wearing Lyrus's face. Baptiste, bristling with daggers, asked how long the disguise lasted. John ran a finger up his longest blade and answered: "Probably about as long as you could stick that within somebody." Baptiste: "Very interesting. I will call you." In the map room, a shirtless Lyrus was letting Asena shoot him with her smart laser, and he barely flinched: "That's pretty good. I've seen better."

### The Vision

John spotted a silver ewer on the Seer's workbench, but the Seer stopped him: he had just made it for Lyrus, and it was a replica meant to be swapped for the real thing. John wanted more, so he gripped the Seer's shoulder and reached for the wizard sight he once had. Both men's eyes swirled silver. Because of her bond with Ralzamon, Asena was pulled into the vision too.

They saw an enormous icy throne room and the Arbiter: taller than Orin, with glowing white wings, spiked black-and-white leathers and eight colored rings. Beside him stood Josiah Parsons, resurrected and glowing with psychic light. With the ease of buttering toast, the Arbiter reached through space and time, pulled out Ralzamon's dying spirit "fresh from your battle," and slammed the two together, "two versions of the same person into one." The blast cracked the ice of the whole throne room. The Arbiter named the new Ralzamon his general, promised him his desires once the Grandmaster was gone, and warned that failure would mean destruction "in every time. In every plane."

> **Ralzamon:** "There is no reason for failure. We are master of the unknown. There will be no secrets for the Arbiter."

Ralzamon's awareness spread across the Void, touching mind after mind. Then he turned and looked straight at John and Asena, with recognition, hatred and despair on his face. He had seen them. Asena summed it up for Lyrus: "Rousey Poo, Josiah… Josiah Poo? Rousey Parsons." Lyrus explained that with the Arbiter's power over chaos, "even death is not beyond his reach." The plan was set: rescue Kenny, find the real ewer, and swap in the fake. It might even be the heroes' ticket home. Everyone put their hands in, including Baptiste, the hammer-swinging Seer, Chester, and **[[Slakura]]**, a tiny pink slime who appeared on Asena's shoulder. Unnoticed by anyone, Asena's laser had started leaking radiation.

### The City of Chains

Lyrus led them through planar back roads into Prexmarin's lower districts. The city's neighborhoods hang on enormous chains rising into fog, and one district below had broken its chain long ago and now hung tilted and abandoned. The safe house was said to be near the Roundhouse tavern. John disguised himself as Kenny and went gambling, because that's what Kenny would do, while Asena tried to walk "normal" and Lyrus whistled with his hands in imaginary pockets. Ninjas gathered on the rooftops.

A roachling gambler let slip the secret: the gang's lead-lined, warded hideout was inside the Roundhouse, and the password was ordering a "shooie," a drink poured from a boot. John also sensed something psychic leeching information from the roachling's mind. Outside, heavy metal footsteps thudded closer, and Lyrus felt a fiendish presence through the stones.

Then a head popped over the rooftops: "Hello, lovelies!" It was **[[Circe]]**, self-proclaimed master of Magitek and "the most nefarious inventor in the city of Beacon," armed with a homemade ray gun and looking for a little goblin. Her muscle followed: **[[Ganyu Sinmal]]**, an enormous suit of armor with a furnace-grate mouth full of violet fire, covered in burning seals. Asena recognized him as a sometime friend, sometime enemy, and his flames burned hungrily at Slakura. John charmed a guard and dressed him up as Kenny as a decoy, but Circe's goggles of all seeing saw straight through it: "You are going to have to get up earlier in the morning to pull one over on Circe, the Master of Magitek."

### The Fight Begins

John's attempt at a secret signal pose failed, so he shot them. A fireball bullet from Krontaka shook the whole district and blew up every ninja at once. Circe stood untouched in her fireproof "baffling blouse of the elements," and was in the middle of explaining it when John shot her twice. She answered with her transformation ray. Asena caught it in silver astral light and shoved it back in her face, which impressed the inventor more than it hurt her, and Circe then shrank Asena to the size of a cat.

The Acolytes offered the Glory of [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]]: her blessing to whoever defeats the most worthy foe in single combat. Lyrus pointed his swords at Ganyu. "We must feed," the armor rumbled. "Oh, I could eat," said Lyrus. "All right, maybe after the fight." He sliced an explosive barrel through a portal and dropped it from the sky, stepped out behind Ganyu, and the two went into full anime power-up mode. Void energy wrapped Lyrus in a black aura as he hacked away, while purple fire roared from Ganyu's wounds and he grew larger. Tiny Asena bounced off Slakura onto Circe, cursed her with a tattoo John helped power, and slipped onto John's shoulder. But her jump across the planes sent a flare through the dimensions, and the episode ended on unknown figures weaving through the nether toward the heroes.
