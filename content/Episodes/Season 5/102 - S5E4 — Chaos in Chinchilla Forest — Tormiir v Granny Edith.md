## What happens
### Opening — the Dread Fortress kitchen
1. **0:00:00** Narration: the camera follows **the Lord of Fate** through the halls of the Dread Fortress. His **fine Italian leather boots clack on the stones** ("with an Italian accent because they're Italian"). He walks with his arms folded behind his back, looking for Thea.
2. **0:00:41** Thea squeaks from behind the kitchen doors: "I'm in here!" **She is packing a picnic basket.**
   - **Food:** peanut butter and honey sandwiches with extra peanuts on top "to make them super crunchy," apple slices and apple juice.
   - **Chips:** she flies to the cabinet and throws things out ("those are Crackers, those are cookies, where are the chips?"). The Lord of Fate says he isn't picky. Thea: "that's the wrong answer. I thought you were in charge because you knew things." She packs **three family-sized bags of Cool Ranch Doritos**, grinding her teeth.
3. **0:02:08** He asks whether she set up the deathmatch. "Of course I did. It was the very first thing I packed." She pulls out **a second giant picnic basket of snacks and water bottles for the champions**, so they stay hydrated while they "tear each other's flesh from their bones and snap each other's femurs."
4. **0:02:44 — The star.** The Lord of Fate is "actually a little impressed. You've been stepping up your game the last few weeks… That's going on the report." Thea asks for a gold star (then three, then five). **He draws a star on her forehead with his ink pen.** "Ow. Thank you." She promises the champions are worth "five cold stars." He: "Let's start with one."
5. **0:03:59 — The doughnut portal.** Thea pulls a doughnut from the basket and blows on it. It grows to **about 12 feet tall, with pink icing and sprinkles, and a void space forms in the doughnut hole.** She presents "Granny Edith" and "Tormiir" (misheard "Granny Death" and "Dormir").
   - Granny: "Well, hello there, dear fellow."
   - Tormiir: "I'm gonna murder all of you."

### The champions
6. **0:05:10 — Granny Edith.**
   - She "just love[s] baking things" and heard people here could do "some good old-fashioned home cooking."
   - **Looks:** a very little old lady with gray curls and giant Coke-bottle glasses. Her apron is dusty with flour and its pockets hold spoons and whisks. She carries **a giant rolling pin as big as she is.**
7. **0:05:45 — The age bracket.** The Lord of Fate complains to Thea: "a few weeks ago, it was an old man, and then it was a teenage boy, and now another elderly woman… can we not shoot for the middle of the age bracket?" Thea insists Granny is middle-aged.
   - Granny: "**my grandpappy lived till he was 200**, so I'm hoping I can live that long."
   - To the Lord: "**stressed is desserts spelled backwards**." She offers him a cupcake from her apron. He finds it "oddly comforting."
8. **0:06:37 — The optics.** The Lord of Fate: "I came into this seat of power rather abruptly, and… not everyone's happy about it. And so if I have old women getting beaten to death in the arena, you see how that's a bad look for me."
9. **0:07:10 — Tormiir.** Thea fumbles the pronunciation ("Tormiir, or is it Tormiir?").
   - **Looks:** a minotaur in **a purple jumpsuit with gold bangles**. Gold bands circle his horns, neck, arms and ankles ("who doesn't love a matching set?"). He carries **a big old brass sword**, and **purple magic is always spilling out of every orifice.**
   - "He just really loves killing old women… He was put on this earth to just flatten geriatrics."
10. **0:08:03** Thea hands both champions giant jugs of water. **Granny pours hers into a giant teacup she keeps on her belt.**
11. **0:08:37 — Consent forms.** The Lord of Fate: "if she's signed a consent form… I guess this works." Thea: "**I don't actually know what the forms say because the chinchillas wrote them**… we will definitely not ever get sued, ever, ever."

### The arena — the Chinchilla Forest
12. **0:09:07** Thea stands in the doughnut hole and pulls its sides down "like a curtain." The scene drops into **the Chinchilla Forest**. She flies through trailing a puff of ink dust.
    - **The chinchillas:** the Lord of Fate says the forest "allows the acolytes this week to see **where we source our local chinchillas**." Thea: the chinchillas "run wild and free and **sometimes nibble on the bones of the champions who've been left to rot here**… so they grow up big and strong."
13. **0:10:09** Granny asks for a stove and swats flies. She doesn't need one to kill a minotaur: "I got **79 great-great-great-grandchildren**, so he's gonna be no problem at all." Tormiir: "I'll wait for them to get old and run them over, too." Thea finds that "really actually considerate."
14. **0:10:49 — The secret kitchen.** Thea whispers to Granny: "**the chinchillas do have like a secret kitchen, but they hide it in different places**." Nobody is supposed to know chinchillas can cook, or "they would always have to serve people… and they are really terrible hosts because they hate people."
15. **0:11:30 — Words of wisdom.** The Lord of Fate stands and adjusts his robes: "champions who are going to die, please make it interesting. I get bored very easily… I kind of had a long week and I need this."
    - Thea gets in both their faces: "I want to see blood. I want to see sweat. I want to see the dirt in this place torn up from every corner of this forest."
    - **Above them hang the arena stands, full of cheering Acolytes:** "They are the ones who have come to witness your story."
16. **0:13:05** **Tormiir wins initiative.** The Lord of Fate: "good job, Thea. This is the start we wanted." "I've been working on my pronunciation."

### Round 1
17. **0:13:37 — Snout flame.** Tormiir points his snout at Granny and blows **a big purple warlock flame**. It's a **natural 1**: "I just sneeze and I burn my own face." Granny: "You're gonna have to try harder than that, dearie."
    - A sidebar asks where a minotaur counts as cow. Tormiir: "I'm mostly cow." "Where does it count?" "The horns."
18. **0:15:28 — Eye juice.** "You know how some people can squirt milk out of their eyes?" **He holds his nose and shoots warlock magic from his eye holes.** It misses. Granny: "**Who taught you your table manners, young man? That's disgusting.**" Tormiir swats a fly.
19. **0:16:51 — Flour in the eyes.** "Now, listen here, little son of [Jim?]. I don't appreciate your attitude." **Granny throws a handful of flour from her apron into his eyes** to blind him. Tormiir defends.
    - Tormiir: "**Time to make biscuits with you, old lady.**" Granny: "Oh, that sounds actually quite delightful."
    - Tormiir: "No, no, like, you're the main ingredient. Like, I'm threatening you." Granny: "Oh, well, thank you, darling. No one's told me that for many years."
20. **0:18:16 — Kiss cam.** **A giant jumbotron appears in the sky with the kiss cam on Tormiir and Granny.** Tormiir offers his cheek, gives her "a little smooch-a-rooney," and adds just before pulling back: "I will kill you." Granny: "Oh, you so cheeky." Tormiir: "I love her. I don't want to kill her anymore. No, I mean, I'm going to destroy her."
21. **0:18:55 — Love tap.** "This is just going to be a little love tap." **Granny swings the rolling pin as he pulls back from the kiss.** Tormiir rolls **another natural 1 on defense.** He spins around with **little cakes circling his head** "to keep it in the theme." He now has two fatigue.
22. **0:20:10 — Belch at Thea.** Thea, hovering close, crows at him. Tormiir uses a free attack to **open his mouth and belch fiery breath at "the little flappy one."** It hits. Thea: "I can't breathe, I can't breathe, it's so strong."
    - **The Lord of Fate snaps his fingers and reverses time so Thea's skin reforms.** He orders her out of range.
    - Thea: "I wanna know… what it smells like."
    - Lord of Fate: "**You are not a champion. You are an admin.** You're supposed to be on this side of the wall. If you want to fight to the death, I can certainly arrange it."
    - She hides behind the wall, peeking over it and glaring at Tormiir.
23. **0:22:29 — Biccy and tea.** Granny nibbles cookies: "**it's 11 o'clock, it's time for [biccy] and tea**." She regains two fighting spirit with her "fortifying cookies."
    - Thea begs a chocolate chip cookie. The Lord of Fate has **his own giant tray of Oreos**.
    - Thea swallows hers whole and you can see the cookie in her tiny neck. Tormiir: "I just really need some milk." Thea punches her throat until it breaks up.
24. **0:23:40 — End of round.** Thea's round roll is a 2. The Lord of Fate: "You should be happy that **I'm not making Doug roll for you**… I could go get him." Thea: "No! I can do my job."
    - Thea announces Granny is ahead ("It means you're losing!").
25. **0:24:32 — Prizes.** Thea offers the winner "one thing that you really want. Maybe a new kitchen, maybe more gold horns… What do cows like? Grass?"
    - Tormiir wants "**Your head on a platter.**" The Lord of Fate: "**Sold.**" Thea: "Granny, I'm rooting for you now."
    - Granny: "I just want the world to be happy… **all you need is loaves.**"
    - Thea suggests she could just chop off her own head; the Lord of Fate is "considering that as well."

### Round 2
26. **0:25:34 — Ear holes.** Tormiir's accent has "flown wildly all over the place… he's a cow on the range." **His ears perk up and fire warlock blasts from both ear holes at once.** Both miss. He sadly cleans an ear with a Q-tip.
    - The Lord of Fate: "I feel terrible for your wife." Tormiir: "No, she loves it."
27. **0:27:05 — Never killed an old lady.** The Lord of Fate: "I'm starting to think that you've never killed an old lady at all." Tormiir admits it: "**I've only killed warriors in battle. Only the biggest and the strongest. I've never killed an old lady.** I'm so ashamed." "This guy doesn't have it."
28. **0:27:39 — Five wars.** Granny consoles him: "**I've lived through five major world conflicts**… you're young, you got your whole life ahead of you. Well, the rest of this match."
    - Thea sits at her feet like a grandchild. Granny starts: "**the year was 1725. I was just but a young girl of 17**… My grandpappy, you see, he had this little farm."
    - The Lord of Fate cuts her off. Granny invites Thea over for tea later. Lord: "You guys can go hold hands at the World's Fair."
29. **0:28:51 — Pep talk as psychic attack.** "It seemed like you're having a mental breakdown. But I believe in you." **Her encouragement counts as a psychic attack.** Tormiir rolls a **natural 20 on defense** and is empowered (+1 fighting spirit, more adrenaline).
    - Thea explains over-adrenaline with weights and treadmills.
    - Tormiir: "You know how you kill rhinos?… You chase them around until they adrenalize and poison their own bloodstream." "We all learned something awful today." Thea: "I don't want to do this anymore."
30. **0:30:48 — Granny's Cookies (signature move).** Granny is upset he didn't laugh at her joke.
    - Tormiir: "I hope it's not what I'm thinking it is." Thea: "Get your head out of the oven." Granny: "Kitchen's getting real hot all of a sudden."
    - **She twirls the rolling pin, builds a big spin and throws it rolling across the floor to flatten him** ("We're making Tormiir cookies").
    - It's a **tie**: he dodges, and both take fatigue. Tormiir: "Mr. Hammond, I would no longer like to support your park."
31. **0:32:47 — Hip flask.** The rolling pin rolls back to her and she catches it, wheezing. "**I'm going to take out my little hip flask, take a little shot**… don't tell anyone about that. It's just between you and me." She clears a fatigue.
    - Her next rolling-pin swing misses. "Stop belching in my face, it's disgusting!"
    - Thea: "You've got the best cookies in all of the Void."
32. **0:33:57** Thea's round roll is 18 ("a total of 20… Zero. Which is probably… the number of orifices Tormiir has"). Tormiir: "More than you'd expect from a cowman." The Lord of Fate: "He has as many accents as orifices."
33. **0:34:48 — The sandwich.** Thea offers water and sandwiches, but not the chips: "The chips are for us." **She throws a sandwich far away into the stands, to Acolyte Alan Wigginson**, taunting Tormiir.
34. **0:35:50 — Horn beam at Thea.** **Warlock power charges between Tormiir's horns "like a mystical tuning fork" and blasts a column at Thea.** "All attacks on Thea are free tonight."
    - **Thea dodges like a matador, waving a small red cloth and stepping aside.**
    - Tormiir watches the Acolyte eating his sandwich: "fuck this, man… I came here to kill the old lady."

### Round 3 — chaos event: the metal chairs
35. **0:37:26** The Acolytes trigger a chaotic event (Tormiir's roll, 13). Thea checks the Lord of Fate brought folding chairs. He: "**I am always sitting on a folding chair just in case.**"
    - Thea: "a glint catches your eye. Is it a shiny bird? A launched trash can? **A silver 1998 Chevy Impala with the Michigan license plates?** No! It's a metal chair hurled at the two of you from the Lord of Fate."
36. **0:38:54** Both fail the save. **The Lord of Fate vibrates and separates into two duplicates of himself.** They rise into the sky, spread their **white wings**, and dive "like synchronized swimmers," **smacking both champions across the face with metal chairs.** Each loses fighting spirit and gains fatigue. **Tormiir is at four fatigue**, one natural 1 from death.
37. **0:40:15 — Mother's milk.** Tormiir reaches into his jumpsuit as if for pistols and **pulls out two bottles of milk, which he chugs** to clear two fatigue. "**Imported from my mother.**" Lord: "Wait, are you still breastfeeding? How old are you?"
    - Tormiir to the stands: "**You all eat cheese. You all drink milk from different species. You're all judging me for drinking my own mother's milk. We're cow people, you freaks.** It's meant for us… Burn in the Seven Hells."
    - Someone asks for some. "No. It's for me from mommy." Thea: "I think we found Tormiir's [soap] box."
38. **0:41:56 — The hug.** Granny waddles over and gives him a big hug that squeezes "a little too tight." It misses. "Oh, you're a very strong boy." Tormiir: "My neck is thicker than her entire body. All the milk… I'm a growing boy."
39. **0:42:29 — Cake.** Granny feels sorry for him and tries to **feed him cake to put him in a food coma** (a status effect). Tormiir rolls **another natural 20 on defense**. He regains fighting spirit but his third adrenaline gives him a fatigue. "This cake made me so sleepy."
40. **0:43:38 — Boxing his ears.** "I've tried to give you a hug. I've tried to give you my cake and you're not having any of it. So it's time to box those ears." The first swing misses.
    - On the second, **Tormiir's natural 20 defense ties it**: both regain fighting spirit, then both gain fatigue. Granny: "We've done, like, no damage to each other."
41. **0:45:10** Thea offers Granny Doritos. Granny: "**I don't like those newfangled snacks. I got some crackers right here.**" They're multigrain, "really, really dry."
42. **0:45:38** Thea has Granny roll the round-end die for her ("I remember when I found you in your kitchen and you were making the most delicious smelling food"). It's a 9.
43. **0:46:10 — The Box of Chaos.** The Lord of Fate: "This entire fight right now is kind of absurd. I think it's time."
    - He reaches into the sky. **Chaos lightning storms crackle multi-colored lightning, and the Box of Chaos slams onto the field.** A column of **pink and yellow fire** shoots up as **a shadowy figure claws its way out.**
    - You sacrifice an action to it for a magical item. Feeling "magnanimous," he makes it free.
    - Thea: "You get a free action and you get a free action!… Everyone gets a free action!" She uses hers to **stuff the entire bag of Doritos into her mouth, bag and all.** "I was gonna eat some of those." "You're a lord now, this is fine." Thea: "Wait, am I a lord now?" "Yes." (Then "He just turns into a mirror," unclear.)

## Who and what appears
### Characters
**Tormiir** (Cucumber). A minotaur warlock ("I don't talk about it a lot").
- **Looks:** purple jumpsuit, gold bangles and bands on his horns, neck, arms and ankles. Purple magic spills from all of his (27) orifices.
- **Powers:** warlock blasts from his snout, eyes, ears and mouth. **Signature move: a purple beam charged between his horns** ("Kamuhamuha wave").
- **Weapon:** a brass sword, turned into **an onyx blade** by the Box of Chaos (destroyed on a nat 1).
- **Milk:** drinks his mother's milk, kept in bottles, an emergency stash in a hidden sporran, and a portal of udders. Rages at anyone else drinking milk or eating cheese, though he admits he loves cheese and milk himself.
- **History:** says he has only ever killed warriors, never an old lady. "On the range my whole life… everywhere and nowhere." Accent drifts, finally Scottish.
- **Death:** a gluten allergy / celiac disease. Loves morning grass and petrichor. Died beside Granny.

**Granny Edith** (guest). A tiny old lady baker.
- **Looks and gear:** gray curls, Coke-bottle glasses, a floury apron full of spoons and whisks, **a rolling pin as big as she is**, a giant teacup on her belt, a hip flask, and an electric beater with an arcane battery pack.
- **Signature move: Granny's Cookies** (throwing the spinning rolling pin; running on top of it).
- **Family:**
  - Her grandpappy lived to 200.
  - She has 79 great-great-great-grandchildren.
  - She grew up on her great-grandpappy's farm "up north."
  - **Gerald** is "waiting for" her in death (relationship unstated).
- **History:** a girl of 17 in "1725." Lived through five major world conflicts.
- **The Box of Chaos** awoke her yellow-eyed inner monster (doubled nat 20 effects).
- **Sayings:** "stressed is desserts spelled backwards," "all you need is loaves," "where there's a whisk, there's a way," "life is what you bake it."
- Favorite food: her great-grandma's bread and butter pudding.

**The Lord of Fate** (Drew). Came into "this seat of power rather abruptly… not everyone's happy about it," and worries about optics.
- Keeps a **report** on Thea; draws stars on her forehead in ink.
- **Reverses time** to heal Thea. **Splits into two winged duplicates** with metal chairs. Summons the Box of Chaos.
- Always sits on a folding chair. **Outlawed tuna juice.** Threatened to have Doug roll for Thea.

**Thea** (Bec). The Lord of Fate's imp assistant, "an admin, not a champion."
