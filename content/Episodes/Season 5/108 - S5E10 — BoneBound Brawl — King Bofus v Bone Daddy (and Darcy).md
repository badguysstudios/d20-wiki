On a frozen mountaintop, the self-proclaimed frog royal **[[King Bofus]]** faced **[[Bone Daddy]]**, a whip-cracking champion returning from an earlier season, along with his skeletal partner **[[Darcy]]**. Over one fight, Bofus turned out to be Bone Daddy's father's ex-lover, and the ghost of a conspiracy theorist made several appearances. The battle ended with a tidal wave of bone that buried both champions beneath a brand-new mountain.

## The Story

### Winter Clothes in the Attic

The night began with [[The Arbiter|the Lord of Fate]] searching [[The Dread Fortress|the Dread Fortress]] for [[Thea]]. He passed through the kitchen, the brewery, [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]]'s old bedroom, his own bedroom and Thea's old bedroom, "which is now an office." He finally climbed a large keep to the attic. There, in a maze of boxes, Thea was bundling a line of [[Goblins|goblins]] into mismatched scarves, mittens and hats in checkers and polka dots. She warned one that it would lose its fingers if it skipped its gloves.

Thea then wrapped a goblin-sized scarf around the Lord of Fate, where it looked like a thin thread, and tried to force a child's glove onto his hand. He refused: "I'm the Lord of Fate. I don't need to wear gloves outside." Thea was unmoved: "You can still get frostbite." Asked where all the clothes had come from, she said simply, "Boxes." The attic also held a horse, some photo albums and a whole box of crowns, every one too big for her head.

The Lord of Fate offered a fix. With a snap of his fingers, he swelled her head "like Megamind… like a pear." Thea collapsed under the weight of her enormous brain, wailing "I got too many thoughts!" He snapped it back, and she resigned herself to buying crowns that fit. She then opened a pink portal where a window had been and announced the night's champions.

### A Frog King and a Bone Daddy

Bone Daddy stepped out first, and the Lord of Fate squinted at him: "Didn't he die? Didn't you die?" Bone Daddy explained, behind very reflective sunglasses, that he could never truly die in any way that matters, as long as "Darcy, my true love, nurses me back with the power of bone and the power of daddy." Thea promptly bowed at his feet. The Lord of Fate begged her not to encourage him, while admitting he had enjoyed the man's "gyrations" last time.

King Bofus arrived in a frog mask topped with a crown. They explained that the mask had not yet been removed from their head. Bofus had fought here before against [[Richard Knottley]], "a man who threw a Peloton bike at me, and this is infinitely weirder." Bofus quickly learned Bone Daddy's given name, Bonald Daddison, and refused to stop using it. They described themselves this way:

> **King Bofus:** "I, the man frog you see crouched before you in a frog-like pose, is 50% man, 50% frog, 50% idiot, and 50% not good at percentages."

Bone Daddy turned out to be a devoted mommy's boy. His mother was the only one allowed to call him Bonald, and her advice about the cold explained his sleeveless coat. Then a skeleton ambled out with her arms in front of her. This was Darcy. Bofus asked whether she was a dog, a child or a mother, and Bone Daddy admitted that "the relationship hasn't really been set in stone yet." Thea nodded: "We've discovered labels are really restrictive."

### The Pom-Pom Portal

Thea handed out pom-pom hats that transformed into each wearer's dream hat. A spare hat grew until its pom-pom became a portal, and Thea flew through on pink impish wings, trailing glitter. Bone Daddy's pom-pom turned out to be made of baby teeth. He explained that "the Tooth Fairy is my ex. Complicated woman, but I'm a complicated man." Bofus's hat sat on their crown, which sat on the mask, and it took the shape of Bone Daddy's mother.

Bone Daddy's actual mother was sitting in the stands beside the goblins, wearing a tooth pom-pom of her own. When he pointed out that nobody had offered Darcy a hat, Thea burst into tears that dripped out of her eyes as literal crocodiles ("This is how crocodiles are made"). She made amends with a wrapped present: a hat with three pom-poms, which Darcy tore open with her long bone claws.

### Whips, Frogs and a Ghost

[[The Acolytes]] hurled blue slushies, and Bone Daddy struck first with a natural 20. He spun his whip overhead and charged in like a cowboy ("Yippee-ki-yay!"), cracking it across Bofus's chest while his mother high-fived the crowd. When he tried to wrap the whip around Bofus's neck, the frog king only blushed. A shudder ran up the mask and the crown dinged. "Joke's on you, I'm into this."

Darcy wrapped Bofus in a crushing hold. As Bofus struggled, they relived the memory of their last fight, and the ghost of Richard Knottley appeared in a tin hat, muttering about "the Chinchillin government." Darcy collapsed into a heap of bones, except for the arms and ribs still clamped around Bofus like a bone jacket. Bofus answered with a gatling gun of frogs fired from under the mask. Bone Daddy whipped every one aside, and each frog hopped off with a small red welt on its behind. One winked at him, and he winked back.

Bofus then ripped Darcy off their body and swung her like a whip, so she coiled around Bone Daddy "like a python made of bones." Bone Daddy shrugged it off with another natural 20. He pulled Darcy's scattered bones back together with a gesture, and the two of them high-fived. Thea flew down to give the flagging king a pep talk, nose to mask, until Bofus roared, "I'm King Man-Frog Bofus!"

### Totino's, Tongues and Big Milk

The Acolytes demanded chaos, and the arena filled with the smell of each champion's favorite food. Bofus resisted. Bone Daddy did not: "I need my Totino's." His mother launched pizza rolls fifty yards, Darcy caught armfuls, and he whipped one into his mouth.

Bone Daddy then raised Darcy's "brothers and sisters" from the soil to seize Bofus's limbs. The corpses were still at the zombie stage, and their skin sloughed off as Bofus simply walked free. To everyone's horror, Bofus stood up on two legs. Darcy's attempt to trip Bofus failed when the frog hopped backwards over her ("This is amateur hour"). A debate about whether Kraft Singles count as cheese summoned Knottley's ghost again, and Thea explained to the audience that every goblin who works at the Fortress gets a pet [[Chinchillas|chinchilla]] with pet insurance. Knottley believed those chinchillas had a government.

Bofus tried to summon hundreds of frogs. Only two came, and Bone Daddy stepped on both of them (they said thank you). So Bofus drew a long pink tongue from under the mask, which stiffened into a razor-sharp sword. Bofus took an anime ninja stance and finally landed a hit, drenching Bone Daddy in drool: "Take some damage, you prick!"

Thea arrived with a tray of 2% milk for strong bones. Bone Daddy drank his, but Bofus poured theirs on the ground, declaring that milk is "made up by big milk." Thea poured hers out too. Bofus then wiped the drool off their chest to reveal a fresh Peloton tattoo: "My brother, my Peloton brother… I miss you, brother."

### The Bone Mech and the Frog Splash

Bone Daddy unleashed his signature move: "Death Armor Reborn in the Combat Yard. D-A-R-C-Y, Darcy!" Darcy's rib cage opened to engulf him, and bones rose from the earth until she became a towering bone mech. Its giant fist, "Daddy's Girl," came down so hard that Bofus's head disappeared into their body. Bofus pushed a thumb into the cavity and re-inflated with a pop and a ribbit. Then they ran up a jagged rock, twisted in midair and slammed onto the mech with the **Frog Splash**, which shrank the machine around its pilot.

Perched on top of the mech, Bofus tried to summon the Tooth Fairy so she could watch Bone Daddy's humiliation. Bone Daddy shrugged it off with another natural 20, and Bofus's own ex tumbled through the portal instead. It was Craig, Bone Daddy's father, who took a seat on the opposite side of the stands from Bone Daddy's mother. "My parents separated when I was four years old," said Bone Daddy. Bofus had home truths for him: "I left your mother alone. I went after your dad." Craig remembered Bofus as a frog prince ("I got a promotion") and cheerfully gave Bofus permission to punch his son in the head. The second punch was a natural 20. A second attempt at the Tooth Fairy, Tina, produced only a single falling tooth.

### The Box of Chaos

The mech grabbed Thea and hurled her at Bofus as a "basketball special." She cracked the sound barrier, and the echo stirred the chaos storms until lightning brought [[The Box of Chaos]] crashing down. A column of yellow and pink fire burst from it, and [[The Shadow in the Box|a shadowy figure with yellow eyes]] hooked its claws over the side: "I'm really not fucking picky. What do you got?" Bone Daddy offered his sunglasses ("I got more, baby"). The figure put them on and, instead of giving him an item, awakened his inner monster, doubling the power of his best strikes. Yellow and pink fire lit between every bone of Darcy, and his flaming fist set Bofus's pants alight ("Toasty buns, toasty buns!"). Thea threw toasted bread to everyone.

Bofus answered by winking at Bone Daddy's mother and feeding the box the pom-pom hat shaped like her. In return they received **[[Morir's Cataclysm]]**, a leather-bound tome whose words flowed off the page and wrapped around their skin in glowing markings. Three tongues shot from under the mask, but they slid harmlessly off Bone Daddy's bones. So Bofus dropped their crown into the box too: "I would give my entire kingdom just to see this man burn." The figure handed over a ring that once belonged to the Arbiter, making the wearer master of time and space. Bofus slipped it onto the tiny finger under the mask that had always held the crown up, and their eyes flashed with multicolored lightning. Thea, meanwhile, fell out of the sky like a shot pigeon and threw a tantrum on the ground.

A final chaos event demanded that the champions fold paper shields against a hail of rocks from the Acolytes. Both failed, and both were left battered and exhausted.

### The Bone Wave

Bone Daddy called his family to him. Darcy's arms grew extra joints to lift his mother and father onto his shoulders, wrapped in a "cloak of calcium." Then a tidal wave of bone rose behind him. Even with the Arbiter's ring working against him, his attack met Bofus's dead even, and the effort was too much for both of them. The wave grew until it overshadowed the mountain, beyond even Bone Daddy's control.

His father whispered in his ear: "Son, you forgot the only rule. You gotta listen for the safe word." "I'm sorry, father," Bone Daddy whispered. His life flashed before his eyes: his mother, his father teaching him to throw, Tina stitching his beanie, and finally Darcy becoming all of it. Darcy's skull looked down at him and nodded: it's okay.

Bofus at last pulled off the mask. Frogs spilled out of it, leaving only one tiny, sad frog head on the neck of a large man. The little frog walked to Craig and embraced him.

> **King Bofus:** "I always loved you, Craig… You turned me from a prince into a king."

"I always loved you too," said Craig, and wiped a tear from the froggy eye. "And at least this time it wasn't death by fucking Peloton," Bofus added. The wave crashed down and piled into a mountain made entirely of bones. Thea declared it "a monument to the battle offered here as sacrifice to the acolytes today." The Lord of Fate, pleased, awkwardly asked the Acolytes about the after party.

## Dried Blood

In the arena locker room, goblins pulled chunks of bone out of both revived champions. King Bofus left the Acolytes with a creed: "If you can, always be at least 50% man, 50% frog, and 100% Bad at potentially maths… 50% Peloton." Bone Daddy's parting advice was simpler: "Never forget to say thank you, daddy." Everyone signed off with "Good night, daddies."

## Who and What Appears
### Characters
**[[Characters/King Bofus|King Bofus / King Man-Frog Bofus]]** (Dr. Bees). Pronouns they/it.
- **Look:** a frog mask topped by a crown. Under the mask is **a tiny, sad frog head on a large man's body**; the crown is held on by a tiny finger.
- **Nature:** claims to be "50% man, 50% frog, 50% idiot, 50% not good at percentages." Leader of a frog nation, "the one with the frogs."
- **History:** previously fought Richard Knottley, who threw a Peloton bike at him, and died in that tie. He is haunted by Knottley's ghost, which now lives "deep within my soul", and has a **fresh Peloton tattoo** on his chest.
- **Craig:** once a frog prince, made king by his ex-lover Craig, Bone Daddy's father ("I got a promotion").
- **Habits:** cannot read. Owns a sad-frog mug. Thinks milk is a "big milk" myth. Has strong views on Kraft Singles. Safe word "amphibian." Into feet.
- **Powers:** fires frogs from under the mask, summons frogs from the ground, the Tongue Sword (and later three tongues), signature move the Frog Splash (swaps the opponent's stats).
- **[[Lore/The Box of Chaos|Box of Chaos:]]** got Morir's Cataclysm (glowing skin markings) for his pom-pom hat, and the Arbiter's ring (master of time and space for a round) for his crown.
- **Fate:** died in the tie, unmasked, kissing Craig. Revived in the locker room (Dried Blood).

**[[Characters/Bone Daddy|Bone Daddy / Bonald Daddison]]** (Johnny Stanton). A returning champion from a previous season.
- **Look:** pale, spindly limbs, sleeveless coat, very reflective sunglasses (and spares), a whip.
- **Family:** a "mommy's boy." Only his mother may call him Bonald. His parents separated when he was four, and he learned "everything I know" from his father.
- **Love life:** his ex is **Tina the Tooth Fairy**, who stitched his tooth pom-pom hat. "Can never truly die in any way that matters" as long as Darcy nurses him back.
- **Tastes:** favorite food Totino's. Drinks only whole milk.
- **Powers:** raises the dead (zombies, "Darcy's brothers and sisters"); signature move **Death Armor Reborn in the Combat Yard (D-A-R-C-Y)**, the bone mech, with the attack "Daddy's Girl"; heroic surge as a tidal wave of bone.
- **[[Lore/The Box of Chaos|Box of Chaos:]]** traded his sunglasses for doubled nat-20 effects (his "inner monster").
- **Fate:** died in the tie, forgetting the safe word. Revived in the locker room.

**[[Characters/Darcy|Darcy]]**. Bone Daddy's skeleton partner, "my true love." The relationship is unlabeled: not familiar, minion or mother, sometimes "mommy." She has long-fingered bone claws and two thumbs, and wears a three-pom-pom hat from Thea. Her bones can scatter and reassemble. She becomes the bone mech and nods "it's okay" at the end.

**Bone Mother / Bone Mummy**. Bone Daddy's mother, in the stands beside the goblins and Thea, wearing a tooth pom-pom hat. She pucks Totino's 50 yards and rides his shoulder in the surge. She no longer speaks to Craig.

**Craig ("Bone Daddy Daddy")**. Bone Daddy's father and Bofus's ex. He appears in the stands through Bofus's failed portal, endorses Bofus punching his son, rides the surge, delivers the safe-word warning, and shares the final kiss with Bofus. Drew said "Craig Dennison?"; his surname is uncertain.

**Tina the Tooth Fairy**. Bone Daddy's ex. Bofus tried twice to summon her; the only result was a single tooth.

**[[Characters/Richard Knottley|Richard Knottley]]** (ghost). Bofus's previous opponent: the Peloton thrower, a conspiracy theorist about the chinchilla government, wearing a tin hat and a tiny mustache. He left a "Kill Switch video." His ghost appears through Bofus.

**[[Characters/Thea|Thea]]**. Has impish pink wings and a glitter trail, long pointy ears, a ridged spine and an arrow-tipped tail. She runs the goblins' winter wardrobe, cries literal crocodiles, brings 2% milk and toasted bread, gets thrown as a basketball, and throws a tantrum after her nat 1.

**[[Characters/The Arbiter|The Lord of Fate]]**. Refuses gloves and resizes Thea's head with a snap. Remembers Bone Daddy's gyrations from last time. He has "a lot of issues" with Thea and puts them "on her record."

**The Box of Chaos's shadowy figure**. Has yellow eyes and claws. "Not picky," but prefers real cheese products. It now wears Bone Daddy's sunglasses and tried on Bofus's pom-pom hat.

**[[Characters/Goblins|The goblins]]**. Bundled up for the cold. Each gets a pet chinchilla with pet insurance.
