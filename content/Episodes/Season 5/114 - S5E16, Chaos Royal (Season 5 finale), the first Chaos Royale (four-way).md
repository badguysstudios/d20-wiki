## What happens
### Opening: the Lord of Fate's gloom
1. **0:10:50** Thea opens: "Welcome, Acolytes, to the Dread Fortress as we kick off season five's finale."
   - A **tiny pink imp, Thea**, flutters down the Fortress's dark, murky hallways calling "Lord of Fate?"

### Round 1

    - **The blade hits Richard with a "ding" and flies off, and so does Richard's mustache.** The sword lies on the ground.

    - **Richard ends up impaled on Tormiir's horns**: "one between the ribs and one just shy of an exit. Like right mid-cheek."
    - Richard tries to "paraglide" past on the horns ("Butterfly in the sky"). He calls Tormiir "Toriel, the elf" by mistake.

    - Attack 1: Copycat rolls a **natural 20 on defense** and dodges by moving like a lamprey: "no one can copy as good as me."
    - Attack 2: a miss. Thea: "Look it up on wikihow."
    - Attack 3: **it hits. A tendril scrapes Copycat's leg and takes "a nice little meatball"** of flesh. Bec as narrator: "Put that in a lunchbox for later."

    - She cheers "drink your milk!" to the crowd. Tormiir: "You dishonored my mother! You sick fucks!"

    - Bobby is delighted. Copycat helps people talk to their moms, and he doesn't know where he comes from. Most people fear the Hunger, but she gave it meatballs. He hopes they become best friends and "help turn all humans and life into one giant ball of flesh."

    - **Tormiir rolls a nat 20.** His magic is "magical electricity anyway," so the zap runs down his horns and hooves into the ground. "That tickles."
