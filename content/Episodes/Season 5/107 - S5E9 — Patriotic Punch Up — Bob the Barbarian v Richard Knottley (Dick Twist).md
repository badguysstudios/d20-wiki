Two fan favourites were brought home to [[The Dread Fortress|the Dread Fortress]] for **Patriotic Punch Up**. [[Bob the Barbarian]], a jort-clad Florida man from the Everglades, faced [[Richard Knottley]], better known as **Dick Twist**, who was freshly revived and launching a run for president. The fight took place on the fortress's own front porch. It featured a weaponised Peloton, a frog pulled from somewhere no frog should be, and a hostage twist that turned a gloriously stupid brawl into a blood feud.

## The Story

### Marco Polo in the Dread Fortress

The night began deep beneath the lava fields, in the cool, heavily air-conditioned halls of the Dread Fortress. [[The Arbiter|The Lord of Fate]] walked those halls in Italian leather shoes that clicked "with an accent because they're Italian," past walls lined with paintings of himself. He was looking for [[Thea]], and a high-pitched "Polo!" echoed back. She was playing Marco Polo. When he pointed out that the game is played in water, she corrected him. She said she had gone through a magic portal and met Marco Polo himself, and he had told her he "went more through the desert than I did through the water."

He tracked her to a small cupboard room with a pile of blankets and, naturally, another portrait of himself. Thea asked whether he had dementia. He reminded her it was Sunday and she should be preparing the Deathmatch. She wanted to hold it right there so they could keep playing, but he refused: "I don't want blood on the stones." When he finally lifted his own portrait, Thea burst out through the canvas and ruined it. She declared that she had been found, but had scared him, "so I still won." The Lord of Fate informed her she was paying for it.

### Dick Twist for President

Through the hole in the frame, Thea opened a small pink portal and brought in the [[Champions|champions]]. Richard arrived pedalling his Peloton, "still traumatized from the frogs." The Lord of Fate reminded him that he had died of a heart attack on that very bike last time. Richard explained that he had eaten 45 cheeseburgers that day. Today it was only 37, because it was a cheat day, and he had switched to lettuce wraps. His platform was to dismantle, on day one, the "swampy government run by [[Chinchillas|chinchillas]]" who wear human skin. When Thea asked whether he was a chinchilla, he raised his eyebrows: "Chinchillas can't do that."

Bob knew nothing about chinchillas, but he loved a good swamp, so he warned Richard there would be a problem. He had at least three gators: "One, two, and Hannah Montana." His sweet Florida boy Robbie was back home in the Everglades, riding their pink fan boat and feeding the gators Publix chicken tenders. Bob had brought extra tenders, and that settled Thea's loyalties on the spot. Richard sneered that Thea wasn't even people and couldn't vote, then announced into the camera that the whole game was rigged. The Lord of Fate replied that they don't acknowledge the cameras. "We paid them to be invisible."

With a clap, the Lord of Fate blew Thea's portal open into a tear in reality. It led to the front porch of the Dread Fortress, beneath the giant skull with its blazing eyes, on the field of lava. [[The Acolytes]] were spilling their drinks, because it was 50% off slushy night. Someone in the crowd held up a "Thea's people" sign and was promptly dragged away by figures in black cloaks. Thea pointed out that they could have just used the front door. He told her she had no sense of showmanship and admitted, "I can only blame myself here. I created you."

### Lightning, Ozone and Tricycles

Bob opened with a roundhouse kick to the face and a backhanded slap "to show my utter disdain." Richard answered with a trick he had learned: harnessing the electric current of the Peloton. Pedalling at a blistering four miles per hour, he blasted a lightning bolt into Bob. Bob fumbled his defence completely and took the full force of it.

Then came the policy debate. Richard demanded to know where Bob stood on the lunar moths eating the ozone layer. Bob liked the sun, so he was in favour of a bigger hole. Appalled, Richard hurled the Peloton at him. Bob crouched into the mud with "the reflexes of other Florida animals, not just gators, but panthers," and the bike sailed over his head. The throw had been a distraction. Richard closed in at a brisk three miles an hour and wagged a finger in Bob's face, and the lecture wore them both down equally. Bob simply replied that all he cared about was doing his sweet boy Robbie proud "as his new dad."

Thea decided she could ride a bike better than either of them. She produced a tiny tricycle with ribbons, clicky spoke beads and a front basket, and the Lord of Fate snapped both champions onto trikes for a best-of-three race. Thea took the first heat. Bob won the second with a natural 20 and the third "by a nose," while Richard somehow made riding a trike look difficult. Afterward, Richard confessed that his job at Peloton was a Scientology-style lifetime arrangement. He also revealed that Big Peloton had a super PAC backing his campaign, alongside Adidas, Payless Shoes and KB Toys.

### Belly Flops and Butt Frogs

Bob cracked a Miller Lite, chugged it, and belly-flopped onto Dick from a great height. His sweat soaked in and slowed Richard down. "It's made of meth, jorts, and beer, baby, the way God intended." Richard steadied himself with deep breathing and screeching. Thea joined in, then brought him a walker with tennis balls on the feet, since he sounded 98 years old.

Richard then reached somewhere personal and pulled out the frog that had been trapped in his orifice for weeks. He called it "a gift from God King Bofidis" and lobbed it like a grenade. It missed. Bob took out a knife and fork, cut the frog up and ate every inch of it. The Lord of Fate observed that it had been sous-viding in Richard's ass for three weeks, and admitted he was now petrified of Bob. So was Richard. Richard struck a T-pose and called down "bolts of Wi-Fi" through his aluminium foil hat, and they cooked Bob's brain with 5G.

### Jorts Everywhere

Still scrambled, Bob summoned his trusty Harley-Davidson, Cletus. Richard shrugged off the tailpipe roar with a natural 20, insisting that the Peloton is the ultimate bike on the road. So Bob reached into the second layer of jorts beneath the first and pulled out two denim-covered fists. The right one landed hard. The left one should have missed, but Richard flubbed his defence so badly that it landed anyway, and the punch knocked his mustache clean off.

Bob explained that jorts should "ride up high and tight to almost expose your genitals and or ass, preferably both." Thea wanted some for everyone, and the Lord of Fate snapped every Acolyte's clothing into jorts. Bob gazed out at the sea of denim. "God, I love it here." Richard, now also in jorts and missing his Hercule Poirot mustache, accused Thea of mocking it away and declared the game rigged once more. He finished by threatening the Lord of Fate directly: "This is my Dread Fortress now."

### The Lord of Fate Strikes Back

"Let me show you what I'm gonna do," the Lord of Fate replied. He raised his hands and drew multicoloured lightning down from the chaos storms above, then blasted it at Richard's Peloton. The bike exploded, and shrapnel chunked into both fighters. Richard swore to barbecue him and feed him to the neighbours. Thea kindly rode over and gave him her tricycle "just in case."

Richard lashed both tricycles to his feet with the remains of the Peloton belt and went rollerblading into battle. He punched Bob on every pass and announced he would soon be eating the Lord of Fate's ass "like corn on the cob." He flipped off the Lord of Fate, who flipped him off back. Meanwhile, Thea asked if she could lick Bob's sweat. Bob squirted it into her mouth from a secret belly-button blowhole, "what all Florida men have," and Thea vomited violently onto the front row. The Lord of Fate explained to the crowd that she had only been sentient for about two months.

### The Hostage Video

Bob held back from finishing things early, because he wanted "a battle that we can enjoy and remember for years and ages." He tried out the "child punch" and "child kick" Robbie had taught him, and briefly bonded with Richard over keto. Then Richard slowly scrolled through an ancient iPhone and showed his new best friend a video. Robbie was locked in a padded room. "We actually picked up a remnant of the hunger after the end of your last battle. The hunger is about to eat Robbie again, if I die."

Bob was horrified. Richard explained that it had been an accident, and that Robbie "knew something about Hillary Clinton." His attempt to rattle Bob backfired. Bob shrugged off the next lightning bolt with a promise: "You've done the one thing you shouldn't do, which is take my son into battle. You'll pay, and you'll pay dearly."

### The Box of Chaos

For the second half, the Lord of Fate flared his white wings, and a tornado of lightning slammed [[The Box of Chaos]] onto the field. Pink and yellow fire shot from its lid, and the shadowy figure clawed its way out once again. Bob sacrificed the "Jort Daddy" friendship bracelet Robbie had made him. The figure slipped it on beside his WWJD and Livestrong bracelets: "This is gonna go great with my collection." The Box repaid Bob badly. The arena drained to grey around a single burning red circle, and [[Dolly]], the haunted porcelain doll, crawled through a portal and stole his strength with her unblinking stare.

Furious, Bob unleashed a signature move that even Robbie had never seen. His jorts grew to cover him from head to toe ("I am become jorts"), and Gator One and Hannah Montana locked onto his hands. "Behold, Acolytes and Richard, this is the Denim Gator!" He spun like a Beyblade and coated Richard in sweat and gator venom. Then he healed himself instead of finishing the job, so Richard could "sit in it."

### The Dance-Off

Bleeding from the crotch, with a pulled hamstring, Richard triggered his Dick Twist special. Both men's pants became tuxedo pants. "Bitch, this is a dance-off." Richard showed off the crop circle clarification, the chinchilla cha-cha and the Bigfoot Boogie, while Bob just copied him. Richard won, recovered some of his strength, and twerked aggressively.

At the Box, the shadowy figure had no idea who Richard was until Thea reminded him: "You're the guy with the frogs in your ass." Richard sacrificed his mustache. The Box offered him a small blue pill to "keep you going all night long." He handed it back, because "I'm a political candidate. I would never be blue-pilled." His second draw gave him a rusty child's spoon with a helping of tetanus, which let him hit Bob with two afflictions at once. The figure, meanwhile, now wore the mustache like a merkin. Thea interviewed Richard about his strategy and concluded, "You're pretty stupid. You might get my vote." Even the Lord of Fate conceded that Dick Twist had a real shot.

### The Death of Dick Twist

Bob pointed out a little graveyard beside the fortress. Then he ripped off his tuxedo pants to reveal a third layer of jorts ("the jorts never end") and stacked everything he had into a second Denim Gator. The gators' teeth dripped with extra venom. **He rolled a natural 20.** As Bob engorged once more, Richard saw the spectral form of his ultimate foe behind him: a giant chinchilla. His final words were defiant: "Dick Twist is immortal, a state of mind more than a being, more than a man."

**Richard Knottley died**, and Bob stuffed every orifice of his corpse with warm, spicy Publix buffalo tenders "to join those damn frogs." But as Richard fell, the phone dropped from his hand. The video had been a live feed, and the [[The Hunger|Hunger]] devoured Robbie. Thea mourned the wasted tenders: "Victory always comes at a loss." Richard's ghost promptly appeared behind her. Bob tried to banish it, but Thea pulled out a little ghost net, explaining, "we'll need you for later," and chased him around the graveyard.

## Dried Blood

Richard's ghost complained that he had been flying into the seventh heaven with open arms until Thea's net said no. While a couple of mages worked to sew his ghost back into his body, he insisted that his death was all part of his plan, and that if he returned a third time, everything he had set in motion would take effect. "Everybody loves a comeback story." He signed off with "Live dangerously."

Bob credited the gods with his natural 20 and wanted only vengeance for his boy. He left the Acolytes with a promise: "Robbie, I won't let the hunger take you again, son. Bet on it." The Lord of Fate replied that they would see how that played out over the season.
