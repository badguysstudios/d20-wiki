Two machines met in [[The Chinchilla Forest]] with every intention of killing each other, and almost immediately started hugging instead. **[[Atlas]]**, a mouthless automaton freshly dragged back from death, faced **[[Frigidoom]]**, a giant irradiated 1950s refrigerator full of living leftovers. The fight became a deranged buddy story: acid hugs, a hamster in a ball, an orange mushroom cloud of [[Double Sun Cola]], pepperoni nipples, and a finish nobody quite knew how to score.

## The Story

### The Missing Chinchilla

The night began deep beneath [[The Dread Fortress]]. Lava churned outside, but the fortress itself was cool and damp. In the caverns below its dungeons, at the far end of a hallowed hall, a gathering of [[Chinchillas|chinchillas]] waited. [[Thea]] counted them over and over and kept getting six out of seven. She was preparing them for a sabbatical and bowed to [[The Arbiter|the Lord of Fate]], and the chinchillas bowed after her in a little wave.

The Lord of Fate walked in holding the seventh chinchilla by the scruff. "Why are the chinchillas running free in the Dread Fortress?" he asked. He reminded Thea that she had been put in charge of them: feeding them, walking them, unionizing them. Thea protested that they poop a lot. He was unmoved: "I'm running an entire realm. I can't be dealing with the chinchilla poop." Thea stomped her feet in a pout, and the chinchillas stomped theirs right along with her. She checked her notes, decided "It was only ever seven," and collected the stray, a plump gray chinchilla holding a small cigarette.

Her plan was efficient, at least by Thea standards. The Deathmatch was being held in the Chinchilla Forest, so she would release the chinchillas there. She swore she had also finished everything on the to-do chart hanging in the fridge "that I know how to read." Then she clapped, a big pink portal lit the dungeon, and she welcomed the night's [[Champions|champions]].

### Cheese Products and Complaints

Frigidoom's first question, on spotting the chinchillas, was "Are these snacks?" Thea threw herself in front of them and shuffled side to side like a small crab: "These are no snacks!" There was no pre-match meal either, because Thea had been busy with the chinchillas.

Atlas arrived staring at his own palms. "Atlas is back. I died. I saw eternity and was one with it. Hell is real even for automatons. Why have I been pulled back into this realm of pain and struggle?" The Lord of Fate's answer: "Mostly because we were bored." Atlas had died in his last battle, against [[Killy Yesman]], the salesperson of Double Sun Cola.

By way of consolation, Thea stuck a still-wrapped slice of processed cheese to Atlas's chest. Atlas has no mouth, which is a very sore spot, and she laid a second slice over his right foot as a shoe. The Lord of Fate recalled Atlas drinking a healthy amount of Double Sun Cola and wondered why he hadn't grown a mouth. Atlas reached for a phantom mouth with a horrible creaking of metal until Thea begged him to stop.

Frigidoom got a cheese slice too, and accepted it graciously: "This makes up for the last battle where that smelly samurai cut my mustache rainbow. I accept this rotten gift." Thea said the mustache had grown back nicely; he explained he'd had to put it back together with some old yogurt. The Lord of Fate declared himself very interested to see how two mechanical individuals would kill each other.

The chinchillas lined up at Thea's clap. Atlas adjusted a ginger-striped one slightly to the right, patted its head and strode to the front. Frigidoom, being a giant fridge, shuffled to the back. They filed into an immense pine forest, its scent described apparently just for [[The Acolytes|the Acolytes]], since Atlas has no nose either. Frigidoom asked whether the forest, as green as his arms, was toxic. Atlas leaned down, consulted a bush, and reported: "your mother, this is a very toxic forest."

Frigidoom then tried to file a complaint. Thea said they didn't take those. The Lord of Fate pointed out that taking complaints was literally her job: "How are we supposed to make the Deathmatch better if we're not taking feedback? This is basic level Arbiter stuff." Thea simply glared at Frigidoom.

> **Thea:** "You can leave a complaint if you survive this match. I hope you don't."

The Lord of Fate rose and flared his wings. The Acolytes filled the sky in an ethereal crowd, spilling blue slushies, starting fights and failing to chant "Frigidoom" because it had too many syllables.

### A Very Violent Friendship

Frigidoom struck first, turning Thea's cheese slice into a razor-sharp shuriken of ultra-pasteurized cheese ("My American cheese!"). Magnetic lightning crackled down Atlas's arms and yanked him aside, and the cheese buried itself in a tree. Frigidoom admitted that was really cool. Atlas, fists raised, replied that "fridges are supposed to be cool." Frigidoom was so smelly and gross that nobody had ever complimented him before, and he covered his face, flustered.

He decided he might have a friend, and went in for a hug. This was a problem, because everyone Frigidoom had hugged before had dissolved into the food mass inside him. "As we are both two beings made of metal, perhaps we need not meet as enemies, but friends," he said, oozing forward on a sizzling trail. The acid only ate the grime off Atlas and polished him shinier than before. Atlas hugged back with crushing magnetic force, "mutual crushing, but it's still very affectionate." Frigidoom squeezed again, the rotting heart in his chest beating so hard his door nearly burst off. He felt warm for the first time in his irradiated life. Atlas answered with a shockwave that blew the leaves off the trees. On the sidelines, Thea squeezed a chinchilla until its eyes bulged, slobbering and snotting into its fur.

"Quit hugging each other," the Lord of Fate snapped. "You're supposed to kill each other."

Atlas promised his "mechanical brethren" that there was another side, and that "we will chase the rainbow together." Then he opened the fridge door and punched inside to rip out the festering thing within. His fist stuck fast in what was essentially pudding. Glowing cylinders twisted out of his forearm and burned from yellow to white-hot, and he blasted lightning straight down his trapped arm. "Are we literally trying to touch each other's hearts right now?" Frigidoom wondered. "Is this what friendship is like?" Two attacks went nowhere. The third caught Frigidoom badly off guard and launched him bouncing across the forest floor into a tree. The smell of deep-fried rotten food was so bad that the chinchillas fanned it away with leaves and Thea vomited behind a bush.

Chaos then hit twice over. A silver saucer beamed both champions up and probed them ("I did not sign a waiver," said Atlas; "I don't know if this is covered in warranty," said Frigidoom). Then **Pooka Pooka**, a hamster in a plastic ball sworn to destroy the world that caged him, came crashing through the trees. Atlas dodged and gave up his own move to try to save his new friend, but his fingers couldn't grip a perfect sphere. Pooka Pooka flattened Frigidoom and rolled off into the distance. "I'll get you next time, giant hamster."

### The Double Sun Rises

Frigidoom thanked Atlas for trying and vowed to touch *his* heart in return. Frozen chicken, marshmallow salad and fuzzy expired clotted cream formed a giant hand that slid between Atlas's plates. Inside, among the gears, it found a canister of fluid, decided it was a soda can, and shook it. The canister was **Double Sun Cola**, "the most refreshing of colas that you can buy on this or any Deathmatch world." Atlas's clockwork revved, his eyes crackled orange, and a lightning mohawk sprang up. He became ten times more radical.

"I am so pumped up and ready to open this fridge and open up a can of whoop-ass that you've got right inside you, brother," rad Atlas announced. Frigidoom shook harder and Atlas overloaded, popping a few bolts and losing a pinky. "Don't worry about it, man! It's just a finger!"

Then Atlas let the cola flow through his entire body. The mohawk evolved into a lightning mo-mullet. "Have you tried Double Sun Cola, baby? It'll make you a new man!" he whispered. His chest opened to show two blazing suns and a soda bottle, and he held Frigidoom in a loving embrace: "Feel the sun, brother." The hit cost Atlas some of his own strength and landed with enormous force. **An almost nuclear blast of orange fluid rose over the canopy**, staining the clouds and raining orange onto the trees.

Frigidoom stood in the crater with his pastel-blue casing shattered. Inside were soaked fizzy bits, a tiny beating green heart and a couple of Cheetos. He drank in the cola and became something new: a fizzy, Tang-orange muscle man. Atlas hauled him up by the arm like a wrestling champion: "Welcome to the rad side." Thea ran about licking the orange mist off the leaves and the chinchillas, shrieking, "I want two suns! I want to be rad! I want to be so fucking cool too!" The Lord of Fate leapt up applauding: "This is perfect!… You get it! You have to kill him! You have to kill him dead!" The mist had also reached the stands and turned every Acolyte into a ripped, mohawked, mulleted mess, so he called a break to let them cool off.

### A Chair, a Box and a Very Rad Chinchilla

After the break, Thea complained that she couldn't remember the show she'd watched. The Lord of Fate conceded that he "didn't design it much for remembering," which was really his fault. Thea wondered aloud whether blaming him made her more powerful than him, and another star faded from her forehead ("Not again!"). When she then hid behind Atlas's leg and declared him her champion, the Lord of Fate folded up his metal chair, stopped time, and appeared behind her. "I didn't want to do this. You're embarrassing me in front of all of the acolytes." He beat her into the ground with the chair, unfroze time and sat back down.

From the Thea-shaped hole, three tiny fingers rose: "Round number three." **[[The Box of Chaos]]**, giant and pink, fell from the sky like a meteor straight onto Thea, crushing her deeper into the earth. Pink and red flames poured out, and a shadowy hand emerged.

Frigidoom spent a moment basking in his new form and grew even more magnificent: veins, sunglasses, a mane of golden spaghetti hair, a Speedo, frosting-trimmed wrestling boots and **two pepperonis for nipples**. Somewhere a Geiger counter ticked out of control. He realised he looked like a pizza and leaned into it, swinging a stuffed-crust arm marked with five peppers to show how hot it was. Atlas blocked it at the perfect moment with a natural 20. "Nice punch, pepperoni nipples," the Lord of Fate called. "These Scovilles aren't done yet," Frigidoom shot back. His radioactive sweat withered the grass into a wasteland, so the chinchillas would need a new home. The next cheesy punch landed with the sound of a gong.

Atlas turned to the Box. Asked whether he was a solicitor, he replied, "I am soliciting nothing but pain for my opponent." He had left his wallet in his other mechanical pants, so he offered the most radical chinchilla ever seen instead: novelty "2000" glasses, tattoo sleeves, snap bracelets and forty limited-edition pogs. The figure with yellow eyes accepted. The chinchilla climbed onto its shoulder, and the lid shut.

Out floated a gloriously tacky plastic cup, and a waterfall of blue slushy poured from the sky to fill it. "I do not have a mouth! God damn it, every time!" So Atlas lifted the Box off Thea, shoved the cup into her arms, spun his torso like a cyclone and hurled them both into Frigidoom. Thea lodged halfway through him, coated in radical slime. The blue slushy mixed with his orange and turned him pukey green, the exact colour of Thea's earlier vomit. "What? Little lady, what did you do to me?" Atlas tried a clothesline, reworked it into a high five, and fumbled it badly. Frigidoom turned the high five into a handshake and then an arm lock.

Still wedged in the fridge, Thea said it was better than the car wash the Lord of Fate had put her through, which led to a sudden argument over whether **Thea is people**. "I am people!" she insisted, brows furrowed. The Lord of Fate blamed hot dogs: "don't ever let them eat hot dogs because then all of a sudden they think that they're people." Atlas, who has no mouth, was declared "the best kind of people," and Thea sat cross-legged in the middle of the fight wearing her thinking face.

### Hot Dogs and the Double Sun Nova

Frigidoom, who had only ever had off-brand sodas, now craved the Double Sun at Atlas's core. "Now Frigidoom's a junkie," the Lord of Fate observed. Two hot dogs shot from Frigidoom's palms, linked by toxic mozzarella into ballpark nunchucks. He would have preferred Nathan's, so Thea scoured the crowd for a small child named Nathan, then snatched another hot dog from someone named Judy to make the nunchucks longer. With twenty peppers now blazing on his biceps, Frigidoom gouged open Atlas's chest and exposed the shining cola core.

Atlas sagged, arms limp, then lifted his head and gestured to the Acolytes, who chanted his name. His core soaked up the habanero heat and turned the cola bright red. He announced a brand-new product from Double Sun Cola: "Then let me give you a taste of the sun. As it goes Nova, baby! Double Sun Nova punch!" It landed. "Hot dog, hot damn," Frigidoom gasped. "Reminds me of when I was just a tiny little toaster," Atlas told him, before vowing that "Atlas is unbreakable."

He anchored himself, coiled back and hurled himself headfirst into the ground to detonate a cola geyser beneath his foe. Frigidoom didn't run. He opened himself wide and drank the whole thing, turning neon and glowing almost like a sun. Atlas, emptied out, lost his mohawk and was suddenly his old logical self: "I feel funny. Why am I in the dirt?" Told that Frigidoom was right where he'd left him, he answered: "Unsettling. Then come, let us finish this."

Thea traded her half-eaten hot dog to the Box and got a blade holding a piece of [[The Shadow King]]'s soul. Each strike would heal its wielder but weigh heavily on their mind. The Lord of Fate had no idea how it got in there; it was from his private collection. Thea could barely lift it, swung once, and tossed it aside: "Stupid Shadow King blade, I think it's dumb."

### Rad Never Dies

Frigidoom saw that his friend was no longer radical, and decided that had to be fixed before anything else. "Atlas, stop acting like you don't have a mouth and speak from your heart." From somewhere deep inside the automaton came a strained "brother," and an ethereal hand reached out to be tagged in. A newly radicalized Thea, with blonde hair, sunglasses and a thick mustache, whipped up the crowd: "Hey there, brother! Do you want Atlas to become rad?" The Acolytes chanted "Rad! Rad! Rad!"

With a chinchilla named Concordae at his side, Frigidoom poured everything he had into Atlas's core to make him **permanently radical**. "You tried to save me from that hamster, and I'm going to save you from yourself." Atlas resisted ("No, stop. This is not sensible"), then gave in completely ("Yeah, baby! Yeah!") as the lightning and mo-mullet roared back. All the neon drained out of Frigidoom until he was a little green blob. Atlas knelt and scooped him up: "I'm here, brother, I'm right here." Frigidoom asked him to win for them both and to kick that weeaboo samurai's ass for him.

Atlas poured one out for the homies, poured his friend into a bottle of Double Sun Cola, and placed it inside his chest beside his heart. Then he began gathering up the fridge pieces so he could rebuild him. "Don't worry, brother. What the folks at home don't know… is that Rad never dies and that the fridge is always filled with leftovers for next time."

The Lord of Fate was briefly lost for words. Was it technically a win? Thea settled it: "We have the technology! They can rebuild him!… It's a win for Atlas! It's a win for the Acolytes! And it's a win for being rad!" She sent a glitter bomb spiralling up to the Acolytes, then announced a refrigerator donation drive for leftover fridges, which the Lord of Fate approved ("We like a Lord of Fate that recycles").

## Dried Blood

Frigidoom's final words came from inside Atlas's chest: "Pour one out for your homies." The advice that came with it: do it outside to save on cleanup. Atlas's last word as champion summed up the whole affair: "Friendship is unbreakable."
