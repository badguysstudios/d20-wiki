## What happens
### Opening — the chinchillas in the Dread Fortress caverns
1. **0:06:10** Bec narrates: in the **Dread Fortress**, with "the hot, hot lava splurging all around," the fortress itself is cool and damp. Deep in the bowels of its dungeons, lower still in the caverns, at the far end of "this hallowed hall," there is **a large gathering of chinchillas**.
2. **0:06:38** **Thea counts the chinchillas over and over and keeps getting six of seven.** She is preparing them for their "[four?] sabbatical." She bows to the **Lord of Fate**, and the chinchillas bow after her in a wave.
3. **0:07:11** **The Lord of Fate walks in holding the seventh chinchilla by the back of the neck.** "Why are the chinchillas running free in the Dread Fortress?" Thea says she was collecting the ones "signed up for sabbatical this particular month."
4. **0:07:29** He reminds her that when they got the chinchillas, **she was put in charge of them: feed them, take them out, "unionize them."** She protests that they poop a lot. He replies: "I'm running an entire realm. I can't be dealing with the chinchilla poop."
5. **0:07:52** **Thea pouts and stomps her feet, and the chinchillas copy her**, kicking up their own dust. She checks her notes ("Was there supposed to be seven or eight?") and decides "It was only ever seven." She flies over and takes **the plump little gray chinchilla, which is holding a small cigarette**.
6. **0:08:33** He suggests gates or small doors to keep them in. Thea explains she is doubling up on chores: since the Deathmatch is in **the Chinchilla Forest**, she'll release the chinchillas there.
7. **0:08:57** Thea swears she cleaned the floors and did "everything else on that **to-do list chart that's hanging in the fridge** that I know how to read."
8. **0:09:14** She claps, and **a big pink portal** lights the dungeon in pink. "Help me welcome two amazing champions, Frigidoom and Atlas!"
10. **0:10:08** **Frigidoom: "Are these snacks?"** Thea gets in front of the chinchillas and dances side to side like a small crab: "These are no snacks!"
11. **0:10:24** **No craft services.** The Lord of Fate asks whether Thea set up a pre-match meal ("One last meal for one of you"). She had to take care of the chinchillas.
12. **0:10:46 — Atlas returns.** Atlas is staring at his palms: "**Atlas is back. I died. I saw eternity and was one with it. Hell is real even for automatons.** Why have I been pulled back into this realm of pain and struggle?" Lord of Fate: "Mostly because we were bored."

(Bec, as narrator: "I thought we weren't using those anymore because they're not real cheese.") **Atlas has no mouth, "a very sore spot."** Thea lays a second slice over his right foot "as a shoe covering."

"**I had to put it back together with some old yogurt.** It's dried up into a nice paste." He rubs it proudly with his **green jello arms**.
17. **0:13:06** Thea: "They're not lactose intolerant, at least… who wants to be killed by milk?… **Stronger than dairy.**" The Lord of Fate is "interested to see how **two mechanical individuals** are going to kill each other."
18. **0:13:44 — The line.** At Thea's clap the seven chinchillas form a strict line. **A ginger-striped chinchilla catches Atlas's eye, so Atlas picks it up, moves it slightly right, pats its head and walks to the front.** **Frigidoom, "obviously a giant fridge,"** has been told the chinchillas can't go into "the contents of his guts." He goes to the back because he's slow.

### The Chinchilla Forest
19. **0:14:52** They file through into **an immense, lush forest; pine fills the air.** Bec asks whether either of them can smell. Atlas: "**I have no nose either.**" Bec: "This is for apparently just the acolytes that I'm describing the [smell] for."
20. **0:15:28** Frigidoom: "This forest looks as green as my arms. **Is this a toxic forest?**" Thea: only if you're allergic to the outdoors. **Atlas leans down and asks a bush, and reports: "your mother, this is a very toxic forest."**
21. **0:16:04 — The complaint.** Frigidoom wants to file a complaint. Thea: "We don't take those." **The Lord of Fate: Thea is the one people submit complaints to, and she's supposed to bring them to him.** "How are we supposed to make the Deathmatch better if we're not taking feedback? **This is basic level Arbiter stuff.**" Thea: "But I don't care what they think." It was on the to-do list she didn't read. "I promise you'll forget all about this." She glares at Frigidoom: "You can leave a complaint if you survive this match. **I hope you don't.**"
22. **0:17:18** The Lord of Fate stands from his chair and **flares his wings**. The Acolytes form an ethereal crowd in the sky, spilling **blue slushies**, starting fights, and **failing to chant "Frigidoom" because it has too many syllables**. "Acolytes! Are you ready for some blood? … **Are you ready for some mechanical components?**"
23. **0:18:08** Thea objects that a "flat d20" isn't flat ("20 flat sides"). Frigidoom wins initiative (17 v 5).

### Round 1 — friendship
24. **0:18:28** Frigidoom: "If you have no mouth, how can you speak?" Atlas: "the same paradox… as to how you are able to think without a brain." Frigidoom: "I feel like I've just been damaged."
25. **0:19:02 — The cheese shuriken.** Frigidoom's sticky arms turn **Thea's cheese slice into a razor-sharp shuriken of processed, ultra-pasteurized cheese**. He whips it like a Frisbee "made of lactose death." Thea: "My American cheese!" It misses. **Magnetic lightning bolts run from Atlas's shoulders to his wrists and pull his bulk sideways**, and the shuriken slices into a tree.
26. **0:20:33** Frigidoom: "I'm not gonna lie, that was really cool." Atlas, fists up: "**fridges are supposed to be cool.**" **Frigidoom covers his face, flattered.** Kyle explains that he's "so smelly and gross," so he has never been complimented.
27. **0:21:05 — The hug.** Frigidoom thinks he may have a friend, and goes in for a hug. He "knows how to eat… how to grapple." Anyone he has hugged before has "dissolved into the food mass that is his inner self." Thea watches sadly. He drags his gooey body, leaving a bubbling, sizzling trail. "**As we are both two beings made of metal, perhaps we need not meet as enemies, but friends.**" It's an acidic hug: good intentions, but still an attack.
28. **0:22:37** It fails (a dirty 20 to defend). **The acid eats the filthy organic matter caked on Atlas and polishes his chassis shinier than before.** Atlas hugs back with magnetic lightning: "mutual crushing, but it's still very affectionate." Frigidoom: "Oh, this kind of tickles." He sounds like a walrus because the magnets are messing with **his ice-machine motor**.
29. **0:24:02** Frigidoom doubles down. **The organic food matter that has "become a beating heart" inside his retro-50s cooling chest beats so hard the fridge door threatens to burst off.** "He feels warm for the first time in his irradiated life." The second squeeze also fails. Atlas narrates a **shockwave of pressure that blows the leaves off the trees.** Frigidoom: "I appreciate all of this friendship so much." Kyle calls it a "Mortal Kombat friendship."
30. **0:26:02** **Thea squeezes a chinchilla far too tightly**, its eyes popping; it squeaks like a squeeze toy and bats at her face while she slobbers and snots into its fur.
31. **0:26:25** **The Lord of Fate: "Quit hugging each other. You're supposed to kill each other."**
32. **0:26:34 — Atlas's reply.** "Despite being separated by death, by fate, and even by manufacturing lines, we have been forced to this place to do deadly battle. **Rest assured there is another side, my mechanical brethren. And I will show it to you. And we will chase the rainbow together.**" **He opens the fridge door and punches into the mass to rip out the festering thing inside.** It misses: his fist clogs in "Play-Doh," and the mass shapes itself around his joints and seams. He is "stuck in pudding." (Dag: "a non-Newtonian fluid.")
33. **0:28:42 — The energy blast.** With his hand stuck, **glowing yellow cylinders twist out of Atlas's forearm and overheat from yellow to orange to red to sizzling white**, then slam back in. All his lightning is pushed down his fist for an energy blast inside Frigidoom. Frigidoom: "**Are we literally trying to touch each other's hearts right now?**… Are we tickling each other now? Is this what friendship is like?" That attack misses too. Thea: "tickle him harder."
34. **0:30:15** Atlas keeps the energy flowing; his fist is white-hot. **On the third attack Frigidoom rolls a natural 1 to defend** ("It's not going to be a repeat of the last fight, is it?"). The gel inside him bubbles and liquefies, and **the blast launches Frigidoom bouncing across the forest floor into a tree.** "Oh, my stomach doesn't feel so good." The spent canisters eject from Atlas's hand like a shotgun being cocked.
35. **0:31:52** The deep-fried, super-cooked rotten food stinks. **The chinchillas grab the biggest leaves they can to fan it away** ("office nightmare kitchen… somebody microwaves fish"). **Thea hops behind a bush and vomits**: "I need a drink of water though, for sure."

### End of round 1 — double chaos

Atlas rolls 5: "**Pooka Pooka, the hamster, the invader of the chinchilla force,** has escaped," crashing through trees in his plastic ball and swearing "to destroy the world that caged him." (Bec: "He's like Armstrong. He only has one ball.") Atlas passes. Frigidoom fails.
39. **0:35:53** **Atlas gives up an action next round so Frigidoom can reroll with advantage.** Thea: "You've got a chance to develop friendship, you loser!" Atlas grabs at the ball, but his fingers can't grip a perfect sphere, and **it runs Frigidoom over anyway.** Frigidoom: "Everything is so fried and now there's a giant hamster. None of this makes any sense." Pooka Pooka rolls off into the distance. "**I'll get you next time, giant hamster.**"

### Round 2 — the Double Sun Cola heart
40. **0:37:31** Frigidoom: "I've been overly cooked and run over by a hamster. This is not my day." He thanks Atlas for trying to save him, and will "return the favor… and try to touch your heart."
41. **0:37:58** The right side of the fridge bulges. **Frozen chicken, marshmallow salad and long-expired clotted cream ("fuzzy little black spots") form a giant hand** that lunges for the gaps in Atlas's plating. It hits: "**You've touched my heart and I'm going to touch yours.**"
42. **0:39:22** Inside Atlas are gears and cogs, and **at his heart is a cylindrical canister of fluid.** Frigidoom's "slimy Dorito finger jelly" clogs the gears, grabs the canister and decides it's a soda can, so **he shakes it "to feel that bubbly sensation of warmth and friendship."**
43. **0:40:21** He powers up the second shake. **Atlas defends with a natural 20**, but Dag narrates that the shaking still works on him. **"Inside of Atlas's heart is a canister of nothing more than Double Sun Cola, the most refreshing of colas that you can buy on this or any Deathmatch world."** His gears go from logical clockwork to a revving Hot Wheel, his eyes crackle orange, and **a lightning mohawk forms. Atlas "becomes 10 times more radical."**
44. **0:41:45** Rad Atlas: "**I am so pumped up and ready to open this fridge and open up a can of whoop-ass that you've got right inside you, brother.**"
45. **0:42:02** Frigidoom: "Yes, this is what I wanted. This is joy and friendship. More, more." His third shake ("shake and bake") hits. **Atlas overcharges, the mohawk grows, a few bolts pop out, and he loses a pinky.** It will make guitar solos harder. Frigidoom: "I'm sorry about the finger there." Atlas: "**Don't worry about it, man! It's just a finger!**"
46. **0:43:29 — Atlas's big turn.** He releases the restraint on the Sun Cola flow so it pours through his body, clearing a fatigue. **The mohawk evolves into a "Mo Mullet" of pure lightning.** He whispers to Frigidoom: "**Have you tried Double Sun Cola, baby? It'll make you a new man!**"
47. **0:44:50** **Atlas's chest opens, his shoulders swell, and two large suns and a soda bottle blaze in the middle**, turning into a fire hose of Double Sun Cola. The orange fluid gathers at his chest as he holds Frigidoom in a loving embrace: "**Feel the sun, brother.**" Heroic surge, power-up and status effect together, plus his signature move **"the best defense," for which he sacrifices six of his own fighting spirit.** The attack rolls 26 to hit; only a natural 20 can stop it. Frigidoom: "Acolytes, help, please." It hits for **nine**.
48. **0:47:17** Dag zooms the camera out over the canopy and birds, and **an almost nuclear explosion of bright orange fluid rises into the sky. The clouds are stained orange and yellow, and orange rain dyes the green trees.** Frigidoom stands in a crater.
49. **0:48:28 — Frigidoom reborn.** **His 1950s pastel blue casing is shattered.** Where the crisper drawer and plastic ice cube tray were, there are only soaked orange fizzy bits, a tiny beating green heart and a couple of Cheetos. **He drinks and absorbs the cola and becomes fizzy, brighter, more pungent and toxic, until he resembles Tang.** "**I'm gonna kick you in the glass, brother. Who is the power of two suns? I'm feeling fresh. But also I feel real beat up right now.**"
50. **0:49:52** **Atlas lifts him up by the arm, Macho Man/Hulk style: "Welcome to the rad side."** A transparent, veiny, muscular orange arm grips Atlas's, and "a Tang-shaped man rises up out of the ashes." Atlas: "What do you wanna be? Tell us what you want to be, little lady, and we'll make it happen… Two suns!"
51. **0:50:46** **Thea runs around licking the orange mist off the leaves and the chinchillas.** "I want two suns! I want to be rad! I want to be so fucking cool too!" She attempts her own air guitar.
52. **0:51:09** The crowd falls silent at "one of the biggest hits of the season," then erupts. **The Lord of Fate stands and claps: "This is perfect!… You get it! You have to kill him! You have to kill him dead!"** He is now "thoroughly on Atlas' side."
53. **0:52:01** Thea points him to the stands: **the orange mist has hit the Acolytes, turning them all into ripped mohawks and mullets.** The Lord of Fate calls a break to let them cool off, and he's grabbing himself a Double Sun Cola. Break cut from the mp3.

### After the break — Thea and the metal chair

That's really, I guess that's really my fault… Next time I'm going to do better."**
55. **0:53:41** Thea: "Then I will blame you." He says she can't. "But what if I did blame you?… Does that make me more powerful than you?" **Another star fades from her forehead** ("Not again!"), and she pouts in the corner.
56. **0:54:10** The Lord of Fate asks "now that you have gotten rad," what Thea is doing and whether there's a problem. The lines are garbled. Frigidoom: "I think you two need to work this out." Thea panics ("he rolled an 18"), declares "**I choose Atlas as my champion**," and hides behind Atlas's leg.
57. **0:55:04** **The Lord of Fate stands, folds his folding chair and stops time.** When she blinks, he is right behind her and she can't move. "**I didn't want to do this. You're embarrassing me in front of all of the acolytes.**" **He beats her with the metal chair two or three times "until you're like an impression in the ground,"** snaps a portal, unfreezes time, and sits back down. He tells her to start round three and summon the Box of Chaos.
58. **0:56:11 — The Box of Chaos.** Three tiny fingers rise out of the hole: "Round number three." **The giant pink Box of Chaos falls like a meteor right on top of Thea, crushing her deeper into the earth.** Pink and red flames pour out and a shadowy hand emerges. Bec explains that it gives a random item "of random willingness of the fate and world itself," in exchange for something of value and an action.

### Round 3 — pepperoni nipples

   - veins form, sunglasses appear, and a giant beaming smile;
   - **a mane of golden spaghetti-like hair**;
   - **two pepperonis become his nipples**;
   - a Speedo, wrestling boots, and frosting as the fur at the boot tops;
   - **a Geiger counter in the distance ticks out of control** as his radioactive form heats up.
   He is now "a full orange fizzy wrestling persona."
60. **0:59:01** "I thank you, Brother Atlas, for showing me this new and wondrous power. **Let me give you a taste of your own fizzy medicine.**" Kyle realizes **he looks like pizza.** His arm becomes "a giant stuffed crust of righteous fury," with **five peppers appearing on the bicep to show how hot the strike is**. "You ready to taste this radical pain, brother?" Bec rules that from now on all attacks need a five-pepper rating.
61. **1:00:32** **Atlas defends with a natural 20**, gaining a second adrenaline and regaining a fighting spirit. The Lord of Fate, from the crowd: "**Nice punch, pepperoni nipples.**" Frigidoom: "Hey, you watch it, man. **These Scovilles aren't done yet.**"
62. **1:01:36** He means to beseech the Box but instead clears his status effect. He flexes, light reflects off his chest, and **radioactive sweat and Double Sun Cola spill onto the forest floor, decaying everything they touch.** Grass withers to cracked, hideous dirt: a wasteland. Lord of Fate: "Really gonna have to find the chinchillas a new house." Thea: "I just got them here." What's a group of chinchillas called? "A herd… A murder." Atlas: "**It's a herd of chinchillas, little lady.**" Thea: "if you just say it with confidence, I'm gonna believe you."
63. **1:03:08** The cheesy fist hits Atlas with **the sound of a gong**, and cheese and bread drip from his chassis. He shakes his head in slow motion "shampoo commercial" style, lightning arcing.
64. **1:03:38 — The radical chinchilla trade.** Atlas: "**I see you trying to open up the box, brother, but that latch seems too heavy. Let me bust out the guns and get that for you.**" The shadowy figure's yellow eyes peek out. Atlas: "You wanna make a deal?… Can you handle this level of ratitude, my man in the box?" Box figure: "Are you a solicitor?" Atlas: "**I am soliciting nothing but pain for my opponent.**" He left his wallet in his other mechanical pants. Instead he offers **the most radical chinchilla ever seen: "2000" novelty spectacles, tattoo sleeves on its tiny arms, snap bracelets, and 40 limited-edition pogs in its pouch.** Thea: "It's definitely not a Petco chinchilla." **The figure accepts. The chinchilla climbs up its arm to sit on its shoulder, and the lid closes** ("I'll be right back"). The box rumbles and hops on top of the still-trapped Thea.
65. **1:06:05 — The cup.** Out floats **an empty plastic cup with rad blue and pink triangles and squiggly green lines.** It shakes and sings, and **a waterfall of blue slushy pours from the sky to fill it.** Drinking it clears one fatigue. Atlas: "**I do not have a mouth! God damn it, every time!**" Thea: it just has to get inside you somehow.
66. **1:07:41** Atlas **lifts the box off Thea** ("Don't thank me yet, little lady"), picks her up and slams the cup into her arms. **His head and legs stay still while his torso spins like a cyclone, and he hurls Thea and the slushy into "the bottom of" Frigidoom.** It hits. **Thea passes in and partway through him, covered in radical slime and slightly more "ratified"; the slushy stays inside.** It clears Frigidoom's fatigue, but **orange and blue make "pukey green," the colour of Thea's earlier vomit, so he becomes less rad.** Frigidoom: "**What? Little lady, what did you do to me?**" Thea: "Totally rad stuff, dude."
67. **1:11:09** Atlas: "Brother, let me help you." He walks over as if to help and goes for a **wrestling clothesline**, then reflavors it as a **high five**. He rolls a **natural 1**. **Frigidoom turns the high five into a Predator handshake and then an arm lock: "you son of a bitch."** They are tied 8–8.
68. **1:13:08** Thea is still stuck halfway through Frigidoom up to her shoulders: "Get out of Frigid Doom… I need help." Frigidoom: "**You all right there, little lady?**" He waves her around to shake off the sludge. Thea: "**It was better than the car wash the Lord of Fate put me through.**" Lord of Fate: "You wouldn't take a bath."
69. **1:13:56 — "Thea is people!"** The Lord of Fate: "You're not people." Thea, suddenly serious, brows furrowed: "**I am people!**" Lord of Fate: "**don't ever let them eat hot dogs because then all of a sudden they think that they're people.**" Frigidoom: "Hot dogs are the best." Atlas: "**I have an assistant, but it does not eat hot dogs. It is a tiny paperclip that sasses me out.**" Thea asks whether Frigidoom and Atlas would become human if they ate hot dogs. The Lord of Fate: "People and human are different things. They're already people… Thea isn't people… We've been over this!" Atlas: "I do not have a mouth!" Lord of Fate: "**That makes you the best kind of people.**" **Thea sits cross-legged in the middle of the fight with her thinking face.**

### Round 4 — ballpark chucks and the Double Sun Nova

That'll do."** Kyle: "That actually will do." The swing at Atlas's chest misses ("Your ball chucks aren't big enough"). **Thea snatches another hot dog "from Judy" and tosses it to him so the nunchucks are a third dog longer.** Frigidoom: "Thank you, Judy. This is a whole bratwurst now."
72. **1:18:23** He charges them with a status effect: **this time twenty peppers on his biceps**, "a bright red burning, bulging rod of bad meat." It hits. **The Scovilles and radiation burn the air and gouge across Atlas's chest, exposing the shiny Double Sun Cola core.**
73. **1:19:50** He reaches for the core one more time: "I need a taste of that drink." **Atlas seems knocked back, arms limp, then tilts his head forward, grabs Frigidoom's hand and gestures to the crowd. The Acolytes chant "ATLAS! ATLAS!"** He uses his adrenaline. **His core soaks up the 20-pepper habanero spice**, and he clears the status effect.
74. **1:21:13** "**Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to bring you a brand new product from Double Sun Cola, Double Sun [Savice/Spice?]!**" The cola turns from orange to bright red. "**So you want a taste, brother? Then let me give you a taste of the sun. As it goes Nova, baby! Double Sun Nova punch!**" It hits, putting two status effects on Frigidoom. **Thea crunches on a pickled hot dog** while she watches. Frigidoom: "**Hot dog, hot damn.** I can feel the heat. Oh, but I'm not gonna let this stop me now."
75. **1:22:40** Atlas: "You got a certain fire in you, kid. **Reminds me of when I was just a tiny little toaster.** But I can't hold back because there's only one truth about Atlas in the Deathmatch. And that is despite the results of the last battle… **Atlas is unbreakable.**"
76. **1:23:01 — The geyser.** Atlas anchors his hands to the ground, stretches his whole body back, pulses faster and faster, and **flings himself headfirst into the ground.** Silence. A sweet citrus smell spreads; **birds bail from the trees and the chinchillas take cover.** He powers up to **detonate a geyser of Double Sun Cola under Frigidoom.** It barely misses (5 v 7).
77. **1:25:12** **Frigidoom doesn't run. He opens his mouth wide, opens his whole body, and absorbs it all.** Bec narrates: "instead of taking you down, it amplifies you by a million… **no longer just orange, but neon. You glow in the darkness as it begins to set in the sun. You yourself become almost a sun.**" Frigidoom: "Oh, yes, this is it. Oh, thank you, Atlas."
78. **1:26:13** **Atlas's mohawk is gone, and he is back to the traditional, logical Atlas:** "**I feel funny. Why am I in the dirt?**" Thea helps push him out ("Your assistance is appreciated"). "But where is Frigidoom? Did I kill him?" Frigidoom puffs out his chest: "**I'm right here where you left me, brother.**" Atlas: "**Unsettling. Then come, let us finish this.**" Thea copies Atlas's two raised fists behind him, "like a small mini you." Both champions are at about five to six fighting spirit.
79. **1:27:53 — The Shadow King's blade.** **Thea trades her half-eaten hot dog to the Box of Chaos.** The Lord of Fate: "Wait, you're not supposed to have a hot dog." The figure shoves it down its mouth. **Thea receives a blade holding "a piece of the Shadow King's soul."** Each strike heals a fighting spirit but gives a fatigue, "as the Shadow King weighs heavily on your mind." Thea: "**I can't even lift this thing.**" **Lord of Fate: "I actually don't know how that got in there. That's from my private collection."** She swings it once, takes a fatigue, and throws it aside: "**Stupid Shadow King blade, I think it's dumb.**"

### Round 5 — the rad transfer
80. **1:29:12** Frigidoom, with two status effects on him and the Box at his feet, notices his friend is "not as radical." He adjusts his gelatinous sunglasses: "**Something's wrong here and I need to fix it before I can fix myself. Atlas, do you want to be radical again?**" Atlas: "**Illogical. ## Who and what appears
### Characters
**Atlas** (Dag / DeadAussieGamer). A huge, lumbering automaton with grey metal plating. He is logical and formal when sober and a wrestler-voiced "brother" when radical.
- **Body:**
  - No mouth (a very sore spot) and no nose.
  - Gears and cogs inside; **his heart is a canister of Double Sun Cola**, which flows through his body.
  - Magnetic lightning bolts run along his shoulders and arms, and a power gauntlet holds glowing cylinders that overheat for energy blasts.
  - When the cola is shaken or released he becomes "radical": a lightning mohawk that evolves into a lightning mo-mullet, orange-crackling eyes, and a sun-and-bottle emblem on his chest.
- **Lore and history:**
  - He died in his last battle ("I saw eternity… Hell is real even for automatons") and was brought back "because we were bored."
  - The Lord of Fate recalls him drinking a lot of Double Sun Cola.
  - Was once "a tiny little toaster."
  - Has an assistant: a tiny paperclip that sasses him.
- **In this episode:**
  - Lost a pinky.
  - Traded the radical chinchilla to the Box of Chaos.
  - Was made permanently radical by Frigidoom's signature move.
  - Won. He now carries Frigidoom bottled in his chest next to his heart and is collecting the fridge pieces to rebuild him.

**Frigidoom** (Kyle / KidCalrissian). A giant, irradiated **retro 1950s pastel-blue refrigerator** full of a living, gelatinous food mass.
- **Body and habits:**
  - Green jello arms and acidic tendrils.
  - A rainbow mustache, which a "smelly samurai" cut in his last battle; he glued it back with old yogurt.
  - Leaves a sizzling trail. Smelly and gross, and had never been complimented or hugged before.
  - Has had off-brand sodas like "Doctor Lightning," never Double Sun Cola before this fight.
- **Transformations this episode:**
  - His casing was shattered.
  - He became a Tang-orange muscle man, then a pizza-like wrestler with spaghetti hair, pepperoni nipples, a Speedo and frosting-trimmed boots, radioactive.
  - The slushy turned him pukey green; then neon and sun-like; then he drained to a green ooze, now in a bottle inside Atlas.
- **Grudge:** wants Atlas to beat "that Kyle… weeaboo samurai" and his princess wife ("Princess Sokka," "uwu princess wife").

**Thea** (Bec). The Lord of Fate's small assistant, with tiny pink feet and three tiny fingers. She flies.
- **Duties:**
  - In charge of the Dread Fortress chinchillas (feeding, walking, unionizing, poop).
  - A to-do chart on the fridge, which she can't fully read.
  - Taking complaints ("basic level Arbiter stuff"), which she doesn't do.
  - Arranging the pre-match meal, which she forgot.
- **Stars on her forehead:** the Lord of Fate takes them away; she lost two this episode, "not many" left.
- **The Lord of Fate:** says he "designed" her and didn't design her for remembering, and that she is "not people." She insists "I am people!"; he blames hot dogs. He once put her through a car wash.
- **In this episode:**
  - Beaten into the ground with his metal chair, then crushed under the Box of Chaos.
  - Thrown through Frigidoom by Atlas.
  - Got the Shadow King's blade and threw it away.
  - Radicalized: blonde hair, sunglasses, mustache.
- Watched "Bessie and the Cowdettes" part one at 20× speed.

**The Lord of Fate** (Drew). Rules the Deathmatch from a **folding metal chair**; has wings. "I'm running an entire realm."
- **Powers:** can stop time and step through portals. Summons the Box of Chaos through Thea.
- Keeps a **"private collection"** that includes a blade holding a piece of the Shadow King's soul; he doesn't know how it got into the Box.
- Sided with Atlas after the big hit. Approves of recycling.

**The shadowy figure in the Box of Chaos** (Drew). Has yellow eyes. It accepted the radical chinchilla, which now sits on its shoulder, and ate Thea's half-eaten hot dog.

**The chinchillas.** Seven of them, kept in the Dread Fortress caverns and released into the Chinchilla Forest for a "sabbatical." They mimic Thea.
- A plump grey one with a cigarette.
- A ginger-striped one.
- **The radical chinchilla:** "2000" glasses, tattoo sleeves, snap bracelets, 40 limited-edition pogs. Given to the Box.
- **"Concordae"**, who helped Frigidoom.
- A group is "a herd" (stated with confidence by Atlas).

**Pooka Pooka the hamster.** Rampages through the Chinchilla Forest in his plastic ball, swearing "to destroy the world that caged him." He rolled off into the distance.

**Off-screen characters:**
- **Killy Yesman**: the Double Sun Cola salesperson Atlas fought before (Dried Blood, out of character).
- **Judy**: someone Thea snatched a hot dog from (unclear).
- **"A small child named Nathan"**: a joke.

  - After this fight it is stained orange.
  - Part of it is decayed into a radioactive wasteland by Frigidoom's sweat, so the chinchillas need a new home.
