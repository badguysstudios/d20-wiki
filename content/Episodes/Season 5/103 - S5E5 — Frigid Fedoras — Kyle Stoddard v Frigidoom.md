## What happens
### Opening: Thea's room becomes an office

The Lord of Fate has put all her things in one box, and **she can sleep in the box next to it**. Thea notes he'd earlier been "so generous by giving me this large closet, and then you took it back." She perches on the edge of a **massive desk that takes up almost the whole room**.
3. **0:06:31 — The complaints.** Every file and box in the room is "**formal complaints about your performance**." Thea protests that last time he gave her "like five stars! I could tell because I had a really hard time getting it off of my forehead because **you carved it in**."

Deathmatches still count, since they're "within the bounds of the Dread Fortress… cosmically speaking." Thea panics: the Fortress isn't big enough for the two champions she pulled in, and she just cleaned it. She darts about with her thinking cap on: "Where's bigger?"

The ice machine kicks into overdrive "from all of the radiation and all of the stuck food," and a deluge of crushed ice full of food bits, "maybe creatures," and bacteria hails onto Thea. **She is beaten, bruised and frozen inside a giant popsicle of his "disposed water."** Frigidoom: "That doesn't always happen.
