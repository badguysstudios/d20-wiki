## What happens
### Opening: Halloween at the Dread Fortress
1. **0:06:41** The Lord of Fate walks through the empty halls of the Dread Fortress. Laughter and giggles echo in the distance. **The goblins are missing from their chores.**
2. **0:07:11** Two small "ghosts" dash from one door to another across the hallway. One is **a small sheet with tiny green feet**. The other follows it with **tiny pink feet**: "I'm gonna get you!"
   - One ghost freezes. Through two cut eye holes it shows **big bright yellow eyes**: "Nothing."
3. **0:07:47** The Lord of Fate asks: "Are those my **Egyptian cotton bed sheets**?"
   - The ghost twirls and flies up, and **Thea** can be seen under the sheet.
   - "Well, there aren't sheets on your bed, so yes." "They're really cozy."
4. **0:08:10 — The star.** He snaps his fingers. Under the sheet, **one of the stars on Thea's forehead heals and fizzles away**. Thea: "Oh, come on!"
   - He tells her she's lucky he isn't taking it out of her paycheck.
   - "I get a paycheck?" "No, but it's the principle of the thing."
5. **0:08:32 — The principal gag.**
   - Thea: "I thought you were the Lord, not a principle." She asks if she can be "the principle," since a principal is "like a prince… so I get a crown!"
   - He gives up: "Just knock yourself out. Whatever. I don't care."
   - Thea keeps calling herself a principal for the rest of the night.
6. **0:09:04 — His room.** Thea opens the door to the Lord of Fate's room. It is decked out in **cobwebs, scattered skeletons and massive pumpkins** "bigger than your head, which is already pretty big if you ask me." She does a lap under her sheet: "Boo!"

### Round 1

    - Teddy: "I've been bamboozled." Thea: "Underused athletic skill that no one ever thought of."

    - Granny: "Young man, I hope you're going to help me up."
    - Teddy: "Oh, no, I'm not grandma. You get up yourself." He giggles "because I like watching old people fall to the ground."
20. **0:20:18** "Well, I never met such a rude young man." **Thea slides under Granny like a flat table and lifts her up.** Granny: "You're so sweet. Lucky I brought some extra cookies."
21. **0:20:30 — The fly swat lands.**

    - She pulls out **a fly swat** ("my nana always had a fly swat in her hand") and swats him on one ear, then the other.
22. **0:21:38 — Teddy's name.**

26. **0:24:59** Thea rolls a 7: "a lucky number… It's on my report card because I'm a principal." The crowd rips off shirts and spills beers and slushies. **Chaos is triggered.**

## Dried Blood (after-show)
- Drew opens: episode 8 of Season 5, "Granny Edith returning versus our new challenger, Teddy Oso. We got **Chunk**, we got **Lubuffin**." To Luboffin: "**You actually didn't die this time.**"
- Luboffin says the tide turned once she remembered the baking puns. Bec suggests making a pun her hero move. Drew: the dice "oddly did seem" to shift after the puns started.
- **Chunk admits he isn't an energy drinker, a motorcyclist, or a gun owner**, and has never beaten up an old person: "I'm just a really good actor." Drew: "Well, now I just feel fucking lied to."
