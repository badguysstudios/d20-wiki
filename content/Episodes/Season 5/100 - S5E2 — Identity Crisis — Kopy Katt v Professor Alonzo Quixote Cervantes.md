## What happens
### Opening — the Lord of Fate's throne room
1. **0:05:33** "The sweet sounds of death ring in the air as the Lord of Fate awaits Thea in his throne room." He calls for her. **Thea has been asleep** on a little pile of dust and straw, with **a small pile of mud for a pillow**. She calls it a "power nap… like how the champions get like a power up." The Lord of Fate: "**You don't get to nap. I don't create you to sleep.**"
2. **0:06:36** Remembering it's deathmatch day, the imp zips around looking for her clipboard. **It's underneath the Lord of Fate on his throne.** She blames it on **a chinchilla**: "Those chinchillas are the absolute worst." He walks down, holds the clipboard out, and **drops it before she can take it**. Thea: "I love picking stuff up from the ground." He: "Good, then it works for both of us." Bec notes there is only one chair in the room, and it's his.
3. **0:07:53** Thea glows with a pink aura. She has found two worthy champions and wonders what color they bleed. The Lord of Fate says blood is "mostly red… I've seen a lot of people die." **Thea gives herself a paper cut with her notepad and bleeds sparkling blue, like glitter.** "Does that mean I'm special?" He tells her she's "not a real person". She takes this as "**I am special.**"
4. **0:09:04 — Where she found them.** The first champion came from **the Social Security Administration office**: "anybody who can put up with the long lines… surely they've got something to prove." The Lord of Fate: "**Did you bring me an old person?**… We can't have people beating up elderly people in the arena… The optics are not good… We'll get the PR team on it." The second she found **fighting space crocodiles behind space waffles**. They said "hell yeah, or something. I didn't actually listen, but it looked positive."
5. **0:10:35 — Employment terms.** The Lord of Fate praises her "don't ask" attitude. Thea asks for a raise and is told **she isn't paid at all**: "**Do you think I got paid as the Arbiter?**" Yesterday's meal was "just to keep you alive… **If you die, I have to go back to doing everything myself.**"
6. **0:11:17** He fixes his robes and settles into the throne. Thea critiques the cushion ("a little more firm on the right side… better butt pressure") and claims she **studied seat formations in college**. He replies: "**I created you a week ago, you didn't go to college.**" Thea: "I did the accelerated program."

### Champion entrances
7. **0:11:57 — Kopy Katt.** Thea claps, and **two bright pink portals** swirl open and grow as she flaps her wings. She announces "the portal of dangerousness, which is not really its name," and the imp bows low. The champion's first line is "**Do you want to give me your social security number?**" Thea offers that Vythorn "has one somewhere."
8. **0:13:16 — The copying begins.** The Lord of Fate objects ("I'm not wearing a bag on my head"), and Kopy Katt **repeats every line he says word for word**: "Why are you copying me?" "Why are you copying me?" He tests her with "**Banana Earthworm Smoothies**," and she repeats that too. Thea can't tell them apart, so she asks what color her blood is. The champion guesses pink. Kopy Katt answers "Blue," and Thea declares her the Lord of Fate. "This is a hoot and a holler."
9. **0:14:39 — Professor Cervantes** walks in calmly with **a leather suitcase**, sets it down and breathes deeply. "**I'm actually very familiar with your combat here. I'm quite a fan.**" He is a "**professor of all things warfare and combat and battle… a professor and a student of Bloodshed.**" Kopy Katt echoes him: "Double the excitement." **He is "rather elderly. Jacked, but elderly."** The Lord of Fate: "when I said don't bring any elderly people into the arena, this is what I meant." The Professor: "**I assure you I am young in spirit.**" He partakes in milk, and his bones "have endured quite a bit, and they shall endure more."
10. **0:16:21** Kopy Katt copies him as well ("What is this creature?" "What is this creature?"). The Professor: "Is this what I am deemed to fight today?" The Lord of Fate: "Luckily, it'll be hard to hear her from the stands."

### The arena — Emmery's Tavern
11. **0:16:45** Thea picked **a tiny tavern with a big giant skull over the fireplace** "to inspire the monstrous fight." It's owned by **Emmery**, who makes a specialty drink called **ale number two**. Thea sells it as supporting small businesses, and says he doesn't mind if they destroy the place: "what else are taverns for?" Nobody knows his last name ("Maybe first name M and last name R?"). She promises the Lord of Fate **50 of Emmery's business cards and his photo ID**. Kopy Katt says she has about **67 IDs**.
12. **0:18:23 — The milk portal.** Thea opens her portals with **a second portal on top that pours a waterfall of milk**, so the champions must walk through it, because of the bone talk. The Lord of Fate opens his own portal and shuts it behind him. Bec: "You don't have a second portal. You have to go through the milk portal." **The Professor basks in the milk, and Kopy Katt mimics his basking exactly.** It soaks "every old man wrinkle."
13. **0:19:34** Inside are stone floors, a warm fire and gentle music. **An old bartender (Emmery) cleans the bar** and says every now and then, "you got to buy something or you got to get out." The Lord of Fate: "It's kind of dirty in here." A sad man lies asleep, or possibly dead, leaking "vomit or blood or something else."
14. **0:20:27 — Identity #68.** **Kopy Katt takes the sleeping man's wallet, ID and credit card.** "Starts with a J." Thea: "That means you have 68 identities now."
15. **0:20:48 — The number two.** The Professor pays politely for a number two. Emmery keeps **wiping the same spot on the bar, which is worn from it**. He says "that's all we have," and then confides that **the secret ingredient is sheep dung**: "I like to smoke the grains over sheep dung. Maybe a little sprinkle in." The Professor asks for a straw because "It gets in the mustache." He says "I love an exotic experience," downs it in full and asks for another. **Kopy Katt mirrors him exactly.** Emmery: "This is the most business I've had all year."
16. **0:22:26** Both fail a Mettle save. **The ale is "not completely liquid… there's some solids in there,"** and both start the match one fighting spirit down, though the alcohol makes them feel fine.
17. **0:24:03 — The fake Lord of Fate.** Kopy Katt taps Thea's shoulder wearing **"the most terribly made mask," which looks exactly like the Lord of Fate**: "I'll take a number two. Bring it to my throne." Thea is amazed at how fast he got down there. She orders more, and Emmery says she has to buy four. She has them **put on the Lord of Fate's tab** and leads a toast "all for the Lord of Fate" at the impostor. The real one: "**I'm up here.**" Thea: "This is magic. I am so impressed."

### Round 1
18. **0:25:31** Thea rules that Kopy Katt doesn't go first, because the four number-two ales she downed "basically equates to eight."
19. **0:26:14** **The Professor throws his full glass at Kopy Katt** ("I was nursing that one for a reason"). She rolls a nat 1 on defense and **gulps down yet another disgusting ale**, compelled to keep up the drinking game. She takes damage and a fatigue. "**I feel like I'm about to take this paper bag off my head and vomit in it.**" The Lord of Fate: "She admitted she's wearing a paper bag. See, everyone heard it. I'm not crazy."
20. **0:27:21 — The bag comes off.** The Professor bullies her: he **grabs the bag she's puking into and throws it back in her face**. Under it is **"an extremely purpley-pilled face" with no eyes, nose or mouth. Instead there is a horribly drawn red-marker smiley face that "looks like a three-year-old drew on it."** The Professor: "I have never in my days seen a creature like you… **Show me your capacity for violence!**" She echoes both lines.
21. **0:29:20** "**I do not speak for myself. I speak for many.**" **She flips through her book, takes out a card, and puts on a terribly made papier-mâché mask of Thea.** Thea: "I knew I had my favorite fighter!" The Professor: "Where did you get the newspaper?"
22. **0:29:51 — The chinchilla stampede.** She **blows a whistle and summons a stampede of chinchillas with towels**. The chinchillas accept her as "the original Thea" and follow her whistles like herded sheep. "**Wipe them down, boys.**" They **towel-whip the Professor**. Bec explains that **these are unionized chinchillas with full health-care benefits, free gym memberships and a fully stocked kitchen**, so they're jacked, and each towel tip is "just a little wet" to snap hard. The Professor goes pale: "in all of my travels and all of my research, I've never encountered a creature as fearsome as a chinchilla."
23. **0:32:49 — The haircut.** Kopy Katt orders the chinchillas to get **hair clippers** and give the Professor a new haircut. "No one's touching the stash." **He doesn't move. His mustache moves itself around the clippers.** It is **sentient**: "I visited a tribe not too long ago of… people with sentient hair… **The chief had the nose hair who could write poems.**" Kopy Katt: "Looks like I won't be able to ruin your appearance so easily." Bec: "That was a close shave." Thea explains the pun to the Lord of Fate at length.
24. **0:34:51 — Thea's performance report.** The Lord of Fate asks Thea for the end-of-round roll, and Thea has the "other Thea" (Kopy Katt) roll it. It comes up 3. "**I want you to know that despite the fact that she rolled for you, this still reflects on your performance report.**" He clicks a pen and makes a note. Thea rerolls a 14 and adds them, theorizing that "because we're both Thea, we just got split and our energy got also split." He: "If I let it slide, then the integrity of the whole process…" Thea offers to get him another number two. "How about you give me a round number two?"

### Round 2
25. **0:36:45 — Throwing the patron.** The Professor asks whether the passed-out man is a corpse ("It doesn't matter") and **picks him up to throw at Kopy Katt**. The man is alive and struggles: "**Hey, hey, hey, hey, hey.**" The Professor: "**I'm sorry, sir. I was really counting on you being dead.**" The man lands on Kopy Katt and knocks her down. "He's heavy… **I'm gonna have to put on some weight.**" Bec points out bags of salt she could stuff into her "trash bag."
26. **0:38:20 — The table.** The Professor grabs the man's table and spins to throw it at both of them. "Unlike the man, the table says nothing when you pick it up." Both dodge. **He throws it straight into the fireplace**, where it roars with heat as spilled alcohol feeds the flames. "**It felt like it was getting cold in here.**" Thea calls him considerate and buys him **three more number twos** (Emmery's minimum), all of which he must drink. Emmery is very happy.
27. **0:40:49 — The ID check.** **Kopy Katt impersonates the bartender:** "You ordered a couple drinks? I'll need to see some ID." The Professor produces about five IDs and asks for the discount. "If you show me all five, I can give you **the senior discount**."
28. **0:41:45 — Identity theft.** She calls **social services**: "**I would like to report identity theft.**" Asked for the last name, the Professor answers "**Thea**." Thea: "Is everyone just going to steal my identity today?" Kopy Katt reads the ID instead: "**Professor Alonzo Quixote Cervantes. Esquire.**" The operator flags all associated businesses, closes his bank accounts, issues a credit alert and cancels all his flights and travel. The Professor: "**I just got to 850.**" It hits ("Roll to financially cripple Professor Cervantes… Identity theft is a crime"). He: "**I enjoy being stabbed. This is just rude.**" She: "Looks like you'll have a hard time covering those medical bills." He: "**Joke's on you, I'm not American.**" "I hope you have travel insurance." "That one's more valid, yes."
29. **0:44:44 — Calling his mother.** "I said capacity for violence. Not fraud." **Kopy Katt calls the Professor's mother while posing as him** ("Mother?"). Bec voices the mother: "**Cervantes, my little Cervantes**… It's been so long… What's my little fighter?" Posing as her son, Kopy Katt tells her he hates her, says that's why he never returned her calls, and says he has **subscribed her email to "all manner of home improvement magazines."** (The Professor: "I actually did that and she was renovating her house.") Then: "I never want to meet you again… **I have some crocodiles to wrestle that are way more important than you.**" The mother: "**I didn't love your other siblings because I chose you**… I've got on the wrong child!" The real Professor is distraught, but also: "**I knew I was the favorite. Hell yeah.**"
30. **0:46:50** The roll is a tie. **The mother thinks back over their life together and refuses to accept it was him:** "**Not my little fighter boy. He would never. He must be drinking.**" Both take a fatigue. Thea tells him to write a very long apology letter, because "flowers aren't gonna cut it this time, fighter boy." He: "We've been estranged for quite some time. This is actually probably for the best."
31. **0:48:36** Thea tries to add her end-of-round roll to the old 17. The Lord of Fate corrects her: it was a 3, so they're at 8.

### Round 3 — the Box of Chaos
32. **0:49:22** "**The Acolytes have chosen to summon the Box of Chaos!**" The Lord of Fate drops it into the middle of the bar, and a pink and yellow column of fire shoots up. **"His own shadow now fueling the box" creeps its hands out**: "Hey, how's it going? What is this place?" Kopy Katt copies the shadow, which says "Stop that" and **shuts the lid on himself**.
33. **0:50:42 — The Professor's sacrifice.** Rogue describes Cervantes as a man of violence whose finances and family bonds have been taken. He is "succumbing to his own inner bloodshed." To the shadow: "Certainly not, my friend. She's kind of the worst." He offers what he holds most dear, because she jeopardized "the thing I care most about in this world. **My money.**" Then: "And again, I guess my mother… That's top 10, I guess." **He winces, grabs his mustache and rips the whole thing off; it keeps its shape.** The shadow palms it: "I don't have one of these."
34. **0:52:46 — The reward: his favorite snack**, **a salad covered in Worcestershire sauce**. "I am an educated man. I have an educated meal." Thea and Bec mangle the word repeatedly ("just a little bit of Sean Connery in there"). He eats it "in the most gentlemanly and educated way" and regains fighting spirit. **The shadow lounges on the edge of the box wearing the now-white mustache**, smoking a cigar: "That's a pretty good deal… I don't have lunch now." The Professor: "That really hurt… it was a pleasure doing business with you."
35. **0:54:59 — The spear.** **He pulls a long, plain, non-magical spear out of his suitcase.** Bec asks if he Mary Poppins'd it. He says he isn't American: "Where did you think I was from?" He hurls it, and it hits. Kopy Katt: "**You may lose me physically for now. But you'll be feeling pain for years to come.**" He: "**Good thing I'm also a lawyer.**" Thea: "It said Esquire." Rogue: "That lore was planted."
36. **0:56:46 — Kopy Katt's apology gift.** She knocks on the box. The shadow: "Ah, fuck, what do you want?" She apologizes for copying him: "**I got a condition, you know. Doctor says… I'm going to therapy.**" She commiserates about feeling confined. He: "I'm literally confined to this box." **She gives him "a brand new chest… three times the size of your regular chest," with multiple compartments and hidden storage**, the red one over there. Thea: "Oh my gosh, the souls will love that." Shadow: "**This never happens to people like me.**"
37. **0:58:47 — The rusty spoon.** The box pops open without the shadow, and **a rusty old spoon** flies out. It can "serve up a tetanus" as an extra status effect. Shadow: "**Listen, this rusty spoon has served me very well in a lot of knife fights.**" He was packing up anyway. **He crawls out with a bag over his back and no legs, like a hermit crab changing shells, dragging himself by one hand to the new chest.** The Professor: "I am taking notes… Fascinating." Kopy Katt says it's "all paid off" by the Lord of Fate. Shadow: "**He never used to treat me this good when I was his actual shadow.**" Kopy Katt: "Lord of Fate's going to see that in the next two weeks… a purchase." Drew narrates it as "what I assume is a stolen chest."
38. **1:01:12 — The spoon attack.** The Professor rolls a nat 20 on defense and gains an adrenaline. He chooses to be hit anyway and look at her: "**Do you really think you go the places I go and not have your tetanus shot?**" She: "I didn't read that on your medical record yet." "And you never shall." Thea: "the tetanus consumed by your white blood cells."
39. **1:02:19 — Break.** The Lord of Fate wants "a drink that isn't filled with sheep shit" from the concession stands. "**Welcome back, Acolytes, to the arena, to this shithole of a tavern.**" (Cut break.) Thea recaps the man-throw and table-in-fire. The Lord of Fate: "it was pretty cool when he threw that guy."

### Round 4 — Chaotic event: paper vs rock
40. **1:04:10** "Despite what the meter looks like," a chaotic event is triggered. Thea calls him "Lord of Chaos" ("It's Lord of Fate, but close enough"). **The Lord of Fate rolls a nat 20**: "Ah, feels good. Feels good being the Lord."
41. **1:05:09** "**An old wives tale is rumored to be of the mysterious power of paper**… The thin slices of trees were capable of fending off the strength of stones." The Acolytes are "all supposed to bring rocks." The champions must build paper shields. Both fail.
42. **1:06:48** Thea flies up and summons the horde of Acolytes: "**Behold! The true power of rocks because paper always sucks!**" They throw boulders, magic stones, obsidian, "**rocks named Sid**," gravel, and "**the shoulder boulder holders**." Both take damage and a fatigue. "**Never choose paper.**"
43. **1:08:01 — Thea's lie.** Cervantes, "a man of honor," corrects her math. Thea wants to go to school to be smart like the Professor. "Didn't you just tell me today that you went to college?" "**I lied.**" "**They didn't accept worms.** As it turns out, they didn't like us." The Lord of Fate: "lying to management. Okay, great."
44. **1:09:05 — The rotary phone.** All this time **the Professor has been on an old-school rotary phone pulled from his suitcase**, dealing with **a credit advisor**. With his money back he throws the phone at Kopy Katt as a power-up: "I'm back at 850." It misses **and lands in the fire, like the table**. Bec: "you've mapped out a trajectory." Thea: "That's what I do at the bowling alley… Straight to the gutter."
45. **1:10:45** He tries to **clothesline her into the fire** as a status effect ("This was all part of my plan"), and misses. Thea: "Maybe college really isn't worth it." The Lord of Fate: "**If he can be a lawyer, anybody can.**" He needs a new phone; there may be one behind the bar.
46. **1:12:18 — The credit score.** Kopy Katt: "you seem to be a little heated." ("Was that the fire pun?") She tries to **cripple his credit score**. Thea says **her own credit score is "around 20"**. The Lord of Fate: "It's something people made up… but it has very real consequences." It fails: "**I just got off of the line with Equifax. I have legal shield right now.**"
47. **1:14:09 — Eight toes.** "There's only one thing left for me to take." Thea: "His toes!" **The Professor has eight toes**, which explains his terrible balance and aim.
48. **1:14:56 — Bullshit University.** Posing as his mother, **"Mrs. Professor Cervantes,"** Kopy Katt calls **the institution that gave him his degree ("Bullshit University," Bec)** and reports that he has **passed away**. The university closes his records and sends the details, at her request, to **the Waffle House in the back** ("Next to a couple of crocodiles," the Professor). He: "**They do not like me at that Waffle House.**" A status effect to embarrass him so he can never show his face at a Waffle House fails. He: "**I have tenure.**"
49. **1:17:18** Kopy Katt: "**There's a process to everything. And destroying your reputation is the number one most important thing. Wounds can heal, body parts can heal, or be replaced, but your identity is everything.**" Thea writes it down. The Professor notes he could replace his missing toes.

### Chaotic event 2 — the frozen chinchilla (1:18:12)
50. Thea's 7 triggers "Chaos!" **The Professor rolls the fate: 18.** The fire goes out. **A gigantic blizzard** bursts against the windows, spreading icicles and snow. The front door opens and in comes **a tiny chinchilla all by itself** (the Professor backs away immediately). It begs for warmth with frozen paws, looking for a snuggle buddy.

So keep buying drinks.**"
52. **1:21:29** Thea: "**Another round for everyone on the Lord of Fate.**" He: "What? You can't just say that. **We're on a budget.**" Thea: "don't take back-sies."

60. **1:35:04** The Lord of Fate tells Thea to "put that thumb back on." Thea: "**I lost it the last time I chopped it off.**"

### Round 7

## Who and what appears
### Characters
**[[Characters/Copycat|Kopy Katt]]** (Kat). A copier with no identity of her own: "I am everyone and I am everywhere… not one is my own." Called "a changeling of sorts" by Drew (Dried Blood).
- **Appearance:** wore a paper bag over her head. Underneath is a purplish, featureless face with a red-marker smiley face drawn like a three-year-old's. She puts on terrible masks (the Lord of Fate, papier-mâché Thea).
- **How she copies:** repeats others' words exactly and mirrors their actions. She keeps a book of cards to pick personas. Carries 67 IDs, 68 after robbing the patron "J".
- **Recruited** from the Social Security Administration office.
- **Abilities and tricks:**
  - A whistle that commands the unionized chinchillas.
  - Phone calls (identity theft, credit, the fake death report). "Your identity is everything."
  - Her signature move copies the opponent's (here a gorilla). She claims "a condition… I'm going to therapy."
- **Relationships:** gave the Box of Chaos shadow a new red chest, supposedly paid for by the Lord of Fate.
- **Partner:** tag-teamed with Emmery.
- Federal agents are reportedly tracking her for her "impressive record."

**[[Characters/Professor Alonzo Quixote Cervantes|Professor Alonzo Quixote Cervantes, Esquire]]** (Rogue). Elderly but jacked.
- **Profession:** professor and student of warfare, combat and bloodshed; also a lawyer, with tenure. His degree is from "Bullshit University" (per the operator).
- **Background:**
  - A fan of the show.
  - Found fighting space crocodiles behind space waffles. Not welcome at a Waffle House near crocodiles.

- **Body and habits:** eight toes, bad aim and balance. Drinks milk. His favorite snack is a salad covered in Worcestershire sauce.

- **Possessions:** a leather suitcase holding a spear, a rotary phone and more. Also carries an 80s brick phone.
- **Money:** credit score 850; values money most of all.
- **Family:** estranged from his mother, whose favorite he is. He has siblings, and his mother has a husband.
- **Death:** died slapped into the fireplace by Emmery-as-his-mother.

- Pink portals, an arrow tail, a credit score around 20.
