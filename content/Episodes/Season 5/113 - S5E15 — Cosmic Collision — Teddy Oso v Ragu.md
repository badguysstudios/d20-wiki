Two returning favourites, the zombie-slaying **[[Teddy Oso]]** and the luxuriously coiffed **[[Ragu]]**, were dropped onto an asteroid in the rings of a roaring gas giant. They fought the longest battle of the season there. The match was booked by [[Thea]] as her last chance to keep her job. It featured a hijacked 1998 Chevy Impala, a frozen [[Chinchillas|chinchilla]], a nipple that changed hands twice, and a finishing kick delivered in genuine slow motion.

## The Story

### The Last Straw

The night began with shouting. "Welcome to D20 Deathmatch, everyone, where we are loud for no reason and you can all suck it," bellowed [[The Arbiter|the Lord of Fate]], ruler of [[The Void]] in the Season of Fate. Thea protested that there was more planned than him screaming his head off, and he conceded there was, "but it's not going to be any good."

Deep in [[The Dread Fortress]], where the walls sweat with condensation as lava meets cool air, the Lord of Fate clacked across the stone in what he called handcrafted Italian leather shoes. Thea pointed out that they were cacti leather, and that cacti leather is also handcrafted. He was hunting for her, "because she's never where she needs to be." He followed noises to the throne room and found her fast asleep in his throne. He crept up, put his face right into hers and asked, "Are you enjoying your nap?" She fluttered off, wiping away drool that poured "like a waterfall," and insisted she was fine.

She was not fine. "I could put up with a lot of bullshit, but this is the last straw," he declared. "You're fired." Thea's first question was whether he was going to burn her. He noted that she had once refused to put her feet where his feet had been, yet had no problem putting her butt cheeks where his had been. Thea reassured him: "Oh, uh, I put a towel down first."

He demanded her clipboard, then relented into a probationary period. She had this one Deathmatch to prove herself, and if she failed, "not only will I fire you, I will destroy you." Thea asked about severance. "Something will be severed, yes."

### Two Returning Champions

Thea had brought back two fighters, because she was sure the Lord of Fate had loved them last time. He had never said so, but she had read his mind, "even though I can't read minds." He allowed her to speak their names to pull them through the portal, "just not to me," so she turned to face the wall and clapped. The wall dissolved into pink glitter dust.

Teddy Oso arrived first. The Lord of Fate recalled that Teddy's zombie count had stood at 12,000 last time. Teddy corrected him: "we're like in the 30,000 range at this point." Thea promised to update his profile on the Dread Fortress Void web. When asked how anyone kills that many zombies, Teddy explained that sometimes you go one at a time to savour the moment, "and sometimes you just like grenade, just thump."

Thea presented Ragu with Vanna White jazz hands, admitting she hadn't asked before "just sucking you through the portal." He didn't mind. "My legs are ready. Ragu is back."

### Out of This World

Another clap, and the Fortress faded away. The champions stood on rock in open space, under endless stars, beside a giant purple planet whose storms thundered audibly across the void. Their arena was its orange ring of meteorites. "I brought you here," Thea announced, "because I thought the two of you were out of this world."

Ragu booed. Thea declared that the crowd laughed uproariously. The Lord of Fate immediately overruled her. In revenge, Thea handicapped both champions for not laughing at her joke, reminding them that "everything Thea says is gold." Ragu offered to "pimp smack your ass," then fumbled his start, and Teddy took the first move, crying, "Praise me! Adore me!" [[The Acolytes]] obliged. They screamed his name, ripped off their shirts, spilled blue slushies and fired the newly stocked Teddy Oso shotguns from the concession stands into the air.

### Round One: Gravity Power

Teddy opened casually. He cocked a hip in a pose Thea compared to a catalogue model and lobbed his axe "all gentle-like, but with a lot of force behind it." Ragu batted it away perfectly. Teddy then flipped his shotgun like a drum major and let one round go, hitting Ragu square in the chest and sending him floating off into space. Thea offered a rope. Ragu declined: he had "gravity power. That's my weight." The Lord of Fate made a note to use that excuse himself.

Ragu answered with his nunchucks, which caught Teddy on the forehead. Teddy took the hit with a dead stare: "I never, never flinch. Never close my eyes." Ragu then spotted a bandage on Teddy's right knee and struck it. That led to an unsettling confession. Teddy had been bitten by a zombie, but he wasn't turning, because "every time I kill a zombie, I eat a tiny little piece of it." He eats it raw, from fleshy bits like "the thumb muscle or like a butt cheek." By his count it came to about 40 or 50 people's worth. Thea, clipboard-less, took notes on her hand. When Ragu tried to rip off the bandage and "beat the zombie back in" him, Teddy stepped aside with a lazy lunge and resumed his pose. Thea fainted.

### The Impala

The Acolytes demanded blood, or possibly ice cream, and the first chaos event struck. A glint appeared overhead. Was it a shiny bird? A silver 1998 Chevy Impala with Michigan plates? No. It was a metal chair, which Teddy dodged and which smacked Ragu. Thea consoled him with a prize: if he won, the Impala was his. He was disgusted at first. Then he noticed it was metal, not plastic, apologised for his outburst, and climbed in. Nobody knew who had given him the keys; Thea had forgotten to lock it. "Look at this 8-track."

Round two became a car fight. Teddy stepped on Ragu's arm and grabbed his "long, luscious blonde hair" to slam his face into his knee. Ragu dodged, corrected him ("This is ultra silver") and, safe inside the car, dared him to "make my millennium." Teddy tried to rip the door off, but Chevy Impalas, Thea explained, "were built to last." When Thea teased that Teddy was just jealous because he rode a motorcycle, Teddy replied that he had better gas mileage. On it he picked up his girlfriends Susie and Betty, one of whom was a zombie. "Not all zombies are violent. Some just want to be loved." He eventually has to shoot them, because they start to smell. That prompted the Lord of Fate to consider a firmer hand with Dread Fortress hygiene, and Thea hurried off in search of deodorant.

Ragu tried to run Teddy over. Then he tried to ram him with what he called his "long doors." Teddy met the charge with one perfect axe swing that gashed the door from end to end. "Your insurance is not gonna like it."

### The Areola Affair

In round three Teddy hauled Ragu out of the car by his herbal-scented hair. He backflipped over him, faked a landing on his groin, then buried the axe in the middle of Ragu's chest. Ragu sobbed over how long that chest had taken to build. Worse, the blow had taken his nipple. Teddy pocketed it. "Is that your pepperoni snack for later?" Thea asked. Ragu fought back with spiked nunchucks that drew blood from Teddy's belly button. A follow-up nail grenade missed, and Teddy's advice was simply to roll higher: "I'm just repeating the zeitgeist."

The second chaos event brought a shivering chinchilla to each champion, begging for warmth. Teddy tucked his into his jacket pocket with a sweat rag. Ragu, drained of blood and body heat, watched his freeze into a tiny ice cube.

In round four Ragu combined his nunchucks for a ranged strike "for my areola." Then he mugged Teddy and snatched the nipple back. Since it had shared a pocket with the chinchilla, it came back hairy, and Ragu stitched it on anyway. "Why is my areola so hairy?" This led to a discussion of the fuzzy navel and the slippery nipple as cocktails. Teddy revealed that "Hairy nipple was my uncle," known to the family as Uncle Nipple. Thea eventually tricked the Lord of Fate into saying the word himself.

### The Box of Chaos

Trying to shake things up, the Lord of Fate summoned [[The Box of Chaos]]. It landed like a shooting star: a pink and yellow chest bursting with flames, a small creepy black hand creeping out of the lid. For Teddy it produced a blue slushie that melted away his exhaustion and cost $14.97. "How the box got a slushie machine, we'll never know."

Ragu's bargaining took longer. The shadowy figure had gone through Ragu's wallet and was still admiring a photo of Teddy's wife it had found there. Ragu offered the chinchilla hairs from his areola. The figure demanded documentation and called in Thea as "the resident chinchilla hair expert." She examined them and confirmed "a clear, true Chilean chinchilla," worth maybe three chinchillas at auction. Ragu received the golden [[Shield of the Golden King]]. He immediately tried to hit Teddy in the face with it, which, Thea gently noted, isn't what shields are for. She coached him to empower himself by chanting his own name. Teddy was unmoved: "At some point, you'll get around to it, I'm sure."

### Surges and Insults

With the fight tied after a break, Teddy found new strength by opening "a little book" and learning to do more each turn. He kicked "Mr. Marinara" in the chest, then cannonballed him in the face, explaining that zombie fighting had taught him to be specific. Ragu surged too and unleashed "the most annoying sound in the world" from his melodic voice. Teddy simply laughed over it. Every good roll Ragu made, Teddy matched, and Ragu's attempt to stomp Teddy's big toe went nowhere. Thea gave him a pep talk about his peerless locks and his one remaining good areola.

The third chaos event was a horde of children in the stands hurling painfully accurate insults: "you dress like me ma, you don't have enough nipples." Both champions shrugged off the emotional damage, though Thea added a few of her own. Then the Lord of Fate realised they had run out of chaos events entirely. Thea warned that if they didn't kill each other soon, she would kill them both.

### The Shield Shatters

Round seven brought Teddy's powered-up "nipple slicer," aimed at the other one. Ragu fumbled his defence completely, and the Shield of the Golden King shattered into "a tiny million pieces." Teddy reloaded his shotgun with exaggerated slowness, flicked away a last scrap of nipple and blasted Ragu in the face. "I am invincible." Asked about the afterlife, Teddy offered Ragu a final word: "Rosebud." Ragu wasn't having it. He blocked Teddy's next attack, a punt to the crotch Teddy called the "Soprano" ("I'm not dead yet."), and swung again and again. "My fate is near. The only thing that's colorful is my boots. So let's go out with a style."

In round eight Teddy began to feel bad for him. He mouthed "I'm so sorry" and brought his gun butt down on Ragu's bowed head, but fumbled and hurt himself instead. With both men so exhausted that a tie would kill them both, Teddy ran to the Impala, got in and ran Ragu over with his own car. Ragu made a last stand and landed two hits while Teddy, by his own account, just stood there with his eyes closed.

### The Roundhouse

The Lord of Fate noted that no fight had gone this far all season. In the ninth round Teddy jumped, faked a kick and landed a real roundhouse to the face, then spun straight into a second. Time slowed. **The kick struck Ragu in the jaw and cracked his gorgeous manscaped face clean off its hinges**, while blue slushies arced through the air in slow motion. Ragu's voice echoed from beyond: "It's officially still a bitch though. You suck."

His body floated off into orbit and froze, "just like the chinchilla that he failed to save earlier," his tongue stuck out as he drifted into space. "Yeah, I died to a fucking roadhouse kick. Tell my wife, Prego, I tried."

Teddy was crowned victor at kill number 30,001, and he won the Impala too. Thea asked whether she had earned her job. The Lord of Fate admitted they were too far into the season to replace her, "so it's more of a logistics thing," and handed back her clipboard. Thea celebrated by grabbing Teddy and swinging him around. The Lord of Fate spread his white wings and announced that the season would close next week with the first D20 Deathmatch Royal Rumble: "Four champions will enter the arena, but only one will leave."

## Dried Blood

Afterwards Ragu admitted he knew he was finished the moment his second areola went. He worried about two green areolas, and the right one still carried chinchilla hair, making him, by Thea's estimate, about 0.3% chinchilla. Teddy credited his win to reading The Secret, since last time he had lost "to an old lady." He planned to pimp the Impala with funky paint, big TVs, fuzzy dice and the dried nipples. Thea warned that the Michigan plates wouldn't work until he registered it and paid the taxes. The Lord of Fate asked why he thought they were giving it away. "It was cheaper than paying to impound it," Thea added. Teddy's lesson for the Acolytes was to skip school and learn a trade like zombie fighting. Ragu's was simpler: "Protect your areolas, man." He floated the idea of a quest to recover them, the "Diary of an areola-is-less."
