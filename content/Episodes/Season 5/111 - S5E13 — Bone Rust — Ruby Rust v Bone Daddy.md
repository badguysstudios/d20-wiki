Two champions came back from the dead for a rematch with fate in **Bone Rust**. One was [[Ruby Rust]], a programmer-turned-python who had been swallowed by [[The Hunger|the Hunger]] and returned as a glitching eldritch nightmare. The other was [[Bone Daddy]], a skeletal showman in hammer pants with his beloved skeleton [[Darcy]] at his side. Their tavern brawl had fire, bone cages, sparkly flatulence and a great deal of body horror, and it ended with the strangest prize in Deathmatch history: a snake given exactly what he wanted.

## The Story

### A Sticky Situation in the Kitchen
The night began with [[The Arbiter|the Lord of Fate]] wandering the halls of [[The Dread Fortress|the Dread Fortress]] and noticing that things smelled "a little bit more fragrant" than usual. [[Thea]] shouted "Nothing! Nothing!" and slammed the kitchen door. Then came the sound of hammering, because she was nailing planks to the door itself rather than to the wall beside it. The Lord of Fate didn't bother with the door. He simply appeared behind her ("Ah, that's cheating!").

He found goblins stacked on each other's shoulders, pouring cinnamon into two boiling vats of sun cola. Thea explained they were "getting into the holiday spirit," and that heating it up "releases the flavors," which she had read in a cookbook once. The cola had cooked down into a thick orange sludge. He switched off the burner and announced her punishment: "We're gonna sit here until you eat it all." Thea did not treat this as a punishment. She shoveled the syrup in with both claws while the goblins spoon-fed her, and her teeth dissolved on the spot.

"I didn't need teeth," Thea said, "and this leaves more room in my mouth for more food." He snapped her teeth back. She asked for teeth like a shark, and he answered "You want extra? Fucking fine." He gave her a second row, but they were human teeth. The imp who had wanted to be "a little piranha" now had two full sets of human teeth and found she "can't stop smiling."

### Back from the Dead (Mostly)
Thea clapped, two portals opened, and the champions tumbled through. Ruby Rust was not pleased: "Why must you keep torturing me? I was devoured whole by some sort of hunger, and now I'm still here." Being eaten by the Hunger had left him unstable. Parts of him glitched and stretched into an abomination of a snake, and his forked tongue whipped out like a shadowy tentacle to lap up the syrup Thea offered. Bone Daddy was just as confused. The last thing he remembered was being swallowed by "an ever growing wave of bones," and after fighting a man and then a half-animal hybrid, he was now being asked to fight a snake.

Thea told them she'd brought them back because "your story isn't finished being told yet." The Lord of Fate gave the honest reason: "mostly because we're bored, and it's hard to find champions this time of year." Thea admitted her revival magic "isn't as good," and that she'd forgotten to heal them first. It came out later that she had handed the job to [[Thomas the Goblin]], who is "working on his certification." The Lord of Fate pointed out that Thomas can't read or write and eats paint chips. Thea's defense was "Well, he's seen straight lines before."

Ruby told his story while his head turned itself inside out. He had once been a snake, then a programmer in software development ("Yes, a Python working programming"), then somehow sentient. "Please let me die," he said. Ruby shed his skin, and there was no skin underneath. The old skin pooled into black ichor that crawled toward Bone Daddy, who cried "Darcy, pick me up!" His skeleton companion scooped him up and carried him clear. Feeling guilty, Thea flew over and put a band-aid on Ruby. Wherever she touched him, real ruby-red scales grew back. Ruby then coughed up his missing helmet, which still held its two drinks: poison on one side, java on the other.

### Last Orders at Emmery's Tavern
The lair slid away "almost as if a boring PowerPoint slideshow has just moved to the left," and the champions found themselves in Emmery's Tavern. It was a dark, gross bar with a monstrous skull heaving over the fireplace, barrels of number two ale ("Don't ask what it takes to make it"), and an old bartender wiping the same spot over and over. The ceiling turned clear to show [[The Acolytes|the Acolytes]] cheering above. "We hate this place anyway," Thea said, "so you can totally destroy it."

Ruby blinked, and his eyelids fused shut while eyes bulged out where his scales had been. "Please kill me, quickly," he begged. Then the Lord of Fate offered a deal: "Ruby, just to make this interesting, if you win, we'll put you back to normal." Ruby said "Oh, thank God" and bared his teeth.

### Bones, Whips and Ground Snake
Bone Daddy sent Darcy to curl Ruby into the fire, but her hands sank into him like a tar pit and he oozed out between her bones. "I've picked up some new tricks since being assimilated into the hunger," Ruby said. Bone Daddy answered with his "Bone Daddy Arcanum," pulling fire from the hearth and breathing it out like a flaming skull. Then he did something truly horrible to the crowd. He ripped the bones straight out of several Acolytes, leaving "bags of meat" in the stands, piled them onto Ruby, and fused them into a shifting bone cage. Ruby had joked at the start that "three is a magic number," and he spent the opening watching nearly every attack find him.

Ruby swirled java and poison in his mouth and spat it on the cage, which dissolved. He split off a decoy blob of black goo, slipped between the table legs and grabbed Bone Daddy by the ankles, then wrapped around him. His venomous bite missed when Darcy hurled a femur into his mouth. Bone Daddy's comment was "Can't touch this." It also turned out that Bone Daddy was wearing hammer pants, or rather "the shortest shorts possible." The Lord of Fate observed, "There's two pythons in the tavern tonight."

Thea headbutted her die, rolled a perfect 20, and cracked out her human teeth so her sharp imp ones grew back. They kept growing, so she ripped them out and pelted the Acolytes with them: "Give those to your tooth fairy!" Her lucky roll set off a chaos event. Ruby's acidic thrashing burned a hole in the neck of a patron sleeping on a table, and he died without ever waking ("We revived him plenty of times, it's okay"). Then the tavern's morning coffee kicked in. Ruby could not hold it, and there was "a lot of poop coming out of that cloaca." Bone Daddy, who "knows how to hold his bowels through a lot of practice," credited his "buns of steel."

Those buns sent Bone Daddy flying to the ceiling, where Darcy caught him like a trapeze artist. As he flipped over Ruby he lashed him with his bullwhip, and on a disastrous roll for the snake, Ruby's skin "just bursts open like a hot sausage." Thea's reaction: "I'm horrified! Do it again!" Darcy dove in and shredded Ruby's back half. Bone Daddy rolled the pieces into "a Ruby Rust meatball" and tried to toss it into the fire, but it missed. The ground snake curled away from the heat and sprouted pseudopods, and then a human hand pushed out of the front of him. "Why did you do this to me?"

Thea, still guilty, pressed a handful of band-aids into the new hand. "It's collusion," the Lord of Fate admitted, but "we never said this was gonna be a fair fight." Bone Daddy glitched and clipped into the same space as Ruby to attack him from inside ("We, we are one"). That was a mistake. A human arm, a bat-wing arm and a tiny porcelain doll's arm reached out of Ruby to hold him there. The doll's arm stuck a band-aid on Ruby's cheek. Ruby's head split down the back, turned inside out to bite, and then he crushed Bone Daddy hard enough to break his glasses. Thea took one lens as a monocle, grabbed an Acolyte bone and posed: "I'm Bone Daddy… Yes, Darcy, you are my best friend."

### Tuna Juice and Treachery
After a break, Thea rolled out a cart of pickle juice and tuna juice shots. She argued tuna juice is the eco-friendly choice when camping and is "kind of like having dragon's breath." Ruby offered his own poison, and Bone Daddy produced a flask of bone marrow. Thea drank all six shots herself. "You want to know what you smell like?" Bone Daddy asked her. "A traitor. I haven't forgotten about that band-aid that you gave Ruby Rust." He promised "Ruby Rust may be first, but you're next," and Thea pouted at the wall.

Thea told the tavern she had been "specially bred from my family to be an invincible worm" before becoming an imp. Her family had wanted her to get a college degree, "so right now I'm a partial disappointment." The Lord of Fate said she could technically die, but then he'd "have to go back to doing all the paperwork and the taxes." He apologized to the Acolytes for getting distracted, bought everyone blue slushies, and gave them the chaos they were chanting for.

The second chaos event came from Thea. She walked between the champions, bent her knees and released "the longest, stinkiest, tuna pickle fart you've ever had." Both champions were knocked back by it and coated in pink glitter from her "special pocket." Skinless Ruby complained that "the glitter is directly between my muscles and the tendons." Thea then shared that Thomas the Goblin keeps eggs in his "butt pocket" and they're cooked by lunchtime.

### Trading With the Box
The Lord of Fate raised his hand, and [[The Box of Chaos]] slammed down with a column of pink and yellow fire. Shadowy hands and yellow eyes crept out of it. Bone Daddy broke free with his "patented bone swirl" and ordered a pick-me-up at the bar. The bartender poured him a stein of number two ale and a "pickle juice" shot that was really the wrung-out bar towel. Bone Daddy hurled the stein at Ruby, and both of them came out of it worse for wear.

At the Box, the shadowy figure asked whether this was a fight to the death and grumbled about the Lord of Fate having his own day: "God, that guy's kind of a cock." Bone Daddy opened his coat to reveal it was lined with unsold hammer shorts. The figure took ten ("they're kind of idiots. They'll buy anything"). His first prize was a black vial, and when Bone Daddy opened it, out came "the shadow of [[The Shadow King]]," which settled heavily on him. When he protested, the figure admitted "I just don't have as much shit as I used to" and handed over the [[Shield of the Golden King]].

### Darcy's Last Stand
"Darcy, to me." The bones of the old cage locked onto Bone Daddy until Darcy covered him completely. He slammed the fireplace skull on top as a helmet: "This is my truest self." His signature slam, "Daddy's Girl," should have ended things, but Ruby dodged it cleanly. While Bone Daddy had been chatting at the bar and the Box, Ruby had spread his tail across the floor "like a paintbrush being pushed down onto a piece of paper." He had threaded tendrils into the patrons and taken over their senses, so he could watch the attack from every angle and slide aside in slow motion.

Ruby then went to the Box himself. He claimed the shield had been his before the Hunger took him ("You gave it to me even though I had no arms"), and he paid with "the last years of this one's life." The shadow drained an old patron to dust ("Oh, yeah, that's the good stuff"), and Ruby received a small box that screamed when opened. He reared like a king cobra and lashed out with dozens of tiny fanged mouths on tentacles, but Bone Daddy's giant golden-shielded skeleton mech, which looked "anime as hell," held firm. The Box, used up, shot off into the sky.

Bone Daddy hurled a barrel of ale at him ("very Donkey Kong-ish"). Ruby crunched open his screaming box, and the barrel missed. Bone Daddy then opened the Darcy armor's ribcage to engulf his rival from its heart, and that missed too. "Darcy, I've never been disappointed in you until this very day," he said. Ruby swelled to Bone Daddy's size, stretched thin like putty and wrapped him in "the big squeeze." Bone Daddy's defense collapsed completely, and **the Shield of the Golden King shattered**.

Cocooned and fading, Bone Daddy wondered aloud whether it was time "to hang up the hammer shorts." He offered to give himself to the Hunger if Ruby would spare Darcy, "she did nothing wrong." Ruby refused: "She is nothing more than a table to me." That was too far. "You listen to me, Rust. No one talks to Darcy like that… She will outlast us all, even the Lord of Fate. If I'm gonna die today, you're gonna come with me." Bone Daddy threw them both into the fireplace and set Ruby alight.

For one brief moment Ruby became himself again: his skin stitched shut and his eyes and jaw settled back where they belonged. He unhinged his mouth and **swallowed Bone Daddy whole**. Burning, he crawled out of the fire toward Thea. "Your problem was bringing me back without considering how I should be." He swelled to the size of the bar and absorbed furniture and patrons through his skin. Then he tensed his "wee snake abs," and on the worst possible roll, **Bone Daddy was ground into bone dust**. Before the end, he punched one hand out of Ruby and let go of a single finger bone of Darcy's. "I hope that finger bone can find her way home." Nobody noticed it creep away.

### Freedom
Ruby kept growing and turned on his hosts: "Where are your protections now?" Thea said "Hold on," conjured five [[Chinchillas|chinchillas]], took the one that volunteered and yeeted it into Ruby's mouth. As it went down, his body deflated, his insides sorted themselves out, and he shrank into "an adorable, non-sentient Ruby Python." "Aw, you fixed it too much," the Lord of Fate sighed. "Now it can't code." Thea kicked open the tavern door ("You deserve to be free! I release you!"), and Ruby slithered out into [[The Void|the Void]]. His last thoughts faded one at a time:

> "Peace. At last. No more deadlines. No more projects. No more rent. No more taxes. Just hunting for mice."

"See," Thea told the Acolytes, "this is what the Deathmatch provides."
