## What happens
### Opening — rock, paper, scissors in the throne room
1. **0:00:02** In **the subterranean castle of the Grandmaster**, Elusia sits on her throne **playing rock, paper, scissors with her goblins**, and is not doing well. Bec takes over the story. **Elusia glares at the goblins, and they change their hands so that they lose to her.** "I win every time. No one ever beats me at this. I'm the best there is in this entire fortress."
2. The goblins file past one after another. **One goblin hangs his head and goes to the back of the line behind the other seven**, so she can beat each of them in turn. She explains that there is strategy to it: "You have to go fast and you have to out-think them… You have to know who your opponent is."
3. **0:01:05 — The Arbiter is challenged.** He says he is "merely an observer to your glorious rock-paper-scissors technique." She calls him up to a spot at her feet anyway. He asks whether it's "one, two, three, and then shoot." She says no: "Why would you have the extra time?"
4. **0:01:49** They play several rounds. The first is a tie ("a nice warm-up"). Then she accuses him of being slower and of cheating, and smacks his hand away. She says he was "a whole two and a half seconds slower."
   - **0:02:29** The Arbiter: "What you're experiencing is video delay." She doesn't accept it.
   - He offers to **close his eyes**. She has him lead "since you're blind." He then declares: "You win, Grandmaster."
   - "Is my fortress what I say goes?" "Yes, I 100% agree with that."
5. **0:03:18 — Why he came.** "It is time for some people to die." She thought it was laundry day. **Laundry day is tomorrow**: the deathmatch comes first, "just in case there's any blood on the clothing."
   - GM: "**I'm glad I didn't hire you, but I brought you here**, so that works as well."
6. **0:03:50** She does something to a goblin's leg (the transcript says "shoot"; possibly "shoo" or "smack"). She tells it: "**You always choose rock.** It's fine… It'll help me win later."
   - She warns the Arbiter: "If you tell me again it's from like **the 7-Eleven or TGI Fridays**, I swear I'm going to be upset."
7. **0:04:18 — The champions under sheets.** "I ripped the finest warriors from all space and time." This time **he doesn't use a portal**. Two figures stand covered in decorative sheets like statues, and **he rips the sheets off dramatically**: "Grandmaster, I give you Viago and Tetra."
8. **0:05:08 — Viago presents himself.** "Viago here. I hear there's some blood that needs to be shed. And I'm ready to do it in your honor."
   - Asked how he wins: "First off, I do it with a lot of rage." He throws up a rock sign: "**This is too much rock and roll for one hand.**"
   - GM: "Part of it is just pure intimidation alone… I like this champion."
   - **0:06:12 Where he was found:** "the wilderness." Pressed, the Arbiter insists it is "a place called the wilderness… a very small location." GM: "No, that's not lazy at all."
9. **0:06:57** Viago heckles while Tetra hesitates: "He's frightened of me. He can't even speak."
10. **0:07:03 — Tetra presents herself.** "**My name is Tetra Kincaid**, and I don't need to do all the yelling and all of the bones. Disgusting. You're very gross. **I like to just keep it simple and murder.**"
    - On her training: "I was trained as far as many people could train me, and then I did figure out more myself."
    - Asked about her kill count ("under your belt? Or tail?"): "It is more than him, I'm sure."
    - "**Anything for someone so beautiful as you, my dear.**"
    - GM: "This is the one I'm cheering for. **The flattery will get you everywhere**… especially here in this court."
11. **0:08:24 — Where Tetra was found.** The Arbiter: "the most foul, gut-wrenching, blood-infested place you could imagine." The GM guesses "**McDonald's?**" It was "**The parking lot outside of Disney.**" GM: "If anyone can survive there, they'll certainly do well in this arena."
12. **0:08:41 — Rock, paper, scissors with the champions.** Each champion must play her once before entering.
    - **Viago** loses or lets her win: "Viago's going to do very well, and I'm glad you know your place."
    - **Tetra** throws the same hand she does: "There! Excellent!"
13. **0:10:27** The Arbiter claps and rips open a portal of **green and purple hues**.

### The arena — the Siren's Nautilus
14. **0:10:45** GM: "Welcome, welcome to **the Siren's Nautilus**."
    - They are in **the ballroom of a sunken ship**. She has made an **air bubble** for them. If they go outside or pierce the walls, "you might find yourself swimming with the fishes," and there's no swimming to the surface.
    - The overgrown ship **begins to move and breathe. "This ship is a living being that you are inside of… my precious beast. I use it to scour the seas and claim whatever happens to fall to its death."**
    - The ground is electrified **like a jellyfish**, dangerous skin to skin.
    - The floor is **luminescent purple tile with pearls along the edges**, with puddles of water.
    - There are exits at either end, "but you do not know where they will take you."
    - "**Be careful. This ship may bite back.**"
15. **0:12:31 — The crowd.** She flies up through the ceiling, which ripples like water. Above, "**almost like a SeaWorld tank**," the crowd circles and looks down "as if through a glass boat bottom": "These are your acolytes… this show is for them. **Win their hearts and you might make it out alive.**"
16. **0:13:38 — Placement.** The d4 rolls put **both champions in the same spot, nose to nose**.
    - Viago: "She won't look for four sides, boy." Tetra: "Yes, you idiot." GM: "I know we struggled to count to two." Counting jokes at Viago's expense run through the whole episode.
    - GM: "Start off face to face, lips to lips." Arbiter: "Eye to eye in a slow dance of death… we want a dirty fight."
    - **The crowd gives Viago a hero point.** Tetra: "I knew he would need a leg up."
17. **0:15:58** Initiative: Viago 17 (with advantage), Tetra 13.

### Round 1 — cat versus dog
18. **0:16:19 — Viago transforms.** He **rages and turns into a dire wolf**.
    - The GM asks for "that iconic dire wolf howl." The sound cut out, "so we got these cute little pup pal clips instead."
    - **He is resistant to everything except psychic damage**: "I'm a totem barbarian." That carries into wolf form.
    - GM: "**Cat versus dog.**" Viago: "That was not intentional."
19. **0:17:58 — Tetra's astral arms.** "**I love killing these stupid canines.**" She spends ki on **Arms of the Astral Self**: big glowing astral arms around her own feline arms, dealing force damage with extra reach. They hit Viago as they appear (he fails the Dex save).
    - Then: "You need to see what the real claws look like." **Two claw strikes: a hit, then a nat 20.** "Yeah, bitch." "Please tell me how you'll deal with this, you stupid dog."
    - **The wolf drops to 16 HP.** She looks at her bloody claws and **picks the little hairs out**.
20. **0:22:05** **The fans give Tetra a hero point too.**
    - GM: "Claws are out right from the get-go… Changing to dire wolf, bringing out massive kitty arms."
    - The GM asks for her end-of-round "secret" d20 (a 7). Arbiter: "Is it really a secret?" Viago: "I don't know what you're doing." GM: "He can't count higher than three probably."

#### Chaos event — the metal chairs (0:32:56)
28. The Arbiter: "**Our acolytes have demanded chaos.**" The GM lets him "redeem that sad five." He rolls an **18**.
    - Laughing, she goes behind her throne. There is shuffling, then a clinging and harshing of metal dragged across the floor. **She brings out two metal chairs, one for the Arbiter and one for herself.**
    - The rules: each champion calls a coin flip. **The Arbiter's chair is for Viago, and the Grandmaster's is for Tetra, "my prize champion."** Call it right and you don't get hit.
    - They use a 1d2: "we haven't gotten the Arbiter to get us cooler coins yet." The Arbiter: "I'm working on it, Grandmaster." GM: "Viago can only count from one to two."
30. **0:35:56** Tetra: "**Tails never fail**, so I'll go with a two." **She's right.** The GM **throws her chair to the ground** in frustration.
31. **0:36:06 — The dive.** "Grandmaster, have mine." **The Arbiter hands over his chair.**
    - **She holds it over her head and asks the Acolytes: "Do we give him the chair?"** She gets them hyped: "**Give him the chair! This is for you!**"
    - She flies above center stage and circles until she makes **a small tornado**. Then, "**like a falcon skydiving**," she brings the chair down on Viago's head.
    - The damage is **"metal chair damage," which nothing resists**: "It is a unique brand." Viago: "It's a celestial metal chair. I am still a wolf, technically."
    - **The rusty chair gives way, and Viago's head goes through the seat. He now wears it as a necklace.** "**Think of this as a collar. If you can break free, perhaps you're as wild as you deserve to be.**" "That chaos is for you."
    - Tetra: "Very well done. And Inquisitor, good manners." (She calls the Arbiter "Inquisitor" several times; see below.)
32. **0:38:09 — The neck twist.** Tetra **grabs the chair around the wolf's neck and twists its head.** It hits (26).
    - **0:38:39** Drew narrates (tagged 02): **"You watch as you snap that neck, it growls in pain, and in your hands you watch as the seven-foot-tall orc, Viago…"** The wild shape ends, and Viago is back in his own body.
    - Tetra: "Oof, I may have made a terrible misstep… **Somehow you've gotten uglier from a dog.**"
    - Arbiter: "He's not smart enough to have feelings."
33. **0:39:43** Tetra: "Let's try to make you a little more prettier with that face." Her claw misses.
34. **0:39:58 — The Sentinel.** Tetra spends ki on **Step of the Wind** to disengage and run 60 ft.
    - **Viago has Sentinel, so she still provokes.** "**That's not just a nice axe, that's a great axe.**" The greataxe hits for **16, and her speed drops to 0.**
    - Tetra: "Oh, well, **you are quite an asshole.** Fine, I will stay here, but I'm not happy about it."
    - GM: "I feel like **we just got a glimpse into the true Tetra.**" Tetra: "I don't even want to take it no more."
35. **0:42:06** The Arbiter rolls a 13. GM: "**It's one of my favorite numbers**, so you are a bit in my good graces."

#### Chaos event — the bouncy castle (1:23:17)

### Round 8 — the pit and the gelatinous cube

67. **1:39:30 — Into the pool.** Viago stands her up, steps back, and **suplexes her over his shoulder into the green pool**, trying not to go in himself: "Either she's gonna die or she's gonna turn into some big bad and I'm gonna die."
    - **She deflects all the acid damage with Body of the Astral Self.**
    - But she is now **knee-deep in a gelatinous cube**, which starts to engulf her. GM: "**The gelatinous cube!**"
    - Arbiter: "Usually found in dungeons, it is a slow-moving object dealing acid damage and engulfing its prey, devouring it slowly."
    - **Tetra's Dex save is a natural 20.** She pops out and kicks the cube, then climbs onto the edge. **The cube follows her out of the pool after "what it thinks is its meal."**
68. **1:42:27 — One life out of nine.** Tetra takes stock: "**Internally, that falling moment, very weird, deal with that later.** Externally, bleeding very much on all of the nice fur that I've been growing for decades… **What is one life out of nine, right?**"
    - She sprints around to **tackle Viago into the cube**. GM: "**Jello fight! In the ring!**" Tetra: "Everyone wants to see it. **He is shirtless. I am clearly a 10.**"
    - She spends ki to try again, but **Viago's chest doesn't move: "it's a wall."**
    - Tetra: "**This has just gotten more awkward as I power arm your pecs.**" Viago: "**You understand why I wear the black pants now, right?**" "No, I don't. I don't like any of this." She compares it to "the scene from Captain America where… she just lightly touches him."
69. **1:45:09** Viago's **axe is "still in me back,"** so he punches her twice, recklessly: once in the face, then he bends her over and hits again.
    - Tetra: "I am very close to death… **me touching your chesticles, your chest**."
70. **1:46:28 — The second healing word.** **Viago drops his rage again and heals Tetra two points**, and **slaps her on the backside: "You like it like that?"** This line is tagged 00 but is Viago's.
    - Tetra: "That is so very, very rude and condescending of you… But you've given me the strength and anger to get up and slash your face for a final time." Viago: "Sorry, this is terrible. I feel terrible."
    - He steps away from "the gooey bits" as the Arbiter brings the cube out of the pit: "**This is a hungry cube.**"
71. **1:47:49** The stomach acid returns. Tetra fails her Con save and **deflects it with her astral self**.

### Final round
72. **1:49:18** Tetra: "I am feeling like an asshole at this point."
    - **Viago honestly tells her she has advantage** because he attacked recklessly. "Thank you for your honesty."
    - A tie gives a glancing staff blow. "I'm very exhausted… why even continue with this?"
    - GM: "**Keep fighting. Keep your spirit. Demand respect. Even if you die, Tetra, keep your dignity.**"
    - **The Arbiter's wager:** "If you're going to die, maybe wait till the next round, because **I've placed some late fight wagers.** I need you to go to the 13th."
    - A 28 hits for 12. **Her last Stunning Strike** is met with another 20-plus Con save. Tetra: "**Cannot be stunned, Viago, the Stunless.**"
73. **1:51:41 — The kick.** Viago shakes his head and **looks up at the Grandmaster, the Arbiter and the cube**. Then he **spins around and kicks her hard** for six bludgeoning.
    - Tetra: "**Well, it was fun, but that is me and I go down.**"
    - GM: "After all these moves, epic in nature… Foot to back, kicks for Tetra where she's vulnerable and she goes down. **A mere simple kick is all it took.**"

### Aftermath
74. **1:52:15 — The frozen cube.** The Grandmaster floats down and **lands on the gelatinous cube, which freezes into an ice block** under her feet. It becomes a stand. "**Come, join me center stage.**" She and Viago rise toward the fans, leaving the Arbiter and Tetra on the ground.
75. **1:53:34 — Tetra's end.** The Arbiter kneels by Tetra, "dying from blunt force trauma to the chest," and **unfreezes the cube**:
    - "**Normally, I would reserve a specimen such as you for my army. But in this case, my pet is hungry.**"
    - **The cube engulfs her body and starts devouring her bones.** Her fur dissolves: "The beautiful fur." Perhaps she gives "**a Terminator 2 thumbs up**" as she goes under.
    - **The Arbiter poofs her, the cube and himself out of the arena.**
76. **1:54:43 — The victor.** GM: "Dear Acolytes, you had asked for a fight of epic proportion… **But only one survives.** One truly poured their soul out on this field, showed their true grit, and brought creativity to our fight."
    - "**Viago!**" The crowd throws up **rock fists** and screams his name, "just as reckless as every single attack… no rhythm."
    - "**Viago, you have truly brought greatness to my field.** You have brought blood and violence… You will join me as we retreat."
    - "Welcome and good night to D20 Deathmatch!"
