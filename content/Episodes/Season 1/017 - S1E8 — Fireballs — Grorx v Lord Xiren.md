## What happens
### Opening: the Dread Fortress
1. **0:00:16** The Arbiter opens: "It is I, the Arbiter, of all things. I mean this specifically, but also other things that you don't know about. **Shut up, nerd.**"
   - We are "in the Dread Fortress, in the halls of Elusia Skypiercer". **The Grandmaster is napping on her throne.**
   - The Arbiter taps his **cane** to wake her.
2. **0:01:03 — The afternoon nap.** Elusia insists an afternoon nap is a requirement for everyone.
   - "It is at least a bare minimum that everyone gets at least six hours of sleep, and I can't get that at night."
   - It works best with **Doug the Pug** beside her.
   - The Arbiter says he has no time for naps, even with time magic ("time waits for no one"), because he is busy procuring subjects for her deathmatches. She calls it "Excuses" and says she is "not as pleasant" without her sleep: "beauty sleep".
3. **0:02:08 — The gift.** The Arbiter claps his hands. Two goblins struggle in with a painting about ten times their height: **a giant velour painting of Doug the Pug**.
   - It is an optical illusion. Depending on how you shift, Doug "either has bones or doesn't have bones."
   - Elusia: "So we get to decide if it's a bones day or no bones day." Arbiter: "**Grandmaster, the bones now are at your discretion.**" She worries that this is responsibility: "It was a lot easier when Doug the pug just decided for us."
4. **0:03:08** Elusia shakes Doug, who also napped with her. He rolls onto his back, little giant tummy out, tongue sticking out, wheezing. He needs a few more hours.
   - The table debates whether you can paint on velour ("magic somehow"). Elusia says she will **commission the Arbiter to create something like that**.
   - Her ruling: "**Doug the Pug does not have bones today, but perhaps our champions will give me bones instead.**"
5. **0:04:39** The Arbiter promises she will be pleased "even on a no bones day". He rips open a portal, and **Grorx** and **Lord Xiren** walk out.

### The champions arrive
6. **0:05:09 — Grorx's payment.** Grorx: "Where am I?" The Arbiter reminds him: "We talked about this. The Deathmatch."
   - Grorx: "I'm to be paid for this."
   - Arbiter: "Should you survive, you will be rewarded handsomely." Grorx: "And if I die?" Arbiter: "I don't see how paying your corpse would make any difference to you." Grorx: "Deal."
7. **0:05:41 — Xiren didn't know.** Lord Xiren had been told he would "put on a show for the Grandmaster". "**Wait. I could die putting on a show for the Grandmaster?**" Arbiter: "What part of Deathmatch do you not understand?" Xiren: "So be it!"
   - Grorx: "New to deathmatches, I see… **Grox the Great will guide you through the whole time. The easy part, you're just gonna die.**"
8. **0:06:30 — Grorx's presentation.** Told to bow before the Grandmaster, Grorx first looks at the goblin carrying the painting ("is it this one?"). Then: "Throne, crown. **How much for the crown?**"
   - Elusia: "My crown is not for sale. This is something that I have earned. You little peasant here in my dread fortress."
   - He introduces himself as "**Grox the Great. Greatest of Goblin Kind, Troll Kind, Orc Kind, and Elven Kind. Not Dragon Kind though, I don't fuck with that.**" The Arbiter: "That's a very specific line."
9. **0:07:57 — Xiren can't find her.** Xiren stares at the pug and a "dark figure", unable to spot the Grandmaster. The Arbiter asks her to make herself known.
   - **She snaps her fingers and a spotlight hits her throne.** It reflects blindingly off the **metal surface of the chair**.
   - Xiren shields his eyes: "**There you are, Grandmaster! … I am Lord Xiren! Your Aasimar, your protector, your champion! My sole purpose tonight, to please the Grandmaster! Whether in victory or defeat, in life or death**…"
   - Grorx: "It's definitely going to be death. Just predicting it now."
10. **0:09:22** The Arbiter is "beyond confused" and offers to take Xiren back. Elusia: "Arbiter, shut it… **My protector. My fighter on the field.** Not that other one who's trying to steal my crown and doesn't even know who I am."
11. **0:09:48 — The crown negotiation.**
    - Grorx offered "a legitimate sale. Proof of ownership."
    - He doesn't want her title, though. "The great is a good title for me." Elusia scolds that "great is just okay of a title… awesome, fantastic, fantastical, wonderful, amazing, phenomenal."
    - Grorx: "**See, you got to keep the standards low. That way you can always rise above the standards.**" If he doesn't, "Then I run away… **I'm the greatest at running away.**"
    - Elusia: "Make sure there is no map where he can greatly run away." Arbiter: "There is no escaping your arena." She adds that there are "plenty of traps".
    - Curious, she asks what the crown is worth. Now that "the bar is so high", Grorx no longer wants it: "It's out of Grorx's price range." He looks "for things that I can resell."
12. **0:11:32 — Credit.** Grorx: "I was gonna use credit." "**You don't take Omnicard?**" Xiren chips in: "**You don't take Orcish Express?**"
    - Arbiter: "The fees are outrageous." Elusia: "**We do not take credit.**"
13. **0:12:24 — The contract.** Grorx: "**I used my contract as a napkin and it got stained.**" Arbiter: "It's still binding."
    - Xiren rereads his contract: "all it says in here is please the Grandmaster." Elusia: "At least we know he's literate."
    - The Arbiter points out the smaller print, "**not responsible for maiming or dismemberment or death**", and hands Xiren **a giant magnifying glass**.
    - Xiren: "That was not communicated to me, but I will proceed anyway because I'm here to please the Grandmaster."

### Travel to the arena
14. **0:13:16** Elusia asks for "a particular map of grandness, of perhaps mystery hidden away in time." The Arbiter widens the portal, and a **wave of hot air and sand** blows through.
15. **0:13:51 — The train.** "We're not traveling by train." The train isn't ready and none of the carts are. The Arbiter: "We'll have to get the goblins on that. Should have been done months ago. You know, public works."
    - Grorx: "They are the worst laborers. You should not hire them." The low-bar thing "is taught to us from a very young age."
16. **0:14:28 — The crowd.** Elusia: "The bar here is set high." She points to the sky. **The hordes of Acolytes are vicious and screaming, and they all wear mock versions of her crown bought in the gift store**, paid in cash "because we don't have credit here." Their fervor has already summoned a chaos event.

    - Plenty of sand, and a small staircase to a second story 20 ft up.
    - It is cool, with a sliver of light from above. "You are clearly underground somewhere." Every sound echoes.
    - "**You are not necessarily stuck here, but it is a good place to begin.**"
    - Her wings pop open, bright and **deep blue** across her feathers. As she lifts off, a cloud rises: **red sand mixed with bone dust**. "This place is ancient."
    - She takes "her grand seat high above in her **secondary throne**."
18. **0:17:22** "Arbiter, get this map started." Arbiter: "**In this economy?**"
    - He wants "the dirtiest, messiest fight that you can muster in this hall of death." He announces that **the Acolytes have demanded two chaos events**.
    - With a wink he repeats the hint that they "may not be trapped in this room", then vanishes in smoke.
19. **0:18:17** Grorx to Xiren: "You're a lot bigger up close." Xiren(?): "You're green." "I mean, that's racist, but that's fine."
20. **0:18:38 — Milk and ice cream.**
    - Xiren admits his eyes are "only partially working."
    - Elusia promises that if he survives he can read the rest of the contract with **a cookie**. He asks for **a cup of milk**: "Fine… But it will be cold, not warm. I don't know who those weirdos are that like to drink warm milk."
    - Grorx: "my stomach hurts talking about milk." **He is lactose intolerant** ("Oh, man, you have no idea"). Arbiter: "You shouldn't have told us that."
    - Elusia: "**The winner only gets ice cream at the end. That's also at the end of the contract.**"
21. **0:19:38 — Placement.** Grorx: "Two managing expectations here… No, the expectations are too high. Gotta go." Both start in the same quadrant. **Xiren has the high ground on the ledge.**
22. **0:21:12 — Wings.** Grorx: "**Am I the only one without wings? Is that something I miss in the contract?**… Where's my manager?" Arbiter: "You were able to bring your own wings." Grorx: "I gotta fire that guy."
    - Xiren: "Never trust a kobold community [?]… It said bring wings."

### Chaos event 1: Doug's barking (0:21:39)
23. The champions roll a 5 and a 6 ("So you're a twin"). **Doug the Pug pops up as if electrocuted** and barks his head off on his tiny struggling paws.
    - **Dog outlines and hieroglyphics on the temple walls light up and bark too**, echoing through the chamber.
    - Elusia fumbles the phrase: "You have loosed… Let loose the hounds of hell." "Unleashed would have been the right answer, damn it."
    - Effect: **no verbal spells for four rounds**, because no words can be heard.

### Chaos event 2: Bones day (0:24:09)
24. **Elusia laughs so hard her feet stomp.** "**This automatically means the no bones day has turned into a bones day.**" Rocks fall from the unsteady cavern ceiling.
    - **Grorx dodges every rock** (24). "This reminds me of when I grew up, the dogs barking at you all the time, people throwing rocks at you, trying to kill you… **every goblin grows up like this.**" "What childhood did you have?" "Goblin childhood." Elusia: "This suddenly got very sad."
    - **Xiren, covering his ears, is hit** (9 damage). "Don't look at the ground, keep your eyes up, even if they're only partially working."
    - **0:26:43** Xiren realizes Grorx "had a very sad childhood", and **the protector in him is slightly distracted** ("That's how we get you"). Then he wipes off the dust.
