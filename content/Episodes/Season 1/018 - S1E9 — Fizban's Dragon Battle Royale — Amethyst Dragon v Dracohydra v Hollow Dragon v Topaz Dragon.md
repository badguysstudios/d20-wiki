For Halloween, the Arbiter cracked open his favorite book and turned the arena's lemonade vendor, a living amethyst die and the shadows of both hosts into four dragons, then set them loose on each other. **Fizban's Dragon Battle Royale** dragged its [[Champions|champions]] from an open sky through three dragon lairs. Along the way it served up sandwich diplomacy, a failed union drive, a baby dragon and a mushroom that may or may not have made someone sing. At the end of it, the most frightened champion in the arena stood victorious with ten heads.

## The Story

### A Halloween Costume Party

The night opened with "Happy Halloween!" and a Grandmaster still putting the finishing touches on her outfit. "Above all things, I always go out for Halloween," declared [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]]. Inspired by "this new Treasury of Dragons," she had made herself an aspect as a Topaz dragon, "more gold than I hoped." [[The Arbiter|The Arbiter]] felt underdressed in what appeared to be a cat costume. "You're a very cute cat," she assured him. "I found this thing in the dumpster," he replied. "It smells like cheese."

The first champion was a familiar face: [[Narfi|Narfi]], who runs the lemonade stand at the arena, "the best lemonade in the entire realm." He had been promised more ice for his chronically warm lemonade, and the Arbiter now offered all the ice he would ever need if he won. Narfi only caught up afterwards: "I'm sorry, what? Fight for what?"

His opponent was [[Vashon|Vashon]], a living amethyst die finally freed from his owner's dice bag. He vowed to "break my shell and let my purple amethyst rain all over your faces." Narfi objected on scientific grounds, since hot things poured on cold lemonade make warm lemonade. "That's thermodynamics." The Grandmaster was impressed: "This guy knows science."

### Four Dragons and a Dice Cup

Knowing the Grandmaster had "a thing for dragons," the Arbiter proposed a battle between them. She liked dragons, she clarified, but did not want to date one, "an important distinction." Then he produced a large leather tome with "Fizban's Treasury" on the spine. Since the night called for it, he suggested the hosts get a piece of the action through their shadows.

With a wave of his hands, he drew out the Grandmaster's shadow, identical to her but in inverse colors. Then he pulled out [[The Shadow of the Arbiter|his own shadow]] once again. The shadow was thrilled as ever: "What the fuck do you want this time?"

The Arbiter pulled tiny dragon forms out of the book's pages and hurled them at the contestants. Narfi's bones cracked as he swelled into a Dracohydra, and from then on he spoke only in the plural: "We are many, many heads." After some debate over the plural of Narfi (Narfai? Narfosis?), he settled on "Narfacist." Vashon took a glowing purple essence to the chest and rose as an Amethyst Dragon, "a dragon all the time." The Grandmaster's shadow became a gorgeous gold-and-orange [[The Topaz Dragon|Topaz Dragon]].

The Arbiter's shadow had a harder time. It broke apart into ethereal light, and the Arbiter had to weld it into a suit of armor to make [[The Shadow of the Arbiter|the Hollow Dragon]]. Its verdict: "I hate it here."

### Battle in the Clouds

The castle bled away into open sky, with a blinding green crystal spinning at its center and the hosts swelling to dragon size above. [[The Acolytes|The Acolytes]] got involved before the fight even started. They hurled everything they'd brought: fish heads, a cat in sunglasses, pizza, flaming people, goblins and "someone's grandmother again." Three dragons dodged it all. The Dracohydra had too many heads to keep track of, and the barrage battered it. "Know that you are the first to draw blood on this field," the Grandmaster told the crowd proudly.

Chaos piled on chaos. The Grandmaster's aspect won a glowing red egg that would hatch in six rounds. Then the whole field was thrown into a game of tag, with the Hollow Dragon starting as "it." The Topaz Dragon promptly formed an alliance with the Dracohydra. "We're BFFs now. We're going to get matching tattoos after this arena." When the Arbiter protested that there were rules, she replied, "There are, and I can make whatever I want." Everyone else avoided the Hollow Dragon. Too many cooties.

The Amethyst Dragon opened by breathing a gravity-warping blast that pinned the Dracohydra in place and lopped off its first head. "For when you cut off one head of the Hydra, two more will grow in its place," Narfi answered, as two more grew. "We are growers, not showers." Vashon then teleported to the far corner of the sky. The Topaz Dragon was disgusted by the hit-and-run. When she reached for the giant crystal, it went white hot and the whole world shifted.

### Home Invasion

The dragons landed in a cave behind a roaring waterfall: the Amethyst Dragon's lair. "I'm coming home," Vashon crooned. "This is my world now." He whispered to the Topaz Dragon that "sandwiches will be ready soon." Then he flew over and clawed her face three times before vanishing again. "Does it look like the sandwiches were good?" she asked, showing off her fresh scars.

The Hollow Dragon's armor exploded in every direction and snapped back together. The Topaz Dragon answered by bending the waterfall at him like a fire hydrant. "Yes, I am a waterbender. Everyone recognize this." When her claws missed, she switched to words:

> "That's why you're called a hollow dragon because nothing's in you whatsoever. No heart, no brain, and probably not even a soul within it. I hope that cuts deep." — the Topaz Dragon

Spurned, the Hollow Dragon tried to unionize the Dracohydra. He pointed out that the Grandmaster made Narfi fight to the death *and* paid minimum wage, and argued they could "control the means of production of the ice." Narfi was horrified: "This is how you get us both fired by doing this union talk at work." The Grandmaster promised a meeting with all vendors and caterers to address their work balance. Narfi had one condition: "Just no more committees."

The cave sprouted mushrooms. Vashon's spectral projection ate a healing one, which did nothing for a projection, then rolled a natural 1 against a target with seven necks. "My projection is a bitch," Vashon admitted. Mortified, he retreated into his bedroom and shut the door. The Hollow Dragon ate a poisonous mushroom and shrugged it off. The Topaz Dragon put up a shimmering protective shell and flew over to knock on Vashon's door with a pep talk: "When you make a good sandwich, you finish it." He cracked the door open with one claw hanging out, then spat an explosive crystal at her. It bounced off her shell, hit the Hollow Dragon and collapsed him into a talking puddle inside his own armor.

The Grandmaster called a timeout for lemonade. Todd from the blue raspberry slushie stand was covering Narfi's stand, slipping around without restaurant shoes. Narfi threatened to snap him in half if blackberries got in the lemonade. Afterwards, the Arbiter gave a shout-out to Wes Wolf, an Acolyte in the nosebleeds who had commissioned a painting of [[Doug the Pug|Doug the Pug]] by Saint Cinder. Then Doug declared a no bones day. To shake off the bad juju, the Grandmaster gave every dragon a little luck.

The fight got uglier. The projection ripped a head off its supposed ally, the Dracohydra ("Why do you hurt us… oh wait, we're friends"). Vashon stripped the Topaz Dragon's shield and crouched over the green crystal: "It is my egg now. I want to stay home. It's scary everywhere else." Breath weapons went off in every direction. "I didn't brush my teeth lately, so suck it," the Topaz Dragon announced before her necrotic blast. The Dracohydra answered with a prismatic spray of lightning, and the Arbiter ruled that it got lighter and faster with every head it lost. When tag ended, the Dracohydra had forgotten to tag anyone, and a little red X exploded on its claw.

The projection then flubbed a second natural 1, and the Grandmaster lost patience. "Arbiter, from here on out, every punishment, every consequence, and every good thing will be doubled in effect." Her own aspect immediately ate double damage from Vashon's singularity breath. The Topaz Dragon teleported on top of him to crush the crystal under his weight. When that failed, she sank her teeth into his neck. "You were the best of us!" The Arbiter suggested her acting classes were going to her head. The Dracohydra then sent its "one smart head" sneaking toward the crystal while six others distracted. Vashon teleported away, so all six bites landed on the Hollow Dragon for a brutal 78 damage. "You're basically the ideal unionized worker in one body. Why are you doing this?" the Hollow Dragon wailed. "You told us it was a fight to the death," Narfi replied. "We fight to the death." Then the smart head touched the crystal.

### The Tower by the Sea

The battle moved to a castle tower on a remote island, the Topaz Dragon's lair. "Welcome home, everyone," she purred. "This is where beauty lies." Her egg hatched into a tiny wyrmling, Junior Topaz, whom she shoved straight out of the nest at the Hollow Dragon. It gummed his leg.

Sandwich diplomacy resumed. The Topaz Dragon offered Vashon a place on her team if he killed the Hollow Dragon. Vashon secretly sent the Hollow Dragon a psychic promise that he would double-cross them. The Hollow Dragon then turned on the Topaz Dragon with a fresh threat: "One day, me and the Arbiter, we're going to kill all of you." He promised a beanbag chair and a soda fountain to follow.

The Grandmaster turned to her colleague. "Care to explain what your aspect is saying?" The Arbiter insisted that "he doesn't speak for me" and that this had "happened last time too." "It has to come from a certain place, doesn't it?" she asked, and promised a long talk later. "He looks like an idiot, you can't take him at his word," the Arbiter pleaded. "Fine, we'll ignore the idiot this time."

Vashon then made his own political turn. "In order for us to eat, we must destroy the mouths of the many." He blasted the Dracohydra and fled to a high perch. Narfi prayed to "Boss" for strength, and she granted it. The Dracohydra leapt off its stumpy legs and drenched Vashon in poison, then grew its eighth head. Doug fired his laser eye to push the arena into one last change.

### Blood in the Water

The Dracohydra had no lair, so the Arbiter made it one: a giant wave swept everyone into an underwater cavern laced with breathable magic. "We feel so much more nimbly bimbly here," Narfi said, twice as fast in the water. The Topaz Dragon, who had "never made a bond" with the Hollow Dragon, finished him with a bite. His last words promised a comeback, with cheese puffs and the Grandmaster as a footstool. The light inside his armor faded, and the pieces drifted to the cavern floor. The Topaz Dragon caught his skull, held it up to the hosts, looked the Arbiter in the eye and crushed it. "That's not fair," the Arbiter said. "It's a fair battle," the Grandmaster replied. (At the table, it came out afterwards that the Hollow Dragon could have pulled itself back together if its pieces stayed close. The crushed skull put an end to that plan.)

"Once team hollow, always team hollow," Vashon declared, and breathed his singularity on everyone. Junior Topaz died. The Topaz Dragon clung on with almost nothing left, and Vashon fled to the map's edge "like a little bitch." The Arbiter wondered aloud why Deathmatches always attract cowards. He then showered a nacho-loving sect of Acolytes below his balcony with cheese ("It's the nacho splash zone!"). The Topaz Dragon used the last of her strength to teleport the stranded Dracohydra into range and whip up a whirlpool. Vashon breathed again and caught them both. She dissolved in the singularity "until just the little school of fishies that come by nip away the rest of my body."

It came down to the die and the lemonade man. "This is it for all the marbles, baby," Vashon declared. His breath came back yet again, and this time the Dracohydra simply shrugged it off: "We are stronger." Then it charged with all ten heads. Ten bites tore the Amethyst Dragon apart. "Sandwiches are mine," Vashon said happily, dying. "This sandwich is so good."

"There is blood in the water and that is enough for our fight," the Grandmaster proclaimed, crowning the arena's own lemonade vendor as the champion. His message to the Acolytes: "Half-priced lemonade for all."

## Dried Blood

Back to his usual self, Narfi credited his victory to teamwork: "We win because we are smarter with heads put together." He saw "opportunities for growth" for the lemonade stand and was even open to adding Vashon's blackberry lemonade, though he couldn't quite remember Vashon's name. His parting wisdom: "When one head has an idea, it's best to listen… listen to those voices and bite the shit out of anything you see, especially if you have a lot of heads."
