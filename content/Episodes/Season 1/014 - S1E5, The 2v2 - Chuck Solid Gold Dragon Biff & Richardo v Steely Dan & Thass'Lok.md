## What happens
### Opening: soaps and the mail (Dread Fortress)
1. **0:00:25** The Grandmaster lounges on her throne of cushy red velvet seats, "watching TV." **The TV is four goblins.** Two hold up a cardboard TV screen while two behind it **reenact a soap opera they heard someone else describe**. They use a pile of props: tennis rackets, wigs, basketballs, a phone, and "a dove for some reason." She is entranced. "What is it that could be by far more important than my soaps right now?"
2. **0:01:16** The Arbiter: "your very important mail has arrived." "Oh, the mail! I love mail time!" She **says "click" into a remote**, and the goblins drop everything and sweep the props aside. She holds out her hands "like an impatient child."
3. **0:01:43** The Arbiter **floats the letter to her telekinetically** ("with my brain powers") from three feet away. GM: "Getting your telos mixed up?… **Weird flex, but thank you.**"
4. **0:02:13** Among the bills is **a beautiful scroll with a small emblem**. The Arbiter claims he can't read: "**I, I, no, I, I can't read.**" It is **from "the Dreaded GM."** The Arbiter asks, "Is he that guy that sells the falafel at the carnival?" GM: "**No, that's Teddy. That's Teddy. Very different.**… No, this one has dreads."
5. **0:03:17 The invitation.** The Dreaded GM is running "this absolutely amazing opportunity over on his stream" (though "he actually doesn't quite know what's going on on his stream"). He has invited them to put on **a small arena show on his channel**. The Arbiter asks, "Does that mean we get to have a deathmatch?"
6. **0:03:43 The endless scroll.** The rest of the scroll unrolls past her feet, down through the room and out into the hallway. It is **completely blank**, "purely length for length's purposes… **a total power move**," because "who can afford that kind of postage?… That's a commentary on the post system here."
7. **0:04:20** The event is **"a very secret donation charity event… for the Dreaded GM on his channel to help him out for some of the hardships his family is going through."** GM: "you know how much Dreaded has just done for the community here… We love Dreaded." The Arbiter: "maybe we could get some of that falafel on the way." GM: "**we'll drop by Teddy's and get the falafel.**"
8. **0:05:09 The champions.** The Arbiter **rips space open with his hands**, and **four champions walk out into the Dread Fortress**. GM: "You really just had those in your back pocket, didn't you?" Arbiter: "some people collect pogs and stones… **I like to collect people that fight to the death.**" GM: "Everybody's got a hobby."
9. **0:05:54** GM: "Champions, you've found yourself inside **the Dreaded Fortress**. This is my home. And I welcome you to it." She sizes them up: "Odd choices, but I think this will work." The Arbiter: "I don't know if they're worthy. We just met. **I found some of these guys at the 7-Eleven.**"

### The arena: the Clock of the Lost
21. **0:18:33** The Arbiter claps. A psychedelic wave rolls across their vision, and they stand **atop spinning gears in a giant column of clockwork**: grinding gears, metallic clanking, pistons, and squeals of distant machinery.
22. **0:19:19** GM: "Welcome, dear champions, to **the Clock of the Lost**." A steady ticking runs constantly in the background. They stay "until there is only one team remaining, or perhaps only one of you left." **The copper-tainted wheels are all one level; anything silver is above or below.** "**These gears will not stop until a magic word has been said**… If you are not quick on your feet, you might find yourself crushed by the gears."
23. **0:20:15** The Grandmaster lands on a gear. Her massive wings have a blue hue that shimmers off the gears. "Do not let the environment be that which takes your life. **Or I will not allow you to return home, victor or not.**"
24. **0:21:07 The stands.** High above are stands filled with Acolytes. GM: "I see you, my chat, my acolytes… Dan, oh no, you're just cheering for yourself in chat… If you can't cheer for yourself, who are you going to cheer for?" Their cheers "may light a fire in them… may give them advantage." She flies to her seat, where goblins wait with **her footstool, popcorn and soda**.
25. **0:22:58 Placement.** Chuck and Steely Dan land in the bottom quadrant, Thass'Lok and Richardo in the top. "**One member from each pairing is facing off against each other on a cog.**"
26. **0:24:27** The Arbiter to the floating grandstands: "Acolytes of the Grandmaster, you have come for dungeons and dice and deathmatches, and you will have blood."
27. **0:24:50** **Richardo rolls a nat 1 on initiative.** He makes a preemptive Dex save to avoid falling and **rolls a nat 20**, so he stays on his feet but goes last. Turn order: Thass'Lok, Steely Dan, Chuck, Richardo.

### Round 1

Thass'Lok strides gallantly but low to the center gear. **He raises his shield toward the warforged, his own partner**, holds his rapier slightly behind him, and keeps one eye on the other lizard.
30. **0:28:22 Steely Dan.** Tape: "**I'm Terry Wogan. It's all in me.**" He makes an Int save "for a couple of neurons" ("wooden stuff inside his mind"), chunks toward Chuck, and **throws a deck of playing cards in Chuck's face: "Gin or glaive."** Chuck: "**I understood that reference there, Tin Man.**"

He rolls a nat 1.** He screeches across the floor, **tapes fly out of him, and he scrabbles on hands and knees** among the spools, "oh, no, no, oh, what," because "that's his entire vocabulary." He **patches a tape back together with magic tape.**

Tape: "**Forever dumb, I want to be forever dumb.**" Then **a judo chop to the neck** lands, and **Chuck grapples him as a bonus action (Tavern Brawler)**, winning the strength-off. Tape: "**Like a virgin, touched for the 35th time.**" Chuck: "**I'm going to need you to stop that. You're making it weird.**" He holds Dan by the back of the neck.

Arbiter: "I thought he was just going to jump into the gears." He rolls a d4 to pick a target and shoots the warforged for 7.

GM: "Feeling a little ganged up on?" Dan: "**That was an original mixtape.**" Richardo: "I had no idea. I'm so sorry." **Bull One dashes 60 ft past Thass'Lok.** (Thass'Lok's player drops from the call here.)

### Chaos event 3: the Arbiter's wrench (1:50:29)

### Chaos event 4: the pillow fight (1:59:21)

The first roll is a 5. **All weapons turn into big fluffy pillows for the rest of the round.** Pillowcases look however the champions like. "**A giant pillow just rampages towards you and poof, and it just body slams you.** While it is incredibly comfortable and soft… filled with duck feathers… it does hurt just enough that you fall and are dead yet again." **Steely Dan dies.** Tape: "**baby fart, doo doo doo doo doo.**"

### Final round
84. **2:07:36** GM: "This, dear champions, will be your final round. Show me what you're worth."

Reckless Attack plus the GM's earlier gift turn disadvantage into advantage, and his strength check hoists the body. **Tavern Brawler makes him proficient with improvised weapons, "in this case it being Steely Dan!"** Dan's player rates himself "D12 to D20"; the GM gives 2d12. It hits for 20.
