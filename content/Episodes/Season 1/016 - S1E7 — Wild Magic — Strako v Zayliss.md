## What happens
### Opening: bone day
1. **0:00:01** The Arbiter flies in through the skull-shaped rock mouth of the **Dread Fortress** to the Grandmaster's subterranean throne room. He finds **Elusia hovering over a small, old pug in a round fluffy chair**.
2. **0:01:02 Doug.** The Arbiter asks when they got a dog. Elusia says "**We've always had Doug.**" She has "always had the goblins take it, but Doug's always been a part of this family and the Dread Fortress." She is offended that he forgot "an entire family member." The Arbiter blames the stress of preparing all the Deathmatches.
3. **0:01:29 The bone test.** Some days **Doug the Pug has no bones and "just kind of collapses."** If he has bones, "then it's bone day and we have a good day in front of us." Elusia compares it to "the creature when it pops out and it sees its shadow." Doug is a "very old, sad-looking pug," barely keeping his eyes open, trying to bury himself in his bed. Lights shine down on him. Elusia digs her nails under him and **stacks his body up like a tower**, then lets go. "**It's a bone day! He has bones!**"
4. **0:03:20** The Arbiter: "Do you know who else has bones? The sacrifices for today's deathmatch." She says he's underestimating how important this is. He calls Doug "**the bone daddy**" and says "I think maybe I'm too stupid to understand." She agrees: "That has to be it."
5. **0:04:26 The champions.** The Arbiter presents "**Strako, the Wild Shadow, versus Zayliss**." He doesn't remember where he got them: "Strako was wandering around somewhere doing something. **Zayliss was outside of the carnival.** It was a whole mess." He adds, "They told me they wanted to die, I think even."
6. **0:05:05 Strako** is alarmed that nobody told him there'd be a pug, then: "**I love puppies. Today, in honor of my fallen boy, I will now fight this battle for Doug the Pug and all the puppies!**" He says "At first I was concerned that **I was once again captured by the Witchlight Carnival**." He is confused about the bones ("For puppy bones?").
8. **0:07:44** Doug teeters and might collapse but stays up. "Doug is happy that I am happy… Good champion choices, Arbiter." The Arbiter says "**Last week was a little bit weird with the cats and the dog thing.** I didn't want there to be any other odd sexual tension this time." Elusia asks for "a good, clean, straightforward fight."
9. **0:08:49 The arena.** Elusia wants "a place with circles, perhaps little like doggy holes. Doug loves to go through those when he has bones." The Arbiter opens a portal to **a tower set adrift in the cosmos**, with stars streaking past. Each corner has a portal:
   - one that looks out into space, with an almost mirrored finish
   - a pit of lava with a fiery opening (the "molten forge")
   - a green portal
   - "**a strange pool of blood with some squirming bodies coming out of it**"
10. **0:10:07 Elusia's welcome.** "This is **the chaos of time and space** here." Doug doesn't like to walk far, so portals make it easier for him to hop through. "It belongs nowhere else in time but here… **My gift to you.**" She challenges them to "be masters of this space and time." Then she flies up with Doug in her arms to the Acolytes, who are doing devil horns: "for Doug the Pug!… For the fight! For the blood! And for the win!" She **lifts Doug up; his body slouches in her arms.** **Doug has his own throne beside hers in the stands.**
11. **0:12:08 The Arbiter's wager.** "I want there to be as much blood as possible. **I have a running wager on about three liters, maybe four even.**" He poofs out.
12. **0:13:14 Placement.** Strako rolls a 20 on initiative and Zayliss a 12. **Zayliss starts by the molten forge. Strako starts behind a wall with cover.**

### Pre-match chaos event: balloon weapons (0:14:08)
13. The Acolytes demand a chaos event before the match even begins. The Arbiter rolls "for Doug": a 2. "Doug's not such a great roller."
14. **0:15:06** Zayliss is holding a **staff** and Strako a **great axe**. Both **turn into balloon weapons** "as if some clown came through and swapped it out," for one round. Doug is "clearly impressed and very happy."

### Round 1
15. **0:16:00 Strako rages** at the sight of his beloved axe turned into a balloon animal. His body pulses with energy. **Wild magic: a tiny fairy starts glowing in the center of his chest.** "How fitting."
16. **0:17:35** He peeks around the corner: "**Zayliss, I have a present for you. Are you ready?**" Zayliss: "Dude, my staff just turned into a balloon." Strako: "Do you believe in fairies?" Zayliss: "Yeah." Strako: "Good." **The fairy shoots out and hovers in front of Zayliss's face** ("Hey, what's up?"). Zayliss looks between it and her balloon staff, really confused.
17. **0:18:40** "I hope you enjoy your present." Strako ducks back into full cover and **the fairy explodes in her face**. Elusia: "Did this fairy know it was going to explode?" "Oh yeah." "Bye-bye!" **Zayliss passes the save easily (26) and holds her balloon staff behind her so it won't get hit.** Strako: "Did you like my fairy?" Zayliss: "It's a little doll." Strako: "I'll be sure to tell her that next time. She may not be so kind."
18. **0:20:29 Parkour.** Zayliss "tactically retreats" 25 ft behind the other side of a wall, then **parkours up the 15-ft wall**: "Parkour! Parkour!" She ends right above Strako, "looking right down at his dome."
19. **0:22:39 Arms of the Astral Self.** "I have extra arms that are coming out of me. **They're green and they start to glow. Thank Zenyatta.**" She **jumps down and the arms burst out as she falls**. Strako saves against it with a "dirty 20." Elusia: "an aerial attack from above!" She lands 10 ft in front of him, inside her astral arms' 10-ft reach. Strako is 7'5" and Zayliss 5'6".
20. **0:24:46** One astral-arm strike hits for force damage. **Strako's unstable backlash: as the punch lands on one side of his face, "a magical hand is going to come back and smack you right in the jaw."** Strako, as she drops in: "Hey." Elusia: "Lots of magic hands." Drew: "Battle of hands." **Zayliss's Stunning Strike: Strako stands "unflinching" and saves.** Her astral hands are fists while her normal arms are crossed.
21. **0:29:26 The balloons burst.** At the end of the round the balloon weapons swell until they pop. **Both champions take a little "balloon bursting damage."** Their **real weapons return**, and the balloons scatter as brightly colored confetti. Elusia: "Doug the Pug is satisfied."
22. **0:30:55 Arbiter vs Doug, begun.** The Arbiter rolls another 2. "**An embarrassment. You can't teach that.**" Doug sighs and "almost slaps you," nearly collapsing. Elusia props him back up: "**You almost removed Doug's bones.**" The Arbiter bows ("My apologies, Doug") and rolls his eyes. Elusia: "You should show more respect to your elders."

### Round 2

He **kisses his axe, smacks it against his shield**, and tells Zayliss: "**You have no respect for me and I don't like it at all.**" He rushes in. The first swing misses as she evades; **an upward slash connects.**

Zayliss sprints for the green portal. **Strako's opportunity attack misses; he slams his axe into the ground seconds after she has gone.** **Zayliss jumps in backwards, flipping him off.** Elusia: "This is very **Deadpool energy**, I like it." Zayliss: "**I respect you! Respect!**"

### Round 3

He flips her the bird and **walks into the blood portal**: "You want blood?" **The squirming bodies grab his ankles and pull him down into syrupy blood. He holds up his middle finger toward Zayliss the whole way down.** He comes out of the space portal with **advantage on his next attack**. He didn't attack or take damage, so **his rage ends**.
32. **0:44:37** Elusia: "Ooh, he's got layers." The Arbiter: "rage is great, but it's a one trick pony… get at the emotional heart of our champions before we… take the blood out of that heart." Elusia: "**Have the heart and then drain the heart.**… Just putting all the organs on display."

## Dried Blood (after-show)
- Drew introduces "Nighttide and Loch Ness, our newest guests."
- **Strako's build:** straight Wild Magic Barbarian to level 10, with the Sentinel and Shield Master feats, to blunt "crazy ki point damage." NyteTide picked wild magic as soon as he saw unstable backlash. **"Strako is a play on Kratos."** He badly wanted the thrown-weapon wild magic result "to be able to throw the ice axe."
- Loch Ness, on seeing a barbarian opponent: "I can't… I like chaos anyways."
- Loch Ness loved the blinding and the Marco Polo bit. NyteTide gave her his position "because… we're content creators… it's a show." He was surprised by the monk's movement: climbing the wall, hammering down from above, and the portal push.
- Bec: the jump over him took "the map from 2D to 3D."
- Drew: the portal-tower map was used in a Patreon playtest. Since there was no second map then, he added being sucked through a portal into the asteroid field to "spice this one up."
- **Retcon idea (not adopted on air):** Loch Ness imagines Zayliss running under, or log-rolling on, the shoved asteroid.
- **Timothy:** Drew wanted to make Strako feel bad. NyteTide thought throwing it away would make Zayliss chase it. The hosts riff that Timothy was also "the pastor," "the single founder of every charitable organization in the region," "**The CEO of Doctors Without Borders**," and had "**Single-handedly adopted 18 orphans**." These are table riffs; their canon status is uncertain. Bec chose the alien's glow colors to match the D20 Deathmatch aesthetic. They want a "remember Timothy / never forget" T-shirt through Highscore T, and an "Alien Timothy worm token." Bec asks, "Can the Arbiter resurrect Timothy?" Drew: "He will need to make a comeback."
- NyteTide was caught off guard by Zayliss's "dude, we're cool." It turned the fight into rivals rather than deadly enemies, and he had messaged Drew beforehand about how to play it. Loch Ness pictured it like Ragnarok, where fighters who don't have to fight just sit and chat. "She's edgy and she's chaotic, but also like she did respect him."
- **Final words (in character? uncertain):**
  - Strako (2:07:11): "**You can still respect someone and nearly cut them in half.**"
  - Zayliss (2:07:37): "**You can respect people who cut you in half.**" / "I feel like they would have been best friends."
  - Drew: "We'll get that beer in Valhalla. We'd have been best friends if you hadn't murdered me."

### Characters
**[[Characters/Strako|Strako, the Wild Shadow]]** (NyteTide). A 7'5" wild magic barbarian.
- **Gear:** an axe he loves ("my baby"), called a great axe at 0:15:04 and later a battleaxe used with a shield, plus a shield.
- **Wild magic seen:**
  - the exploding fairy, which rose from his chest twice
  - magical-hand and light-bolt backlash
  - the "Care Bear" light that blinds
  - a bolstering rune that peels off the axe into his chest
- Uses Shield Master shoves.
- **Lore:**
  - Loves puppies; fought "in honor of my fallen boy."
  - Feared he'd been "once again captured by the Witchlight Carnival."
  - Expects Valhalla and to "kill all those gods."
  - Mentions "you may have heard of me."
  - Killed Timothy, mayor of the Worm's Town, through tag. Wants revenge on the fairy.
- **In this episode:** he was tagged by Doug, shoved a whole asteroid with his hero point, and won by cleaving Zayliss. The Grandmaster carried him hand in hand to the fans.

**[[Characters/Zayliss|Zayliss]]** (HeyLochNess). A 5'6" monk with a staff and glowing green **Arms of the Astral Self**.
- Found "outside of the carnival." Says weird things like this happen to her and she's "accustomed to it."
- **A chaotic "bro"** who genuinely respects Strako and flips him off constantly.
- **Climbs walls** (parkour), deflects missiles, uses Stunning Strike (never landed), evasion, and ki-point dodge.
- Found and threw **electrifying asteroid rocks**, accidentally killing Tabitha.
- **Killed by Strako. The Arbiter secretly revived her to 1 HP "for my army."** She told him she's "a cat person" and joined his grudge against Doug.

**[[Characters/Doug the Pug|Doug the Pug]]**. A very old, sleepy pug who has "always" lived in the Dread Fortress with the Grandmaster.
- The goblins look after him.
- Some days he has no bones and collapses. If he stands when stacked up, it's "bone day," a good omen.
- Has his own throne beside the Grandmaster's in the stands. Eats popcorn and snacks.
- **Rolls d20s with his mouth** and beat the Arbiter in four of five roll-offs. The Arbiter won with a 15 and a nat 20.
- Can sprout tiny wings and wear a head bubble to fly.
- Started the game of tag. Gave Zayliss a "final snuggle."
- Also called "the bone daddy."

**[[Characters/The Arbiter|The Arbiter]]** (Drew).
- Didn't know Doug existed and blames Deathmatch stress.
- Is **color blind**.
- Bet "three liters, maybe four" of blood and claimed he won at "three and a half pints."
- **Rages after losing to Doug and summons a meteor swarm.** The Grandmaster says he "clearly has anger issues."
- Reads Cosmo.
- **Secretly revives Zayliss for "my army."** Admits he's "a cat person" and vows to "show that pug… show them all."

**[[Characters/Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer]]** (Bec). Devoted to Doug and labels her levers clearly. "There are no rules except for the rules that I make up." Warns that fans have murdered a champion on their own before.

**[[Characters/Timothy|Timothy]]**. A glowing caterpillar-like alien on the asteroid, mayor of the Worm's Town. He brought peace and prosperity, had just defeated "the Republic," and was killed when Strako tagged and threw him. His society collapsed into self-destruction.

**[[Characters/Tabitha|Tabitha]]**. Another worm, killed by Zayliss's missed rock.

**[[Lore/The Acolytes|The Acolytes]]**. They throw hot dogs and beer, award hero points and trigger chaos events.

**[[Characters/Goblins|The goblins]]**. They take care of Doug.

### Places
- **[[Places/The Dread Fortress|The Dread Fortress]]**, its skull-shaped rock mouth, and the Grandmaster's subterranean throne room.
- **[[Places/The portal tower adrift in the cosmos|The portal tower adrift in the cosmos]]** ("the chaos of time and space"). Its four portals, as observed:
  - green: smells of spring and citrus; it healed Zayliss on her second pass and dumped her out of the space portal on her first
  - blood pool: squirming bodies pull you down; it sent Strako out of the space portal, then into the fire portal
  - fire/lava "molten forge": burns on exit
  - mirrored space portal: grants advantage on the next attack
  - A clock there moves in a different time.
- **[[Places/The desolate asteroid field|The desolate asteroid field]]**: three asteroids with about 20 ft of breathable atmosphere around them and freezing space beyond. It holds electrifying "magnetic" rocks and glowing worm-aliens.
- **[[Places/The Worm's Town|The Worm's Town]]**: Timothy's colony, now collapsed.
- **[[Places/Valhalla|Valhalla]]**: Strako's expected afterlife.
