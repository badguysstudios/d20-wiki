## What happens
### Opening: the throne room
1. **0:00:02 — Setting the scene.** Drew opens the finale. "Deep in the bowels of this hellish castle lies the throne room where the Grandmaster sits atop **Her Iron Throne**." The Arbiter's feet clack on the stone as he opens the giant doors.
2. **0:00:50 — The ruler's handbook.** The Grandmaster is sitting and drinking from a glass: "That's what rulers do. You sit on a throne and you drink out of a glass and you rule."
   - The Arbiter asks if there's a handbook. She says it is **a secret handbook passed only from ruler to ruler**: "You would know this if you're a ruler, but you are not."
   - **The Arbiter takes out a small notepad and notes that rulers sit and drink.**
3. **0:01:46 — Doug the Pug.** Beside her big throne is **a much smaller, adorable throne where Doug the Pug sits**.
   - Told it's the season's final show, Doug perks his ears. "It takes his entire body to bark."
   - **The Grandmaster slips and nearly calls the Arbiter "Grandmaster."** "Maybe I am giving my throne up to you… Never, no."
4. **0:02:39 — The champions.** The Arbiter opens a portal and introduces them "for the pleasure of your looking balls": **Lucien Braque versus Monteclaire Dufresne**.
5. **0:03:13 — Lucien's entrance.** Lucien is a **somewhat greenish half-orc**: super tall, with "the direct swimmer's body" and a **stiff pompadour**.
   - He bows with his forehead almost to the ground: "I am **the wordsmith of kings and queens, the narrator of the nether realms, the orator of the oracles**, and I am blessed by your presence."
   - The Arbiter: "Flattery will get you a lot of places here in the Dread Fortress." The Grandmaster adds her own welcome.
6. **0:04:19 — Monteclaire's entrance.** "This man sounds like a lot of fluff. I cannot wait to rip that fluffing out of his body."
   - The GM: "The man seems to be a dictionary and that is a hard book to tear apart."

### The arena: the Grand Coliseum
7. **0:05:09** A cool evening breeze greets them through the portal. The arena looks like "a very mundane, standard… battle arena. Almost like a coliseum."
   - The GM: "For this ending, I take you to no far off location… **this is our grand coliseum in the cool of night with the stars overlooking your grand battle.**"
   - The Acolytes encircle it. The columns may give cover or a launch point.
8. **0:06:22 — The GM thanks her Acolytes.** She flies up and thanks them for "the energy, the food, **the grandmothers you have sacrificed week after week**." She promises "nothing short of greatness tonight." She takes her seat high above, and **Doug appears at her side**.
9. **0:07:17 — Placement.** Both champions roll the same d4 spot, so Drew starts them on **opposite sides of a 20-ft stone column** ("Peek-a-boo"). Initiative: Monteclaire 17, Lucien 8.

### Round 1
10. **0:09:39 — Monteclaire's background.** "I am royalty myself, having been **a lord of a town. I was kicked out. We're not going to talk about that.**" Then: "He's got the gift of the gab, but **I got the gift of the stab.**"
11. **0:10:08 — Two crits.** Monteclaire runs up and scores **a natural 20 with his longsword**, one-handed. He then **crits again with the dagger in his other hand**: "You seem to be needing some more holes poked in you, son… I was told there would be blood, but I was unaware there would be so much of yours."
12. **0:11:53 — The countered Hunter's Mark.** Monteclaire tries to cast Hunter's Mark: "I got you in my sights and I just don't want you to ever leave."
   - **Lucien counterspells it with a speech:** "you're going to blow your load way too soon and you're going to disappoint everyone here. And then the fates will not sing about you. They will only whisper terrible things."
   - The GM: "It happens to all of us." Monteclaire: "**I am 93 years old.**" Lucien: "So it's happened to you more."
13. **0:13:45 — Mordenkainen's sword.** Lucien **smashes his sword against his shield and drags it along, throwing sparks**. A second sword forms from the first and moves behind Monteclaire: **Mordenkainen's Sword cast at 7th level**. It hits for force damage.
   - Lucien's own longsword glances off. "Ooh, no, no, that's not good… It happens to us all."
14. **0:16:30 — Self-inspiration.** Lucien gives himself Bardic Inspiration, whispering: "**come on, man, you got this. You promised yourself it wouldn't happen this time. You got this. People like you.**"
   - The GM imagines "People like you" sewn inside his jacket.
15. **0:17:50 — The GM's labels.** She orders the Arbiter to sew "**I am a delight. People like me**" inside all her clothing.
   - He objects that it's the holidays and he can't find a tailor. She says "You can learn how to sew."
   - **He sends a couple of mage hands to her chambers to start sewing the words of affirmation into all her clothing.**
16. **0:18:40 — Doug wins again.** The Arbiter's round roll is 13. **Doug "throws a 14 once again."** The GM: "A small undead pug can beat you every single time." The Arbiter: "Classic Doug, always having to one-up me."

### Chaos event 1 — the blue singer (0:19:07)
17. **0:19:58 — The singer.** Lucien rolls 17. High in the stands, **a woman with bright blue hair and sapphire skin** walks down through the crowd. Her robes trail behind her ("the longest train you've ever seen"). Her soft song seeps into the champions' minds.
   - Monteclaire saves (27). **Lucien fails and is paralyzed for one round**, frozen in its beauty.
18. **0:21:24 — Monteclaire recognizes the song.** It is "**I'm blue, da ba dee da ba die.**" Monteclaire: "My God, why? Not this. Take my ears, but not this… **I'm gonna be dancing on his corpse to this song.**"
19. **0:22:49 — The GM banishes her.** "Be gone, foul creature. We already have music here… **we don't have the copyrights to that song.**" She banishes the singer from the Coliseum.

### Round 2
20. **0:23:19 — Vow of Enmity.** "I hate to be doing this to you with listening to this new age hippie music." Monteclaire casts **Vow of Enmity (Channel Divinity)**.
   - He stabs the paralyzed Lucien with a "nice stabby and slashy rapier." He calls himself "**a paladin of retribution**."
21. **0:24:59 — "Southern discomfort."** Monteclaire reaches down as if to help Lucien up, then **punches him in the face**: "this is what we call a **southern discomfort**." The GM: "The shame!"
   - Lucien keeps concentration on the sword with "a little tiny orcish grunt."
22. **0:26:17 — The sword follows.** Monteclaire moves to a pillar, and **the floating sword takes an opportunity attack**.
   - Monteclaire: "I prefer doing the stabbing myself, but you youngsters got… new age spells." The GM: "It's called delegation. It's a sign of leadership."
23. **0:27:41 — Fighting while paralyzed.** Ruling: Lucien can't move his body, but his mind can still move the sword. He wills it into another hit, for **34 force total from the sword**.
   - Monteclaire: "Every problem has a solution." Arbiter: "But does every solution have a problem?"
24. **0:28:55 — The ear worm.** The song slips from Lucien's brain "**as if a little blue worm now crawls out from your ear, a literal ear worm**." The GM also riffs on "an ale worm… it's been in the ale for a while."
25. **0:29:20** The Arbiter rolls 18. "I was cheered on by the Acolytes," and they trigger another chaos event.

### Chaos event 2 — balloon weapons (0:29:36)
26. **0:30:24 — The clown.** Monteclaire rolls a 10 ("I'm average, which is good for any man"). Lucien: "All great heroes die."
   - **Every sharp weapon pops and becomes a brightly colored balloon weapon**, made by **a small clown at the side** who pretends to hide the magic.
   - It lasts two rounds and covers Lucien's magic sword too. The balloons deal "balloon damage."
   - **A balloon that breaks may not be traded back for the real weapon.**

### Round 3
27. **0:32:10 — Toll the Dead.** Monteclaire's balloon squeaks against the magic sword: "I feel like I'm fighting a child, like at a party." He casts **Toll the Dead**. Lucien's inspiration doesn't save him: "Ow! Ugh! What is that terrible sound?"
   - Monteclaire then **Misty Steps 30 ft away**.
28. **0:34:45 — The clown's reply.** Monteclaire scolds the clown: "They're not here for your entertainment… He can speak. I can stab. And you, you're just annoying, sir."
   - **The clown angrily twists a middle-finger balloon.** Lucien: "It's not a snake. I made you a snake." The Arbiter: "Finally… a clown with some attitude… A true professional."
29. **0:35:56 — The rocks.** Lucien peeks around the pillar: "First, the voice that paralyzed me. Now a clown in the corner. **It's almost like my ex-wife showed up twice.**"
   - He casts **Animate Objects on ten rocks** around Monteclaire. The pebbles rise "shaking with energy with their newfound sense of identity." They face their creator, then turn on Monteclaire and **pummel him with tiny shrill voices**. Three hit.
   - The GM: "In the words of **Tim Gunn**, make it work."
30. **0:39:13 — The balloon rapier pops.** Monteclaire ripostes with his balloon rapier and **destroys one rock**. **The rapier pops and is gone.** The GM: "He used the cheap balloons. We don't pay him a whole lot."
   - Monteclaire asks for a token so he can attack the clown. The Arbiter: "I have a new enemy… I already need to make 10 rocks."
31. **0:41:04 — The sword that wasn't there.** Lucien sends his sword toward Monteclaire in "a wrestling strut… **Razor Ramon coming down the aisle**." The table then realizes Animate Objects is concentration, so **the sword had already vanished**.
   - Lucien: "Check your concentration, ladies and gentlemen." Arbiter: "If you're gonna cheat in my game, be better at it."
   - Monteclaire's headcanon: it disappeared mid-strut. Asked whether he thinks it's invisible: "There's a lot of fuckery happening."
32. **0:43:12 — The dedication.** Lucien picks someone in the stands: "this victory will be for the one next to you." The GM: Nighttide typed last, and next to him is **Emu**, so "this is for Emu."
   - The roaring Acolytes **award both champions a hero point**.
33. **0:44:04** The Arbiter rolls 17. "Doug doesn't move, doesn't feel bothered to need to prove himself."

### Round 4
34. **0:44:30 — "Bring the pain."** The Arbiter: "this is a season finale and you haven't wowed us yet."
35. **0:44:58 — Chickens.** Monteclaire tries to flee the rocks with another Misty Step: "I'm not going to be rolling with these rocks." **Lucien counterspells**: "you can't keep running. **The Grandmaster does not like chickens.**" GM: "I don't like chickens and **I don't like turkeys.**"
36. **0:45:37 — The power slide.** Monteclaire disengages and charges straight at Lucien. He fails an athletics check, loses control of his body, and **slides right up to Lucien and ends on his knees in front of him**: "**They call this the halfling power slide.**"
   - GM: "Supplication?" The Arbiter adds "possibly your bowels." Monteclaire: "I am old."
37. **0:47:16 — The proposal.** Looking up: "**Well, I'm here. What do you want to do with me?**" His Persuasion beats Lucien's Insight. The Arbiter: "He makes you an indecent proposal… Doesn't seem like his heart's in it."
   - Lucien: "I only like 100%, 100% of the time… while I appreciate the offer, I will have to politely decline and **rescind any consent you might think has been passed through between us.**"
38. **0:48:46 — The rock barrage.** Lucien bonks him on the head with the balloon sword, and it holds with a squeak.
   - The nine rocks bounce over. Monteclaire: "I'm getting attacked by the little troll rocks from frickin'…" GM: "Frozen."
   - Lucien reads the spell text and asks, "Montclair, can you read?" Monteclaire: "I know there's letters." GM: the rocks attack "while Montclair is trying to sound out some words we threw in the sand for him."
   - **Six rocks hit from every direction.** Lucien: "**They will speak of your sacrifice.**" Monteclaire is "looking very bloody."
39. **0:53:01 — The balloon longsword pops.** Lucien's second balloon strike lands with Bardic Inspiration ("Going for the gold"). He tries Searing Smite, but it's ruled out because he's already used his bonus action.
   - Monteclaire: "I'm remembering I'm on my knees just getting pummeled."
   - **The balloon longsword pops on the spikes on Monteclaire's shoulder.**
40. **0:54:46 — Doug at the door.** The Arbiter rolls 8. **Doug starts barking frantically: someone is at the front door.** The GM lets him check it alone and calls a break: "watch the halftime show."

### Round 5 (after the break)
41. **0:55:31 — Doug is missing.** The GM asks where he is. The Arbiter assumed she was watching him.
   - She went for water and popcorn and told Doug he could check the door alone. "I'm sure Doug will be back soon. Doing pug things." The Arbiter: Doug can watch the VOD.
42. **0:56:48 — Thunderous Smite.** "His jaw's loose from all the rocks hitting him across the face." **Monteclaire stands**: "you may have a rock army but… let's see how you do against some real elements."
   - He casts **Thunderous Smite** and hits with his longsword, "no longer a balloon" ("this long, long, long, not average sword").
   - **Lucien is knocked back 10 ft and prone.** Bleeding against the pillar, Monteclaire says: "I hope you made a will, because you're about to go down, son… I'm in my 90s."
43. **1:00:36 — The boy bands.** Lucien holds concentration and the rocks keep circling ("Both **NSYNC** and the **Backstreet Boys** are still there except for **JC**. He's gone").
   - Monteclaire: "I'm in danger… if I'm gonna go out, I may as well get banged to death by these rocks."
   - Monteclaire: "**You are a true adversary, young poet.**" Lucien: "**I will write a sonnet about this and about you and about your glorious first two rounds.**" Monteclaire: "that's about the review I get every time."

### The attack on the Dread Fortress (1:02:25)
44. **1:02:25 — Silence.** The crowd goes deadly quiet, all looking past the arena walls. Lucien: "Watch out, my ex-wife's back." A low rumble follows.
45. **1:02:53 — The Tarrasque.** **The western wall of the Coliseum is blown apart as a Tarrasque breaks through.** It throws debris across the field and chomps at the walls.
   - **Dozens, if not hundreds, of soldiers "of all types and shapes"** flood into the arena and the stands.
   - **The Acolytes are attacked:**
     - **Outbound Emu** is ripped in half by goblins.
     - **Shady Man** has a limb chopped off.
     - **Kristen** is hit in the face with fireballs.
   - Lucien is thrown to the middle of the arena.
46. **1:04:25 — The Arbiter holds the Tarrasque.** **The Arbiter teleports in front of it: "No further!"** He **holds it in place with his time magic.** "Grandmaster, you have to get to safety."
47. **1:04:58 — The Grandmaster's stand.**
   - She refuses: "**I will not leave my arena. Sound the bells… the Dread Fortress is under attack.**"
   - To the Acolytes: "never before have I asked you to step into the arena, but **the arena has now reached out to you. You must defend your seats.**"
   - She flies down to the champions: "The Dread Fortress is at risk of being taken over. I implore you to join my ranks and defend not only these stone halls, but **the place I call home.**"
48. **1:05:45 — Her gifts.**
   - **Two sparkling purple vials** restore them fully, as if after a long rest.
   - **She summons their lost weapons up from the sand.** "Do not let go of them."
   - A mound of sand reveals **a large pink chest ringed in magical fire**: "**a secret gift of this arena. A loot chest**." The fire only burns those who enter it, and it's there so their opponents can't reach the chest.
   - **Kills earn blood points, and blood points open the chest.** Drew: each kill is one point, three points buy one roll, and points can be shared. **Chat grants one reroll.**
   - Drew calls it "**a box of chaos**" and "this magical chaos box". Touching it sends chaotic energy surging through the body.
49. **1:07:26 — The Northern Gate.** "I leave you to defend while I go grab one more for this battle." She moves to **the Northern Gate**, which is fastened with multiple locks: "**Arbiter, what locks have you put on this?**" He's too busy straining under the Tarrasque to help.
50. **1:08:50 — Doug returns.** **Doug flies into the arena and guards the Southern Gate**, barking his head off. More waves are coming.

### Horde wave 1 — death dogs and the Aarakocra Dogmaster
51. **1:10:02 — The first strikes.** An **Aarakocra commanding two-headed death dogs** rolls a nat 20 on initiative. It **throws a javelin into Monteclaire**: "**Bring it on, bird boy.** I've taken a lot more damage from a lot less sharp things today."
   - It clacks its beak and sends a death dog in. Monteclaire uses **Brace** and clobbers it: "**Be gone, evil mutt.** Get off my heels."
52. **1:12:38 — Synaptic Static.** Lucien points at the dogs: "**Your brains, while you have two, are the size of puppies.** And therefore, I tell you to be gone."
   - **Synaptic Static kills all three death dogs**, earning 3 blood points.
53. **1:15:05 — The chest's first prize.** "You must touch the box." Lucien: "**He touched the butt. I'm gonna go touch the butt.**"
   - He crosses the fire, takes a little damage, and rolls badly. He uses chat's reroll and gets item B. The GM: "a small B comes buzzing out. No, I'm just kidding."
   - **He gets Keoghtom's Ointment**: a glass jar smelling of aloe with four charges. It heals, cures poison and disease, and can be shared with his fellow champion.
54. **1:18:04 — Combat inspiration.** Lucien gives Monteclaire **combat inspiration**: "good sir, you have this. I believe in you. **I felt the bite of your pointy end. And I don't recommend it for other people.**"
55. **1:19:18 — Bless and the pillar.** Monteclaire: "You've been nothing but gracious this entire fight, even though you did quite handily put me on my knees. Allow me to return the favor."
   - He **casts Bless on both of them**. "I'm kind of old. I move pretty slow." He ducks behind a pillar to break the Aarakocra's line of sight.
   - The death dog misses him twice. "**Aha, jebaited.**" His riposte "**slice[s] both your heads off at once**," obliterating it.

### Chaos event 3 — trebuchets (1:21:58)
56. **1:22:08 — Flaming rocks.** Creaking trebuchets beyond the walls hurl **giant flaming rocks** onto the arena and into the stands. "I'm assuming they're rocks… Manure's also flammable."
   - **Lucien rolls a nat 1 and takes the full blast.** Monteclaire takes half, and the Aarakocra is hit too.
   - **In the stands:**
     - Phoenix (nat 20) dances through the rocks.
     - **Gator is crushed and set on fire.**
     - Vegeta sidesteps but feels the heat.
57. **1:24:41 — Defending the gates.** **Doug's bark echoes down the south hall and holds back another horde: "He is the defender of the south gate."** The GM opens the first of the Northern Gate's locks: "Two more physical locks to go."
58. **1:25:19 — The dive.** The **Aarakocra Dogmaster** flies up and **dive-bombs Monteclaire, eagle-slicing him with its talons**.
59. **1:26:35 — The cleave.** Lucien **drags his sword through the flames and casts Searing Smite** ("Time to leave a mark"). He steps beside Monteclaire and gives him a little pat on the shoulder, then strikes. **The flaming sword comes down on top of the Aarakocra's head and cleaves it in two so the beak splits**: "perfect side-by-side symmetry." Lucien: "**Wings are such an unfair advantage, always unfair.**"

### Horde wave 2 — doppelganger, flameskull, red dragon wyrmling
60. **1:28:54 — Wave 2 arrives.** The Acolytes hold off the first brunt. Then **a doppelganger, a flameskull and a red dragon wyrmling** surround the champions.
   - **The doppelganger takes Lucien's shape.** Lucien: "**it took me a long time to do this. A lot of energy, a lot of money, a lot of doctors. I'm not gonna just allow you to take my branding.**"
   - Its speech is "a foul mimic of human speech." The GM: "Why does it sound like a Dalek?"
61. **1:30:22 — "Who summoned these menagerie of beasts?"**
   - The Grandmaster asks the champions to cover her while she unlocks "an ungodly beast."
   - The Arbiter (see Who's talking) urges her to run. She refuses: "**I will never, ever give up the reins of D20 Deathmatch.**"
   - The Arbiter: "I can't hold this Tarrasque much longer."

### Chaos event 4 — the pulsing ring of fire (1:31:18)
62. **1:31:32 — The ring pulses.** The GM rolls 13. **The chest's fire ring grows and pulses out in waves** that everyone must jump: once at the start of the round and once at the end, stronger the second time. "Jump is like hopscotch… Jump rope."
   - Lucien barely gets singed. Monteclaire fails even after spending his combat inspiration.
63. **1:34:05 — The flameskull.** It hits Monteclaire with a 3rd-level **magic missile** and a **fire ray**: "**I do not like this flaming skull one bit.**"
64. **1:35:17 — Command.** "Y'all just coming in here interrupting my near-death experience… take a chill pill." Monteclaire casts **Command at 3rd level** on all three.
   - **The doppelganger grovels prone.** The flameskull can't be prone, so he orders it to **halt**. The wyrmling resists.
   - "**Good luck with that pulsating fire, idiots.**"
65. **1:37:11 — The kind exchange.**
   - Monteclaire: "I always believe it's nice to see **the youth of today just handling business**… you're always inspiring others."
   - Lucien: "You're a fine, fine young chap. And **I apologize for beating you senseless with rocks.**"
   - Monteclaire: "If you're going to be anything, be efficient. And son, **you are efficient at killing.**"
66. **1:37:52 — Friendly fire.** **The wyrmling's fire breath hits everyone.** Monteclaire rolls a nat 20 and takes nothing; Lucien uses Shield Master and takes nothing. Only its allies burn: "Literal friendly fire."
   - **The second, stronger pulse of the ring makes the flameskull's bone crack, and it explodes into dust powder.** GM: "Don't breathe it in. That's actually very dangerous for your lungs."
   - **No blood point**, since no champion made the kill.
67. **1:40:58 — The nat 1.** "I really don't like the look of my doppelganger." Lucien scrapes sword on shield again and summons Mordenkainen's Sword behind the wyrmling. **He rolls a natural 1. He trips and falls on his own blade.**
68. **1:42:49 — The other pocket.** Lucien: "that was not the intended purpose. Everything is fine." He **opens his other pocket, which has "everyone loves you" stitched inside**: "You were right. I'm good at this."
   - The GM: "You're a delight. People like you." **The Acolytes pause mid-fight, turn to him and chorus "you're a delight. People like you," then go back to throwing blows.**
69. **1:43:39 — The doppelganger dies.** Monteclaire drops Bless and casts Searing Smite: "I don't like there being two of them… This one just doesn't have the charm."
   - He **cuts it off at the knees so it looks like Lucien on his knees**: "**now this is what I was imagining happening earlier**." Then he **stabs it through the throat**. It chokes on its own blood and dies.
71. **1:45:49 — The wyrmling.** Monteclaire slices "this little baby bird of a dragon" with his longsword and searing fire. It survives, its little wings shaking as it "rears back roaring at you in its cute little way."
   - "**Hey, Big Horn Daddy, you're next.** He looks like a terrifying **Krampus**."
72. **1:47:59 — The red gems.** **Monteclaire tosses his blood points to Lucien. They fly across as little red gems.** Lucien: "Oh, courteous of you." The wyrmling bites Monteclaire.
73. **1:49:02 — Hold Monster.** Lucien walks back through the flames toward the chest. "**You just stay there, big fella. I'll be back.**" He casts **Hold Monster** on the devil, which saves and just laughs at him.
   - **His second chest roll produces a Mace of Smiting**, which can be shared.
74. **1:51:20 — The neck.** Lucien inspires Monteclaire: "Don't let the little bird get you down. **All's we gotta do is grab him by the neck and twist real hard. Bird's dead.**"
   - Monteclaire: "I feel like that's most creatures." Lucien: "**The neck is the weak spot. And just birds.**"
75. **1:52:10 — The devil is held.** **Lucien spends his hero point** on a second Hold Monster ("Fuck it"). The Acolytes' "wisdom, their strength, they give unto you." **The horned devil is paralyzed.**

### The turkey dragon (1:53:38)
76. **1:53:38 — The gate opens.** "Please open. Please. Is this the right key?" **The third lock clicks, the chains around the gate drop, and the gate opens.** "Come out, foul beast… Come defend me." **Out slithers a turkey dragon.**
77. **1:54:25 — "Of my own creation."** Lucien: "I thought you said you hated turkeys." GM: "**I have made a monster which I fear as well. I took a note from Batman's page.**" Lucien: "You take your fear and you make it your strength… Branding."
   - **The turkey dragon stomps the wyrmling to death.** It ignores Monteclaire, since he is in the Grandmaster's good graces.
   - Monteclaire: "**This is pure nightmare fuel. I've been brought to… a hellish landscape of chaos.**"
78. **1:55:24 — Banishment.** The devil burns resistances trying to break the hold. Monteclaire: "**I'm gonna side with the turkey on this one. It is absolutely more haunting than you, Mr. Hornboy.**"
   - He casts **Banishment and spends his hero point** to give the devil disadvantage. It fails.
   - The paralyzed devil, "angry that he never got to attack," realizes what's happening, eyes aflame, and **poof, he's gone**. Monteclaire: "**Be gone, Thot.**" He kneels on one knee to hold concentration.
79. **1:58:01 — The stands wait.** The Grandmaster **pets her turkey dragon**: "**It turns out I should have just trusted our champions to begin with.**"
   - The invaders in the stands pause but don't run. "It's like they're holding their breath, waiting, looking for a sign."
   - GM: "**He's gone. Leave this place or we'll have your lives.**"
80. **1:58:44 — The red dragon.** At the sound of wings, **a red dragon lands on the turkey dragon** and **breathes fire on the champions**. Both fail and are badly burned.
   - Monteclaire: "**Out of the frying pan, into the fire. Am I right, Lucien?**" Lucien: "**this is just getting ridiculous.**"
   - **The invading army cackles at the Grandmaster, beating weapons against their chests and armor and roaring in the stands.**
81. **2:00:30 — Cranberry sauce.** "You have to fight now, go!" The turkey dragon's claws miss. Then **it unleashes a barrage of gigantic cranberry sauce from its mouth (acid damage), with a giant gobble roar that shakes the rocks and walls.**
   - **The red dragon is frightened.** The turkey puffs itself up, "massive, looking like the giant feather ball that it is," claws the ground in dirt, dust and cranberry sauce.
   - **The dragon flies away.**

### Aftermath — the Arbiter taken (2:03:05)
82. **2:03:05 — The Acolytes win.** With "their champion now gone," the Acolytes fight back harder and **overpower the invaders, chopping goblins and dragonborn down to size.** The Arbiter is still holding the Tarrasque, sweat pouring down his face.
83. **2:03:59 — The Arbiter is swallowed.** Arbiter: "**Grandmaster! I can't hold it anymore!**" He loses control. **The Tarrasque bites down and swallows him whole, carrying him in its mouth.** He is visibly not dead, still alive inside.

- **Age and build:** 93 years old, apparently a halfling ("halfling power slide," "small, not-so-big fighter").

- **Style:** Southern-styled ("son," "southern discomfort"). Has spikes on his shoulder armor.
