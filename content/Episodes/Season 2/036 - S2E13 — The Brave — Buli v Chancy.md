## What happens
### Cold open — the "fog"

### Opening — the Dread Fortress kitchen
2. **0:00:49** In the Dread Fortress, the Arbiter looks for the Grandmaster, who is **once again absent from the throne room**. A warm, gooey, sugary smell "seduces" him down the hall. He is "easily seduced by good smelling foods." The Arbiter: "I don't even bother with perfume. I just spray like bacon or cookies on me."
3. **0:01:13** He finds her **in the kitchen**, among ovens and pots, hovering over her latest batch of **chocolate chip cookies for the Acolytes tonight**.
   - The spread also includes **red velvet, sugar dough, confetti, and Easter sugar cookies with "a little chickadee in the middle."**
   - Some cookies are store-bought: "I didn't have enough time to make all of them."
   - She wears **an apron that says "Grandmaster", not "grandma."**
   - **None are for the champions.** "Even though I enticed them last time and then they decided to discard them."
4. **0:03:11** **The Arbiter tries to swipe a cookie and fails** (sleight of hand 9). **She smacks his hand hard.** "You aren't one of them." "Why do they get cookies and I don't? I work here." She tells him he can eat after work.
5. **0:03:44 — Doug.** The Arbiter asks whether Doug got a cookie. Dogs can't have sugar or chocolate, but "**he did get a raw egg earlier**." The Arbiter: "**Fucking Doug.**" She offers him a raw egg. "I don't want an egg, but I do want to watch people fight to the death."
6. **0:04:07** She takes off her oven mitts and apron, dusts off, puts on her **crown**, and asks repeatedly whether she looks presentable. **The Arbiter casts Prestidigitation to throw the flour off her.**
7. **0:04:43** The Arbiter slices a rift in space and time and introduces "**Buli the Brave versus Chancy!**"

13. **0:09:05** The two have never crossed paths. Buli: "Usually so much death when Buli finished, there's no one left to tell tale." Chancy: "I'm starting to think you're a liar." The Grandmaster stands between them: "Bard, no music yet."

### The arena — the ruined castle by the sea
14. **0:09:48** The Arbiter opens a portal; a spring breeze blows through, and they land on **a cliff above the water**.
15. **0:10:14** The Grandmaster's description:
    - "A place I'll be honest, **I don't come to very often**."
    - A picturesque seaside with salty air, and **a castle in ruins**: pieces everywhere, broken staircases, and **a deep well that echoes, uncovered**.
    - A high point about 20 ft up. The ruins are mostly climbable but unstable.
16. **0:11:11 — The legend.** "This used to be a beautiful castle where **a lovely maiden** stayed… **there was supposed to be a nice little wedding here, but it never happened.**" She **pulls sheets down over a few portraits**: "You don't need to know about any of that."
17. **0:11:49** She flies up to **a clear arena of wooden stands in the sky**, full of Acolytes. They chant the champions' names ("adorable, I mean fierce") and beg for songs.
18. **0:12:33 — The Grandmaster's speech:** "Everything you do, you do it for the eyes that will tell your story… for the eyes here **who will continue to echo and carry out your legacy**." She sits **next to Doug** high in the sky.
19. **0:13:23** "Dungeons and dice and deathmatches." **Placement:** Chancy starts on the side of the cliff. **Buli starts inside the castle ruins.** They can't see each other.
20. **0:14:02** The Acolytes award **each champion one hero point**.
21. **0:14:56 — Gerald, the Box of Chaos.**
    - The Acolytes summon it. **A purple box with pink and yellow flames slams down from the sky**, and **a small shadowy figure works his way out the side with a small claw**.
    - It lands **at the bottom of a 10-ft drop below the cliff**. It stays in one place and either champion can use it.
    - Using it means sacrificing something, as a free action.
22. **0:16:39** Bec makes "a small little secret roll."
23. **0:16:46 — Initiative.** Chancy goes first (18). Buli gets 14 even with advantage, and claims he is letting Chancy go first: "**Buli is fair**… so that when I destroy you, it will be that much sweeter!"
24. **0:17:30 — The intimidation spat.**
    - Chancy: "Come out, come out wherever you are… you're probably just hiding from me because you're too scared."
    - Intimidation roll-off: **Chancy 29, Buli 9** ("I'm just saving everything for later"). "Your words cut and slice across this field."
    - **Buli has disadvantage on attacks this round.** "I will win even with all disadvantage. Use more, more, more!"

"**If I can't see him, he can't see me.**" Stealth 5: Chancy can't see Buli's eyes, but could possibly see the rest of him.

54. **0:52:03 — The sacrifice.** Buli needs his weapons, so **he takes off all his clothes, including a fur pelt cloak**. "I don't need this anymore. It's very hot anyways. I'm very hot."
