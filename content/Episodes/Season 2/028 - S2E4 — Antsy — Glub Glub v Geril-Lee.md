## What happens
### Opening — the Grandmaster's chambers
1. **0:00:00** Drew opens with "Welcome back to the Thunderdome. I'm sorry, the Dread Fortress." He then sets the scene: the subterranean castle in a field of lava, deep underground, and the Grandmaster's chambers.
2. **0:00:43 — The portrait.** Elusia stands studying an old oil-style painting. She **takes three steps back and throws a dagger that lands dead between the portrait's eyes.** The painting shows a being very like her with vast open blue wings.
3. **0:01:13** She asks whether the Arbiter knows who it is. He cocks his head ("Maybe if I stand upside down") and says he doesn't. Elusia: "**No, of course you wouldn't, no, because you haven't been here that long.**" She explains it is **her great-great-grandfather Skypiercer**: "**He's an ugly motherfucker, so don't tell me that I look like him.**"
4. **0:01:58** The Arbiter "assumed you were kind of just always in existence." Elusia explains the birds and the bees: "When a Mr. Skypiercer and a Mrs. Skypiercer love each other very dearly, they come together and they make a baby Skypiercer." The Arbiter: "**You know what I understand, Grandmaster, is deathmatches.**"
5. **0:02:43 — The legacy.** She hated her ancestor, "but gosh darn, did he enjoy a good fight." She has been **craving more blood**, remembering "**growing up in the arena where I every day battled and practiced preparing for the legacy that would be given to me.**"
6. **0:03:09** The Arbiter agrees "our quota of blood has not been met lately." She wants "someone to lose a limb or a finger or an ear at least." He jokes that a lost ear could make a painting of her. She asks for someone "whose blood would be worthy to make of my own portrait."
7. **0:03:44** The Arbiter announces **Glub Glub versus Geril-Lee**.

17. **0:10:25** The Arbiter to the Acolytes: "It has been weeks since we've seen real bloodshed… I promise you tonight, chaos will reign supreme."

### Round 2

38. **0:35:25 — The rage.** "**Glubglub! Boros action! RAGE!**" He froths at the mouth, and even the little cannon is afraid as the veins bulge across his pectorals. The crowd screams his name. One club smash hits (10). He drags the club along the ground with blood in his eyes and smashes again (12, halved by Uncanny Dodge to 6). "**Rub-a-dub-glub, as they say.**" The crowd chants "**Rub-a-dub-glub-glub!**" Glub Glub: "Ankles hurt though, not lying."
