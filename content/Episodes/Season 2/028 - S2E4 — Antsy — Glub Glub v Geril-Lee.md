**Glub Glub**, self-proclaimed smartest hobgoblin of his tribe, faced **Geril-Lee**, a small and apparently terrified fighter, in the old hunting grounds of [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]]'s family. What began as a solemn tribute to bloodline and legacy turned into a vulture-sound contest, a splinter emergency, a blessed walrus butthole and a sequence of escalating assaults on one hobgoblin's undercarriage. It is also, somehow, a story about a lonely genius who finally made a friend.

## The Story

### The Portrait

The night opened with a slip of the tongue: "Welcome back to the Thunderdome. I'm sorry, the Dread Fortress." Deep underground, in her chambers within [[The Dread Fortress|the Dread Fortress]], the Grandmaster stood studying an old oil painting of a being much like her, with vast open blue wings. She took three steps back and threw a dagger that landed dead between its eyes.

[[The Arbiter|The Arbiter]] cocked his head ("Maybe if I stand upside down") and admitted he didn't know who it was. It was her great-great-grandfather Skypiercer, she explained, adding, "He's an ugly motherfucker, so don't tell me that I look like him." When the Arbiter confessed he'd assumed she had simply always existed, she gave him a brisk talk on the birds and the bees. He countered that what he understood was deathmatches.

She had hated her ancestor, she said, "but gosh darn, did he enjoy a good fight." She was craving blood, remembering a youth spent battling and practicing every day for the legacy that would be hers. The Arbiter agreed the blood quota hadn't been met lately. The Grandmaster wanted someone to lose "a limb or a finger or an ear at least," someone whose blood would be worthy of her own portrait. The Arbiter announced the match: Glub Glub versus Geril-Lee.

### The Champions

Glub Glub introduced himself with pride and a hint of sorrow: "Me name am Glubglub, smartest of Hobgoblin tribe, smarter than Chieftain. Me make wheel, me make claw, but Glubglub make no friend." Geril-Lee, by contrast, seemed to think there had been a mistake and asked where she was. That exasperated the Arbiter, who said they'd had "a whole conversation in the portal on the way here. You're embarrassing me in front of the Grandmaster."

The Grandmaster walked between them, a hand on each shoulder. Glub Glub eyed his opponent's legs ("Kneecaps on small one look like clockwork. I break and make new"), but she forbade bloodshed on the sacred chamber stones, which were hard to clean. Glub Glub pledged to "challenge everyone for Arbiter Birdlady." The Grandmaster sized up Geril-Lee too: "Sweet and timid in nature, but something tells me a little more fierceness lurks below on this surface."

For an arena she chose [[The Grandmaster's great-grandfather's old hunting grounds|her great-grandfather's old hunting grounds]], near the waterfall, so the roar would fill the champions' ears. The Arbiter gathered magical energy with great ceremony, then simply ripped a portal open with one finger.

### Into the Hunting Grounds

The champions arrived on either side of a broken stone bridge over a treacherous river, among old trees that "have seen many battles." High above the treetops, [[The Acolytes|the Acolytes]] chanted and did the wave roughly five million times. The Grandmaster warned that she would end either of them herself if they embarrassed her bloodline. Then she flew to her giant throne, beside an equally large one where [[Doug the Pug|Doug]] the pug lay, "yet to be determined if today is a bones day or not."

The first chaos arrived with a caw. The champions were too close to a nest, and both parent birds attacked. The Arbiter had to correct the Grandmaster: they were vultures, because "Our crow guy was indisposed… It's Terry. He was out of business. It's, you know, the recession." A vulture-call contest followed. Glub Glub produced something closer to a chicken, while Geril-Lee's cry was so high-pitched nobody could hear it. Doug lost his mind anyway, and Glub Glub won by default.

Then the timid fighter showed her teeth. Muttering "I'm scared," Geril-Lee backflipped across the gap in the bridge and went for Glub Glub's throat with two daggers. "Glubglub feels blade on skin. Feel good," he said. Her second dagger slipped, but she recovered with a promise to drink his blood for breakfast. When he called that unsanitary, she replied, "It is in my village." His great club came down hard, but she twisted away from the worst of it.

Glub Glub proved himself a scientist as well as a mathematician (the Grandmaster offered "Math Titian" for his gravestone). He planted a wooden flag and hammered it into **Boom Boom**, a little wooden-legged cannon that spat flame. Then he pulled down his "Magic rock goggles!", which were sticks with no lenses. The Acolytes were delighted enough to grant both champions their favor.

### Vultures, Lightning and the Box

The crowd chanted for chaos, and the Grandmaster revealed that [[The Box of Chaos|the Box of Chaos]] was hidden somewhere in the arena. She took care to separate it from "the no-no box. We do not touch the no-no box." The good box burned with pink and yellow flames and granted something grand in exchange for a sacrifice. The vultures dove ("Oh, sky black chicken"), and then a storm rolled in, battering both fighters with lightning.

Glub Glub ran for a tree, Boom Boom trotting after him: "Everyone know, safest place of lightning under tree… It's science." Geril-Lee, finding this stupid, sheltered under the bridge. As they fled, both spotted something shimmering in the waterfall: the Box of Chaos, underwater and still burning. Up in the stands, Doug took a die gently in his mouth and rolled far better than the Arbiter. "Someday, someday I'm going to kill that dog with my… If he wasn't already dead."

Geril-Lee slid under the bridge and landed a perfect strike on Glub Glub's ankles, then followed with punches to his belly. She stuck out her tongue to show off its eight piercings, which failed to frighten him. In answer he flew into a frothing rage that scared even Boom Boom, and smashed into her twice. The Acolytes took up his new chant: "Rub-a-dub-glub-glub!" When a vulture's beak couldn't pierce his rage-hardened hide, it pooped on him and flew off. "Still smell better than most people I know," he said. In the stands, the Arbiter finally beat Doug at rolling, for the first time in about twenty-five tries. "Ah, suck it, Doug!"

### The Butt Tickler

Geril-Lee asked whether Glub Glub wore pants. Learning it was "Loincloth only," she summoned an invisible servant whose sole job was to tickle him beneath it whenever she attacked. The Grandmaster approved: "You may summon the butt tickler." Glub Glub, nervous, asked for "Only first knuckle. Please," and explained that "Glub Glub was at college. It was experimental time."

Distracted and conflicted ("Glubglub, very mixed feelings right now"), Glub Glub ordered Boom Boom to fire and swung wildly. Then disaster struck: he got a splinter from his own club. The Grandmaster was moved. "Splinters are unbearable," she declared, and the match paused so he could be treated.

### The Box and the Beasts

When the fight resumed ("Blows have been stricken, balls have been tickled," the Arbiter recapped), Geril-Lee made for the waterfall. Glub Glub's attempt to stop her went badly. She tightened the servant's grip on him, healed herself in the shallows, then cut off her long raven hair ("I'm gonna take my long and luscious raven hair and Mulan it") and threw it into the Box. The flames swallowed it, and out floated a **Gray Bag of Tricks**, a bag of fuzzy balls that turn into creatures when thrown.

Glub Glub freed himself with a very careful punch that narrowly missed his own testicles and dispersed the servant ("Balls free! Yes!"). Then he focused all his magic into a tiny bone and created **Bony**: a little skeleton with big eye sockets and no jaw, so it seemed to smile forever.

> **Glub Glub:** "Glubglub only friend, Bony."

The chaos escalated. Doug barked so wildly that the forest echoed with magic, and both champions transformed for a round: Geril-Lee into a panther, Glub Glub into a monstrous walrus ("That was Glubglub's nickname in college"). The panther pounced, missed, and landed on a pebble as though she'd meant to. The walrus rolled lazily into the water and mooned her. Wondering aloud whether his walrus form had a cloaca or a butthole "for science," he so impressed the Grandmaster that she blessed him. "Your butthole has been blessed." A giant emerald die then fell from the sky like a meteor, though nobody ever learned what it did, and a magical darkness swallowed the field.

### The Weasel

Blind, Geril-Lee pulled a weasel from her bag and sent it after Glub Glub's very low-hanging parts. "Even through the water and the rain, the funk abides," the Arbiter observed, as it ran figure-eights around his legs. Glub Glub answered with showmanship. He ripped off his loincloth and roared, "Are you not entertained?", then bent over and released a fart worthy of Valhalla. The Acolytes chanted his name, and he punched the "tainted warrior" dead. Then he realized his loincloth was gone and begged the Acolytes not to look.

### The Flood

The skies cleared, and the Arbiter reached the final threshold of chaos. He rose, flared his wings and raised the river over its banks. Both champions were swept downstream and battered against the rocks, and the emerald die washed up on the western shore.

In the rapids, Geril-Lee chivalrously declined to target his exposed "downstairs region" and went for the arteries in his legs instead. She landed two perfect strikes in a row. "Glubglub wants to call hacks," he protested. Bleeding, he spotted Bony floating downriver and snatched him up, stroking the shivering skeleton. Then he handed Bony a twig, and the two attacked "together as one… with the power of friendship."

### The Ant

For her finale, Geril-Lee conjured a single ant into Glub Glub's "short and curlies." As it made its way toward the blessed butthole, she cut him twice. Then, as the ant crawled in, she unleashed a flurry of blows and rolled two perfect strikes in a row. "No! No, I said first knuckle, not first ant." Glub Glub went down.

In the pouring rain, bleeding, he dropped to his knees before her and presented Bony with a shaking hand.

> **Glub Glub:** "You are superior fighter. Smarter, stronger, faster than Glubglub… Please, Boney, only friend. Geril-Lee, please teach him in your ways of master fighter."

Geril-Lee accepted, promising to use Bony "for my own devastation on other people." Then she politely extracted the ant, so he wouldn't die with it inside him. The Grandmaster lifted her from the rapids and into the sky, while the Arbiter dragged Glub Glub off by the ankle, his head bouncing off the rocks. Held up before the Acolytes, who showered the field with food and keys, Geril-Lee was crowned the winner. The Grandmaster's parting advice: "protect your buttholes as you move throughout the week."

## Dried Blood

Afterwards, Geril-Lee was said to feel confused and guilty about what she'd done. She also had a new responsibility: raising Bony, whom she vowed to teach to "aim better… Strike true, strike hard." At the table, Glub Glub's player reflected that whether he won or not, "he finally made that friend that he's always been looking for." The panel concluded the match had settled the whale question for good. As the Arbiter put it: "It's not about blood and death. It's about knowledge."
