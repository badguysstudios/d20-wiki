## What happens
### Opening: the Valentine's Day candy
1. **0:00:14** The Arbiter arrives and asks the Grandmaster for "a piece of that." She asks how he knew. He points out **all the wrappers**. Someone asks "Did the goblins tell you?" (speaker uncertain), and the GM says "**Did Thomas tell you? Doug!**" The Arbiter: "Who the fuck is Thomas?"
2. **0:00:40** The Grandmaster **quickly shuffles the last of her Valentine's Day candy behind her throne** and insists there is none left. If anyone sees her eating later, it's because she bought more.
3. **0:00:58** The Arbiter suggests she's "being a little childish." She asks if he is judging her intake. He backs down: "You are consuming the correct amount of candy at all times. I forgot."
4. **0:01:19** She tells him that, since he is **doing the auditing**, she bought it **50% off**: "**The Acolytes know you don't buy candy before Valentine's Day. You buy it after.**" He should have bought his own.
5. **0:02:03** In his excitement, **Doug pounces out from behind the throne**, and a pile of chocolate wrappers sprawls across the floor.
   - The Arbiter: "**he's an undead dog he can eat chocolate… what is it gonna do kill him again**."
   - The GM says the Arbiter doesn't like Doug. He says Doug doesn't like *him*, and "makes it very clear every time he rolls higher than me."
   - Doug gives the GM huge puppy eyes. She calls him "clearly the cutest thing and you're a monster." The Arbiter insists he's "cool" about it. GM: "Did you describe yourself as cool?"
6. **0:03:25 — Recruiting.** The Arbiter admits that this week's champions are "a little odd." It's getting harder to find people willing to fight to the death. He cold-calls, and people say no.
   - The GM reminds him that they **offer champions "all the money or wishes that you want"**, and "That's what we set aside some of the treasure for."
   - Arbiter: "**all of that money seems to be going to candy.**"
   - GM: "I want to start seeing that you're actually paying our actual champions as you're supposed to. We'll be following up on this later."

### The champions
7. **0:04:38** "For your Deathmatch pleasure… may I present **Dadrith versus Baron Voltágè**." The Arbiter stumbles over the name.
8. **0:05:02 — Dadrith:** "I am Dadrith, **dad of dads**. I bring the ideals of fatherhood all across the multiverse and I'm here today to teach you a lesson, son."
   - Baron: "Son?" The Arbiter: "He's not my father." Baron: "Clearly, I'm the elder here."
   - **GM: "I laid my father to rest like 800 years ago."** She looks at Dadrith for a moment ("are you my father?") and decides no.
9. **0:05:55 — Baron Voltágè:** he will "finally put down this **thorn in my side**. You've been here for too long. You're going to pay, Dadrith, for what you did to my strong hand today." Then: "**the elitist of electricity, the supplier of shock**, Baron Voltágè." (Transcribed "Baloron.")
   - GM: "Is he okay? Does he need some water?" Baron: "Only if you want to give me an unfair advantage against this whelp."
10. **0:06:45 — The pronunciation gag.** The Arbiter asks whether it's just "voltage."
    - Baron: "**No, it's Voltágè! It's said the exact way it's spelt!**"
    - The GM repeats "Voltage" dozens of times trying to get it right.
    - The Arbiter asks whether it's a family name, or German. The answer is "**It's Barovian**." The Arbiter: "have you ever been to Barovia, Grandmaster?"
    - GM: "Is it really just a fancy way of saying voltage?" Arbiter: "I think this guy might just be an electrician."
    - **Baron: "Enough! I did not come here to be belittled!"** The GM apologizes and promises respect.
12. **0:08:25** Asked about his battles, Dadrith says: "**everyone that tries to be a father that does not live up to the ideals of fatherhood faces my wrath.**" The GM likes "a good value to the heart."

### The arena: the eternal turtle
13. **0:08:49 — The vacation.** The GM has been talking about a vacation: "A little sand, a little beach… palm tree waving over a fruity coconut drink." The Arbiter proposes they "double dip": watch people fight to the death, "get a pina colada, dip our toes in the water." The GM waves him down the **hallway filled with doors**.
14. **0:09:32** The Arbiter rips a tear in space. Out waft the smell of sea salt and the sound of seagulls ("That wasn't a seagull sound." "Close enough.").
15. **0:09:49 — The GM's welcome.** "Welcome, dear champions, to **the endless sea**… for **the eternal turtle grants you passage** for now."
    - "The burden of two champions is quite heavy, so it will not bear the weight of both of you for long. One of you will have to kick the other off and **claim yourself king of this island** or queen or whatever title you want."
16. **0:10:34 — What the island offers.**
    - "**The shell of cover, the bones that crack beneath your feet**," and hidden treasures.
    - "**All that glitters is not always gold**, perhaps something worth even more to you in this sand."
    - Her wings open and blow up a poof of sand, and she flies up.
17. **0:11:13 — The Acolytes** circle overhead, chanting, with **coconut drinks with tiny umbrellas in every color** (or crazy straws). They are sloshing drinks and putting on sunscreen.
    - GM: "Acolytes! Today, we bring you to the warmth of the sun… **Originally in the cold and ice and snow**, find yourself and seek the warmth of blood, of violence."
    - She takes her seat and **throws a bone to Doug**.

### The Box of Chaos and the burning sand
19. **0:12:23 — The Box of Chaos.** The Arbiter snaps his fingers. **A large pink box with yellow and pink flames** flips open and explodes onto the edge of the island. "The box provides magical items to champions… but it demands a sacrifice."
20. **0:13:29** The Acolytes have already triggered a chaos event. The GM lets **Dadrith roll it because he's been "impressing me with the puns."** For starting positions, **Baron lands right by the Box of Chaos**, and **Dadrith lands in the brush across the island**.
21. **0:14:57 — Chaos event: the floor is lava.**
    - A sizzle, then an ashy smell, then heat. "**It's your feet that are starting to burn.**" The sand is "so hot… you might as well call it lava."
    - Dex saves. **Baron uses Flash of Genius** to boost his. GM: "you've got a fancy sounding name, so I'm sold."
    - **Dadrith casts Silvery Barbs, which he plays as "the dad point."** Lights gather in his finger, and the point is meant to distract Baron. Baron still rolls 19 ("That's why you're the genius"). **Dadrith takes the advantage for himself.**
    - Both take a small initial burn. **The sand stays hot for two rounds** until "the sea breeze as our eternal turtle flies through the ocean" cools it.
22. **0:19:06** Baron **hops onto the Box of Chaos with a little trail of sparks** ("That's a cute hop. We like that."). Dadrith moves off the sand.
23. **0:19:19** Initiative goes to **Baron (23)**, with Dadrith on 7. The Acolytes award **one hero point each**. GM: "There is nothing that angers me more… than seeing a hero point wasted."

### Before round 1: the son is summoned
24. **0:20:24** **Dadrith spends his hero point immediately.**
    - He warns Baron off the box: "**do not touch that chest or I'll get very cross with you… Its contents are mine.**" He draws his crossbow.
    - He points at the sun: "**Sun, come forth.**" He casts **Summon Celestial (defender)** next to Baron. This is the son/sun pun. GM: "you are now commanding the sun as if it were your son."
25. **0:21:16 — The son's look.**
    - A beautiful, solar-colored celestial spirit that does a **slow-motion Fabio hair flick**.
    - It wears a deep-V shirt ("to the belly button"). **On the GM's suggestion, it switches to a big floral-print Hawaiian shirt.**
26. **0:22:35** The celestial readies its reaction to stop Baron opening the chest. Dadrith: "**Do not let him, son, open that chest.**"

### Round 1
27. **0:23:18 — Baron's power-up.**
    - "Cute, but I have no need for whatever meager trinket lie within this chest. **Now to begin my unnecessary power-up sequence!**"
    - He flexes his "little manlet shoulders." **His suit deploys piece by piece in slow motion, Iron Man-style**, as he casts **enlarge** on himself. He stands there making loud Dragon Ball Z noises. Someone calls out "His power is over 9,000!"
    - His artificer defensive field gives him 11 temporary HP.
28. **0:24:34** GM: "he said it was unnecessary, but ultimately the show really matters… **I'm putting my money on him.**"
    - The Arbiter wonders whether this is his final form. GM: "I've been watching some Dragon Ball Z, but I'm not that good with the commentary on it yet." Then: "He's 30,000!"
29. **0:25:17 — The newspaper.** Dadrith spins his hands as if winding something up, with a sound of warping paper, and **a rolled-up newspaper appears next to Baron**. This is a **5th-level spiritual weapon**.
    - GM: "Some of us are going to have a little childhood trauma flashbacks right now."
    - It hits (with advantage from Silvery Barbs). The force damage is mostly absorbed by Baron's temporary HP. Baron just keeps screaming while his armor keeps extending.
30. **0:28:01** Dadrith runs "down the spine" toward the chest, keeping off the sand. GM: "**A little crab walks out and then he stops because he's cooked.**" He jumps a stretch of sand with an Athletics check, shouting "**Daddy's coming!**"
31. **0:30:10 — The first dad joke (Word of Radiance).**
    - Dadrith: "**I learned today that you can't run through a campground. Do you know why, Baron?… But you can't run past tense.**" The radiance bursts on "tense."
    - He uses Silvery Barbs again ("No, no, no! Pass tense!"). **Baron still passes.** Sand explodes around him as he tanks it and stares.
    - Dadrith: "Come on, it was a good one!" GM: "You actually like these jokes?" Dadrith: "**They are passed down from dad to dad. I travel the multiverse looking for the best ones.**"
32. **0:32:27 — The holy coconut.** The celestial pulls a **mace from under its Hawaiian shirt with a fuzzy coconut on the end**. All three swings glance off Baron's extending armor (AC 20). Baron: "Pathetic."
    - Dadrith: "**It's okay, son. I'm not angry. I'm just disappointed.**" "That hurts the most."
33. **0:35:02** GM: "I'm remembering a lot of these conversations. Maybe I should go see my therapist again."
    - **Arbiter: "You could always just go visit your dad. In the graveyard."** GM: "Not gonna, no, no. Arbiter, just roll your dice."
    - He rolls a 7: "suck it, Doug." He calls Doug "a monstrous dog." GM: "He's a dog."

### Round 2
34. **0:35:55 — A ménage à Voltágè.** Electricity courses over Baron's armor. "Some very cute tricks, but how about you try this on for size? **A menage a Voltágè!**"
    - He casts **mirror image** and splits into four. GM: "**four barons of enlarged size stomping around this tiny island**… This is insane and I love it."
35. **0:36:44 — Baron's hero point.** He uses it for a second spell. **The four Barons stretch their arms toward each other and lightning surges between them.** He punches up at the sky, the lightning arcs into a gathering cloud, and **Call Lightning** strikes Dadrith.
    - Dadrith saves, and the dice come up pitiful. **The GM, "having seen this show that you put on," lets him reroll one d10**: "That show deserves a bigger boom." It's still a small hit.
    - He renews his defensive field.
36. **0:39:48 — The Arbiter's shoes.** "Dadrith! Here. Borrow these." The Arbiter **lifts his black-and-white referee robes, pulls off his black Chuck Taylors and throws them to Dadrith**, giving him +10 ft movement: "You're way too slow. Good pair of chucks and I'll fix that right up." GM: "I hope those don't smell."
37. **0:40:33 — "Dispel is Dispel."** Dadrith takes a track runner's stance, sprints and kicks back sand, and draws his shield and mace. He fakes an attack, then casts **Dispel Magic** with the pun "Dispel is Dispel" ("this spell is dispel").
    - **The Acolytes throw popcorn.** The GM polls them, nat 1 or nat 20, and enough say 20 that he gets advantage. GM: "**I'm now doubting the judgment of my acolytes, but they have spoken.**"
    - The Arbiter turns it into a spell-attack roll-off. **Dadrith wins, and the mirror images shatter.**
38. **0:43:35** Baron: "Oh, what is this? My unnecessary charge-up sequence?" Dadrith: "**Now it's time that you stop reflecting and actuate.**"
39. **0:43:47 — "Make me proud."** The newspaper misses. Dadrith tells the son: "come on, son. Make me proud." **One coconut finally lands** with a hollow ring.
    - Dadrith: "I'm proud of you, son." He gains temporary HP from the bond.
    - **The son: "Yes, Papa."** GM: "It's a beautiful moment, but we're done with it… Enough crying on this field." The Arbiter rolls a 12.

### Round 3
40. **0:46:42 — Iron Man flight.** Baron: "**I'm going to make you pay for wasting so much of my time, Dadrith!**"
    - **His armor opens at the back to reveal a thruster crackling with lightning** as he casts **fly**. GM: "Hello, Iron Man."
    - He renews his barrier and flies straight up 30 ft.
    - **Opportunity attacks:** Dadrith swings with a hooking motion, "**You stay here!**", and the son swings too. GM: "Will the son surpass the father?" Both miss. **The swing kicks sand into Dadrith's own face.**
41. **0:48:46 — The crossbow sacrifice.** The sand has cooled.
    - Dadrith: "**I'm glad that's off… my chest.**" He tosses his crossbow into the Box of Chaos. GM: "I was half worried that you were going to toss your son in."
    - The Arbiter, to the box: "**This is what you were born for. This is why I created you.**"
    - **The son lends advantage**: "Come on, Papa. You can do it." The rolls are high.
42. **0:50:46** The box slams shut, then rips open in a burst of flames that give no heat, just a flash of light. **Out rise a pair of winged boots** (flight, 30 ft, up to four hours).
    - Dadrith: "**I'm sorry, referee. I will be wearing the boots instead.**"
    - Arbiter: "I can't believe you rolled flying boots after he started flying." Dadrith: "You can fly! I can fly too!"
43. **0:51:46** Dadrith flies up face to face with Baron. His mace clinks off.
44. **0:52:30 — The spanking.** The newspaper swings up to "try and spank you."
    - GM: "Where would you like to be spanked, Baron?" Baron: "It's up to the attacker."
    - **Dadrith has it hit Baron's feet**, for solid force damage.
45. **0:54:15 — The critical coconut.** The angel (it has 40 ft flight) lands two coconut strikes, **one a nat 20**, for about **40 radiant damage**. "**I'm proud of you, I really am.**" The angel: "**Thank you, Papa.**"
    - Baron: "**Oh, you little wimp.**"
    - GM: "It's a small… waterfall going down into the sand, dying all this crimson down below. It's fine… **it's just a flesh wound.**"
46. **0:56:26 — The break.** The Arbiter needs a break after "that nasty critical." The GM wants a refill of her coconut drink. **She looks at her coconut, realizing coconuts were the weapon of choice: "Maybe I'll go get a pineapple instead."**

### After the break
47. **0:56:52** "Welcome back, Acolytes, to the arena for the conclusion…"
    - **Name trouble again.** GM: "Like when I say I go to **Targè. Instead of Target.**" Arbiter: "Volage, eh?" Baron: "Voltage!"
    - GM: "They will call you Mr. Baron." "**Baron Voltage!**" The Arbiter gets a pen to write it down phonetically.
48. **0:58:07** Dadrith: "**I have not figured out how to say your name. And then you did that sparky thing and it struck me. Lightning struck me.**" GM: "That one flew right over my head."
49. **0:58:45 — Pre-interviews.**
    - Baron: "**I'm going to start tucking my entrails back in, and then I promise you… things are going to get spicy.**"
    - GM: "I told you early on that I was rooting for you, and I don't want you to not only disappoint me, but embarrass me." **She grants Baron Bless for two rounds: "You have my favor… Don't make me regret it."**
50. **0:59:34** Dadrith: "Fatherhood is strong. However… I got overwhelmed in this deathmatch, so I believe that I need to change my approach."
    - He tells the newspaper: "**you were never needed… Be a father instead!**" **It transforms into a wizened father with cupped hands and spectacles**: "Just tell me how you're feeling. I'm here to help you with my wise words."
    - GM: "**This is what I like to see in the arena. It's our chance to truly find ourselves, to ground, to correct, to build in strength.**"
51. **1:00:47** GM to the Arbiter: "**Do you know that four comes after three?**"

### Round 4: the soul sacrifice
52. **1:01:01 — The pep talk.**
    - Dadrith: "Baron! I believe in you, Baron. Hit me as hard as you can."
    - Baron: "**Going to, but not because you want me to, because I want to.**" Dadrith: "Of course, this is your choice."
    - Baron: "It is my choice, and I'm going to follow through with it."
    - Dadrith: "Make me proud… **You make me proud no matter what, even if you do miss.**"
    - GM: "This is very rare that you get such a great pep talk from your opponent."
53. **1:01:52** Baron coughs up blood and tucks his guts back into his armor. **He smirks at Dadrith through the blood on his chin, spreads his arms, and drops backward, plummeting toward the chest.**
    - Dadrith: "**I would not be doing you any favors if I made this easier on you, Baron.**" His opportunity attack misses "with a proud look." The angel misses too ("a little lasso of… coconuts").
54. **1:03:47 — The soul.** Baron: "**Grandmaster, I offer you the most valuable thing in my possession, my blackened soul!**"
    - **He dives straight into the Box of Chaos.** A flash of black lightning crackles over it, a thundercrack sounds, and the clouds darken.
    - GM: "**That is a hefty price to pay.**" He gets double advantage.
55. **1:05:15 — The choice.** Because he sacrificed his soul, the Arbiter offers a choice. ("Grandmaster, are you sure we have to give him these items?" "We have to. They've earned it. He gave his soul.")
    - The choice: a **belt of cloud giant strength** or a **ring of three wishes**.
    - He already wears **gloves of ogre strength**, so he takes the **Ring of Three Wishes**. GM: "Be ever careful… As with any wish, the wording must be particular."
56. **1:06:34 — The burning.** The lid slams shut: darkness and flames.
    - "For the first time, you didn't feel the heat from the outside, but inside you feel it burning. It almost **burns your soul clean out of your body**… Your soul just flies out the back of your body… **you no longer have a soul**."
    - The box spits him onto the sand in a puff of flame, with the ring on his hand.
57. **1:07:18** GM: "the Box of Chaos has perhaps introduced enough chaos… **Bury the box deep, deep into the ground. Or in the turtle, the eternal turtle.**" The box snaps shut and dissipates. (Aside from the GM: "Oh, ask for all the Valentine's Day candy.")
58. **1:07:57 — First wish.** Baron wishes to regain all hit points.
    - **He crawls out with his armor blackened and seeping black smoke. His wounds are filled in by "this black pulsating goo."**
    - "**Now, all the pain you have inflicted on me, Dadrith, I will return to you tenfold!**" He flies 30 ft away and renews his barrier. He is now at full health.
59. **1:09:43 — Killing with kindness.** Dadrith: "I will keep testing you then."
    - The father-newspaper offers wise words ("you don't have to be so aggressive with your exchanges"), and they hit for solid force damage. GM: "the classic killing them with kindness."
    - The angel flies out "to bring divine justice to this very angry boy" and lands two hits, 34 radiant. GM: "**Your Celestial is getting better each time. He's learning… I'm so proud of you!**"
60. **1:12:39 — The mace finally lands.** Dadrith flies beside Baron, "just… be there, present."
    - "**I'm so proud of your growth now that you've sold your soul and you have a ring. You're quite amazing. I unfortunately have to hit you with my mace.**"
    - **Nat 20 on his first mace hit of the night**, with Divine Strike fire, for 23. The GM notes his many nat 20s. Baron holds concentration on fly. GM: "You don't look very good."

### Chaos events: the storm and the Arbiter's gravity
61. **1:15:32 — The storm.** The Arbiter rolls a 2, but first another chaos event fires.
    - The sun fades. Thunder rolls "like drums rolling over and over." **A torrential downpour and dark clouds come overhead, and lightning strikes both fighters.**
    - **The GM says "Voltágè" confidently at last**, and gives him advantage: "you are familiar and work with lightning."
    - Dadrith asks about his magic resistance. GM: "**It is not magic. This is just nature doing its thing.**" Dadrith: "It's primordial."
    - Dadrith Silvery Barbs Baron again ("I can't let you off easy"). **Baron counters with his halfling Lucky trait** ("offling ratio trait" in the transcript).
    - Both take half damage. "This storm rages on like the Acolytes cheering… energized by their intensity."
62. **1:18:52 — Doug's roll.** The GM: "Let us see what **Doug** will roll." It's an 8, "enough, Arbiter, for you to bring your own chaos."
63. **1:19:07 — Gravity.** The Arbiter cracks his knuckles: "You're feeling a little too light on your feet."
    - **He increases the gravity. The atmosphere presses down, and the eternal turtle sinks into the ocean.** Con saves; Baron's Flash of Genius isn't enough, and both fail.
    - **Both plummet into the sea**, unable to fly under the weight. **Shark fins circle them.** "The two of you look into each other's eyes and you realize this is pretty fucked up."
    - Initiative is rerolled: Baron 11, Dadrith 4.
64. **1:21:14** GM: "**These sharks, they do know the smell of blood. They'll hold their line, but if you cross into it, you may become a snack.**"
    - (The shark tokens don't show on the map. GM: "They're invisible… a very special kind of shark… known for their camouflage.")
65. **1:22:48 — The son is lost.** Concentration saves.
    - **The celestial dives into the water under the gravity and the sharks rip it to shreds.**
    - The spiritual weapon also dissipates under the force.

### The last round in the water
66. **1:23:58 — The 8th-level lightning.** Baron: "**It would seem you're at a bit of a tactical disadvantage now.**"
    - Darkness envelops him and his eyes crackle with lightning as he channels the storm. **He uses the ring to cast Call Lightning at 8th level, with an extra die from the raging storm.**
    - Dadrith fails the save and takes 51 lightning damage. He is still up. GM: "He's been holding back."
67. **1:25:50** Dadrith: "that was pretty shocking. **Let's heat things up.**"
68. **1:26:29 — Into the depths.** Baron's artificer teleport boots (called "Boots of Trash" at the table, displayed only as "Boots") teleport him **15 ft down into the water**. GM: "This is a bold strategy, Cotton."
    - **The sharks are none the wiser.** In the murky deep water, his stealth is good.
69. **1:28:47 — Dad vision.** The Arbiter wants a real perception roll: "Fuck passive perception… Rolling dice is fun." **Nat 20.**
    - Dadrith: "**With my dad vision, I'll keep a track of you!**"
    - Arbiter: "You Doctor Strange this, you see 14 million different outcomes."
70. **1:29:32 — The grill.** **Coals appear in Dadrith's hands, fly to Baron and wrap around him, and an ethereal grill comes down.** He casts **5th-level heat metal** on Baron's metal armor, for 27 fire damage. **Baron falls.**
71. **1:30:54 — Last words.** GM: "What final words slip from your lips as you begin to fall?"
    - **A gargled "DAMN YOU, [Dadrith?]!"** (transcribed "DAMN YOU DON'T WORK!"). **The heat metal boils away enough water to make a bubble** around him so he can shout.
72. **1:31:39 — Divine intervention.** **Dadrith's face goes stoic and sad. "Surrounded by salt water, he adds to the salt water with his own tears."**
    - "**This isn't right.**" He calls to all the dads and uses Divine Intervention: "**let this one live and take me instead.**"
    - **The GM ("You're a dad. This feels for you") widens the odds to 20 numbers.**
    - The Acolytes pick the range. Pesh abstains because the Baron gave his soul to the Grandmaster. Chat users Dreadlord and Dag both pick 25–44.
    - Prayer: "**Moxie and the Eternal Fathers protect me instead of Baron and guide Baron on a better path.**" He also asks them to "guide him and teach him."
    - **The roll is 63, and the intervention fails.** Dadrith nods: "He's taking another one. Another son."
73. **1:34:58 — The ring.** Dadrith sees **the Ring of Three Wishes glint on Baron's lifeless hand** as the body drifts down, and swims for it.
    - The bubble has grown enough to speak underwater. Dadrith: "**Come. You are someone's son. Hold on. Don't give up.**"
    - Baron makes his save and is conscious enough for one last act.
74. **1:36:58 — The scowl and the clone.** **Baron gives Dadrith one final scowl. The ring glows briefly, he coughs blood into the water once more, and he drifts away into the darkness.**
    - "**But unknown to Dadrith and possibly even the Grandmaster and the Arbiter, 120 days later, one of the sharks… beaches itself on top of the turtle.**" It gasps, convulses and goes still.
    - "**A robotic fist bursts out of the shark's stomach. And then a cloned Baron Voltágè crawls forth out of the shark.**" He used his third wish to **cast clone, with the shark as the container**. Baron: "**Ah, Dadrith!**"
    - GM (narration): "**Your survival will be kept in secret till the two of you meet again in total surprise, I imagine.**"
75. **1:38:31 — The memento.** Believing Baron dead, the Grandmaster waves the clouds away.
    - She orders the Arbiter: "**Go to the beach and lay down a memento. Something of kindness to remember. Our dear, kind, brave, Baron Voltágè.**"
    - Arbiter: "**Was his name Bartholomew?**" GM: "No. Not even close." She wants it phonetically spelled underneath.
    - Arbiter: "**There's a lot of people that die in this arena. I'm supposed to remember the names?**"
76. **1:39:33 — The GM's speech on remembrance.** "I don't want your excuses… Their names may be difficult, but today they fought. And **you will remember his name**. On that sand… his blood covers those sands."
    - To the Acolytes: "**We may not be able to say it with the perfect accent, but we will say his name. We will remember it for every champion that enters my halls, for every bone broken and blood spilled on sand, dirt, and in water… for no fight is unseen by us all.** And we will take their hopes, their inspirations, their intensity with us as we go through our lives."
77. **1:40:54 — The victory.** Dadrith: "That was just so well said. Thank you."
    - The GM: "your heart was in it through and through." Dadrith uses his boots to rise before the Acolytes.
    - Dadrith: "**I know that your dads love you.**"
    - GM: "**May this be a fight to remember that we fight for what we love, the values that we hold, and that we never forget who we are.**" She presents him "in a bit of a Jeopardy sort of way."
    - Dadrith: "**You can all call me Dad!**" The crowd chants "Dad! Dad!"
    - "Welcome and good night to D20 Deathmatch!"

## Dried Blood (after-show)
### In-world content (canon, Dried Blood)
- **1:48:09 — Drew's epilogue** (in-world narration, Dried Blood). Pesh begins: "[the clone] of Baron Voltágè is probably property of Grandmaster and Arbiter now." Drew then narrates:
  - "**as the Arbiter goes to place your headstone on the island of the Grandmaster**,
  - "He's going to **retrieve the Box of Chaos** and he's going to **take it back to his laboratory** where he is going to **take your soul and put it into a metal like automaton… golem… for his army**.
  - "So he's going to take your black soul and shove it into this metallic body that he's created."
- Bec: "You may have to fight yourself in the future."
- Dread: "technically you became a father at that point… you were the first soul and then there's the second soul."

### Out-of-character (creator notes)
- Pesh played Baron as a "**Saturday morning cartoon villain trope**." Bec saw chat calling him "**young Frankenstein energy, crazy scientist**."
- Pesh was also on a special-event **2v2 episode** that aired on Dread's channel, not D20's, where he did a **Macho Man Randy Savage** voice the whole time. Drew didn't realize Pesh is **Australian**. Pesh: "I used to live in Canada, so I could blend in."
- This was Dread's first time. He decided on a cleric, hit the random name generator, and got "Dadrith," so the character became all about dad stuff. Quote: "you can easily design a character to win, but it's better to design a character that's going to be fun and entertaining."
- Pesh built Baron with high AC and saves to tank "five or six attacks every turn." It was his first level 11 artificer. **Clone was near the top of the alphabetical list of 8th-level spells** in a D&D Beyond tab, and showed "bright green."
- **Box of Chaos history (Drew):** "we've had people use their own blood, cut off an antler, give up weapons or… precious mementos. **We've never had someone jump into the box and sacrifice their own soul.**"
- Dread on Dadrith's family: "**I actually want to know who Dadrith's child is… How many kids he actually has… that secret backstory that you'll find out later.**" Bec: "more details to come."
- Dread had planned Divine Intervention for drama, on himself or on Baron.
- Favorite moment for Bec and Drew: Baron's "not because you told me to" exchange. Bec expected a Terminator-style moment from the shark clone; Drew compared it to an after-credits *Pirates of the Caribbean* scene.
- Dread: "two will enter, one will leave… no, two entered and two left." Bec: "it's very cool that that match isn't over." "And it isn't."
- **Pesh: Baron chose clone "out of spite… Won't let him know he saved my life."**
- Dread may homebrew a **"Domain of Parenthood"** cleric subclass.
- Bec on the candy roleplay: "is it role play if it's inspired by real events… I did not buy that much chocolate, I bought two bags."
- Final words:
  - Dread: "**Be good. Have fun. And, you know, be home by curfew… You can break curfew if you want. I'm not your dad.**" (Bec: "Are you trying to be a cool dad now?")
  - Pesh: "everyone just be good to each other… Han shot first."

## Who and what appears
### Characters
**[[Characters/Dadrith|Dadrith]]** (DreadedGM). A cleric, "dad of dads," who travels the multiverse spreading "the ideals of fatherhood" and collecting dad jokes "passed down from dad to dad."
- **Mission:** fathers who don't live up to fatherhood "face my wrath."
- **Faith:** prays to "**Moxie and the Eternal Fathers**" (deity names as heard). He has magic resistance and Divine Strike (fire).
- **Spells and powers:**
  - The "dad point" (Silvery Barbs).
  - The **rolled-up newspaper** (spiritual weapon), which later becomes a spectacled, wise-words father.
  - His "**son**," the summoned sun-celestial.
  - Word of Radiance delivered as a dad joke; Dispel Magic ("Dispel is Dispel"); heat metal as an ethereal grill.
  - "Dad vision."
- **Gear:**
  - Mace and shield.
  - A crossbow, sacrificed to the Box of Chaos.
  - **Winged boots** from the Box.
  - He was given the Arbiter's black Chuck Taylors, then said he'd wear the boots "instead."
- **In this episode:** he tried to give his own life to save Baron, and wept into the sea. He won. Calls the crowd "You can all call me Dad!"

**[[Characters/Dadrith's son|Dadrith's son, the celestial]]** (summoned).
- **Appearance:** solar-colored, Fabio hair, floral Hawaiian shirt.
- **Weapon:** a mace topped with a fuzzy "holy coconut."
- **Lines:** calls Dadrith "Papa."
- **Fate:** landed a crit; torn apart by the sharks when the gravity pulled it into the water.

**[[Characters/Baron Voltágè|Baron Voltágè]]** (TalesofPesh). Insists on the pronunciation ("said the exact way it's spelt"). The family name is "Barovian."
- **Titles and build:**
  - "The elitist of electricity, the supplier of shock."
  - **A halfling** (uses the halfling Lucky trait; "manlet shoulders").
  - An artificer in transforming Iron Man-like armor with a back thruster. Has a defensive field, Flash of Genius, gloves of ogre strength and teleport boots.
- **Grudge:** calls Dadrith a long-standing "thorn in my side" (unexplained).
- **In this episode:**
  - **Sacrificed his "blackened soul" to the Box of Chaos for the Ring of Three Wishes.** He no longer has a soul.
  - **Wishes used:** (1) full heal, after which black goo filled his wounds and black smoke poured from his armor; (2) an 8th-level Call Lightning; (3) **clone**.
  - Killed by heat metal underwater.
  - **120 days later, a clone burst from a beached shark on the turtle, unknown to Dadrith and possibly the GM and Arbiter.**
- **Dried Blood epilogue:** the Arbiter took Baron's soul from the Box to his laboratory and put it in a metal automaton/golem for his army.
- **The GM's favor:** she bet on him and granted him Bless.

**[[Characters/Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer]]** (Bec).
- **Candy:** hoards half-price post-Valentine's candy behind her throne.
- **Family:** "**I laid my father to rest like 800 years ago.**" She jokes about her therapist.
- **[[Characters/Doug the Pug|Doug:]]** defends him as "the cutest thing."
- **Champion pay:** wants to see champions actually paid out of the set-aside treasure.
- **Vacation:** wants a beach holiday.
- **Remembrance:** gave the speech on remembering every champion's name.

**[[Characters/The Arbiter|The Arbiter]]** (Drew).
- **Duties:** does the auditing and cold-calls champions.
- **Appearance:** wears **black-and-white referee robes and black Chuck Taylors**.
- **[[Characters/Doug the Pug|Doug:]]** feuds with him ("A monstrous dog").
- **Powers:** **increased the arena's gravity** and sank the turtle.
- **Names:** bad with them ("Bartholomew?"). He was ordered to lay a memento for Baron.
- **Dried Blood:** keeps a laboratory and is building an army.

**[[Characters/Doug the Pug|Doug]]**. The GM's **undead dog**; "he can eat chocolate… what is it gonna do, kill him again." He hides behind the throne with the candy wrappers. **The GM's chaos die is "what Doug will roll."** He doesn't like the Arbiter. The GM throws him a bone.

**[[Characters/Thomas|Thomas]]**. Named by the GM ("Did Thomas tell you?"); unidentified.

**[[Characters/Goblins|The goblins]]**. Possibly suspected of telling the Arbiter about the candy (speaker uncertain).

**[[Characters/The eternal turtle|The eternal turtle]]**. A giant turtle that swims "through the endless sea" with an island on its back. It "grants you passage for now" but can't bear two champions for long. It sank under the Arbiter's gravity.

**[[Characters/The sharks|The sharks]]**. They circle the fighters and "know the smell of blood." They hold their line but eat anything that crosses it. They shredded the celestial, and one became Baron's clone vessel.

### Places
- **[[Places/Elusia's throne room|Elusia's throne room]]**, with a **hallway filled with doors** leading to arenas.
- **[[Places/The endless sea|The endless sea]]** and **the island on the eternal turtle's back**: brush and foliage, scalding sand, bones underfoot, the shell for cover, hidden treasure, crabs. Baron's memento/headstone was laid on its beach ("the island of the Grandmaster," Dried Blood).
- **[[Places/The open ocean around the turtle|The open ocean around the turtle]]**: deep, murky water full of sharks.
- **[[Places/Barovia|Barovia]]**: Baron's family origin.
- **[[Places/The Arbiter's laboratory|The Arbiter's laboratory]]** (Dried Blood).

### Items
- **[[Lore/The Box of Chaos|The Box of Chaos]]**: pink, with yellow and pink flames. It gives items for sacrifices, and its fire burned Baron's soul out. It was buried/dissipated after use; in Dried Blood the Arbiter retrieves it.
- **[[Items/Ring of Three Wishes|Ring of Three Wishes]]** (all three charges used). A belt of cloud giant strength was offered and refused.
- Winged boots.
- The Arbiter's black Chuck Taylors.
- The GM's Valentine's candy; the Acolytes' umbrella coconut drinks.

### Lore statements
- Champions are promised "all the money or wishes that you want," paid from set-aside treasure.
- The Acolytes come from places of "cold and ice and snow" (as said in the GM's address).
- The Box of Chaos can take a soul.
