## What happens
### Opening — the Dread Fortress
1. **0:00:00** Intro: "Welcome to D20 Deathmatch! We have returned to the Dread Fortress!" A table gag: someone screams the intro out ("I could do this all fucking day"). The GM declares **Tuesday night screaming night** and "jots it down on her calendar."
2. **0:00:42** The Arbiter walks the halls of the Dread Fortress. His heels clack, servants scurry, **goblins chatter in the corners**, and the air is damp. He peeks into the Grandmaster's throne room: "Grandmaster, are you here this week?"
3. **0:01:07** The Grandmaster is pacing with her hands behind her back: "Where have you been?" She heard the clacking down the hall: "**Are you wearing tap shoes?**" He says they're **Italian leather**.
4. **0:01:39 — The dead man's shoes.** The Arbiter took the shoes from a man he killed, whose dying words were "**take care of my Italian leather shoes.**" A perception check (19) shows **metal pieces on the soles**. GM: "He's a liar. He's a dead liar, but he's a liar." The Arbiter calls the dead man "a shyster" and refuses to keep wearing them, so he takes them off and goes barefoot.
5. **0:02:30 — Shoe policy.** The GM rules that **shoes are required in the arena** ("spikes and gross stuff on the ground, blood… It's a safety thing. It's insurance."). The Arbiter snaps his fingers and puts on **vacation sandals**, also taken from a corpse: "**I have a fine collection of shoes from all the people I've killed.**" GM: "I don't want to know."
6. **0:03:34 — Introductions.** The Arbiter announces "**Ulfgar Greyblade versus Snoop & Dog.**" Snoop can't find where the Arbiter's voice is coming from ("I'm standing literally right here").
7. **0:04:02 — Snoop.** Snoop's own **fine Italian leather shoes have heels** to make him a couple of inches taller ("makes people think more of me"). "**Hi, I'm Snoop. This is my dragon dog. He's a silver dragon.**" It's hot in here, and he has never done this before. He says Dog is very well-versed in battle and "really the backbone of this partnership." Snoop relies on him for his day-to-day routine.
8. **0:05:10 — "How high are you?"** The GM means his height. Snoop hears the other meaning: **he ate magic mushrooms on the way in** and is "incredibly" high. "I'm really not sure where I am right now… the world takes care of everything." His height is **about four foot six and a half**. He stands on his toes for extra height. Arbiter: "Sounds about fighting height to me."

You may know me as Mcswiggins, the Everthirsty, the toughest son of a bitch in Drakboldor [?], and the pride of the drunken master.**" The GM points out her very large, bedazzled crown: "It's okay, dog, we'll get you a crown." He starts his introduction again from the top and loses track of whether he already said it.
10. **0:07:27 — The name mix-up.** "What is a Mcswiggins?" He sees "Ulfgar" written up somewhere and insists it's Mcswiggins. The GM sidebars: did the Arbiter write the wrong name? The Arbiter: "**This is what happens when you put out a general call for people to fight to the death**… their friends sign them up… I'm not in charge of the sign-up list… This guy's obviously drunk. He doesn't even know his own name."
11. **0:07:43 — Mushrooms.** Snoop wonders whether it's the purple mushrooms. Mcswiggins asks if he's still holding. The mushrooms are in **Dog's saddlebag**. Snoop explains: **purple mushrooms make you light and happy; red ones he takes right before a fight, and Dog says they help him fight better. "I think he calls it a blood frenzy?"** He gives Mcswiggins "the biggest one I have left. You're kind of a big guy." Mcswiggins is a broad dwarf of about **4'8"**, so he looks up only slightly, "at an angle."
12. **0:09:40** Mcswiggins asks Snoop, whom he takes for an audience member, about Dog's fighting style. The GM hushes it ("you don't have to give that away"). Snoop: "We're the ones, we're fighting. You and me, and Dog." Mcswiggins: "**I'm fighting two people? That hardly seems fair.**" Arbiter: "To be fair, one's not a person." The Arbiter promises to make it up to Mcswiggins afterwards "if you're not dead." Mcswiggins: "No one ever dies." GM: "Well, one of you will have to die tonight… challenge your opponent to draw their very last breath."

### The arena — a lost plane
13. **0:11:08** The Arbiter rips open a portal, and dank, moist, stale air flows out. **The ground is squishy like a membrane, crossed by vines**, and the air is hot and thick, "**as if you were walking through someone's fart**." The GM revises the image: "**it looks like you're walking on a testicle.**" Mcswiggins: "You should write poetry."
14. **0:12:20** The walls weave in curves, making **S's**. Their nooks hold **shelves that shimmer, but nothing is visibly on them**. GM: "I've decided to take you to an interesting plane. **Lost plane for those things that are lost.** But even here, they can be hard to find. So search around and perhaps you might be able to find that which has been forgotten."
15. **0:13:07** As she opens her wings and flies up, the fog briefly clears and the cool air feels heavenly. Above sit **the Acolytes, whose farts are falling down onto the arena**. A science check: farts are heavier than air, so they sink. GM: "**Welcome to the arena of lost farts.**" She promises fighters "short in stature" but epic fights, "and there's also **a dog that's not a dog**."
16. **0:14:37 — Is Dog a dog?** Snoop: "He's a dog. Look at him." Mcswiggins: "It's a very scaly dog." "Dogs are scaly. Why do I feel like the crazy person here?" GM: "a hairless, scaly dog." Dog: "Woof." **Doug the Pug**, seated next to the Grandmaster, barks back, and his whole body shakes when he barks.
17. **0:15:26** Snoop: "Why do I feel like I'm walking on testicles?" Arbiter: "Don't ask questions you don't want to know the answer to." GM: "Someone lost theirs." Mcswiggins: "Those are some big nuts."
18. **0:15:54** The Arbiter flies up: "**There are ponchos underneath your seats for those of you in the front row. Mind the splash zone.**" Both champions start in the S-pockets of the map, with no line of sight to each other. The Acolytes demand the Box of Chaos *and* a chaos event.
19. **0:17:12** The initiative loser rolls the chaos event. Mcswiggins gets 15 and Snoop & Dog 14.

## Who and what appears
### Characters
**[[Characters/Snoop|Snoop]]** (RunawayRobot_). A short adventurer, about **4'6½"**, who wears **heeled Italian leather shoes** to look taller.
- **[[Items/The mushrooms|Mushrooms:]]** he arrived high on magic mushrooms kept in Dog's saddlebag. **Purple ones make you happy; red ones cause a "blood frenzy"** and he takes them before fights. He ate a red one before Fury of the Small.
- **Fighting style:** a Drakewarden-style rider who mounts Dog. Spear, shield (Shield Master shove), invisibility (a trait, not a spell), favored foe, Fury of the Small, Mounted Combatant.
- **Personality:** laid-back, friendly, voice gets higher when angry. Calls himself "the non-winners," not losers. Recommends pets for mental health. Mixes up "Doug" and "Dog."
- **Lore:** "I've been down this alley before" with a Box of Chaos, and "the last one I saw didn't talk."
- **In this episode:** befriended Gerald, gained and lost two lions, saved Dog with his hero point, killed the Aeorian Reverser, and shoved Mcswiggins into the hole with the "long live the king" line. **The Arbiter bet on him.**

**[[Characters/Dog|Dog]]** (RunawayRobot_). Snoop's "dragon dog," a scaly **silver dragon** drake companion that says "woof." It **bites ankles**, breathes a **cone of lightning**, can be ridden, and can be clapped back to Snoop's side. Two natural 20s; "the real MVP." Snoop calls him "the backbone of this partnership." Everyone else insists he is not a dog.

**[[Characters/Mcswiggins|Ulfgar Greyblade / "Mcswiggins, the Everthirsty"]]** (bonus_stage_rob). A broad dwarf, about **4'8"**, a barbarian with warlock (Hexblade) abilities.
- **Titles:** "the toughest son of a bitch in Drakboldor [?], and the pride of the drunken master."
- **Name:** he insists he goes by Mcswiggins. The sign-up said Ulfgar; the Arbiter blames friends signing people up.
- **Gear and powers:**
  - Greataxe holding ancestral protectors he invokes as "**Jamison, Johnny, and Jack**."
  - **An enchanted, endlessly refilling flask.**
  - Mage Slayer ("Mage Bane"), Eldritch Smite, Hexblade's Curse.
  - Beer-colored ice tomb ("tomb of Lastavus").
- **Personality:** drunk and confused; at home in fart air ("born in them"). Pees on the arena wall and into the hole. "Eat, drink, be merry"; treats his mental health with drink. Thought Gerald was "Greg." Loves "the men of Italy," a band of mustached bards.
- **In this episode:**
  - Got a **lion barrier tattoo** from Gerald.
  - Sucker-punched Snoop during a handshake.
  - Tried to ride the Reverser and was thrown.
  - Fell into the abyss.
  - **In Dried Blood, the Arbiter caught him with a plunger and took him to his lair, "I'm going to have a use for you very soon."**

**[[Items/Gerald, the Box of Chaos|Gerald]]** (Drew). The creature in the Box of Chaos: yellow eyes, shadowy claws.
- Talks.
- **Ate both bags of Snoop's mushrooms, and his eyes turned to yellow spirals.**
- Rummages through a cluttered box interior.
- Gave the golden lions, then **ate one**.
- Defends himself: "It could have been anybody's lion"; "Lions can either be ferocious or they can fit in your pocket."

The other, the "busted lion"/"cat," missed repeatedly, drank the melted beer, rolled a natural 1, and was **retired by the Grandmaster to sit beside Doug**, where it fell asleep drunk.

**[[Characters/The Aeorian Reverser|The Aeorian Reverser.]]** Summoned out of the lost plane's hole. It nearly crit Dog, threw Mcswiggins, and was killed by Snoop.

Snoop calls him a "non-dog" and a "very cute lizard"; the Arbiter says Doug "rolled like shit" last week.
