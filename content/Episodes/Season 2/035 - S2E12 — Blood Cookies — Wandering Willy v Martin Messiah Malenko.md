## What happens
### Opening: the Dread Fortress TV room
1. **0:00:16** The Arbiter walks the halls of the Dread Fortress looking for the Grandmaster. Her voice echoes from **the back TV room**. "We have a TV room." "When did we get a TV room?"
2. **0:00:54** She kept it secret because she hates sharing her shows with him: "You always like and watch all the worst things and it ruins my algorithm." She pays for it. The Arbiter asks: "In this economy?" She replies: "TV is important to me putting up with you. Helps with my patience."
3. **0:01:24 — The Oscars.** She has been watching the Oscars red carpet and calls them "the worst looks I've ever seen." "I haven't seen a single shield or sword or even a stick resembling a wand… who shows up in an event being completely unarmed?" The Arbiter says there's not enough bloodshed for him. He thinks someone should "put an arrow in their chests when they've talked too long." The GM agrees: "They're sitting ducks." She clicks off the TV.
4. **0:02:30** It's Sunday, so it's Deathmatch night. She has lost track of time and wants a morning reminder of what day it is. The Arbiter offers to send **the goblins** to wake her. "No, not them. They're just very loud… **Doug, get Doug to wake me up.**" He agrees to send Doug, and she has it added to the list on "a little sticky note."
5. **0:03:13** The Arbiter has "a couple of champions. They're a little eclectic." He introduces them: **Wandering Willy versus Martin Messiah Malenko!**

### The champions
6. **0:03:47** Willy isn't paying attention and has to be told it's his turn. "Oh, hey. What's up? I'm Willy." He is hot and "like a breeze." He compliments the Grandmaster's wings: "I bet you get a good breeze."
7. **0:04:32** (Speakers are mixed here.) Someone says they don't know how they got here: "that was some good flour… I just sort of, you know, got shot and then here I am." Willy: "Oh, man, getting shot. Oh, hey, I like this guy… This guy's nice." The Arbiter tells him he has to fight "him" to the death.
8. **0:05:13 — Martin.** "My name's Martin Masai Malenko. You can call me Masai. You can call me Martin." He asks how he got here. The GM answers: "Magic." Martin: "**It's always goddamn magic every time.**" Willy: "I've smoked that before." A guy named **Mike** once gave Willy something "magical," and afterwards he believed him. Willy has "heard of plane shifting." Martin explains to him that they'll be in one-on-one combat.
9. **0:06:14 — The reputation.** The GM explains that the Deathmatch usually brings in accomplished champions to tell "their own great chaotic event story." The Arbiter admits: "**These are the most nat one rolling individuals in the entire universe. That's why I brought him here.**" Martin: "That was one game, one bad game… seven nat ones later I'm here." Willy: "Sometimes life finds a way, and other times you end up here fighting your best friend, Martin. I know we just met, but I feel like there's a connection."
10. **0:07:29 — Friendship necklaces.** Willy offers to make matching friendship necklaces. Martin suggests ale and "a good time." Willy offers one to "**grandmother**," and the GM corrects him: "the name is Grandmaster." The Arbiter: "You can make jewelry with each other's bones after you've killed each other." Someone warns that "you cannot ask the Grandmaster her age… There is Deathmatch etiquette." Someone apologizes: "New realm, new planes, my apologies, won't happen again." The GM: "Maybe by the end of this, I'm going to be making a new necklace out of somebody's bones." Willy: "If I'm gonna make a necklace, you can't do it out of me, because then I can't make a necklace." The Arbiter: "I can't argue with it, so it must be a good point."
11. **0:09:17 — The cookie wager.** The GM shuffles everyone out of her TV room. "Grand Master, not your grandmother. **Yes, I do make good cookies**, but none of you will be getting those unless you put on a good fight. The winner gets cookies, any kind of cookie you want."
    - Willy wants **snickerdoodles**.
    - Martin is "more of a sugar cookie person." The GM: "It's because you're so sweet, Martin." The terms: **Martin wins sugar cookies, Willy wins snickerdoodles**, and the winner is whoever has the most health at the end.
    - Martin asks whether he can switch to cake halfway through. Willy asks for a grab bag. The GM rules they **may change their choice up to two times** (red velvet, confetti), but they get one type, no mixed bag.
12. **0:11:11** The Arbiter: "You know what goes good with cookies?" Martin: "**Blood!**" The Arbiter: "I've never did my cookies in blood. Well, you will tonight." He opens a rift, and **putrid moss and fog** roll out of the portal.

### The arena: the graveyard
13. **0:11:44** The GM brings them to "**perhaps one of my favorite places, where the eternal dead sleep.** I figured the two of you could bring some warmth to this very cold environment." She warns that the dead "do sleep here on occasion, they wake." Whatever is buried with them can be taken. Her wings blow the moss and fungus on the headstones as she flies up.
14. **0:12:32** The Acolytes shimmer above "almost as ethereal as ghosts," already chanting for Martin, Willy, snickerdoodles and sugar cookies. "Team snickerdoodles" signs are up.
15. **0:12:56 — The shirt gag** (unclear in the transcript). The crowd yells for a dance. Willy says he isn't wearing a shirt, so he **grabs a rag off the ground and wraps it around his body**. Martin: "It's okay, Machio. It'll be all right."
16. **0:13:32** The GM floats with her jaw open and her shoulders slumped. "I've got two cookie lovers for you… Maybe there will be blood. Maybe there's gonna be a lot of hugging." She flies to her throne seat **next to Doug the Pug** "as if stomping," sulking.
17. **0:14:09 — Housekeeping.** The Acolytes have summoned **the Box of Chaos** behind a tree near a crypt in the top-right corner: a purple box with yellow and pink flames, and a Void-like figure with flaming eyes peering over the lid. It "contains every magic item ever," given for a sacrifice ("the more meaningful the better"). The crowd awards each champion **one hero point**.
18. **0:15:49** Placement: Willy starts at one end. Martin starts behind **a half-broken wall** between them. The Box is to the north. Dust swirls and the Acolytes spill beer.
19. **0:16:36** Initiative: **Martin rolls a 4** ("These bones don't work the way they used to." The GM: "You just got back from the dead"). **Willy rolls 18.**

### Round 1: the Box of Chaos
20. **0:17:11** Willy heads for the box, hoping for "some unique flower power." He invites Martin along: "We can go together and talk to him."
21. **0:18:13 — Gerald's cough.** Gerald coughs. "It happens to me all the time… I got some lozenges for you." **Willy gives Gerald throat lozenges.** Gerald: "I got a lot of magical items." Willy: "Got any flowers?" Gerald does the gag of **fake-walking down stairs** into the box.
22. **0:19:19 — The ghost lantern.** Cold flames shoot up and Gerald returns holding a **ghost lantern** glowing blue and green, with shapes swirling inside. A restless spirit is trapped in it. It can shed light, act as a mage hand, and **stabilize Willy if he falls unconscious within 10 ft**. The GM: "It's giving you lip to lip action and CPR… It cares about you." Willy: "**This is lit. Get it? Because it's a lantern.**" Willy becomes "a middleman" for Martin.
23. **0:21:24 — The lucky bullet.** Martin says the dead don't keep their stuff. He gives Gerald "**my lucky bullet**. I save it… for just the occasions where I have to fight to the death." The GM: "Here's hoping you won't regret giving away that bullet." Martin claims it's reverse psychology: "that's the one where I only miss with… I'm never going to roll one ever again now."
24. **0:23:09 — The Orb of the Veil.** A small onyx sphere floats into Martin's hand. It gives +2 Wisdom, darkvision and advantage on finding hidden things ("Big brain time." The GM: "Your forehead swells a little bit"). **It is cursed.** He must keep it on him, non-magical flames within 30 ft go out, and **his fire damage is halved**. Martin: "Oh no! That's so bad!… **I'm a light cleric, I have lots of fire things!**" Gerald cackles and closes the lid, sure he has pulled one over on Martin. Willy: "Hey man, you didn't even roll for that one."
25. **0:25:52 — Shiloh.** Martin asks Willy whether he likes nature. Willy: "Me and nature, we're like, I love nature." Martin casts Spiritual Weapon. **Blue divine light shines from under his eyepatch, and a massive songbird appears: "my daughter Shiloh."** She tweets. Petting is fine, but **don't sing to her: "the last time that happened, she did, you know, sort of rip somebody's head off. She does not like music at all."** Willy: "I'm no bard… I can't carry a tune, mostly because I don't carry much."
26. **0:27:19 — The grave-digging offer.** Martin: "You want to start digging some graves?… Four hands are better than two. My mama always say it." The Arbiter: "Grandmaster, is he trying to get him to dig his own grave?" The GM shushes him. Willy hears it anyway (perception 18): "Why would I need my grave dug?" The GM: "It's the new rage of beds."
27. **0:28:10 — The Arbiter's wives.** The Arbiter says a grave is a gift so loved ones aren't left holding the bag. "**I buy a gravestone for all of my wives**… one at a time. They end up in the graves." How many? "**Who can keep track?**" The GM: "You should keep track."
28. **0:28:59 — Martin's family.** Martin has had "just the one" wife, and points to his rifle. Willy "knew a dryad once" but gets confused; the GM doesn't count the dryad. Is the rifle sentient? Martin: "**This here's my wife Val**." He pulls out two twin revolvers: "**This is Machiel, and also this is Cassandra. This is my family.** We're on some sort of a fucked up road trip. They didn't agree to this. **They were polymorphed.** It's a long, drawn-out story, but kids are watching." The GM: "Is this like Mambo No. 5?"

### End of round 1: Doug's roll and the dognapping
29. **0:30:49** The Arbiter's end-of-round roll is a 3. The GM: "We're gonna call on Doug for that one." The Arbiter: "**Fuck Doug.** Why do you always have to call Doug?"
30. **0:31:00 — Doug's pedestal.** Next to the GM's throne there is now **a small stage just big enough for Doug the Pug**. He bites a squishy plush d20, which squeaks, and tosses it into the arena: a 7.
31. **0:31:35** The Arbiter: "**One day I'm going to skin that dog and wear him as a hat.**" The GM: "Doug is well loved here." Martin asks to take Doug home. "He's not a prize." Martin says he wants to adopt. The GM: "**You should adopt more dogs.** So I appreciate that behavior." She gives Martin **a d6 inspiration** for being pro-adoption.
32. **0:32:17 — The plot.** Willy puts an arm around Martin: "**Ownership is one eighth of the land.** If you just steal the pug, then you could own the pug… We could get Doug and we could just blow this." The GM overhears (perception 19): "You cannot steal my dog." She's confident Doug won't leave, so she puts him back on his pedestal and lets the champions make their appeal.
33. **0:33:40 — Command.** Willy gives Martin help on persuasion. Martin wants to establish "who is the sort of alpha" and **casts Command on Doug: "roll."** The GM makes Doug a stat card on the spot (Wisdom 1; "pugs aren't known for their wisdom"). The Arbiter imposes disadvantage on Doug's save. **Doug fails, hops down and rolls over magnificently in the graveyard dirt**, belly up with a little wiggle. Martin: "It's not pug kidnapping… I will heavily convince him with magic." The Arbiter: "I won't steal them, I'll just brainwash them."
34. **0:36:32 — First blood.** Martin goes to scratch Doug's chins, and **Doug bites his hand**, unhappy about the spell. The GM laughs: "Get him, Doug. **No one makes Doug do anything that Doug doesn't want to do.** You have to get him treats." Martin: "**There's blood, guys, there's blood! For the cookies.**" A **small magical cup appears and collects the drops of blood** into vials.
35. **0:38:07 — Willy's appeal.** Willy offers Doug freedom: "I've got a lot of friends you can meet and you could roam free." He rolls persuasion. The GM counters with **a tiny doggy treat** and advantage ("because I have food"): "Would you like to stay here instead where you're loved and respected in every single way?" She beats him 10 to 9. **Doug hops back on his pedestal, floats to her feet and eats the treat.** "That's how you win a dog's heart." Martin: "I guess I always was a bird person."
36. **0:39:49 — "Sometimes I just am a dog."** Willy **wild shapes into a dog** (mechanically a wolf). Doug barks at the intruder. Willy nuzzles and cuddles Martin's leg. Martin: "Oh, you suck up."
37. **0:40:57** The Arbiter: "**I want to see some blood, or I'm going to start making you bleed.**" Martin: "I don't really do the stabbing sort… But the cookies. Alright, I'll do it!"

### Round 2: fetch
38. **0:41:53** Martin backs off without an opportunity attack ("No, man, we're good") and starts a game of fetch. He shakes **a yellow-golden sphere**: "Here, boy… Is she the ball?" He throws it at dog-Willy. It's **a holy hand grenade**, and he casts **Fireball**. The GM: "Do it! He's an imposter dog anyway!" The Arbiter: "That is not a real dog. I know real dogs." **The cursed orb halves the fire.** The GM: "You are real lucky he's got that orb."
39. **0:43:55** The dog form is gone. Willy is back to himself with **singed eyebrows**: "Whoa, man. Whoa, not cool." Martin: "Wrong, wrong, wrong ball… It won't happen again." He laces his belt with more holy hand grenades.
40. **0:44:12** The GM holds up **a plate of fresh-baked snickerdoodles** from the kitchen to remind Willy what he's fighting for. Willy: "Oh yeah, I knew there was a reason I was doing this."
41. **0:44:40** **Shiloh lands on Willy's shoulders and nuzzles him, then digs her talons in** "a little bit too tight," drawing blood. Martin: "A little to the left… No hard feelings, right?" Willy: "**I'm not done with you yet.**"

### Chaos event: the storm (0:45:52)
42. The Arbiter rolls a 15. The Acolytes "are getting a bit thirsty for blood," and the first chaotic event triggers. Martin rolls for it for drawing first blood.
43. **0:46:11** The winds whip through the dead trees and headstones. **Clouds billow in angry blues and blacks. Lightning webs across the sky and strikes down in several spots.** Both make Dex saves.
44. **0:47:57** Martin argues for a Wisdom save: his gunslinger reflexes spot the blue surge in the clouds first. The GM rules that his wisdom tells him the lightning will be bad, but that "doesn't mean you have the ability" to dodge it. Willy: "All right, I'll just fireball the dog." Martin: "I did not fireball the dog… I gave it a holy hand grenade." **Martin takes the full blast; Willy takes half.**
45. **0:49:40** In the flashes, the Acolytes scream and cheer and throw **milk** ("Grandmas and cookies"), beer and tea into the arena. It mixes with the rain and covers the champions: "For the cookies."
46. **0:50:23 — Cookie per hit.** The GM: "**For every hit you make, I will give you one more cookie**," including hits by Shiloh. The Arbiter: "I'm not going to find a better deal than that here in the arena." Martin loads "a couple of special rounds."

### Round 3
47. **0:51:08 — Call Lightning.** Willy: "You gotta appreciate storms… thunderstorms are really something special and sometimes, you know, if you are really in that headspace after a really nice set of nature's meal and you get ascension and you kind of understand the storm, sometimes you can call to it and it just responds." He **casts Call Lightning**, boosted by the existing storm. Martin: "Now what the hell is he talking about?" He half-saves. The GM: "He knows lightning doesn't strike in the same place twice, so he stayed in the same spot."
48. **0:52:57** Willy: "You're kinda mean." He moves away from the bird to the other side of a tree, holds up the lantern over a grave and says "what do you think, Leeroy?" **Shiloh claws at his neck** as an opportunity attack. **Each champion gets one more cookie.**
49. **0:54:30 — Kevin the corpse.** A corpse animates right in Willy's face, **coughs on him and coughs up a small pouch**: **dust of sneezing and choking**, which looks like dust of disappearance. Willy: "**I think Kevin goes back to sleep.** Bury him back down. Didn't mean to wake you… Just dealing with some stuff."
50. **0:55:45 — Fanning the hammer.** "You said every single shot counts. Well, time to miss a couple." Martin walks forward in slow motion and **lifts his eyepatch; the sun god's holy symbol shines from his eye.** He **fans a revolver for six flaming incendiary rounds** (Scorching Ray at 5th level). "**Time to turn up the heat!**" The GM: "You turned the heat up to medium." The orb halves it again, and one ray is a glancing blow. When the barrel goes red hot he blows on it and holsters it. **Martin now has six cookies.** Shiloh dives at Willy's legs to bring him to one knee, but misses. Martin: "Don't worry. You get her next time."
51. **0:59:30** Martin digs up a grave too (perception 26: "Well, hey, Willy, it's a grave, look"). He finds **a potion of healing**. "You do a little bit of good. You get some cookies… **I like this place.**" He offers to feed Doug a couple of cookies, pending the GM's approval.
52. **1:00:55** The Arbiter rolls a 10. The GM: "Everyone's getting a little too intense… **Doug, would you like another cookie?**" She calls a 10-minute break "since you rolled a 10, and also because that's how much time I planned for."

Martin fails badly and the ground becomes difficult terrain.

I was talking about you." The Arbiter: "**I will disintegrate you.**" The GM: "**No disintegrations.** You don't need to be in part of this fight." She counts it as a hit.

57. **1:04:53 — The first hug.** Martin: "You need a hug, you want to just hug this one out?" Willy: "We're going to get on common ground?" He asks Martin to call off the bird. Martin: "**Shiloh, take five.**"
58. **1:05:32 — The dead wake.** As they go in for the embrace, a guttural sound rises: "Foolishly, you did an earthquake in a graveyard." **Hands claw out of the cracks. Three partially disintegrated zombies, missing eyeballs, jaws askew, rise around them.**
59. **1:06:39** Willy: "I'll fix this, Martin." To the skeleton: "We were just scaring him, okay? This is a little much… **Two's a tango, five's, like, just way more than I can count.**" Martin: "Don't forget the kids." The GM: "These things… don't have a brain."
60. **1:07:41** The GM asks whether the hug is genuine. Martin: "It wasn't going to be, but now we got some zombies here, so I'm questioning my life choices." Willy keeps his arms out "wanting to hold you, Martin, in an embrace of friendship and companionship."

62. **1:09:40 — "It's noon somewhere."** "Just a sign of good faith." Martin glows with divine radiance in a misty wind and turns serious for a moment. "**It's at noon somewhere in the world right now.**" He unleashes **Radiance of the Dawn** as a gun barrage: a rifle at the zombie behind him, two pistols at the others, and "by accident" some stray bullets at Willy's feet. The GM: "**We entered the Wild West.**" **The clouds part and dawn rises over the graveyard.** **Two zombies die**; the third hangs on. Willy is hit too. Martin: "Oh, crap. Sorry about that. Didn't mean to hit you."
63. **1:11:59** The surviving zombie breathes in Martin's ear. The GM: "It's a mouth breather. It doesn't have a nose." Martin **fires the blue-and-white pistol into his own leg like a stim pistol (Healing Word at 5th level)**. His wounds don't close; **they shine with divine, searing light**, and the air around him gets warmer. The GM: "Are you like a glow stick?" Martin: "**Gona always used to call me little tiny son**… I've been through some shit." Willy: "**I got a pocket full of sunshine.**" Martin: "Willy, come on, **group hug with the zombie.**"

### Chaos event: the Arbiter's fog (1:13:54)

68. **1:17:03** The GM: "Creepy organ plays in the background." The Arbiter: "**It's just Pocket Full of Sunshine, but really slow and a minor key**," and he sings it. The GM: "**You are a bard, I knew it!**"
69. **1:17:20** Both champions gasp for air. Willy: "I breathe normally." The Arbiter: "with all the years of lung damage." Initiative is rerolled at 9 and 8. "Both are very humble." They each insist the other go first. Martin goes first.

### Round 5: the Harm spell and the nat 20

The GM: "**Did we invite a god by accident?**" The Arbiter: "We have wings, it's not that big a deal."

He sniffs Willy out to the right side of the cathedral. His persuasion beats Willy's insight: Willy hears him over his crying and finds him genuine.

### Round 6: the team-up
81. **1:31:49** Willy reappears, dejected, then turns with a twinkle in his eye. "**I think they're trying to drive a wedge between us.** What if we teamed up?… **There's no I in team. There's a me, if you mix the letters around. But you know what's better than me? Us.**" He offers to share his cookies.
82. **1:32:52** Martin: "**I never really enjoyed fighting. I'm trying to get away from all that sort of stuff.** And you seem like a swell individual. Of course I'll share my cookies… **We're teaming up!**"

## Who and what appears
### Characters
**Wandering Willy** (ArcaneAdam). A druid and a self-described "man of the leaf."
- **Personality:** laid-back and easily distracted. Always friendly; calls everyone "man."
- **Gear and powers:**
  - Carries an assortment of "stoner snacks": brownies, sour gummy worms, chips, candy bars.
  - Wild shapes into a dog.
  - Spells: Call Lightning, Erupting Earth, Heal, Cure Wounds. Can go invisible (Hidden Step).
- **Items gained:** a **ghost lantern** (restless spirit inside), **dust of sneezing and choking**, and a **Deck of Many Things** from Gerald.
- **Lore and quirks:**
  - A guy named Mike once gave him something "magical."
  - He "knew a dryad once."
  - Attended Boulder Gate Community College Online and didn't graduate; a high school teacher called him a loser every day.
  - Gives Gerald throat lozenges. Named the coughing corpse Kevin. Said "Leeroy" while holding up the lantern (unclear whether that was a name for it).
  - Talks to skeletons ("you all have a hive network").
- **In this episode:** survived Martin's 7th-level Harm with a nat 20, then proposed the team-up.

**Martin Messiah Malenko** (Sensei_Suplex), also "Masai."
- **Who he is:** an aasimar light cleric of a sun god, flavored as a gunslinger. He wears an eyepatch, and the sun god's holy symbol shines from the eye beneath it.
- **His family:** he claims to be dead or recently back from the dead ("got shot"). His **wife Val is his rifle**. His **revolvers Machiel and Cassandra** are his kids. All three were **polymorphed**: "a long, drawn-out story." Someone named **Gona** called him "little tiny son." His mama always said "four hands are better than two."
- **Shiloh, his "daughter":** a massive songbird (Spiritual Weapon) who hates music and once ripped someone's head off.
- **Powers and gear:**
  - Holy hand grenades (Fireball), fanned incendiary rounds (Scorching Ray), and a gun barrage (Radiance of the Dawn).
  - A blue-and-white "stim pistol" (Healing Word); a shot to his own heart for Death Ward; Warding Flare from his eye.
  - Radiant Soul: halo and wings. Harm at 7th level.
- **Items:** gave away his lucky bullet and got the **cursed Orb of the Veil**. Found a potion of healing.
- **Personality:** a sugar-cookie lover, pro-adoption, and wants Doug. "I never really enjoyed fighting. I'm trying to get away from all that sort of stuff."

**The Bone Brothers.** Willy and Martin's tag-team name. They wear friendship bone necklaces made by the Grandmaster, and they asked for a future two-versus-two match. The GM said she was "open to this idea."

**Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer** (Bec).
- Has a secret **TV room** in the Dread Fortress that she pays for; watched the Oscars.
- Wants a daily morning reminder of the day, delivered by Doug, not the goblins.
- Bakes cookies, and by the end accepts being "a grandma."
- Wins Doug back with a treat. Can be bribed with snacks.
- **Rebukes the Arbiter for the sneak attack.** States the champions are always brought back and that nobody dies permanently "except for a few accidents."
- Makes the bone necklaces. Gives the Acolytes the cookies.

**The Arbiter** (Drew).
- Recruited the "most nat one rolling individuals in the entire universe."
- **Claims many wives, each given a gravestone "one at a time"; he can't keep track.**
- Wants to skin Doug and wear him as a hat. Threatens to disintegrate Willy. Sings "Pocket Full of Sunshine" in a minor key.
- **Sends Gerald to shoot the champions, then accuses the GM: "You always let them live."**
- Storms off to **his own arena with a statue of himself**, where he raises losers of past Deathmatches from the shadows: "Our time has come."

**Gerald** (Drew). The figure in the Box of Chaos.
- Has a cough. Fake-walks down stairs.
- Cackles over the cursed orb. Calls himself "a paid employee" (per the Arbiter).
- **Shoots at the champions with Martin's lucky bullet on the Arbiter's order.** Gives Willy a Deck of Many Things ("That's my whole job here").

**Doug the Pug**. Now has his own small floating pedestal-stage beside the GM's throne and rolls a squeaky plush d20 for the Arbiter's bad rolls.
- Wisdom 1 (GM).
- Was commanded to roll over, then bit Martin for it: first blood of the match.
- Chose to stay with the Grandmaster for a treat.

**Kevin**. The corpse that coughed up the dust; Willy's name for it.

**The zombies**. Three, raised by Willy's earthquake. Martin's dawn killed two.

**Karn, Baron Voltage (Voltágè), "and many others"**. Named as "losers of past D20 Deathmatches," raised from the shadows of stone in the Arbiter's arena.

**Mike, Gona, Val, Machiel, Cassandra**. Named in the champions' backstories (see above).

- **The Dread Fortress TV room** (the "back TV room").
- **The graveyard "where the eternal dead sleep"**: one of the GM's favorite places. Moss- and fungus-covered headstones, a crypt, dead trees, and dead who "on occasion… wake." Things buried there can be claimed. No name is given.
- **The broken-down cathedral** up the hill, where the Arbiter's fog seals the champions in.
- **The Arbiter's own arena** on another plane: a courtyard with a large statue of him and a railing. He "has made use of [it] even recently for his own nefarious purposes."
- Boulder Gate Community College Online (Willy's school, a joke).

- Ghost lantern; Orb of the Veil (cursed); dust of sneezing and choking; potion of healing; Deck of Many Things.
- **Martin's lucky bullet**: given to the Box, then fired at the champions by Gerald.
- The magical cup that gathers blood "for the cookies."
- Holy hand grenades; Val, Machiel and Cassandra (the guns).
- **Friendship bone necklaces** woven from the graveyard's bones.
- Doug's plush d20 and his pedestal.
- The GM's plate of snickerdoodles and Doug's treat.

### Lore statements
- **The GM, stated: champions are always brought back; nobody dies in the Deathmatch permanently "except for a few accidents every now and then."** The matches exist to "push and challenge and create better fighters."
- **The Arbiter, stated: "You always let them live."**
- The Arbiter has a private arena and is gathering past losers: "Our time has come. Won't be much longer now."
- The Acolytes attend as shimmering, ghost-like figures above the arena.
- **Chaotic events:** the GM triggers them on the Arbiter's end-of-round roll. When it hits her threshold (18), the Arbiter runs his own.
- The GM's closing theme: "your fate is always your own."
