Season 2 of the Deathmatches opened with a grudge match. **[[Asena]]**, a dragon-blooded brawler from the dimension of Northport, faced **[[Ralzamon]]**, a long-dead psychic "master of the unknown" who, by his own count, had fought her "like, five times" and haunted her nightmares. Their arena was the kitchen of [[The Dread Fortress|the Dread Fortress]] itself. Over the night the Acolytes rained flaming snowmen on the floor, an alien saucer came down through the peeled-back ceiling, and a pair of snake arms settled the matter. In the final moments the Arbiter did something nobody else saw.

## The Story

### A Meal Order and a Bones Day

The season began in the old halls of the Dread Fortress, where the stone was "loose and crumbling with age." [[The Arbiter]] strutted through the corridors whistling and pointing at the cooks, "channeling his best Tobey Maguire from Spider-Man 3." He found [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] on her throne in a foul mood. [[Doug the Pug]] sat with her, and a huddle of [[Goblins|goblins]] at her feet were trying to take down her dinner order. The goblins could barely write ("We don't have a great education program in the Dread Fortress yet"). She wanted macaroni noodles, bacon grease and soy sauce, and the kitchen had no bacon grease.

"Finally, somebody here with half a brain," she said when the Arbiter walked in. "Thank goodness you've returned." She told him things had been "a bit of a chaotic mess" while he was away. She called him her right-hand man, then added that she could obviously manage without him but wanted him to feel important. "DoorDash doesn't deliver here," so would he please run her order down to the chefs?

The Arbiter suggested a Deathmatch before dinner. The decision fell to Doug. The Grandmaster gave him a little shake, the pug sat bolt upright, and she declared, "It's a bones day. Perfect. Then we will have a battle."

### Old Enemies

The Arbiter tore open "a spectacular, a little showy" portal. Asena spoke first, a seasoned portal traveller who found "this trip was pretty lovely." She introduced her opponent as "Ralzypoo" and urged the Grandmaster to say it to his face: "He loves it." At home she fought as a pugilist. For this arena she had chosen to lean into her dragon heritage and become "a version of myself that is just more epic and more dragon-y." The Grandmaster was charmed: "You have captured my heart right away… I am keeping my eye on you."

The talk of dragons set the Arbiter reminiscing. It "wasn't too long ago" that he and the Grandmaster had been dragons, he said. She agreed that she had been "the most gorgeous Topaz dragon. Just beauty. And then I got killed." He insisted "they cheated," and she shut the subject down.

Ralzamon arrived floating above the molten rock. He was a wretched being of ghostly ectoplasm, dressed half for a jungle expedition and half as "a megalomaniacal madman." He pointed one hand at his nemesis and spoke:

> **Ralzamon:** "Her energy does not belong in this world! It corrupts everywhere that it goes! If I could not banish it from my own world, then I will snuff it out in this one! For I am Ralzamon, master of the unknown."

Before the fight the Grandmaster asked for tombstone requests. The Fortress keeps a tombstone guy on staff, a craftsman who can work materials from any realm, and since they are weekly customers he offers a discount for friends and family. Ralzamon found marble "a little gross." Asena proposed "Ralzy Poo lies here," which offended him deeply: "Ralzamon isn't even my real name… What would my mother, my poor dead mother think of that?" Asena shot back that he didn't even know his real name. The Arbiter said he had missed this place, and opened one more portal to the sound of lutes and flutes.

### Into the Kitchen

The champions stepped into the Dread Fortress kitchen, which the Arbiter claimed was envied by every mansion and hotel. It had tables loaded with food and knives, a second-floor balcony with a twenty-foot drop, trophies mounted on the walls, and a very deep, dark well in one corner. "I don't have to clean it up," the Grandmaster said. "So food fight if you want." She proved it by hopping onto a table and kicking the food off it. "Make this place unrecognizable." Then she flew up to her throne in the sky beside Doug.

Ralzamon took the balcony, "looking down on you, as only appropriate." Asena started beside the well. She moved first, and she pointed out that she rolled better than he talked.

[[The Acolytes]] opened with Dodgeball and hurled everything they had. Down came pickles, dim sum, a live octopus, cats, toilet paper, mismatched battle slippers, snowmen on fire, and Christmas trees shedding their ornaments. The Grandmaster had to warn one Acolyte, Belle, not to throw her dog. Ralzamon ended up bruised and buried. Asena announced she had needed a snack and started eating the barrage. She ate the cheese, pie and durians, but she refused the raccoon, because "raccoons are sacred animals, okay?" Eating her way through the onslaught spared her half the pain.

Then two shiny vials appeared on the center table, one red and one blue. The Arbiter also announced that the Acolytes had summoned a [[The Box of Chaos|Box of Chaos]]: "It's a box with lots of chaos in it." It was full of magic items, and anyone could take one for a sacrifice of their own choosing. It went to a corner by the window, and nobody claimed anything from it that night.

### Red Pill, Blue Pill

Asena's friend John had told her about *The Matrix*, so she ran straight for the red vial. "Again with these incursive anachronisms," Ralzamon groaned. "Why do you corrupt the plot so badly?" She threw it back like a shot without checking what it was. The blue vial flew up to Ralzamon ("Over the lips and through the gums, look out, Tommy, here it comes"). The red made her blazingly quick, "going in bullet time." The blue made him "one with the universe" and far deadlier.

Asena opened her campaign of terror by karate-chopping a jar of milk off the table. "Take that," she told him telepathically, "now you can't have any milk." Ralzamon was unmoved: "I'm lactose intolerant." Asena filed that away for later. She then summoned her dragon's frightful presence, which happened to take the form of the spilled milk swirling around her feet.

Ralzamon rose into the air, and the trophies on the back wall began to glow. He sniffed out "the stench of Jill and Carmichael" behind her new power. Asena admitted she had never really understood his whole shtick. Ralzamon was exasperated: "We've done battle, like, five times. I've traumatized you. I haunt your nightmares… Do you go to the story meetings?" She explained that the next day everything "just kind of goes away." His words still got under her skin a little, and the Grandmaster told him, "You found a thread that you might want to keep pulling on."

### A Saucer in the Ceiling

The Acolytes gave both champions a burst of heroism, which the Grandmaster likened to chugging a Mountain Dew. Doug was allowed to roll the next chaos event himself. The kitchen ceiling peeled back and curled upward into the sky, and a silver saucer of dazzling colours lowered itself down a beam of light. Asena happened to know all about it. She had learned about alien abductions that very morning, in another world. This was a spaceship, and at the end of the next round it would suck someone up whether they liked it or not. The Grandmaster quietly added that the aliens had been known to give "good gifts and negative gifts."

### Teeth, Fire and a Mental Prison

Ralzamon's plan was simple: "You're really good at punching. I can float… don't get punched." Asena sprouted spectral dragon wings and flew straight at him ("Personal space, personal space"). Her first punch was a natural 20, and her fist hit his jaw with a force he had never felt before. In the Grandmaster's words: "Teeth flying jumbled, the dentist is gonna be real expensive later."

"Not so fast," Ralzamon answered. He poured all the trauma of their shared history into a blast of hellfire: "Rebuke that shit." Asena took it on the chin and punished the spell with another punch. She tried to stun him, and he shrugged it off. That critical first punch had also wrapped him in clinging wisps of dimensional mist. Ralzamon's player had designed that trick for her himself, back home, and he cried, "I made this crap for you!"

Ralzamon refused to be "terrified of a freaking milk dragon." He called on the crystal heart where his consciousness normally lives "when not imprisoned in this fleshy apparatus." He invoked the powers of the Zul'quian jungles, and the roar of a jaguar cleared his head. Somewhere in the stands, baboons began hurling eggs ("Keep your red butts in your seats").

Then he struck at the fear he knew was there. He sealed Asena inside a **mental prison** full of ninjas, void slimes and his own face mocking her from every direction. She spent her heroic boost to fight it off a second time, and did even worse. "You're breaking my heart!" cried the Grandmaster. If Asena tried to leave the prison, it would hurt her far more.

Ralzamon drifted down and hobbled toward the Box of Chaos at half speed ("I'm just a dude"). On the way he reached out telepathically to the saucer, in his best alien voice: "Hail and welcome to our world. The specimen that you requested is ready for collection, immobilized through psychic means." The aliens bought it completely. "Excellent, excellent." He then shook off the wisps of mist, which the Grandmaster said looked like cool smoke, except "it makes it seem like you farted." Ralzamon sighed, "There was a chance to be cool and we just blew it."

### Abducted

The beam came for Asena, and she went willingly, because aliens sounded really cool. Ralzamon objected that moving her should set off the prison. The Grandmaster ruled it a teleport ("beam me up, Scotty") that would return her to the same spot. "Have fun on your vacation," said Ralzamon.

His new friends rewarded him with a healing potion for delivering the specimen. It didn't seem like much, so they felt sorry for him and threw him another: "You seem thirsty. Here you go." Meanwhile Asena lay strapped to a table while Ralzamon chanted "Extract the brain, extract the brain." The experiment turned out to be a gift. Her arms became two slithering snakes, each one an extra brain she could talk to. The aliens zapped her back into her prison and flew away, and the ceiling folded shut as if they had never been there. With three minds working on the problem, Asena broke out of the prison, just barely.

"I don't want to die," said Ralzamon. "Oh, I want you to die," said Asena.

### The Final Boop

Ralzamon fled past the skulls and furs on the wall and pulled a huge clock down behind him, like a movie duel with thrown candelabras, "except, you know, as far away as possible." Asena hopped over it. "Bring it on," he sighed. "Commence the punching."

She punched him and yelled **"Boop!"** The snake bit him. "You boop me, I boop you," Ralzamon answered, and burned her with a second blast of hellfire. Her dragon blood did nothing against fire. She bit back until his arms were dotted like "a vampire saw you as a snack." The Arbiter added that he was "not not promoting cannibalism." Then Asena let loose a final flurry. Both snakes struck, and Ralzamon fell.

Dying, he delivered a prophecy:

> **Ralzamon:** "You will bring doom! Doom upon the world that you call home! You are the harbinger of elder evil… Death is but a door, time is but a window. I'll be back."

The Grandmaster landed on a barrel of mead. She said she did not doubt the danger he spoke of. Asena admitted she didn't know much about her past. The Grandmaster told her to do some family digging, and offered some advice: "There have been plenty who have called me good or evil, hero or villain. Don't let those labels stick to you." Then she lifted the fallen champion's head and honoured him. His blood would seep into the stones of the Fortress: "You are the veins that keep this alive and you will not be forgotten." She asked the Arbiter to tend to their champion.

The Arbiter dragged Ralzamon to the well and **threw him in**, and then jumped in after him. As they fell through what seemed like eternity, he touched the body and brought Ralzamon back. "That was quite a show, especially the mental prison. I'm a big fan. And I think I could use a general for the days to come… Come with me. We have much to plan for."

Above, unaware, the Grandmaster waved away Asena's snakes ("You are free from your pythons"). Asena took her hand: "I like having fingers again. Do you think I could try some of that meat?" The Grandmaster raised their "queen of the fight" before the roaring stands and announced that drinks were on the Dread Fortress.

## Dried Blood

In the after-show, Ralzamon left a final warning: "Do not trust in otherworldly magic, for it seeks only to corrupt your universe and eat it from the inside out." Asena tried to remember her own catchphrase and came up with "cut the bullshit and tell me what you need to know, or something like that." Ralzamon's player assured her that getting it slightly wrong only made it more authentic. The players explained that the two come from a genre-hopping campaign that has taken them through pulp adventure, dragons-versus-mechas and the stars. Ralzamon terrorised Asena with a mind prison the first time they met, but after months of facing him she no longer finds him that scary.
