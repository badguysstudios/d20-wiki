**Season 2** of D20 Deathmatch is the season where the Arbiter comes home to [[The Dread Fortress]] and the weekly bloodshed settles into a rhythm: a strange domestic scene with [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]], two [[Champions|champions]] pulled from somewhere in time and space, an arena, and a lot of chaos. Most of its fights stand alone, and they range from tender to filthy. A lovers' quarrel ends in a shared vacation home, a cookie wager ends in friendship necklaces made of bones, and one champion dies with an ant in his butthole. Underneath the jokes the season is building something darker. More and more of the fallen are carried off by [[The Arbiter|the Arbiter]] "with the utmost respect," and not all of them stay dead. [[The Box of Chaos]] also stops being a simple loot chest this season and becomes Gerald: a voice, a personality and a lost love.

## Background

The season opens with the Arbiter returning to the Fortress after time away. The Grandmaster greets him as "somebody here with half a brain" and admits things were "a bit of a chaotic mess" without her right-hand man. He tells the halls he "really miss[ed] this place." There are signs of trouble from the start. A few weeks in, he confesses that "the traitors escaped the other day," the ones who had been planning to overthrow the Dread Fortress. The Grandmaster is furious: "You have one task."

## The Story

### Prize Fight

The season began in the Dread Fortress kitchen, after the Grandmaster failed to order macaroni with bacon grease and [[Doug the Pug]] declared it "a bones day." In [[Episodes/Season 2/026 - S2E2 — Prize Fight — Asena v Ralzamon|S2E2 — Prize Fight — Asena v Ralzamon]], the dragon-blooded pugilist [[Asena]] faced her old nemesis [[Ralzamon]], who called himself the master of the unknown. Asena drank a red vial, chopped a jar of milk onto the floor at her lactose-intolerant foe, and was abducted by aliens, who sent her back with snakes for arms. A Flurry of Blows finished Ralzamon, but not before he prophesied that she would bring doom to her world. The Grandmaster honoured him as "the veins that keep this alive." The Arbiter then threw his body down the kitchen well, jumped in after him, and revived him on the way down: "I think I could use a general for the days to come."

### A Lovers' Quarrel

Sick of formulaic Hallmark romance, the Grandmaster got the real thing in [[Episodes/Season 2/027 - S2E3 — A Lovers' Quarrel — Lethe v Sorry|S2E3 — A Lovers' Quarrel — Lethe v Sorry]]. The seven-foot goliath [[Lethe]] of the Witchlight Carnival fought their ex [[Sorry]], a crybaby satyr barbarian from [[Strixhaven]], in the Grandmaster's private winter town. The fight had glitter-bomb pixies, a goblin snatching hand axes ("[[Kenny]]'s got to pay his rent") and folding-chair attacks from both hosts. Then Sorry ugly-cried mid-swing and Lethe finally confessed they had wanted to ask her to join the carnival. The Grandmaster declared "today, love will win." Both were named champions, and the Grandmaster let them use her retreat for as long as they liked.

### Antsy

In [[Episodes/Season 2/028 - S2E4 — Antsy — Glub Glub v Geril-Lee|S2E4 — Antsy — Glub Glub v Geril-Lee]], the Grandmaster threw a dagger into the portrait of an ancestor ("He's an ugly motherfucker") and sent the hobgoblin scientist [[Glub Glub the Glorious|Glub Glub]] against the timid, blood-drinking [[Geril-Lee]] in [[The Grandmaster's great-grandfather's old hunting grounds]]. The fight became a war on Glub Glub's undercarriage, waged with an Unseen Servant, a weasel and finally a single ant. He fought back with a flag-cannon, a walrus wild shape, a blessed butthole and his new skeletal friend Bony. Two critical strikes from Geril-Lee ended it. With his last breath, Glub Glub entrusted Bony to her.

### The Gauntlet

For her birthday ("I am, after all, 284," which nobody believed), the Grandmaster got a crystal that warps time and space from the Arbiter. She also got a speed race through her oldest training grounds, [[The Jungle Gauntlet]]. In [[Episodes/Season 2/029 - S2E5 — The Gauntlet — Dan Kowalski v Gordy Gotta Get Em|S2E5 — The Gauntlet — Dan Kowalski v Gordy Gotta Get Em]], two bowling buddies, the satyr [[Dan Kowalski]] and the goblin rogue [[Gordy Gotta Get Em]], ran parallel lanes past squirrels, alligators, manticores, scuba-diving trolls and vengeful vegetables. Gordy sacrificed a ceremonial circumcision dagger to the Box, and Dan sacrificed part of his horn for a Sphere of Annihilation. A lovestruck Babau was waiting at the end. Gordy killed it, then fell to Dan's sleep spell and a Thunder Step. Dan became the first champion to survive the gauntlet since the Grandmaster herself.

### Hand to Hand Combat

The Grandmaster was angry about the escaped traitors and bored by a ball game with no rams or tigers in it. She demanded something grand. In [[Episodes/Season 2/030 - S2E7 - Hand to Hand Combat, Bargo v Carn|S2E7 - Hand to Hand Combat, Bargo v Carn]], the grammar-free fighting-school graduate [[Bargo]] faced the glittering gem dragonborn [[Carnelian]] in a collapsed crystal-mining civilization. Bargo greased a chain of [[The minitaurs|minitaurs]] into the chasm, rode a polymorphed bear and survived two psionic "bitch"es. The Acolytes rejected Carn's plea for mercy, and Bargo sawed off his head. Out of sight, the Arbiter sewed it back on: "Pull yourself together. I might have use for you."

### Father's Love

After fights over half-price Valentine's candy and whether Doug can eat chocolate ("what is it gonna do, kill him again"), [[Episodes/Season 2/031 - S2E8 — Father's Love — Dadrith v Baron Voltágè|S2E8 — Father's Love — Dadrith v Baron Voltágè]] took the fight to the back of the eternal turtle in [[The endless sea]]. [[Dadrith]], "dad of dads," fought with dad jokes, a spectral rolled-up newspaper and a summoned celestial "son" in a Hawaiian shirt. [[Baron Voltágè]] answered with mirror images and an Iron Man flight suit. He also became the first champion to sacrifice his own soul to the Box, which gave him a [[Ring of Three Wishes]]. The Arbiter sank the turtle with crushing gravity, and Dadrith boiled the Baron in his armour underwater. In secret, the Baron spent his last wish on a clone that crawled out of a beached shark 120 days later. In Dried Blood, the Arbiter carried the Box and the Baron's blackened soul back to his laboratory to build a metal body "for his army."

### Bees

The Grandmaster had taken up Zumba. Her goblins [[Thomas the Goblin|Thomas]] and Leed wheeled out her squeaky throne for [[Episodes/Season 2/032 - S2E9 — Bees — Rosa v Veronica|S2E9 — Bees — Rosa v Veronica]], set in [[The bazaar]], a sun-blasted desert market she had "acquired." The halfling [[Rosa]] brought bees and a bee costume with a poisoned butt dagger. The half-elf bladesinger [[Veronica]] was lassoed into the Box of Chaos, where she seduced it with a handful of nuts and left with a ring and a tattoo reading "Gerald." Rosa drew the Talons from the Deck of Many Things and lost every magic item she had. Then Veronica whipped the Arbiter off his ledge, and he caught her blade bare-handed: "I invented Gerald." The Grandmaster hired them both as bee farmers. The Arbiter erased the tattoo and Veronica's memories of Gerald, leaving her just enough to know something had been taken. Veronica called it "my villain origin story."

### Filicide

The Grandmaster overslept because of daylight saving time. The Acolytes then presented Gerald with a brand-new box. In [[Episodes/Season 2/033 - S2E10 — Filicide — Glassy v Thornabub|S2E10 — Filicide — Glassy v Thornabub]], [[Thornabub]] came from the Nine Hells to drag his runaway daughter [[Glassy]] home and marry her off. Glassy fought him with lightning learned from her boyfriend and the eldritch magic her heritage gave her. A giant sky magnet tore the arena open, and Thornabub rode it down onto her. As she lay dying, she signed an infernal contract without reading it, trading her boyfriend Jerry's soul for her life. Thornabub won and went on a pub crawl with the Arbiter. In Dried Blood, the Arbiter confided that he had "been trying to overthrow the Grandmaster for years." The Grandmaster told Glassy, "This is not your last chapter."

### Secrets

Fuelled by Major Melon soda, the Grandmaster watched [[Episodes/Season 2/034 - S2E11 — Secrets — Blaktoof v Zorhel|S2E11 — Secrets — Blaktoof v Zorhel]] in an abandoned mining cavern. The bugbear blood mage [[Zorhel]] faced [[Blaktoof]], champion of the War of a Thousand Toofs, who traded his great-great-grandmother's gold teeth so that Gerald could have a golden grill. The two duct-taped butt stingers to themselves, and Blaktoof bragged loudly about the one hidden on his boot. Then a Booming Blade split Blaktoof open, revealing three goblins in a trench coat: [[Gaz]], Gork and Mork. Zorhel summoned a shadow "Shadowtoof" and killed all three in an acid flood. "We lived like kings."

### Blood Cookies

In [[Episodes/Season 2/035 - S2E12 — Blood Cookies — Wandering Willy v Martin Messiah Malenko|S2E12 — Blood Cookies — Wandering Willy v Martin Messiah Malenko]], the Grandmaster bet cookies on a graveyard fight between the druid [[Wandering Willy]] and the gunslinging light cleric [[Martin Messiah Malenko]], whose guns are his polymorphed family. They tried to steal Doug, raised zombies by accident and traded a Harm spell for a heal. Then they refused to finish each other off and became the Bone Brothers. The Arbiter wanted blood and had Gerald fire Martin's lucky bullet at their backs. The Grandmaster rebuked him openly: "Don't you dare go behind my back again." He fled to his own arena, where, beneath a statue of himself, past losers including Carn and Baron Voltágè rose from the shadows. "Our time has come. Won't be much longer now."

### The Brave

In [[Episodes/Season 2/036 - S2E13 — The Brave — Buli v Chancy|S2E13 — The Brave — Buli v Chancy]], the Grandmaster baked cookies only for the Acolytes and set the tiny owlbear [[Buli]] the Brave against [[Chancy]], a fairy bard who claimed to have slain 10,000 dragons. They fought in a seaside ruin haunted by [[Christina the ghost]], a bride still searching for her missing Frederick. Buli stole and drowned Chancy's lute and was polymorphed into a butterfly. He even recruited Doug, and he rose twice through sheer rage. It became the longest fight the show had seen. A Cloud of Daggers finally dropped him, and the Arbiter tossed his body down the well. According to Dried Blood, he is still falling, back up on one hit point: "I'll be back."

### Shrooms

The Arbiter clacked in wearing Italian leather shoes taken from a dead man. In [[Episodes/Season 2/037 - S2E14 — Shrooms — Ulfgar Greyblade (Mcswiggins) v Snoop & Dog|S2E14 — Shrooms — Ulfgar Greyblade (Mcswiggins) v Snoop & Dog]], the hosts opened a lost plane with membrane floors and an atmosphere of fallen Acolyte farts. The mushroom-addled halfling [[Snoop & Dog|Snoop]] and his silver drake Dog faced the drunk dwarf barbarian Ulfgar Greyblade, better known as [[Mcswiggins]] the Everthirsty. Snoop fed Gerald both kinds of mushrooms. Mcswiggins slam-dunked one of Snoop's lions into the Box. Together they fought off an Aeorian Reverser, and Snoop shoved Mcswiggins down a hole that was, apparently, a toilet. In Dried Blood, the Arbiter dove after him with a plunger and took him to his lair: "I'm going to have a use for you very soon."

### Black Hole Sun

The Arbiter had spent a week fixing the Fortress sewers. He found the Grandmaster wrestling Doug in the bath and opened a portal from the splashed bathwater. In [[Episodes/Season 2/038 - S2E15 — Black Hole Sun — Professor Stabbz v Mad Cass O Weary|S2E15 — Black Hole Sun — Professor Stabbz v Mad Cass O Weary]], the cowardly goblin [[Professor Stabbz]] of the University of Stabbz faced [[Mad Cass O Weary]], a furious, glass-eating tiefling monk. Cass walked on lava, dragged the Box of Chaos across the arena and told it to shut up. A furious Gerald handed Stabbz an eighth-level Sunburst for free. Cass faked a friendship, then betrayed it, and fought on while knocked out cold. Finally the Arbiter tore open a black hole, and the Sunburst blasted Cass into it, middle finger last. Dried Blood ruled that Stabbz will hear his laughter from another dimension as he dozes off.

### Pub Brawl

The Grandmaster forgot she already owned a fully working brewery, built under the kitchen "last fall." In [[Episodes/Season 2/039 - S2E16, Pub Brawl - Broose Scorchtail v Dewdrop Grizzlebee|S2E16, Pub Brawl - Broose Scorchtail v Dewdrop Grizzlebee]], the laid-back harengon [[Broose Scorchtail]] fought the balding, book-taught fairy [[Dewdrop Grizzlebee]] among her tanks. Gerald handed out free school supplies and an alchemy jug of honey from the bee farm. Dewdrop traded away his notebook. The rounds ran out, so the Arbiter turned back time, then axed every vat and flooded the arena with beer. Dewdrop drowned onto Broose's braced blade. Afterwards the Grandmaster told Broose a secret: "No one actually ever dies at the death match."

### Running Threads

**The Arbiter's army.** This is the season's quiet main plot. Ralzamon is revived as a "general," Carn is sewn back together, Baron Voltágè's soul goes into a golem, and Mcswiggins is plucked out of the abyss. Meanwhile the Arbiter grows openly resentful of the Grandmaster's mercy. "You always let them live. Every time." He confesses his overthrow ambitions over drinks with Thornabub, and by Blood Cookies the fallen are rising in his private arena. The Grandmaster still treats him as her mediocre partner, but she knows he "doesn't always agree with my policies."

**Gerald and the Box of Chaos.** The Box goes from "a box with lots of chaos in it" to a character. Gerald peeks out with shadowy claws and flaming eyes. He coughs, wants rent, gets the Acolytes' gift of a new box, adopts a zombie raccoon named Francisco and sports a golden grill. The Arbiter claims him as his creation ("This is why I created you"; "I invented Gerald"). After seducing the Box, Veronica wore Gerald's name until the Arbiter erased it and her memories with it. When Mad Cass found a carved "G plus V" heart on the lid, he decided it stood for gin and vodka.

**The Grandmaster's hobbies.** Each episode opens on a new one: Hallmark movies, Zumba, birthday drinks from a pineapple cup, discount candy, a war on daylight saving time, a secret TV room, cookies, dog baths and the brewery she keeps forgetting. Under the comedy, her speeches keep returning to remembrance. Champions' blood "seeps into the stones," and she told the Acolytes, "we will say his name."

**Doug versus the Arbiter.** Doug, the undead pug, rolls whenever the Arbiter's chaos roll fails. He usually does better, using a squeaky plush d20 from his own pedestal. "Suck it, Doug" becomes the Arbiter's catchphrase, along with threats to skin him. In return Doug farts at him, bites a champion and once stormed off in tears.

**Recurring chaos.** Dodgeball barrages from the stands, folding-chair attacks from both hosts, polymorphing steeds and the floor turning to lava all return across the season. So do hero points, and the Grandmaster's rule that nothing angers her more than one going unused.

**The bee farm and the goblins.** Rosa and Veronica's bee farm becomes part of the Fortress economy, and its honey turns up in the Box by the finale. The [[Goblins|goblins]] scurry through every scene, taking orders, holding antennas, staffing HR and, in one case, posing as a legendary warrior.

## Aftermath

The season closes with the Fortress apparently in order and the Grandmaster raising a glass to Broose. Every thread of the Arbiter's scheme is still loose. Ralzamon, Carn, Baron Voltágè and Mcswiggins are in his keeping, and the Grandmaster has warned him once, in public, never to go behind her back again. Some survivors remain at large. A clone of Baron Voltágè walked out of a shark, Veronica has vowed revenge from the bee farm, and Buli is still falling down the well.

> "Our time has come. Won't be much longer now." (the Arbiter, before his army of fallen champions)
