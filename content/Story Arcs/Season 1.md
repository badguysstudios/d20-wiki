Season 1 is where it all began. Deep in [[The Dread Fortress|the Dread Fortress]], [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] was bored, and [[The Arbiter|the Arbiter]], her long-suffering servant in all things, kept finding new ways to fix that by dragging champions from across the Void and beyond into her arenas to fight to the death. Across fourteen episodes the show found its shape: chaos events summoned by a bloodthirsty crowd, a cursed doll that would not stay gone, and a pug whose bones decide the mood of the day. Most fights stood alone, but a darker thread ran underneath. The Arbiter kept quietly carrying dead champions back to his lab. The season ended with the Fortress under siege and the Arbiter swallowed whole.

## Background

The show's opening narration sets the stage. Far beyond the dimly lit taverns and the snow-clad peaks lies "a dark void outside of all space and time," where the Dread Fortress calls to the lost. The Grandmaster, Elusia Skypiercer, is "retired from battle" but "yearns for the days of glory and bloodshed." She has tasked the Arbiter with curating a spectacle: heroes, villains, monsters and beasts "ripped from time and their plane of existence to fight for survival in her arena." Her worshippers, [[The Acolytes|the Acolytes]], fill the stands to watch.

## The Story

### Episode 1: The First Champion

[[Episodes/Season 1/010 - S1 Ep1 — Ditch Em' Darrell v Eshrinn|S1 Ep1 — Ditch Em' Darrell v Eshrinn]] opened in the Grandmaster's throne room, where she was so bored she was playing "bocce ball for one" with stones. The Arbiter brought her **[[Ditch Em' Darrell]]**, a trinket-loving brawler with a battleaxe named Ramona, and **[[Eshrinn]]**, a snake-like master of spells and poisons. They fought in a cavern where "all that glitters down here really is gold." The fight took in a charm spell, a poisoned ring trick, a bouncy-castle floor, the Arbiter smashing Eshrinn with a rusty metal chair and the Grandmaster hurling a chair back at him "for missing." Then a dragon rose out of the gold with both champions clinging to its back, and the Arbiter flipped it upside down. Darrell's blast finally killed Eshrinn, and the Grandmaster crowned "our first champion." In the after-show the Arbiter sank Eshrinn's body into a secret lair and brought her back to life: "I've got a plan for you."

> "Victory may hurt, but it lets us know that we are alive."
> — the Grandmaster

### Episode 2: Magic, and the Birth of Dolly

In [[Episodes/Season 1/011 - Magic — S1 Ep2 — Mageic v Amren|Magic — S1 Ep2 — Mageic v Amren]], the Grandmaster tried to knife an apple off the Arbiter's head, tripped on her dress and cut her own hand ("You will tell no one of this"). The bumbling showman **[[Mageic]]** faced the fire genasi **[[Amren]]** in her abandoned abbey. A creepy porcelain doll appeared on the fountain, burned a rune into Mageic's palm and became his assistant. He named her "Dolly Parton," she bit Amren ("I hug with my teeth"), and Amren golfed her to pieces. The crowd threw everything they had, including someone's own grandmother, who did not survive. The Arbiter's hidden gas pocket blew up the cathedral, and Amren survived one blast of cold at a sliver of life before the floor turned to lava and she melted into it. Mageic won. As Amren hardened, the Arbiter knelt and asked, "Would you like to live?", then remolded her from the lava into "the perfect weapon" and vanished with her.

### Episode 3: The Shadow Is Born

[[Episodes/Season 1/012 - S1E3 — Blood Mage — Sunozar v the Shadow of the Arbiter|S1E3 — Blood Mage — Sunozar v the Shadow of the Arbiter]] began badly for the Arbiter. His second champion had claimed to be a boxer but only owned boxers, the dogs. As punishment, the Grandmaster ordered him to reach into his chest and rip out his own shadow to fight, and he tore its wings off for good measure. On [[The Disregarded Cliffs]], the half-orc blood hunter **[[Sunozar]]** and his talking flame-sword battled **[[The Shadow of the Arbiter|the Shadow]]**, who complained about sharing a birthday with the Arbiter "for thousands of years." The Shadow used a spell to turn Sunozar against the Grandmaster, which led to the Arbiter's "third cheeseburger" excuse and her sharp reply: "Your shadow is trying to come for my crown." A giant carried the fight into the clouds, and the newly height-fearing Sunozar fell off its palm until the Grandmaster caught him in exchange for worship of her wings. Sunozar decapitated the Shadow and ate his last candy in its dying face. The Grandmaster bottled the crowd's chaos for the next battle.

### Episode 4: Master Kleen

In [[Episodes/Season 1/013 - S1E4 — Multiclass — Kleen v Ivanna Kishu|S1E4 — Multiclass — Kleen v Ivanna Kishu]], the genie-pact warlock **[[Kleen]]** faced the flirtatious aasimar druid **[[Ivanna Kishu]]** in the snowy, abandoned [[Kirken's Mine]]. The bottled chaos from last week opened the fight with Acolyte dodgeball. Ivanna turned into a "very sexy" hippogriff and a striped dire wolf, a round of mandatory Pig Latin had the Arbiter pelting everyone with pebbles, and his avalanche shoved both champions onto a rickety bridge over a frozen waterfall. When they kept retreating, the Grandmaster walled them in with snowbanks, and Kleen's final volley dropped Ivanna into the snow. His victory lap was a literal shower of filth from 52 arena toilets, raining down on the Acolytes. The Arbiter carried Ivanna to his laboratory and revived her: "You will kiss again."

### Episode 5: The First 2v2

[[Episodes/Season 1/014 - S1E5, The 2v2 - Chuck Solid Gold Dragon Biff & Richardo v Steely Dan & Thass'Lok|S1E5, The 2v2 - Chuck Solid Gold Dragon Biff & Richardo v Steely Dan & Thass'Lok]] was a charity show for the Dreaded GM and the arena's first team fight. It paired the wrestling dragonborn **[[Chuck Solid Gold Dragon Biff]]** with the bull-summoning lizardfolk **[[Richardo]]** against the cassette-tape warforged **[[Steely Dan]]** and the edgy lizardfolk **[[Thass'Lok]]**. They fought atop the spinning gears of [[The Clock of the Lost]]. Thass'Lok spent much of the fight attacking his own teammate. [[Dolly]] returned, bonded with Steely Dan, bit Richardo and kissed her new friend back to life before vanishing. A pillow-fight chaos event let Richardo smack the Grandmaster in the face and let Richardo's spectral bull, Bull One, flatten Steely Dan with a pillow. Chuck then picked up Steely Dan's corpse and clubbed Thass'Lok to death with it.

### Episode 6: Belly of the Beast

[[Episodes/Season 1/015 - S1E6 — Belly of the Beast — Viago v Tetra|S1E6 — Belly of the Beast — Viago v Tetra]] opened with the Grandmaster bullying goblins at rock, paper, scissors. It moved to [[The Siren's Nautilus]], a living sunken ship. **[[Viago]]**, a raging barbarian who fought as a dire wolf, clashed with the flattering, fast-talking cat monk **[[Tetra Kincaid]]** in "cat versus dog." The Grandmaster dive-bombed Viago with a metal chair until his head went through the seat. The ship then tilted and dumped both champions into its stomach, where a hungry gelatinous cube waited. After a bouncy-castle floor, a wall-to-floor suplex that turned "very romantic" on the way down, and Viago twice healing his own opponent out of arrogance, a simple kick to the back ended it. The Arbiter didn't revive Tetra: "Normally, I would reserve a specimen such as you for my army. But in this case, my pet is hungry."

### Episode 7: Wild Magic, and Doug

[[Episodes/Season 1/016 - S1E7 — Wild Magic — Strako v Zayliss|S1E7 — Wild Magic — Strako v Zayliss]] introduced **[[Doug the Pug]]**. According to the Grandmaster, "We've always had Doug," and some days he has bones and some days he doesn't. **[[Strako]]**, the Wild Shadow, and the chaotic monk **[[Zayliss]]**, both plucked from near the Witchlight Carnival, fought on a portal tower adrift in the cosmos. The match quickly became the arena's strangest friendship ("Dude, I respect you"). The Arbiter kept losing roll-offs to Doug until he called down a meteor swarm in a fit of rage. Sucked out into an asteroid field, Strako tagged a glowing alien to get out of a game of space tag and threw it away. It exploded, and it turned out to be [[Timothy]], beloved mayor of Worm's Town. Strako cleaved Zayliss, they promised each other a beer in Valhalla, and Doug gave her body a final snuggle.

### Episode 8: The Fusion

In [[Episodes/Season 1/017 - S1E8 — Fireballs — Grorx v Lord Xiren|S1E8 — Fireballs — Grorx v Lord Xiren]], the credit-happy goblin **[[Grorx]]**, who tried to buy the Grandmaster's crown, faced the devoted aasimar protector **[[Lord Xiren]]** in [[Kabehe's Temple]]. Doug's barking drowned out every spoken spell. A mummy befriended by Xiren turned on him once the charm wore off, and Dolly rose from a tiny sarcophagus to hold Xiren's hand before the temple collapsed on her: "We don't know the fate of Dolly." Outside, Xiren fireballed his own feet, leaving both champions on the brink. The Grandmaster refused to cut their thread: "Clearly, these are bonded souls." A coin flip let them both live, but in one body with two heads.

### Episode 9: Fizban's Dragon Battle Royale

[[Episodes/Season 1/018 - S1E9 — Fizban's Dragon Battle Royale — Amethyst Dragon v Dracohydra v Hollow Dragon v Topaz Dragon|S1E9 — Fizban's Dragon Battle Royale — Amethyst Dragon v Dracohydra v Hollow Dragon v Topaz Dragon]] was the Halloween special and the first monster battle royale. The arena's own [[Narfi|Narfi]] became a many-headed Dracohydra and **[[Vashon]]**, a living amethyst die, became an Amethyst Dragon. They were joined by the Grandmaster's shadow-aspect as a Topaz Dragon and the Arbiter's Shadow, summoned again ("What the fuck do you want this time?"), as a Hollow Dragon. The fight hopped from open sky to three different lairs and somehow became a debate about unionizing the concession stands. The Hollow Dragon vowed that he and the Arbiter would one day "kill all of you" and put in a beanbag chair, and the Grandmaster promised the Arbiter "a long talk later." Her aspect crushed the Hollow Dragon's skull, and the Dracohydra, grown to ten heads, devoured the Amethyst Dragon. Narfi's victory speech: "Half-priced lemonade for all."

### Episode 10: The Bear

[[Episodes/Season 1/019 - S1E10 — The Bear — Diamond Joe v Milliac|S1E10 — The Bear — Diamond Joe v Milliac]] opened in the Arbiter's lab, where he was gloating that "this entire castle will be mine." He hid everything just before the Grandmaster walked in. **[[Diamond Joe]]**, the elderly, pill-popping dragonborn artificer who built Steely Dan, had come looking for "my boy." He fought **[[Milliac]]**, a woodland druid lured by a flyer promising pie and punch, at an abandoned black-powder dig. Joe opened by punching himself in the face under a curse and then handing his opponent a cream pie. Milliac answered with polar bears, a duplicated bear named Honey Boo Boo, a bear conga line and, at last, a giant bomb made of black powder in the shape of a bear, which dropped him. The Arbiter secretly revived him ("I guess I might find a use for you somewhere"). Joe's crude request for a final favor got him dropped from the sky. In the after-show, the Arbiter showed Joe a room full of scrap where Steely Dan's parts lay waiting.

### Episode 11: Gift Horse

[[Episodes/Season 1/020 - S1E11 — Gift Horse — Cosmonius v El Hada Rocha|S1E11 — Gift Horse — Cosmonius v El Hada Rocha]] began with the Arbiter failing to recognize the Grandmaster after she changed her hair. Two dazzling champions from [[The Feywild]] met in the Fortress's own generator room: the flattering centaur grappler **[[Cosmonius]]** and the peacock-feathered luchador **[[El Hada Rocha]]**. The Arbiter worked them like puppets on strings, an event gifted by the Acolyte Kalindra. Then the crowd threw their grandmothers into the ring, followed by grandfathers and a "Grandma Siren." The grandparents broke into a civil war over cookies until the Arbiter shipped them all off to a Florida retirement home. Through a giant fire crab, an electrified map and a beheaded donkey piñata, the two traded blows until both fell on the same exchange. With no champion to give, the Grandmaster asked the Acolytes to "Remember the names of El Hada Rocha and Cosmonius."

### Episode 12: Feast

For [[Episodes/Season 1/021 - S1E12 — Feast — Potato v Dinkledink|S1E12 — Feast — Potato v Dinkledink]], the Fortress was decked out for the holidays. The Arbiter had recruited the champions from a mall Santa line. The timid **[[Potato]]** and the rat-like **[[Dinkledink]]** were shrunk down onto a giant feast table, where Dinkledink vowed to take Potato's mother out for fried fish and never call her again. Potato, who had never known a mother or a present, called him "Daddy" instead, and a strange father-son bond took hold. After a giant swallowed them both, Potato prayed to [[Tiamat]] and rose as Starfall. Father and son fought a cake elemental together until it killed Starfall. Dinkledink buried his son under a placemat with a star cookie, and the Grandmaster cancelled the next week's Deathmatch so everyone could be thankful for "family, friend, or foe."

### Episode 13: Snow-clad

[[Episodes/Season 1/022 - S1E13 — Snow-clad — Highfrost v Bryltyn|S1E13 — Snow-clad — Highfrost v Bryltyn]] found the Fortress crumbling and ankle-deep in sewage, and the Grandmaster setting up anti-clone secret handshakes with everyone. The kilted northern warlock **[[Highfrost]]** fought the stoic **[[Bryltyn]]** in the frozen fishing village where Highfrost's family once lived. Bryltyn claimed he had killed them all. A toast of ancestral ale poisoned them both, the Grandmaster started a game of tag, and a line of ducklings turned the match into a fight for duck liberation. Highfrost fed them bread he kept in a satchel under his kilt. An avalanche swept the champions onto the docks, and Bryltyn's last axe opened Highfrost's chest. Highfrost's parting words were a final kilt-flash and a message for Bryltyn's mother.

### Episode 14: Eyes of Chaos (Finale)

The finale, [[Episodes/Season 1/023 - S1E14 — Eyes of Chaos — Lucien v Monteclaire (Season 1 finale)|S1E14 — Eyes of Chaos — Lucien v Monteclaire (Season 1 finale)]], pitted the silver-tongued half-orc bard **[[Lucien|Lucien Braque]]** against the pompous, 93-year-old paladin **[[Monteclaire Dufresne]]** in [[The Grand Coliseum]]. The duel had its share of earworms, balloon swords and a squad of animated rocks. Then Doug ran off to bark at the front door, and the western wall exploded as a Tarrasque broke through, leading an army that tore into the Acolytes. The Arbiter held the beast with time magic while the Grandmaster armed both champions and revealed a fire-ringed loot chest, [[The Box of Chaos|the Box of Chaos]], whose prizes were bought with kills. Doug guarded the Southern Gate alone, and the Grandmaster unlocked the Northern Gate to release a turkey dragon "of my own creation." Its cranberry-sauce breath drove off a red dragon. But the Arbiter's grip failed, and the Tarrasque swallowed him whole, still alive, then answered a distant horn and left.

### Running Threads

**The Arbiter's secret work.** All season the Arbiter quietly kept the dead. He revived Eshrinn, Amren, Ivanna Kishu and Milliac, and mentioned an "army" over Tetra's body. His Shadow twice spoke openly of taking the Grandmaster's crown, and he was caught gloating over a plan to make the castle his own. He brushed it all off: the Shadow is just "the worst parts of me," and "he looks like an idiot, you can't take him at his word."

**The Arbiter as punching bag.** He is unpaid ("I don't pay you"), has no HR to complain to, owes money to "some really nasty people" from his wagers, and sells candles on the side. He loses almost every roll to a pug. The Grandmaster mocks his dice so constantly that "C minus, buddy" counts as praise.

**The Grandmaster's whims.** Each episode opened on a new obsession: coloring books, chugging water, naps, goblin soap operas, rock, paper, scissors, holiday decorating, secret handshakes. In the arena she picked favorites on flattery alone, caught falling champions, cursed bad bagpipers and, when the dice demanded it, rewrote the rules entirely: "As you know, there are no rules. Except for the rules that I make up."

**Doug the Pug.** From his first appearance he became the season's mascot and the Arbiter's nemesis. He got his own throne, a velour portrait that shows him with or without bones, tiny wings for a game of space tag, and a heroic stand at the Southern Gate in the finale. "A small undead pug can beat you every single time," the Grandmaster observed.

**Dolly.** The doll who asks "Will you be my friend?" appeared in three fights. She was shattered in the abbey, befriended Steely Dan in the gears, and was buried in a collapsing temple. Each time she branded a new friend's palm, and each time the Arbiter was visibly spooked: "Oh no, not this again."

**The crowd and its chaos.** The Acolytes summoned chaos events week after week: dodgeball barrages of hot dogs and toilets, metal chairs, bouncy castles, balloon weapons and too many games of tag. Above all they threw grandmothers, a running gag the Grandmaster saluted in the finale when she thanked them for "the grandmothers you have sacrificed week after week."

## Aftermath

The season ended with no winner crowned. Both finalists survived and fought side by side, and the Grandmaster raised both their hands. The Coliseum was left dark and cold, the western wall torn open, and the Arbiter was carried off alive inside a Tarrasque that "shimmered in a way that a normal creature wouldn't." Doug was safe in her arms. Shaking and holding back tears, the Grandmaster named the fallen and surviving Acolytes and promised to rebuild:

> "We are a delight. We are light. And we will never lose. This is our ground. This is our coliseum. This is our home."

She closed with a vow for what came next: "Season two will have new heights and new battles so that all who enter or dare to challenge the Dread Fortress do so with caution. Their boots will shake."
