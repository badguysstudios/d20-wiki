The **Live Shows** are the Deathmatches that [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] and [[The Arbiter|the Arbiter]] staged in person, in front of packed rooms of [[The Acolytes|Acolytes]] at GCX Orlando and at Full Sail University's Hall of Fame. Each one is a standalone fight. They have a flavor all their own: the crowd does not just watch, it rolls the dice, gets drafted as weapons and shields, and now and then gets killed for its trouble. Across five shows the arena saw a fake death undone by a stolen sword, a child booped to death, a chicken murdered in cold blood, and a man who paid the Grandmaster to resurrect his opponent. They are some of the loudest, silliest and most crowd-soaked [[Champions|champions]]' battles in the history of [[The Void|the Void]].

## Background

Every live show opens with the Arbiter hyping the crowd and introducing the Grandmaster, and most of the time he introduces her as the one who defeated [[The Shadow King|the Shadow King]]. The phrasing changes from year to year ("she who calmed the chaos," "she who shattered the Shadow King, to she who pierces the sky"). The audience in the room becomes the Acolytes for the night, and at most of the shows the Grandmaster reminds them that their cheers and jeers can inspire, heal or wound the fighters. As she put it at GCX in 2023, "in this arena, every voice matters."

## The Story

### GCX Orlando 2022: Lord Snacc v The Showman

The first live show, [[Episodes/Live Shows/049 - Live - GCX Orlando 2022 — Lord Snacc v The Showman|Live | GCX Orlando 2022 — Lord Snacc v The Showman]], nearly didn't happen. The Grandmaster announced that she had brought the [[The Dread Fortress|Dread Fortress]] "from further North Orlando," and then discovered that the Arbiter had packed a toothbrush but no champions ("Where else do you put champions?"). She pulled two volunteers from the crowd. The first was **[[Lord Snacc]]** (played by BDTeej), a barbarian from a faraway land ("some would even say in space") who worships a spherical god. The second was **[[The Showman]]** (played by RogueExMachinca), a masked bard whose knives are named after the cast of *Friends*. The Arbiter tore open a portal, and the Grandmaster described the autumn forest clearing beyond it as "perfect for this setting sun of someone's life tonight."

The Showman terrified Snacc with his bowler hat, and the Acolytes helpfully shouted out the barbarian's other fears: bees, leaves and, above all, cheese. A game of tag left a pus-filled blister on Snacc's arm. Then [[The Box of Chaos|Gerald]] crawled out of the Box of Chaos. Snacc fished his "other horn" out from somewhere near his pants and sacrificed it for a healing potion, leaving Gerald to wonder whether he was supposed to sit on it. The Showman took a different approach and simply asked Gerald what *he* wanted ("I guess I'd like to paint and start a family"), then gave up Phoebe for a patriotic little shield. Joey the animated knife landed a critical hit, the crowd's clapping turned into thunder, and lightning arrived as thrown ping-pong balls. Snacc's flaming Inferno Hammer seemed to bury the bard for good, until the Showman revealed he was "just sleeping," rose again, and stabbed Snacc with a sword he had dug out of the forest floor. It was the Grandmaster's. Two glaive strikes in the seventh round finally finished him. He spent his last breath promising to haunt the fro-yo stand, and **Lord Snacc** was crowned the winner. The Showman's death, as it turned out, was not final.

### Full Sail Hall of Fame 2023: Jenny Gray v Chef GPT

At [[Episodes/Live Shows/063 - LIVE at Full Sail Hall of Fame 2023 — Jenny Gray v Chef GPT|LIVE at Full Sail Hall of Fame 2023 — Jenny Gray v Chef GPT]], **[[Chef GPT]]** (played by KidCalrissian), a robot artificer "here to serve murder," faced **[[Jenny Gray]]** (played by ErinEberhardt), a high-school druid hoping to make it to prom. The Grandmaster snapped the necklace from her neck and threw it down, and a blue portal swept both into a sulfurous volcanic wasteland. Chef began by sinking into the lava. His first escape attempt sent him running blind, straight into his opponent.

What followed was pure chaos. The fight featured the "dogs of many things" hidden in the crowd (one of them farted on Chef hard enough to burn his lungs), a rabid [[Chinchillas|chinchilla]] that Chef shrugged off ("We have put them inside of the oven many times"), a dance-off and a pillow fight, and an audience member named Austin who reprogrammed Chef "to brutalize everything." Jenny became Precious, a six-pound pink Pomeranian. The Arbiter ripped the spine out of an Acolyte over a stale piece of popcorn. Jenny turned Chef into a frog and shook him in her teeth, and Frog Chef spat his tongue into her eye and pulled it out, half-blinding her, then slicked the lava with grease made of his own frog oils. A failed roller, Luis Sanchez, was drafted as Jenny's shield and melted away under a lime-juice acid arrow. Chef then levitated the puppy out over the lava and let go. As she fell, Jenny transformed "as if Cinderella at the ball," wearing the most gorgeous ball gown, and she died with no regrets.

> **Jenny Gray:** "I just wanted to kiss someone cute at prom tonight."

**Chef GPT** won, and he said only, "Dinner is served."

### GCX Orlando 2023: Navik v Ragu (Prego)

[[Episodes/Live Shows/116 - LIVE at GCX Orlando 2023 — Navik v Ragu (Prego)|LIVE at GCX Orlando 2023 — Navik v Ragu (Prego)]] opened with the Grandmaster's grand illusion. Smoke turned the room into bubbling red lava, with the great skull entrance of the Dread Fortress looming behind. The Arbiter used the moment to recruit. He listed the perks of working at the Fortress, including the chinchilla and goblin unions, a mental-health chinchilla and unlimited snow cones. He also complained that union rules meant he only worked Sundays. He announced that they had "thrown off the shackles of our former dungeon" for a new style of combat. Then **[[Navik]]** (played by Baddie), the self-appointed Lord of the Undead and possibly middle management, faced **[[Ragu]]** (played by cBC), a "filthy, dirty animal" famous for the Ragu squat.

Navik spent the fight raising Acolytes as minions: a freshly buried dragonborn, a skeletal centaur, a Hufflepuff who went for the ankles. One Acolyte on Ragu's side was dropped into a deep fryer "like a mozzarella stick." After announcing that he had just sharted, Ragu pooped out the Box of Chaos ("This is a really shitty box") and traded his nunchucks for a small blue pill. Navik then turned Ragu's own cheering friends against him ("You know what Caesar feels like now," the Grandmaster told Ragu). Ragu evolved into **Prego**, got his own energy beam bounced back at him, and survived a string of disasters, including a child human shield whom Navik "booped" to death and raised. Prego even healed himself with "the power of Parmesan." It all ended in a hailstorm of frozen flying-wombat poop. Navik raised the fallen Prego into his army, Prego kissed his boots, and **Navik** declared, "Evil always wins!"

### Full Sail Hall of Fame 2024: Lief Tover v Sheriff Tapatio S. Picante

In [[Episodes/Live Shows/128 - LIVE at Full Sail Hall of Fame 2024 - Lief Tover v Sheriff Tapatio S. Picante|LIVE at Full Sail Hall of Fame 2024 - Lief Tover v Sheriff Tapatio S. Picante]], the Arbiter clapped open a portal and brought in two men he knew from bingo. The first was **[[Lief Tover]]** (played by ProfessorBroman), a trash viking from the deepest depths of a garbage dump. The second was **Sheriff Tapatio S. Picante** (played by Christian Hammer), a lawman whose guns had been confiscated and who rode a chicken named Clucky Cheese. The Grandmaster bet on Lief and the Arbiter "hitched his saddle" to the Sheriff. The Grandmaster then announced the arena as "the deep, dark jungles of Florida," complete with alligators and "huge-ass mosquitoes."

Lief opened with a bomb packed with used diapers and soon killed Clucky Cheese outright. The grieving Sheriff answered by beating him with the dead chicken ("That's pretty foul"). The chaos events were a hula-hoop contest, a hunt for one prize hidden among the Grandmaster's "big gold balls," and a game of hot potato "except now it's just hot balls." Lief cast a spell called Ball. He also ruined the Sheriff's keto diet with pancake berry bites, and he stuffed the lawman into a trash bag. The Sheriff's real enemy, though, was his own posse. One natural 1 after another left him more and more worn down. When an Acolyte named Austin rolled his fifth, the Grandmaster carried him to his grave: "As we know, death is never the end, isn't it?" **Lief Tover** won, and the Grandmaster won her bet.

> **Lief Tover:** "But like I said in the beginning, trash lasts forever."

### Full Sail Hall of Fame 2025: Ringmaster Talon v Maximilian Maxwell III

The last live show, [[Episodes/Live Shows/128 - Full Sail Hall of Fame 2025 (live show), Ringmaster Talon v Maximilian Maxwell III|Full Sail Hall of Fame 2025 (live show), Ringmaster Talon v Maximilian Maxwell III]], was fought on the stage itself. **[[Ringmaster Talon]]** (played by Jagger) was a blood-soaked showman who runs a circus for Lichter the Lich and hadn't washed his clothes in twelve years. **[[Maximilian Maxwell III]]** (played by Charles) wore white gloves, had never fought a Deathmatch, and openly bought his way onto the stage. The Arbiter allowed it, because the Grandmaster's bath bombs don't pay for themselves.

Max hired Acolytes for everything: as a living weapon, as a target, and as a stone-wall defender. Talon drained three volunteers of their blood until it poured out of every orifice, then caught a splinter of spine in the eye and gambled on his own exhaustion. Murder unicorns stabbed both champions, and in the second round that gamble killed him. Max then paid the Grandmaster to bring him back, and she drove Talon's own scythe into his heart to restart it. The Arbiter made Max pay for that bargain with some of his life: a black goo crawled up both champions' nostrils and drained Max's life into Talon until the two were even. Then came eggs full of the Void's brand-new currency, Void Bucks, a giant food truck, and a blood tsunami blocked by back-to-back 20s that Max had paid for. When the Grandmaster grew impatient, she sped up time to make Max bleed faster ("Am I not fucking awesome?" "Six out of ten"). In the end Max buried Talon under hundreds of thrown soup cans. Talon's last words were "At least it's chicken noodle," and **Maximilian Maxwell III** celebrated "Death by SpaghettiOs!"

### Running Threads

**The workplace.** The Grandmaster and the Arbiter spend every live show bickering like a boss and an employee. She charges him rent ("I just work here"). She was angry about a Fortress wing he built without permits. The Fortress offers vision coverage but not dental, because "they lose teeth so often in these fights." He insists his boots are Italian leather, and she insists they are not. He is also quick to point out that she couldn't do the taxes without him.

**The Acolytes as cast.** At these shows the crowd is part of the fight. Turtleman threw a knife for the Showman. Austin reprogrammed Chef GPT and, a year later, rolled the die that killed the Sheriff. Matthew rolled twin 20s to save Max. The Acolytes also suffered for it: there was the popcorn guy, Luis Sanchez, the deep-fried Acolyte, the booped child and Talon's three blood donors. When the Grandmaster saw Acolytes slipping in Chef's grease, she was unbothered: "It's okay, they were unnamed NPCs."

**The Box of Chaos, Doug and the chinchillas.** Gerald turned up twice. At GCX 2022 he made his first live appearance and admitted that he wanted to paint and start a family. At GCX 2023 he was pooped out by Ragu. [[Doug the Pug]] was left at home in 2022, so an Acolyte rolled for him instead. The [[Chinchillas]] went from a rabid summon at Full Sail in 2023 to a unionized workforce at GCX later that year, and had a fully stocked kitchen by 2024.

**Callbacks.** At GCX 2023, the Arbiter sent a would-be challenger "back to the corner like last year," a nod to the sign-breaker the Grandmaster banished at GCX 2022. Round cards that run out before the fight does and the Arbiter's closing "Go home! Get out of here!" also recur.

**Death and remembrance.** Under all the jokes, the Grandmaster returns again and again to the same idea. Champions should tell their story "so that you may be remembered," give it their all, and die without regrets. To her, death is "merely yet another challenge that we will conquer."

## Aftermath

Each live show ended with a winner standing, and none of them carried a plot forward on its own. At GCX 2022 the Grandmaster asked the Acolytes to "burn them in your hearts," and that is what these fights have become: remembered battles more than chapters of a larger story. The Showman's death did not stick. The 2025 show left the Void with its own printed money, and the Arbiter had the last word on the way out: "You can't have your money back."
