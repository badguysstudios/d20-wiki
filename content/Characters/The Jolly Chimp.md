(Ben's spirit). Disembodied arms of matted black fur, pale wrinkled knuckles, yellowed antique-dusty nails and tiny cymbals on each finger. Its visage: a bloody red fez, hypnotic red/black/white eyes and a grin of perfectly white flat teeth.
