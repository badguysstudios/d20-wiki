## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/074 - Into the Mirror 10 Shatter + Into the Mirror 11 Mortaurios (ITM session 3)|Into the Mirror 10 Shatter + Into the Mirror 11 Mortaurios (ITM session 3)]]
(Miss_Magitek). An Aasimar (Scourge) with Radiant Consumption, a longsword and a shield.
- **From "a part of" the Void long ago; she was killed and arrived in the Sands.** Her friends here died and became sand.
- Seeks vengeance on whoever made this place and wants to return.
- Her inner self is angelic and white-winged.
- She knows the city's name and how to summon its portal.
- **Her arrow killed Locket.**

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/077 - Into the Mirror Session 4 (YouTube parts 12–13) - Royal Audience and Otherside|Into the Mirror Session 4 (YouTube parts 12–13) - Royal Audience and Otherside]]
. Newcomer to the party, called "glowing one" by Flynry.
- Was welcomed into the city hundreds of years ago and is on good terms with the queen. She has "taken care of various outlanders for her."
- She was being attacked by outlanders when the party found her.
- Wants out of the plane. She didn't know who made the realm.
- **Wields a blade with divine light**: Lay on Hands, Hunter's Mark, Divine Smite.
- Went through the mirror first and killed the ice dragon.

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/080 - Into the Mirror 14 — The Scholar|Into the Mirror 14 — The Scholar]]

- Killed or finished the dragon with two back-to-back nat 20s.
- Found the white soul gem and keeps it.
- Directs the search for the orrery ("I command").
- Has never been to the new location.

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/088 - Into the Mirror 15–16, Old Friends - Sky|Into the Mirror 15–16, Old Friends - Sky]]
(Miss_Magitek; the YouTube cast says Locket)
- **Background:** A "traveler of these realms" who helped bring the others here. Has been in the Sands longer than the rest and believed there was no way out. Knows of the king (who has an ailment) and the queen.
- **The oath:** a paladin who admits, perhaps only to the table, that she is an **oath breaker** paladin. Swore a blood oath of fealty to the party against Vythorn.
- **In the fight:** knocked unconscious by Steel Wind Strike, healed, then scored a smiting critical on Sky.

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/093 - Into the Mirror 17, The Way Out|Into the Mirror 17, The Way Out]]
(also heard Cerys; player uncertain)
- Aasimar: Channel Divinity Abjure Enemy, radiant consumption ("supernova"), Haste.
- **Has been in the sands longer than the others and brought them to the city (Mortorios).** "That's all I knew."
- Gave a torch, rations and a shield to Sky; received a **+2 longsword with holy damage**.
