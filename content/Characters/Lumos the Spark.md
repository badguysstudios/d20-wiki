## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 3/050 - S3E1 — Kill em with Kindness — Lumos the Spark v Ferndell Ironwind|S3E1 — Kill em with Kindness — Lumos the Spark v Ferndell Ironwind]]
(Lumos's player).
- **A high elf**, obsessed with corgis from time spent with "the faith [fae?] folk," who ride corgis.
- **She owns Maximus, a corgi who can fly.**
- **In another dimension she is an office worker and bounty hunter**, a rival of Ferndell, and very wealthy ("quadrillion" dollars, per her will and Dried Blood).
- **Lactose intolerant**; her yogurt is special-order strawberry Greek, high in protein. She has cute boots in her favorite color (one heel cracked).
- **Spells this episode:** Healing Word, Word of Radiance, Wall of Fire, Fireball, a self-heal via hero point, Fast Friends. She carries a mace and has Daylight, Cure Wounds, Mass Healing Word and Mass Cure Wounds on her sheet.
- **Immune to blindness and deafness for the rest of the match** after saving vs Ferndell ("LASIK").
- **Died to Phantasmal Killer (Ferndell's mom). She left everything to Maximus**, and was revived for Dried Blood.
