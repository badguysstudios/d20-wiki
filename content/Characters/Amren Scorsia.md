(Miss_Magitek). Fire genasi from the Elemental Plane of Fire; flaming hair that flares when angry ("like Hades"); long black staff with a red gem; lava-skin armor (mage armor); sorcerer (Strength of the Grave). Proud, hates being called an assistant, wants a worthy fight. **Died in the lava, left as a lava statue; secretly remolded from lava by the Arbiter "into the perfect weapon."** (One of his secret recruits.)
