**The Scourge** is a self-proclaimed avenger of justice, a sleeveless aasimar "blessed since he was a little aasimar with the presence of the divine," who always speaks of himself in the third person. He was pulled into the Void to face the necromancer [[Bone Daddy]] in [[Episodes/Season 4/082 - S4E4 — Death Dealers — The Scourge v Bone Daddy|S4E4 — Death Dealers — The Scourge v Bone Daddy]]. He won by crushing his rival in a giant snake's coils and holding him close for one last heartfelt question. His victory prize from [[The Shadow King]] was the sort of reward that makes winning look like a mixed blessing.

## Story

### S4E4 — Death Dealers

The Scourge came through [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]]'s blood-soaked portal into the throne room of [[The Dread Fortress]] already in the middle of a wrestling promo. "The Scourge awakens," he announced, before condemning Bone Daddy as a "no good necromancer digging up people's pets and their grandmas." He was also furious that his rival was stealing his look, since "everybody knows the no-sleeves thing is the Scourge's thing." Bone Daddy called him a dingus, and the Grandmaster had to push the two apart with her bare arms. Before stepping into the arena, the Scourge raised his hand to ask about the [[Chinchillas]]. When he learned that every goblin on staff gets one for their mental health, he asked whether he could join the payroll too. The Grandmaster told him they would see if he was worthy first.

The arena was a dark dungeon used for "arm fights," a cavern littered with severed limbs from earlier Deathmatches. The Scourge started by scolding the fathers among [[The Acolytes]] who treat their children's seats as a babysitter: "a real dad's not a babysitter." Then he summoned two jet-black, white-striped Quetzalcoatlus, "just like me," and named them **Hammer and Anvil**. He turned himself into a giant constrictor snake and slithered straight at his opponent. Bone Daddy answered by raising a brood of skeleton "children." The Scourge admitted that Bone Daddy was "a very attractive man that fills the Scourge with some uncomfortable emotions."

The fight turned into a brawl of chaos. The Grandmaster dove from her throne and bent a metal chair around his skull, leaving cartoon birds circling his head. "Hey, those aren't the birds I intended to summon," he complained. From [[The Box of Chaos]] he bought a chronometer, a tiny hourglass of golden sand that reminded him of "my own sainted mother watching over the Scourge." He paid with PayPal, plus Bone Daddy's head "when this is all over." He used it at once to slow time and then burst into searing radiance. The Grandmaster summed it up: "Your bones were cracked and now you're a glow stick." His divine nature shrugged off most of Bone Daddy's necrotic magic. He spent much of the night missing, partly because Bone Daddy hypnotized him with a gyrating dance. His birds kept him alive, and Anvil landed a devastating dive out of the rafters. Meanwhile his foe merged his skeletons into a giant named [[Darcy]] and climbed inside her like a mech. Darcy then stabbed out her own only eye, and the Scourge twisted the knife: "See, she's starting to understand all those birthday parties and ballet receptions that you missed."

A cold snap shrank everyone a size and raised their voices ("Look, it's not always like this. I'm just tired, okay?"). Then the Shadow King dropped the fighters onto floating planks inside an electric Hexadome. Pressed close at last, the Scourge promised Bone Daddy "a warrior's death," and finally landed a hit. **He wrapped his coils around his rival and unleashed his full divine fury**, whispering, "Why wouldn't you let me love you, Bone Daddy? Why didn't you love me back?" Bone Daddy fell out of Darcy's chest and asked her forgiveness, and she carried him off the platform into the void. The Scourge urged the crowd to "fly your Bone Daddy flags with pride" and vowed statues to him in every graveyard. The Shadow King then gave him a keepsake. He tore a bone from a corpse in the arena and **shoved it, along with his whole arm, down the Scourge's throat**: "There! Now a piece of Bone Daddy can be with you forever." Whether the Scourge survived was never said.

## Personality and Abilities

The Scourge is all bravado and big feelings, a wrestling showman through and through. At the table, his player described him as "the macho man with the Undertaker's gimmick." He goes sleeveless as his signature look. Bone Daddy's jab about "luscious locks" suggests he has none to speak of, and the Scourge insisted that "you can't mock what a person can't change." He is quick with an insult ("I fell asleep in the middle of that long, boring monologue"). He is just as quick to admit tender or confusing feelings, which made his rivalry with Bone Daddy part grudge and part unrequited crush. He also posts online, under the name "absolutely not the scourge 1469."

His divine blessing makes him hard to hurt with dark or holy magic. When fully lit, his radiance burns everything near him, including, as he put it, his own "angelic flesh." He can take the form of a giant constrictor snake, summon beasts to fight for him, heal himself, and call down a crushing holy strike. His followers carry his banner into battle.

## Relationships

- [[Bone Daddy]]: His rival and, apparently, his unrequited love. He defeated him, mourned him, and vowed to build him statues.
- [[Darcy]]: Bone Daddy's skeleton "daughter," whom the Scourge needled about her absent father.
- **Hammer and Anvil**: The two black-and-white Quetzalcoatlus he summoned and named. "Daddy loves you both."
- [[The Shadow King]]: Called him maybe the best champion yet, then rewarded his win by ramming a bone down his throat.
- [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer]]: Broke a chair over his head, and reserved judgment on putting him on the payroll.
