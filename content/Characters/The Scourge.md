## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 4/082 - S4E4 — Death Dealers — The Scourge v Bone Daddy|S4E4 — Death Dealers — The Scourge v Bone Daddy]]
(BDaveWalters)
- **A Scourge aasimar**, "blessed since he was a little aasimar with the presence of the divine."
- His "sainted mother" watches over him; the golden-sand hourglass reminds him of her.
- Goes sleeveless and claims the no-sleeves look as his gimmick.
- **Resistant to necrotic and radiant damage.** His radiant glow burns even his own "angelic flesh."
- In this episode he fought as **a giant constrictor snake** (wild shape) alongside **Hammer and Anvil**, two black-and-white Quetzalcoatlus he conjured. Anvil is called "her."
- His followers carry his banner ("the Black Neathers [?]").
- Wants a mental-health chinchilla and a spot on the payroll.
- Inferred to be bald (the "luscious locks" jab; Dried Blood wig and Yeti jokes).
- Has mixed feelings for Bone Daddy ("uncomfortable emotions," "why didn't you love me back"). Posts as "absolutely not the scourge 1469."
- Won. He received the Box of Chaos chronometer, then had a bone rammed down his throat by the Shadow King.
- Vows Bone Daddy statues in every graveyard.
