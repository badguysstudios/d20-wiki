**Witch Hunter**, born Ricardo, was a centaur paladin who came to the arena to kill a bug and ended up falling for him. He handed out pamphlets for his god, bloomed marigolds whenever he saw someone attractive, and was allergic to healing potions. His Deathmatch against [[Grickle]] on [[The Frozen Lake]] began as a farce and ended as one of the strangest, saddest love stories the arena has seen.

## Story

### S4E6 — The Hunt

Witch Hunter came through the Grandmaster's blood-summoning portal into [[The Dread Fortress]] in the dark days when [[The Shadow King]] sat on the throne. [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|The Grandmaster]] was a ruler in name only. His first words were "Is this the bathroom?", and he blamed her for the building's lack of centaur accessibility. The Shadow King agreed with him. When Grickle the thri-kreen arrived, Witch Hunter took one look and declared, "Oh my god, you have a dump truck for a thorax." He then tried to talk Grickle into following "our lord and savior, Maliki." Marigolds began blooming all over him, and he explained what that meant. The Grandmaster plucked one off him and called the pairing perfect: "One who is ready to fight and hates the other, and the other loving the one who hates them."

On the frozen tundra, a chaos event gave each champion a magical mount that could take any beast's shape. Witch Hunter, noting that a horse was "technically my cousin," chose a naked, mustachioed, forty-something human with a dad bod and named him Bruce. The fight that followed was mostly a comedy of errors. Witch Hunter swung so badly that he and Bruce both flipped into the snow. He blasted Grickle with holy smites, one of which wrapped the bug in a rainbow to the tune of "Colors of the Wind" and left marigolds blooming from his wounds. In return he lost a nipple to Grickle's scimitar, and it went straight into an Acolyte's mouth. When Grickle's thrown war pick clipped the other one, the Grandmaster ruled it had become a small piercing "nipple dagger." She did this after Witch Hunter admitted the Shadow King's horrifying true face had excited him. A blinded Grickle later ran face-first into the dagger and lost an eye. Along the way Witch Hunter revealed that his real name was Ricardo. He had been a bounty hunter "until like an orphanage incident," then rebranded to hunt "anyone who doesn't believe in Maliki and uses magic." Most of his 69 kills, he admitted, came from that orphanage.

[[The Box of Chaos]] did him no favors. Bruce, freezing in the Shadow King's conjured storm with his face stuck to his own backside, rolled into the box as a sacrifice. It rewarded Witch Hunter with half a gallon of healing potion. He drank it, discovered he was allergic to the red dye, and choked on it. He traded the box his family's lucky rabbit's foot keychain and got a second potion of exactly the same kind. Then Grickle shot the ice out from under him, and the heavy centaur plunged into the freezing water.

The ending turned everything around. Grickle, moved by how kind Witch Hunter had been to him, ran over and tried to haul him out with all four arms. The two of them only managed to slap each other's hands away in what the Grandmaster called "the most awkward thing of a rescue mission." Even the Grandmaster cheered him on: "Oh, Witch Hunter Ricardo! Do not give up this fight tonight! Survive!" Instead, Witch Hunter gave Grickle his grandmother's glaive, wished him well, and let go. In his heavy chainmail, he sank into the cold. As he went down, his frozen marigolds broke off and floated to the surface "almost as a beautiful funeral." Grickle made grieving snow angels with the glaive clutched to his chest, until the Shadow King crushed him under a giant black boot. In the Shadow King's era, deaths in the arena stuck.

## Personality and Abilities

Witch Hunter was a centaur in heavy chainmail who fought with his grandmother's glaive and carried a spear and a stack of Maliki pamphlets. He was relentlessly upbeat, shamelessly flirtatious and quick with a strange line. He hit on his opponent, wondered aloud whether the two of them should start an OnlyFans, and called the fight a "love story for the ages." When he found someone attractive, marigolds bloomed on his body. He pressed their oil into his own skin to heal, caressing himself in a way that left the Acolytes "slightly uncomfortable, but also slightly aroused."

As a paladin, he could call down holy and searing smites, conjure a shimmering shield of faith, and mend his own wounds. Nothing could frighten him. He said he had learned to fight rogues from playing "an unhealthy amount of World of Warcraft." He had once had a gluten intolerance, which becoming a paladin cured. He never learned to swim, he had no father figure, and he had romantic feelings toward bug people. After the Grandmaster misspoke, he also gained the title "Witch King" by what the Arbiter called a "battlefield promotion."
