## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 4/084 - S4E6 — The Hunt — Witch Hunter v Grickle|S4E6 — The Hunt — Witch Hunter v Grickle]]
(D'Angelo). A centaur paladin.
- **Faith and origins:**
  - Worships **Maliki** and carries pamphlets about him.
  - **Real name Ricardo.** He was a bounty hunter until "an orphanage incident," then rebranded. He hunts "anyone who doesn't believe in Maliki and uses magic."
  - His kill count is 69, "most of that's an orphanage."
- **Body and gear:**
  - **Marigolds bloom on him when he finds someone attractive.** He uses their oil for Lay on Hands.
  - Wields **his grandmother's glaive** and wears heavy chainmail.
  - Lost one nipple to Grickle's scimitar. The other was clipped by Iron Fang, then turned into a piercing "nipple dagger."
- **Quirks:**
  - Played World of Warcraft (Caustic Phoenix: "canon").
  - **Allergic to the red dye in healing potions.**
  - Once had a gluten intolerance, cured by becoming a paladin.
  - Never learned to swim; had no father figure.
  - Called "Witch King" after a "battlefield promotion."
- **In this episode:** drowned under the Frozen Lake after giving Grickle the glaive.
