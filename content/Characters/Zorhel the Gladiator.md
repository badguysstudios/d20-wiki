## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 2/034 - S2E11 — Secrets — Blaktoof v Zorhel|S2E11 — Secrets — Blaktoof v Zorhel]]
(Drakoniques). A **bugbear** and "one of the most famous gladiators on my lands."
- **Combat:**
  - Fights with **a halberd made of his own blood**, drawn from his wounds. He also uses blood Shield and a blood spiritual weapon.
  - Part warlock (Hexblade's Curse, Hex, Thirsting Blade, Eldritch Sight, Darkness, Booming Blade, Summon Shadowspawn) and part paladin (aura of protection). Has the Sentinel feat.
  - Speaks Draconic, gruffly, when casting.
- **Backstory:**
  - His mother wanted him to be a doctor. He spent a few years in medical school but is queasy at death in operations.
  - **He became a gladiator to pay for medicine for his very ill little sister**, because bugbears aren't welcome most places except arenas.
  - His sister recovered and became a blacksmith. **He sacrificed the first, badly made short sword she forged to the Box of Chaos.**
  - Player-stated in Dried Blood: he learned from fellow students who drew too much blood.
- **Personality:** loves posture and ergonomic chairs, and is kind to Doug. Has zero Perception and can't climb ("PE class"). Hates daylight savings.
- **In this episode:** won the Iron Flask shadow demon (**Blaktoof 2.0**) and summoned **Shadowtoof**. Won the fight and was flown up to the Acolytes by the Grandmaster.
