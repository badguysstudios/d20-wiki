## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Skypiercer (Prequel)/001 - Skypiercer Part 1|Skypiercer Part 1]]
(sensei_suplex).
- Shield and spear; greatsword "the only time I use" it (1:16:07). One eye glows gold and the other purple when he takes up the greatsword (1:16:29). Scars and a small mohawk under his helmet (1:42:23).
- Shield sentinel of the **Order of the Sacrificent** (1:46:21). "I bleed for my allies." Uses the Rite of Revitalization / Crimson Rite and a blood curse that raises the fallen.
- Grew up in the gladiator pits (2:26:54). Commands "an army of only several hundred" (2:01:28). Elusia: "everyone studies your words. You are the standard for the training" (2:01:51).
- Thornos: "a pillar, a rock … a cornerstone of this city and its defense" (1:53:36). Named General.
- Battle cry: "Tonight, we dine in hell!" (1:14:31). "The only eyes I fancy is the gods, specifically the gods of death" (1:42:50).
- Drafts Lucien into his brigade; "Training begins at four in the morning" (2:13:02). Pledges to go to the Void with Elusia.

### [[Episodes/Skypiercer (Prequel)/002 - Skypiercer Part 2|Skypiercer Part 2]]
"I was just a simple gladiator at first" (0:19:18). Wields **Defender / Groundbreaker**. Trains ~500 at Atheranon: "We will win as brothers in arms" (3:33:11). Uses the blood curse of the anxious (hemocraft).

### [[Episodes/Skypiercer (Prequel)/003 - Skypiercer Part 3 (finale)|Skypiercer Part 3 (finale)]]
Knight of Chaos. Spears himself to pin Agdrel. Saved by Vythorn's time magic, but left with a large wound. "Today, I fight as the sword of the Empress."

### [[Episodes/Season 4/076 - S4E1 — Shadows Reign — Atalantos v Kaja|S4E1 — Shadows Reign — Atalantos v Kaja]]
(Sensei_Suplex). Raised from his sarcophagus by the Shadow King's cursed magic, his body covered in shadowy etherealness.
- A human **Blood Hunter** who calls himself "the spear of Cor'thun" and fights "for Cor'thun." He carries a spear and shield, plus a greatsword on his back: **the spear of Cor'thun becomes "the blade of Cor'thun."**
- **One divine golden eye and one shadow-infested eye**, which the Shadow King calls a kinship ("one shadow being to another").
- Has a "dusty old" first dagger from his uncle, which he gave to Robert Hartley's soul. Wins the Death Club robe-of-eyes crop top.
- Calls himself "an old man." Creed: "every scar is a lesson." He longs for a glorious warrior's death and dies happy and proud.
- In this episode: Rite of the Frozen, Blood Curses of the Eyeless and the Fallen Puppet, the Brand of Castigation (the symbol of Cor'thun), Shield Master shoves, Tides of Vitality. Healed by the Shadow King's blood. Killed by Kaja.
