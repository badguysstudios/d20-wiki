## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 5/100 - S5E2 — Identity Crisis — Kopy Katt v Professor Alonzo Quixote Cervantes|S5E2 — Identity Crisis — Kopy Katt v Professor Alonzo Quixote Cervantes]]
(Kat). A copier with no identity of her own: "I am everyone and I am everywhere… not one is my own." Called "a changeling of sorts" by Mudcat (Dried Blood).
- **Appearance:** wore a paper bag over her head. Underneath is a purplish, featureless face with a red-marker smiley face drawn like a three-year-old's. She puts on terrible masks (the Lord of Fate, papier-mâché Thea).
- **How she copies:** repeats others' words exactly and mirrors their actions. She keeps a book of cards to pick personas. Carries 67 IDs, 68 after robbing the patron "J".
- **Recruited** from the Social Security Administration office.
- **Abilities and tricks:**
  - A whistle that commands the unionized chinchillas.
  - Phone calls (identity theft, credit, the fake death report). "Your identity is everything."
  - Her signature move copies the opponent's (here a gorilla). She claims "a condition… I'm going to therapy."
- **Relationships:** gave the Box of Chaos shadow a new red chest, supposedly paid for by the Lord of Fate.
- **Partner:** tag-teamed with Emmery.
- Federal agents are reportedly tracking her for her "impressive record."

### [[Episodes/Season 5/110 - S5E12 — Imitation Game — Kopy Katt v Ragu|S5E12 — Imitation Game — Kopy Katt v Ragu]]
(Cat). A returning champion and identity thief.
- **Appearance and gear:**
  - Wears a paper bag over her head, replaced mid-fight with a reusable D20 Deathmatch bag.
  - **A trash bag over duct tape and fishnets.** She has never taken the trash bag off. Yellow boots.
- **Her real face:** light-purple skin, bald, no ears, blank, with **a marker-drawn face of two eyes and a smiley**. It smears when rubbed. "I've never had an identity to begin with… I am everyone, but no one at the same time."
- **Identity theft:** 69 or 74 stolen identities last time (per Thea), **420** now, including credit cards and livelihoods. She keeps a notebook of them.
- **The fail-safe chip:** a chip in her brain that, on her death, releases all stolen identities and a candy-store coupon.
- **Copying:** she can copy faces and voices, including the Lord of Fate's, but her mustache gives her away. She copies power-ups and moves.
- **Possessions:**
  - **The old Box of Chaos** (the Lord of Fate's "old chest"), kept from her last win, full of tricks: feather boas, Silly String, the photo album, the Prego mask.
  - **The Lord of Fate's cell phone**, with access to his "fate book" account and two-factor authentication.
  - Gold star stickers. **A spare brain in her pocket** (given to Thea).
- **She had given the box's shadowy figure his new box.** Here she gave him a USB-like chip, her trash bag and the Lord of Fate's phone, and asked him to coffee.
- She said "This might be my last fight." **She died by her own can opener on a natural 1** and was dragged off by goblins.

### [[Episodes/Season 5/114 - S5E16, Chaos Royal (Season 5 finale), the first Chaos Royale (four-way)|S5E16, Chaos Royal (Season 5 finale), the first Chaos Royale (four-way)]]
(guest). A shapeshifter ("foul flesh sculptor") who takes others' forms: the Lord of Fate, Thea, Tormiir ("Thornir"), Dick Twist.
- Was reincarnated after losing her last fight. Has attended group therapy ("before I stole all their identities").
- Her phone powers can reach the FDA, the IRS, a mug manufacturer and Tormiir's mother.
- **Winner, fused with the Hunger.** Calls the Lord of Fate "my other half."

### [[Episodes/Season 6/119 - S6 Ep3 — Ether Drifts Part 2 — Team A deathmatch (Atlas v Robbie Clark and the Hunger v Copycat)|S6 Ep3 — Ether Drifts Part 2 — Team A deathmatch (Atlas v Robbie Clark and the Hunger v Copycat)]]
