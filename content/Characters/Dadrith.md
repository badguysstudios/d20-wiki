## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 2/031 - S2E8 — Father's Love — Dadrith v Baron Voltágè|S2E8 — Father's Love — Dadrith v Baron Voltágè]]
(DreadedGM). A cleric, "dad of dads," who travels the multiverse spreading "the ideals of fatherhood" and collecting dad jokes "passed down from dad to dad."
- **Mission:** fathers who don't live up to fatherhood "face my wrath."
- **Faith:** prays to "**Moxie and the Eternal Fathers**" (deity names as heard). He has magic resistance and Divine Strike (fire).
- **Spells and powers:**
  - The "dad point" (Silvery Barbs).
  - The **rolled-up newspaper** (spiritual weapon), which later becomes a spectacled, wise-words father.
  - His "**son**," the summoned sun-celestial.
  - Word of Radiance delivered as a dad joke; Dispel Magic ("Dispel is Dispel"); heat metal as an ethereal grill.
  - "Dad vision."
- **Gear:**
  - Mace and shield.
  - A crossbow, sacrificed to the Box of Chaos.
  - **Winged boots** from the Box.
  - He was given the Arbiter's black Chuck Taylors, then said he'd wear the boots "instead."
- **In this episode:** he tried to give his own life to save Baron, and wept into the sea. He won. Calls the crowd "You can all call me Dad!"
