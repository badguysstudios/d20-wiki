## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Skypiercer (Prequel)/001 - Skypiercer Part 1|Skypiercer Part 1]]
Faucius's emissaries, protectors of the Dread Fortress (0:08:34). Vythorn: "There are none that remain. All slain [on] the night of the Long Shadow." (2:35:14). The Night of the Long Shadow is Agdrel's coup.

### [[Episodes/Skypiercer (Prequel)/003 - Skypiercer Part 3 (finale)|Skypiercer Part 3 (finale)]]
The old order: petrified statues in the Ceremonial Chamber, kept by Agdrel as trophies. Named: **Sir Eros, Sir Rickson, Sir Boros**. Elusia calls them "the highest order." The new order: Kaja and Atalantos.
