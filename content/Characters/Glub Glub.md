### [[Episodes/Season 2/028 - S2E4 — Antsy — Glub Glub v Geril-Lee|S2E4 — Antsy — Glub Glub v Geril-Lee]]
(Dave / carefulcantrip). A **hobgoblin**, "smartest of Hobgoblin tribe, smarter than Chieftain." He speaks in broken third person.
- **Background:**
  - Says he made the wheel and the claw, but "make no friend."
  - Went to **college**, where he was a science major ("experimental time"). He had a girlfriend there who did the butt-tickling "once."
  - College nicknames: "Walrus" and "Tripod." Motto: "Try everything twice."
- **Habits and looks:**
  - Wears a loincloth only, no pants: "part of tribes culture."
  - Hasn't washed in days ("vinegar flavor").
  - Uses the metric system.
  - Very low-hanging; very ticklish.
- **Gear and powers:**
  - Great club.
  - Rage with Totem of the Bear resistance.
  - Reckless attack and the Sentinel feat.
  - "Magic rock goggles": sticks with no lenses.
  - **Boom Boom**, a small wooden-legged eldritch cannon built from a wooden flag and hammer. It follows him and spits flame.
  - **Bony/Boney**, a tiny homunculus skeleton made from a small bone: big eye sockets, no jaw, a permanent smile. Bony is his only friend.
- **In this episode:**
  - Got a splinter from his own club.
  - Was a walrus for a round and had his butthole blessed by the Grandmaster.
  - Ripped off his loincloth and farted for the crowd's favor.
  - Lost to Geril-Lee and gave her Bony with his dying words.
  - Dragged off by the ankle by the Arbiter.
