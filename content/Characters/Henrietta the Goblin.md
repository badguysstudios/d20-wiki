**Henrietta the Goblin** is a small, adorable goblin with bright blonde hair who works at [[The Dread Fortress]] during the reign of [[The Shadow King]]. Her job is to bring the right thing at the right moment, whether that is a Hydro Flask for [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] to throw or a cup of black coffee for a champion about to fight. In two Deathmatches, she turned up with exactly what the scene needed.

## Story

### S4E12 — Racoons
Henrietta first appeared in [[Episodes/Season 4/092 - S4E12 — Racoons — Sam Cletus v Ben Zappa|S4E12 — Racoons — Sam Cletus v Ben Zappa]], the match between [[Sam Cletus]] and [[Ben Zappa]]. She had brought Hydro Flasks into the arena, and the Grandmaster put the last of them to good use. After Sam apologized to "Shakira," the Grandmaster bounced Henrietta's final Hydro Flask off his head.

The flask did no damage, "probably because your head's pretty empty anyway." Sam's skull was apparently "full of liquor, just sloshing around," like Darth Vader in a bacta tank but filled with whiskey. He swayed "like Johnny Depp… Pirates of the Caribbean." Henrietta also did some rolling on the Shadow King's behalf that night, so she served the man in charge as well as the Grandmaster.

### S4E13 — Swole
She was back in [[Episodes/Season 4/094 - S4E13 — Swole — Storm on the Horizon v Grug the Wizard|S4E13 — Swole — Storm on the Horizon v Grug the Wizard]]. [[Grug the Wizard]] had just told the story of how a "unintelligent barbarian" became "the most powerful wizard that one has ever seen." When he finished, Henrietta ran up to his opponent, [[Storm on the Horizon]], and offered her a cup of black coffee. Storm downed it in one go.

## Personality and Abilities
Henrietta is tiny, eager and easy to spot thanks to her bright blonde hair. She is described as adorable. She is quick on her feet: when a champion needs fuel, she runs up with it. Her skill is keeping the arena stocked, whether with flasks sturdy enough to throw at a drunk man's head or black coffee for a champion.

## Relationships
- [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer]]: Henrietta supplied the Hydro Flask the Grandmaster threw at Sam Cletus.
- [[The Shadow King]]: Henrietta did some rolling on his behalf in S4E12.
- [[Sam Cletus]]: He was hit on the head by the last of her Hydro Flasks, though he took no damage.
- [[Storm on the Horizon]]: Henrietta offered her a cup of black coffee before her fight, which Storm drank in one go.
