## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 3/061 - S3E14 — Magic Duel — Talbot Cage v Seth Kaiser|S3E14 — Magic Duel — Talbot Cage v Seth Kaiser]]
(Michael / pthofficial). A wild-magic caster, wizard and sorcerer spells both.
- **Pulled into the Void while heating his coffee with magic.**
- **Appearance:**
  - Age 28, aged to **48 mid-fight by wild magic**.
  - Malnourished; tattered green robes and strappy sandals, plus socks after the purple portal (one with a toe hole).
  - Magical burn scars.
  - **Mismatched eyes: emerald green, and dark purple with black capillaries.** Both ignite into flame when he channels power.
  - Hair unwashed for a month. Lost one eyebrow and some hair to the orange portal.
- **Hands:** **seven fingers on one hand, from a wild-magic accident.** He cut one off for Gerald and received the **Vorpal Pinkie**.
- **"Rage Cage":** an instinctual fugue state triggered by the magic damage. Normal Talbot wanders "inside the back of his head" during it. In his words, he usually "black[s] out" and comes back to everyone dead.
- **Companion:** Mr. Nutters, the squirrel.
- **Gear and spells:** a blade and a whip. Dancing Lights, Fire Bolt, Infestation (twinned), Fireball, Expeditious Retreat, Booming Blade. Three luck points, all used.
- **Death:** burned to death by Seth's white dragon after choosing to fail the save.
