*First appearance: [[Episodes/Season 3/061 - S3E14 — Magic Duel — Talbot Cage v Seth Kaiser|S3E14 — Magic Duel — Talbot Cage v Seth Kaiser]] · [watch it on YouTube](https://youtu.be/fU8cs88zRJw)*

**Talbot Cage** was a malnourished, scorch-scarred spellcaster whose magic was so unstable that it dragged him into [[The Void]] while he was only trying to warm up a cup of coffee. Played by Michael, he faced [[Seth Kaiser]] in a Halloween Magic Duel. He arrived with a squirrel on his shoulder, a spare finger on one hand and a habit of blacking out into a feral fugue known as "Rage Cage." He lost to a summoned white dragon. He went out on his own terms, though, and [[The Acolytes]] saluted him by raising their middle fingers.

## Story

Talbot made his entrance at "the masquerade pre-party party, obviously," held in the ballroom of [[The Dread Fortress]]. He rose through a portal made from a splattered pumpkin, with his squirrel Mr. Nutters squeaking furiously at him to "use the magic." He was baffled and immediately went looking for a way out, and [[The Arbiter]] told him flatly: "There's no exit. You've been summoned for the Deathmatch." He wore a mask too, but only because it soothed Mr. Nutters. That prompted [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] to declare, "This man is nuts. Literally, he has to be nuts for us all to like him." When Seth sneered at him as a "third-rate something with a fourth-rate squirrel," Talbot offered to forfeit, and the Grandmaster answered with two words: "No forfeiting."

The arena of [[Episodes/Season 3/061 - S3E14 — Magic Duel — Talbot Cage v Seth Kaiser|S3E14 — Magic Duel — Talbot Cage v Seth Kaiser]] was a cornless corn maze full of colored portals. Talbot strode straight into the orange one, believing its glow was an exit sign, and it singed his hair. He came out near Seth and promised him "a taste of my power." Wind whipped around him, his mismatched eyes burst into flame, and Mr. Nutters fled into the maze in terror. The great display turned out to be a few little dancing lights ("The climax was so disappointing," said the Grandmaster). Seth's summoned fey then trapped him in a cube of magical darkness. When a pair of giant laser-firing eyeballs fell from the sky, Talbot's wild magic drew one of them straight to him for a second blast, and Rage Cage took over: an instinctual fugue in which he fired bolts of flame out of the darkness. They all missed, and his retreat back through the orange portal cost him an eyebrow, which he insisted he was "styling it slowly through walking through portals."

Still in the fugue, Talbot exhaled a wind that sent the maze's spiders, centipedes and fleas swarming over Seth's summoned fey deer, which the Grandmaster had nicknamed the "pickle goat." The bugs gnawed its front hooves down to bone. He then reached [[The Box of Chaos|Gerald]] and, without flinching, cut off one of the seven fingers on his hand as a sacrifice. Gerald shut his eyes, squeamish at the blood squirting out, but took the finger and gave him back the **Vorpal Pinkie**, a prosthetic finger with an obsidian switchblade. Talbot's only comment was "I wasn't using that." [[Doug the Pug]] bit a pumpkin and left a cloud of farts so foul it was almost vomit-inducing, and both champions fought one-handed for a while, the other hand clamped over nose and mouth (Talbot used his bloody, still-six-fingered hand). Talbot then lashed his whip around a gargoyle, failed to pull it down, and swung across "fucking Tarzan style" to drop-kick the deer instead. When a grinning, handless riddle creature demanded a three-letter word beginning with T, the ordinary Talbot finally surfaced from "the darkness inside the back of his head" and shouted "Toe!" It was the wrong answer. The fugue broke, and the creature's arm came crashing down on him.

Back in his own body, Talbot pulled off what the Grandmaster called **"quite possibly the most brilliant move of the evening."** He hurled a Fireball at the explosive pumpkins surrounding Seth, the deer and the riddle beast, set them up like powder kegs, and yelled "You've fallen for my trap card!" Then everything went wrong. Gerald, now Seth's friend after a generous trade of cards, yanked Seth into the box for safety. The deer escaped as well. Wild magic aged Talbot from 28 to 48 in an instant, and his aching back sent the Fireball far off target. The purple portal left his new varicose veins glowing and somehow put socks under his sandals. [[Dolly]] rose from the ground and engulfed him in a putrid green cloud. He enchanted his socks, raced to Seth and begged for a truce while secretly readying the pinkie, but Seth saw through it and summoned a turtle-shelled white dragon that scorched him to one knee. Three pinky strikes all missed, including one the Grandmaster helped along by turning back time for a moment ("Time magic's my thing," protested the Arbiter). Talbot raised the blade in the middle-finger position and said "Just fucking do it." He chose not to resist the dragon's final breath and handed over his vorpal finger with his last words:

> "I just wanted to warm up my coffee."

The Acolytes raised their middle fingers in solidarity. On the railing, Mr. Nutters shook his head in disappointment, then took off his fez and held it over his heart as Talbot burned away to a ball of flame.

## Personality and Abilities

Talbot was in his late twenties (until the wild magic got to him), underfed and unwashed. He wore tattered green robes, strappy sandals and, eventually, a pair of socks with a hole in one toe. Magical burn scars covered his hands, neck and face. One eye was deep emerald green and the other a dark purple with black veins, and both ignited when he channeled power. A wild-magic accident had left him with seven fingers on one hand, and he joked that he probably had spare organs too. His constant companion was Mr. Nutters, a squirrel in a vest and fez.

His magic mixed wizard and sorcerer spells, all of it unpredictable. When he took magical damage, he lapsed into **Rage Cage**, a feral fugue. By his own account he usually "black[s] out" and wakes to find "everybody's dead." He fought with a blade, a whip, fire, swarms of insects and, eventually, the Vorpal Pinkie. He was nervous and confused, and he never stopped looking for the exit. In the Dried Blood after-show, he left one piece of hard-won advice: "If your coffee ever gets cold, just make it iced coffee… Your wild magic will throw you into a deathmatch and you'll die."

## Relationships

- [[Seth Kaiser]]: His opponent and killer, who called the final blow "a mercy" and admitted, "I don't feel good about that."
- [[The Box of Chaos|Gerald]]: Took Talbot's spare finger in exchange for the Vorpal Pinkie, then saved Seth from Talbot's pumpkin trap.
- [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|The Grandmaster]]: Refused his forfeit, but "wept for him" and bent time to give him one last swing.
- [[The Arbiter]]: Summoned him and told him the only way out was "in a body bag."
- [[Dolly]]: Rose from the arena floor and choked him with her necrotic cloud.
