**Cornelius Antonio Bunga**, "C.A. Bunga, Cowabunga for short," is a slice of buttered sourdough toast with a syrup mustache, a leaf top hat and impeccable manners. He was one of three guests at the great feast in [[The Dread Fortress]] that opened the Season of the Trials, and it turned into a murder mystery when [[Thea]] was found dead in the gravy. A married gentleman, a dairy businessman and a devoted keeper of his own butter, Bunga spent the evening fending off people who wanted to eat him. Along the way he turned up the key clue that cracked the case.

## Story

Bunga waddled up the bloody carpet worrying about the heat ("Well, good heavens, that's gonna be hell on my butter"), steadying himself on a breadstick cane. [[The Arbiter]] declared him "a fancy gentleman." [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|The Grandmaster]] took one look and asked for a bite. He declined firmly ("I think not, madam") and told her he was married. That set the pattern for the night. The newly created Thea dipped a finger in his syrup and spun off on a sugar rush. Lady Elizabeth Barry Ann Poopshoe, a Loxodon aristocrat, batted her eyelashes at him and called him "delicious." Bunga said he belonged to Lady Tortiliana, and Lady Elizabeth sneered that this was her rival. He pretended to bow and squeezed a pool of butter onto the floor in front of her. She stepped neatly over it and swept off calling everyone trash. When Jercasso, a hungry little trench coat, leaned in for "just a little piece to take home," Bunga swatted them with his cane, rolled between their legs, popped up and kicked them in the tushy.

At cocktail hour he ordered a goblet of melted butter, unsalted, because he was on a diet. He also talked shop with Skelman, a skeleton dungeon designer who wanted to hire him. Bunga mentioned that he ran a dairy back home and wondered whether [[Tim]] the gelatinous cube's acidic goo could work with milk (Skelman had seen Tim eat cows whole, and "the milk did not remain"). The two came up with a butter trap to replace a dungeon's oil trap, since anything set alight would only "brown nicely." When someone hoped Bunga's notebook was a Pop-Tart, he shut it down at once: Pop-Tart was an ex, "and that jelly was toxic."

Once the candles went out and Thea turned up dead, suspicion fell on Bunga. His dinner knife sat slightly askew, and the Arbiter found a napkin by his place with green blood on it. Lady Elizabeth was so quick to call the knife suspicious that a goblin had to lead her away. Bunga answered that the syrup limbs that made up his arms and feet left a sticky residue on everything they touched, so a napkin he had handled would be sticky. To make the point he finger-gunned a shot of syrup at [[Stuart]], who blocked it with his dinner plate and then shoved his face into his food. The whole hall then reached over to feel how sticky Bunga was. Later he caught the fleeing Jercasso with one sticky arm laid across the table. Their coat burst open to reveal three striking [[Chinchillas]] who had been stealing food. The Arbiter's own accusation was short: Bunga was "full of gluten. That's it, that's the entire accusation." Bunga said he did not want to babysit "that child."

His best moment came when he studied [[Iris]]'s stitched-up arm through a pair of handheld spectacles. He found that the black thread the "doctor" had pulled from Thea's body matched the hodgepodge in her stitches. Soon after, the Arbiter used his "time eyes" to run the night backwards, and everyone saw that Iris's was the murdering hand. Bunga sympathised with her vengeance ("As a married host, I kind of understand her sentiment"). He also suggested the Arbiter bring back her murdered husband so she could get closure. The Arbiter told him "You're the kind of mind that we could really use around this place." Bunga said he was very interested but would need to talk to the mistress first. He held up Skelman's skull so it could watch Iris blow Tony apart, and he ended the night holding Skelman's big toe, which the doctor had licked and stuck onto a femur. The Grandmaster toasted him by name as the banquet's graduates were sent off.

## Personality and Abilities

Bunga is a hip-high slice of sourdough, toasted and buttered golden on both sides. He wears a leaf cut into a little top hat and glued to his crust with syrup. His syrup eyes blink, he has a glorious syrup mustache, and he walks with an appetizer breadstick for a cane. He smooths the butter down his front like a tie and squishes "like a nice little roll" when poked. He speaks with genteel formality, which makes it funnier when he snaps, "I'm a piece of fucking toast. I don't know anything about eating."

He is loyal to his wife, Lady Tortiliana. The couple can't have children, and he dislikes hearing his ex brought up. For a gentleman he fights dirty, with tricks his body can supply. He can pour slick pools of butter, shoot syrup from his fingers, roll up and unroll to slip past enemies, and grab people with arms so sticky they can't break free. He is also sharp-eyed and cool under accusation, and he came out of the evening as one of its better detectives.

## Relationships

- [[Iris]]: fellow guest whose stitching he matched to the thread found on Thea; he sympathised with her revenge and argued for her closure.
- [[Thea]]: dipped a finger in his syrup at their first meeting; he was briefly suspected of her murder.
- [[The Arbiter]]: accused him of being "full of gluten," then praised his mind and seemed to offer him work.
- [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|The Grandmaster]]: wanted a bite of him; he refused, and she later toasted him by name.
- [[Stuart]]: got a syrup shot to the face when Bunga demonstrated how sticky he was.
- [[Tim]]: Bunga wondered whether Tim's goo might work in his dairy business.
- Lady Tortiliana: his wife, and Lady Elizabeth's rival.
