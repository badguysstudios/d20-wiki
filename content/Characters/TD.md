## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 7/134 - S7E6 — Bone Force — Jerry Bonesman v TD|S7E6 — Bone Force — Jerry Bonesman v TD]]
(Crit Bard)
- **Who it is:**
  - A construct. Its skin has a metallic sheen, its blue eyes are artificial, blue crystals hover around it, and it wears a functional cape.
  - Pronouns at the table are he and they.
  - Speaks Draconic as well as Common. Has no tongue and cannot eat or drink.
- **Its maker:**
  - Created by "**my master**." It doesn't know how it was made, and it hums a tune its master hummed.
  - "**Only one has ever beat me**": the master, by context.
  - A memory log shows a **bluish-skinned, possibly draconic person celebrating a first birthday: "the one he was created for."**
- **Its directives:**
  - Training others ("each one teach one"); "**his directive is to protect**."
  - Its **second directive is necromancy**, and its eyes turn green.
  - Its eyes turn red in "obliteration mode."
- **Personality:** speaks in tests, lessons and math riddles; asks for well-being "in a series of numbers."
- **Gear and spells:**
  - An abjuration wizard: Mage Armor, Arcane Ward, Armor of Agathys, Blade Ward, Wall of Force, Protection Rune, Counterspell, Arcane Vigor, Mirror Spell, Synaptic Static, Mirrored Agony, Mirror Image, Silvery Barbs, Psychic Lance.
  - Wears **a 25-ft intestine belt** from Thea.
- **In this episode:**
  - The Grandmaster's chosen champion. Defended Jerry against Seavold, killed him, and won the pizza (intestine toppings).
  - Was last seen holding Jerry's bony hand.
