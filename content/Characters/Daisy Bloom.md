## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 4/085 - S4E7 — Wings — Misery v Daisy Bloom|S4E7 — Wings — Misery v Daisy Bloom]]
(TAYvanbiljon). A paladin ("Captain Paladin," per Caustic Phoenix) in plate.
- **Gear and abilities:** two warhammers (Two-Weapon Fighting), a javelin, three lanterns and a glow stick. She casts Find Steed and Sanctuary, has "stupid protection spells" but no healing, speaks Primordial, and animal handling is her dump stat. She has a "mysterious patron."
- **Lore and personality:**
  - Her makeup "is not makeup"; she was "born this way."
  - Her favorite sauce is ketchup.
  - She brings "the power of friendship and love and light" and came to make Misery her friend.
  - Her orphan story: parents killed by a supernatural force of evil; chosen by an alien; drank liters of her parents' blood mixed with dirt; "took out like 15 villages"; mobs calm her.
  - Pockets her rib bones.
  - Says she pulls different steeds from the Void each time.
  - Devoted to Criss Angel (has a poster, prays to him).
  - Over 30.
- **Transformation:** cast Find Steed inside herself and became 12% moth (moth on top, human legs).
- Thigh-choked Misery. Decapitated by Misery's vorpal crit.
