## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 4/091 - S4E11 — Beatdown — Sprinkles v Nigel|S4E11 — Beatdown — Sprinkles v Nigel]]
(RRollplaying). A **halfling** dressed like a waiter: rolled shirt, vest, slacks, dress shoes.
- **Personality and lore:**
  - A **pacifist** and gardener. Wins employee of the month for being good-natured.
  - Hands out friendship bracelets in many colors.
  - Was a PA on "Ack Blattom" and worked on *Tooth Fairy* with the Rock.
  - Grew up with many siblings who called him "stinky head." Self-conscious about acne and the hair on his feet; proud of his hygiene.
  - Made a bet that he could win with the power of friendship.
  - **Inside him lives a "dark passenger," whom he calls "she… my friend… she made a bet with me." She gets angry so he doesn't; he counters her with kindness. "I made a mistake… a long time ago. And now I'm living with it."**
- **Gear:** a trowel (summoned by the Shadow King), a grappling hook signed by Nigel, a silver pistol on his sheet, and the **cursed cloak of Nigel's face** from the Box of Chaos.
- **In this episode:** his body is left behind when Melchizedek moves into Nigel. He gardens a flower field with Nigel the Goblin, then is drowned in slime.
