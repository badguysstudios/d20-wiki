## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 2/030 - S2E7 - Hand to Hand Combat, Bargo v Carn|S2E7 - Hand to Hand Combat, Bargo v Carn]]
Twelve (eleven counted) tiny baby minotaurs, "a certain unique breed that have evolved within these dark caverns," armed with mini axes.
- Four were gusted off the ledge. The rest fell in a hand-holding chain after Grease.
- **One became Bargo's admirer and companion** before falling with the others.
