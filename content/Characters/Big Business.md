## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 7/130 - S7E2 — Under the Oil Tree — Big Business v Bog Boy|S7E2 — Under the Oil Tree — Big Business v Bog Boy]]
(Nate). A towering **Warforged paladin**, 6'7"–7'9", wreathed in steam. He was killed and then fused into Bog Boy.
- **Body:**
  - Shovel/scoop hands that combine into a bulldozer (a "Transformer").
  - A core that glows bright green and goes "cha-ching."
  - "Odor cannons" and turbines that spray gold coins and cash; exhaust pipes and slot-machine sevens on his leg.
  - A chest receptacle, head scanners, a clear-coat shield, and an internal stock ticker that prints money.
  - "Bleeds money," "like a piñata"; a "money port."
- **Gear and powers:** maul, shield of cash, Lay on Hands, smites, Bless.
- **History and claims:**
  - Claims the swamp as his land and wants to build a shopping mall on it.
  - **Poured oil through the swamp "trying to grow an oil tree."**
  - Says he backhanded Bog Boy when Bog Boy was a little turtle, and **"He was a human child. Then I put him in the oil tree."**
  - **Clementine was his "fish wife" and left him.**
  - Used to hang out with **Mandroid**, who connected machines and vending machines.
  - His only instinct is capitalism. He offered Bog Boy a low-paying mall manager job.
  - His last thoughts were of "all them poor little dollars he got at home."
- **After death:** Griselda integrated his scraps into Bog Boy, and his consciousness lives on inside him.
