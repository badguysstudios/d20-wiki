## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 3/059 - S3E11 — Blood Feud 1 — Havoc v Isha Inverraseth|S3E11 — Blood Feud 1 — Havoc v Isha Inverraseth]]
Green smoke in a deceptively sharp gold lamp; he takes payment in blood. He is touchy about being called a shrub or bush, and is a knockoff genie with limited powers. He granted Isha's wish to return to the starting points.
