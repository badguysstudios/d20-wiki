### [[Episodes/Season 2/029 - S2E5 — The Gauntlet — Dan Kowalski v Gordy Gotta Get Em|S2E5 — The Gauntlet — Dan Kowalski v Gordy Gotta Get Em]]
(endlesschad). A **satyr**: "Danimal on the weekends, Daniel to my mother." He has horns and cloven hooves.
- **Background:**
  - Says his mom and dad "fell in love" and that he's "part mountain goat."
  - **Hates being called Daniel.**
  - Has a cousin, "Dan Koala," and a friend, **James** (sent condolences via a ghost, so inferred to be dead).
  - Likes partying, kombucha and bowling (wants the six-pound pink ball).
  - Quotes made-up wisdom from "my friend" Socrates, Aristotle and Leonardo da Vinci.
  - "Dan knows his horticulture."
- **Gear and powers:**
  - Plays a **wargong** kept in a magic pocket; performance expertise.
  - Magic resistance, Mirthful Leap, persuasion proficiency; speaks Common and Sylvan; no darkvision.
  - Spells: Earthen Grasp, fire bolt, sleep, dimension door, tidal wave, Summon Draconic Spirit (**"Gerard the Dragon"**, red with antlers like his), mage armor, Thunder Step.
- **In this episode:**
  - **Cut off part of his own horn** for the Box of Chaos and got the Sphere of Annihilation, which he destroyed in the dungeon.
  - Wore a candle in his broken horn.
  - Charmed the snakes and the Babau.
  - **First winner of the Gauntlet.**
