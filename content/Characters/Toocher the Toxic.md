## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 4/083 - S4E5 — Toxicity — Toocher the Toxic v Sho Nin|S4E5 — Toxicity — Toocher the Toxic v Sho Nin]]
(twooftheyangs).
- **Who he is:**
  - A grung (frog person) whose parents were both frogs; hatched from an egg. He has a tragic, long backstory "requiring several flashbacks."
  - A professional wrestler. Level 6 Battle Master fighter / level 4 Inquisitive rogue.
  - Poisonous skin, exceptional leaping, Tavern Brawler.
- **Look:** shirtless, in pants and a shield, with a flamboyant red "statement" scarf.
- **Gained in this episode:** Bracers of Defense shaped like red arm scarves, from the Box of Chaos.
- **His family:** a relative had an inner wind ability (not flatulence); Toocher's Second Wind draws on it, and at the end his own "inner wind" made his warts glow.
- **His act:** trash talk, and "partners… putting on a good show."
- **Result:** won the match and inspired red-scarf merch ($29.99).
