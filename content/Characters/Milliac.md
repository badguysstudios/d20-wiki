**Milliac** is a big-bellied bear of a man from "the woods, down the block" who came to the Void because a flyer promised pie and punch. In [[Episodes/Season 1/019 - S1E10 — The Bear — Diamond Joe v Milliac|S1E10 — The Bear — Diamond Joe v Milliac]] he fought the elderly inventor [[Diamond Joe]] in an abandoned black-powder mine. He spent the fight turning into polar bears, summoning more polar bears, and finally building a bear out of explosives. He lost to his own bomb, but [[The Arbiter]] quietly scooped up what was left of him and flew off.

## Story

Milliac arrived through the Arbiter's portal from a cluttered lab in the depths of [[The Dread Fortress]]. He announced that he didn't know who this "Steely Joe" was and had only come because "I was told there'd be pie and punch. I come bringing the punch." [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|The Grandmaster]] took this as proof that her flyers worked. The arena was Farcheck's old black-powder camp, littered with barrels and pots of explosive powder. Before the first blow, a shimmer of chaos coated both champions' skin. When it took hold, it made every punch thrown at an opponent swing back into the attacker's own face.

The fight opened more like a first date. Diamond Joe strolled over to ask Milliac's favourite pie ("Cherry Pie," sung with feeling) and then handed him a cream pie while the chaos sorted itself out. The pie excited Milliac so much that "Milliac can't control the beast inside," and he burst into a polar bear. In bear form he sniffed out the hidden powder barrels. Then, **drawing on his days in a traveling circus, he balanced a barrel on his feet, spun it, and hurled it at Joe**, who had nodded off mid-fight. The blast landed hard. His attempt to heal afterward fixed roughly one paper cut.

Joe struck back with thunderous farts, lightning breath, and a shove that slammed the bear into a second barrel. A flying tackle meant to knock Joe off the ledge sent Milliac sailing off it instead. A kindly "helping hand" from Joe's still-switched-on thunder gauntlet jolted him back into human form. Milliac answered by rubbing powder between his palms and heating Joe's armor from the inside ("it looks like you're a little cold, don't worry, I will heat you up"). Rolling in grease and rage, he summoned a polar bear he named **Honey Boo Boo**. The Arbiter split Honey Boo Boo into two bears, and a parade of dancing bears swept the whole arena off the high ground. After taking bolt after bolt, Milliac shrugged off a lightning strike so completely that the Grandmaster declared him resistant to lightning from then on.

The bears did not last. Honey Boo Boo Prime fell to Joe's lightning, and a final launching fart flung the surviving copy and Milliac's last bear form into the air and down again. That left Milliac human and barely alive. With the Acolytes' roar behind him, he refused to go out quietly. **"In honor of Honey Boo Boo," he drew every grain of black powder in the arena into a gigantic black bear with a fizzing fuse for a tail, and dropped it on everyone, himself included.** Joe shielded himself against the blast and survived, and Milliac did not. [[Doug the Pug]] ran over to lick his singed face. The Arbiter then grabbed him by the foot, muttered that "I guess I might find a use for you somewhere," secretly healed him just enough to breathe, and flew him away unseen.

## Personality and Abilities

Milliac is cheerful, earnest and a little simple, with a big belly "for a reason" and an encyclopedic love of pie: apple, blueberry, boysenberry, elderberry, and above all cherry. He doesn't wear much clothing, disapproves of Joe's pills, and has been practising his growl ("rawr, rawr"). He takes offence when talked down to as a cute little bear, and he gets openly emotional about his summoned companions.

He fights like a woodland ranger with a druid's heart. He shifts into a polar bear, his "natural shape," whenever he gets excited. He can whip himself across the arena in a burst of speed that trails dust (the Grandmaster asked if he was Sonic the Hedgehog). He can heat metal armor, heal his own wounds, mark a foe so he can follow them, and shape loose earth with his will. Whenever he summons animals, they are always polar bears: "That's my power. I just summon bears. It's quite unbearable." He also keeps javelins in his backpack, though grease tends to ruin his grip.

## Relationships

- [[Diamond Joe]]: His opponent, who fed him pie, called him "Pie Boy" and "Milli Vanilli," and beat him with farts, lightning and protective magic.
- [[The Arbiter]]: Secretly revived Milliac after his death and carried him off, saying he might find a use for him.
- [[Doug the Pug]]: Ran to the fallen Milliac and licked his face.
- [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|The Grandmaster]]: Cheered him on ("Be strong, Milliac. I cheer for you") and granted him resistance to lightning.
- **Honey Boo Boo**: His summoned polar bear, later duplicated by the Arbiter, whose death he avenged with the black-powder bear.
