**Fil**, also called **Philomena** and **the God of Fate**, is the most dangerous being the show has met. She is a true god, far more powerful than [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]], [[The Arbiter|the Arbiter]] or anything in the old legends, and she treats mortal lives as chips on a betting table. She steered the Arbiter's lost soul back from the dead, put an apocalyptic tome in his hands and crowned him in a vision of power. Then she left smiling, with a debt still owed.

## Story

### Vythorn's Return Part 1
After the Arbiter, Vythorn, died at the hands of the Shadow King, his soul clawed its way out of the Sands of Eternity. On the way out, Fil sidetracked it. She told Vythorn that he had lost his parents to his own uncontrolled chaos. It was a story that would not survive the next chapter.

### [[Episodes/Vythorn's Return/096 - S4E15, Vythorn's Return Part 2|Vythorn's Return Part 2]]
Here Fil showed her hand. She was a chestnut-haired woman who called the silent forest her "home," and she made her servants out of dirt. Both [[Ise]] and [[Biff]] were shaped from the ground by her. She ran tests, and those who failed would "hang out with me for the rest of eternity," either as dirt or in the "sleep of the ages." She spoke of "bets going on up above" and of the "God of Fates," plural, and she complained of having "less to bet on." She also kept up a correspondence with [[Master Skreca]], sending him letters, gifts and visitors.

Fil had bound Ise to her as a crow "pet," and she granted Biff eternal sleep. She has one known pet peeve: she hates the phrase "fate's decided this." Ise, for her part, showed Vythorn the truth that Fil had left out, which was that the Shadow King had killed his parents. By the end of the chapter, Fil had given Vythorn the Testaments of [[Morir's Cataclysm]], a tome of enormous power and destruction.

### [[Episodes/Vythorn's Return/098 - S4E17 (Season Finale) - Vythorn's Return Part 3|Vythorn's Return Part 3]]
Vythorn woke far from his companions and far from the Ruins of Giant's Breath. He lay on soft ground under a sunset, with birds, bugs and distant cheering around him, and dug his hands into the warm dirt. He had found home. Fil shook him awake: "Get up, Vythorn… Wake up."

She explained that she and her side had "placed our bets" on him, and that bringing him home was "a gift." When he needed a push, **she froze time itself** to urge him on. With the Cataclysm in hand, Vythorn destroyed the Shadow King's body, though the King's soul escaped. Fil then wrote him a contract that made him **Lord of Fate**, with full command of [[The Void|the Void]]. She attached one condition:

> "We will be watching… or it will all go away. Because we can do that."

All the way through, she spoke as "we." She never said who the others were.

### The Season of Fate and [[Episodes/Vythorn's Return/115 - S5 Ep17 — Vythorn's Return Part 4|Vythorn's Return Part 4]]
The Season of Fate that followed, with Vythorn ruling as Lord of Fate, turned out to be a vision of what ruling could look like, and only he lived through it. At its end, he found himself back in a familiar scene: torn-up ground, angry clouds and a downpour of rain, a memory "that has not yet been completed." Fil whispered in his ear the question she kept asking: "Who are you going to become?"

She reminded him of their deal: "The book was given. The void laid out in front of you." Then came the warning: "We will have to come to collect." Fil left with a Cheshire Cat smile and an echoing chuckle. As she went, the frozen rain began to fall again and the Grandmaster could move once more.

### Since Then
Back in the real timeline, the Arbiter went on serving under the Grandmaster, still holding the Cataclysm. He has told her what he went through, and he has warned her about the threat Fil poses. When the Pieces of a King adventure spoke of "something big that could kill us all," it meant Fil. The Season of Vigilance carries hints of her. The twins of Season 4 hate the God of Fate, and Fil is the god they meant.

## Personality and Abilities

Fil is playful, smug and terrifying in equal measure. She talks about souls the way a gambler talks about horses, and she seems to enjoy mortal struggles as long as they pay out. She bristles at the idea that fate is ever simply "decided." She appears as a woman with brown chestnut hair, and she likes to whisper to people rather than shout at them.

Her power is on a different scale from anyone else's. She shapes living servants from dirt, freezes time, and can hand out command of the entire Void in a written contract. Her realm, like the Ruins of Giant's Breath, lies outside all material planes, the Void included. Nobody knows whether she is one of many gods or simply fond of a royal "we." She can wrap people in endless sleep, and she treats tests and deals as binding. When she says she will come to collect, she means it.

## Relationships

- [[The Arbiter]]: her chosen bet. She guided his soul, gave him the Cataclysm, made him Lord of Fate in a vision, and is owed a debt.
- [[Ise]]: shaped from dirt by Fil and bound to her as a crow "pet."
- [[Biff]]: another of her dirt-born creations, granted eternal sleep.
- [[Master Skreca]]: her correspondent, to whom she sends gifts and visitors.
- [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer]]: held still in frozen time until Fil departed, and later warned about her by the Arbiter.
