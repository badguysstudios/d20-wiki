**Geril-Lee** is a small, nervous-seeming champion with eight tongue piercings, twin daggers and a startlingly dark streak. She was played by winterstales. She arrived in the Void insisting there had been a mistake, then won one of the strangest fights in Deathmatch history in [[Episodes/Season 2/028 - S2E4 — Antsy — Glub Glub v Geril-Lee|S2E4 — Antsy — Glub Glub v Geril-Lee]]. She beat [[Glub Glub the Glorious|Glub Glub]], the self-proclaimed smartest hobgoblin of his tribe, by refusing to fight fair in the most creative ways she could think of.

## Story

Geril-Lee came out of [[The Arbiter|the Arbiter's]] portal confused and frightened. "Please, mister. Uh, I think there's been a mistake… I'm a little bit of a fighter… Where are we?" The Arbiter was mortified, since the two of them had apparently had "a whole conversation in the portal on the way here." [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|The Grandmaster]] wasn't fooled. She called the champion "sweet and timid in nature, but something tells me a little more fierceness lurks below on this surface." The fight took place in the Grandmaster's great-grandfather's old hunting grounds, beside a roaring waterfall and a broken stone bridge. Within moments Geril-Lee proved the Grandmaster right. She backflipped over the gap in the bridge and went straight for Glub Glub's throat with both daggers, then cheerfully announced, "I'm gonna drink your blood for breakfast." When he objected that this was not sanitary, she replied, "It is in my village."

Geril-Lee fought fast and dirty. She slid under the bridge to slash at Glub Glub's ankles, landing a perfect natural 20, and dodged the worst of his great club. When he raged at her, she used a minor spell called Annoyance to make him sneeze at the worst possible moment. Once she learned that Glub Glub wore no pants, only a loincloth, she summoned an invisible servant whose sole job was to tickle his backside every time she attacked. She played innocent with "big, big innocent eyes" so he wouldn't catch on. Then she waded into the waterfall, where [[The Box of Chaos]] burned beneath the water. As her sacrifice she cut off her long raven hair and threw it in. In return the Box gave her a Gray Bag of Tricks, though she was left looking "like a rabid animal."

The fight only got stranger from there. A bark from [[Doug the Pug]] turned both champions into beasts for a round, and Geril-Lee became a panther facing a monstrous walrus. Her great pounce missed, and she landed on a pebble as though that had been the plan all along. Back in her own body, she pulled a weasel from her new bag and sent it after Glub Glub's most vulnerable region. He eventually killed the creature by punching it. The Arbiter then flooded the river and swept both fighters downstream. Geril-Lee announced that she would "be fair and not go for that region," since his loincloth was now gone. She used her knowledge of the major arteries in the legs to aim, and landed natural 20s on two attacks in a row. "Eat my dust," she told him.

**The final blow came from an ant.** Geril-Lee conjured a single ant with her spell and sent it crawling through Glub Glub's "short and curlies" while she attacked. As the ant reached its destination, she unleashed a flurry of blows with two more natural 20s in a row. "It's the ant butthole magic," she declared. Glub Glub fell to his knees, called her the superior fighter, and handed her his only friend, a tiny jawless skeleton named Bony, asking her to teach him her ways. She promised to "gladly take your boney and utilize it for my own devastation on other people." Then she gave her fallen foe one final courtesy and politely removed the ant "so that he doesn't die with an ant in his butthole." The Grandmaster lifted her into the sky before [[The Acolytes]], told them never to underestimate her, and named her the winner.

## Personality and Abilities

Geril-Lee is small enough that Glub Glub kept calling her "halfling," "small one" and "tiny puppet girl." She has eight tongue piercings, which she flashed at her opponent to intimidate him. She lost her long raven hair to the Box of Chaos. On the surface she is timid and polite, prone to saying things like "I'm scared" between attacks. Underneath that is a cheerful menace who talks about drinking blood and eating skin as if they were ordinary village customs.

She fights with a dagger in each hand and relies on acrobatics, backflips and slides. She has an uncanny knack for dodging out of the worst of a blow, and she can strike in a rapid flurry with her fists while still holding her blades. She can heal herself in a pinch and move like the wind. Her real weapon is imagination. She used a minor spell to cause sneezes and summon ants, sent an invisible servant on a tickling mission, and fired creatures out of her Gray Bag of Tricks. Every one of these tricks was aimed at the one place her opponent least wanted it.

## Relationships

- [[Glub Glub the Glorious|Glub Glub]]: Her opponent, whom she defeated. With his last words he entrusted her with his only friend, Bony.
