## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 2/028 - S2E4 — Antsy — Glub Glub v Geril-Lee|S2E4 — Antsy — Glub Glub v Geril-Lee]]
(AJ / winterstales). Small; Glub Glub calls her "halfling," "small one," "tiny puppet girl," "puny puppet thing." Timid at first ("I'm scared"), with fierceness beneath.
- **Looks:** **eight tongue piercings**. Long, luscious raven hair until she sacrificed it to the Box; now she "look[s] like a rabid animal."
- **Fighting style:**
  - Twin daggers, acrobatic backflips, Uncanny Dodge and sneak attack.
  - Flurry of Blows, Patient Defense, Step of the Wind, Wholeness of Body.
  - Shield (unused), Unseen Servant, and the cantrip **Annoyance** (sneeze, ant).
- **Unsettling claims:** "drink your blood for breakfast" is normal "in my village." "I'm gonna eat your skin."
- **What she gains:**
  - The **Gray Bag of Tricks** from the Box of Chaos. She used its weasel.
  - **Bony**, inherited from Glub Glub.
  - The Grandmaster lifted her up to the Acolytes as the winner.
