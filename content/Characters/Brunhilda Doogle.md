(Caustic Phoenix). Yuan-ti (Caustic Phoenix calls it "yaunty"), Gloom Stalker ranger, level 13. Coral-snake colors: red and black with gold instead of yellow, and silver ("Red touch yellow, kills a fellow"). Speaks with a hiss. Spells: thorn whip, poison spray, fear, greater invisibility, frostbite; hide in plain sight. Immune to poison (she says). Ate the purple "dog berries" many times. Befriended a constrictor snake, which White Hat killed. **Died, tied.**
