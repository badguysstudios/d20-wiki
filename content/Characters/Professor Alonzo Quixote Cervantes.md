### [[Episodes/Season 5/100 - S5E2 — Identity Crisis — Kopy Katt v Professor Alonzo Quixote Cervantes|S5E2 — Identity Crisis — Kopy Katt v Professor Alonzo Quixote Cervantes]]
(Rogue). Elderly but jacked.
- **Profession:** professor and student of warfare, combat and bloodshed; also a lawyer, with tenure. His degree is from "Bullshit University" (per the operator).
- **Background:**
  - A fan of the show.
  - Found fighting space crocodiles behind space waffles. Not welcome at a Waffle House near crocodiles.
  - Not American (implied from the Mary Poppins exchange, but his country isn't named).
- **Body and habits:** eight toes, bad aim and balance. Drinks milk. His favorite snack is a salad covered in Worcestershire sauce.
- **The mustache:** sentient. He once visited a tribe with sentient hair, where the chief's nose hair writes poems. He sacrificed the mustache to the Box of Chaos.
- **Possessions:** a leather suitcase holding a spear, a rotary phone and more. Also carries an 80s brick phone.
- **Money:** credit score 850; values money most of all.
- **Family:** estranged from his mother, whose favorite he is. He has siblings, and his mother has a husband.
- **Death:** died slapped into the fireplace by Emmery-as-his-mother.
