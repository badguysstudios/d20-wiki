### [[Episodes/Season 7/133 - S7E5 — Rock and a Hard Place — Iowa-Ska v the Pebble|S7E5 — Rock and a Hard Place — Iowa-Ska v the Pebble]]
(CaptainRoBear; heard on mic as "Ayahuasca," also "Iowa Scott," "Iosca," "Isla Ska").
- **Who he is:**
  - **A green dragonborn fighter**, mid-forties, after "30 years in middle management." Dad-bod gut; full tropical-feather headdress; Conan-style loincloth.
  - **He has three eyes**, per the Pebble, all crooked; his "third eye is opening."
  - Patriotic Iowan ("for the corn… wooden bat baseball… America… the Iowa caucus").
- **Gear:**
  - A halberd (Vex, topple, cleave), a compound bow bought off eBay and shot twice, two javelins, rope, and a poison breath weapon.
  - A jug of unknown liquid and a "dad fanny pack" of supplements: lion's mane, AG1, a "five stack," NeuroGum, a gas-station stamina RX, and a Chuck and Clouds vape with DMT.
- **Possessions and life:**
  - **A Vespa with an untenable loan ($118 a month extra)** that he refuses to lease out of "self-respect." Its paint got scratched in this fight.
  - A new archery range membership. Listens to the Joe Rogan podcast. Thought this was "a simulation."
- **Odorongo**, a leopard he believes is with him; he later calls it "my jaguar." Inferred: Odorongo isn't physically there (1:44:35 "Odorongo who isn't there"; 0:05:33 "the leopard that I believe to be with me"). Spoken at 0:06:40 as "Physically."
- **Body lore:** says dragonborn don't use condoms. Hinted at two/bent anatomy as jokes; saw off the freeze with a "steam vent." Shrimp-pattern burn scars on his face from the hibachi.
- **In this episode:** won. Killed the Pebble while hallucinating him as Destiny 1 and 2.
