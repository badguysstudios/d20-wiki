First appears in [[Episodes/Season 1/011 - Magic — S1 Ep2 — Mageic v Amren|Magic — S1 Ep2 — Mageic v Amren]]. Appears in 7 episodes.

## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 1/011 - Magic — S1 Ep2 — Mageic v Amren|Magic — S1 Ep2 — Mageic v Amren]]
Chaos-event doll: lullabies, eyes that turn solid black, floats, a death-grip handshake that leaves a rune; "I hug with my teeth" (poisonous bite). Became Mageic's assistant; shattered by Amren's catapult; appeared again as an illusion. The Arbiter is afraid of her.

### [[Episodes/Season 1/017 - S1E8 — Fireballs — Grorx v Lord Xiren|S1E8 — Fireballs — Grorx v Lord Xiren]]
"A classic friend of the arena": a small hovering doll that rose from a tiny glowing sarcophagus.
- "Will you be my friend?" Her death-grip handhold brands a friendship mark.
- Has an eight-inch dropping jaw with sharp teeth.
- **Probably crushed in the temple collapse; her fate is officially unknown.** "We will remember Dolly in our hearts."

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/025 - S2E6 — FoTA - Sands of the Arena (Fate of the Arbiter, Part 2)|S2E6 — FoTA - Sands of the Arena (Fate of the Arbiter, Part 2)]]
a porcelain doll from a chaos event ("Play with me"; "We're going to be friends"). She burned a necrotic rune into Locket, served her, and died in the Fire Storm.

### [[Episodes/Season 3/055 - S3E6, Homunculus - Coen v Alfucius|S3E6, Homunculus - Coen v Alfucius]]
(voiced by Caustic Phoenix as Dolly; by Sam once she is Coen's familiar).
- An ancient, demonically possessed porcelain doll from the second chaos event. She came out of a cracked rock under a dark cloud.
- **Burned a "best friend rune" into Coen's palm and became her familiar.** Coen renamed her Aragorl.
- **Appearance:** porcelain limbs, a furry torso, big glassy eyes and a beak of tiny poisonous teeth. Her fur went gray, then black-and-white spotted; she is pink, white and black by the end. Her top tuft was singed black. She has a big pocket.
- **Fire-immune.** She can teleport and go invisible.

### [[Episodes/Season 3/061 - S3E14 — Magic Duel — Talbot Cage v Seth Kaiser|S3E14 — Magic Duel — Talbot Cage v Seth Kaiser]]
. A giant necrotic doll that rises from the ground like a corpse and emits a putrid green cloud. She has porcelain eyes and asks "Do you wanna be my friend?"
- "**Dolly returns to the arena**," so she has appeared before.
- She calls out "One candy!" and fled from the Grandmaster into her hole.

### [[Episodes/Season 4/081 - S4 Ep3, Swindlers - Griff v Cappin Fodder|S4 Ep3, Swindlers - Griff v Cappin Fodder]]
"Our favorite haunted doll," with porcelain skin, glassy eyes and long lashes. Asks "Will you be my friend?" Casts unholy fire, has "a mind of her own," and explodes in a poison bomb. **Attacked by both players for the first time.**

### [[Episodes/Season 5/107 - S5E9 — Patriotic Punch Up — Bob the Barbarian v Richard Knottley (Dick Twist)|S5E9 — Patriotic Punch Up — Bob the Barbarian v Richard Knottley (Dick Twist)]]
A chaos event from the Box. She drains the color from the arena and steals three fighting spirit through eye contact.
