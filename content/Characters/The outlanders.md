## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/074 - Into the Mirror 10 Shatter + Into the Mirror 11 Mortaurios (ITM session 3)|Into the Mirror 10 Shatter + Into the Mirror 11 Mortaurios (ITM session 3)]]
Four desert raiders who toyed with Cerys; she calls them "outlanders."
- The archer with the heavy bow: decapitated by Flynry.
- The spear and javelin wielder: killed by Cerys.
- One in purple clothes: killed by Elianna.
- A **Tabaxi** with hand crossbows: killed by Elianna.
- All turned to sand.
