## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 2/031 - S2E8 — Father's Love — Dadrith v Baron Voltágè|S2E8 — Father's Love — Dadrith v Baron Voltágè]]
(TalesofPesh). Insists on the pronunciation ("said the exact way it's spelt"). The family name is "Barovian."
- **Titles and build:**
  - "The elitist of electricity, the supplier of shock."
  - **A halfling** (uses the halfling Lucky trait; "manlet shoulders").
  - An artificer in transforming Iron Man-like armor with a back thruster. Has a defensive field, Flash of Genius, gloves of ogre strength and teleport boots.
- **Grudge:** calls Dadrith a long-standing "thorn in my side" (unexplained).
- **In this episode:**
  - **Sacrificed his "blackened soul" to the Box of Chaos for the Ring of Three Wishes.** He no longer has a soul.
  - **Wishes used:** (1) full heal, after which black goo filled his wounds and black smoke poured from his armor; (2) an 8th-level Call Lightning; (3) **clone**.
  - Killed by heat metal underwater.
  - **120 days later, a clone burst from a beached shark on the turtle, unknown to Dadrith and possibly the GM and Arbiter.**
- **Dried Blood epilogue:** the Arbiter took Baron's soul from the Box to his laboratory and put it in a metal automaton/golem for his army.
- **The GM's favor:** she bet on him and granted him Bless.

### [[Episodes/Battle for the Crown/047 - S2 Ep19, Battle for the Crown Round 2 - Team Grandmaster (Cosmonius & Locket) v Team Arbiter (Silky Slim & …|S2 Ep19, Battle for the Crown Round 2 - Team Grandmaster (Cosmonius & Locket) v Team Arbiter (Silky Slim & …]]
(TalesofPesh).
- **Who he is:** **gave his soul to the Arbiter, who put it in a new robot body.** He joined because "the Arbiter just offered dental." Title: "the Electacult [?] of Electricity, the Servitor of Shock."
- **Body:** **a halfling inside the armor.** He has a helm HUD (advantage on initiative), shoulder harpoon-syringe, electro gauntlets, lightning rod, boot thrusters and a VTOL-style back jet. He no longer has lungs.
- **Abilities:** artificer shield, Flash of Genius, haste, Enlarge, Lightning Bolt, Fly, Call Lightning, Ashardalon's Stride.
- **Personality:** tried rebranding from cackling to "servitor noises," then went back to screaming.
- **In this episode:** fell to the altar, woke the Kraken and played dead. Revealed as a slimy, disfigured baby with one cybernetic arm. Dying words condemn the Grandmaster and Arbiter "bicker[ing] over something as trivial as a throne while gambling with our lives." Last words: "Why the long face?"
