## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 5/102 - S5E4 — Chaos in Chinchilla Forest — Tormiir v Granny Edith|S5E4 — Chaos in Chinchilla Forest — Tormiir v Granny Edith]]
(Cucumber). A minotaur warlock ("I don't talk about it a lot").
- **Looks:** purple jumpsuit, gold bangles and bands on his horns, neck, arms and ankles. Purple magic spills from all of his (27) orifices.
- **Powers:** warlock blasts from his snout, eyes, ears and mouth. **Signature move: a purple beam charged between his horns** ("Kamuhamuha wave").
- **Weapon:** a brass sword, turned into **an onyx blade** by the Box of Chaos (destroyed on a nat 1).
- **Milk:** drinks his mother's milk, kept in bottles, an emergency stash in a hidden sporran, and a portal of udders. Rages at anyone else drinking milk or eating cheese, though he admits he loves cheese and milk himself.
- **History:** says he has only ever killed warriors, never an old lady. "On the range my whole life… everywhere and nowhere." Accent drifts, finally Scottish.
- **Death:** a gluten allergy / celiac disease. Loves morning grass and petrichor. Died beside Granny.

### [[Episodes/Season 5/114 - S5E16, Chaos Royal (Season 5 finale), the first Chaos Royale (four-way)|S5E16, Chaos Royal (Season 5 finale), the first Chaos Royale (four-way)]]
(guest). "The great minotaur," a muscle-bound warlock-type.
- **Powers and gear:**
  - Purple crackling magic channelled through his horns; it is "magical electricity," so lightning grounds through him harmlessly.
  - A two-handed greatsword that returns to his hand. He lit it by licking it with his tongue.
- **Body:** greases and shaves daily, with gorilla-pattern hair.
- **Personality and lore:**
  - Fiercely protective of **cow's milk as sacred and maternal**; hates seeing Acolytes drink it.
  - Devoted to **his mother**, who would "jump through a portal again" to nurse him if he were dying.
  - Hints he "paid for college" with something lewd ("Only cows").
  - The Acolytes wear Tormiir t-shirts.
- **In this episode:** wanted the mug for his mama "so she could drink out of it. Water." Allied with Richard late. Fell to the Hunger's bite on his mother. His ghost watched from a bench and became part of the felted friend.
