**Tim** is one of the many strange residents of the Void who still made an impression. He is best known as a gelatinous cube living downstairs in the kitchen of [[The Dread Fortress]]. He is a wobbling block of acidic goo with a big appetite and, at least once, a tactical use.

## Story

### S4E8 — Blood Rain

The name Tim first came up among the vendors working the arena during [[Episodes/Season 4/086 - S4E8 — Blood Rain — Fatale v Claudia|S4E8 — Blood Rain — Fatale v Claudia]], the Deathmatch between [[Fatale]] and [[Claudia]]. Tim ran a peanut store alongside Kevin's stall of deep-fried butterfly wings. These were small pieces of the bustling world around the bloodshed.

### S6E1 — Murder at the Dread Fortress

Tim's big moment came in [[Episodes/Season 6/117 - S6E1 — Murder at the Dread Fortress (TTRPG Murder Mystery)|S6E1 — Murder at the Dread Fortress (TTRPG Murder Mystery)]], when a murder mystery spread through the halls of the fortress. Downstairs in the kitchen lived a gelatinous cube, and its record was alarming. It had once caught three adventurers at the same time and had devoured cows.

Skelman found a use for the cube and turned it into a decoy. Tim never got a starring role, but the quietest thing in the kitchen still got put to work.

## Personality and Abilities

Tim is a gelatinous cube, a translucent mass of acidic goo. His acid has one odd habit: it leaves bones alone. The goo is worth about a silver piece an ounce, so the goo itself has value. He also needs no rest, which makes him a tireless presence below the fortress.

His appetite is his defining trait. A creature that can catch three adventurers at once and devour cows is something people tend to keep downstairs, and preferably out of the way. Skelman, at least, found him useful as a decoy.
