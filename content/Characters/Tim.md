**Tim**, also known as **Tim the Wicked**, is one of the many strange residents of the Void who never fought in the arena but still made an impression. He is best known as a gelatinous cube living downstairs in the kitchen of [[The Dread Fortress]]. He is a wobbling block of acidic goo with a big appetite and, at least once, a tactical use.

## Story

### S4E8 — Blood Rain

The name Tim first came up among the vendors working the arena during [[Episodes/Season 4/086 - S4E8 — Blood Rain — Fatale v Claudia|S4E8 — Blood Rain — Fatale v Claudia]], the Deathmatch between [[Fatale]] and [[Claudia]]. A peanut store ran alongside Kevin's stall of deep-fried butterfly wings. These were small pieces of the bustling world around the bloodshed, where Acolytes could grab a snack between deaths.

### S6E1 — Murder at the Dread Fortress

Tim's big moment came in [[Episodes/Season 6/117 - S6E1 — Murder at the Dread Fortress (TTRPG Murder Mystery)|S6E1 — Murder at the Dread Fortress (TTRPG Murder Mystery)]], when a murder mystery spread through the halls of the fortress. Downstairs in the kitchen lived a gelatinous cube, and its record was alarming. It had once caught three adventurers at the same time and had devoured cows.

During the investigation, Skelman found a use for the cube and turned it into a decoy. Tim never got a starring role, but the quietest thing in the kitchen briefly became part of a murder plot.

## Personality and Abilities

Tim is a gelatinous cube, a translucent mass of acidic goo. His acid has one odd habit: it leaves bones alone. The goo is worth about a silver piece an ounce, so even his leftovers have value. He also needs no rest, which makes him a tireless presence below the fortress.

His appetite is his defining trait. A creature that can catch three adventurers at once and swallow whole cows is something people tend to keep downstairs, and preferably out of the way. That ordinary-looking kitchen arrangement made him easy to use as a decoy.
