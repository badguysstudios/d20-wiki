## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 1/017 - S1E8 — Fireballs — Grorx v Lord Xiren|S1E8 — Fireballs — Grorx v Lord Xiren]]
(DadGotGameTV). An **Aasimar, "your protector, your champion"**, with wings.
- Abilities: a sorcerer (twinned spells, firebolt, thunderclap, misty step, friends, magic missile, fireball, scorching ray) with the healing hands feature (never used).
- **Came believing he was only there to put on a show; his contract said "please the Grandmaster."**
- Eyes "only partially working": he couldn't find the Grandmaster until she lit herself with a spotlight.
- Promised a cookie and cold milk if he survived.
- A friend-maker:
  - Praised Doug and received his blessing.
  - Befriended the mummy MJ.
  - Bonded with Dolly, whose mark burned into his palm.
  - Wanted to go back for her.
- Knows "great lawyers" and asbestos-abatement people.
- Started "turning Russian" (needs vodka).
- **Deliberately mispronounced Grorx's name** (Granshain, Grorix) until the end (Dried Blood).
- **Blew himself up with his own fireball** to end the match.
- **Fused with Grorx.** He laments his lost legs ("hideous bowed and green").
