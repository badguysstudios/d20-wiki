## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 1/015 - S1E6 — Belly of the Beast — Viago v Tetra|S1E6 — Belly of the Beast — Viago v Tetra]]
(theshortnord, "Mike").
- **Body:** a feline **Tabaxi** monk (Way of the Astral Self) with **white fur she has grown "for decades"** and uses a hibiscus-and-mint conditioner on.
- **Style:** wears black because it's slimming. Speaks with a heavy accent and broken English ("he is asshole") and says "my dear."
- **Gear:** a quarterstaff kept behind her head, claws, and darts.
- **Abilities:** Arms and Body of the Astral Self, Stunning Strike, Patient Defense, Step of the Wind, Feline Agility, climbing, and slow fall.
- **Found by the Arbiter in "the parking lot outside of Disney."**
- **The GM's favorite this episode** ("I picked you early on").
- Hates canines.
- Calls the Arbiter "Inquisitor" and flirts with both the GM and the Arbiter.
- Rolled many natural 20s.
- **Fate:** defeated by Viago's kick. **The Arbiter fed her body to his pet gelatinous cube instead of keeping her for his army.**
