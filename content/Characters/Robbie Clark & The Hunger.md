### [[Episodes/Season 6/118 - S6 Ep2 · Ether Drifts Part 1 · TTRPG One Shot (Team A mine mission)|S6 Ep2 · Ether Drifts Part 1 · TTRPG One Shot (Team A mine mission)]]
(player inferred as Cinder). A symbiotic pair who share one consciousness.
- **Who they are:**
  - Robbie is an eight-year-old boy. **The Hunger is an Eldritch Horror** with tentacle mouths, many eyes and teeth.
  - Robbie claims "eons of knowledge… born of dying stars and wailing souls."
- **Personality:**
  - Not allowed contacts, email or Reddit; doesn't watch scary shows.
  - Hands out lollipops and makes finger guns. Plays at walkie-talkie radio talk.
  - Calls the team "the Super Friends."
- **Powers:**
  - The Hunger forms organic spears and toothy tendrils.
  - **Signature move:** boy and infinite flesh become one, splitting Robbie into a form of teeth and eyes.
- **Fear:** the stars fading, leaving only them for eternity.
