**Akumu** is a half-elf paladin and warlock, played by versachiko, who was pulled into the Void straight out of a ditch and into a fight to the death with the gamer [[Jinx]]. She is a cheerful, staggering disaster whose blood is "half vodka" and whose deity is Captain Morgan. She is also one of the few champions to punt a chinchilla into a sun, and she did it twice. She won anyway. Her parting advice to [[The Acolytes|the Acolytes]] was to stop drinking.

## Story

### [[Episodes/Season 7/131 - S7E3 — Chinchilla the White — Akumu v Jinx|S7E3 — Chinchilla the White — Akumu v Jinx]]

Akumu came through [[The Arbiter|the Arbiter]]'s portal with a simple explanation: "I fell asleep in a ditch, and now I'm here, and I'm really confused about it." She had never heard of [[The Dread Fortress|the Dread Fortress]]. When [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] offered her water, she asked whether it had vodka in it, so the Arbiter conjured her a full goblet, which she judged to be "potato water." She told Jinx, "I have big sword. Big sword is good," and then the Arbiter flung them both onto an orange asteroid in deep space, beneath millions of cheering Acolytes.

Jinx struck first, carving into Akumu with twin scimitars and a rat-like echo of herself. Akumu barely flinched: "It's a good thing I'm so drunk… my blood is half vodka." She swore an oath of enmity against Jinx and held her back with a hand on her head. She realized she had no weapons until [[Thea]] came sprinting across the asteroid with them, tripped, and sent them flying. Akumu caught them ("I'm so good at catching things") and answered with her "Drunken Sword style." During a chaos event, the Grandmaster asked the champions to warm a pair of freezing [[Chinchillas|chinchillas]]. Jinx tucked hers into her onesie. Akumu drop-kicked hers toward the distant sun, which made a section of the crowd go gray with fur and raise a forest of tiny middle fingers in her direction.

When Jinx came at her again, Akumu splashed a bottle of fireball whisky into a shimmering amber wall, then drank her own shield to heal. She chugged even more fireball to patch herself up properly. When Jinx's echo dove in to protect its maker, Akumu landed a **natural 20** and sliced the "Jinx rat" out of existence. The next chaos event sent a giant hamster ball with a chinchilla inside rolling toward them, and Akumu punted it into the sun as well ("Here goes Denny"). Weary and battered, she walked straight past Jinx's shadow, took its blow ("I made a choice"), and called on Captain Morgan as the arena came to smell "like a dive bar." She brought her sword down and broke Jinx's controller in two, along with Jinx herself. Then the first chinchilla she had kicked into the sun returned in shining platinum as **Chinchilla the White**. It stitched Jinx back together, and Akumu responded by leaping onto its shoulders "like Groot and Rocket." She told it, "I feel like I sent it on a journey and it came back better," and the great chinchilla agreed, grateful for the trip.

When [[The Box of Chaos]] crashed onto the asteroid, Akumu offered it her grandmother, Bailey, who was also a bottle of Bailey's that Akumu had carried her whole life. Akumu asked Bailey whether this was how she wanted to go out, and reported that she said yes. The shadowy figure inside the box drank Bailey down ("Your grandma is silky smooth"), and in return gave Akumu the legendary battleaxe [[Thaneater]]. Then Akumu revealed she was also a warlock and blasted Jinx from across the asteroid with eldritch beams, and tiny worms dragged her opponent's body underground. "I killed my grandma for this, so, you know. It was worth it," Akumu told the Grandmaster. Her victory message made the Acolytes pour their drinks out in a waterfall: **"Hey, drink water, not vodka. I'm built wrong. Go be built right."**

## Personality and Abilities

Akumu is a half-elf about the same height as Jinx, which made their attempts at a menacing stare-down awkward. She is cheerfully, permanently drunk: vodka is her blood, her oxygen and, by her account, her mother, and she insists water "will literally kill me." She admits "I'm not smart," and at the table her player explained that Akumu acts on the first bad thought that pops into her head. That accounts for most of the chinchilla situation. She licks her own blood off her arm as "recycling," calls herself an ecologist for controlling the chinchilla population, and is very particular about her name: "Akumu! Mu, like cow!"

In a fight, she swings a greatsword in her own "Drunken Sword style" and channels holy power through her blade on behalf of her deity, Captain Morgan, who she believes will find the One Piece. She can conjure a defensive wall from a bottle of fireball whisky, heals herself by chugging more of it, and keeps a warlock's eldritch blast in her back pocket for opponents who stay out of reach. She left the arena carrying Thaneater, a legendary battleaxe that deals deathly damage and heals its wielder on its best strikes.

## Relationships

- [[Jinx]]: Her opponent, a gamer in a unicorn onesie. Akumu offered her vodka, was refused because drinking on stream is against the rules, and killed her twice.
- Chinchilla the White: The chinchilla she kicked into the sun. It came back glorified, revived her opponent, let her ride on its shoulders, and seemed grateful for the journey.
- Bailey: Her grandmother, a bottle of Bailey's that Akumu carried since she was "a wee one" and sacrificed, with Bailey's consent, to the Box of Chaos.
- [[Thea]]: Brought Akumu her weapons and later tossed her a bottle from a safe distance.
- [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer]]: Cheered her on ("You're doing great, though, sweetie"), asked her not to imprint, and declared her the survivor.
- [[The Arbiter]]: Supplied her vodka, though he worried about supporting her alcoholism, adding, "but we're really not parents."
- [[The Box of Chaos]]: Took her grandmother and gave her Thaneater.
