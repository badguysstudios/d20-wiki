## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 7/131 - S7E3 — Chinchilla the White — Akumu v Jinx|S7E3 — Chinchilla the White — Akumu v Jinx]]
(versachiko). Champion and winner.
- **Who she is:**
  - A half-elf, about Jinx's height.
  - Paladin with Vow of Enmity, smites and Lay on Hands; also a warlock with Eldritch Blast. Fights with a greatsword ("Drunken Sword style").
  - **Intelligence −1** ("I'm not smart").
  - Arrived after falling asleep in a ditch, confused. Had never heard of the Dread Fortress.
- **Vodka and blood:**
  - Severe alcoholic. "My blood is half vodka." "My oxygen is vodka." Water "will literally kill me." Her mother "is vodka."
  - Licks her own blood as "recycling."
  - **Her deity is Captain Morgan.**
- **Her grandmother, Bailey**, is a bottle of Bailey's that Akumu carried her whole life. She sacrificed her to the Box of Chaos with her consent.
- **Her Shield spell is "fireball" from a bottle**, splashed into an amber wall. She drinks fireball to heal.
- **She kicked two chinchillas into the sun**: one in the freezing event, one in a hamster ball ("Denny"?). One returned as Chinchilla the White, and she rode on its shoulders.
- **Gained the legendary +3 battleaxe Thaneater** from the Box of Chaos.
- Insists on "Akumu, mu like cow," not Akuma.
