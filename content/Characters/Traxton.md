## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 4/097 - S4E16, The Dragon and the Spider, Ricky Red Dragon v Traxton (Spider)|S4E16, The Dragon and the Spider, Ricky Red Dragon v Traxton (Spider)]]
(theshortnord, Mike)
- An elf, "the coolest elf from my part of the woods." His mom calls him Traxton; he goes by "Spider," a one-word name "like Seal." He likes spiders.
- Age stated as 45, "young for an elf." Caustic Phoenix later says 46. A late bloomer: unbroken voice, no muscle mass. Emo, a My Chemical Romance fan.
- Shops at Earthbound. Ordered a salad at the steakhouse and is pro-vegan. His dad loves "Sweet Caroline." Has halitosis.
- **Was class president briefly, under the slogan "feeling forward," after cheating in the vote.** His classmates answered with the hashtag "feeling betrayed."
- Spells and forms: Darkness, Counterspell, Raulothim's Psychic Lance, Destructive Wave (5th level). Was a bat ("Bat Boy") from the portal circle.
- **Traded his hero point to the Box of Chaos for puberty.** It arrived, along with a wooden phallic immovable rod holding one Fireball.
- Won the match. Gave a "stay cool, cool babies" speech in Ricky's sunglasses. **Turned into a tarantula by the Shadow King and kicked out of the arena.**
