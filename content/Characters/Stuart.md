**Stuart** is the Dread Fortress's IT support. He works from a small, humid closet full of screens in the basement.

- He introduced himself as "**Stuart the Gray**" ("because all the great wizards are gray") at *Murder at the Dread Fortress* (S6E1). He wants to move to the magic department but doesn't have a magic bone in his body. The Grandmaster says he's brilliant, which is why there haven't been any hacking issues.
- Whenever the stream's tech breaks, it's Stuart's fault. He has been threatened with firing, offered a bonus, given PTO, had his pay docked, and once pranked the Arbiter on "October 11th."
