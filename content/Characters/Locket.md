## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/024 - S2 Ep1, Fate of the Arbiter (Fate of the Arbiter Part 1)|S2 Ep1, Fate of the Arbiter (Fate of the Arbiter Part 1)]]
(Miss_Magitek)
- A tabaxi with Russian-blue fur and goggles, armed with a longbow and daggers. She prefers high ground.
- A trap expert who designs traps. She has killed beasts and aberrations but never a man. Her ears are not for sale.
- Got the **potion of storm giant strength** from the Box (she had wished for a new bow) and drank it in the fight. Found two potions of healing and gave one to Toddleheim.
- Used Flame Arrows.

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/025 - S2E6 — FoTA - Sands of the Arena (Fate of the Arbiter, Part 2)|S2E6 — FoTA - Sands of the Arena (Fate of the Arbiter, Part 2)]]
(Miss_Magitek). A **cat-like** Gloom Stalker ranger: feline agility, claws, a climbing speed, and runs on all fours. Longbow with flame-imbued quiver, short sword, Hunter's Mark. Her **Fear spell turns her face feral**.
- **Has a twin sister**. She is curious by nature and picks up the doll.
- From the box: a **medallion of thoughts shaped as a heart-shaped locket**, plus a faint halo glow. Used Detect Thoughts on the Arbiter.
- **Killed the Deathlock** with an arrow to the skull.
- From the Seer: a **Helm of Brilliance as a jeweled choker**.
- Bonded briefly to the doll "Dolly"; was duplicated in a portal, and the duplicate burned.
- The potion of storm giant strength from the last episode has worn off.

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/045 - S2E17 — Fate of the Arbiter - The Hall of Fates — Toddleheim, Locket and Flynry reach the Grandmaster|S2E17 — Fate of the Arbiter - The Hall of Fates — Toddleheim, Locket and Flynry reach the Grandmaster]]
(Miss_Magitek). A furred cat-like ranger: Tabaxi, inferred from "Tabaxi or a Ranger thing" (2:29:14).
- Fights with +1 short sword, offhand blade and a bow with flame arrows. Has Keen Mind and can climb.
- **Smitten with Baptiste.** She kept his **pure silver +1 dagger**, and her mirror glance showed Baptiste.
- **Bought the elven chain necklace** (+1 AC, deer pendant) for 5 gold, asking for something "to impress somebody."
- Tired of living with "all of you"; wants a beach.

### [[Episodes/Battle for the Crown/047 - S2 Ep19, Battle for the Crown Round 2 - Team Grandmaster (Cosmonius & Locket) v Team Arbiter (Silky Slim & …|S2 Ep19, Battle for the Crown Round 2 - Team Grandmaster (Cosmonius & Locket) v Team Arbiter (Silky Slim & …]]
(Miss_Magitek).
- **Who she is:** a Tabaxi gloom stalker ranger who "specializes in bows." Her signature move is flame arrows, lit by touching her quiver. She prays to **Chantia [?]**.
- **Feelings and ties:**
  - She hates the Arbiter: "a phony… a fake… You have no idea what I've been through." They "have a lot of history together."
  - The Arbiter cites "your friend Lyrus" as someone who died.
  - Her friends include **Toddleheim** and **Flynry [?]**; they've had "so much bad luck against the Arbiter."
  - She is "taken" and likes **Baptiste**, a beautiful elven boy with long silver hair. "Maybe we can practice our stabbing later."
  - She dislikes being near people and claims rocks.
- **Item:** owns a **Helm of the Gods** (a gift, barely used), used to cast Dissonant Whispers.
- **In this episode:**
  - Mudcat first blood.
  - Ignited Slim's lube with a nat 20.
  - Killed Silky Slim, apologizing and blaming the Arbiter. Slim's ghost now haunts her.
  - Survived and won.
