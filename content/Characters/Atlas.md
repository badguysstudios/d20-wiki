## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Playtests/005 - Playtest 2 (Pre-Launch) — Atlas v Bromir|Playtest 2 (Pre-Launch) — Atlas v Bromir]]
Warforged built by a monastery of priests to defend "the bulwark of justice." Abjuration engine (arcane ward), shield master, longsword, a siege engine on his back; casts spells (steel wind strike, tidal wave, mold earth, passwall, disintegrate, Tenser's transformation, channel divinity Rebuke Violence). Speaks of himself in the third person: **"Atlas is unbreakable."** Signature pose honoring the Grandmaster with an orb of light bearing her face. Summons **Halifax**. A tiny engraving somewhere on him reads "Nokia" (player joke, 1:56:18). Winner of Reaper's Rock; sent home to fulfill his charge; promised a return.

### [[Episodes/Season 5/099 - S5E1 — Rise of the Lord of Fate — Atlas v Killy Yesman|S5E1 — Rise of the Lord of Fate — Atlas v Killy Yesman]]
(Dag). A large robot.
- **Body:**
  - Heavy iron boots, gyros and an ejecting chassis.
  - **Floating fists the size of goblins**, which work gyroscopically, by magnetic repulsion and with rocket turbines.
  - Four eye sockets and a faceplate. Electricity turns yellow when he's charged.
  - A chest window that plays audio clips ("your mother," "Nice hat, bro").
  - An antiseptic-spray finger and a port that takes AA batteries.
  - **No mouth**, which is "a great source of misery." His voice runs over Bluetooth.
  - **Powered by a Double Sun Cola canister core.**
- "Defender of the innocent, breaker of bones and bitches." His autocorrect is aggressive. Abilities: repair protocols and Unbreakable Force.
- **Gained Kenny's missing arm** (a small green goblin arm out of his sternum).
- Died after ripping out his own brain. He was carted off by goblins to be rebuilt; in Dried Blood he is being rebuilt in the locker room.

### [[Episodes/Season 5/109 - S5E11 — Appliance Adversaries — Atlas v Frigidoom|S5E11 — Appliance Adversaries — Atlas v Frigidoom]]
(Dag / DeadAussieGamer). A huge, lumbering automaton with grey metal plating. He is logical and formal when sober and a wrestler-voiced "brother" when radical.
- **Body:**
  - No mouth (a very sore spot) and no nose.
  - Gears and cogs inside; **his heart is a canister of Double Sun Cola**, which flows through his body.
  - Magnetic lightning bolts run along his shoulders and arms, and a power gauntlet holds glowing cylinders that overheat for energy blasts.
  - When the cola is shaken or released he becomes "radical": a lightning mohawk that evolves into a lightning mo-mullet, orange-crackling eyes, and a sun-and-bottle emblem on his chest.
- **Lore and history:**
  - He died in his last battle ("I saw eternity… Hell is real even for automatons") and was brought back "because we were bored."
  - The Lord of Fate recalls him drinking a lot of Double Sun Cola.
  - Was once "a tiny little toaster."
  - Has an assistant: a tiny paperclip that sasses him.
- **In this episode:**
  - Lost a pinky.
  - Traded the radical chinchilla to the Box of Chaos.
  - Was made permanently radical by Frigidoom's signature move.
  - Won. He now carries Frigidoom bottled in his chest next to his heart and is collecting the fridge pieces to rebuild him.

### [[Episodes/Season 6/118 - S6 Ep2 · Ether Drifts Part 1 · TTRPG One Shot (Team A mine mission)|S6 Ep2 · Ether Drifts Part 1 · TTRPG One Shot (Team A mine mission)]]
(player is Australian).
- **Memory:** "does not possess memories… fragments… familiarities acknowledged." He says his memory banks have been corrupted many times, "like in the Dread Fortress."
- **Family (his own claim):** his mother was a bucket with no eyes; his father was an engineer who gave him no mouth. He has no orifices.
- **Body:**
  - Floating rocket fists joined to his shoulders by plasma arcs.
  - Shin tank treads, later swapped for mine-cart wheels that lay their own track.
  - USB ports, a sarcasm chip, and a gorilla-stance transformation with a spine cannon fed by Double Sun Cola silos.
- **Powers:** Unstoppable Force (sacrifices HP).
- **His wit:** sells "Atlas subscription services" and carries a Double Sun Cola syringe.
- **Fear:** staying conscious with the power off, forever.
- **In this episode:** updated after the aliens turned him off and on. **Died gloriously** (his head exploded) killing Theresa, and was being pieced back together by the GM.

### [[Episodes/Season 6/119 - S6 Ep3 — Ether Drifts Part 2 — Team A deathmatch (Atlas v Robbie Clark and the Hunger v Copycat)|S6 Ep3 — Ether Drifts Part 2 — Team A deathmatch (Atlas v Robbie Clark and the Hunger v Copycat)]]
(guest). A robot/construct with a faceplate, huge electric fists and heavy boots. **No mouth (until this fight) and no butthole: "it is a port."** He wears pants.
- He arrived "not completely built": just boots, head and tiny arms.
- He combined with Thea as **"Fatlas"** ("Phatlas," per Copycat).
- Abilities: Unstoppable Surge, Immovable Force; "yeet protocol"; magnetic energy.
- Says he learned about "Mateship" on **planet Australia**.
- Item gained: the **blue pill**.
- **Converted by the Hunger's spores: given feeling, a mouth and an exit port.** Allied with Robbie. Revived in his original form.
