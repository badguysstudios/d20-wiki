## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Live Shows/049 - Live | GCX Orlando 2022 — Lord Snacc v The Showman|Live | GCX Orlando 2022 — Lord Snacc v The Showman]]
(BDTeej). A large barbarian "from a faraway land… some would even say in space." He worships **a spherical god**. The Showman calls him a viking.
- **Gear and powers:**
  - The **Fourth Horseman**, "a large multi-barreled blaster."
  - **A glaive.** The table also calls one of his attacks a shotgun ("a classic loadout").
  - **The flaming hammer of inferno and doom** ("Inferno Hammer"), used with a hero point.
  - Rage and frenzy. Resistant to piercing, slashing and bludgeoning.
- **Horns:** he wears one **broken horn**. He keeps **his "other horn"** in his pants and sacrificed it to Gerald.
- **Personality:** "I do not fear death!" Low Wisdom ("400 brawn"). He was afraid of the Showman's bowler hat, and the Acolytes added bees, leaves and cheese. He came to love cheese. He knows "a great ramen place" and wanted fro-yo; the GM promised to buy fro-yo for the winner.
- **In this episode:** he won the GCX Deathmatch.
