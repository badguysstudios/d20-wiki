(MyNameIsByf). A green-skinned lizard person ("lizard hands," a "real green" toe; "some lizards are colorblind").
- Family and background:
  - Has many brothers and sisters. One teased him as "Gregor" (a sore spot). His "mom and pa" are mentioned.
  - **His big sister fights with a polearm and has disemboweled people.**
- Seeks honorable duels, but accidents always happen when he's in an arena.
- Dual-wields two pistols that turned out to be **sentient**.
- Wounds and gains:
  - Lost a pinky toe, which he turned into the **toe pendant of flying**, unused.
  - Lost a pinky to the shadow shot and **two more fingers to Daffodil**.
  - **His colorblindness was cured** by the nat-20 save.
- Seems smitten with the Shadow King: salutes him, "Was I also easy go?"
- Killed by his own pistol, then slipped on his blood.
