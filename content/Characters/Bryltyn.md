**Bryltyn** was a champion of the Void's Deathmatches who insisted there was no one else like him, despite a nagging suspicion that the arena had seen his face before. In [[Episodes/Season 1/022 - S1E13 — Snow-clad — Highfrost v Bryltyn|S1E13 — Snow-clad — Highfrost v Bryltyn]], he faced the kilted warlock [[Highfrost]] in a snowbound town. The fight became a war of insults, unwanted kilt reveals, liberated ducklings and a deadly game of tag. He came out of it bloodied, one axe short, and victorious.

## Story

Bryltyn introduced himself with few words: "I'm just a warrior. There are none like me. That is all." [[The Arbiter]] wasn't convinced and recalled "a bunch that look almost exactly like him in the other deathmatches." Bryltyn had an explanation: "They were all fakes. Clones, perhaps." [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|The Grandmaster]] took his side at once: "See? Clones!" When she ordered him to fill the halls with his voice, he announced that he was "here to bring some pain," and he mocked Highfrost's accent. Highfrost then revealed that his uncle had once lived in this very town in the north. Bryltyn asked if he knew what had happened to them, and answered his own question: "I killed them all."

The match began with Bryltyn behind a fence, unable to see his foe. Then a beam of eldritch force came arcing over the rooftops, "with the spirit of your ancestors guiding your hand," and struck him on the head. "This is rigged," he complained, though he called it "just a scratch." He replied with a snowball packed with chunks of ice, which missed. When the Grandmaster asked what made him angry, he named "insufficient enemies on the field. This man is nothing more than a charlatan." Then he flew into his rage, screaming into the sky. In that state almost nothing could truly hurt him except attacks on the mind. A ray of frost iced over his skin, and the ice melted away as steam in the heat of his fury. His greatest wound in this stretch came when a draft lifted Highfrost's kilt. The sight was judged traumatic enough to hurt him outright. The Grandmaster offered him a reward if he impressed her, but he waved it off with "Keep your potions." He found a glowing crystal in the rubble instead, which tasted icy cold, "as if you're drinking Listerine," and blinked him up onto a rooftop. From there he answered Highfrost's tribute to a grandpappy who "could drink with the fishes" with "It's funny. Now he's sleeping with them."

Then the chaos began. The Grandmaster landed beside Bryltyn, offered her hand, squeezed it tight and cried "Tag, you're it!" A glowing tag was now on him, and it would hurt him badly if he couldn't pass it on. He offered Highfrost a friendly handshake, but the warlock had seen a game of tag before and refused. Highfrost then turned to taunts about Bryltyn's mother and landed a perfectly delivered one. It left Bryltyn shaken and doubting her ("She had a whole second life"), and he briefly wondered: "Could this be my father? Will I be battling my father?" Highfrost followed up with a bagpipe performance so bad that it wounded the Grandmaster herself. Bryltyn climbed to the peak of the roof, 30 feet up, and leapt off. He slid down with gravity behind his handaxe and left Highfrost bloodied in the snow. Then, face to face, he slapped the tag onto him: "Look who's it, bitch."

The tag did not stay put. Highfrost passed it to a straggling duckling, then flung bread into Bryltyn's face. The whole flock mobbed him, Mama Duck landing square on his shoulder, and the little one tagged him right back. "These chaos events have not been my friend," he grumbled. In the end both men were bleeding into the snow, "almost like that scene out of Kill Bill." Bryltyn took an all-in attitude: "If you die, I die. Everything's fine." His next swing went wrong when one handaxe broke on Highfrost's metal chest, and Highfrost river-danced out of reach before he could pass the tag. Then an avalanche roared down the mountain and swept them both to the docks. There the tag went off. His palm glowed red and burned, and the light pulsed through his veins. A coin flip went his way and his rage dulled the rest, so he survived. Beside the frozen ocean, he charged with his last axe and finished it, telling the blow himself: "A slash, a deep gash in his chest. The blood sprays onto the snow, onto the stones, into the ocean… Be at peace with your family, foe." The Grandmaster came down and held out her hand again, "this time no tag in sight," and he rose "as light as a feather" as she carried him off to his fans. At the table, Highfrost's player revealed that he had a mind-piercing finale planned that Bryltyn's rage couldn't have blocked: "Defeated by a coin toss."

## Personality and Abilities

Bryltyn was blunt, proud and humorless in the funniest possible way. He was certain of his own uniqueness, which suited a man whose look apparently resembled half the arena's past champions. He described himself as "a very honest man." He seemed to have no use for ducks ("We don't feed the ducks") and no idea what friendship was ("Friends? What is that word?"). His opponent called that "the scariest thing you've said all night." He still had a dry wit, and promised to find Highfrost a therapist "if he lives."

In battle he was a bear-totem barbarian who fought with two handaxes, though he left the arena with only one. When he raged he screamed to the sky and grew so tough that fire, frost, force, necrotic power and even duck pecks barely touched him. Only harm to the mind got through, which is why taunts and terrible bagpipe music did him real damage.

## Relationships

- [[Highfrost]]: His opponent. Bryltyn claimed to have slaughtered Highfrost's family's village, briefly feared Highfrost was his father, and killed him on the docks.
- [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer]]: She cheerfully tagged him with a deadly game of tag, then personally flew him to his fans after his victory.
- [[The Arbiter]]: He doubted Bryltyn's claim to be one of a kind and fretted that the fight would end too soon for the paying crowd.
