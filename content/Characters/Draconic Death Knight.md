## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/043 - Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 4 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 4 (A Crossover)]]
"Lord of this fortress"; a rotting, reanimated dragonborn knight in dark unholy armor, with shield and longsword.
- Fire resistant and immune to fear. Uses Staggering Smite.
- Slain by Lyrus. **His soul became a green-and-black hellfire orb that exploded on his slayer.**
