## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Vythorn's Return/095 - S4E14, Vythorn's Return Part 1 (Vythorn alone in the in-between; the God of Fate)|S4E14, Vythorn's Return Part 1 (Vythorn alone in the in-between; the God of Fate)]]
(Caustic Phoenix). The living black-and-gold substance of the swamp river, with faces and eyes inside it. It eats magic, mocks him with "they didn't give you permission," and ate his boots. "We are trapped, and now you are trapped." **It speaks as "we" and tells him to "ask who we are,"** but it's not stated whether it is part of the God of Fate. The light that led him out says "It's time. Wake up." A head in the lake invites him back "if they don't like you." The post-show says these are beings stuck in the in-between.
