## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 2/026 - S2E2 — Prize Fight — Asena v Ralzamon|S2E2 — Prize Fight — Asena v Ralzamon]]
(PodsofWar)
- **Origin:** from the dimension of **Northport**, a Coriander Society character. Normally **a pugilist**; here a monk "more dragon-y," tapping her **dragon heritage** for the arena.
- **Powers:**
  - Telepathy.
  - Spectral draconic wings (Wings Unfurled).
  - Aspect of the Wyrm frightful presence, which manifested as swirling milk.
  - Step of the Wind, Stunning Strike, Flurry of Blows, Mage Slayer.
  - **Dimension Strike**, a custom feat: force damage on a crit, plus wisps of mist that slow the target, hamper its attacks and block teleport.
- **Personality and lore:**
  - Forgetful ("the next day, everything just kind of goes away").
  - Not cautious.
  - Thinks **raccoons are sacred animals**.
  - Her friend John told her about *Matrix*.
  - Learned about aliens that morning in a dimension called Ico.
  - Says she doesn't know much about her past.
  - Calls Ralzamon "Ralzypoo."
- **In this episode:**
  - She was abducted willingly by aliens and given **snake arms with two extra brains**; the GM later removed them.
  - She won; the GM called her "queen of the fight."
  - Ralzamon calls her a corrupting energy and "**harbinger of elder evil**"; the GM says she doesn't doubt she is a danger.

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/040 - Coriander Society, Fate of the Faithful Part 1 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society, Fate of the Faithful Part 1 (A Crossover)]]
(PodsofWar)
- **Abilities and gear:**
  - A martial fighter with silver-misty-light fists.
  - Can run up walls and has slow fall.
  - Double-barrel shotgun and laser gun.
  - **The Fang of Glotharius** (planar slip).
  - "Wails from the grave."
- **Connection to portals:** "connected to a lot of planes," and has power over portals that barely affects Lyrus's.
- **Flaw:** "no patience for sitting around… direct action."
- **Deathmatch history:** **a former Deathmatch champion who fought Ralzamon in the Kitchen of the Dread Fortress**, using milk froth against his lactose intolerance. She remembers crowds chanting her name. She also tells John of dreams of a Santa-carriage space trip, which John has never believed.
- **In this episode:** keeps the finest spoon.

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/041 - Coriander Society - Fate of the Faithful Part 2 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society - Fate of the Faithful Part 2 (A Crossover)]]
(PodsofWar).
- Has fought in the Grandmaster's arena before and fought Ralzamon in the Void. Champions are sent home without memories; she remembered only as dreams.
- "Somewhat of a dragonborn"; the Seer senses a dragon nature "not from this plane." She says she is probably older than the Seer.
- Smart laser stolen from an airplane (now leaking radiation). Also a ray gun, planar slip, and astral-light reflection.
- Mental link (telepathy); a bestow curse spell tattoo from John.
- **Currently cat-sized.**

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/042 - Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover|Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover]]
(PodsofWar). Shrunk to house-cat size, then restored and enlarged.
- Has the slime companion **Slakura** (key-form, slime trails).
- **Powers:** telepathy; force-glowing punches; a once-per-day astral shift; a primal connection to nature and astral energies; "dragon fear."
- Doesn't care about gold (once bought hot dogs with it).
- Distrusts Circe ("I hate her"). Claims a version of Circe (a "him") exists in her world.
- Found the Tumult map and the ewer plant. Called "the Dragon of Zul'Qir" by the GM.

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/043 - Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 4 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 4 (A Crossover)]]
(PodsofWar). Was a Deathmatch champion, returned home by the Grandmaster, and kept her memories.
- **Family past:** her possible mother kept an occult chamber; she carries the expedition photo of her family.
- **Wears a crown taken from Santa Polka's tomb during a dreamscape** visit that was "also like really there."
- Monk-like: runs up walls, sneak attack, astral chains, Wails from the Grave. Darkvision.
- Gave Lawrence her blood as a divination anchor. Carries a potion of force resistance and the distance quill.
- Has "another brain" and thinks "paper" thoughts.

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/044 - Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 5 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 5 (A Crossover)]]
(PodsofWar). A pugilist.
- Was shrunk earlier and restored by the cookie.
- Gear and powers:
  - **The Fang of Goltharius**, an amulet with astral wings and soul-ripping, which **now grants Dimension Door once a day**.
  - A laser gun ripped from a giant machine, now drained. Slakura.
- Backstory:
  - As a little girl she was taken to **the tomb of Sennepolker**, before a hungry ancient dead thing.
  - Her life was changed by the jungle expedition on which Josiah Parsons died.
- She **fought Ralzamon in a Deathmatch** in the Void and won. She spared him, letting him fall into the pit.
