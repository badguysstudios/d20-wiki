## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 4/085 - S4E7 — Wings — Misery v Daisy Bloom|S4E7 — Wings — Misery v Daisy Bloom]]
(voiced by Caustic Phoenix). A glowing, mid-sized moth summoned by Find Steed, with a harness. She told Daisy her name, carried her 6–7 inches off the ground and winked at her ("You be you"). **Cut apart by Misery. She dissolved into black and white butterflies and dust, which the Acolytes breathed in: "We are all Janet."** Once called "Jaina."
