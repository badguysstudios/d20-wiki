## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/025 - S2E6 — FoTA - Sands of the Arena (Fate of the Arbiter, Part 2)|S2E6 — FoTA - Sands of the Arena (Fate of the Arbiter, Part 2)]]
.
- A white-scaled, blind dragonborn smith and magic forger. Bumps into anvils; "a little bit of blind humor."
- Was "quite a ruffian in my younger days… the ladies, they liked me."
- Sees what is inside people and manifests it into items. Had not made a magic item in over 25 years.
- Can't make the whole Orrery of the Wanderer. Is a "homebody" and tired; sleeps on a box.

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/041 - Coriander Society - Fate of the Faithful Part 2 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society - Fate of the Faithful Part 2 (A Crossover)]]
. An ancient blind white Dragonborn blacksmith with missing teeth and a glowing turquoise crystal warhammer.
- Sees true natures, past and future, and has prophetic dreams. His sight has been "clouded as of late."
- Usually roots out moles; found none.
- Made the replica silver ewer for Lyrus and is forging Chester's knife.
- Lyrus calls him "that old charlatan" who fakes blindness for sympathy.

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/043 - Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 4 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 4 (A Crossover)]]
. An NPC of the Faithful, apparently a craftsman as well as a seer.
- Admires the artwork and helped the divination with no trouble at all.
- **Ralzamon's presence "has certainly clouded my vision."**
- **Forged Chester's rainbow Damascus knife.** Gave a parting vision (a stored 12).

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/044 - Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 5 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 5 (A Crossover)]]
. Stumbling, sleepy, "sharper than you think." His vision is clear again. He'll decide the ewer's use. Got the hypno gun.
