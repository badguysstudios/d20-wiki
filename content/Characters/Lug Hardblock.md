**Lug Hardblock** is a wealthy, entitled barbarian and aspiring real-estate developer, played by Zac (ZacSpeaksGiant). He came into the Void to flatten a sweet little grandma and spent the evening being hated by the crowd more than any champion in recent memory. Then the fight turned into a family therapy session, and he left it a crowned co-champion. He thinks therapy is for the weak, and he still means to build high-rise condominiums on everything he sees.

## Story

### A Father's Son

Lug arrived through the [[The Arbiter|Arbiter's]] portal in [[Episodes/Season 7/135 - S7E7 — A Father's Son — Agatha Middlebrooke v Lug Hardblock|S7E7 — A Father's Son — Agatha Middlebrooke v Lug Hardblock]] with a "Hoo-yah" and a correction for [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]]: "Correction, champion. Singular." The Arbiter liked him at once. His opponent was [[Agatha Middlebrooke]], a kindly grandmother who handed out toffees, and Lug announced that beating her would give him the confidence to put on his shoes and socks in the morning. The arena was an asteroid in [[The Ether Drifts]]. Lug charged across it, swelled to two or three times his size, and hit Agatha with the golf swing his dad had taught him. She flew off into space and came back in a poof of lavender.

Agatha's answer was cruel in the kindest way. She filled the field with illusions of Lug's handsome, square-jawed father, and every one of them said, "I'm proud of you, son." Lug fell for it completely ("I'm going to win this for you, Papa"). He also missed the cat that leapt out of his father's arm and latched onto his face. This was Lord Cookie Mill, Agatha's familiar, a killer animal that clung to him like a cat tree and shrugged off every attempt to hurl it into space. When Lug went to hug one of the illusions, it melted in his arms. Agatha then commanded him to "Share," and he blurted out that his father had never shown him a moment of warmth. Lug still pressed on. He set his axe alight, spiked Agatha into a crater full of worm civilization like a one-man volleyball team, and found a cracked asteroid full of potential condo plots.

[[The Acolytes]] loathed him. They pelted him with slushies, and their hatred made the cat's blows land twice and then three times as hard. After a break, Lug used the crowd's favor to smash Agatha's space helmet, leaving her with only a few breaths of air. She told him, "Good shot. He would have been proud," then cast Little Death on herself and drifted away lifeless. Lug declared that "Silencing women works." The Grandmaster raised his hand with a forced smile and announced him the winner, and he promised to turn Agatha's old home into a soup kitchen before knocking that down for condominiums too.

Then Agatha's ghost appeared and told him the truth: she was his grandmother, kept away by his father, and she had cursed his father into the cat. She possessed Lug, made him hug himself ("No, no, no, I hate this… this is what therapy is for"), and whispered that he could be a better man than his father before the cat tore into him again. Shrunk back to normal size and badly bloodied, Lug grabbed the cat and smashed it so hard it burned up in the asteroid's thin atmosphere and burst like a firework. "And if that really was my dad, good riddance." Agatha offered him a choice: fight his grandma, or live free of his father's yoke. Lug chose freedom: "The first time in my life, I don't need to win." He thanked her, and the baffled Grandmaster crowned them both champions.

## Personality and Abilities

Lug is a self-described rebel who "pushes boundaries," an only child, and his father's "special boy." He has one eye, a very strong chin, and a space helmet he made masculine and cool by drawing flames on the sides. Everything in his life was handed to him and he will not admit it. His father paid people off so Lug came first in everything, including his first egg and spoon race, and he got into college for free. Even so, he sneers at "handouts," at the poor and at therapy, and he insists that no male in his line cries. His one dream is clearing land for high-rise condominiums, whether that land is a worm town or his grandmother's house. His parting wisdom to the Acolytes was to tape his mouth shut while sleeping and try "mewing" for a stronger jawline.

In a fight, Lug flies into a rage that makes him enormous, and his main talent is launching people across the battlefield. He fought with a greataxe that he could set ablaze, a warhammer, and later a huge cleaver-like axe the Grandmaster called "the Lamborghini of great axes." He also claims to be very good at killing cats, "just like you taught me."

## Relationships

- [[Agatha Middlebrooke]]: his opponent, revealed to be his long-absent grandmother. He killed her, she came back as a ghost to hug him, and they were crowned co-champions together.
- His father ("Papa"): taught him golf and how to kill cats, called him his special boy, and was cursed into the cat Lord Cookie Mill. Lug killed him.
- [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer]]: called him a spoiled kid and switched her cheers back and forth during the fight, then crowned him a champion with very mixed emotions.
- [[The Arbiter]]: liked Lug on sight and predicted he would win.
- [[The Acolytes]]: hated him so much that the crowd's scorn tripled the damage he took.
