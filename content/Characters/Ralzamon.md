## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 2/026 - S2E2 — Prize Fight — Asena v Ralzamon|S2E2 — Prize Fight — Asena v Ralzamon]]
(TrmntdByGnomes)
- **Nature:** "master of the unknown." A long-dead being of ectoplasm, dressed half jungle explorer, half megalomaniac.
  - "Ralzamon" is an assumed name, not his real one. He has a "poor dead mother."
  - **His consciousness normally resides in a crystal heart** and he calls this body a "fleshy apparatus."
  - He was once "a mere mortal."
  - He's lactose intolerant and finds marble gross.
- **Powers:** psychic. He used levitation, Hellish Rebuke (twice), Lucky, **Pact of the Talisman invoking the Zul'quian jungles**, and **Mental Prison** (ninjas, void slimes, his own mocking face).
- **History with Asena:** they've fought "like five times." He mind-prisoned and haunted her. He believes her energy corrupts worlds and failed to banish it from "my own world."
- **In this episode:**
  - He fooled the aliens into thinking he was one of them and got two healing potions.
  - He was killed by Asena.
  - **He was revived in secret by the Arbiter in the well as "a general for the days to come."**
