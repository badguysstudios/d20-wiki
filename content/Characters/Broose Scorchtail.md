## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 2/039 - S2E16, Pub Brawl - Broose Scorchtail v Dewdrop Grizzlebee|S2E16, Pub Brawl - Broose Scorchtail v Dewdrop Grizzlebee]]
(swoleinitiativenetwork). A **6'3" rabbitfolk ("Herongorn," likely Harengon) with an Australian accent** ("mate," "crikey," "ripper," "how you going").
- **Look and personality:**
  - Wears a **pink "PARTY ON" poncho with purple ghost flames**, made of wool. Says it makes him "lithe."
  - White fur. **Likes fire.** On holiday for "R&R."
  - Wanted to take his opponent out for a drink, and regrets killing a "friend."
- **Gear and abilities:**
  - Druid magic: Druidcraft (grows a carrot), a **Wildfire Spirit shaped as a flaming carrot**, Moonbeam, Thorn Whip (vines with carrots), Flame Blade.
  - Martial abilities: longsword/scimitar, superiority dice (parry, bracing attack), Action Surge, Second Wind, Rabbit Hop. His bunny feature means no opportunity attacks when he hops away.
- **In this episode:** licked a haste mushroom and drank honey from the alchemy jug, then a potion of mind control, which he used to charm Dewdrop.
- **Jokes about himself:**
  - Delivers Easter eggs ("Easter's no joke").
  - Claims (Dried Blood) that "John J. Kilcullen" is his pseudonym and that he wrote Melee for Dummies.
  - Lost count of his age "after three."
- **Won by bracing attack** while being drowned. He was given a cold brew by the GM and toasted by the Acolytes.
