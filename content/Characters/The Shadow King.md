**The Shadow King**, born **Agdrel**, is the great villain of D20 Deathmatch. Played by **Mudcat**.

## History
- Brother of Faucius Golden Shield and uncle of Elusia. On **the Night of the Long Shadow** (about 700 years before Season 1) he killed his brother and seized [[The Dread Fortress]]. He loved Elusia's mother, his sister-in-law.
- He reigned for **300 years**. He killed the parents of many chaos-gifted children (including Vythorn's) to foster them and siphon their power. He says he was more of a father to Elusia than her real father, and calls her "daughter."
- Defeated by Elusia, Atalantos and Kaja in [[Skypiercer]]. Vythorn then sealed his soul in [[The Box of Chaos]] as Gerald.
- A piece of him manipulated Vythorn, and he was rebuilt. In the **Season 3 finale** he killed [[The Arbiter]] and took over. **Season 4** was his reign, with the Grandmaster a captive in all but name. Deaths under him are permanent unless a later season brings someone back.
- In **Vythorn's Return** the Arbiter destroyed his body with Morir's Cataclysm, but his soul escaped. Pieces of him are scattered across the Void and the hells.
- His old Dragon Knights became the Shadow Knights, the Order of the Ascendant Shadow (Pieces of a King).
