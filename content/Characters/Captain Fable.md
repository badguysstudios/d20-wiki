## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 3/054 - S3E5, Edge Lords - Captain Fable v Verryn Ruth|S3E5, Edge Lords - Captain Fable v Verryn Ruth]]
(sensei_suplex).
- **An eight-foot minotaur in pirate attire** with a deathly necromantic aura.
- **A fallen paladin**: Oathbreaker, with Battle Master manoeuvres. A fortune teller and storyteller ("let me tell you a fable").
- **Gear:**
  - A massive buster greatsword.
  - Javelins charged with lightning.
  - **A skeletal necromantic hand** under one glove.
  - Bones of corpses carried as treats.
  - A blunderbuss from "an actual campaign", traded away.
  - Gained **the Far Realm Shard** and **a black ship with a gold crown emblem**.
- **Powers:** thunder and lightning; Divine Sense, Spirit Shroud, Control Undead, Commander's Strike, Aura of Hatred, Thunderous Smite.
- **Backstory (stated this episode):**
  - Once a happy pirate captain and paladin.
  - His crew was slaughtered while every god and pantheon (the sea goddess, the storm god) ignored his prayers.
  - "**They took everything from me. My name, my crew, my life.**" He doesn't know what he is: "the gods took that from me as well."
  - He now aims to **slay/cull all the gods**.
  - Creed: **strength in unity, never from higher powers.** His crew has no leader; all are equal.
  - Insists he's a bull, not a cow, and has feelings.
- Has "a soft spot": pets Doug.
- Claims he found the blunderbuss in an abandoned haunted house. Mentions "Ingot, Philo" and "Moana" (unexplained).
- Says "they" told him to kill one person and he could do what he wanted.
- **Winner.**
