**Christina the ghost** was the spectral maiden of a ruined castle who drifted into the Deathmatch between [[Buli]] and [[Chancy]] in [[Episodes/Season 2/036 - S2E13 — The Brave — Buli v Chancy|S2E13 — The Brave — Buli v Chancy]]. She was a jilted bride with a voice that filled the whole space. She was searching for Frederick, the man who never showed up on their wedding day.

## Story

Christina haunted the ruined castle in search of Frederick, who never arrived on the day of their wedding. When she appeared mid-fight, she became a third force on the battlefield. She shoved combatants back with a blast of wind, struck them with a deathly touch, passed straight through bodies, and let out a screech like a thunderclap. When she came up against a polymorph, she stripped the disguise away.

Her story ended the way it had to. Christina finally caught up with Frederick, and she was last seen strangling him as the two of them faded into the distance. Nobody saw where they went or whether she ever let go.

At the table, the Grandmaster admitted that the ghost's rather ordinary name had been picked automatically, not chosen by the storytellers: "We do not choose the names for some of these automatically listed."

## Personality and Abilities

Christina had long blonde hair, a tiara and a flowing white gown, and she seemed only half present in the world. She glowed a pale blue. Her eyes burned yellow and then orange, and her voice echoed through every corner of the space.

Her powers suited a ghost with unfinished business. She could hurl a gust of wind, drain life with a necrotic touch, move through solid bodies, and unleash a deafening thunderclap screech. She could also undo transformations. As she cut through one disguise, she declared:

> "That's not who you really are."

## Relationships

- Frederick: her missing groom, who failed to show up on their wedding day. She searched for him and was last seen strangling him as they faded into the distance together.
