**Thea** is [[The Arbiter]]'s small, pink, impish assistant at [[The Dread Fortress]]. Voiced by **Caustic Phoenix** (Bec).

> Not to be confused with **Thea Plintos** of House Plintos in Cor'thun ([[Skypiercer]]), a different character.

## Who she is
- About a foot tall, pink and potato-shaped, with no neck, unimpressive wings and, surprisingly, hair (she sheds it when stressed).
- The Arbiter made her from a worm "plucked from the soil." In his words it's "a business relationship," and he is not her father. She calls him **Papa**.
- She can't read or write ("You know I'm illiterate"), but she opens his mail anyway.
- Her blood is **teal** and changes with her recent diet.
- The Fortress has "a good dental program," and she has made a fortune from the Tooth Fairy.
- She summons champions through portals (once, a fart portal mixed with glitter).

## History
- **Season 5 (the Season of Fate vision):** the Lord of Fate's new assistant.
- **Season 6:** created for real in *Murder at the Dread Fortress* from the worm that "sucked up the blood of the arena." Iris murdered her the same episode, and the Arbiter brought her back ("Just because I created her"). She was about seven weeks old by the Season 6 finale.
- **Season 7:** put in charge of finding champions, with mixed results. She makes her own segment, *Thea Teaches*, in which [[Claude]] the janitor is an evil wizard. She was stuck as a pink T-Rex after a Box of Chaos polymorph wand (S7E6–S7E7), and she vacuums up disintegrated champions and tries to piece them back together ("This is going to be my greatest puzzle").
