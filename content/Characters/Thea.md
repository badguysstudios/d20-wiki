**Thea** is [[The Arbiter]]'s small, pink, impish assistant at [[The Dread Fortress]]. She is roughly a foot tall and shaped like a potato, and she was once a worm. Now she summons champions, runs errands she forgets, wrangles the [[Chinchillas]] and gets thrown at people, all while pursuing gold stars and employee of the month. She is voiced by Caustic Phoenix. She should not be confused with Thea of [[Plintos|House Plintos]] in [[Cor'thun]], a different person from the days of [[Skypiercer]].

> "A mindless worm that I plucked from the soil to do my bidding." — the Arbiter, describing his ward

## Story

### The Season of Fate
Thea first appeared in the Season of Fate, a vision of rule that only the Arbiter lived through. In it he reigned as the Lord of Fate, and Thea was his brand-new assistant. In [[Episodes/Season 5/099 - S5E1 — Rise of the Lord of Fate — Atlas v Killy Yesman|Rise of the Lord of Fate]] he granted her his old title, "the Arbiter." She explained her credentials: as a worm she had drunk champions' spilled blood and lived among their bones, so she knew their injuries, weaknesses and strengths. Her flesh went gray when she meditated. She opened portals by clapping, without much control over what came through, and that night it was chinchillas with towels, an AA battery and hibachi chefs. She stole the Acolytes' dice for the end-of-round rolls, called for "blood cannons," proved bad at counting, and took over the closing speech. Somewhere in all this she chopped off her own thumb and fed it to the crowd.

The weeks that followed built her legend one disaster at a time. She claimed variously to have a mother, to have gone to college (a lie), and to have been "specially bred" by a loving family "to be an invincible worm." When someone asked whether she could die, the Lord of Fate answered, "No, unfortunately." She bled sparkling blue glitter, had a credit score of about 20, and was thrown as a weapon so often that it became a job description. She face-hugged [[Professor Alonzo Quixote Cervantes|Cervantes]], was caught mid-javelin flight by [[King Bofus|King Man Frog Bofus]]'s tongue, was hurled at [[Granny Edith]] by [[Teddy Oso]] (she had openly taken Granny's side), served as a basketball in [[Bone Daddy]]'s match and as a fart gun against [[Copycat|Kopy Katt]], and was flung through [[Frigidoom]] by [[Atlas]]. Frigidoom's ice also froze her into a popsicle. She ended that night by pulling Frigidoom's plug.

Her working life was a long fight for gold stars, which the Lord of Fate carved into her forehead. She had five ink stars by the end of the Chaos in Chinchilla Forest match and earned a sixth against Frigidoom. She claimed seven, lost and regained one, lost two more in a single night, and had some stolen by a copy before the Lord of Fate partly restored them. Her bedroom, originally a large closet, became his office, so she slept in a box. The Lord of Fate classed her as a 1099 independent contractor with no paid time off, and the Acolytes helped her with the tax paperwork. Formal complaints piled up about a farmer's pig's feet and about Mrs. Humphrey's children, who had been rolled down a hill. She kidnapped champions to book them and rented [[The Crimson Keep]], a wedding venue with a cleaning crew included. She laced everyone's corn dogs with laxatives "as a bonding experience," and on one occasion licked up [[Bob the Barbarian]]'s leg sweat ("spice"). She gave [[Robbie Clark and the Hunger|Robbie Clark]] New Balance shoes, and cried when [[The Hunger]] refused her hug.

She lost her teeth and her wings during one break and duct-taped the wings back on. Later she lost her teeth again and regrew them, first as two sets of human teeth and then as endlessly regrowing sharp ones, all while brewing sun cola with the goblins. She revived champions, let [[Thomas the Goblin]] heal them, and gave [[Ruby Rust]] band-aids. She found a 10,000-email backlog on a 1995 Windows computer, launched "Thea answers emails," duct-taped Bofus's cheeks, kicked both champions in the shins, and joined [[Kyle Stoddard]]'s Simperi. She once caught [[The Shadow King]]'s blade and threw it away. She also became briefly radicalized, complete with blonde hair, sunglasses and a mustache. For the season finale she organized the first Chaos Royale to cheer the Lord of Fate up, and she delivered the season's closing address while holding Dylan the chinchilla.

### The Season of the Trials
In the real timeline, Thea was born in [[Episodes/Season 6/117 - S6E1 — Murder at the Dread Fortress (TTRPG Murder Mystery)|Murder at the Dread Fortress]], created from the worm that "sucked up the blood of the arena." She was immediately murdered by [[Iris]] and found face down in gravy. As a "corpse" she kept talking. The Arbiter brought her back ("Just because I created her"), and she finished the night perched on Iris's shoulder. Along the way she revealed a gold-star chart, a friendship with [[Tim]], a ban on hot dogs except "backwards," and a claimed trademark on "time eyes," which she says earns her money every time the Arbiter casts the spell.

On [[The Ether Drifts]] she was given mind-speech. She delivered the chaos event, and a piece of her skull went into [[The Box of Chaos|Gerald]], whose power she then channelled into Atlas. In the Deathmatch that followed she was Atlas's backpack, then his core (together they made "Fatlas"). She swallowed a blue pill on his behalf and was ejected as an escape pod. She had also forgotten to send the invitations. During Tip to Tip she glowed neon pink from eating glow sticks, was lured to a mausoleum by a lich grandfather for hide-and-go-seek, and asked [[Richard S. Skelman]] to prom. She threw her poop at him out of love, tore off [[Richard Knottley]]'s arm with a rocket pack, and paid four teeth in rent to sit inside the Box of Chaos. She broke the round-three card over her knee. The Arbiter now wants to send her to a therapist. One team named itself after the mission to "find Thea, clearly the most chaotic object in the universe."

For The Hunt for #2 she threw a kitchen party at which goblins and chinchillas drank the Dread Fortress dry. She assembled Team C through a pink spiral portal, licked a gold star and handed it to [[The Duke]], and announced that the Arbiter gives her an unlimited budget. By the season finale she was "about seven weeks old." She could count to three, had fed the receipts to [[Doug the Pug|Doug]], and was keeping her farts in a jar.

### The Capture the Flag Crossover
At the [[Episodes/Season 6/123 - × Wounded Warrior Project, Capture the Flag charity crossover (title not in metadata)|Wounded Warrior Project charity crossover]], Thea arrived in boxing gloves, carrying a steak knife. The Arbiter then transformed her into her "true form": the **Abyss Devourer**, a gargantuan purple worm thirty to forty feet long, with multiple tongues and rotating rings of teeth. She refused to eat one combatant because of how he tasted and split a tongue on a stalagmite. Beans's Wall of Fire killed her, and she slid back into the ravine. She was resurrected, and by the end she was Thea the Imp again.

### The Season of Vigilance
In Season 7 the Arbiter gave Thea the job of finding champions, with mixed results. In [[Episodes/Season 7/129 - S7E1 — A Soup Served Cold — DeeDrah v Gazpacho Man Andy Gammage|A Soup Served Cold]] she skipped the work for "a craft… project." She heckled [[Andy Gammage]] from among the insulting children and debuted *Thea Teaches*, her own unapproved commercial. In it, [[Claude]], the Fortress's French janitor, is an evil wizard. She refused to explain the commercial and hopped up the steps farting. By the next week the Grandmaster was calling her "a potato of a brain." Thea still wrote the champion profiles, tasted the vodka first, tripped while carrying [[Akumu]]'s weapons, gave the cheese professor's head to Doug, and played darts with the Grandmaster. When she had nothing to do, the Grandmaster noted that there was "nothing more terrifying than Thea idle."

Against [[The Pebble]] she stole a mic for karaoke and was outscreamed. She owed the Pebble a one-time call, so she shielded him. She got hit with a poison balloon, clamped a halberd with her butt and rode it, did burnouts with [[Iowa-Ska]], and finally carried the dead Pebble off with a potion. In [[Episodes/Season 7/134 - S7E6 — Bone Force — Jerry Bonesman v TD|Bone Force]] she opened the champions' portal with a fart and volunteered "as tribute." Then [[Jerry Bonesman]]'s knockoff polymorph wand, a prize from the Box of Chaos, turned her into a pink T-Rex. She body-slammed and gnawed a Wall of Force and napped by the fountain. She stayed a T-Rex long enough to wreck a Fortress hallway before the Arbiter restored her.

After that she was sent for toilet paper and ran around the lava fields instead. She vacuumed up a fart smell to make a candle. When both [[Livid Lady Lin]] and [[Gandor Thalion Umbrae]] were reduced to dust, she vacuumed them up too, along with a Gilmore Girls DVD, and set about piecing them back together: "This is going to be my greatest puzzle." In the last match of the season she ate the welcome packet and pooped out the contract. She also promised the chinchillas triple pay, mopped the soaked grass, and admitted she had removed Claude's arms "earlier," though she says she'll give them back.

## Personality and Abilities
Thea is pink, about a foot high and potato-shaped, with no neck and unimpressive wings that shed glitter. She has long pointy ears, a ridged spine, an arrow-tipped tail and tiny bony fingers. She is missing a thumb, and she claims at least five eyeballs. She also has hair, which she sheds when stressed. Her blood has been glitter-blue, green with fear and, most recently, teal, changing with whatever she has been eating. Her jaw can open wide enough to swallow something her own size, which she uses on hot dogs, Gushers, doughnuts, goldfish crackers, glow sticks and the occasional ankle. She is lactose intolerant, and has been told: "Don't feed the Thea."

Her main talent is portals: pink, glittery and summoned by claps, pixie dust or farts. She has also summoned chinchillas, spoken with animals, discovered she can give a shock, and by her own account cannot die. She cannot read or write, though she opens the Arbiter's mail anyway. She is loud, bloodthirsty, devoted to the Deathmatches, and stays pink by rolling in champions' blood. She insists, loudly and often, that she is people. The Acolytes agree, and there is a hashtag. Her guiding philosophy: "If you just let people think you're stupid, you don't have to be in charge of anything." She has been called "too stupid to be held accountable." She says the Fortress has "a good dental program," and through it she has collected a great deal of Tooth Fairy money.

## Relationships
- [[The Arbiter]]: her creator and boss, who says he gave her "your consciousness and your existence." He calls it a business relationship and denies being her father. She wanted to call him Daddy and settled for **Papa**.
- [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|The Grandmaster]]: Thea works for her and the Arbiter, and is the one who cleans up. They play darts, and the Grandmaster thinks little of her brain.
- [[Claude]]: the Fortress janitor, cast as the evil wizard of *Thea Teaches*. She is currently holding his arms.
- [[Iris]]: murdered her on her first night, then gave her a shoulder to sit on.
- [[Doug the Pug|Doug]]: the recipient of her receipts and a severed cheese head.
- [[Chinchillas]]: her responsibility, from feeding and walking to unionizing and triple pay.
- [[Stuart]]: the Fortress IT man, who resets her email password.
- [[Richard S. Skelman]]: her prom proposal and the target of her love-thrown poop.
- [[The Pebble]]: she owed him a call, shielded him, and carried him off when he fell.
- [[Atlas]]: wore her as a backpack, then as a core.
