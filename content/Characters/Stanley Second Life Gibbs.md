**Stanley Second Life Gibbs** was a hooded cowboy gunslinger who called himself "the fastest gun in the West" and "judge, jury, and executioner." He was pulled into [[The Void]] for a Western-saloon duel against the accident-prone [[Gideon Regalis Electus Gregorius the 537th|Greg]]. A small floating drone kept bringing him back from the dead, so he died ten times in a single Deathmatch, more than any champion had before. The eleventh death was the one that stuck.

## Story

Stanley arrived during the Shadow King's rule, when [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] smashed [[The Shadow King|the Shadow King's]] new PlayStation, crushed her amulet and opened a portal for the champions in [[Episodes/Season 4/087 - S4E9 — Shootout — Stanley Second Life Gibbs v Gideon Regalis Electus Gregorius the 537th (Greg)|S4E9 — Shootout]]. He introduced himself as "Stanley Second Life McGibbs" and told the Grandmaster, "I am the answer to the Call of Duty." The arena was an abandoned ghost-town saloon with a piano playing to itself. Stanley opened with a trick shot through a flying ace of spades, which also put a hole in an Acolyte's cowboy hat. He then took to Greg at once ("Oh, I think I like you, Gregory"), proposed drinking wagers, and offered to turn his back with his hands in the air to give Greg a free shot. Greg's polite bow sent both pistols tumbling out of their holsters. Both went off, and Stanley was dead before the duel had properly begun.

That was when the drone showed itself. It was a tiny mechanical figure with one eye, and it sparked Stanley back to life in a flash of light. The Grandmaster would only say that such people "made a deal of some sort" and that "their secrets are theirs to keep." Stanley leaned into it. He threw himself into [[The Box of Chaos]] after the Shadow King fed it a pulped Acolyte's soul. His corpse came flying back out holding a greater silver pistol, and once revived he named it **Daffodil**. He caught about eight shots of tequila from a vendor in the stands, then sat down in a chair and kept sipping whatever stale beer was on the table. From there he died over and over. A shot from Greg made his heart explode. A bullet shattered his tumbler and a glass shard opened his neck. A ricochet off the floor caught him between the eyes, the Shadow King's prismatic rings left him a skeleton in his chair, and at one point he simply lost the will to live. The Grandmaster complained that the respawn was terrible and that this was "basically spawn killing," so the drone started reviving him in a different spot.

The Shadow King then gave Greg some advice: "maybe shoot the little contraption." Stanley fought on. He fired a shadow-magic round from his old pistol, but it only blew off Greg's pinky and accidentally gave him perfect vision. When his own healing came up short, the Grandmaster tossed him an extra die: "Second wind for second life. I got you, Gibbs." Greg's next shot ricocheted around the saloon and dropped three chandeliers, and the last one snapped Stanley's neck. Then a bullet went straight down Stanley's barrel and fired his own round back into his face. For his last big play, he melted his hero point into a single golden round. Daffodil bloomed into a lava-dripping flower and fired it "at the speed of light," taking two of Greg's fingers. Soon afterwards Daffodil's holster melted to ash and the gun clattered to the floor.

By then Greg's remaining pistol had revealed it was sentient and was fighting on its own. Three beautifully placed shots shattered the drone, and the last bullet ricocheted off a broken piece into Stanley's chest. "Would you look at that? Seems there won't be a second life for me after all," he said. He tipped his hat, so to speak, to "quite the performance," and asked Greg to bring his big sister next time. **Stanley died for good.** Greg died moments later at the hands of his own gun, and the match ended with no winner. In Dried Blood, the drone admitted it was "quite sick of reviving that sack of shit," a man who flung himself "into a mysterious flaming box" and sat still under falling chandeliers. It ended with: "And quite frankly, I will miss him."

## Personality and Abilities

Stanley wore a hood, not a hat, and he tipped it anyway. He spoke in a slow, friendly cowboy drawl and handed out nicknames like "Timmy 10 guns" for Greg. He never turned down a drink, a wager or a big sister ("I never said no to hanging out with anybody's big sister"). He mentioned an old pal "Jack" behind the bar and drank tequila like a professional. He had no dog, despite the Grandmaster's guess that "Justice" might be one. He took his own deaths with remarkable calm, offering notes like "maybe go for more center mass next time." At the table, his player said he simply "really wanted to be a cowboy."

In a fight Stanley was all flair: card tricks, elaborate pistol twirls, and six bullets tossed in the air to land in the cylinder. He carried an old pistol that fired transparent rounds full of swirling shadow magic, and later Daffodil, the silver pistol from the Box of Chaos. His real lifeline was the drone, which healed him with little holographic hands and revived him each time he died. It grew visibly more exasperated as the night went on. Once the drone was gone, so were his second lives.

## Relationships

- [[Gideon Regalis Electus Gregorius the 537th|Greg]]: His opponent and fast friend. Stanley liked him from the start, was killed by him repeatedly, mostly by accident, and asked him to bring his big sister next time.
- [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|The Grandmaster]]: Begged him to "quit dying," threw popcorn at his drone to make it wake him up, and gave him a second wind for his second life.
- [[The Shadow King]]: Gave away Stanley's secret by telling Greg to shoot the drone. Stanley called it "pretty underhanded."
- The drone: His tiny, one-eyed reviver and the source of his nickname. It said it would miss him.
