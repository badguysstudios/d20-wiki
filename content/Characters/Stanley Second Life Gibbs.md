## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 4/087 - S4E9 — Shootout — Stanley Second Life Gibbs v Gideon Regalis Electus Gregorius the 537th (Greg)|S4E9 — Shootout — Stanley Second Life Gibbs v Gideon Regalis Electus Gregorius the 537th (Greg)]]
(BDTeej). Introduced as "Stanley Second Life McGibbs" and called Gibbs elsewhere. "**The fastest gun in the West.**"
- Wears a hood, not a hat. Speaks with a cowboy drawl.
- Calls himself "judge, jury, and executioner." Has no dog. An old pal "Jack" is behind the bar (whiskey joke). An expert tequila drinker.
- **Dies and is revived by a small floating mechanical drone** with a single eye and holographic healing hands. The Grandmaster says such individuals "made a deal of some sort" to keep coming back, and "their secrets are theirs to keep."
- **Died 10 times this episode.** The cause of each:
  1. Greg's bow misfires.
  2. The Box of Chaos (he threw himself in).
  3. A heart explosion from a gunshot.
  4. A glass shard in the neck.
  5. A floor ricochet between the eyes.
  6. Lightning and poison from the prismatic rings.
  7. Losing "the will to live."
  8. A chandelier snapping his neck.
  9. His own round backfiring into his face.
  10. Final: the drone shattered and a ricochet to the chest.
  (The order above follows the transcript; Teej's own count was "10".)
- Weapons: an old pistol with shadow-magic rounds, and **Daffodil**, a greater silver pistol from the Box of Chaos.
- Last wish: that Greg bring his big sister next time. Calls Greg "Timmy 10 guns."
