**Jameson** ("like the liquor") is a squirrel from [[Bezil]]'s neighborhood. He wears a cowboy hat and a big cowboy mustache as bushy as his tail, chews a silver toothpick, rolls his own cigarettes and carries two revolvers. He has an ex-wife. He hates Bezil for what Bezil did to his begonias.

## Story
In [[Episodes/Season 7/139 - S7E11 — The Cat's Meow — Zhora Bloodfang v Bezil|S7E11 — The Cat's Meow — Zhora Bloodfang v Bezil]], Jameson was watching from a railing at [[The Ether Drifts]]. Bezil pointed him out as proof that the squirrels in his neighborhood hate him. [[Zhora Bloodfang]] decided the enemy of her enemy was her friend and invited him onto her shoulder. Jameson drew his revolvers, spun them on his fingers and fired them into the air, sealing the pact.

When Bezil spent his heroic surge summoning his draconic spirit, Jameson emptied his revolvers through it from the crowd: "That's for what you did to my begonias, you son of a bitch." Bezil answered that the begonias were never in season and were ugly too, and Jameson calmly rolled a cigarette. Later, when Bezil told Zhora to go pet Jameson instead, the squirrel declined. He doesn't much like being manhandled, and "No one could really domesticate me anyways. Ask my ex-wife."

## Relationships
- [[Bezil]]: his sworn enemy. Bezil wants to put him in a stew with kraken.
- [[Zhora Bloodfang]]: his ally, who calls him "brother."
