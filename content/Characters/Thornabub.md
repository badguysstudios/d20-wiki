## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 2/033 - S2E10 — Filicide — Glassy v Thornabub|S2E10 — Filicide — Glassy v Thornabub]]
(Glorious George). A winged devil from the Nine Hells who **rules a small demiplane of hell**.
- **Family:**
  - Glassy's father. His wife is Violtha.
  - Mentions a son whose sword he beat him with (Dried Blood names the brother Adam).
- **Career:** a dealmaker in timeshares and soul coins with "connections" to Asmodeus. He **once interned for Asmodeus**, back when he was "a shmedium." "It's the family business": he tried for years to teach Glassy to torture properly.
- **Body:** he has **two claw-mark scars across his back under his wings, left by Glassy's mother.**
- **Gear and powers:**
  - Warhammer, shield, chainmail, holy symbol.
  - Charger feat and Channel Divinity: Touch of Death.
  - Blindness/Deafness, Thaumaturgy, Aura of Vitality, Toll the Dead, Spiritual Weapon (a massive flaming hammer), Animate Dead, Dispel Magic.
- **What he wants:** to drag Glassy home to Avernus and marry her to the rakshasa Mahadi so she can spawn infernal legions to take over the material plane.
- **What he gave up:** returned the Sun Sword to the box. Lost his armor, shield and hammer to the magnet.
- **Outcome:** won by downing Glassy and taking **Jerry's soul** by infernal contract. He's expecting her (and Jerry) at Christmas. Left for a pub crawl with the Arbiter via Luskan.

### [[Episodes/Battle for the Crown/048 - S2E20 — Battle for the Crown Round 3 — Flynry & Prof. Stabbz v Thornabub & Mcswiggins|S2E20 — Battle for the Crown Round 3 — Flynry & Prof. Stabbz v Thornabub & Mcswiggins]]
(GloriousGe0rge), Team Arbiter. Called **Satan** by Mcswiggins ("actual Satan", "Beelzebub Lucifer").
- Has **hands, wings, hooves and a tail**. A "deal maker," a trickster; flies.
- **Has a daughter.** He says the Deathmatch worked out things between them, and he asks whether she wants a rematch.
- **Became the Arbiter's close friend over drinks and "family struggles"**; the Arbiter was setting up a guest room for him.
- **Powers:** Guiding Bolt, Holy Weapon ("of the nine hells"), Charger, Death Ward, Action Surge, Second Wind, Cloudkill. Resistant to fire.
- **His pillow:** red silk, covered in his enemies' blood "and a few other fluids."
- **In this episode:**
  - Secretly Death Warded himself and Mcswiggins before the fight.
  - Bullied Gerald for a 9th-level scroll (Meteor Swarm).
  - Was the last one standing; made a beer slushie.
