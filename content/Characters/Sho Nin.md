## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 4/083 - S4E5 — Toxicity — Toocher the Toxic v Sho Nin|S4E5 — Toxicity — Toocher the Toxic v Sho Nin]]
(Forgeling).
- **Who he is:**
  - **15 years old.** Level 6 Kensei monk / level 4 wizard. Toocher calls him "Sho Nin Protagonist/Pootagonist."
  - Confident outside, full of self-doubt and unworthiness within. Overwhelmed by the arena's grandeur.
- **The wind:** a latent wind power passed down to him that skipped a generation. He is on a quest to understand it, and perhaps his mother, "the musky maiden," has answers. Twice a voice in the wind gave him bad advice (look to the stars, look to the north).
- **In this episode:**
  - Got the Moon Sickle +3 by trading his scabbard to the Box of Chaos.
  - Used bladesong and green-flame blade, and cut Toocher's leg open.
  - Rolled five natural 1s.
  - Was disarmed and killed with his own sickle; the wind carried his body away.
