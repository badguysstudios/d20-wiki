## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 6/122 - S6 Ep6 — The Hunt for 2 - Part 1 — Team C (Frigidum, Bob the Barbarian, the Duke) v Billy|S6 Ep6 — The Hunt for #2 - Part 1 — Team C (Frigidum, Bob the Barbarian, the Duke) v Billy]]
(Sensei Suplex). **A rat man with a hat and a curled mustache**, also called "Ratman," "the saint of shit," and "flower rat."
- **His hat holds** a tablet, a second Ratman, a Zippo, an entourage of rat people, a shotgun (from his cloak), a katana, pocket sand, and an RPG (from his head).
- **He has never drunk alcohol before tonight**, but has drunk "the smelliest of sewer waters."
- **Wants** a bathroom made of all cheese and to put on a magic show. A tarot-card trick drew the Tower.
- **Stage fright:** has never performed for more than three people. Self-described "master improviser."
- **Thea's gold star made him "my first fan."** He will betray the team to become leader: "It's in my nature." Wears a chastity belt (Dried Blood).

### [[Episodes/Season 6/123 - S6E7, The Hunt for 2 - Part 2 (season finale). Team C final - Frigid Doom v Bob the Barbarian v the Duke|S6E7, The Hunt for #2 - Part 2 (season finale). Team C final - Frigid Doom v Bob the Barbarian v the Duke]]
(Sensei). A rat man magician with a bottomless hat, from Brooklyn, New York, and the sewers of New York City.
- **Habits:** his fecal matter holds enemies' corpses and cheeses (one from 1832). He believes cheese ages like wine. Favorite food: ham.
- **Tricks:**
  - Rat minions in overalls with wrenches, and rat spotters with binoculars.
  - A metal-laced diaper, a rat trap, an exploding "Duval!" plushie gator, artillery and two nuclear strikes.
  - A giant New York hood rat with Glocks. A bazooka. Rats with Molotovs.
  - "Shitlord" awakening (golden glow, brown hair to blonde) with a shit bomb. Ultra Instinct dodging.
  - The New York combo: pizza, a Mets fastball, a subway train.
- Lost his right leg to Denise and maimed his own other foot with his train.
- Got smelling salts from the Box of Chaos. Writes his own rules ("It is canon now").
- **As a rat child he revered "the one true king, the shit lord" as a deity** and dreamed of leading an army in his name. In his last words he calls himself "a simple rat man in a single outfit bought from a dollar store."
- Eaten by Frigid Doom and revived. **Vows to stage an uprising and come for the Arbiter's "soul and your throne"** (Dried Blood).
