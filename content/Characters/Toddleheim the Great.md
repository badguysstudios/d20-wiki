## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/024 - S2 Ep1, Fate of the Arbiter (Fate of the Arbiter Part 1)|S2 Ep1, Fate of the Arbiter (Fate of the Arbiter Part 1)]]
(Ahnubyss)
- A dwarf wizard, short, bearded, cocky. Also called "**the Hot Toddy**." Others mangle his name: Toddy, Tiddlywinks, Timmy, Timothy, Trevor, Toto, Thomas.
- Says he specializes in Firebolt and loves acid splash because "they scream rather loud," and enjoys killing those who challenge him.
- Reads Dwarvish. Claims to have seen cursed rings like the Arbiter's "many, many eons ago."
- Got drunk on the cellar brandy and played the fight hungover. He kept his healing pearl.
- Spells used: Benign Transposition, invisibility, Mold Earth, Sleep (abandoned), Magic Missile, Burning Hands, shield, Misty Step, acid splash.
- Ended the fight at 2 HP.

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/045 - S2E17 — Fate of the Arbiter - The Hall of Fates — Toddleheim, Locket and Flynry reach the Grandmaster|S2E17 — Fate of the Arbiter - The Hall of Fates — Toddleheim, Locket and Flynry reach the Grandmaster]]
(Ahnubyss). An old, well-travelled wizard.
- **A werewolf since the castle ward;** he can half-transform (yellow eyes, teeth, claws, hair).
- Drank the Seer's potion of aging: physically ~12 years younger.
- "This was supposed to be my last adventure."
- **Ripped off Kenny's arm** while a werewolf (per Kenny).
- Spells this episode: Fire Bolt, Misty Step, healing, Create Bonfire, Magic Missile, Mold Earth (pushed beyond its limits).
- Refused to look into the mirrors and heard only a squeaking like glass cleaning. **Talked Flynry back: "we're your family now."**
