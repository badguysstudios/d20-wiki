## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 2/037 - S2E14 — Shrooms — Ulfgar Greyblade (Mcswiggins) v Snoop & Dog|S2E14 — Shrooms — Ulfgar Greyblade (Mcswiggins) v Snoop & Dog]]
(bonus_stage_rob). A broad dwarf, about **4'8"**, a barbarian with warlock (Hexblade) abilities.
- **Titles:** "the toughest son of a bitch in Drakboldor [?], and the pride of the drunken master."
- **Name:** he insists he goes by Mcswiggins. The sign-up said Ulfgar; the Arbiter blames friends signing people up.
- **Gear and powers:**
  - Greataxe holding ancestral protectors he invokes as "**Jamison, Johnny, and Jack**."
  - **An enchanted, endlessly refilling flask.**
  - Mage Slayer ("Mage Bane"), Eldritch Smite, Hexblade's Curse.
  - Beer-colored ice tomb ("tomb of Lastavus").
- **Personality:** drunk and confused; at home in fart air ("born in them"). Pees on the arena wall and into the hole. "Eat, drink, be merry"; treats his mental health with drink. Thought Gerald was "Greg." Loves "the men of Italy," a band of mustached bards.
- **In this episode:**
  - Got a **lion barrier tattoo** from Gerald.
  - Sucker-punched Snoop during a handshake.
  - Tried to ride the Reverser and was thrown.
  - Fell into the abyss.
  - **In Dried Blood, the Arbiter caught him with a plunger and took him to his lair, "I'm going to have a use for you very soon."**

### [[Episodes/Battle for the Crown/048 - S2E20 — Battle for the Crown Round 3 — Flynry & Prof. Stabbz v Thornabub & Mcswiggins|S2E20 — Battle for the Crown Round 3 — Flynry & Prof. Stabbz v Thornabub & Mcswiggins]]
(Bonus_Stage_Rob), Team Arbiter.
- **Background:**
  - Dwarf-height ("a tall five") and a barbarian with an axe.
  - Has a lifelong struggle with alcohol.
  - Arrived with a plunger on his face, convinced he's in hell ("falling for 30 hours"). Still "feeling the mushrooms from last time."
- **Powers:**
  - **Rage chant:** "Hear me, Jameson, Johnny, and Jack… We're on the attack. Get angry."
  - Eldritch Smite.
  - **Tomb of Levistus, which encases him in beer-ice.**
- **His "pillow"** was the Arbiter's couch cushion.
- **In this episode:**
  - Took a crit fireball from the duck bomb.
  - Called Gerald "Greg / Jeffrey / Jim."
  - Fed Thornabub to the box and got a weapon of fate (later shattered).
  - Swan-dived into the box and was saved by Death Ward.
  - Gave his death save to Thornabub, who called him "McWackles."
