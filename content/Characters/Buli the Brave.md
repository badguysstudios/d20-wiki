## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 2/036 - S2E13 — The Brave — Buli v Chancy|S2E13 — The Brave — Buli v Chancy]]
(BunnyDreadful).
- **Species:** a tiny owlbear (player, Dried Blood). The Grandmaster calls him "owlet" and "owlbear."
- **Body:** 3 ft 1 in, feathered, with claws ("not hands"). His owl head spins around when he's furious.
- **Speech:** says "wage" for rage and "Fredwick" for Frederick.
- **Gear and abilities:**
  - Many axes: a great axe (snapped at the hilt) and a battle axe.
  - Tiny bagpipes.
  - A fur pelt cloak and clothes, sacrificed to Gerald.
  - A barbarian with rage, frenzy, reckless attack and Relentless Rage, plus Charger and Tavern Brawler.
- **Claims:**
  - Destroyed an Aboleth with his bare hands and bathed in its blood.
  - "Owlbears make the best warriors… we don't even have tear ducts."
  - "400 (500) brains."
  - Prays to "the great owlbear spirits."
- **Epithets:** the Brave, the Brains, the Fierce, the Strong, the Murderer, the Destroyer, the Great, the Burned, the Deathless.
- **In this episode:**
  - Took Chancy's small lute from "the rack of weapons that they said anybody could have" and dropped it into the well.
  - Flew by spell scroll and was polymorphed into a butterfly.
  - Rose twice from zero before dying. His body was thrown down the well by the Arbiter.
  - In Dried Blood, he is falling forever: "I'll be back."
