## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/040 - Coriander Society, Fate of the Faithful Part 1 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society, Fate of the Faithful Part 1 (A Crossover)]]

- **Introduces himself as** "ex-wizard, coriander agent, private investigator, and reluctant follower of a very particular dragon goddess" (Tiamat, her symbol on his coat). He has lost his wizard status ("can't soul gaze anymore").
- **Background:** from Earth. Says it's Christmas, not a corporate holiday.
- **Gear:**
  - The gun **Krontaka**, which hungers for fey blood and can drag him.
  - Spell bullets.
  - The **red kiss** talisman (corruption).
  - Coral that becomes a water trident.
  - **A case of carrying/briefcase** holding a wrench, a rubber chicken, life rafts, an anchor, a quarter ton of dynamite, and now **thousands of wooden spoons, Knight armor and swords, and Hathim's head**.
  - A duel-disk camera.
  - An earpiece radio to Asena.
- **Other:** has a cat, **Chester**. Can do dragon fear and mirror image.

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/041 - Coriander Society - Fate of the Faithful Part 2 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society - Fate of the Faithful Part 2 (A Crossover)]]
(NinjaManMat1). A warlock, formerly a wizard ("wizard sight that he used to have").
- Gumshoe and master of disguise, with a Magitek cell phone that plays metal and keeps local Void time.
- Speaks Infernal; specialist in killing fiends; has a fear aura.
- **Krontaka ("Island's Bane"):** a bronze revolver forged from an ancient (Celtic warrior's) spear and a slain devil's blood. It is sentient enough to send emotions. He fires spell bullets with it.
- Cat Chester. A sword from the tombs on Ashen is being forged into Chester's knife.
- Goal: find a way home.
- Prophecy: a choice between "what you want" and his honor; one path home, another into "a duty thrust upon you."
