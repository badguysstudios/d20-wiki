## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Pieces of a King/124 - Pieces of a King Ep 1 — DӒMMERUNG|Pieces of a King Ep 1 — DӒMMERUNG]]
(CriticalBard)
- She has dark skin, shifting green-tan eyes, linens, a blood-ruby headpiece with silver, a **mask that always covers her face**, and a bronze scimitar she blesses with sand daily.
- **A constant sand aura** that reacts to her mood (it slithers when she's annoyed, goes spiky when Grimnar calls her out).
- **She can become sand (sand form or whirlwind).** Her **cheek briefly turned to glass** when lightning hit her sand.
- **Mantras:** "the sand provides"; "for I am it"; "It permeates all." "**The only one that I serve is the Lord.**"
- She has tremorsense and used it to read Stolas's heartbeat. Her home realm is within the Void, outside the fortress.
- **Interested in planar travel, including to planes that may no longer exist**; Grimnar thinks she is anxious to leave.
- She saw a neutral presence watching her from a black hole in the Void. She is from a land where sandworms are surely a thing.
- Spells and abilities: Guidance, Death Ward (a sand cage around Muffy's heart), Erupting Earth, Abrasive Blast, Heat Metal, Sand Dervish. She has negative Charisma.

### [[Episodes/Pieces of a King/125 - Pieces of a King Ep 2 — THE WAR COUNCIL|Pieces of a King Ep 2 — THE WAR COUNCIL]]
(CriticalBard)
- A sand being: "She is the sand, the sand is she." Has tremorsense (30 ft) that detects heartbeats.
- Rituals with an ankh in sand; prays to and serves **the God-King, "the dark Lord she originally served"** (pyramids, sarcophagi, desert). "In her mind, she still serves one person only."
- "I was not created to eat. I was created to protect." A guardian; meditates rather than sleeps.
- Turns sand into glass and sculpts; casts wall of stone from sand; leaves a glass fingerprint when casting guidance.
- Commander Kaim interrupts her meditations (DM's improvised image).

### [[Episodes/Pieces of a King/126 - Pieces of a King Ep 3 — The Warhead|Pieces of a King Ep 3 — The Warhead]]
(CriticalBard).
- "Born of the desert sands," scaled with rough hands.
- **Doesn't sleep**; meditates in drifting sand. **Doesn't dream.**
- Divines through a sand dome; has tremorsense.
- Sand form, tree stride, death ward (sand around the heart), longstrider, shillelagh on her scimitar.
- **"I was the war mind for my domain"; created to protect someone else's power and survival.**
- Says what the party seeks "resides within this Warmind."

### [[Episodes/Pieces of a King/127 - Pieces of a King Ep 4 - The Warmind|Pieces of a King Ep 4 - The Warmind]]
(QR0WNTV)
- A sand being created to guard the god-king's pyramids, possibly from "Hardicure" (spelling uncertain).
- Doesn't sleep, has tremorsense, takes sand form (a tornado lower body) and swaps places with allies through sand.
- Serves the God King, not the Grandmaster, and feels no empathy "unless the God King commands it." Her first desire is to return home or repair what was shunted into the Void.
- **She now holds the remnant cylinder inside her chest as a living canopic jar.**
- Charmed Glauccous by forcing sand into his mouth, then placed a 30-day Geas on him. Sensed that the God King lives.
