**Ivanna Kishu** was an aasimar druid, a self-described "protector," who was pulled into the Void to fight the warlock [[Kleen]] in a frozen, abandoned mine. Her name is a pun ("I wanna kiss you"), and she lived up to it, blowing kisses at her enemies and promising to smooch them between spells. She lost the fight, but her story didn't end in the snow. [[The Arbiter]] carried her off to his laboratory and quietly brought her back to life.

## Story

Ivanna was summoned to the Dread Fortress's throne room alongside Kleen, where [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] had spent the evening complaining about the blandness of water. Ivanna made her pitch with a smile and a threat. Violence, she explained, is only one way, "but there are always other ways. There is Ivanna Kishu ways." She swore she was "definitely not OP at all" and announced that she was going to beat Kleen senseless, and then kiss him. Asked what she wanted as a prize, she was promised "the finest eyeball juice dish" from the Grandmaster's chef. She declared she was "drooling already."

The arena was **[[Kirken's Mine]]**, a snowbound ruin far in the north. Her night started badly. In the opening chaos event, [[The Acolytes]] pelted both champions with whatever they had on hand. Kleen dodged most of it, but Ivanna took a faceful of pasta, a cookie, a teacup and a cute pumpkin, and possibly a toilet. She answered Kleen's first barrage of eldritch blasts by smearing the blood off herself and licking it back up ("Not so Kleen, I see"). Then she called down a beam of moonlight and let the Acolytes choose what she would become. They picked a hippogriff. Her arms became white wings, and lipstick appeared on her new beak. The Grandmaster called it "very sexy, sexy Hippogriff," though, as she admitted, "it's a pretty Hippogriff, it's not a strong Hippogriff." Kleen proved it by blasting it out of the sky from six hundred feet away with a perfect shot.

Ivanna refused to break. Her mind held so firmly that the Grandmaster ruled her moonbeam would burn twice as hard. She then healed herself back from the brink simply **by brushing her hair**, a recovery the Grandmaster said she had never seen before. Ivanna wanted to become a lion, settled for a dire wolf "with stripes," won the crowd's blessing in a howling contest, and gained a sheet of protective ice from the Arbiter. He insisted this "has nothing to do with the fact that I placed a small wager on you." She leapt a wide gap and burrowed into a snowdrift to hide. During the Round of the Pig, when every word had to be spoken in Pig Latin or the speaker got a rock to the head, she fumbled her incantations, collected a pebble, and still brought an ice storm down on Kleen with the crowd's favor. Even Kleen's magical snowball and an eldritch blast shaped like Mr. Kleen himself couldn't knock her out of wolf form.

Finally she dropped the wolf entirely. "Ivanna comes back! I am tired of all of this. Let's end it." She struck Kleen with lightning just before the Arbiter grew bored with the distance between them and buried the mine in an avalanche. Both champions fled onto a rickety bridge above a frozen waterfall. There Kleen hit her with a brutal volley and a snowball that evened the fight. Ivanna healed by drinking her own blood back in, which prompted the Arbiter to offer an introduction to his blood-bank friend [[Larry]]. She prayed to the Acolytes for a blessing and found a snowball of her own. When the Grandmaster walled off the bridge and forced the two to stop running, Ivanna shrugged off Kleen's attempt to confuse her and blew him a kiss. Her own snowball flopped at his feet ("I'm proficient in chess balls"). Kleen spent the crowd's favor on one last flurry of blasts, and **Ivanna collapsed into the snowbank**, unable to drink her blood back this time.

> **Ivanna Kishu:** "You stinky balls. I don't want to kiss you."

As the Acolytes were caught in Kleen's victory-lap "shitstorm," the Arbiter grabbed Ivanna by her wings and vanished with her into his laboratory. He laid her on the table and **revived her**, looking down at her body with a promise: "I'm not done with you yet. You will kiss again."

## Personality and Abilities

Ivanna was flirtatious, defiant and unbothered, a fighter who treated a deathmatch as a show and blew kisses in place of taunts. Her running catchphrases were "Me too" and "I'm gonna kiss you," and her insults ran toward "stinky balls." She didn't take orders well, and she kept smiling through blood loss, rocks to the head and an avalanche.

She was a shapeshifting druid with plenty of tricks. She turned into a lipsticked hippogriff with polished nails and a striped dire wolf, and she commanded moonbeams, ice storms, lightning and confusing magic. She also had a touch of sorcery, which she used to stretch her spells across the battlefield. Her healing was her signature: she mended wounds with healing hands, by brushing her hair, and by licking her own spilled blood back into her body, which the Grandmaster compared to "that weird Skyrim dude who could just eat wheels of cheese and heal." As an aasimar, she had wings.

## Relationships

- [[Kleen]]: Her opponent in Kirken's Mine, the warlock who outlasted her healing and killed her on the bridge.
- [[The Arbiter]]: Bet on her, shielded her wolf form with ice, and secretly revived her after her death.
- [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer]]: Hosted her fight, promised her an eyeball juice dish, and penned her in on the bridge to force a finish.
- [[The Acolytes]]: Pelted her with snacks and toilets, then picked her hippogriff form and blessed her howl.
