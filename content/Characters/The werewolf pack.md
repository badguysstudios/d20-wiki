### [[Episodes/Season 3/059 - S3E11 — Blood Feud 1 — Havoc v Isha Inverraseth|S3E11 — Blood Feud 1 — Havoc v Isha Inverraseth]]
(Havoc's pack / family). Dozens of werewolves who appear on the rooftops when Isha asks to leave. They ordered Havoc to finish Isha long ago: "Because you're going to finish it. Or we will." Member names heard: "Asmodai," "Gerald" (uncertain). One reference to an "elder wolf" (1:04:08).
