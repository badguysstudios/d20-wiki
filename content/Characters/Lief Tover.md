## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Live Shows/128 - LIVE at Full Sail Hall of Fame 2024 - Lief Tover v Sheriff Tapatio S. Picante|LIVE at Full Sail Hall of Fame 2024 - Lief Tover v Sheriff Tapatio S. Picante]]
(guest; creator not confirmed). The "Trash Man," called "Trash Viking" by the Sheriff.
- **Background:**
  - Lives in a garbage dump, "my place of residence."
  - Abandoned by his grandfather at two months old, before learning to speak.
  - Raised partly by the dump's coyotes, who fed him by vomiting in his mouth.
  - A cockroach taught him "to fly."
  - His mother told him he'd never read, and he can't read.
- **Claims and allegations:**
  - Studied dirt his whole life.
  - Hasn't eaten in ten days.
  - The Sheriff says he cheats at bingo and was in prison ("three square meals").
  - "Me and Kreek go way back," and he names "Krieg" in round 6. Possibly a Borderlands Krieg cosplay; unconfirmed.
- **Fighting style:** magic learned in the trash dump: a poisoned diaper bomb, acid, the soda-can bullet, "Ball," pancake berry bites, the trash bag. "I don't need a gun."
- **Outcome:** won, by outlasting the Sheriff ("trash lasts forever"). Gave trash to his supporters as gifts and fashion.
