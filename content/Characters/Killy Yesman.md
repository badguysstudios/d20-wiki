## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 5/099 - S5E1 — Rise of the Lord of Fate — Atlas v Killy Yesman|S5E1 — Rise of the Lord of Fate — Atlas v Killy Yesman]]
(Yang). A **Double Sun Cola** salesman found on his billboard.
- **Look:** twig legs; **enormous undulating balloon pecs**; the logo on his chest and shirt; **a hat with soda cans and two straws**; long blonde hair with a shiny bald spot; "the world's most punchable face" with an immovable chin.
- **Powers:** heals by sipping soda; exploding and corrosive bottles; cola as wound sealant; butt cushion.
- **Sponsor breaks:** the giant logo in the sky, and logos painted on Atlas's chest and head.
- **Lore:**
  - "Consider[s] my life to be worthless." Has a girlfriend with a five-year-old iPhone.
  - Was told drinking cola would regrow his hair, but says he doesn't care about being bald.
  - Gave his hat to the Box of Chaos and got smelling salts.
- **In this episode:** he drank Atlas's core and swore they would sell more soda together. He is the winner.

### [[Episodes/Season 6/120 - S6 Ep4 — Tip to Tip - Part 1 (TTRPG One Shot)|S6 Ep4 — Tip to Tip - Part 1 (TTRPG One Shot)]]
(Yangyang). A Double Sun Cola promoter.
- Very muscular with undulating pecs, very thin legs, majestic blonde hair, "the world's most punchable face." Said to wear sunglasses.
- His hat holds two cola bottles with straws.
- Pulls ever-larger bottles from his shorts: regular, deluxe two-handed, a special red bottle, the magnum, and the four-magnum "small rocket."
- Says yes and follows stronger voices. The GM had never met him.
- Chosen leader of Team 3D, then recalled by Double Sun Cola HQ for two months of retraining.
