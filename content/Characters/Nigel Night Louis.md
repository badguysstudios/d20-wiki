## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 4/091 - S4E11 — Beatdown — Sprinkles v Nigel|S4E11 — Beatdown — Sprinkles v Nigel]]
(theplaymatt). A **changeling** actor who sculpted his own chin.
- **Appearance:** long white-gray slicked hair, a salt-and-pepper beard, a Tommy Wiseau coat over a Renaissance shirt, sunglasses, and a perpetually hung-over look.
- **Awards:**
  - BAFTA, Cable Ace, a Teen Choice Award (for "Sponge," the CW's gritty SpongeBob, which came with a sliming), a Tony (best actor in a play), and a SAG award.
  - He also has an Emmy (later used by Melchizedek).
  - He wants an EGOT and an Oscar.
- **Credits:**
  - "There Won't Be Blood" ("You drink my smoothie!").
  - A CW Paddington remake.
  - The Britney Spears musical.
  - A movie where he only ate dirt.
- **Habits:** sponsored by bubbly; drinks "teardrop" bubbly before performances. Eats raw asparagus and dirt. Weeps at the sky once a day. Watches his own movies to cry and yell at himself.
- **Powers:** a method-acting barbarian-bard. Uses Hideous Laughter, Suggestion, a truth spell, Silvery Barbs, Cutting Words, a berserker frenzy, and Phantasmal Force cast on himself.
- **In this episode:**
  - Lost his face to the Box of Chaos and got an Ioun Stone of Fortitude.
  - Snapped his Teen Choice award to dual-wield it.
  - Jumped into the box and died. His corpse was worn by the box's shadow spirit, then fought over from inside.
  - His inner child, a Newsie, killed Melchizedek.
