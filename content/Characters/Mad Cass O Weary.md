## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 2/038 - S2E15 — Black Hole Sun — Professor Stabbz v Mad Cass O Weary|S2E15 — Black Hole Sun — Professor Stabbz v Mad Cass O Weary]]
(DeadAussieGamer). A **tiefling monk**, resistant to fire. He has **glowing yellow eyes**, a wide puckish grin and a **mohawk that becomes a mane of fire** when he uses hellish rebuke. He fights with fists and **a boomerang**; the GM calls him Australian.
- **Joining the match:** a last-minute substitute when the other champion's "portal provider was down." He wiped his ass with the Arbiter's contract and stapled it to the Arbiter's head.
- **Personality and lore:**
  - Constantly drunk ("19 drunk"); his drinking "started when my dad went out for some cigarettes."
  - Eats glass (he's done it before) and drank the GM's mouthwash.
  - Has a chiropractor appointment coming up.
  - Hates people with cool names.
  - Dag: "a violent sociopath," not wise; "heart of a barbarian."
- **Abilities:** deflect missiles, walking on liquid and up walls, slow fall, "drunk(en) luck" (ki to cancel disadvantage), Tavern Brawler and Grappler, stunning strike, flurry of blows.
- **In this episode:** he fought on after knocking himself unconscious under hold person.
- **Death:** burned by the Sunburst and sucked into the black hole while laughing and giving the middle finger. "I'm gonna come for you in your nightmares!"
- **Dried Blood canon:** Cass has become a Freddy Krueger figure, "burned alive in a different dimension."
