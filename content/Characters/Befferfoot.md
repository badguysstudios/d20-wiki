### [[Episodes/Season 3/060 - S3E12 — Blood Puppets — Basil Herringbone v Befferfoot|S3E12 — Blood Puppets — Basil Herringbone v Befferfoot]]
(Chunk, Perception Studio). A half-orc with broken grammar ("I here to kill").
- Has sharp nipples.
- **Gained +2 Intelligence and a bigger forehead from an alien abduction.**
- **Abilities and gear:** fire bolt, shield ("the coward spell"), misty step, sneak attack, uncanny dodge, evasion, short sword, short bow, crossbow (crossbow expert).
- Ate the thrown underwear ("Half-orc things").
- **Greatest fear: two bees at a time.** He was briefly allergic, then became immune: "Bee-Man."
- **His Corgi Rolando** was his emotional-support animal until a stray bolt scared him off.
- Fought to buy Mama a birthday present (jewelry, per Dried Blood).
- Killed by Basil's nat-20 kick.
