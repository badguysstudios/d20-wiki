## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 3/059 - S3E11 — Blood Feud 1 — Havoc v Isha Inverraseth|S3E11 — Blood Feud 1 — Havoc v Isha Inverraseth]]
(Connie Chang). A Lycan (werewolf) and **beast-hide shifter**; in wolf form a **black dire wolf** with gold eyes when raging. **New alpha of the Kingslayer clan.**
- **Gear and powers:**
  - The 60-lb maul (gnarled steel head, jeweled pommel).
  - Bear-spirit rage (resists all damage but psychic), reckless attacks and a luck point.
  - Her rage manifests as a harmless pillar of primal light and a soundless wind around her.
  - Has an eagle spirit.
- **Lore:**
  - Arrived in the Void while bathing in a lake in **the Whispering Woods**.
  - She has been to Disney World and loved it.
  - **Childhood:** Isha's protector. She beat off bullies with a stick and gave Isha her first undercut.
  - **The night:** after wolves slaughtered their village, the wolves sent her to hunt and kill Isha. She tore Isha "within an inch of" her life and left her alive.
  - She believes the pack attacked because vampires were driving them from their hunting grounds.
  - Calls the Grandmaster and the Arbiter "heretics" / "pretender to a god."
- **Acts and defiance:**
  - Refused to strike first and refused the arena's "game."
  - **The first champion to attack the Box of Chaos**, and Gerald burned her for it.
  - At the end she cried and swung to kill, choosing "let's die together."
