First appears in [[Episodes/Skypiercer (Prequel)/001 - Skypiercer Part 1|Skypiercer Part 1]]. Appears in 2 episodes.

## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Skypiercer (Prequel)/001 - Skypiercer Part 1|Skypiercer Part 1]]
One of the richest men in Cor'thun; merchant guild leader; name on many buildings, statues of him; "charity" benefactor; made his fortune off the southern district's workers; said to hold himself above the emperor; rumored to be behind a mine cave-in outside the city caused by cost-cutting (2:23:16–2:24:24).

### [[Episodes/Skypiercer (Prequel)/002 - Skypiercer Part 2|Skypiercer Part 2]]
Head of House Plintos, leader of the Merchants Guild. Died **six years** before this episode, stabbed by Throm's mother on Throm's orders, because he wanted to give his wealth away to the people (1:45:58). His death was hidden by the whole family.
