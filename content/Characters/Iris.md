## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 6/117 - S6E1 — Murder at the Dread Fortress (TTRPG Murder Mystery)|S6E1 — Murder at the Dread Fortress (TTRPG Murder Mystery)]]
. A dead bride. She wears her own torn wedding gown, has reddish hair and stitches all over, carries a weathered bouquet, and her limbs and eyes come loose.
- Recently dead. Wine leaks from **stab holes in her stomach** ("I was sad").
- **Both eyes are now wine-stained red.**
- She stitches herself with flesh and odd strands, including black thread.
- **She and her husband were stabbed and killed by the Arbiter on their wedding night, at a feast.** The venue had been double-booked for a Deathmatch.
- **She murdered Thea with a knife at the banquet to get vengeance**, then **blew up her resurrected husband Tony with a rocket launcher**. "I've never felt more alive." The Grandmaster praised her energy.
- She says she's there "to eventually compete."
