## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 7/134 - S7E6 — Bone Force — Jerry Bonesman v TD|S7E6 — Bone Force — Jerry Bonesman v TD]]
(Kyle). "Bones Man, not Bones Min."
- **Who he is:**
  - A skeleton in glowing purple and dark cobalt robes with leather gauntlets. Ossified horns; a necrotic mist of tiny grasping hands follows him.
  - No eyes, no glowing orbs, no brain ("all bones, no brain"). Wisdom −1.
  - A "punch skeleton" pugilist (moxie, haymaker, fisticuffs, brace, Dread Hand) with "black magic"/eldritch invocations: Evil Eye, Armor of Agathys, Otherworldly Walk, Misty Step, Bane, Form of Dread, Frightful Avatar.
- **His cause:** wants revenge on adventurers who kill level-one skeletons; "too many of my skeleton brothers are dead." He keeps a bag of things collected from low-level adventurers.
- **His gods:** channels "**the Dreadlord / bone lord Bonar**" and, in this fight, Seavold.
- **In this episode:**
  - Sacrificed his shattered wrist and hand to the Box of Chaos and got a **wand of polymorph (seven charges)** made from a large animal femur.
  - Turned Thea into a T-Rex. Became "the Great Bones Man."
  - Offered to be Seavold's knight protecting all undead and was rejected.
  - Realized "have I become the thing I was fighting against?" He passed the torch to TD and died as dust.
