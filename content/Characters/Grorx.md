## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 1/017 - S1E8 — Fireballs — Grorx v Lord Xiren|S1E8 — Fireballs — Grorx v Lord Xiren]]
(TimothiustheGr8). A small **goblin** in **heavy plate** with a **shield** and **longsword**.
- Abilities: casts as a **4th-level warlock** (Armor of Agathys, eldritch blast, misty step, shield) and uses **Branding Smite** and **Shield Master**. Throws javelins.
- **Claims greatness over goblin, troll, orc and elven kind, but "not Dragon Kind."** Sets standards low so he can rise above them; "the greatest at running away."
- Money: tried to buy the Grandmaster's crown on credit (Omnicard, Orcish Express). Resells things. Holds many credit cards and throws them away when maxed. "I don't know math."
- **Lactose intolerant.**
- **Goblin childhood: barking dogs and people throwing rocks at him.**
- **His father designed the locking mechanism of the door in Kabehe's Temple.**
- Claims he "may or may not have slain seven or eight of my siblings… at home, so it doesn't count." His mother has a dreadful glare.
- **Used his contract as a napkin.** Without wings ("I gotta fire that guy," of his manager).
- Moves with a bow-legged, spider-like waddle that intimidated Xiren.
- Hit-and-run style; his "patent move" per the crowd.
- **Ends fused with Lord Xiren in one two-headed body.**
