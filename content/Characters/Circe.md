## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/041 - Coriander Society - Fate of the Faithful Part 2 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society - Fate of the Faithful Part 2 (A Crossover)]]
(GM). "The most nefarious inventor in the city of Beacon."
- Gear: ray gun of amazing transformation/transmogrification (patent pending), stupendous size-changing ray, gorgeous goggles of all seeing (truesight), baffling blouse of the elements (fire-proof), jet shoes.
- Hunting Kenny for the Arbiter's side.
- Now under Asena's lose-your-action curse.

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/042 - Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover|Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover]]
(GM voice). From **Beacon**, a world where villains need gimmicks.
- **Gear:** a five-mode ray gun (shrink/grow, frigid freezing, "sizzling spicy heat," fire, etherealizing); flying boots; an elemental-immunity blouse; goggles that see through illusion and magical darkness.
- **Allegiance:** joined the Arbiter to advance her villain career and found it's "for life." Ralzamon tried to fry her brain for disloyalty. Has now switched sides in exchange for protection and "get me off world."
- Undecided on her "school of villainy."

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/043 - Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 4 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 4 (A Crossover)]]
(GM). "The master of magitech/technomagic."
- **Ray gun that shrinks and enlarges ("bear expansion device")**, flying boots/shoes, **goggles that see invisibility**.
- Saw a Shadowlands vision and dismissed it with data.
- Kept getting stuck in the portcullis.

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/044 - Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 5 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 5 (A Crossover)]]
(DM).
- Flying boots, and the **Ray Gun of Amazing Transformation** with many modes: freeze/cold, the stupendous size-changing ray, the Dapper Density Altering Ray (etherealizing, partly disintegrating), and the bodacious beastomorph ray (chicken).
- Wants a report on cryo-freezing and "tissue samples." Leaves the Void with John and Asena.
