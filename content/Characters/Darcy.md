## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 4/082 - S4E4 — Death Dealers — The Scourge v Bone Daddy|S4E4 — Death Dealers — The Scourge v Bone Daddy]]
- A skeleton with a few blonde hairs, one green eye and a lipless smile.
- She elbow-dropped through her siblings to form a giant skeleton, which became Bone Daddy's bone mech.
- She stabbed out her own only good eye on a nat 1, wept a single tear from her "void eye," and carried Bone Daddy to his death.

### [[Episodes/Season 5/108 - S5E10 — BoneBound Brawl — King Bofus v Bone Daddy (and Darcy)|S5E10 — BoneBound Brawl — King Bofus v Bone Daddy (and Darcy)]]
Bone Daddy's skeleton partner, "my true love." The relationship is unlabeled: not familiar, minion or mother, sometimes "mommy." She has long-fingered bone claws and two thumbs, and wears a three-pom-pom hat from Thea. Her bones can scatter and reassemble. She becomes the bone mech and nods "it's okay" at the end.

### [[Episodes/Season 5/111 - S5E13 — Bone Rust — Ruby Rust v Bone Daddy|S5E13 — Bone Rust — Ruby Rust v Bone Daddy]]
Bone Daddy's skeleton companion ("there's not a bad bone in her body," "great hip bones").
- Grapples, throws a femur, catches him on the ceiling, feeding-frenzies, and forms his death armor.
- Ruby called her "nothing more than a table."
- **A single finger bone of hers escaped the tavern unnoticed and survives.**
