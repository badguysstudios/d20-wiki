## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/024 - S2 Ep1, Fate of the Arbiter (Fate of the Arbiter Part 1)|S2 Ep1, Fate of the Arbiter (Fate of the Arbiter Part 1)]]

- A werebear who prefers bear form, with a one-eyed Goliath human form. Commander of the Eastern Outpost.
- Talkative, sarcastic, a self-described smasher, not a strategist. Bad at housekeeping.
- Infected as a boy when werebears destroyed his village. **The Arbiter avenged his family, and later appointed him to the outpost for the Grandmaster.**
- Gave out the healing pearls and offered the Box of Chaos.
- **Sided with the party over the Arbiter and was disintegrated.** His ash joined the Arbiter's vortex. Possibly a "Toddy" in his past.

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/025 - S2E6 — FoTA - Sands of the Arena (Fate of the Arbiter, Part 2)|S2E6 — FoTA - Sands of the Arena (Fate of the Arbiter, Part 2)]]
(mentioned). Commander of the eastern outpost. Killed by the Arbiter (by an obliterate spell, per the players). He disliked his humanoid form and "was a talker." Considered the Arbiter "like a father."

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/041 - Coriander Society - Fate of the Faithful Part 2 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society - Fate of the Faithful Part 2 (A Crossover)]]
Lyrus's late father. He guarded the Box of Chaos at the eastern outpost for many years until his death. Strict; "never saw the Arbiter's betrayal." The Seer: "a great man." The Arbiter is described as taller than Orin.
