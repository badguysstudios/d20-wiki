## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 7/130 - S7E2 — Under the Oil Tree — Big Business v Bog Boy|S7E2 — Under the Oil Tree — Big Business v Bog Boy]]
(Peter). A gnarled old snapping-turtle **Tortle, a Way of the Elements monk**.
- **Appearance:** spiked, spray-painted shell full of refuse and fishing net; apron made from a Rammstein band shirt; moss and bugs.
- **Lore:**
  - Calls himself "Chico del Panto" in Spanish.
  - Claims squatter's rights on the swamp. Has never left the swamp or seen a bull, "only an upturned firebird."
  - Loves the Lord of the Rings books. Sits "like Winnie the Pooh."
  - **Was a four-year-old human boy before he was turned into a turtle ("toad")**; the player adds "a teenage mutant ninja turtle… in Florida."
  - **Promised the swamp witch Griselda to help turn everyone into frogs** (fewer resources, "everybody gets smaller"), "the price for her [Clementine's] fin."
- **In this episode:**
  - Won the Deathmatch.
  - Proposed to Clementine with a nut from Big Business.
  - Was fused with Big Business's remains by Griselda, becoming taller and stronger with a second consciousness. "Now half robot, half turtle" (Dried Blood).
