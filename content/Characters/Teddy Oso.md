## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 5/106 - S5E8 — Graveyard Grudge — Granny Edith v Teddy Oso|S5E8 — Graveyard Grudge — Granny Edith v Teddy Oso]]
(Chunk / PerceptionStudio). "Mr. Teddy Oso" (Jr. "the third" or "the second"; his father named him).
- **Background:**
  - A survivor of the zombie apocalypse who has killed "12,000 maybe" zombies (canon range 7–12,000).
  - He **shot his own zombified grandma in the face** and carried her head as a shrunken-head memento, until he sacrificed it to the Box.
- **Kit:**
  - A shotgun he calls "17"-round; he uses the stock as a golf club and the barrel to drink from. "Not an expert on guns."
  - A little axe, lost stuck in Granny's rolling pin.
  - **Endless sugar-free vegan energy drinks.** His blood is "like 90% energy drink."
  - Takes blood pressure medication.
- **Signature move:** "Leave Him in the Dust." A motorcycle appears from nowhere.
- **Weakness:** can't refuse delicious-looking food (the Granny Smith apple).
- Has "beef" with Thea. Enjoyed beating up an old person.
- **In this episode:** died in the lava with a thumbs-up.

### [[Episodes/Season 5/113 - S5E15 — Cosmic Collision — Teddy Oso v Ragu|S5E15 — Cosmic Collision — Teddy Oso v Ragu]]
. Apocalypse survivor and zombie fighter with a shotgun and an axe (blurb).
- **His zombie count rose from 12,000 ("last time") to "the 30,000 range," and Thea counts this kill as 30,001.**
- **Bitten on the right knee by a zombie** (bandaged). **He eats a tiny raw piece of every zombie he kills to build immunity**: about 40–50 people's worth, from the thumb muscle, butt cheeks or shoulder.
- Rides a motorcycle. Girlfriends Susie and Betty, one of them a zombie. Says **not all zombies are violent; some just want to be loved.**
- **His wife is a zombie** ("she is now").
- Never closes his eyes when hit. Signature moves: the casual hip pose, the drum-major shotgun flip, the golf-swing uppercut.
- Uncle "Nipple" ("Hairy nipple was my uncle").
- He used his heroic surge to learn an extra action from "a little book."
- Keeps the **chinchilla** in his jacket pocket. **Won the 1998 Chevy Impala.** Concessions sell **Teddy Oso shotguns**.
- In Dried Blood he says he lost last time "to an old lady" and reads The Secret.
