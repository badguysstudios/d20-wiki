## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 5/101 - S5E3, Eldritch Fangs - Ruby Rust v Robbie Clark and the Hunger|S5E3, Eldritch Fangs - Ruby Rust v Robbie Clark and the Hunger]]
(Cinder / Cinderblock Sally).
- A boy from a small town who said goodbye to his parents.
- The Lord of Fate claims Robbie ran off at 10 and is a teenager. Robbie says he doesn't understand object permanence and has trouble remembering things "because I am a child."
- Gave up 10 years of his life and some of his organs to the box.
- Emerged clean and unharmed at the end. Speaks in "Oh boy" and "G. Willikers" style.

### [[Episodes/Season 5/105 - S5E7 — Ducks of Death — Robbie Clark & the Hunger v Bob the Barbarian|S5E7 — Ducks of Death — Robbie Clark & the Hunger v Bob the Barbarian]]
(Cinderblock Sally). A small child "adventurer and hero of the land" bonded to the Hunger through a backpack he has never taken off.
- His memory goes back only "about yesterday" and is "full of blank spots." He remembers waking up next to a dead snake (his previous match).
- He doesn't know what a birthday is, whether he has parents, or what an alien is. He stopped going to school "when I was too young."
- **Gave his birthday memories to the Box of Chaos and received the Shield of the Golden King.** Thea gave him New Balance shoes.
- His moves are the child punch and child kick.
- **Freed from the Hunger at the end; left for Florida with Bob on a fan boat.** His jeans became shorts.
