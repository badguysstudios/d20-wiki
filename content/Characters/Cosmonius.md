## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 1/020 - S1E11 — Gift Horse — Cosmonius v El Hada Rocha|S1E11 — Gift Horse — Cosmonius v El Hada Rocha]]
(RobertHartleyGM). A large chestnut centaur from **the Feywild**.
- **Look:** olive-skinned strongman torso; long Fabio-like hair; handlebar moustache and beard; leather fist wraps. By the end, his hair and moustache are singed and there is chocolate in the moustache.
- **Titles:** "the Galloping Grappler," "the Harrowing Hooves," "Chopsbuster" (a named punch, then a title), "Piñata King."
- **Abilities:** a monk. Uses Step of the Wind, Flurry of Blows, Stunning Strike, Focused Aim, Deflect Missiles, slow fall, and Hand of Harm/Healing ("a bit of a healer"). Fey-touched (Bless, Misty Step, invoked by bowing to the fairy queens of the Seelie Court). Savage Attacker.
- **Habits:** says his own name in case someone is scrying him; finger guns; dressage trot.
- Has "fought your kind [fairies] back at home multiple times."
- Grandmothers adore him, until they get jealous.
- Wore the piñata's donkey head; kept two full-size candy bars in his hand wraps.
- **Died** to El Hada Rocha's final Hellish Rebuke.

### [[Episodes/Battle for the Crown/047 - S2 Ep19, Battle for the Crown Round 2 - Team Grandmaster (Cosmonius & Locket) v Team Arbiter (Silky Slim & …|S2 Ep19, Battle for the Crown Round 2 - Team Grandmaster (Cosmonius & Locket) v Team Arbiter (Silky Slim & …]]
(RobertHartleyGM).
- **Who he is:** a centaur monk from the Feywild with a chestnut horse body, topless, with Fabio hair and a much-admired mustache. He assumes someone is always scrying on him and finger-guns nobody.
- **Previous fight (his own account):** he beat "a bloody grandma hitting me with a handbag" and "the old fairy folk" who cast Ashardalon's Stride. They couldn't hit him with seven attacks. He kept a chocolate bar from that fight's piñata.
- **Abilities seen:**
  - Fey-touched Bless and Misty Step, leaving pixie dust trails.
  - Evasion, Slow Fall, running up vertical walls.
  - Hand of Harm and Hand of Healing (punching heals into an ally).
  - Stunning Strike; Sentinel opportunity attacks.
  - "Equine build" makes climbing hard for him.
- **Self-given names:** the galloping grappler, the chops buster, the piñata king, the harrowing hooves, the Kraken Combatant ("menu list of names").
- **In this episode:**
  - Renamed Slim "Slippery Slim."
  - Dropped the Baron into the pit twice and landed on him from 50 ft.
  - Fought the Kraken.
  - Held the dying Baron's hand and called "Enough bloodshed."
  - Survived and won.
- Ghost Slim says his roots show a bad dye job; he begged him not to tell anyone.
