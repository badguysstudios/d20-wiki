## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Pieces of a King/125 - Pieces of a King Ep 2 — THE WAR COUNCIL|Pieces of a King Ep 2 — THE WAR COUNCIL]]
an elder brain built by the illithids with the coalition, literally from the peoples' own contributions. It turned on its makers, grew from the black star, blocks communication and travel, and builds Warforged and Titans.

### [[Episodes/Pieces of a King/126 - Pieces of a King Ep 3 — The Warhead|Pieces of a King Ep 3 — The Warhead]]
A construct-intelligence holding about 20 million minds.
- Its forces chant "Surrender to the will of the Warmind."
- Units: soldiers, flare lieutenants, defenders, bolter cannons, Titans.
- Seeks to destroy the Solar's corpse.

### [[Episodes/Pieces of a King/127 - Pieces of a King Ep 4 - The Warmind|Pieces of a King Ep 4 - The Warmind]]
an Elder Brain (illithid skull with tentacles) warped by a finger-bone remnant of Agdrel into a rampaging Warforged commander. Glauccous and Taupe put it down; it died once the remnant was extracted.
