## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 5/100 - S5E2 — Identity Crisis — Kopy Katt v Professor Alonzo Quixote Cervantes|S5E2 — Identity Crisis — Kopy Katt v Professor Alonzo Quixote Cervantes]]
. Owner and bartender of Emmery's Tavern.
- Brews the number two ale, smoked over sheep dung; it's the only drink.
- Wipes the same worn, dented spot all the time. Has an extremely long arm.
- Needs the money. Saved the frozen chinchilla in his armpit.
- **A changeling**: he took the forms of Kopy Katt, the Lord of Fate and Mama Cervantes, and delivered the killing blow.

### [[Episodes/Season 6/122 - S6 Ep6 — The Hunt for 2 - Part 1 — Team C (Frigidum, Bob the Barbarian, the Duke) v Billy|S6 Ep6 — The Hunt for #2 - Part 1 — Team C (Frigidum, Bob the Barbarian, the Duke) v Billy]]
. Keeper of **Emmery's Tavern**; he endlessly wipes the bar.
- **Brews his patented number two ale using sheep dung to dry the grains** ("about mouthfeel").
- He is the Dread Fortress's favorite distributor and shipped 50 barrels to the Fortress.
- Offered his reserve in exchange for solving the attacks. Keeps a lost-and-found of Black and Milds.
