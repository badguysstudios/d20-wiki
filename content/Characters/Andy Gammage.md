## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 7/129 - S7E1 — A Soup Served Cold — DeeDrah v Gazpacho Man Andy Gammage|S7E1 — A Soup Served Cold — DeeDrah v Gazpacho Man Andy Gammage]]
(Jay Foster). Also answers to Andrew and Andy.
- **Who he is:**
  - **Intercontinental Cold Soups Champion.**
  - Has been **dead for 20 years**, killed by **Mean Gene, his best friend, who literally stabbed him in the back over the gazpacho pot**. He fights to **win back his full mortality**, take on Mean Gene for the world title and become **the Gazpacho King**.
  - Wants to make **his Miss Elizabeth** "Queen Elizabeth."
- **Body and habits:**
  - **Missing toes from "a cold soup accident."** Sensitive about it.
  - Has **skin pockets** in his biceps, elbow crease and vest.
  - Hasn't showered in "probably 30 years"; calls the stink his "heavenly aura."
  - Trains and makes soup in "the danger room." Hates hot soup. Catchphrase "Oh yeah."
- **Powers:**
  - Rage (red face, steam from the ears). Known "in some of the clubs around LA" as a bear, so he resists fire while raging.
  - Cutting Words "from my bard side." **Swallow Magic** (ate an Eldritch Blast in two gulps; called a first in the arena).
  - **A heavenly aura with big golden wings.** Finger guns.
- **Weapons and gear:** steel chair (now dented), barbed-wire two-by-four, maul, soup ladle.
- **This episode:**
  - **Sacrificed his Intercontinental Championship belt to the Box of Chaos** for the **sleeveless denim jean jacket (+3 AC)**.
  - Swallowed Gerald's fingers. Hit himself in the taint with his own chair.
  - The Arbiter likes him ("You can stay").
  - Won.
