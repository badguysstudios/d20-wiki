**Thomas the Goblin** is one of the small, unsung workers who keep the Deathmatches running. He is the goblin who quietly binned the Arbiter's party invitation, and later the trainee healer who was trusted with a job he could not possibly do. He is mentioned far more than he speaks, but every story about him makes the Void a little stranger and a little funnier.

## Story

### Magic Duel

Thomas first came up in [[Episodes/Season 3/061 - S3E14 — Magic Duel — Talbot Cage v Seth Kaiser|S3E14 — Magic Duel — Talbot Cage v Seth Kaiser]], the night [[Talbot Cage]] faced [[Seth Kaiser]]. The occasion was "the masquerade pre-party party, obviously," and [[The Arbiter|the Arbiter]] was put out to discover he had never received an invitation. [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|The Grandmaster]] asked Thomas, who seemed to be in charge of sending the invitations, whether he had sent it.

The truth came out later. Thomas had thrown the Arbiter's invitation in the trash. The Grandmaster delivered the news with no attempt to soften it: Thomas loves everyone, she explained, which made his one exception all the more pointed.

> "Turns out Thomas doesn't like you… You should take it personally, because Thomas loves everyone." (the Grandmaster)

### Bone Rust

Thomas turned up again during the Season of Fate, in [[Episodes/Season 5/111 - S5E13 — Bone Rust — Ruby Rust v Bone Daddy|S5E13 — Bone Rust — Ruby Rust v Bone Daddy]], where [[Ruby Rust]] fought [[Bone Daddy]]. By then he had a new career. [[Thea]] admitted that she hadn't overseen a round of healing herself and had let Thomas the Goblin handle it instead. He was "working on his certification," she said, though he had not actually finished it.

The Lord of Fate, which was the Arbiter's title in that timeline, was not impressed. He pointed out that Thomas cannot read or write, and asked what on earth made Thea think he could put together a snake. Thea defended her trainee on the grounds that "he's seen straight lines before." The Lord of Fate's answer: "He eats paint chips."

Later in the same night, Thea passed along another piece of Thomas lore. According to Thomas, he keeps eggs in his "butt pocket," and his body runs so hot that a raw egg goes in at breakfast and comes out cooked by lunch. The table quickly added a mock warning not to try this at home, citing salmonella and the finer points of sous vide.

## Personality and Abilities

Thomas is, by the Grandmaster's account, a warm-hearted goblin who loves everyone, with one firm exception. He is willing to take on responsibility, whether that is sending invitations or patching up wounded champions, even when his qualifications are unclear or plainly absent. He cannot read or write and he eats paint chips, but he has seen straight lines before.

His one claimed physical gift is a body temperature high enough to cook an egg over a morning, which he puts to practical use with his butt pocket. Nobody has confirmed this. Nobody has seemed eager to.

## Relationships

- [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer]]: He works for her, apparently handling invitations. She seemed to enjoy telling the Arbiter what Thomas thinks of him.
- [[The Arbiter]]: The only person Thomas doesn't love. He threw the Arbiter's party invitation in the trash. In the Season of Fate, the Lord of Fate openly doubted Thomas's fitness as a healer.
- [[Thea]]: His supervisor in healing during the Season of Fate. She let him handle a round of healing before his certification was finished, and she passes along his stories.
