## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 1/014 - S1E5, The 2v2 - Chuck Solid Gold Dragon Biff & Richardo v Steely Dan & Thass'Lok|S1E5, The 2v2 - Chuck Solid Gold Dragon Biff & Richardo v Steely Dan & Thass'Lok]]
("Ghost in the Bay," per the GM; Forgeling). A lizardfolk with rapier, shield, daggers, Hunter's Mark, Colossus Slayer, Uncanny Dodge, Zephyr Strike, Fog Cloud and fancy footwork. Mostly naked.
- **Serves "the mistress," the Lady of the Ocean.** He drowns people in "the black water bays."
- Saw the gears as the warforged's domain and **betrayed his teammate from the first turn.** Later felt betrayed by everyone.
- Died saying "Lady of the Ocean, take me."
