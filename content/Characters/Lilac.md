## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 3/070 - S3E17, Season 3 Finale - Soot Mustam v Huey Leatherface|S3E17, Season 3 Finale - Soot Mustam v Huey Leatherface]]
The Grandmaster's **pet flying dragon**, guardian of the treasure room. She doesn't actually smell of lilacs, and her tongue sticks out.
- The Arbiter mind-controlled her into flooding the room with fire.
- She accepted Huey's elixir, which made her faster and the room hotter.
