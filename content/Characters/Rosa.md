## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 2/032 - S2E9 — Bees — Rosa v Veronica|S2E9 — Bees — Rosa v Veronica]]
(guest; see player-credit question)
- **Appearance:** a halfling, "two and a half feet tall," with long pink hair, a sunny disposition, an **eye patch** she passes off as a battle wound, and very tight clothing. She has "only one good eye."
- **Class:** a rogue who can't do magic.
- **Gear:** daggers (one poisoned and worn as a stinger "off my butt"), a short sword, a rapier, a sling or "bee cannon," a rope, an oil flask and a tinderbox.
- **Bees:**
  - Wears a fluffy, fluorescent **bee costume**.
  - Summons bees, a swarm "in a ball," and carries poison "in my special place."
  - Rode a polymorph steed she believed was a bee, **Steve**.
  - The zombie bees like her.
- **Items:** won the Ioun Stone of Absorption and the Deck of Many Things from the Box of Chaos, and **lost both to the Talons card**.
- **Personality:** an optimist ("deck half full").
- **Ambition:** asked to become the Arbiter and was refused.
- **Outcome:** hired as the Grandmaster's bee farmer.
