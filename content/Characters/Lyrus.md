## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/025 - S2E6 — FoTA - Sands of the Arena (Fate of the Arbiter, Part 2)|S2E6 — FoTA - Sands of the Arena (Fate of the Arbiter, Part 2)]]
. **Orin's son.**
- A werebear in black plate, wielding **two Void swords of "solidified gloom."** The swords cut portals in reality, create singularities, and shatter portals like mirrors.
- **True form: a Goliath**, bald, 7 ft, grey skin, tiger-stripe tattoos.
- Heard the Arbiter confess and vowed to take his head "someday."
- Saved the trio, and dove into the void to save Locket. Exhausted by the magic.
- Leads a small band at a covert raid hideout in a blacksmith shop; **"I guess you would call us the Faithful."**
- Phrases: "the cut of your jib," "two in the hand, one in the pantry."

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/040 - Coriander Society, Fate of the Faithful Part 1 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society, Fate of the Faithful Part 1 (A Crossover)]]

- **Appearance:** a 7–8 ft white-furred **werebear** in grey and black plate armor over furs.
- **Weapons:** two light-swallowing black **void swords**. A cut can open a singularity that sucks the victim in, or a portal that sends limbs or halves elsewhere.
- **Portals:** slices portals in space to dodge, teleport, dive through enemies' chests, and let allies attack through them.
  - The sword that lets an ally attack through a portal is a **greater silver sword** that can cut an astral silver cord on a crit.
  - Artificer-infused teleport boots.
- **Other powers:** paladin powers (smites, lay on hands, an aura of protection); green flame blade that burns with black fire.
- **Family and past:** son of **Orin**. The Arbiter saved Orin as a child when his village burned, and later betrayed and murdered him.
- **The rebels:** leads Orin's former men from **the Eastern Outpost**, who deserted the Grandmaster's forces to serve her "from the shadows." Many are wanted for treason.
- **Personality:** calls himself "servant of the Grandmaster" ("in a way"). Old-fashioned about looting the dead. Rambles a little "like an old man". "Not a tactician… a soldier." His inner struggle is living up to his father's legacy.
- **In this episode:** killed Hathim by decapitating him through a portal.

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/041 - Coriander Society - Fate of the Faithful Part 2 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society - Fate of the Faithful Part 2 (A Crossover)]]
. A leader of the Faithful, newly in leadership.
- Daily life: sleeps on his warped work table; eats Frank's food daily, "dead inside."
- Background:
  - Wanted to be a cartographer; his strict father Orin made him a soldier. He draws the Faithful's maps himself.
  - Not a planner: "kick down the door and portal in."
  - A tinkerer who crafted his own metal gauntlets and boots.
- Abilities and gear:
  - Void swords that cut portals.
  - Divine sense (shows as void cracks through the stones), smites, green-flame blade, misty step, stone's endurance.
  - **Magic Missile rune tattoo on his palm from John.**
  - **Now carries the Ogre Revolver**, and accidentally shot someone through a wall.
- Prophecy: "You will fight… again and again. Until you are amongst the sand." The Seer says he is not a great man, "Not yet."
- Accepted the Glory of the Grandmaster duel against Ganyu Sinmal.

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/042 - Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover|Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover]]
. A **"Werebearer"** (bear-folk) Faithful member with **Void Swords** of ethereal blackness.
- Can absorb their void/chaos energy to go "even further beyond": black eyes and black chaos flames.
- Abilities shown: smites, green-flame blade, Lay on Hands, Misty Step, Vortex Warp (an ethereal one-person portal), an Artificer "repulsion" blast, Stone's Endurance, teleporting boots, a +1 aura.
- Defeated Ganyu Sinmal from inside its mouth.
- Serves the Grandmaster and offered Circe redemption "to serve the Grandmaster in her glory." Gives Kenny orders.
- His glued boot "was my father's." Thinks he's funny (the chair joke). Claimed he couldn't swim, then said he was kidding.

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/043 - Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 4 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 4 (A Crossover)]]
. A **Goliath who is also a werebear**, and stays in bear form "all the time now" in memory and honor of his father.
- **Background:**
  - His father **Orin** was turned as a child in an attack on his village that killed his family. **The Arbiter saved Orin from that attack.**
  - **The Arbiter later killed Orin**; Lyrus heard of the death only secondhand.
- **Role:** a member of the Faithful. He took part in the storming of the eastern stronghold when the Faithful believed the Arbiter had been kidnapped.
- **Powers and gear:**
  - **Opens portals by stabbing his two swords (void swords) into the air** and ripping reality. Returning somewhere is easier than going somewhere unseen.
  - Green-flame blade, divine smite, Hunter's Mark, misty step, Stone's Endurance.
  - Resistant to psychic damage. Level 12.
- **In this episode:** made giant by Circe and used as a bridge. Duelled and killed the Draconic Death Knight. Last seen engulfed by the hellfire orb.
- Funded by chat member "Jack" (600 gp, 400 ep, then another 1,000 gp).

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/044 - Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 5 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 5 (A Crossover)]]
. A member of the Faithful, described by the DM as a "werebear Goliath warrior."
- Normally about 9–10 ft tall (Large). Circe made him gargantuan, then shrank him back.
- Driven by **his father's legacy** and his fight against the Arbiter.
- Gear and powers:
  - Void Swords and green flame blade.
  - **Thunder gauntlets** (artificer-made).
  - Smites, lay on hands, Vow of Enmity, Stone's Endurance, guardian armor.
  - Misty step / void step, and **Vortex Warp** (rips a teleport portal).
- Personality and lore:
  - Rich ("I'm loaded"). Has "a little bit of a temper. I'm working on it."
  - Scared of heights. Firearms are "not my thing."
- In this episode:
  - Survives a Hellball and a Soul Tear, and beats down a Bogatyr mecha.
  - Warps the frozen Vargas in front of the shadow monster.
  - Keeps the (replica) ewer for the Seer.
  - Gives John 400 electrum. Says the Faithful aim "to reach the Grandmaster."
