## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/077 - Into the Mirror Session 4 (YouTube parts 12–13) - Royal Audience and Otherside|Into the Mirror Session 4 (YouTube parts 12–13) - Royal Audience and Otherside]]
, queen of the city of the dead.
- Older, silver-white hair, poised and healthy-looking. Her crown is made of glowing jade-green soul gems.
- **Her reflection matches her exactly.**
- Welcomed Cerys into the city hundreds of years ago.
- Wants to leave the plane and "deliver my people back to the living." Sends the party for the Orrery of Dreams.
- Laws are unwritten. Troublemakers are made outlanders and wander the sands forever.
- Elianna senses she is truthful but holding things back.
