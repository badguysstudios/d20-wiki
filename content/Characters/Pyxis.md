**Pyxis** was a huge, bulky mammoth in armor who carried a war hammer and a grudge, and was unfailingly polite about both. He arrived in [[The Void]] for the [[Episodes/Season 6/123 - × Wounded Warrior Project, Capture the Flag charity crossover (title not in metadata)|Wounded Warrior Project Capture the Flag crossover]] looking for the three people who had killed his loved ones. He found them all in the same room. He spent the match cleaning up after a duck, quietly abandoning that duck, and finally calling down the moon itself to finish his quest with his dying breath.

## Story

Pyxis stepped into a food-laden hall of [[The Dread Fortress]] with a warm "Oh, hello there, buddy," and won over [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] at once; she called him "tender and sweet." Then he explained why he had come. He was looking for whoever killed his loved ones: "a little fairy, a strange centaur person, and a cobra of some kind." Across the room stood [[Beans the Terrible]], a pixie, [[Grog the Malodorous]], who was part horse, and [[Solid]], a cobra-like Yuan Ti. "What are the odds?" said the Grandmaster. "I think you're exactly where you're supposed to be." Pyxis answered by smacking his war hammer down on the table, and everything on it jumped. At 9'5" he described himself as "a little small for my height." His siblings had been taller: "They were a little taller than me, but not so much anymore."

The match opened as a team gauntlet, and Pyxis was put with [[Perry]], who promptly scurried up onto his shoulder, and [[Dave the Doom Duck]]. In the kitchen, where the teams had to clear every scrap of food, Pyxis lay down on the table and told Perry to shovel it all onto him ("These flavors are so delectable"). Then he turned himself invisible so the food vanished along with him. While Grog's team was busy taunting them through the wall, Pyxis caught Solid's voice over an intercom and froze. "That sounds like the voice I heard on a very horrible day long ago." In the room of sleeping [[Chinchillas]], Dave left spiteful droppings in front of every bed. The invisible Pyxis followed behind and wiped them up, because he felt bad for the chinchillas. "Oh, Dave's gonna remember that," the narration warned. At the alchemy tables he took meticulous notes while Perry solved the vial puzzle. He did the combination himself while invisible, so Dave couldn't see, and slipped through the portal, leaving the duck alone with the glassware: "You didn't see me… I'm right behind you, buddy."

The Acolytes' cash then summoned a mini-boss, and it turned out Pyxis had paid for it himself. "Oh, I would never do such an evil thing," he protested. [[The Arbiter]] pointed out that he didn't seem to understand the mini-boss would fight both teams. [[Thea]] was transformed into a giant purple worm, the Abyss Devourer, and went after the other team first. Pyxis suggested Dave lock them in with a ring of fire, and was delighted when the duck obliged: "Oh, Ducky, you're so good now. I like him." Beans answered in kind, and Pyxis got caught in the return flames.

When the gauntlet collapsed into a last-one-standing free-for-all, Pyxis promised, "Oh, I plan on living, don't you worry." He took to the air on steps of night and brought down a column of moonlight on his enemies ("Moonbeam!"), declaring afterward, "I'm still pretty." Beans answered with a doubled blast of starlight that nearly finished him. Pyxis then flew down, grabbed Solid by the head, poured necrotic magic into him in midair and dropped him thirty feet. Solid floated gently down. Then Dave turned his rear toward the sky and launched a flaming, lava-like fireball at Pyxis ("You dirty duck!… Don't do it!"), and Pyxis didn't dodge it. As it exploded, he prayed to **Selûne**: "Let me slay this murderer of my family as I die." The whole day turned to night. The moon guided him down onto Solid's head, and his finger poked out Solid's last good eye. The fall killed Solid outright. Solid's final words were a confession: "yeah i did kill your family that's it." Pyxis died in the same moment. The Grandmaster gave them a farewell: "Redemption found in Pyxis, the objective made complete, and now both eyes taken."

## Personality and Abilities

Pyxis was a towering armored mammoth with the manners of a kindly grandparent. He called people "dears" and "sweetie," praised his team ("Good job, everyone. I'm so proud of you all"), and scolded Dave mildly as "a naughty duck" with "a dirty mouth." Under the courtesy he had real steel. He came for vengeance, slipped away from teammates when it suited him, and didn't flinch at the deathblow. Solid referred to him once as "this robot friend."

He was a cleric of Selûne, goddess of the moon. He fought with a war hammer and with her magic: turning himself invisible, calling down moonbeams, stepping through the air on steps of night, and inflicting necrotic wounds by touch. At the very end, his goddess answered his prayer directly.

## Relationships

- [[Solid]]: The "cobra" among his family's killers, recognized by voice. The Grandmaster's farewell suggests Pyxis had taken one of Solid's eyes on that day long ago. He took the other as he killed him, and Solid confessed at the end.
- [[Beans the Terrible]]: Matched his description of the "little fairy." The Grandmaster allowed it might be them "or another three." Beans blasted Pyxis with starlight in the final fight.
- [[Grog the Malodorous]]: Matched the "strange centaur person" Pyxis was hunting.
- [[Dave the Doom Duck]]: His teammate in the gauntlet. Pyxis cleaned up after him, ditched him at the alchemy tables, and cheered his ring of fire. Dave's fireball dealt him his fatal wounds.
- [[Perry]]: His teammate. Perry rode on his shoulder and shoveled the kitchen's food onto him.
- [[Thea]]: Pyxis paid for her transformation into the Abyss Devourer.
- [[Chinchillas]]: He secretly cleaned Dave's mess away from their beds.
