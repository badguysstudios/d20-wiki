**Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer** rules [[The Void]] from [[The Dread Fortress]] and presides over every Deathmatch. Played by **Caustic Phoenix** (Bec).

## Who she is
- Retired from battle, she yearns for the days of glory and bloodshed, so she tasks [[The Arbiter]] with curating a spectacle every Sunday.
- She is of a very long-lived people. Nobody truly knows her age, and her own claims about it (such as "I am 284") are lies.
- Mostly a martial fighter with extraordinary abilities. She can use magic, including smaller-scale time magic, but the Arbiter is the true master of chaos magic.
- She wields the Grandmaster's Bow (the Oathbow) and has great wings. Her hair was blue in [[Skypiercer]] and is blonde later.
- She briefly wore an eyepatch in Season 7 after scratching her eye playing darts with [[Thea]]. There was a memo: don't bring it up.

## History
- Daughter of Faucius Golden Shield. On **the Night of the Long Shadow**, about 700 years before Season 1, her uncle Agdrel killed her father and seized power as [[The Shadow King]]. The Knights of Chaos sacrificed themselves to send young Elusia away to Cor'thun, outside the Void.
- In Cor'thun she was trained in combat by Empress Juntikar.
- In [[Skypiercer]], about 400 years before Season 1, Vythorn brought her back. With Atalantos and Kaja she defeated the Shadow King and became Grandmaster, starting the Deathmatches.
- Kaja was her love after Skypiercer, until Kaja's natural death.
- In Season 4 she was the Shadow King's captive in all but name, still running Deathmatches for him.

## Themes
Elusia's story is about death and a very long life: accepting that the people around her will die, and that this is what makes them matter. It's part of why the Deathmatches celebrate death.

## Relationships
- [[The Arbiter]]: her servant in all things, her chaos-magic expert, sometime traitor and constant annoyance. "There's no Grandmaster and Arbiter. There's the Grandmaster and then the Arbiter."
- [[Doug the Pug]]: her beloved pug.
- [[Thea]]: she supports Thea, at least more than the Arbiter does.
