## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/041 - Coriander Society - Fate of the Faithful Part 2 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society - Fate of the Faithful Part 2 (A Crossover)]]
(GM). An armored spirit-eater from Yokai Blossom (Anime 5e). Fiendish energy, violet furnace fire, burning seals. Grows when hurt. Possibly a different instance from the one the heroes knew.

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/042 - Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover|Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover]]
(GM). A towering, armored, fiery titan.
- Metal grate mouthpiece, purple belly fire, the "spirit eater gate"; grows to huge, then gargantuan.
- **A dead creature fused inside the armor, animated by Ralzamon**, who possessed it with ghostly blue eyes ("My master").
- Destroyed by Lyrus and fell into the Beast Below's grasp.
