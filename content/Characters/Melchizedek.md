## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 4/091 - S4E11 — Beatdown — Sprinkles v Nigel|S4E11 — Beatdown — Sprinkles v Nigel]]
(RRollplaying). Sprinkles' dark passenger.
- **Rage form:** demonic flame eyes, cracking and elongating bones, a dropped jaw, and knife-claws.
- **True form:** a nine-headed dragon-snake. Called "she" at 1:51:28 and 2:04:11; calls himself "a Lord of Darkness."
- **"An angel who was scorned from heaven for my transgressions against my god,"** now "a being of pure hatred and pain."
- Calls Sprinkles "my flesh puppet… the quivering whelp."
- Loathes dancing, until the Footloose dance. Turns out to be good at yodeling.
- Took over Nigel's corpse with the face-cloak. Killed by child-Nigel's bone "Oscar."
- Last thought: "hurt people hurt people."
