## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 3/054 - S3E5, Edge Lords - Captain Fable v Verryn Ruth|S3E5, Edge Lords - Captain Fable v Verryn Ruth]]
(Ascension_Matt).
- **A tiefling hexblade warlock** with hooves and **bat-like wings that fold into his back**. Calls himself "a humble, forlorn soul," "blessed by the shadow," "thirsty for glory."
- Draws power "**from the abyss, from chaos itself**"; his patrons give him power, but they're "quite poor" at controlling him.
- **Gear and powers:**
  - A shadowy greatsword pulled from the abyss.
  - Mage Armor ("armor of chaos"), Minor Illusion, Hexblade's Curse, Armor of Hexes.
  - Summon Shadowspawn (despair), Darkness, and sight through magical darkness (eyes change colour).
- **Body:** bejeweled nipple rings (gave one to Gerald). A "**MOMS ONLY**" tattoo on his chest.
- **Gained the Sentinel Shield as a hand-buffed codpiece.**
- "**This isn't my first life.**" Expects to return by chaos.
- Decapitated by Fable. **Later seen leaving the locker room carrying his head** (Dried Blood).
