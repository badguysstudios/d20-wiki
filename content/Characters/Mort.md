## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 3/053 - S3E4, Crypto Monkeys - K-Boom v Ignacio Marchade|S3E4, Crypto Monkeys - K-Boom v Ignacio Marchade]]
**A monkey with wings that Ignacio gave him** (taken by K-Boom to be a Wizard of Oz monkey; Ignacio denies it). Found in the Appalachian Mountains.
- Sacrificed to the Box of Chaos. He then hangs out with Doug on the Grandmaster's pedestal and **rolls the plush D20 for the Arbiter's end-of-round rolls**.
- Promised his own bedroom. Wept at the double death, was held up Simba-style, and flew off throwing confetti.
