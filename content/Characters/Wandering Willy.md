## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 2/035 - S2E12 — Blood Cookies — Wandering Willy v Martin Messiah Malenko|S2E12 — Blood Cookies — Wandering Willy v Martin Messiah Malenko]]
(ArcaneAdam). A druid and a self-described "man of the leaf."
- **Personality:** laid-back and easily distracted. Always friendly; calls everyone "man."
- **Gear and powers:**
  - Carries an assortment of "stoner snacks": brownies, sour gummy worms, chips, candy bars.
  - Wild shapes into a dog.
  - Spells: Call Lightning, Erupting Earth, Heal, Cure Wounds. Can go invisible (Hidden Step).
- **Items gained:** a **ghost lantern** (restless spirit inside), **dust of sneezing and choking**, and a **Deck of Many Things** from Gerald.
- **Lore and quirks:**
  - A guy named Mike once gave him something "magical."
  - He "knew a dryad once."
  - Attended Boulder Gate Community College Online and didn't graduate; a high school teacher called him a loser every day.
  - Gives Gerald throat lozenges. Named the coughing corpse Kevin. Said "Leeroy" while holding up the lantern (unclear whether that was a name for it).
  - Talks to skeletons ("you all have a hive network").
- **In this episode:** survived Martin's 7th-level Harm with a nat 20, then proposed the team-up.
