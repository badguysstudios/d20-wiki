First appears in [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/088 - Into the Mirror 15–16, Old Friends - Sky|Into the Mirror 15–16, Old Friends - Sky]]. Appears in 2 episodes.

## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/088 - Into the Mirror 15–16, Old Friends - Sky|Into the Mirror 15–16, Old Friends - Sky]]
- **Appearance:** a horned tiefling leader of outlander raiders, with a spinning, glowing glaive.
- **Magic:** casts Bane, Hellish Rebuke and Counterspell. Resistant to fire.
- **Claims:** calls himself "a steward of this place, in a way" and says "There's only one way out of there."
- **Motive:** robs travelers to feed "mouths" and mentions going "back home." Offered Toddleheim surrender.
- **Status:** barely standing at the end.

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/093 - Into the Mirror 17, The Way Out|Into the Mirror 17, The Way Out]]

- Young-looking leader of the outlanders, who call themselves "free thinkers." Wields a glaive. Commands an army that includes an Orc Raider, a wizard, dragonborn and mages.
- Black-market dealer in artifacts and soul gems. Lives in the hollow soul-gem cavern; has been there a few decades.
- **Distrusts the queen; calls sand sickness a lie and the city's walls a prison.** "Not really the book learning type."
- **Doesn't like leading**, and refuses calls to invade Mortorios.
- **The Sword's reflection shows him as a pickpocket**, always thinking about finding things.
