**The Arbiter**, also known as **Vythorn the Endless Scholar**, is [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]]'s servant in all things and the curator of every Deathmatch. Played by **Mudcat** (Drew). Mudcat also plays [[The Shadow King]].

## Who he is
- A true master of chaos magic and "master of all time and space." He opens the portals, picks (or delegates the picking of) champions, and runs the arena.
- Much older than Elusia. He served her father before the Night of the Long Shadow. He has a birthday ("timeless, has no birthday" was a lie).
- Look: glowing **white wings** (they turned white from the effort of making the Box of Chaos), his **rings** (eight colored gems), Italian leather shoes (probably fake), and a rusty, seasoned metal chair, which is "his thing."
- He has three nipples with his motto, "**When you can, you should**," tattooed across them.
- He keeps a ledger of everyone he's saved and the favors they owe, and a kill list. He can be capricious.

## History
- **Skypiercer:** Vythorn crossed out of the Void to bring Elusia home. Afterwards he resurrected Agdrel, ripped out his soul, rearranged AGDREL into GERALD and sealed it in [[The Box of Chaos]].
- **Seasons 1–2:** he secretly revived fallen champions and built an army ("thousands of thousands"), under the box's corrupting influence. He co-opted the Children/Servants of the Void. The Faithful resisted him (Fate of the Arbiter, Fate of the Faithful).
- **Battle for the Crown:** he tried to overthrow the Grandmaster with his army of resurrected champions and failed. He wore sackcloth and chains through Season 3 as punishment, and was freed for good behavior in the Season 3 finale.
- **Season 3 finale:** he died stepping in front of the Shadow King's attack on [[Doug the Pug]].
- **Into the Mirror and Vythorn's Return:** his soul went to the Sands of Eternity, escaped, was sidetracked by [[Fil]], gained the tome Morir's Cataclysm, and used it to destroy the Shadow King's body.
- **Season 5 (the Season of Fate):** a vision of himself ruling as the Lord of Fate, which only he lived through. Some of its champions and things (like Double Sun Cola) carried over into reality.
- **Season 6:** he created [[Thea]].
- **Season 7:** now "upper management." He bought the 100-floor wizard's tower he wanted (S7E8).

## Quotes
- "We don't put people in a labyrinth for them to finish it. We want them to die."
- "Nobody's in charge of the box. It's chaos in a box."
- "When you can, you should."
