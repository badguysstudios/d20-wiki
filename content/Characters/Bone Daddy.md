## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 4/082 - S4E4 — Death Dealers — The Scourge v Bone Daddy|S4E4 — Death Dealers — The Scourge v Bone Daddy]]
(Johnny Stanton)
- A necromancer: 6'7", dark hair, sleeveless, pale skin, "luscious locks." Carries a whip.
- Raises skeleton "children" and calls the arena his nursery. Denies being an absentee father.
- Children: Dabby, Dibby, Doobie, Gabi/Gibby, Dobby, Dino, and **Darcy**, his favorite.
- Collected all 50 state quarters and fed them (two Wyomings included) to the Box of Chaos for an **amulet of health** with a bleeding skull pendant.
- Died: Darcy carried him off the Hexadome platform into the void below.

### [[Episodes/Season 5/108 - S5E10 — BoneBound Brawl — King Bofus v Bone Daddy (and Darcy)|S5E10 — BoneBound Brawl — King Bofus v Bone Daddy (and Darcy)]]
(Johnny Stanton). A returning champion from a previous season.
- **Look:** pale, spindly limbs, sleeveless coat, very reflective sunglasses (and spares), a whip.
- **Family:** a "mommy's boy." Only his mother may call him Bonald. His parents separated when he was four, and he learned "everything I know" from his father.
- **Love life:** his ex is **Tina the Tooth Fairy**, who stitched his tooth pom-pom hat. "Can never truly die in any way that matters" as long as Darcy nurses him back.
- **Tastes:** favorite food Totino's. Drinks only whole milk.
- **Powers:** raises the dead (zombies, "Darcy's brothers and sisters"); signature move **Death Armor Reborn in the Combat Yard (D-A-R-C-Y)**, the bone mech, with the attack "Daddy's Girl"; heroic surge as a tidal wave of bone.
- **Box of Chaos:** traded his sunglasses for doubled nat-20 effects (his "inner monster").
- **Fate:** died in the tie, forgetting the safe word. Revived in the locker room.

### [[Episodes/Season 5/111 - S5E13 — Bone Rust — Ruby Rust v Bone Daddy|S5E13 — Bone Rust — Ruby Rust v Bone Daddy]]
(Johnny Stanton). A skeletal "leather daddy" (Robert's term).
- **Appearance and gear:** duster/coat lined with his patented hammer shorts; whip; glasses (broken this fight); flask of bone marrow.
- **Build:** "buns of steel," shaped like "a single meatball on a stick."
- **Powers:** the "Bone Daddy Arcanum" (fire); pulls bones from Acolytes to make skeleton swarms and bone cages; "the patented bone swirl"; Darcy death armor topped with a skull; signature move "Daddy's Girl."
- **History:** Earlier fights against "a man" and "a half man, half animal hybrid"; last consumed by "an ever growing wave of bones." His parents are a mother and his father Charles. "Bone Daddy's always been Bone Daddy."
- **In this episode:** He called Thea a traitor ("you're next"), considered retiring, and begged Ruby to spare Darcy. **He died swallowed and crushed.** His last act was releasing Darcy's finger bone.
