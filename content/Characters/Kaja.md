## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Skypiercer (Prequel)/001 - Skypiercer Part 1|Skypiercer Part 1]]
(stellaluna).
- Warlock; necromancy; "form of dread" (0:23:51; described 0:24:03); resistant to necrotic damage (0:55:48).
- Purple blood, thick like syrup (1:03:47). Cold to the touch, no body heat (1:38:06). High heels. Pointed, dagger-like fingernails (2:21:37).
- Dies often and returns: "there are forces … at work beyond our comprehension … And I merely serve one of them" (1:32:35); "We have a special relationship" (1:35:01).

- Elusia calls Kaja and Atalantos "legacies" (0:19:39).

### [[Episodes/Skypiercer (Prequel)/002 - Skypiercer Part 2|Skypiercer Part 2]]
Necromancer; "my army is portable" (0:07:58). Fond of Cor'thun's library. Has "had a few accidents in the past" (killings) (0:30:27). Can explode a body into mist (0:31:52). Has no blood in her face, so she can't blush (0:26:48). Wields **Life Stealer**. Begins teaching "the Great Dread" to Atheranon's mages.

### [[Episodes/Skypiercer (Prequel)/003 - Skypiercer Part 3 (finale)|Skypiercer Part 3 (finale)]]
Knight of Chaos. Calls her necromancy students her "death club" and apprentices. Stone heart focus. Otherworldly Guise: skeletal dragon wings. Death speaks through her: "As a servant of death, death also serves me" (1:39:43). "Kneel before my queen" (1:58:07). Kisses Elusia.

### [[Episodes/Season 4/076 - S4E1 — Shadows Reign — Atalantos v Kaja|S4E1 — Shadows Reign — Atalantos v Kaja]]
(stellalunaTV). Raised alongside Atalantos. She says she was "having such a great time on the other side."
- Warlock: **Form of Dread** (a dress of shadows, ghostly hands, an undead face) and an **otherworldly guise** (bone wings; immune to fire and poison, and to necrotic during the fight).
- Spells: Eldritch Blast, Vampiric Touch, Danse Macabre (skeletons).
- A ballroom dancer ("the more experienced dancer should lead"). Atalantos calls her "a general."
- Speaks of "my mistress" (who is not said). **Lost an ear to Atalantos.** Branded with the symbol of Cor'thun.
- Killed Atalantos. Pierced the Shadow King with a flame Sword of Cor'thun. **Her head was twisted off by the Shadow King.**
