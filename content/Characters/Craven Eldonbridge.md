**Craven Eldonbridge** is a leonin with gray and black fur and a staff that throws off light, and the leader of Atheranon during the events of [[Skypiercer]]. He appears during Elusia's return to [[The Void]]. He met her party with real suspicion, changed his tune the moment he learned who she was, and led the army's charge on horseback in the finale.

## Story

### [[Episodes/Skypiercer (Prequel)/002 - Skypiercer Part 2|Skypiercer Part 2]]

Craven, lit staff in hand, led the travelers to Atheranon. His welcome was not warm. He recognized the scholar among them, and he did not like what he saw:

> "The Endless Scholar? Right hand of the Shadow King?"

He drew on [[The Arbiter|Vythorn]] then and there. Vythorn answered by revealing his companion: "I've brought with me the savior of the Void… Elusia, daughter of the Golden King." Craven dropped the hostility and knelt before [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|Elusia]].

As leader of Atheranon, Craven also held a chest of Faucius's treasures.

### [[Episodes/Skypiercer (Prequel)/003 - Skypiercer Part 3 (finale)|Skypiercer Part 3 (finale)]]

When the war came, Craven did not watch from the back. He led the army's charge on horseback.

## Personality and Abilities

Craven is a leonin, and he is easy to spot by his gray and black fur and the staff he carries, which projects light. He is quick to draw on someone he sees as a threat, and he was blunt about it even with the Shadow King's right hand standing in front of him. He was just as quick to bend the knee once he knew he was in the presence of the Golden King's daughter. He was a leader both in Atheranon and in battle, where he rode out at the front of the charge.

## Relationships

- [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|Elusia]]: He knelt to her as the daughter of the Golden King when Vythorn presented her as the savior of the Void.
- [[The Arbiter|Vythorn]]: He greeted him with suspicion and a drawn weapon, knowing him as the Endless Scholar and right hand of the Shadow King.
- [[The Shadow King]]: Craven drew on Vythorn the moment he recognized him as the Shadow King's right hand.
