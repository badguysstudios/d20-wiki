## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 6/117 - S6E1 — Murder at the Dread Fortress (TTRPG Murder Mystery)|S6E1 — Murder at the Dread Fortress (TTRPG Murder Mystery)]]
. "Richard Skeleton Skelman," "Dungeon Designer and Keeper of the Year," from a long line of Skelmans.
- A skeleton, "one quarter of a guy." He was resurrected without his clothes. He doesn't remember his own murder but knows he was murdered.
- **His voice and thought come from his magical bone, his big toe.**
- His dungeon application was rejected for an overblown bone budget, with an insulting imp messenger.
- He uses Tim and the Poopshoe chutes ("leaky") in his dungeons, and imports monsters from China. He wants to hire Bunga for a butter trap.
- He noticed Thea's body first.
- **Fired a rocket at the Arbiter, which was thrown back as a homing missile. He is reduced to a skull and his toe**, which Dr. Octo stuck to a femur.

### [[Episodes/Season 6/120 - S6 Ep4 — Tip to Tip - Part 1 (TTRPG One Shot)|S6 Ep4 — Tip to Tip - Part 1 (TTRPG One Shot)]]
(Joel). A skeleton; "Richard," "Dick."
- Known to the Grandmaster and "beloved by the fortress."
- Has no eyeballs and sees by echolocation (chattering his teeth). His bones are held by "magnets" and detach at will. No feeling.
- Buried his lover **Josephine** alive in the family plot.
- Makes dungeons for a living, a business passed down from his lich grandfather. Signature move **the Bone Zone**; builds **the Bonetron**.
- Family: mother **Meredith** (said "Bertha" first); **Auntie Ethel** (a ghost; he ruined a dinner once); sister **Dick Arena**; a lich grandfather with "plenty of other grandchildren." "We're all dicks. That's the family name."
- Keeps a gelatinous cube friend on him. Likes pie.
- Says he solved a murder mystery involving Thea ("a real proper dick on that case").
- Leaves carrying Josephine's arm and his grandfather's rib.

### [[Episodes/Season 6/121 - S6E5, Tip to Tip - Part 2 (Richard Knottley v Richard S. Skelman, the leadership Deathmatch)|S6E5, Tip to Tip - Part 2 (Richard Knottley v Richard S. Skelman, the leadership Deathmatch)]]
("Skelly," "Skelvin" to Thea).
- **Background:**
  - An old, dead skeleton who has "existed as a skeleton for a millennia" and has been "this way for thousands of years," trying to ascend.
  - **His grandfather** is buried at the mausoleum; Team B killed him "again" last time.
  - Has "buried so many." The zombie arm/hand he carries may be his **ex-girlfriend's** ("maybe"; plotline unresolved).
  - Never went to high school prom: "nobody asked me."
  - Remembers merging with Knottley into "one giant body" last time.
- **Francis:**
  - A gelatinous-cube "jelly slime friend" that holds his bones together. He gets a new one "about every week."
  - Francis waves with pseudopods and saved Skelman's life in Round 5.
- **The bone zone / danger dungeon:**
  - A space he can expand into. He "owns" it and subcontracts its work: the statue and his trap-setting "lackeys."
- **Personality:**
  - Holds firm opinions on ethics (prom with a minor, possession and charm spells as "inherently evil").
  - Hates cardio. Played Captain Falcon "on my N64 days, back when I had flesh and bone."
- **This episode:**
  - Got the Shield of the Golden King, smelted into golden dice; they shattered on his first defense.
  - **Won and became leader of Team B, renamed "Team D" (for dicks)**, though he now regrets not choosing "Dick Dynasty."
