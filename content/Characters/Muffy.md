## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Pieces of a King/124 - Pieces of a King Ep 1 — DӒMMERUNG|Pieces of a King Ep 1 — DӒMMERUNG]]
(JustSeum)
- A 2'5" light-haired fennec fox with bright-blue eyes and a jolly, deep, warm laugh. A photoreal painter **devoted to the Grandmaster**, and a Zealot barbarian.
- **Gear:**
  - **Her paintbrush is her weapon**: always pristine, it cuts like a blade or is soft at her will, and she keeps it sheathed.
  - **Her palette** (it fills with paint when held) is her shield.
  - Her paint corrodes metal.
- **Painted the mustached Grandmaster portrait**, now hung at two feet in a courtyard. Her signature finisher is painting mustaches.
- **Can't swim**; she thinks she nearly drowned (canon from a nat 1).
- She carries the relay stone. Her double nat 20 saved the squad from the stampede. This is her first mission with Grimnar and Nehebka.

### [[Episodes/Pieces of a King/125 - Pieces of a King Ep 2 — THE WAR COUNCIL|Pieces of a King Ep 2 — THE WAR COUNCIL]]
(JustSeum)
- A small fennec-fox creature with large ears; can stretch about three inches taller on her hind legs.
- A painter ("I paint there") who reveals images with a dry brush on the one sheet she always carries.
- Carries a "call to home" on her back. Ran Cabals and Krakens badly. Her mother "just called me Muffy."

### [[Episodes/Pieces of a King/126 - Pieces of a King Ep 3 — The Warhead|Pieces of a King Ep 3 — The Warhead]]
(JustSeum).
- A dog, one of a whole litter with many siblings. A painter with a paintbrush weapon; barbarian rage.
- Runs the game Cabals and Krakens.
- Owns a mustached portrait of the Grandmaster.
- Painted the 35th portrait (a gift) and a mustache on the Solar.
- **Defined the Void salute.**
- "I'm committed to the Grandmaster. If she asked me to do anything, I would do it."

### [[Episodes/Pieces of a King/127 - Pieces of a King Ep 4 - The Warmind|Pieces of a King Ep 4 - The Warmind]]
(JustSeum)
- Paints in the air with **her dead brother Meir's paintbrush**, which makes ink without paint and has quill-like bristles.
- Rallied an RBA squad as its "mascot" and killed a Titan with a damage-type rainbow. Choked Taupe unconscious.
- Cheerful and cold-blooded ("Where would you like to die?"). Carries a waystone.
- Learned that Meir may be alive at Umbracor, and sent him a note.
