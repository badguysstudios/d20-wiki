## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 2/039 - S2E16, Pub Brawl - Broose Scorchtail v Dewdrop Grizzlebee|S2E16, Pub Brawl - Broose Scorchtail v Dewdrop Grizzlebee]]
(TheShortNord). A **fairy scholar**: "an old, red-haired, out-of-shape man with a monk haircut… retired Boston cop look."
- **Body:**
  - A receding horseshoe of red hair that recedes further each time he uses psionics, down to "five hairs."
  - Very hairy "Robin Williams" forearms.
  - Self-described as **turtle/tortoise-shaped, a "turtle fairy"** and "turtle unicorn fairy," "a Psyduck in a fairy's body," and "a very heavy, dense potato."
  - Has wings but can't fly in his heavy armor.
- **Background:**
  - Has **studied the Arbiter and Grandmaster "for ages."**
  - Learned to fight from **Melee for Dummies** (pronounces it "M'lee"). He found it on the floor of a "magic half-priced books"; the author is "Richard Stabbs."
  - He **paid a man a lot of gold to train him and got books**.
  - Wanted to prove "people of science can throw down." Is jealous of Broose's hair.
- **Abilities:** crossbow, daggers, longsword, short sword, Reduce, Faerie Fire, psionic strike, psionic protective field, psi-powered leap, telekinesis, Indomitable, Second Wind, Action Surge.
- **In this episode:**
  - Gave Gerald his **notebook** for a potion of speed, then was slowed by a mushroom.
  - Was charmed by Broose and broke free.
  - Was blessed by the Grandmaster. Wore Broose's fur as a toupee.
  - Rolled a record number of nat 1s. **Drowned in beer.**
