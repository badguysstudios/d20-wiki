## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/064 - Into the Mirror, Session 1 (YouTube Parts 1–6 - Flynry Dies, Flynry Wakes, Flynry Walks, Survivors, Reflect…|Into the Mirror, Session 1 (YouTube Parts 1–6 - Flynry Dies, Flynry Wakes, Flynry Walks, Survivors, Reflect…]]
(ParallaxStella).
- A **tiefling and the Grandmaster's personal assistant**; carries a book and takes notes constantly.
- About 5'2", curly black hair, light violet horns, light purple tail, red glasses on a teal-and-black reading chain.
- Carries a vial of potent brandy and can cast Guidance.
- Loathes the Arbiter. **Devoted to the Grandmaster ("oh, my sweet lady")**; her mirror showed them snuggled together in a snowy cabin.
- **Sent as chaperone.** Made the Grandmaster a promise to come back safely. The Grandmaster sees herself in her.

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/071 - Into the Mirror 7–9 - Claws of Sand, Shifting Sands, Hourglass (session 2)|Into the Mirror 7–9 - Claws of Sand, Shifting Sands, Hourglass (session 2)]]
(ParallaxStella)
- **The Grandmaster's personal assistant.** Never in battle before; learned "one or two things" from the Grandmaster.
- Clumsy: learned healing because she is "constantly tripping."
- **Promised the Grandmaster she would come back safely.**
- Spells: faerie fire, Shield of Faith, Toll of the Dead, Spiritual Weapon (a scythe), hellish rebuke (purple flames), Cure Wounds.
- Keeps a notebook and sketches Flynry for the Grandmaster.
- First kill this episode. Saw the ziggurat in Truth.

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/074 - Into the Mirror 10 Shatter + Into the Mirror 11 Mortaurios (ITM session 3)|Into the Mirror 10 Shatter + Into the Mirror 11 Mortaurios (ITM session 3)]]
(ParallaxStella). A librarian who "didn't get out much".
- Says she "was sent here to protect you."
- Keeps a notebook and sketches everything. Casts sleep.
- Killed two sleeping outlanders with a dagger, praying over each. Wants to go home.

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/077 - Into the Mirror Session 4 (YouTube parts 12–13) - Royal Audience and Otherside|Into the Mirror Session 4 (YouTube parts 12–13) - Royal Audience and Otherside]]
(ParallaxStella).
- A book-carrying scholar who draws.
- **Secretly viewed the royals' reflections in Flynry's sword.**
- Put attackers to sleep in an earlier fight.
- **Made the two remembrance stones** from the sand remains of the party's friend.
- In the fight: Banishment (failed), Healing Word, Guardian of Faith (a winged spectral centaur), Shield of Faith (a glowing cross).

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/080 - Into the Mirror 14 — The Scholar|Into the Mirror 14 — The Scholar]]
(ParallaxStella)
- Casts find traps as a visible wave of white light; danger normally shows up red.
- Reads Celestial and translates the inscription.
- Brings the orrery to Flynry's sword, which triggers the transport.

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/088 - Into the Mirror 15–16, Old Friends - Sky|Into the Mirror 15–16, Old Friends - Sky]]
(ParallaxStella)
- **Background:** Close to the Grandmaster and familiar with the Dread Fortress and its history. Contracts are part of her daily routine.
- **Tools:** carries a notebook and two pens, and a book she used to bash the Arbiter.
- **Actions:** tackled the Arbiter; carries the Orrery, which she hid in her cleavage. Casts Silence, Sanctuary, thaumaturgy, Counterspell, Mass Cure Wounds and Toll the Dead. Flipped off the Arbiter.

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/093 - Into the Mirror 17, The Way Out|Into the Mirror 17, The Way Out]]
(ParallaxStella)
- Tiefling cleric: Hellish Rebuke (once per long rest), spiritual weapon, Create Water, Command, Banishment, Cure Wounds, Sanctuary. Speaks Common, Infernal, Abyssal, Celestial.
- **Carries the orrery hidden in her shirt** (the "third boob" running gag). It vibrates and glows blue near the Oasis.
- Treats Flynry as the leader. Empathetic; **asks every NPC if they're happy**.
- **Traded her amulet (rose-gold chain, emerald) for a Periapt of Health.**
