## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 1/019 - S1E10 — The Bear — Diamond Joe v Milliac|S1E10 — The Bear — Diamond Joe v Milliac]]
(Ramji / ramjiv). An **elderly dragonborn artificer** in self-built power armor of fused metallic plates. He speaks with an old British accent.
- **Creator of Steely Dan**, whom he calls "my boy." He came to the Deathmatch because the Arbiter promised to reunite them.
- **Gear and powers:**
  - Thunder gauntlets that he forgets to switch off.
  - A **Defensive Field** of radiant energy.
  - A **lightning breath weapon** and a "repulsion breath." He blames his bad breath on being a dragonborn.
  - Thunderwaves he plays as magnified farts.
  - A shoulder-mounted firebolt emitter like a Predator laser, run on batteries.
  - **Boots of the Winding Path** (15-ft teleport).
  - Grease, Lightning Bolt, Absorb Elements.
  - **A bag of pills**: the blue Haste pill, plus "whoopsie whoopsie" and "space out" varieties. They give him senior moments.
- **Personality and lore:**
  - Philosophy: "better living through alchemy." Claims the armor is all that keeps him in one piece.
  - Catchphrases: "libation and lubrication," "the older the leather, the better the weather."
  - Says he's from "the big city," and was a pool shark in his youth.
  - As a youth he had 9-volt batteries dropped across dragon scales.
  - His mother was "a worldly woman."
  - Wanted an invitation to the dragon battle royale.
- **In this episode:** he won. He was promised ice cream and a reunion with his son, and was dropped by the Grandmaster after asking for a handjob.
