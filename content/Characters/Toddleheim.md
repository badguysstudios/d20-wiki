## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/025 - S2E6 — FoTA - Sands of the Arena (Fate of the Arbiter, Part 2)|S2E6 — FoTA - Sands of the Arena (Fate of the Arbiter, Part 2)]]
(Ahnubyss). An old spellcaster: Enlarge/Reduce, Acid Splash, Magic Missile, Burning Hands, Cause Fear, Misty Step. Resistant to poison.
- **Backstory:** he once lost control in a bar and burned it to the ground; everyone died ("not good people, but it wasn't the death that they deserved").
- From the Box of Chaos: **one use of Fire Storm**, paid for with his own blood. He wiped out the second wave with it.
- **Bitten by a werewolf and gained lycanthropy**; kept his mind; Lyrus talked him back. His eyes flash yellow.
- From the Seer: a **potion of longevity (−12 years)** and a **dimensional loop** ring.
- "A terrible traveler" who wants passport stamps. Driven by adventure and "one last hurrah"; may not make it to retirement.

### [[Episodes/Battle for the Crown/046 - S2E18 · Battle for the Crown Round 1 · Team Grandmaster (Toddleheim & Veronica) v Team Arbiter (Ralzamon & …|S2E18 · Battle for the Crown Round 1 · Team Grandmaster (Toddleheim & Veronica) v Team Arbiter (Ralzamon & …]]
(Ahnubyss). "The Tattletailor"; the Arbiter also calls him "the Great."
- A short dwarf with a large gray beard, a hat and nice robes; "very suave."
- "**I have been a wizard for over 125 years.**"
- **Spent his hero point to become a werewolf**, keeping his wizard abilities. In werewolf form he resists bludgeoning (and slashing, per the berserker ruling).
- Spells and actions: Cause Fear, Misty Step, Acid Splash. He considered Sleep.
- **In this episode:**
  - Chased Glub Glub's stick, then slapped him with it.
  - **Was charmed by holding Boney.**
  - Fell into a blood frenzy and mauled Ralzamon.
  - Was dropped by Ralzamon's Eldritch Blast, survived, and was raised on an iceberg pedestal as a victor.
- The player is new to D&D.

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/064 - Into the Mirror, Session 1 (YouTube Parts 1–6 - Flynry Dies, Flynry Wakes, Flynry Walks, Survivors, Reflect…|Into the Mirror, Session 1 (YouTube Parts 1–6 - Flynry Dies, Flynry Wakes, Flynry Walks, Survivors, Reflect…]]
