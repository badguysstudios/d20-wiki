## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Live Shows/049 - Live | GCX Orlando 2022 — Lord Snacc v The Showman|Live | GCX Orlando 2022 — Lord Snacc v The Showman]]
(RogueExMachinca). A masked man, "legally" named the Showman, who goes by "Showman" on a "second name basis."
- **Look:** a bowler hat (round, weird-shaped, out of fashion), and bandages that leave him unable to see out of his left eye.
- **A bard** with no gods. He likes long walks on the beach and has a Disney annual pass.
- **His knives, named after the Friends cast:**
  - Chandler and Monica (Monica "my favorite… she gets around").
  - Joey, also called his favorite later.
  - Phoebe ("the weird one"), sacrificed to Gerald.
  - Rachel, given to Turtleman.
  - Ross ("I don't like Ross"; "a paleontologist"), auctioned to an Acolyte.
- **Spells:** fear, suggestion, Bardic Inspiration, Crown of Madness, animate objects, Enemies Abound.
- **A custom feature to feign his own death** ("I've had practice").
- **Claims:** started with more than two livers and has a questionable "hobby."
- **Gifts:** got a +1 AC neck-shield from the Box of Chaos. Stole the Grandmaster's sword from the forest floor, and she took it back.
- **Fate:** died to Lord Snacc. His last words were a vow to haunt the fro-yo stand.
