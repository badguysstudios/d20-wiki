## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 5/104 - S5E6 — American Amphibian — King Man Frog Bofus v Richard Knottley|S5E6 — American Amphibian — King Man Frog Bofus v Richard Knottley]]
(guest, unnamed in metadata).
- Former Peloton instructor, banned from a couple of counties, from Denny's (where the Peloton instructors meet with non-stationary bikes) and from Zumba; "the IHOP saw me coming."
- Online name Dick Twist, after his dance (also his signature move).
- Wears a tinfoil hat (against Wi-Fi radiation), sunglasses, fake mustaches (he carries spares), American flag pants, later a fake beard and reading glasses.
- Runs the conspiracy webinar/podcast "Knottley's Real World". Conspiracies: the Chinchillian government, the chinchilla/vole/mole cabal, the Nintendo Switch, lunar moths eating the ozone, eating our neighbors.
- Financial advisor: Cricket, an old guy in Montana.
- Claims six foot; actually 5'10½. Afraid of heights. Says he's "very clearly a hot dog."
- Drove to the arena on his Peloton bike.
- Rejected the blue pill ("I only take red pills"). Wore the Arbiter's blue ring wrapped in a mustache.
- Died of a "coronary"/"pulmonary" after his Peloton lightning heroic surge. Left a dead man's switch recording.

### [[Episodes/Season 5/107 - S5E9 — Patriotic Punch Up — Bob the Barbarian v Richard Knottley (Dick Twist)|S5E9 — Patriotic Punch Up — Bob the Barbarian v Richard Knottley (Dick Twist)]]
(Prototopics). An old man, "87" by his own claim and "98" by Thea's.
- **Back from death:** he died last time of a heart attack on his Peloton after 45 cheeseburgers, "about three weeks ago."
- **Look:** a removable, lopsided "real" mustache (Hercule Poirot style), an aluminum foil hat and sunglasses.
- **Running for president** on the "Peloton party":
  - Platform: dismantle a **government of chinchillas wearing human skin**, and stop the **lunar moths eating the ozone layer**.
  - Backers: a **Big Peloton super PAC**, Adidas, Payless, KB Toys and Toys R Us.
- **Peloton:** describes his tie to the company as a Scientology-like lifetime job.
- **Powers:**
  - Lightning from his Peloton, and "bolts of Wi-Fi" through his foil hat.
  - The Dick Twist special ability: a tuxedo-pants dance-off (crop circle clarification, chinchilla cha-cha, Bigfoot Boogie).
- **Frogs:** a frog trapped in his orifice, "a gift from God King Bofidis / King Bulfus" (spelling uncertain). He hates King Bulfus and is "still traumatized from the frogs."
- **Other details:** colorblind for 42 years, tried keto. **Has children** (his own claim, in his death monologue).
- **Robbie:** had Robbie imprisoned with a remnant of the Hunger, which ate Robbie on his death. He blames "guys who work for other guys who work for some black suits."
- **This episode:** killed by Bob. His ghost was netted by Thea and is being sewn back into his body by mages.

### [[Episodes/Season 6/121 - S6E5, Tip to Tip - Part 2 (Richard Knottley v Richard S. Skelman, the leadership Deathmatch)|S6E5, Tip to Tip - Part 2 (Richard Knottley v Richard S. Skelman, the leadership Deathmatch)]]
