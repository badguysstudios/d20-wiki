**The Pebble** is a small but very handsome, very muscular four-foot-tall dwarf. He wears shades and a leather jacket and treats every fight as a pro wrestling match. He swaggered into the Void to face [[Iowa-Ska]] in the Chinchilla Forest, armed with a signature microphone, an endless supply of steel chairs and a lifelong vow never to break character. He lost, but he gave [[The Acolytes|the Acolytes]] a one-dwarf show of promos, flashbacks, hot tags and a People's Elbow. He went down on his own terms: still selling, still talking, and still asking when he would finally get his title shot.

## Story

### [[Episodes/Season 7/133 - S7E5 — Rock and a Hard Place — Iowa-Ska v the Pebble|S7E5 — Rock and a Hard Place — Iowa-Ska v the Pebble]]

The Pebble entered [[The Dread Fortress|the Dread Fortress]] locker room to a crowd chanting his name. He grabbed his microphone and announced, "Finally, the Pebble has come back to D20 Deathmatch." He immediately mistook his opponent for "like a lizard." [[Iowa-Ska]], a middle-aged green dragonborn in a feathered headdress, corrected him firmly. The Pebble then fumbled his first promo and blamed a lack of mirror practice. [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|The Grandmaster]] admired that he had brought his own microphone and offered him [[Thea]] as "a walking megaphone." He said he would let her know if the Pebble required it.

In [[The Chinchilla Forest|Chinchilla Forest]], Iowa-Ska's halberd drew first blood. The Pebble was startled: "I thought this was supposed to be fake fighting… you actually, like, cut me up." He answered with a raised eyebrow so commanding that Iowa-Ska dropped to his knees. Then he pulled a shiny steel chair from the crowd, which annoyed [[The Arbiter|the Arbiter]], who considered chairs his own signature. When the first chaos struck, the Acolytes pelted him with everything in their pockets. Then Thea grabbed a microphone and flooded the arena with screeching. The Pebble simply out-yelled her, so loudly that the trees shuddered and Thea fled. As his prize, she owed him one free call for help. He then raised ice ropes and turnbuckles from the ground for his finisher. Iowa-Ska clipped his legs as the elbow dropped. The Pebble insisted, "this is why in my universe that combo is banned."

When the Acolytes surged their power into him, the Pebble took off his glasses and remembered his father, **the Boulder**. The Boulder fought storm giant after storm giant until one plunged its sword where no sword should go, while young Pebble watched. His chair turned to lollipop-pink swirls ("This one's for you, dad") and folded over Iowa-Ska's head. **The clouds took the shape of the Boulder giving a thumbs up.** He read his next line off his hand, "The state of Iowa sucks," and then caught Iowa-Ska's arrow in midair and flicked it back. Later, near death, he cashed in Thea's debt. She flew in as a living shield, even clamping the halberd between her cheeks. He then hot-tagged in his rival Imbe, an eight-foot minotaur with a robotic arm, to take Iowa-Ska "to Suplex City." The Arbiter allowed it: "As long as somebody dies, we don't really care."

It was not enough. Iowa-Ska roped the minotaur and sent a stampede of [[Chinchillas|chinchillas]] at the Pebble. The Pebble grabbed the lead chinchilla, [[Frank]], who complained about his union rep and his sciatica. When the Arbiter offered him one wild chance at a far bigger blow if he could guess a number, the Pebble picked "the only number the Pebble knows," 69. He rolled 79. In a game of rock, paper, scissors, his own name forced him to throw rock, and Iowa-Ska's paper crushed his hand "from rock to pebble." In the end, Iowa-Ska drove his halberd into the Pebble's barbed-wire tramp stamp. As the Pebble's heart slowed, Thea tugged his body off to the locker room and put a small potion to his lips: "It'll one day be your time." Whether he was revived has not been revealed.

## Personality and Abilities

The Pebble is bald, glistening and proud of it. He makes sure his muscles shine "in high definition," and he once oiled himself up so thoroughly that an old lady in the crowd fainted. He refers to himself only in the third person, delivers trash talk with a wrestler's rhythm, and lives by the creed "If you're not lying, if you're not cheating, if you're not stealing, you're not trying." He considers breaking kayfabe a sin, because "for all the fans out there, they have to believe just this one night that wrestling is real." He claims a long career against cow men, bird men, lizards and bears, and he wants a championship push.

Under the showmanship is a real fighter. He is both a bard and a monk. His words from the microphone can wound, he can heal his own body, and he can snatch an arrow out of the air. As a glacier dwarf he also commands ice and frost: he slides away across it, shoots up icicles, and summons wrestling ropes and turnbuckles from the ground. His signature moves are the steel chair to the head and the People's Elbow.

## Relationships

- [[Iowa-Ska]]: His opponent and killer. In his dying thoughts, the Pebble predicted the dragonborn would "get a sponsorship from the people."
- [[Thea]]: He out-screamed her and won her one-time service. She served as his shield, was offered the job of his manager if she protected him, and dragged his body to the locker room with a potion.
- Imbe: His minotaur rival and tag partner. "I'm going to kick your ass at some point, but the Pebble needs a little bit of help."
- The Boulder: His father, a fighter killed by a storm giant. The Pebble fights to honor him.
- [[The Arbiter]]: Resented the Pebble's shinier chairs and approved his tag team on the grounds that somebody would still die.
- [[Frank]]: The unionized chinchilla the Pebble grabbed and threw about two feet.
