## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 7/133 - S7E5 — Rock and a Hard Place — Iowa-Ska v the Pebble|S7E5 — Rock and a Hard Place — Iowa-Ska v the Pebble]]
(SenseiSuplex).
- **Who he is:**
  - **A four-foot-tall, very muscular dwarf**, a **glacier dwarf from the snowy tundras of the Azure Valley** with ice/frost powers.
  - **Bard and monk.** Pro wrestler persona: shades, leather jacket, signature microphone, steel chairs, the People's Elbow, promos read off his hand.
  - Bald, with a barbed-wire tramp stamp.
- **Fighting and history:**
  - Claims to have fought cow men, bird men, lizards and bears.
  - **Son of the Boulder**, a wrestler or fighter who battled storm giants and was killed when a storm giant plunged its sword up his backside while young Pebble watched.
  - **Rival and tag partner: Imbe.**
  - Refers to "my universe" where his combo is banned. Lives by "If you're not lying, if you're not cheating, if you're not stealing, you're not trying." Never breaks kayfabe.
- **In this episode:**
  - **Won Thea's one-time service** by outscreaming her.
  - **Killed by Iowa-Ska**, then dragged off by Thea, who gave him "a small potion" in the locker room. Inferred revival: "It'll one day be your time."
  - Dried Blood: "the Pebble is only a call away."
