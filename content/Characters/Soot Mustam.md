## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 3/070 - S3E17, Season 3 Finale - Soot Mustam v Huey Leatherface|S3E17, Season 3 Finale - Soot Mustam v Huey Leatherface]]
(WintersTales / "AJ").
- **Who he is:**
  - A **12-year-old Goliath boy**, about six foot, extremely muscular ("looks like he's 25"). "Gohan-shaped."
  - An **animal trainer** in the style of a Pokémon trainer, who wears his **hat backwards for power**. The hat "dictate[s] enemies."
  - Collects **baubles/bobbles/bells** ("legally distinct" badges). His most precious is **the emblem of his hometown**, which he gave to the Box of Chaos.
- **Powers and companions:**
  - **Paladin warlock**, through "sponsors": a celestial being and a warlock patron.
  - Greatsword. **Blindsight (10 ft)**, from training blindfolded.
  - Familiar **Marymoo**, a "lightning flying squirrel" that comes from a "blue and white square" (dice cage). Marymoo was "never in a cage."
  - Summoned steed **Flamewing** (a Pegasus/"flame horse"), which Huey sold to Gerald.
- **Background:**
  - From a **Goliath village where kids leave home around 10**. Training there included blindfolded "blind steps" while people shot at them.
  - **He feels he must prove to his mother that he's not the weakest in the village.** He says his "entire village hates me." His mother "used to yell at me, I make poor decisions."
  - Became a warrior a year ago, at 11. "Haven't been hugged enough as a child."
  - Calls himself "the greatest," "a troubled youth," "an angry boy."
- **In this episode:**
  - Rolled **four nat 1s** (opportunity attack, hero-point reroll, intimidation, the final athletics).
  - **Died by falling into his own Cube of Annihilation**, hoping the gods would bring him back.
