**King Man Frog Bofus** is the self-proclaimed "King of the frog people, man people frogs": a short, ripped, suspiciously oily British monarch in a deflating frog mask, with a tape-measure tongue and an army of tiny frogs living under his headgear. He fought twice in the Season of Fate, when [[The Arbiter|the Arbiter]] ruled the Void as the Lord of Fate. His first bout ended with no winner. By his last he had been killed and rebuilt so many times that all he wanted was to die for good. He finally lost to [[Kyle Stoddard]] and the power of anime, which was almost certainly the worst way he could have gone.

## Story

### American Amphibian
Bofus arrived at [[The Dread Fortress]] through a portal [[Thea]] produced by breaking wind. She had kidnapped him and his opponent, [[Richard Knottley]], and kept them in the back locker room while the Lord of Fate brooded at a window over the protesters outside. Bofus introduced himself as "50% man, 50% frog, and 100%… something else." He declined to say which half was which, nicknamed Richard "Mr. Tinfoil," and, after Thea's portal, proposed joining the rioters to overthrow the hosts. He also made it clear that, as a king, he did not pay taxes: "I receive them. Your taxes probably."

The fight took place atop [[The Crimson Keep]], a cheap rental venue where people kept dying. Richard opened by hurling a half-eaten Snickers, and Bofus's retort literally became a shield that deflected it. When Richard threw Thea at him like a javelin, Bofus caught her on his tongue, licked her, and set her down gently. His main tactic was to reach under the mask and fling fistfuls of tiny, Skittle-colored frogs, aimed at "the mouth, nostrils, any orifice." The frogs got down Richard's windpipe and into both of his ears. Bofus refused to rest ("That's for coward"). Instead he pulled cheeseburgers from his boots and built a distracting cheeseburger fortress, and between blows he whispered cheese facts and nut facts meant to wound Richard's American pride.

His signature frog splash missed the first time, when Richard caught him one-handed in midair. It landed the second time, smothering Richard in a body that showed him what his own could look like "if only he committed himself." Bofus won a dance-off by barely moving, caught a thrown Peloton bike, and dangled it out of reach with arms that hang below his knees. At [[The Box of Chaos]] he sacrificed a frog and received a screaming vial of black soul-liquid, which he kept and never used. Then he slicked the arena floor with his own body oil.

The end came as both champions stood on the edge of exhaustion. Richard, who had been pedaling the whole time, channeled lightning off his Peloton, and Bofus could not get clear. "Electricity is my only weakness," he admitted, and he submitted to the blast. Richard died of a coronary, and Bofus did not survive either, so the match had **no winner**.

### Between Fights
Bofus was revived after his first death, and by his own account he fought and lost again. In that battle he lost his true love, [[Bone Daddy|Bone Daddy Daddy]]. He came to mourn Richard as his "best friend slash enemy," and had a Peloton tattoo inked across his chest in Dick Twist's memory.

### Froggy Style
When Thea summoned him again to face [[Kyle Stoddard]] in [[The Duck Village]], Bofus was a haggard wreck: "Again? You have brought me here again? Why?" The Lord of Fate explained that [[The Acolytes]] simply enjoyed watching him fight to the death, so they kept fixing his broken body. He now had an adamantium skeleton and some "special features" from the [[Goblins]]. When Thea offered any prize he wanted, he asked the price of permanent death. Kyle spent the introduction proselytizing for his beloved Princess Sakura-chan, while Bofus slammed his head into the paving stones.

Bofus opened with his new "tag team partner," a 12 Gauge Shotgun he pulled from under his mask and loaded with his loyal frog citizens. Kyle shredded the oversized mask without touching the tiny head beneath, and carved through the O of the Peloton tattoo. The fight then descended into butts. Kyle poked Bofus's backside in a "secret Naruto technique" until Thea taped his cheeks shut, and Bofus answered with a conga line of hundreds of frogs bound for Kyle's rear: "Defend your own butthole." He also projectile-vomited onto Kyle's collector's shirt and made out with a Princess Sakura body pillow. Low on hope, he begged for mercy ("You wouldn't hit a frogman, would you? Who's having a very bad day."), and it worked.

Kyle nearly converted him to the Simperi with a chibi figurine and promises of dental coverage. The pitch fell apart at the fedoras: "I have a crown. I cannot replace it with a fedora. This would be sacrilege." At the Box of Chaos he traded away the figurine for the Lord of Fate's ring, and later traded the body pillow for the bow of [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]], which he turned into a second, slightly shinier shotgun. Dual-wielding like a gunslinger, he unleashed tidal waves of frogs while the spirits of Richard and Bone Daddy Daddy patted his shoulder: "good job, kid."

Kyle, transformed into a full Princess Sakura cosplay, became untouchable, and Bofus began begging for the end. The Lord of Fate couldn't stand it either and handed him back his shotgun for one last shot: "be at peace, son." **It misfired.** His rebuilt body, no longer able to hold itself together, clutched the gun wrong, and the blast went back into his tiny head and blew it clean off. Kyle then wore the head, with a fedora on it, as an accessory. Bofus's last thought was "At least I didn't die a nerd."

## Personality and Abilities
Bofus is a small king with enormous dignity, most of it unearned. He is a "respectable five foot two" with arms that reach below his knees and a body that is mostly a man's: human hands, ripped muscles and an oily sheen that may be sweat or a frog gland ("Stuff can be two things"). He also, he insists, has a cloaca. On top sits a green frog mask with hand-drawn cartoon eyes and a "crown" of yellow fabric sewn to it. By his second fight the mask was tattered, with bags stitched beneath it, and it hid a tiny real frog head. He wears lycra wrestler pants, keeps cheeseburgers in his boots, and loves a Snickers, particularly a half-eaten one.

He fights like a wrestler who ran out of ideas and kept throwing frogs anyway, and somehow it works. His arsenal includes the frog splash, a whip-like tongue, frog-loaded shotguns, tidal waves of frogs, and vomit when the smell of Axe body spray gets too much. He is British and leans on it, needling Americans about Kraft Singles and peanut butter. Beneath the bluster runs a streak of weary self-awareness: "I rule a frog nation. I've used it to climb up a butt and punch a pillow today. This is really who I am."

## Relationships
- [[Richard Knottley]]: his first opponent. They killed each other, and Bofus later mourned him as Dick Twist, his "best friend slash enemy," with a memorial Peloton tattoo.
- [[Bone Daddy|Bone Daddy Daddy]]: the true love Bofus lost in an earlier battle. Bone Daddy Daddy's spirit appeared beside him in his final fight.
- [[Kyle Stoddard]]: the anime devotee who nearly converted him, defeated him, and then wore his head as an accessory.
- [[Thea]]: the imp who kidnapped him twice. He caught her on his tongue in his first fight and told her to read her emails in his second.
- [[The Arbiter]]: as the Lord of Fate, he kept having Bofus rebuilt for the crowd's amusement, though he once asked Bofus to punch Richard in the face for him.
- Frogs: his loyal subjects, used as ammunition, armor, waves and occasionally sacrifices.
