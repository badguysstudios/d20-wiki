### [[Episodes/Playtests/005 - Playtest 2 (Pre-Launch) — Atlas v Bromir|Playtest 2 (Pre-Launch) — Atlas v Bromir]]
Champion with boulder fists ("Bromir's Boulder Fists"), wrestler persona ("brother," "Oh yeah!," "the smackdown"), fighter with sorcery (enlarge, jump, thunderwave). Has a **boyfriend**.
