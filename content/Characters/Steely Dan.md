## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 1/014 - S1E5, The 2v2 - Chuck Solid Gold Dragon Biff & Richardo v Steely Dan & Thass'Lok|S1E5, The 2v2 - Chuck Solid Gold Dragon Biff & Richardo v Steely Dan & Thass'Lok]]
(the4thculture). "Steely Dan the third, the victor of the cosmic dragon's duel." A large warforged paladin with a glaive, Polearm Master, Sentinel, smites and Action Surge. Immune to fear.
- **Speaks only through cassette tapes of misheard lyrics** loaded into his chest. He rewinds them with a pencil.
- Mini discs are embedded in his armor, and a compartment holds "Now That's What I Call…" CDs.
- He can project karaoke lyrics on his forehead.
- Intelligence of one; can't remember Thass'Lok's name.
- **Befriended the haunted doll**, which branded him with a rune and revived him once.
- Killed by Bull One's pillow, then used as a club.

### [[Episodes/Season 1/019 - S1E10 — The Bear — Diamond Joe v Milliac|S1E10 — The Bear — Diamond Joe v Milliac]]
("Steely Dan the Sir"). Built by Diamond Joe. "Quite the instrument of destruction… in the previous Deathmatch." Described as an 8–9 ft steam-powered contraption covered in cassette tapes and minidiscs. **His parts are in a scrap room the Arbiter showed Joe** (Dried Blood).
