**Glotun** is a politician the party met during the [[Skypiercer]] prequel. He arrived bearing favors and left without the one thing he wanted. He was a fighter before he took up politics, and he hid his real errand under a friendly offer of help: he wanted someone with the emperor's ear to steer a public waterway toward [[Plintos]].

## Story

In [[Episodes/Skypiercer (Prequel)/001 - Skypiercer Part 1|Skypiercer Part 1]], Glotun approached the party and opened with generosity, offering favors to anyone who might need them. The offer came with strings. Once the pleasantries were done, he revealed that he wanted access to the emperor so he could lobby to divert the **southern aqueduct** for the benefit of Plintos. The party heard him out and turned him down.

In [[Episodes/Skypiercer (Prequel)/002 - Skypiercer Part 2|Skypiercer Part 2]], a closer look at Glotun gave more context to his scheme. His wealth was described as "Ivy League rich," comfortable and well-connected rather than gaudy. He also seemed to believe that Plintos was paying him for his trouble. Whether Plintos ever actually paid him, the story did not say.

## Personality and Abilities

Glotun was portly and balding, and he wore loose, toga-like robes that suited a man of the senate floor more than a soldier. He had fought once, though by the time the party met him his weapons of choice were favors, flattery and backroom deals. His manner was that of a practiced lobbyist: open with a gift, close with the ask. He carried money without flaunting it, the quiet kind of rich that expects doors to open.

## Relationships

- [[Plintos]]: Glotun lobbied on their behalf to divert the southern aqueduct, and he appeared to believe they were paying him for it.
