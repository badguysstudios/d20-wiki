## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Playtests/009 - Playtest 6 (Pre-Launch) — Michael v Victoro Cassalanter (Mudcat vs Caustic)|Playtest 6 (Pre-Launch) — Michael v Victoro Cassalanter (Mudcat vs Caustic)]]
(Caustic Phoenix). No head; blind and deaf (but somehow has darkvision and passive perception); speaks and tastes through **mouths with tongues in his palms**; a rocky iron shell; big sword with 10-foot reach; iron fists. Immune to fire (and healed by it), bludgeoning, piercing, charm and much more; advantage on saves against spells. Loves dancing (hula, cha-cha) and **hugs**; wants friends; "a hollow shell" trying "to fill it with friendship and dancing." Named himself Michael ("It's a strong name"). Befriended Barbara (briefly) and **adopted the barking dog**. Banished for a minute to a plane of flowers and daisies.
