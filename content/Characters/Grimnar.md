## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Pieces of a King/125 - Pieces of a King Ep 2 — THE WAR COUNCIL|Pieces of a King Ep 2 — THE WAR COUNCIL]]

- A bear-like Void Knight of the Dread Fortress, about 20 years in its service, with burned fingers.
- A devotee of chaos ("the wheel of chaos"; Nehebka calls him "the acolyte of chaos"). He holds that chaos is not evil: "It can be wielded… trying to hold on to too much will lead to ruin."
- **Can mimic others' abilities**: he copied Nehebka's tremorsense and the Physician's magic resistance. He senses chaos visions and crackles with chaos lightning.
- Whittles figurines; carved David. Eats whole fish. Took a sandy finger and sandy rum without hesitation.

### [[Episodes/Pieces of a King/126 - Pieces of a King Ep 3 — The Warhead|Pieces of a King Ep 3 — The Warhead]]
.
- Old-ish and stiff, and stretches loudly.
- Called "the bear" by Trissavin; looms "like a giant bear." That he is a bear is inferred from these lines.
- **Adopts resistances from creatures he reads** and **copies spells cast on him** (death ward), along with chaos-energy tells: eyes going black void, multicolored lightning.
- Turns lightning into fireballs and fires chaos-energy eye lasers. Using his power chars his hands and burns fur and skin around his eyes.
- Finds the Grandmaster "stuck up" but won't report the mustache portrait.
- Has heard soldiers' tales of a world-sized harvested corpse in the Void.
- His answer to the alliance: "We will do what chaos allows."
