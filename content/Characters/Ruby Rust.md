## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 5/101 - S5E3, Eldritch Fangs - Ruby Rust v Robbie Clark and the Hunger|S5E3, Eldritch Fangs - Ruby Rust v Robbie Clark and the Hunger]]
(Rob / Robert Hartley GM). The YouTube blurb calls him a "colossal python."
- **Who he is:**
  - A giant constrictor snake who works as a coder. He uses Java, not Python, Ruby or Rust.
  - He types with his tail on a big-button ergonomic keyboard.
  - He doesn't get out much and learned to fight from martial arts and action movies.
  - He calls himself a zoologist. Has a cloaca and no nipples. Has a nictitating membrane.
- **Gear:**
  - A drinking helmet with hot java on one side and a cartoon poison bottle on the other. It was cracked in round 5.
  - Shield of the Golden King (lost to the Macarena).
  - Morir's Cataclysm (an extra power-up, shown as glowing markings).
- **Body sacrificed:** cut off part of his tail by autotomy (it regrows), snapped off an incisor, ate his own lower half like an Ouroboros.
- **Abilities:** constriction, the Big Squeeze, a never-used swallow-whole ability, shedding his skin, hoop-rolling, burrowing.
- **End:** consumed into the Hunger's biomass. His last thought was his unfinished website. He calls himself "Ruby Red" in Dried Blood. Pronouns vary between he and they.

### [[Episodes/Season 5/111 - S5E13 — Bone Rust — Ruby Rust v Bone Daddy|S5E13 — Bone Rust — Ruby Rust v Bone Daddy]]
(Robert Hartley). A massive serpent with crimson/ruby, diamond-shaped scales. Wears a **helmet holding two drinks, poison and java**.
- **History:**
  - Once a snake, then a programmer ("a Python… software development", "IT") who became sentient.
  - Died in an earlier fight: "devoured whole by some sort of hunger."
  - Says he had the Shield of the Golden King before (with no arms to hold it).
- **In this episode:**
  - Returned glitching and assimilated into the hunger: tentacles, inside-out head, eyes erupting from scales, a humanoid hand, bat-wing and porcelain-doll arms. He can assimilate others and share their senses.
  - Begged to die until the Lord of Fate promised to restore him if he won.
  - Traded a patron's remaining life to the Box of Chaos.
  - **Killed Bone Daddy, then turned on Thea and the Lord of Fate.**
  - Was reduced by Thea's chinchilla to a non-sentient python and released into the Void: "Just hunting for mice."
