## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 3/061 - S3E14 — Magic Duel — Talbot Cage v Seth Kaiser|S3E14 — Magic Duel — Talbot Cage v Seth Kaiser]]
(Rogue / rogueexmachina). A self-described **duelist** who traveled "across dimensions" seeking a challenge.
- White studded coat with a big **K** logo; mossy mullet/K-pop hair; perfect posture.
- **His rune buckler holds his card deck**, from which he "plays" spells:
  - Pot of Greed
  - Summon Fey (the "pickle goat" deer)
  - face-down "trap" cards
  - Phantasmal Force
  - Benign Transposition
  - Summon Draconic Spirit (**a white, blue-eyed, turtle-shelled dragon, his "most loyal companion,"** which returns into his cards)
  - Silvery Barbs
- **Card collector:** sleeve protectors, a trade binder, a Holographic Charizard. **He gave his whole binder to Gerald and became Gerald's friend.**
- Athletics −1 ("I play children's card games"). "I don't need friends. I have card games."
- **Used a wish (from Gerald's ring-of-three-wishes card) to learn the riddle's answer.** He won, and the GM then blew him up with the ticking pumpkin (if he was the recipient).
