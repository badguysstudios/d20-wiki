### [[Episodes/Season 1/016 - S1E7 — Wild Magic — Strako v Zayliss|S1E7 — Wild Magic — Strako v Zayliss]]
(NyteTide). A 7'5" wild magic barbarian.
- **Gear:** an axe he loves ("my baby"), called a great axe at 0:15:04 and later a battleaxe used with a shield, plus a shield.
- **Wild magic seen:**
  - the exploding fairy, which rose from his chest twice
  - magical-hand and light-bolt backlash
  - the "Care Bear" light that blinds
  - a bolstering rune that peels off the axe into his chest
- Uses Shield Master shoves.
- **Lore:**
  - Loves puppies; fought "in honor of my fallen boy."
  - Feared he'd been "once again captured by the Witchlight Carnival."
  - Expects Valhalla and to "kill all those gods."
  - Mentions "you may have heard of me."
  - Killed Timothy, mayor of the Worm's Town, through tag. Wants revenge on the fairy.
- **In this episode:** he was tagged by Doug, shoved a whole asteroid with his hero point, and won by cleaving Zayliss. The Grandmaster carried him hand in hand to the fans.
