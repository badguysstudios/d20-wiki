## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 4/092 - S4E12 — Racoons — Sam Cletus v Ben Zappa|S4E12 — Racoons — Sam Cletus v Ben Zappa]]
(Stake).
- **Who he is:**
  - **A wood elf from the deep woods** (a swamp region), **217 years old**, **one of Meemaw's 27 kids** (or grandkids).
  - Named after his father (Sam) and his great-grandpappy (Cletus).
  - Drinks every day, from a Starbucks Hydro Flask ("made of magic") that replaced his clay pots after an assault charge.
  - No shoes until 17. Hasn't paid taxes in 17 years. Writes Space Jam fan fiction.
  - Has a cousin Darla. Chews meth and does coke; got into meth through Meemaw's cookies.
  - Fights for prize money to cure Meemaw.
- **How he fights:** a self-described bad drunken monk with Tipsy Sway, Deflect Missiles (with his bottle), Silvery Barbs ("pocket sand"), quivering palm, Stunning Strike and a sawed-off shotgun.
- **What happened to him this episode:**
  - The aliens gave him +2 Intelligence.
  - He fused with Dolores in the Box of Chaos to become Deletus, a wood-elf-sized were-coon, then got inverted (raccoon face, human teeth, raccoon penis).
  - The genie turned the penis into a raccoon vagina, and he became **Samantha Cletus**.
  - Flattened by the Jolly Chimp inside the box. He swore he'd come back for Ben "for life."
