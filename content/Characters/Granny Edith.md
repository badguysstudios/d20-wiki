## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 5/102 - S5E4 — Chaos in Chinchilla Forest — Tormiir v Granny Edith|S5E4 — Chaos in Chinchilla Forest — Tormiir v Granny Edith]]
(guest). A tiny old lady baker.
- **Looks and gear:** gray curls, Coke-bottle glasses, a floury apron full of spoons and whisks, **a rolling pin as big as she is**, a giant teacup on her belt, a hip flask, and an electric beater with an arcane battery pack.
- **Signature move: Granny's Cookies** (throwing the spinning rolling pin; running on top of it).
- **Family:**
  - Her grandpappy lived to 200.
  - She has 79 great-great-great-grandchildren.
  - She grew up on her great-grandpappy's farm "up north."
  - **Gerald** is "waiting for" her in death (relationship unstated).
- **History:** a girl of 17 in "1725." Lived through five major world conflicts.
- **The Box of Chaos** awoke her yellow-eyed inner monster (doubled nat 20 effects).
- **Sayings:** "stressed is desserts spelled backwards," "all you need is loaves," "where there's a whisk, there's a way," "life is what you bake it."
- Favorite food: her great-grandma's bread and butter pudding.

### [[Episodes/Season 5/106 - S5E8 — Graveyard Grudge — Granny Edith v Teddy Oso|S5E8 — Graveyard Grudge — Granny Edith v Teddy Oso]]
(Luboffin). A returning champion, called "the favorite of all favorites."
- **History:**
  - She expected to "rest after the last fight and see Gerald." The Lord of Fate says she died last time. Her signature move hasn't hit "once in two fights."
  - Her late husband **Gerald** died "many years ago." They met on a train, went dancing every week, and had "a long and lovely life together." She carries a photo of him.
  - **69 great great grandchildren** (canon per Dried Blood). About eight children, unless she forgot Harry.
  - Her great grandma's **bread and butter pudding** is her favorite snack. **Her mother sang her a lullaby** as a child.
  - She was **a jump rope champion** in her youth. She uses puns her great great granddaughter taught her.
- **Kit:** apron of baking tools.
  - Fly swat.
  - **Magical rolling pin that glows with runes.**
  - Magic frying pan, knife, goggle glasses, giant grater.
  - Hard raisin cookies "for people I don't like" and chocolate chip cookies.
  - Bladed hand mixer, broom, spectacles, rotten eggs, bread.
- **Signature move:** "Granny's Cookies" (flatten like a cookie). It missed twice.
- **Personality:** kind to everyone, especially Thea and even the Box, until her temper breaks.
- **In this episode:** won by knocking Teddy into the lava with a boulder loaf.
