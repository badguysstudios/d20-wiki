**Claude** is the janitor of [[The Dread Fortress]].

- A small man with a tall blue hat (slightly askew), a bushy beard and tiny feet. He is French and only says "huh huh."
- [[Thea]] is convinced he's an evil wizard (and the office lunch thief). He's the villain of her *Thea Teaches* segments, and she once took his arms off ("I'll give them back").
- He plays chess with the Grandmaster and is better at it than the Arbiter. She sends him back to clean the toilet when she loses.
- Nobody has had him take "the magic test."
