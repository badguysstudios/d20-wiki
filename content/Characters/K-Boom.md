**K-Boom** (also known, he insists, across the different realms as Kyle Boomishnow and Kaboom) is a gnome artificer who loves explosives, cigarettes, cryptocurrency and, as he realized halfway through his own entrance, arson. He came to [[The Void]] to prove that his K-Boom Coin would be "the best cryptocurrency that the realm has ever seen" by winning a Deathmatch against [[Ignacio Marchade]]. He didn't win. Neither did anyone else. Along the way he set himself on fire, tried to buy the [[The Box of Chaos|Box of Chaos]], turned into a gorilla out of misplaced sympathy, and gave one of the arena's most memorable death-scene sales pitches.

## Story

K-Boom arrived through the Arbiter's portal in [[The Dread Fortress]] and greeted his hosts as "Master Chief and Arbiter." He immediately went after Ignacio's winged monkey, Mort, as "copyright infringement just waiting to happen." In the arena, a half-frozen lake beneath snowy mountains, he pulled out a microphone and a homemade spotlight and delivered a stirring independence-day speech for "the 14th of August," ending with "K-Boom coin on sale now." [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|The Grandmaster]] wept and blessed both champions. When [[The Arbiter]] told him he had what it took to be president, K-Boom said his father used to say the same thing "before he died tragically from a house fire that I caused accidentally."

The fight began badly and got stranger. His first fire bolt missed, but a shoulder-mounted rocket launcher of his own making peppered Ignacio with magic missiles. He was proud of it: "built in my basement after my father died… before I delved into crypto." When the Box of Chaos came into play, he offered it one K-Boom Coin, "one of the first in existence," supposedly worth 50,000 gold. [[The Box of Chaos|Gerald]] offered to buy four. K-Boom said he wanted a cool item, not gold. His pitch then collapsed so badly that something inside the box locked the lid and slid a deadbolt across it. Moments later, standing far too close, he hurled a vial, flicked his cigarette after it, and caught himself in his own fireball. He lost his eyebrows but kept the mustache, which "grows back faster." The lake turned out to have healing magic, so he dunked himself and tried to convince Ignacio the water burned. It didn't work.

K-Boom then went for the dirty tricks. He flicked a switch on his chest, and his pauldrons opened to release a swarm of magical daggers around him. After a burst of blinking away and charging back in, he chugged an energy drink, smashed the can on his forehead, grabbed Ignacio's thighs underwater and sent lightning through the lake. Both men wore metal, so both got shocked. When Ignacio skewered him full of holes, K-Boom remembered his father's wisdom, "when in doubt… return to monkey," and drank a brown vial that turned him into a giant ape. He had done it, he later explained, to help Ignacio reminisce about the monkey he'd sacrificed to the box. As the ape he smashed Ignacio's pinky toe and cracked the lakebed. Then two javelins to the chest broke the spell, and he shrank back into "your typical, now eyebrowless, K-Boom." Ignacio's next lance strike landed squarely in the nuts.

When the Arbiter unleashed a water weird from the deep pit in the middle of the lake, the two rivals called a truce: "Enemy of my enemy is my friend." It lasted about one round. K-Boom gave Ignacio a half-hearted thumbs up, ran, and tossed two vials behind his back for a fireball that took Ignacio down. He then slid down the rocks to cradle his burning rival, promising he'd be remembered thanks to a new product line, "NF trinkets," starting with a stick-figure Ignacio card. With his dying breath, Ignacio renamed him **K-Boom Marchand**. K-Boom dropped him back in the water and turned to pitch the crowd. Mid-spiel, the water weird's tail wrapped around both champions and dragged them into the black water. **Both died, and the match had no winner.** The Grandmaster stamped all over his NFT trinkets and ordered the Arbiter to dive in and recover the bodies.

## Personality and Abilities

K-Boom stands 3'2" on a good day ("the doctors say I'm like three foot one and a half, but I round up… for the ladies"). He wears a long robe covered in trinkets, vials and gears over plate armor, and he always has a cigarette going. He calls himself a sleazy salesman, and he never stops selling: even dying, he was reciting the coin's web address. He kept calling the Grandmaster "Master Chief," and he corrected her about her own crypto: "That is Doug coin. Doge coin is completely different." The [[The Acolytes|Acolytes]] booed him for it. In the after-show he explained that he "never had a lot of love when he was a child." He had lost his family young to "an arson accident that is definitely not my fault legally," and he makes up for it by getting people to fall in love with him and buy his merchandise.

His arsenal is all homemade: pauldrons that release a cloud of daggers, a Predator-style shoulder rocket that fires magic missiles, fireballs made from a vial and a well-thrown cigarette, and a brown vial that turns him into a giant ape, "K-Konga, the Crypto Monkey." He can also cast spells in the middle of a melee, and he fights dirty, cleverly and usually far too close to his own explosions.

## Relationships

- [[Ignacio Marchade]]: His opponent, punching bag, brief ally and, by the end, something like a tragic soulmate. Ignacio died declaring "I love you, and I always have, even though you killed me."
- [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer]]: She wept at his speech, but never got him to call her by her real name, and she trampled his NFTs after he died.
- [[The Arbiter]]: He encouraged K-Boom to use his father's death as fuel. Afterward he fished K-Boom's body out of the lake.
- [[The Box of Chaos]]: The first champion to name his price to the box, and the first to have it lock its door on him.
- [[Doug the Pug]]: The Grandmaster's pug and the face of "Doug coin," the cryptocurrency K-Boom insisted was not the one that ruled the Void.
