## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 2/030 - S2E7 - Hand to Hand Combat, Bargo v Carn|S2E7 - Hand to Hand Combat, Bargo v Carn]]
(Chunkerrific). The full name "Bargo Blakely" is used only in the Arbiter's introduction.
- **Speech and schooling:**
  - Speaks in the third person with a "loose grasp of grammar."
  - **Went to "fighting school, not grammar school"** and graduated with an **"Apple Plus,"** the best grade on the fighting school's fruit scale (cucumber worst, orange, pineapple, apple best).
- **Family:** "Bargo" is a family name, and he is "**the 17th Bargo**."
- **Gear:** a cape, short sword, dagger, and ball bearings in his backpack.
- **Abilities used:** Gust, Shield (cast four times), Grease, Fire Bolt, Misty Step, Magic Missile, Uncanny Dodge.
- **In this episode:**
  - Killed the minitaurs, including one that idolized him after his cape flourish.
  - Ate nothing, but was tempted by the floral smell of the slowing mushroom.
  - Won the Acolytes' vote, decapitated Carn, and was told he has "graduated from this arena."
