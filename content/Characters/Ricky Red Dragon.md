**Ricky Red Dragon** was the champion of the Season 4 finale, a man in sunglasses, flip-flops and no pants who insisted he was a mighty red dragon trapped in human flesh. He fought the angsty elf [[Traxton]], better known as Spider, in a forest clearing [[The Shadow King]] had been building as a festival venue. He played dead, juggled fake beards and blew up his opponent's newly dropped testicles. He went down as one of the great bluffers of the arena, because his last words were a confession: "I'm not really a dragon."

## Story

Ricky stepped through [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]]'s blood portal into the Shadow King's throne room in [[The Dread Fortress]]. He walked straight into a private techno pre-party and a crowd of freshly shaved [[Goblins|goblins]]. He explained that he had **partied too hard at a techno concert** the week before and been cursed into a squishy human "mortal coil." He wanted his scales back, and the Grandmaster declared that "absolutely doable" as his prize. He even shared a bonding moment with the Shadow King, who had "had skin and then didn't have skin and now ha[s] skin again." The moment soured once the King agreed with Spider that Ricky didn't look much like a dragon.

In the arena, Ricky opened by teleporting into a tree for the high ground and, having no wings, **T-posed to assert dominance**. It worked well enough to unnerve Spider, right up until Spider cast Darkness on him. Ricky, who is **terrified of the dark**, fell out of the tree backside-first. He spent his crowd-given boost on a Banishment fueled by "all of my butthurt anger," only to lose a duel of counterspells. He fought back with a zigzagging clip-art Fire Bolt and a joy-buzzer handshake that crackled with lightning. Between attacks he handed Spider his entire bin of wintergreen mints for his bad breath. A glowing portal circle armored him in purple, at the cost of opening each turn with a lyric from an annoying song: "Friday" first, then "Sweet Caroline" with the whole crowd singing along.

[[The Box of Chaos]] made him stronger. Ricky fed it one of the unwashed spare beards he kept in his pockets, and it gave him **the beards of many things**. He fastened the white one over his existing beard and felt "like a warm summer's day," growing vastly more experienced and harder to hit. Then Spider's gift from the box, a phallic rod holding a single Fireball, met Ricky's own Fireball tip to tip. **Ricky rolled a perfect 20, the fireballs touched, and the explosion caught them both.** Ricky took the full blast and was left barely alive. He collapsed and played dead, tongue hanging out ("People die at parties all the time"). He secretly healed himself by sipping from one of the healing rods that sprouted from the scorched ground. He conjured an illusion of the Acolyte Ristin to assure Spider the body was dead. When Spider stood over him flexing, Ricky cast Wall of Force straight up between his legs and launched him twenty feet into the air.

He crawled back to the Box, dived face-first into it, and came out with a +3 Christmas nutcracker maul. He never got to swing it. Spider unleashed a Destructive Wave of unprocessed teenage angst, and Ricky crumbled to dust. His parting wisdom to Spider was "All that angst you have built up, it never goes away." His last words went to the Shadow King: "I'm not really a dragon." The furious Grandmaster threw a popcorn bowl full of rocks at his remains. The Shadow King sniffed that he had known all along: "Dragons don't have to tell you they're dragons. You just know."

## Personality and Abilities

Ricky was pompous, cheerful and endlessly talkative. He was a self-described "dragon that goes to raves" who claimed to be 6,012 years old and kept a fake beard on to prove it. He wore **sunglasses from Costco**, flip-flops and a top with pockets, but no pants. He was also colorblind, which made his nutcracker's red-and-blue paint job a matter of faith. He spoke in bro-isms ("If you build it, they will come, bro"), offered to share his life story, claimed that words hurt his feelings, and mentioned a political career he hoped to uphold "one day." Whether his borrowed body had a cloaca remained, legally, a question he could not answer.

In a fight he relied on spells and misdirection rather than muscle. His tools included Misty Step, Banishment, Counterspell, Fire Bolt, Shocking Grasp, Fireball, Minor Illusion and Wall of Force. His best weapon was nerve: he kept needling the Grandmaster about her arena's dial-up Wi-Fi, and his fake death fooled Spider completely. At the table, his player admitted he had kept escalating the jokes because he expected a thunderbolt any moment. The Grandmaster later explained that insulting her was fine, but insulting her arena was not.

## Relationships

- [[Traxton]]: His opponent, the 45-year-old "teenage" elf Spider. Ricky came to like him ("He's growing on me. Do I have to kill him?") and was "part of this man's awakening" before Spider killed him.
- [[The Shadow King]]: Bonded with Ricky over life without flesh, then sided with Spider in mocking him. He ended up on the receiving end of Ricky's dying confession.
- [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer]]: Promised him his scales back. He spent the match baiting her, and she pelted his dust with a bowl of rocks.
- [[The Box of Chaos]]: The tormented soul inside traded him the beards of many things for a dirty beard, then gave up a nutcracker maul after Ricky dived in face-first.
