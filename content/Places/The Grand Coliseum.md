**The Grand Coliseum** is the arena the Grandmaster chose to close out Season 1. For this finale she took her champions to "no far off location" and picked something almost shockingly ordinary: a classic coliseum under the night sky, ringed by stone columns and roaring [[The Acolytes|Acolytes]]. It didn't stay ordinary for long. By the end of the finale one of its walls was gone.

## Description

Champions who stepped through the portal were met by a cool evening breeze and a sight that, by Deathmatch standards, was almost boring. It looked like "a very mundane, standard… battle arena. Almost like a coliseum." There was only open sky, starlight and stone. Columns stood around the fighting ground, and a quick-thinking champion could duck behind one for cover or use it as a launch point. The Acolytes encircled the arena to watch.

The Grandmaster introduced it this way:

> "For this ending, I take you to no far off location… this is our grand coliseum in the cool of night with the stars overlooking your grand battle."
> — Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer

The Coliseum has two named gates. The **Northern Gate** kept a turkey dragon shut away behind three physical locks. The **Southern Gate** was held by none other than [[Doug the Pug]]. Somewhere in the Fortress there is also "the front door."

## History

The Grand Coliseum hosted [[Episodes/Season 1/023 - S1E14 — Eyes of Chaos — Lucien v Monteclaire (Season 1 finale)|S1E14 — Eyes of Chaos — Lucien v Monteclaire (Season 1 finale)]], the last fight of Season 1, between [[Lucien]] and Monteclaire. The Grandmaster said she was taking the champions to "no far off location": a grand, simple stage for a grand finale.

The simplicity didn't survive the fight. Before the finale was over, the Tarrasque had destroyed the Coliseum's **western wall**, which made the arena's "mundane" first impression rather ironic.

## Notable Figures

- [[Lucien]]: one of the two champions in the Season 1 finale fought here.
- [[Doug the Pug]]: held the Southern Gate.
- [[The Acolytes]]: filled the stands around the arena to watch the finale.
