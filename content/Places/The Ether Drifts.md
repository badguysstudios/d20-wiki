**The Ether Drifts** are a region of asteroids drifting in open space in [[The Void]].

- **Wormtown:** a tiny underground civilization of multicolored worm people lives on an orange asteroid there. Its mayor, **Mayor Timothy**, was killed by Strako in the Wild Magic asteroid round (S1E7).
- **Season 6:** the *Ether Drifts* one-shots (Parts 1–2) took place here.
- **Season 7:** Akumu v Jinx (S7E3) was fought on the orange asteroid, and tiny worms dragged Jinx's body underground. In Agatha v Lug (S7E7), Agatha was spiked into a crater holding the worm town and crushed 60 worms on the way down. Lug wants to build condos on their land.
- Something innocent dies here every time.
