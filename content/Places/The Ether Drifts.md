**The Ether Drifts** are a belt of asteroids at the far edge of [[The Void]], where the wreckage of lost worlds floats aimlessly in the shadow of a huge purple gas planet. They have hosted a mining mission, a three-way Deathmatch and more than one brawl on the same small orange rock. That rock happens to be home to a town of tiny worm people who keep getting caught in the crossfire. Something innocent dies here every time.

## Description

The Ether Drifts lie "at the edge of all of void space, home to a wreckage of worlds and locations and asteroids that float around aimlessly." Large orange asteroids hang in the shadow of a vast, gaseous purple planet. Meteorites streak through the sky and crash down without warning, and the ground is pitted with craters. Gravity is lower here than at [[The Dread Fortress]], though, as [[The Arbiter]] admitted, "We never really talked about what the gravity is at the Dread Fortress, but it's different here."

The Drifts have at least one mine, and one orange asteroid is home to **Wormtown**, a tiny underground civilization of multicolored worm people living in a crater. Wildlife is not what anyone expects in deep space. When a slightly adorable deformed raccoon named Jimothy crawled out of a crater, the Arbiter protested that "you don't see a lot of raccoons in the Ether Drifts." The Grandmaster replied, "Well, you don't go around the Ether Drift very often, do you?"

When [[The Acolytes]] come to watch, they sit in floating grandstands. On one occasion these were zero-gravity stands, and the Acolytes wore helmets with tubes so they could still get their beers inside. ("Don't worry about it. It works. It's mechanically sound.")

## History

### Wormtown's first tragedy

The worm people's troubles began in the Wild Magic asteroid round of Season 1, Episode 7. That round was fought on the orange asteroid, and [[Strako]] killed Wormtown's mayor, [[Timothy|Mayor Timothy]].

### The Ether Drifts one-shots

In the Season of the Trials, the Arbiter lost contact with "miners, I guess" working deep in the Ether Drifts. They had been gathering "a certain mineral… integral to the fight against [[The Shadow King]]." In [[Episodes/Season 6/118 - S6 Ep2 · Ether Drifts Part 1 · TTRPG One Shot (Team A mine mission)|Ether Drifts Part 1]], he clapped his hands and portalled a team of champions to the edge of a mine on the large orange asteroids. Their job was to find out what had happened and bring the mineral back. A meteor shower greeted them on arrival, with meteorites crashing down all around, but everyone kept their footing while the Acolytes watched from a giant grandstand.

In Ether Drifts Part 2, the Arbiter's sound wave opened another portal to the Drifts for Team A's Deathmatch. [[Atlas]], [[Robbie Clark and the Hunger]] and [[Copycat]] fought on an asteroid beside the gas planet, in the lighter gravity, while the Acolytes looked on from a floating grandstand.

### Season of Vigilance

The orange asteroid came back in Season 7 for [[Akumu]] versus [[Jinx]]. After the fight, tiny worms emerged and dragged Jinx's body underground.

The Drifts returned again for [[Agatha Middlebrooke]] versus [[Lug Hardblock]]. [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|The Grandmaster]] sent them off with a simple instruction:

> "Whatever you do, make sure you're the only one who leaves this planet alive."

She then put on her spatial helmet and flew up to join the Acolytes. Jimothy the raccoon made his appearance during the fight. When the Arbiter asked what he was doing on an asteroid in space, the Grandmaster said, "Don't ask questions. Jimothy goes where Jimothy wants."

The worms fared worse than usual. Agatha was spiked into the crater that holds their town and crushed sixty of them on the way down. Lug, for his part, wants to build condos on their land. Agatha and Lug ended the fight as co-champions, and the worms were left to count their losses once again.
