**The bloodfalls structure** is a building on a cliffside, and it is exactly as cheerful as the name suggests. It appeared in [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/042 - Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover|Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover]]. Somewhere inside, a plant held an ewer. There were four ways to approach the place.

## Description

The structure sits on a cliff, and blood runs through it. Channels of blood pass beneath grates in the building. Inside is the plant that held the ewer.

There are four known approaches:

- **The front tunnel**
- **The blood channel**
- **The cliff**
- **A secret cave tunnel:** a hidden way in.

## History

The bloodfalls structure appeared in [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/042 - Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover|Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover]]. There, the plant holding the ewer was inside, and the four approaches offered a choice of front tunnel, blood channel, cliff or secret cave tunnel.
