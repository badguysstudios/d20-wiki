**The bloodfalls structure** is a building set into a cliffside, and it is exactly as cheerful as the name suggests. It appeared in [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/042 - Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover|Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover]]. Its prize was an ewer, held by a plant somewhere inside. Anyone who wanted that ewer first had to choose how to get in, and none of the choices were pleasant.

## Description

The structure clings to a cliff, and blood runs through it. Channels of it pass beneath grates in the building, so visitors walk over the flow rather than through it, at least on the floors that have grates. Inside is the plant that held the ewer, the object that made the place worth visiting at all.

There are four known ways in, each with its own flavor of misery:

- **The front tunnel:** the obvious route, for those who like a door.
- **The blood channel:** the route through the blood itself.
- **The cliff:** straight up the outside of the building.
- **A secret cave tunnel:** a hidden way in, for anyone who could find it.

## History

The bloodfalls structure appeared in [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/042 - Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover|Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover]]. There it stood as a place to be reached and broken into: the ewer was inside, guarded by the plant, and the four approaches gave anyone after it a choice of front door, blood, cliff face or secret passage.
