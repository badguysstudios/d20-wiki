## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Season 5/106 - S5E8 — Graveyard Grudge — Granny Edith v Teddy Oso|S5E8 — Graveyard Grudge — Granny Edith v Teddy Oso]]
a moonlit graveyard of tombstones with a giant mausoleum, an angel statue and a full moon. Skeletal hands rise from the ground. Thea's favorite spooky place, with no echo; "came pre-decorated." It was flooded with lava in round 8, then cooled.

### [[Episodes/Season 6/120 - S6 Ep4 — Tip to Tip - Part 1 (TTRPG One Shot)|S6 Ep4 — Tip to Tip - Part 1 (TTRPG One Shot)]]
(in the Shadowlands): the Skelman family plot, with a graveyard, astroturf over a grave, the angel statue and plaque, the stairwell, a three-door trapped corridor, and the grandfather's one-door chamber. Many imps reportedly come by.

### [[Episodes/Season 7/132 - S7E4 — The Cheese — Sila v Ol' Pumpernicklejack|S7E4 — The Cheese — Sila v Ol' Pumpernicklejack]]
a huge moonlit graveyard with fog, well-fertilized grass, a stone perimeter fence, shallow graves (below the water table), champion graves and unmarked graves. Turned to lava and left "covered in lava and corpses."
