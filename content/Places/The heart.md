**The heart**, also called the outlander cavern, is a vast hidden city built inside the hollow shell of an enormous soul gem. You can't find it by walking. You have to ask the sand to take you there. It first appeared in [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/093 - Into the Mirror 17, The Way Out|Into the Mirror 17, The Way Out]], when [[Sky]] led an army beneath the dunes and into one of the strangest homes anyone in the story had seen.

## Description

From the inside, the heart looks like a giant crystal that someone carefully scooped out. The cavern glitters with green and blue gems and crystals in every direction, and visitors described it as being "inside a giant hollow crystal." It is no ordinary rock. It is a colossal hollowed soul gem, "the remnants of a vast soul" that still holds "a lot of unspent time." Soul gems are said to grow larger the more life their owner had left when they died, so a gem big enough to hold a city is an unsettling thought.

Hundreds of thousands of people live there. Their stilt houses rise from the cavern floor, connected by pulley elevators that haul residents up and down the glittering walls. The people make their living mining the crystal around them. That is a trade with an expiry date, because the mining can only go so far before the whole place collapses.

Among the landmarks are Sky's carved hall and Cyrus's shop. A small cave also holds a polished patch of soul gem, smoothed until it works as a reflective surface.

## History

The heart was revealed in Into the Mirror 17. Out in the green blowing sand under the celestial sky, Sky halted the army she was leading and whispered three words. Most of the company missed them, but [[Flynry]] caught them clearly: **"Take me to the heart."**

This was no portal. Instead the sand cracked, parted and fell away into a staircase leading down into a chasm. At the bottom, an archway opened onto the enormous gem-lit cavern, with its stilt houses, pulley elevators and hundreds of thousands of crystal miners.

## Notable Figures

- [[Sky]]: knows the words that open the way to the heart, and has a carved hall there.
- Cyrus: keeps a shop in the cavern.
