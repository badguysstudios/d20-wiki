**Yord** is the home world of the adventurers [[Asena]] and [[John Carmichael]], a place with a 1930s flavor where, in the words of the scholar [[Lawrence Booker Green]], everything "runs on pulp logic." It lies far beyond [[The Void]], and it was dragged into the Void's affairs when [[The Arbiter]] reached across dimensions and took its most notorious villain, [[Ralzamon]], Master of the Unknown, for his own scheme. Getting Ralzamon dealt with and getting back home to Yord drove much of the heroes' adventure in the Fate of the Faithful crossover.

## Description

Yord is a world of jungle expeditions, ancient tombs and psychic masterminds, where the dramatic choice tends to be the right one. Its known landmarks include the **Zolpian Jungle**, the tomb of **Santa Polka**, and the **Crystalline Cavern**, where Ralzamon's psyche was stored in the moments before his final defeat.

Pulp logic is not just a style in Yord. It seems to be how the place works. When Asena needed to give Lawrence some of her blood for a divination anchor, she slashed her palm "like they do in the movies" because, as she put it, "The blood sounds way cooler." Lawrence pointed out that she could have used the forearm, then let it go with a shrug: "the world of Yord runs on pulp logic."

## History

Long before the Void came into it, Yord was where Asena's story with Ralzamon began. Ralzamon was once simply **Josiah Parsons**, a man on an ill-fated expedition to the jungle that changed Asena's life forever and took his. He returned as Ralzamon, Master of the Unknown, a psychic mastermind, and he became the nemesis of Asena and John. Asena also once took a crown from Santa Polka's tomb, during what she described as "a little dreamscape thingy… it was like a dream, but we were also like really there."

In [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/043 - Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 4 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 4 (A Crossover)]], the story of how Yord lost its villain came out. Ralzamon had been "plucked from the world of Yord right before his final defeat at the hands of Asena and John Carmichael," made the newest recruit of the Children of the Void, and put to use by the Arbiter, who wanted his psychic powers to spy on the Faithful. Lawrence pieced together what had happened. When Asena had fought in the Void, [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] had sent her home as a champion, so she left no echo behind. The Arbiter, however, had kept an echo of Ralzamon, and when he grabbed both versions at the moment of death, he fused them into one being. Lawrence reasoned that the part of that fusion that "still originally belongs in Yord" might be the thread that could carry the heroes home, though he admitted the idea was "completely unproven." Asena's blood became one of his anchors for the attempt. As for the crown, Lawrence set that question aside as "a mystery for another day… after we get back to Yord."

In [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/044 - Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 5 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 5 (A Crossover)]], Asena, John and their ally [[Lyrus]] finally cornered Ralzamon. John's battle speech sent him off in Yord's honor:

> **John Carmichael:** "You've become that which you hate, Parson, and I'm going to destroy you. So just lie back, think of Yord, and just know that your spirit will return there soon."

As Asena raised her fist again, she no longer saw Ralzamon. She saw the man from the photograph, Josiah Parsons. He begged her to stop, warning that "the one who binds me" would come looking for her if she killed him. He told her that she and John were fighting to protect "my world, Yord," and asked to "die alone and leave it out of his sight." When Asena asked whether he still thought she was the downfall of Yord, his answer was grim: "Things I've seen are far worse than anything you might be." Asena let him fall into a pit with a shadowy thing. She reported that they had "killed half of Ralzamon," and John came to believe that the remaining half would act as a kind of double agent, fouling the Arbiter's operations and keeping his gaze off Yord. Lyrus accepted it: "Well, the enemy of my enemy, I guess."

Getting home took a feat Lawrence had never managed before, leaving the Void. He worked a spell matrix from the collar's emotional link to Asena and Josiah Parsons, together with a tuning fork attuned to Yord. Before they left, John worried that things dying in Yord might find their way into the Void. Lyrus only winked and promised to help with "your cleanup" once the Arbiter was dealt with. This time Asena was not "forcibly dragged along in the wake of the Arbiter's terrible hand" but "pulled back by your own memories." The heroes hurtled out of the Void and back to Yord, landing in the heart of the Crystalline Cavern at the very moment before their old victory. Perhaps at long last, they would never have to fight Ralzamon again.

## Notable Figures

- [[Asena]]: native adventurer and Ralzamon's longtime foe, who once took a crown from Santa Polka's tomb.
- [[John Carmichael]]: Asena's partner in the fight against Ralzamon.
- [[Lawrence Booker Green]]: the scholar who worked out the spell that brought the heroes home.
- [[Ralzamon]]: Master of the Unknown, born Josiah Parsons, Yord's psychic mastermind, taken by the Arbiter just before his final defeat.
