**Mortaurios**, the City of the Dead, has no road leading to it. It cannot be found on foot, however long a traveler wanders the desert. The only way in is to say its name out loud in the wasteland, and the wasteland takes you there. Behind its walls is a bustling city that seems almost ordinary: busy markets, paved streets, and people who know their place. The eerie part is who isn't there.

## Description

Mortaurios is a walled city built on a trapezoidal platform several miles across, rising out of the desert. Its streets are paved, and its bazaars are packed with crowds. A clear social hierarchy runs through the place, and everyone in it seems to have a rank.

For all the bustle, something is missing. There are no children anywhere in Mortaurios, and almost no wildlife. In a city of the dead, that silence may be the most telling detail of all.

## History

The way to Mortaurios came as a simple instruction: **"You need only speak the name in this desert, in this wasteland, and it will take you there."** Because the name itself was the key, it had to be handled carefully. [[Elianna]] gestured for it to be written down rather than spoken, so that no one could say it and slip off to the city alone.

[[Cerys]] wrote the name in the book and passed on the last piece of advice:

> "Whisper that into the wind."

The name was spoken, and the travelers stepped into the city at last. This happened in [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/074 - Into the Mirror 10 Shatter + Into the Mirror 11 Mortaurios (ITM session 3)|Into the Mirror 10 Shatter + Into the Mirror 11 Mortaurios (ITM session 3)]].
