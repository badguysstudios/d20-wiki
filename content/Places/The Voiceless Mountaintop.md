**The Voiceless Mountaintop** is a cold, windy peak beneath a blood-red sunset, chosen as the arena for [[Episodes/Season 5/101 - S5E3, Eldritch Fangs - Ruby Rust v Robbie Clark and the Hunger|S5E3, Eldritch Fangs - Ruby Rust v Robbie Clark and the Hunger]]. It owes its grand and slightly baffling name to [[Thea]], who picked it herself. It did not stay snowy for long, and by the end of the night it was a little shorter than when it started.

## Description

The mountaintop is a snowy summit battered by wind, lit by a red sunset. The [[The Acolytes|Acolytes]] watched from an arena floating above the peak, looking down on the champions as they fought on the frozen ground below.

The place is not as permanent as a mountain ought to be. When the sun reversed during the fight, the snowy peak turned into a desert. It is also rented land, a detail that mattered once the damage began.

## History

Thea announced that she would take the champions to "this really cool" place called the Voiceless Mountaintop, and she insisted that [[The Arbiter|the Lord of Fate]] repeat the name back to her. He was not impressed.

> **The Lord of Fate:** "Can you name me one mountain that has a voice? … Did you name this location?"
> **Thea:** "I… may have?"

The Voiceless Mountaintop was not her first idea. Thea had already rejected "the mountain," "a really big hill," "cold nipples" and "the hard nipple place" before settling on the name that stuck.

[[Ruby Rust]] and [[Robbie Clark and the Hunger]] fought there. The peak changed under their feet when the sun reversed and the snow gave way to desert. Then [[The Hunger|the Hunger]] drained the land itself, and the mountaintop **shrank two inches**. This was an awkward thing to do to a property that was only being rented.
