## Appearances

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/071 - Into the Mirror 7–9 - Claws of Sand, Shifting Sands, Hourglass (session 2)|Into the Mirror 7–9 - Claws of Sand, Shifting Sands, Hourglass (session 2)]]
a realm of the dead apart from other underworlds. Only souls attached to or consumed by it come here.
  - Cold blue sand like glass shards, a howling wind, a celestial "void" sky.
  - No sun or star movement; bodies stay in stasis.
  - The dragon bones where the party stands.
  - "Your experience here is what your mind makes it."

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/074 - Into the Mirror 10 Shatter + Into the Mirror 11 Mortaurios (ITM session 3)|Into the Mirror 10 Shatter + Into the Mirror 11 Mortaurios (ITM session 3)]]
where the dead from the Void arrive. **Death here is final; you become sand.**

### [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/088 - Into the Mirror 15–16, Old Friends - Sky|Into the Mirror 15–16, Old Friends - Sky]]
- Once adjacent to the Living Plane, with doors and trade.
  - Cut off about 2000 years ago; reachable only through mirrors since.
  - Has multiple cities and cultures, with outlanders roaming.
  - The area outside the library dampens some magic.
