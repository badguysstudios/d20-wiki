**The tower in the in-between** is the home of [[Fil]], the God of Fate. Fil described it in a single line that sounds like either a welcome or a warning: "Here is my home. It is in between all." [[The Arbiter|Vythorn]] found it during his lonely wanderings in [[Episodes/Vythorn's Return/095 - S4E14, Vythorn's Return Part 1 (Vythorn alone in the in-between; the God of Fate)|S4E14, Vythorn's Return Part 1 (Vythorn alone in the in-between; the God of Fate)]]. It is cozy, quiet and strange in all the ways a god's house probably should be.

## Description

The tower stands on a carved-out spit of land. The grass around it is undisturbed, as if nobody had ever walked on it. A cart sits outside, two torches burn, and seven lanterns glow in the windows, matching the lanterns on the boat. The front door is wooden and has no handle, so getting in is apparently up to the house and not the guest.

Rising up through the tower is a massive young tree with purple branches, flowers and brightly colored leaves. It looks less like something planted there and more like the building was simply built around it.

The top room is warm and lived-in. It has a hearth and a cauldron, and an ornate ceiling hung with lanterns. Shelves of books and scrolls fill the room, but their spines and pages are all blank. It is a library of a fate-god with nothing written in it, or nothing yet.

## History

The tower first appeared when Vythorn, the Arbiter, was drifting alone through the in-between. After his death his soul had gone to the Sands of Eternity and slipped free of them. Before he could go anywhere else, Fil found him and brought him to this house in between all things. Fil's realm lies outside every material plane, the Void included. That makes the tower one of the very few places Vythorn has been that sits beyond the reach of the world he knew.

## Notable Figures

- [[Fil]]: the God of Fate, who calls the tower home.
- [[The Arbiter]]: Vythorn, who was Fil's guest here in Vythorn's Return.
