**Strixhaven** is a great university of magic somewhere out among the worlds the [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|Grandmaster]] pulls her champions from. No Deathmatch has ever been fought on its grounds, but it keeps turning up in the champions' stories. For one pair of champions it was the place their love story went wrong. For others it is a handy measuring stick: if you are good at magic, you say how you rank against Strixhaven.

## Description

Strixhaven is divided into colleges, and students wear their college's emblem with pride. **Lorehold** is one of them, and [[Sorry]] still carried its emblem on her shield years after leaving. There are potion classes, essays to write and tests to pass, and field trips that apparently go straight into dungeons with the younger students in tow.

By Sorry's account, the coursework was "based solely on… regurgitating information." That suited her nicely. Her one spell, comprehend languages, only works if she touches the writing, and she does that by eating the paper. Her old partner [[Lethe]] wondered aloud how she ever graduated, and suspected the professors simply "couldn't actually find the papers to grade." Sorry admitted that not everything went down easily: "that one essay on bugbears… kinda didn't sit very well."

The school also has a reputation for deciding who belongs. Being told you "wouldn't fit in to Strixhaven" was enough to send at least one person running.

## History

### A Lovers' Quarrel

Strixhaven first came up in [[Episodes/Season 2/027 - S2E3 — A Lovers' Quarrel — Lethe v Sorry|S2E3 — A Lovers' Quarrel — Lethe v Sorry]], when two former lovers were pulled into the Void to fight in a snowy winter town the Grandmaster had let them use. It started with teasing. Lethe mocked Sorry's paper-eating study habits and asked how she had ever made it through school. Then the fight took a strange turn. A shop in the town carried Lethe back to **the week the Witchlight Carnival came to Strixhaven**, when Lethe had waited outside Sorry's potion class. The memory hung over the scene like an overlay of the old campus and only made Lethe angrier. They planted their glaive and waited. "Waiting, I love it," said the Grandmaster.

The Sorry who answered was a version who had grown up without Lethe in her life. She was confident, grinning, sword drawn, with her Lorehold shield on her arm: "I'm here. You called? Bring it." Lethe assumed she was still a student, or perhaps a teacher by now. Sorry was offended by both guesses. She had graduated, and when the Void grabbed her she had been leading younger students on a dungeon field trip: "There are little ones that are probably wandering around lost without me." She did not think much of the job: "I'm not an adventurer… I'm a glorified babysitter."

Then the truth came out mid-fight. Lethe had wanted to ask Sorry to run away with them and the carnival. But Sorry had said Lethe wouldn't fit in at Strixhaven, and Lethe heard that as *we don't fit together*. "So I left." Sorry lowered her sword with tears streaming down her face and told Lethe what she had really meant all along:

> "Of course you don't fit in at Strixhaven. You're better than all of them." (Sorry)

### Name-Dropping in the Arena

Strixhaven came up again in Season 3's "Homunculus," where [[Alfucius the Everbright]] woke in the Void to face [[Coen]]. He was a wizened old man in flowing purple robes and a very pointed hat. He bowed low before the Grandmaster and listed the company he had kept, including the librarians of Candlekeep, the black staff of Waterdeep, and "the magic weavers of Strixhaven and Eberron." None of them, he insisted, was as powerful as her.

In the [[Episodes/Season 3/070 - S3E17, Season 3 Finale - Soot Mustam v Huey Leatherface|S3E17, Season 3 Finale - Soot Mustam v Huey Leatherface]], [[Huey Leatherface]] used the school as a landmark. He introduced himself to the Acolytes as "the best dang artificer in this side of Strixhaven." He makes potions and "little tinkery things," throws them "real good," and carries a blunderbuss called Old Bessie. He went on to face [[Soot Mustam]] in the season's last fight.

## Notable Figures

- [[Sorry]]: graduate of Strixhaven's Lorehold college, who later led field trips there.
- [[Lethe]]: Sorry's former partner, who left with the Witchlight Carnival after believing they didn't fit in at Strixhaven.
- [[Alfucius the Everbright]]: an elderly wizard who claimed to have served alongside Strixhaven's magic weavers.
- [[Huey Leatherface]]: a self-described top artificer from "this side of Strixhaven."
