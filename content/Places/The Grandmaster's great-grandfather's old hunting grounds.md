**The Grandmaster's great-grandfather's old hunting grounds** are a stretch of wilderness whose name hints at a long family history in the Void. Old trees, a roaring waterfall and a broken bridge over dangerous water made them a fittingly dramatic place for a fight to the death. They served as the arena in [[Episodes/Season 2/028 - S2E4 — Antsy — Glub Glub v Geril-Lee|S2E4 — Antsy — Glub Glub v Geril-Lee]], where [[Glub Glub the Glorious|Glub Glub]] faced [[Geril-Lee]].

## Description
The grounds are dominated by old trees, which are said to "have seen many battles." The forest gives the place a weathered, storied feel. Beyond the trees, a roaring waterfall pours into a treacherous river that churns into rapids downstream, so a wrong step means a very bad swim.

Across the water stands a broken stone bridge, a crumbling crossing that is more hazard than help. High above it all, straight up in the sky, float the grandstands of [[The Acolytes]], giving the crowd a clear view of every stumble near the river's edge.

## History
The hunting grounds appeared as the battlefield for the Deathmatch between Glub Glub and Geril-Lee in S2E4, "Antsy." The champions fought among the ancient trees, the waterfall and the shattered bridge while the Acolytes watched from the sky above.

## Notable Figures
- [[Glub Glub the Glorious|Glub Glub]]: one of the two champions who fought here in "Antsy."
- [[Geril-Lee]]: Glub Glub's opponent in the same Deathmatch.
- [[The Acolytes]]: watched the fight from grandstands floating high above.
