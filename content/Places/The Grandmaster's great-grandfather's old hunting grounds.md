**The Grandmaster's great-grandfather's old hunting grounds** are a stretch of wilderness that once belonged to the hunts of the Grandmaster's great-grandfather. Old trees, a roaring waterfall, a broken stone bridge and a treacherous river made them a fittingly dramatic place for a fight to the death. They served as the arena in [[Episodes/Season 2/028 - S2E4 — Antsy — Glub Glub v Geril-Lee|S2E4 — Antsy — Glub Glub v Geril-Lee]], where [[Glub Glub the Glorious|Glub Glub]] faced [[Geril-Lee]].

## Description
The grounds are home to old trees, which are said to "have seen many battles." There is also a roaring waterfall and a treacherous river that runs into rapids downstream.

A broken stone bridge rounds out the scenery. Straight up above the sky float the grandstands of [[The Acolytes]], who watch the action from on high.

## History
The hunting grounds appeared as the battlefield for the Deathmatch between Glub Glub and Geril-Lee in S2E4, "Antsy." The champions fought in a setting of old trees, a waterfall and a broken bridge while the Acolytes watched from the sky above.

## Notable Figures
- [[Glub Glub the Glorious|Glub Glub]]: one of the two champions who fought here in "Antsy."
- [[Geril-Lee]]: Glub Glub's opponent in the same Deathmatch.
- [[The Acolytes]]: watched the fight from grandstands floating high above.
