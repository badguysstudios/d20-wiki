**The Chinchilla Forest** is where the Void gets its chinchillas. It is a stretch of tall trees and fresh air where the little creatures run wild, roll in talcum powder and grow big and strong nibbling the bones of champions left to rot there. It has hosted three Deathmatches so far. The forest has suffered for it: by the end of its second fight, part of it was stained orange, cratered and decayed into a radioactive wasteland.

## Description

At first glance the forest is lovely. It is a lush pine woodland of tall trees and clean air, with a chinchilla den tucked among the trunks. For Deathmatches the canopy is opened up, and a ring of stands hangs above it so [[The Acolytes]] can look down on the carnage. The ground drinks blood, which is probably for the best.

The [[Chinchillas]] themselves are the main attraction. According to [[Thea]], they "run wild and free and sometimes nibble on the bones of the champions who've been left to rot here… so they grow up big and strong." They are also said to keep a roaming secret kitchen. Giant plastic pools of talcum powder sit around the forest, and the chinchillas roll in them.

## History

### S5E4: Chaos in Chinchilla Forest

The forest made its debut in [[Episodes/Season 5/102 - S5E4 — Chaos in Chinchilla Forest — Tormiir v Granny Edith|S5E4 — Chaos in Chinchilla Forest — Tormiir v Granny Edith]], during the Season of Fate. Thea opened the way by standing inside a doughnut hole and pulling its sides down "like a curtain." The scene dropped into the woods and she flew through, trailing a puff of ink dust. [[The Arbiter]], ruling that season as the Lord of Fate, explained that the setting let the Acolytes "see where we source our local chinchillas." [[Tormiir]] and [[Granny Edith]] then fought among the trees.

### S5E11: Appliance Adversaries

The Deathmatch returned to the forest in [[Episodes/Season 5/109 - S5E11 — Appliance Adversaries — Atlas v Frigidoom|S5E11 — Appliance Adversaries — Atlas v Frigidoom]]. Thea had decided to double up on her chores: since the match was in the forest, she would release the chinchillas there. It went badly for the scenery. By the time [[Atlas]] and [[Frigidoom]] were done, the forest was stained orange. Pooka Pooka had knocked down trees, and two craters scarred the ground: one from a blast and one from Atlas's geyser hole. Worst of all, Frigidoom's sweat had decayed part of the forest into a radioactive wasteland, and the chinchillas needed a new home.

### S7E5: Rock and a Hard Place

The forest was back in the Season of Vigilance for [[Episodes/Season 7/133 - S7E5 — Rock and a Hard Place — Iowa-Ska v the Pebble|S7E5 — Rock and a Hard Place — Iowa-Ska v the Pebble]], with tall trees, fresh air and a chinchilla den once again. By this point the chinchillas were unionized. [[Iowa-Ska]] and Thea spent part of the fight "just rocking out," and Iowa-Ska was having the time of his life:

> "I'm still doing burnouts! Go faster! I don't care if it's scratched, I'm having fun for the first time!"

The Arbiter confirmed the location ("this is Chinchilla Forest"). Iowa-Ska then stage-dived into one of the talcum-powder pools. The Grandmaster looked at him and said, "It looks like you've done so much cocaine." He climbed out of his "chinchilla ecstasy" just in time to see a minotaur charging at him. His opponent in the match was [[The Pebble]].
