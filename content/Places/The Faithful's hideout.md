**The Faithful's hideout** is a converted blacksmith's shop, all cold forges and hanging sheets. It served as the base of the Faithful, the resistance that stayed loyal to [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] while [[The Arbiter|the Arbiter]] worked against her. It is not a palace or a fortress. It is a smithy with a schematics room and exactly one good cushion. For about six months, though, it was home.

## Description

At its heart the hideout is an old smith shop, with its forges still in place and the working floor of a blacksmith's trade around them. Its occupants made it livable on a budget. Sheets strung up as partitions divide the space into something like rooms. Magic fans keep the air moving through a building that was designed to hold heat.

The shop has a schematics room, where planning happens. It also has a living room. The living room's most notable feature, and possibly its only real comfort, is a single good cushion. Anyone who has ever shared a flat can guess how fiercely a cushion like that gets defended.

## History

The hideout first appeared in [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/044 - Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 5 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 5 (A Crossover)]]. In that episode it was a blacksmith's forge with a smith shop and a living room with one good cushion.

It turned up again in [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/045 - S2E17 — Fate of the Arbiter - The Hall of Fates — Toddleheim, Locket and Flynry reach the Grandmaster|S2E17 — Fate of the Arbiter - The Hall of Fates — Toddleheim, Locket and Flynry reach the Grandmaster]]. By then it had plainly become a makeshift headquarters, with its forges, sheet partitions, magic fans and schematics room. It had also been home for about six months. That is a long time to live behind bedsheets in a workshop, and a good measure of how long the resistance had been holding out.
