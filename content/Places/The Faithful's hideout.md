**The Faithful's hideout** is a converted blacksmith's shop, all forges and hanging sheets. It served as the base of the Faithful, the resistance that stayed loyal to [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] while [[The Arbiter|the Arbiter]] worked against her. It is not a palace or a fortress. It is a smithy with a schematics room and exactly one good cushion. For about six months, though, it was home.

## Description

At its heart the hideout is an old smith shop, with its forges. Its occupants made it livable. Sheets strung up as partitions divide the space into something like rooms. Magic fans round out the improvised comforts.

The shop has a schematics room. It also has a living room. The living room's most notable feature is a single good cushion.

## History

The hideout first appeared in [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/044 - Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 5 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 5 (A Crossover)]]. In that episode it was a blacksmith's forge with a smith shop and a living room with one good cushion.

It turned up again in [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/045 - S2E17 — Fate of the Arbiter - The Hall of Fates — Toddleheim, Locket and Flynry reach the Grandmaster|S2E17 — Fate of the Arbiter - The Hall of Fates — Toddleheim, Locket and Flynry reach the Grandmaster]]. By then it had plainly become a makeshift headquarters, with its forges, sheet partitions, magic fans and schematics room. It had also been home for about six months. That is a long time to live behind bedsheets in a workshop.
