**The Jungle Gauntlet** is the Grandmaster's "oldest of training grounds": a jungle obstacle course with two lanes. It runs from the log and squirrel trees, over a 45-foot cliff and a lily-pad river with a waterfall, through a forest thick with pollen, past a clearing belonging to the Box of Chaos, and into a cave full of ghosts and skeletons. It hosted the Gauntlet between [[Dan Kowalski]] and [[Gordy Gotta Get Em]].

## Description

The Jungle Gauntlet is less an arena than a gauntlet in the literal sense: a run of stages, one after another, with two lanes. It opens among the log and squirrel trees. Beyond them is a 45-foot cliff, then a river crossed by lily pads, with a waterfall.

Past the river is a forest hazy with blue and yellow pollen. Then comes a clearing that belongs to [[The Box of Chaos]], which is never a comforting thing to meet in the middle of a race. The final stage is a narrow cave crowded with ghosts, skeletons and webs. It also holds treasure chests and a portal rune circle.

## History

The Jungle Gauntlet appeared in [[Episodes/Season 2/029 - S2E5 — The Gauntlet — Dan Kowalski v Gordy Gotta Get Em|S2E5 — The Gauntlet — Dan Kowalski v Gordy Gotta Get Em]]. The Grandmaster presented it as her oldest of training grounds for Dan Kowalski and Gordy Gotta Get Em. Its stages were the trees, the cliff, the lily-pad river, the pollen forest, the Box of Chaos clearing and, finally, the haunted cave with its rune circle.
