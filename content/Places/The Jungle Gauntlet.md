**The Jungle Gauntlet** is the Grandmaster's "oldest of training grounds": a jungle obstacle course with two lanes. It runs from logs and squirrel-filled trees, over a sheer cliff and a waterfall river, through a forest thick with pollen, and into a cave full of the dead. It hosted the Gauntlet between [[Dan Kowalski]] and [[Gordy Gotta Get Em]], a Deathmatch where the course was nearly as much of an opponent as the other champion.

## Description

The Jungle Gauntlet is less an arena than a gauntlet in the literal sense: a run of stages, one after another, with two lanes. It opens among the log and squirrel trees. Beyond them is a 45-foot cliff, then a river crossed by lily pads, with a waterfall nearby for anyone who misjudges a jump.

Past the river is a forest hazy with blue and yellow pollen. Then comes a clearing that belongs to [[The Box of Chaos]], which is never a comforting thing to meet halfway through a race. The final stage is a narrow cave crowded with ghosts, skeletons and webs. It also holds treasure chests and a portal rune circle, so whatever waits in the dark, there is at least a way out.

## History

The Jungle Gauntlet appeared in [[Episodes/Season 2/029 - S2E5 — The Gauntlet — Dan Kowalski v Gordy Gotta Get Em|S2E5 — The Gauntlet — Dan Kowalski v Gordy Gotta Get Em]]. The Grandmaster presented it as her oldest of training grounds, and Dan Kowalski and Gordy Gotta Get Em ran its stages: the trees, the cliff, the lily-pad river, the pollen forest, the Box of Chaos clearing and, finally, the haunted cave with its rune circle.
