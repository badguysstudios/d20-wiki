**The abandoned dwarven fortress** is a long-deserted stronghold tucked into the mountains, a day's ride away. It appears in [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/024 - S2 Ep1, Fate of the Arbiter (Fate of the Arbiter Part 1)|S2 Ep1, Fate of the Arbiter (Fate of the Arbiter Part 1)]]. Whoever built it is long gone, but they left plenty behind: blades, bones, wine, a gambling room, and a carved message hinting at something far more valuable buried deeper in the stone.

## Description
The first thing anyone notices is the columns, shaped like an old man's face. They give the place the feel of being watched by a grumpy grandfather made of rock. Beyond them, a back gate opens onto a great hall that has been thoroughly demolished.

Past the ruined hall lie the trap halls, a string of rooms that alternate between deadly and oddly domestic. One holds a blade track. Another is a cellar of bones and wine. There are bedrolls where someone once slept and a gambling room where someone once lost money. Further in are a bookcase and a crystal room sealed by an iron gate with three bolts.

At the heart of the fortress is a cathedral chamber lit in purple. Somewhere inside, a carving mentions "the hammer that resides in the mountain" and "the treasure of the east."

## History
The fortress's history before it was abandoned is unknown. Its builders, its fall and the meaning of its carving have not been explained. All that is known is what remains: the wrecked great hall, the trapped passages and the purple-lit cathedral at its core. These were explored during [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/024 - S2 Ep1, Fate of the Arbiter (Fate of the Arbiter Part 1)|Fate of the Arbiter Part 1]].

The carving's two phrases, "the hammer that resides in the mountain" and "the treasure of the east," are the fortress's clearest clues to its secrets. What exactly they point to remains a mystery.
