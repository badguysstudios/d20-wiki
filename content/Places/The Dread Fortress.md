**The Dread Fortress** is the seat of [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]]'s power and her home. It is a giant orc skull of rock, burning-eyed and broken-tusked, squatting in a sea of lava at the very center of [[The Void]]. It can be seen from every realm in the Void, and visitors swear it follows you. Inside its mouth lies a damp, sprawling underground castle that has been a tyrant's lair, a construction site, a holiday showpiece and, most of the time, the place where champions wait to find out how they are going to die.

## Description

From the outside, the fortress is a monolith shaped like a humanoid **orc skull**, with one broken tusk and eyes full of fire, rising from hundreds of miles of scorching lava field. Volcanoes rumble around it, and steam from the lava drifts against its walls. It is so visible from every direction that travelers have used "the great orc skull and its flaming lava eyes" as the compass rose of the Void. Its glow is bright enough to cast shadows on distant spires.

Visitors enter through the skull's open mouth and descend into a **subterranean castle**. The air is cool, the stone halls drip with dew and lava condensation, and the walls are hung with portraits of the Grandmaster. Among them is the portrait of a man with a golden shield: Faucius Golden Shield, her father. Deeper down are meeting rooms, feast and dining halls, a banquet hall, a kitchen big enough to fill an entire floor, and at least 200 empty rooms "up above." The fortress is roughly divided into thirds, and there are dungeons, caverns and sewers below the lowest of them.

The heart of it all is the **throne room**: an iron-and-wood door (on some days, giant wooden doors), a throne of metal and bone with red velvet seats, a hearth, a purple rug and a stone face of the Grandmaster carved above the throne. Since Season 3 the Arbiter has had his own chair beside it, and goblins have been known to perch on top of the throne.

The rest of the castle is a rabbit warren of oddities collected over the years:
- **Living quarters:** the Grandmaster's bed chamber (with a David Copperfield poster), her master bathroom with new marble sinks and tile floors, and, later, her private bathroom.
- **Service areas:** a pitchfork closet with a golden handle that doubles as a pantry for rice and dry goods, a brewery, an armory, a printing press, a gym (weights, no Pelotons) and an attic full of goblin winter clothes, photo albums, a box of crowns and, inexplicably, a horse.
- **Leisure and training:** a koi pond room, a small black room with a stage, the chinchilla room (a training simulator), training pits and a labyrinth with no toilets.
- **Behind the scenes:** a lobby and prep room where champions wait, the locker room where fallen champions are revived, concession stands, a portal relay room with five gates and an arrivals-and-departures board, and **Stuart's hot little closet full of screens** in the basement.
- **The Arbiter's spaces:** a scholarly wing called "the Realm of the Arbiter," a screening room, an office, and a secret lab full of cadavers.
- **Doug's twelve refrigerators of cheese.**

The staff include [[Thea]], [[Stuart]] in IT, [[Claude]] the janitor, a great many goblins and servants, and the unionized [[Chinchillas]]. The Grandmaster also keeps a standing army.

The fortress has also turned out to be awake. In Season 7 the skull spoke for the first time, and revealed its name was **[[Seavold]]**. Nobody had ever asked. It explained that it is only a head ("just a noggin, some good teeth"), called the champion in front of it "an ant" and dismissed the Deathmatch as "a farce."

## History

### The Shadow King's Seat

The fortress has been an orc-skull rock for as long as the story goes back. In the flashbacks of [[Episodes/Skypiercer (Prequel)/001 - Skypiercer Part 1|Skypiercer Part 1]] it was the home of young Elusia, who had her own chambers there and called its walls unscalable and unbreachable. They were breached anyway, from the inside. On the Night of the Long Shadow her uncle Agdrel killed her father, Faucius Golden Shield, in the throne room. In the **Ceremonial Chamber**, the Knights of Chaos gave their lives in a blood rite to send Elusia out of the Void to safety.

Agdrel ruled from the fortress as [[The Shadow King]] for 300 years. When Elusia finally returned in [[Episodes/Skypiercer (Prequel)/003 - Skypiercer Part 3 (finale)|Skypiercer Part 3]], she found her home in ruins. The Ceremonial Chamber had become Agdrel's trophy room, and the banquet hall, with its magical night-sky ceiling, lay wrecked. The great wood-and-iron throne room doors were broken and the red carpet burned. Hallways lit by magical lanterns still led past kitchens, game rooms, servants' quarters and guest rooms, and deep within the fortress sat the laboratory of [[The Arbiter]]. After defeating the Shadow King in [[Skypiercer]], Elusia took the fortress for herself and rebuilt parts of it. She sometimes claims she built the whole thing herself. That's a joke, though some in the Void may believe it.

### The Grandmaster's Home (Season 1 and the Early Deathmatches)

By the time the Deathmatches began, the fortress was described as a "subterranean castle" with champions summoned down to its lowest level, as in [[Episodes/Playtests/004 - Playtest 1 (Pre-Launch) — Silky Slim v Escanor the Echo|Playtest 1]]. [[Episodes/Season 1/010 - S1 Ep1 — Ditch Em' Darrell v Eshrinn|S1 Ep1]] set out the classic tour: down through the skull's mouth, past portraits of the Grandmaster and the feast halls, to the iron door of the throne room. It was a grand place, if not a well-stocked one. Food, it turned out in [[Episodes/Season 1/011 - Magic — S1 Ep2 — Mageic v Amren|S1 Ep2]], is hard to come by there. Somewhere inside, the Arbiter brought fallen champions back to life ([[Episodes/Season 1/013 - S1E4 — Multiclass — Kleen v Ivanna Kishu|S1E4]]). For [[Episodes/Season 1/021 - S1E12 — Feast — Potato v Dinkledink|S1E12]] the whole place was decked out for the holidays with wreaths, garland, lights and a tree.

### Fate of the Arbiter and Season 2

When the Faithful marched on the fortress in [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/024 - S2 Ep1, Fate of the Arbiter (Fate of the Arbiter Part 1)|Fate of the Arbiter Part 1]], they crossed the lands between it and [[The Eastern Outpost]], including a broken stone bridge. They entered through the skull's mouth into cool, dank halls lined with Elusia's portraits, and found an illusion waiting for them in the throne room.

Season 2 put the building itself to work. The **kitchen**, a two-level hall of crumbling stone with a balcony, trophy-covered walls, barrels of mead and a wall clock, became the arena for [[Episodes/Season 2/026 - S2E2 — Prize Fight — Asena v Ralzamon|Asena v Ralzamon]]. In one corner sat a deep, dark, seemingly endless pit, and the Arbiter threw [[Ralzamon]] into it. Later episodes revealed a gong solo echoing through the halls, the Grandmaster's bed chamber and pitchfork closet ([[Episodes/Season 2/033 - S2E10 — Filicide — Glassy v Thornabub|S2E10]]), pantries and a fridge in the lower third of the castle ([[Episodes/Season 2/034 - S2E11 — Secrets — Blaktoof v Zorhel|S2E11]]), and a sewer system serving her freshly renovated bathroom ([[Episodes/Season 2/038 - S2E15 — Black Hole Sun — Professor Stabbz v Mad Cass O Weary|S2E15]]).

The sewers mattered more than anyone expected. In [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/045 - S2E17 — Fate of the Arbiter - The Hall of Fates — Toddleheim, Locket and Flynry reach the Grandmaster|The Hall of Fates]], [[Toddleheim]], [[Locket]] and [[Flynry]] fought their way through miles of pipe and acid sewage full of "magical waste," past a smugglers' chamber, a drain chain, service rooms and a gated tunnel, to a dead end under the floor. Meanwhile the Arbiter held court in an ice-covered throne room he called "my throne room," a shadowy mirror of the fortress he had conjured for himself.

During the [[Episodes/Battle for the Crown/046 - S2E18 · Battle for the Crown Round 1 · Team Grandmaster (Toddleheim & Veronica) v Team Arbiter (Ralzamon & …|Battle for the Crown]], the Arbiter's coup made the fortress a war zone of halls, stone steps, armory and printing press. He made plans to move his own statue into the throne room. By [[Episodes/Battle for the Crown/048 - S2E20 — Battle for the Crown Round 3 — Flynry & Prof. Stabbz v Thornabub & Mcswiggins|Round 3]] the redecoration was underway, and a water bed had been parked in front of the throne. At the [[Episodes/Live Shows/049 - Live - GCX Orlando 2022 — Lord Snacc v The Showman|GCX Orlando 2022]] live show, it was joked that the fortress had been brought "from further North Orlando" and was getting a new wing added, without permits.

### Renovations (Season 3)

Season 3 was the season of the contractors. The Arbiter, wearing sackcloth and chains as punishment for his coup, worked at a paperwork desk in the throne room ([[Episodes/Season 3/050 - S3E1 — Kill em with Kindness — Lumos the Spark v Ferndell Ironwind|S3E1]]). In [[Episodes/Season 3/052 - S3E3 — Couples Therapy — Chade v Kairen|S3E3]], one room under renovation had a pool of water in the middle, a sink overflowing into it, and a demolished load-bearing wall. It was described as "a place we've seen a person die once or twice." By [[Episodes/Season 3/053 - S3E4, Crypto Monkeys - K-Boom v Ignacio Marchade|S3E4]] the hallways were still cracked, but **the kitchen was finally done**, and a basement offered free cowbells. [[Episodes/Season 3/054 - S3E5, Edge Lords - Captain Fable v Verryn Ruth|S3E5]] added a closet behind the throne, a basement full of plush d20s and a goblin-renovated kitchen. Other discoveries that season included a berry bush behind the castle and the Grandmaster's craft workshop below the dungeon.

In [[Episodes/Into the Mirror/064 - Into the Mirror, Session 1 (YouTube Parts 1–6 - Flynry Dies, Flynry Wakes, Flynry Walks, Survivors, Reflect…|Into the Mirror]], deep stairs beneath the throne room led down to the locked **Hall of Fates**, also called the Hall of Mirrors. Its mirrors are a deception: an otherworldly entity that eats souls.

### Darkness Again (Season 4)

At the close of [[Episodes/Season 3/070 - S3E17, Season 3 Finale - Soot Mustam v Huey Leatherface|the Season 3 Finale]], the Shadow King returned and the fortress went dark again, "as it once was the first time that you entered." Through [[Episodes/Season 4/076 - S4E1 — Shadows Reign — Atalantos v Kaja|Season 4]] it stood amid constant volcanic eruptions, reachable only by magic. The throne room was redecorated in his theme, the halls grew filthy, musty and candlelit, and the Grandmaster was a captive in all but name. She still ran the Deathmatches for him from inside her own home. The throne room gained a grill ([[Episodes/Season 4/085 - S4E7 — Wings — Misery v Daisy Bloom|S4E7]]) and a giant map table of the Void ([[Episodes/Season 4/086 - S4E8 — Blood Rain — Fatale v Claudia|S4E8]]). Champions waited in a prep room off a long, pillared hallway, then descended into echoing, dungeon-like lower levels.

### The Season of Fate (Season 5)

In the Season of Fate, the vision of a timeline in which the Arbiter ruled, the fortress became the Lord of Fate's seat. [[Episodes/Season 5/099 - S5E1 — Rise of the Lord of Fate — Atlas v Killy Yesman|S5E1]] found it silent and "straight-laced," hung with blue tabards and pictures of "what used to be the Arbiter." It now had a front office, an air-conditioned interior and the Lord of Fate's portraits everywhere, along with protesters outside and a back locker room ([[Episodes/Season 5/104 - S5E6 — American Amphibian — King Man Frog Bofus v Richard Knottley|S5E6]]). The Lord of Fate's bedroom wore Halloween decorations. Thea's old bedroom had become an office, and the attic held its famous horse ([[Episodes/Season 5/108 - S5E10 — BoneBound Brawl — King Bofus v Bone Daddy (and Darcy)|S5E10]]). Far below the dungeons, caverns held a "hallowed hall" where the chinchillas were kept.

[[Episodes/Season 5/110 - S5E12 — Imitation Game — Kopy Katt v Ragu|S5E12]] added a library, a downstairs brewery, mega screens throughout, a dungeon bathroom and a basement closet holding Thea's costume stash. Thea later nailed planks across the kitchen door. The [[The Acolytes|Acolytes]] watched from hard metal chairs, and the usual candles and fur rugs were swapped out for drabness at the finale. In [[Episodes/Vythorn's Return/115 - S5 Ep17 — Vythorn's Return Part 4|Vythorn's Return Part 4]], the upper halls stood abandoned and cobwebbed, and in the dungeons below, the vision had Elusia in a cell.

### The Season of the Trials (Season 6)

In [[Episodes/Season 6/117 - S6E1 — Murder at the Dread Fortress (TTRPG Murder Mystery)|Murder at the Dread Fortress]], the castle became a crime scene. Down through the hot and humid "sulking mouth" were a bloody carpet, a reception hall with a diamond mosaic floor and a stage, the banquet hall with its long tables and chandelier, the kitchen downstairs where [[Tim]] lives, and the magic department and IT. That same episode saw Thea created from a worm that had sucked up the blood of the arena, murdered, and brought back by the Arbiter. The rest of the season showed off the Arbiter's screening room and office, concession stands, a courtyard, the chinchilla cage behind heavy doors, Thea's sign shed, and the Grandmaster's stash in the kitchen.

### Pieces of a King

[[Episodes/Pieces of a King/124 - Pieces of a King Ep 1 — DӒMMERUNG|Pieces of a King]] gave the fullest tour yet. It took in breweries and training pits, [[Grimnar Stormclaw|Grimnar]]'s office and [[Nehebka]]'s chamber, and a courtyard with a gallery. It also visited the domain of dread called "the Realm of the Arbiter," the scholarly wing where he presides over a retinue of knights and researchers. Stolas's equatorial had a galaxy door, an eye-orb doorkeeper and a ceiling that looks out on the Void. There was also the portal relay room and the goo pit, a resurrection tube full of goo.

### The Season of Vigilance (Season 7)

Season 7 revealed even more of the castle. The chinchilla room, the koi pond room, the brewery, an intercom, a lobby where champions wait and cows grazing "right outside the fortress" all turned up in [[Episodes/Season 7/130 - S7E2 — Under the Oil Tree — Big Business v Bog Boy|S7E2]]. Stuart's screen closet appeared in [[Episodes/Season 7/131 - S7E3 — Chinchilla the White — Akumu v Jinx|S7E3]]. In [[Episodes/Season 7/132 - S7E4 — The Cheese — Sila v Ol' Pumpernicklejack|S7E4]] the throne room briefly became a chess table, and [[Doug the Pug|Doug]]'s twelve cheese refrigerators came to light. A labyrinth was under design by [[Episodes/Season 7/133 - S7E5 — Rock and a Hard Place — Iowa-Ska v the Pebble|S7E5]].

Then, in [[Episodes/Season 7/134 - S7E6 — Bone Force — Jerry Bonesman v TD|S7E6]], the skull itself finally spoke as Seavold, and the Arbiter's secret lab of cadavers was exposed. The same episode left Thea polymorphed into a T-Rex, and by [[Episodes/Season 7/135 - S7E7 — A Father's Son — Agatha Middlebrooke v Lug Hardblock|S7E7]] she had wrecked a hallway so badly its foundation needed repair. [[Episodes/Season 7/136 - S7E8 — Private Bathrooms — Livid Lady Lin v Gandor Thalion Umbrae|S7E8]] closed out the tour with the Grandmaster's private bathroom.

## Notable Figures

- [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer]]: born here, exiled from here, and its ruler since defeating the Shadow King.
- [[The Shadow King]]: ruled from its throne for 300 years, and again in Season 4.
- [[The Arbiter]]: keeps a laboratory, a scholarly wing, an office and a chair beside the throne.
- [[Seavold]]: the skull itself, only recently on speaking terms.
- [[Thea]]: the Grandmaster's assistant, created and murdered here, and the reason one hallway needs a new foundation.
- [[Stuart]]: IT, from a hot little closet full of screens.
- [[Claude]]: the janitor.
- [[Tim]]: lives downstairs by the kitchen.
- [[Doug the Pug]]: owner of twelve refrigerators of cheese.
- [[Chinchillas]]: unionized residents of their own room and cage.
- [[Grimnar Stormclaw|Grimnar]] and [[Nehebka]]: kept an office and a chamber here during Pieces of a King.
