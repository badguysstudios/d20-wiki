**The Dread Fortress** is the seat of the Grandmaster's power and her home, at the center of [[The Void]].

## Description
- A rock structure shaped like a giant **orc skull** with a broken tusk and burning eyes, rising from a field of lava. It can be seen from every realm in the Void.
- The mouth of the skull leads down to an underground castle: cool, dripping stone halls lined with portraits of the Grandmaster, meeting rooms, feast halls, a banquet hall and the throne room (iron door, a throne of metal and bone, a hearth).
- In Season 7 the skull spoke for the first time. Its name is **Seavold**, and no one had ever asked it before (S7E6). It is only a head ("just a noggin, some good teeth"), and it called the champions "an ant" and the match "a farce."

## Rooms and residents
- The throne room, the koi pond room, the brewery, the chinchilla room (a training simulator), the Grandmaster's private bathroom (S7E8), the Arbiter's secret lab of cadavers (S7E6), the labyrinth (no toilets), Stuart's hot little closet full of screens in the basement, and Doug's twelve refrigerators of cheese.
- Staff include [[Thea]], [[Stuart]] (IT), [[Claude]] (janitor), the goblins and the unionized [[Chinchillas]].
- The Grandmaster also keeps a standing army.

## History
- The Dread Fortress was [[The Shadow King]]'s seat during his 300-year reign. Elusia took it after defeating him in [[Skypiercer]] and rebuilt parts of it.
- She sometimes claims she built it herself. That's a joke, though some in the Void may believe it.
