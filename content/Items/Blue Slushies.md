**Blue slushies** are the official drink of bloodshed. Wherever the floating grandstands appear, [[The Acolytes|the Acolytes]] are holding them, spilling them, hurling them at each other and pouring them over their own faces, usually while ripping off their shirts. They began as stadium snacks sold alongside hot dogs. By the Season of Vigilance they had become something closer to a currency of favor: a champion drenched in enough blue slushie fights like someone with a whole crowd at their back.

## Description

Blue slushies are blue, cold and extremely sticky. When [[TD]] was pelted with them, it ended up coated in "sticky blue raspberry syrup," which settles the flavor. They are sold at the arena's concession stands next to hot dogs and plizzies. Before big nights the staff of [[The Dread Fortress|the Dread Fortress]] make sure the stands are restocked. They are not cheap. As [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]] reminded one crowd, they are "expensive in this economy."

The crowd mostly drinks them as a formality. Their real purpose is to be thrown. A good moment in a fight brings a shower of them down from the stands, and Acolytes have been known to aim them at the champion they like best. A champion who gets doused with slushies gets a jolt of adrenaline. One who collects five of them finds their next blow landing with devastating force. The Acolytes' favor lasts only as long as their mood, though. As [[The Arbiter|the Arbiter]] put it, the slushies help "until they don't like you."

There is only one flavor that counts. In one telling the crowd spilled their "blue and only blue slushies."

## History

The slushies first appeared during the Shadow King's reign, in [[Fatale]]'s fight against [[Claudia]]. After the Grandmaster snapped her fingers to give everyone "a bit of a reset," the match broke so the crowd could fetch blue slushies and the hot dogs could be cleared off the ship.

They became a fixture of the crowd during the Season of Fate, the vision in which the Arbiter ruled the Void as the Lord of Fate. Acolytes threw them at each other just before [[The Box of Chaos]] slammed down between [[Robbie Clark and the Hunger]] and [[Bob the Barbarian]]. They flew as [[Bone Daddy]] squared off with [[King Bofus]]. During [[Atlas]]'s fight with [[Frigidoom]], the crowd spilled them while failing to chant "Frigidoom," because the name has too many syllables. They flew everywhere at [[Copycat|Kopy Katt]]'s match with [[Ragu]], where Acolytes tore off their breakaway jeans "as is tradition." In [[Ruby Rust]]'s rematch with Bone Daddy, the Lord of Fate apologized for getting distracted and declared "**Blue slushies on me**," and attendants and goblins handed them out to the whole crowd. When [[Teddy Oso]] cracked Ragu's face off its hinges with a spinning kick, the moment came in slow motion, with blue slushies spilling everywhere.

In the Season of the Trials, the Grandmaster, [[Thea]] and the Arbiter rushed to restock the concession stands with blue slushies, hot dogs and plizzies before the Ether Drifts bout. They were late anyway. For the season finale Thea made sure the slushies were full while [[Doug the Pug]] yelled in the background. Thousands of Acolytes then threw them over the wrecked tavern where [[Emmery]] was wiping the bar and [[Frigidoom]], Bob the Barbarian and [[The Duke]] fought it out.

In the Season of Vigilance the slushies stopped being decoration. In [[Episodes/Season 7/129 - S7E1 — A Soup Served Cold — DeeDrah v Gazpacho Man Andy Gammage|S7E1 — A Soup Served Cold — DeeDrah v Gazpacho Man Andy Gammage]], the Acolytes aimed theirs straight at [[Andy Gammage]]. He guzzled one and fought on, fortified:

> **Andy Gammage:** "It's no gazpacho, but it'll do."

[[Big Business]] and [[Bog Boy]] were greeted by a ring of slushie-holding Acolytes. Big Business later regretted eating a raw steak on top of them ("between the slushies, I ain't feeling too good"), and the Grandmaster blamed "a combination of raw meat and blue slushies." Slushies and beer rained on [[Jinx]] as the crowd chanted her name in her fight with [[Akumu]]. In [[Episodes/Season 7/132 - S7E4 — The Cheese — Sila v Ol' Pumpernicklejack|S7E4 — The Cheese — Sila v Ol' Pumpernicklejack]], drunk Acolytes poured them over their faces while stomping on [[The minitaurs|the minitaurs]] in the crowd. The Grandmaster then warned [[Sila]] and [[Ol' Pumpernicklejack]] not to waste the crowd's generosity:

> **The Grandmaster:** "They have… poured out their blue slushies, which are expensive in this economy."

[[Iowa-Ska]] and [[The Pebble]] were both doused until each held five slushies' worth of fury. At the end, the Acolytes stood and poured out the rest in honor of the winner: "This is for your victory. This is for your legacy." On Father's Day the crowd threw slushies over each other with their fathers beside them, and splattered TD in blue syrup during its fight with [[Jerry Bonesman]]. When [[Agatha Middlebrooke]]'s toffee generosity stopped her match with [[Lug Hardblock]] for a toffee break, the Arbiter sent the crowd off to "go get yourself some blue slushies and some toffees."

In [[Episodes/Season 7/136 - S7E8 — Private Bathrooms — Livid Lady Lin v Gandor Thalion Umbrae|S7E8 — Private Bathrooms — Livid Lady Lin v Gandor Thalion Umbrae]], the slushies flew over a snowy mountain in the cold. The crowd chanted every name except [[Gandor Thalion Umbrae]]'s, which was too long. They then gave [[Livid Lady Lin]] five blue slushies, and with them she cast off his attempt to fill her head with doubt. Gandor got so angry his orb exploded. The Acolytes cheered with blue slushies once more under the graveyard moon of [[Episodes/Season 7/137 - S7E9 — Necro-Nightmare — Squiggles v Ray S'urdead|S7E9 — Necro-Nightmare — Squiggles v Ray S'urdead]], as [[Squiggles]] faced [[Ray S'urdead]].
