*First appearance: [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/042 - Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover|Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover]] · [watch it on YouTube](https://youtu.be/PVwtTWKQKz4)*

**Lawrence's Tuning Fork** is one of the smaller, stranger objects to pass through the Void during the Fate of the Faithful crossover, when the Coriander Society crossed paths with the Grandmaster's world. As the name says, it belonged to someone called Lawrence. Next to company like a stick of TNT and a bloodletting set, a humble tuning fork stands out for how quiet it is.

## Description

It is a tuning fork, the kind of two-pronged instrument that rings a clear note when struck. What, if anything, it did beyond that isn't known. Whatever its purpose, it is remembered as Lawrence's.

## History

The tuning fork first turned up in [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/042 - Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover|Coriander Society - Fate of The Faithful Part 3 — A Crossover]]. There it turned up alongside rather louder company, including John's TNT.

It appeared again in [[Episodes/Fate of the Faithful/043 - Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 4 (A Crossover)|Coriander Society — Fate of the Faithful Part 4 (A Crossover)]]. By then the collection of gear around it had grown more unsettling: a vintage bloodletting set and a vial of [[Asena]]'s blood. Where the fork ended up after the crossover is unknown.
