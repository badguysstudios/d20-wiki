**Lyrus's Void Swords** are the blades Lyrus carried through the Fate of the Arbiter saga. Their story is short and grim. They turn up beside a pair of severed hands that shriveled to nothing, and by the time the party reached the Hall of Fates, one of the swords had lost its edge.

## Description

The weapons are a set of more than one sword, named for their owner and for the Void, the strange realm outside time and space where the Deathmatches are fought. What powers, if any, the name implies has never been spelled out. They are remembered more for what happened around them than for any trick they performed.

## History

The swords first come up in [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/025 - S2E6 — FoTA - Sands of the Arena (Fate of the Arbiter, Part 2)|S2E6 — FoTA - Sands of the Arena (Fate of the Arbiter, Part 2)]]. There they are tied to an unpleasant prize: hands severed from [[The Arbiter]], which shriveled away. The trophy was not quite what it seemed. The hands belonged to a visage of the Arbiter, not the Arbiter himself, so the party's victory was emptier than it looked.

The blades appear again in [[Episodes/Fate of the Arbiter/045 - S2E17 — Fate of the Arbiter - The Hall of Fates — Toddleheim, Locket and Flynry reach the Grandmaster|S2E17 — Fate of the Arbiter - The Hall of Fates — Toddleheim, Locket and Flynry reach the Grandmaster]]. In that episode, [[Toddleheim]], [[Locket]] and [[Flynry]] finally made their way to [[Grandmaster Elusia Skypiercer|the Grandmaster]]. By then the swords were showing their wear, and one of them had been dulled.
