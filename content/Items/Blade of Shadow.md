The **Blade of Shadow** is a weapon with a dark bargain built into it. It holds a piece of [[The Shadow King]]'s soul, and it knits its wielder's wounds with every blow they land, but the longer they hold it, the more it drains them. It appeared twice during the Season of Fate, first in the hands of [[Bob the Barbarian]] and later as one of the Lord of Fate's own treasures, until [[Thea]] decided she'd had enough of it.

## Description

The blade's power comes from the fragment of the Shadow King trapped inside it. Every strike it lands heals the one holding it, which makes it a tempting prize in a fight to the death. The catch is that simply carrying it saps its bearer's stamina, so a champion who leans on it trades a slow grinding exhaustion for those quick recoveries.

When the blade first appeared, Thea gave a newcomer-friendly summary of its former owner:

> "Ooh, that's an old bad guy. He was bad. We were like, we didn't like him."

## History

Long before the blade turned up in the arena, the Shadow King had shown what his shadow could do as a weapon. In the Season 3 finale, after the clash between [[Soot Mustam]] and [[Huey Leatherface]], he turned his own hand into a blade of shadow and thrust it at [[Doug the Pug|Doug]]. [[The Arbiter]], rising from unconsciousness, threw himself in front of the blow and took it through the chest and heart. "Run, run, Doug, run," he urged, and Doug yipped and scampered between [[The Dragon Knights]] and out through a small doggy door.

The Blade of Shadow itself surfaced in [[Episodes/Season 5/105 - S5E7 — Ducks of Death — Robbie Clark & the Hunger v Bob the Barbarian|S5E7 — Ducks of Death — Robbie Clark & the Hunger v Bob the Barbarian]]. As fire turned the sky an eerie pink and purple, like an artificial sunset, Bob the Barbarian was handed the weapon with the warning, "You hold a piece of the Shadow King's soul." Each hit he landed healed him, even as the blade wore him down. Bob went on to win the match against [[Robbie Clark and the Hunger|Robbie Clark]] and [[The Hunger]].

The blade came back in [[Episodes/Season 5/109 - S5E11 — Appliance Adversaries — Atlas v Frigidoom|S5E11 — Appliance Adversaries — Atlas v Frigidoom]], during the fight between [[Atlas]] and [[Frigidoom]]. This time it was presented as part of the Lord of Fate's private collection, with the same gift and the same curse: healing with every strike, exhaustion with every moment held. It didn't stay in play for long, because Thea discarded it.
